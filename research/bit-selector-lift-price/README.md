# A paid-repair obstruction for selector sharing in the BIT native

**No new κ.** The current native's main-only scalar word is exactly identity.
A division-free selector lift consequently misses111 shears per additional
plane. We construct a literal rank-one-query repair and prove that its
complete paired-caller moment at κ=1/200 is negative for every fixed even
plane count. No optimality of this repair and no general shared-core
impossibility are claimed.

The proof is [PROOF.md](PROOF.md); exact finite/source evidence is
[RESULT.json](RESULT.json), reproduced by `node research/bit-selector-lift-price/verify.js`.

The immutable native input and its [notices](inputs/SOURCE-NOTICE) are retained.
All five canonical foundational assumptions remain unchanged.

Independent review and a separate reverse-adjoint/exact-arithmetic verifier
are in [review/REVIEW.md](review/REVIEW.md). The literal caller is frozen at
