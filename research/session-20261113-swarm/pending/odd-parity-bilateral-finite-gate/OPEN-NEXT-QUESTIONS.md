# Remaining global question

The receipts prove feasible bilateral half-unit injections on the odd
parity balls \(R=5,9,11\). They do not prove that every finite subset of the
unbounded odd-parity candidate graph satisfies Hall's condition, and they
do not select a compatible matching across all radii.

The next mathematical obligation is a uniform matching argument for the
countable graph whose vertices are odd-total-parity vectors in \(\mathbb Z^4\)
and whose edges satisfy the exact error-one and bilateral half-unit tests
in PROOF.md. A construction would need to specify one injective global
choice. A compactness argument alone would first require Hall's condition
for every finite input subset, not just full balls of three radii.
