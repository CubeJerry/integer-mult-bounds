## Optimistic complete bill and retained backing

Assume, solely for the local ledger, a common admitted F and fixed finite
inventory K. Let T_env cover all other events and their producers in the
actual interval. Let T_move include every part of the28141 service, not only
its positive child. With fixed graph/adapter constants, a sufficient local
bill has the form

T_local <= C_res K(F+1) + C_init(F+1) + 5*4096(F+1)
           + T_move + C_ret(F+1) + C_route*(8+1)*(K+3)*(F+2).

T_interval additionally includes T_env and its charged routing; alternatively
route all9582 source events and add their separate primitive bills. The
8-contact projection is not a complete physical execution with other events
free. One-time reservation can be skipped only at an already-reserved entry.

The source recipe expands T_move into36 left records, a positive rank-2f
child,44 right records, descriptor setup, all complete-field returns and
retirement. The selected bound for a monomial record retains
C_move*V*((f log p)^tau+1), plus charged descriptor work. All80 is a finite
compile-time count; f,precision and actual child volume vary with the input.
A future direct leaf implementation with frozen f cutoff could instead prove
O_cutoff(F); the recipe alone proves no such literal transition bill. No
header-length, rank2, complete-factor or V/W normalization cost is waived.

For history, retain entry history plus O(K(F+1)), the actual MOVE scratch
increment and the environment's new extents. Bank1526 remains allocated
through its full lifetime, including after its last read; every larger prior
extent and opaque exterior remains in entry history. Retiring its bits never
subtracts that backing. Any variable-size format change has its own scan,
repack and retained-extents bill; it is not absorbed into a fixed constant.
