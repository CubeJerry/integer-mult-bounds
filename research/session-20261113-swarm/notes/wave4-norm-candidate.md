# Unverified wave-4 norm-slack candidate

For rational orthogonal U and a full-rank affine period lattice of an integer-output rounder, propose that maximum error equals supremum outward Euclidean norm increment. An error e on a maximal coset has a scaled direction lambda=c U^T e in the period lattice, giving an unbounded exact ray. This is restricted and does not solve #6619 for arbitrary nonperiodic bijections. Actual #6509 fixed-sum inputs x=1-t-it,y=t+it appear to give norm squares 4t^2-2t+1 and 4t^2+2t+1; proof/source review pending. #6758 has a full-rank native period and sharp sqrt8 error, a potential conditional application.
