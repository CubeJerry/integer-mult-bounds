The construction supplies a reusable way to correct several old receivers
after adding dense unknown masks to three retained packet fields. The masking
uses no additional owned MOVE. It uses six real original records as ascending
carriers, six already-paid raw ground backups to remove their nuisance, and
present raw originals at Hub for their inverses. Every step is in the literal

The all-one member retains three canonical unknown-coordinate projections at

## Quantified source and fanout lemma

Work over any characteristic-two field and any positive whole-record width V.
Let raw donors x_i have independent binary parameters beta_i. Group the donors
according to the old receiver facets in which their native lines lie. For a
nonempty receiver group G, acquire

    u_G = sum_(i assigned to G) beta_i x_i

in a zero-initialized stationary owner at the actual intersection B_G of those
facets. Each contributing original really visits B_G while still raw, performs
its ADD, and continues to Hub. A packet serving receiver a acquires the sum of
u_G over groups containing a at its actual Hub source. Its old retention/reset
captures this extra mask and clears it from every subsequent packet stage.
The retention must be the only changed-packet observer before reset; its only
other readers are the enumerated affected outputs. Those output records must
not be read before correction. Both conditions hold literally here.

If one output can read u_G directly on its ascending route, it does so. If the
needed correction chart is incompatible with the rest of that route, choose
a released original carrier X_c with native line inside B_G and a real raw
backup x_c already available at ground. Move X_c to B_G and add the actual
u_G into it. Then X_c=x_c+u_G moves upward to the affected receiver facet.
Before the output leaves ground, add its actual raw backup x_c to it. At the
facet add the present carrier X_c. These two ADDs supply exactly u_G to the
output, with no initial-y contamination and no assumed raw-value query.

Use distinct carriers for distinct fanout branches. Their masks depend only
on donor originals outside the carrier set, and all donor originals remain
raw. Once a carrier reaches Hub, add exactly its selected donor originals to
it. This is the explicit inverse X_c+=(sum beta_i x_i). The original map is
I+N with N^2=0; its diagonal carrier coefficients remain1. The branches do
not replace two originals by the same bare sum. This avoids a singular
original map and does not count ground backups as Hub information.

Tensoring the scalar identities with I_V proves the whole-record statement.
The physical word changes only by omitting the matching ADDs when beta_i=0.
The MOVE, INIT and CLEAR skeleton is fixed. There are57 independent binary
parameters in this instantiation; it is not a claim of arbitrary scalar
multiplication outside the native ADD model.
