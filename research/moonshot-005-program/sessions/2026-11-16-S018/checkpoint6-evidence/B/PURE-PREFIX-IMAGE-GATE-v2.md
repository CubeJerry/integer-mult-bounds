# A pure first-column prefix cannot provide an independent421 image

**Universal fixed-template criterion; root independently reviewed the v1 mathematical proof.**
This complements the clean-first-two-entry gate by covering some genuinely
nonclean first entries. Its assumptions concern exact Gaussian word classes,
not merely one-column support or the rank of a sampled physical matrix.

Let E=L C U Q L, C=[[s,b,c],[t,d,e],[u,f,g]], and define

    K=(a s a,a t a,0)^T,
    W=(0,s,u)^T,
    Z=(a b a,b a+a d a,f a)^T,
    F=(a c,c+a e,g)^T.

Literal multiplication, without any clean conjugation assumption, gives

    L C U=[K+W a,-K a-W a²+Z,F].                       (1)

Suppose K is pure Gaussian rank2 blockwise, K a is pure rank3, and

    K a [q10 a,q11 a,0] is pure rank4.                 (2)

All coefficients may be noncommuting or singular. C,Q are ordinary units;
there are no individual block-unit assumptions. These conditions can be
verified wordwise in a fixed finite coefficient algebra. They do not assert
that all small-support words are Gaussian.

**Theorem.** E cannot be ordinary-equivalent to independent421 at all
sufficiently large faithful widths for the complete fixed finite catalogue.

Only the term in(2) can contribute rank4, because every other word in E has
at most three A factors. Hence E4 equals the negative of(2), and rank(E4)=D
forces K injective with im(E4)=im(K). The last input column has rank3 part
-K a q12, so q12=0. Its rank2 part is now K q02, with image contained in
im(E4). Directness of the independent rank2/rank4 images forces q02=0.
Q-unit then makes q22 a unit; the ordinary last-column part(0,cq22,gq22)
forces c=g=0. C-unit makes e a unit. The last endpoint column is now
(0,a e q22,0), filling the entire rank1 image with the middle output bank.

If the top block of K had a nonzero kernel, K would send a nonzero vector
into that middle bank. Injectivity of K and directness of the rank4/rank1
images exclude this. Thus a s a and s are units. Q_A is a unit because
Q is block lower triangular with unit q22.

Project to top/bottom output banks. Exactly as in the reviewed inverse-cut
proof, J E=[M,0] with M=D0(a) B U_A Q_A L_A and B=[[s,b],[u,f]] a unit.
The rank4 output column is ordinary, has zero bottom and a unit top block,
because its image is im(K), a graph over the top bank. Selecting that endpoint
column yields P^-1(Jv)G4=Q_A L_A z, with P=D0(a) B U_A. The left word set
has column support at least4 (inverse degree2 before G4); the right is F1.
Faithful tensor separation contradicts nonzero Jv. This completes the proof.
