## The chronological construction

Start with the pinned pure52 D4 source. Choose primary roles a=21,64,65 and
respective lower frames B=(16,18), (23,71,81), (22,68,85). The old first packet
retentions are KEEP_C0/1/2 at M0; the old next captures are TRIAD16/23/22 at
J16/J23/J22. M0 has rank5 and each J has rank4. Put

    C_j = J_t intersect F_b,

where t is the first frame role and b the second. Each C has rank3. The frame
lines are pairwise orthogonal, C is orthogonal to every frame line, and both C
and every frame line lie in F_a. These are checked exact rational inclusions,
not assertions inferred from dimension alone.

All frame originals are immutable in the source. Delete their old donor ADDs
and old routes, thereby projecting their coordinates out of the retained old
circuit. The three mutable originals X21/X64/X65 have exactly two old writes
each. Every old donor read of these originals belongs to the replaced first
packet encoder. Delete those writes and reads together. No other old content
duty uses these mutable fields. Original Y records are not donors in the
predecessor. Remove the old instructions for all replaced receiver roles.

First move each primary Y_a from ground to M0, capture its actual initial
arbitrary y_a in a newly initialized stationary owner there, then move Y_a to
F_a. For each frame use the cyclic interval cache construction above C. A raw
X_i leaves actual stationary copies at C plus its cyclic intervals of lengths
1 through m-1, then visits F_a, adds into Y_a, and continues to Hub. Thus the
primary really holds y_a+q, where q=sum_(i in B)x_i. Move X_a raw to Hub.

At its existing first Hub departure load P with x_a+q and route its rank7 stop
to F_a. Execute Y_a+=P and then P+=Y_a(updated). The primary finishes as
y_a+x_a and the packet becomes y_a+q. The old intermediate rank6 stop still
lies below F_a; the old descent to M0 remains legal. At M0 add the actual
initial-y snapshot into P, leaving exactly q, and clear that snapshot.

The existing KEEP_C capture takes q, but its old resetting ADD is omitted.
The old H/Q/private lower encoder terms are omitted too. Therefore the same
packet carries q along the already-paid M0-to-J descent. At J the existing
TRIAD capture takes q and the existing TRIAD-to-P ADD resets P exactly to0.
All later lower computations agree with the coordinate-projected predecessor.

After all old source work, descend each TRIAD from J to C by one rank. Each
frame Y waits at ground until then, reads q at C, follows the reverse cyclic
interval chain, reads every other raw frame value, and finishes at its native
facet with y_i+x_i. For m=2 or3, every interval read is at a real cache left
before that source original reached Hub. There is no historical-value query.

The source/cell assertions and the projection comparison are algebraic
identities, so the native replay certifies the concrete geometry rather than
substituting a finite scalar experiment for the universal argument.
