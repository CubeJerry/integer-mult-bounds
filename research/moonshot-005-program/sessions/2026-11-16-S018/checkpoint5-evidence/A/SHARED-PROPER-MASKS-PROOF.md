## General immutable-projection and shared-correction lemma

Let a characteristic-two native word have an immutable set D of raw originals
whose coordinates are not demanded by the already-completed endpoints. Delete
every old ADD sourced by one of those originals, and replace their old routes.
Every retained old record then equals the coordinate projection P_D of its old
value, except for the selected originals themselves, which remain actual raw
inputs on their replacement routes. This follows inductively through ADD,
MOVE, INIT and CLEAR; the selected originals have no old destination ADD. Old
completed endpoints remain correct.

Consider an owned packet whose first changing segment ends in a retention R
followed by R-to-packet reset. Require that the retention is the only observer
of the changed packet before its reset, or explicitly enumerate and repair
every additional observer. Suppose R's only other readers are specified output
records, and that no corrected output is read before its correction is complete.
The present source has no Y donor uses at all. Choose a mask made from selected
raw inputs in D. If every
selected native line lies in an actual proper chart B that each affected output
can visit after reading R, stage each input at B, capture its actual raw value
in a zero-initialized stationary owner, and then move the input to Hub. Load
the same mask into the packet at its actual first Hub departure. The retention
now contains P_D(R_old)+mask; its old resetting ADD still clears the packet
exactly. Give each affected Y the same mask from the real B caches. It cancels
the extra retention contribution, while every lower packet computation remains
coordinate-projected. One stationary cache can correct several outputs when
their monotone paths all pass through B. No copy moves upward and no past
record value is queried.

The lemma applies to arbitrary whole-record width, every characteristic-two
field, and every binary mask choice supported by these actual source/correction
charts. It does not assert arbitrary masks outside the common local subspace.
For multiple cache charts on one output route, those charts must form an actual
