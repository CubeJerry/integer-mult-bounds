## General transcript lemma

Let J be r original input roles with pairwise nonorthogonal native lines:
L_i is not contained in F_j for every i,j in J, including i=j. Fix all other
original X and Y inputs. Each free input x_j in J is an independent record
over the same finite alphabet of size q^V. At a specified legal cut, assume
that every live field at chart H is a function only of the inputs x_j whose
native lines are contained in H. For a binary-linear cut this is checked
exactly by its original-column coefficients and actual chart containment.

Consider any continuation with a fixed, data-independent schedule, zero
births, cocharted deterministic arithmetic, and the required monotone upward
routes for original records. Owned records may move up or down through the
legal nested charts. No operation may read an uncocharted record or acquire
input data without a real record. Every field retains the stipulated whole-
record alphabet; changing record widths or encoding data in routing choices
is outside this grammar.

Record the complete value carried by each future owned strict downward MOVE
in a transcript T. After any operation, every field at H is a function of T
and of the inputs x_j with L_j contained in H. Initially this is the stated
cut condition. Cocharted arithmetic and zero birth preserve it. An upward
MOVE only enlarges the local input set. At a downward MOVE, append that whole
field to T; its new value is then a function of T alone at the smaller chart.
CLEAR cannot introduce information. This proves the invariant inductively,
even for nonlinear deterministic arithmetic within the fixed schedule.

Each final Y_j at F_j has no local J input, because all the lines in J are
nonorthogonal to L_j. Its required increment recovers x_j from T. Therefore
the r independent input records are jointly functions of T. If m records
descend, T has at most q^(mV) possible values while the inputs have q^(rV),
so m>=r. In the linear grammar this is equivalently a rank-rV bound, with
each descended field supplying at most V new dimensions. Arbitrary temporary
Y feedback, original borrowing, nonlinear cocharted operations and later
restoration cannot bypass this information count within the stated model.

A useful variant grants rho whole-record input functions to the initial
global transcript. Then m+rho>=r. In a binary-linear cut, any rho-dimensional
space B such that every field row is local modulo B supplies such a grant.
This is a generous sufficient grant for a lower bound, not a free physical
source in a positive construction.
