## Initial-chart transcript theorem

Fix a legal cut and the complete value f_k of every live field there, at its
actual chart H_k. Consider any continuation with a fixed data-independent
schedule, equal finite whole-record alphabet A, zero births, cocharted
deterministic operations, monotone upward original routes and legal nested
owned moves. Grant every future owned strict downward payload globally and
put them, in chronological order, into T. Upward owned moves may also be
allowed as a relaxation. No uncocharted reads, wider records or adaptive
schedule information are granted.

At every point, a field currently at H is a function of T and of the initial
cut values f_k whose INITIAL charts satisfy H_k subset H. This holds at the
cut because the field itself is one of those f_k. Cocharted operations and
zero births preserve it. Upward moves enlarge the available initial set.
For a downward move its payload is appended to T, so at the smaller chart
its new value is known from T alone. CLEAR creates no information. This is
an induction on actual operations, including arbitrary nonlinear arithmetic
and intermediate original-Y feedback. It never treats an old value as a
new physical source in a positive construction: T is only an optimistic
information grant for the lower bound.

Let U be unfinished independent input roles. Fix all other initial X inputs
and all initial Y inputs. For each receiver i in U, let S_i contain every
j in U on which any initial cut field at H_k subset F_i can depend. In a
binary-linear prefix this is the union of actual nonzero X columns of those
fields. Grant all raw x_j, j in S_i, separately; this only helps the receiver.
Its required final increment x_i is therefore a function of T and x_{S_i}.

Choose W subset U whose directed dependency graph i->j for j in S_i cap W
is acyclic, with no self loops. Fix the inputs outside W too. A sink recovers
its x_i from T alone. Remove it and continue in sink-elimination order;
every coordinate previously needed as side information has already been
recovered from T. Thus the entire independent tuple x_W is a function of T.
If m whole fields descend, |A|^m >= |A|^|W|, so m>=|W|. This applies even
to nonlinear continuations. The graph is an optimistic coordinate grant;
it ignores whether all of its charts can be visited on a single legal
receiver chain. Any such incompatibility can only make decoding harder.

This is the retained acyclic side-information lemma applied to the new
initial-chart transcript invariant. It does not require native pairwise
nonorthogonality. The earlier local-input/clique theorem is a special case.
No optimization or maximality claim is needed for an explicit acyclic witness.
