# The full512-control algebra cannot supply this421 interface

**Universal coefficient-algebra theorem, pending independent review.**
This closes the entire current G512 algebra even when the first entries are
nonclean, include sector projectors, and couple all three banks. The proof is
structural; complete finite source-word class checks certify its hypotheses.
It is not a theorem inferred from failed caller samples.

## Actual algebra and exact source facts

Let G be the actual512-element root-four monomial group generated by
Ha,Hb,J,D_a,D_b,CZ_ab,Qc. Here J is the active-square swap, J²=I and
J Ha J=Hb. Let H be its256-element subgroup omitting J. The actual group is
G=H disjoint-union HJ, with no discarded global phase. For every h in H,
a h a^-1 is ordinary. Every g in G has a² g a^-2 ordinary.
Write O_G,O_H for the finite spans of their tensor lifts with actual phases.
The literal class facts, verified on the complete generator lists, are

    A³ G A: every word has rank4,
    A J A² G A:256 words have rank2,256 have rank3,
    A J A has rank2, A J A² has rank3,
    A J A^-1 has rank2 and is nonmonomial.              (1)

All statements concern the fixed finite word catalogue. At a faithful width
V>=V0, distinct projective words are separated with their actual width phases.
Coefficients may depend on the permitted width, and no scalar-sector unit
assumption is made.

## Theorem and scope

Let E=L C U Q L be the literal three-native architecture. C,Q are arbitrary
ordinary units. Assume only

    C00,C10 in O_G,
    q02,q12,q10,q11 in O_G.                            (2)

Every other C,Q block is unrestricted ordinary. Then E cannot be ordinary-
equivalent to independent421 at all sufficiently large faithful widths.
In particular arbitrary3x3 units over O_G at both interfaces are excluded.

## Leading rank4 image and q12

Write s=C00=s0+s1 J and t=C10=t0+t1 J, with s_i,t_i in O_H. Put

    V0=(a s0 a^-1,a t0 a^-1,0)^T,
    V1=(a s1 a^-1,a t1 a^-1,0)^T.

These are ordinary block columns. The first-column prefix is

    K=(a s a,a t a,0)^T=V0 a²+V1 a J a.

K is pure rank2 and K a is pure rank3. All rank4-capable terms of E come
from -K a [q10 a,q11 a,0]. By(1), its J summand has only ranks2,3, while
its other summand is entirely rank4. Hence

    E4=-V0 a³[q10 a,q11 a,0].                          (3)

Rank(E4)=D forces V0 injective and im(E4)=im(V0).

The last input column has rank3 part -(V0 a³+V1 a J a²)q12. It must vanish.
Multiply this equality on the right by a. Its V0 summand is pure rank4 and
its V1 summand has only ranks2,3 by(1). Faithful separation forces
V0 a³q12a=0. Therefore q12=0. This step uses the complete source classes,
not a mistaken assertion that every three-source word stays rank3 after a.

## A source-square coset separation forces q02

The rank2 part of the last input column is now

    V0 a²q02+V1 a J a q02.                             (4)

Because the rank4 and rank2 endpoint images are ordinary and disjoint,
there is an ordinary block row r with r V0=I and r E2=0. For example use
the rank4 row of the ordinary endpoint inverse and normalize its ordinary
unit factor on V0. Algebraic inverses remain in the fixed finite ordinary
algebra; this is not a free physical inverse operation. Applying r to(4)
gives, for an ordinary k=r V1,

    a² q02+k a J a q02=0.                             (5)

The word sets a² G and O(a J a)G are projectively disjoint. Indeed an equality
A² h=lambda K A J A h', with h,h' in G and K ordinary monomial, would imply

    K=lambda^-1 (A² h(h')^-1 A^-2)(A J A^-1).

The first factor is monomial by the square-transport property of G; the
second is nonmonomial rank2 by(1). Their product cannot be monomial. This
contradicts the stipulated K. The actual phase lambda changes no support.
Consequently(5) forces a²q02=0 and hence q02=0. No invertibility of q02
or any sector projector has been assumed.

## Finish by the inverse cut

Q's last column is now(0,0,q22)^T, so q22 is a unit. The ordinary part of
E's last input column is(0,C02 q22,C22 q22)^T; it forces C02=C22=0.
C-unit makes C12 a unit. The last endpoint column is therefore pure rank1
and fills the middle output bank. Since im(E4)=im(V0), injectivity of V0
and directness of the rank1/rank4 images make the top block of V0 a unit.

Thus the rank4 ordinary output column has zero bottom and an invertible top
block. Project E to top/bottom. As in the independently reviewed inverse-cut
proof, Jout E=[M,0], where

    M=diag(a,I) B U_A Q_A L_A,
    B=[[C00,C01],[C20,C21]] a unit, Q_A a unit.

The selected rank4 endpoint column gives P^-1 v G4=Q_A L_A z, with P the
prefix diag(a,I) B U_A, v an injective ordinary column and z ordinary.
The inverse prefix lies in F2; the right side lies in F1. Every left word
has column support at least4 before comparison with right support at most2.
Faithful tensor separation forces v=0, a contradiction. This proves(2).

## Concrete remaining freedom

The admitted complete square-normalizer is much larger than G. An actual
spectator swap S_ab outside this G has

    A³ S_ab A rank2,
    A J A² S_ab A rank4.

A spectator CNOT in the reverse direction has both displayed words rank4.
Thus adding the full six-element GL2(F2) spectator subgroup changes the
rank-selection pattern on the two first-column summands. The uniform
separation in(1) fails, and this theorem does not exclude that enlarged
1536-element algebra. These are exact source-positive escape controls,
not a completed421 endpoint. The next construction must use such selective
rank4 channels, a different ordinary algebra, or another native architecture.

obligations remain. No new kappa. Credit @CubeJerry, retained source, tensor,
