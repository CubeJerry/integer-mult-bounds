# Reinitialize a known-source cut with actual ground preloads

**Working theorem for independent review.** This is a source/continuation reduction, not a completed BIT supplier or a new exponent.

## Exact interface and construction

Over a field k with whole records of width V, consider an actual cut. Every original X_i contains raw independent x_i at a chart H_i containing its native line L_i. Every original Y_i contains y_i+b_i x_S at a chart J_i reached by ascent from ground. Every live owned record Z_a contains c_a x_S at its actual chart C_a. The coordinate set S and linear rows b_i,c_a are fixed. No original y column occurs in an X or owned record.

Let F be a fixed finite legal linear continuation; arbitrary intermediate Y feedback is permitted. It ends with all X at Hub, all Y at their required native facets and all owners retired. In the original execution, X finishes raw and Y has a specified correct diagonal endpoint. Every birth, move, read and retirement is paid. Existence of a complete such continuation is not assumed.

Initialize all cut owners to zero, keep cut X raw, and start cut Y with just its arbitrary y column. Symbolically execute the same F. Let M be its final X-to-X matrix and D_i the difference between its final Y_i increment and the original correct final Y_i increment. Each D_i is supported on S by linearity: all changed cut values were functions of x_S.

If M is invertible, replace the old source prefix as follows:

1. Before any original leaves its native line, initialize B_s at L_s, copy raw X_s into it, and descend B_s to ground, for every s in S.
2. At ground preload Y_i by -D_i x_S using the actual B_s, then explicitly clear the B_s after all reads.
3. Move X_i directly to H_i and Y_i directly to J_i, omitting zero-distance moves. Initialize every old owner at zero at its cut chart C_a.
4. Execute exactly F. Apply M^{-1} to the actual originals now co-located at Hub through a finite sequence of invertible ordinary row operations.

This gives the same original endpoints as the old execution. The final inverse needs no movement. All added ordinary operations, owners, storage and retirements remain charged.

For a fixed binary template, minus equals plus, M^{-1} is chosen over F2, swaps use three ADDs, and the finite word tensors with I_V for all positive widths and characteristic-two fields. Nonbinary scalars need their own implementation and cost. Arbitrary width-dependent block matrices alone do not imply uniformity.

## Proof and restoration boundary

The old cut has y-free X/owners and Y=y+b x_S. Correctness on every arbitrary original y therefore forces the homogeneous final blocks F_XY=0 and F_YY=I. Zeroing known cut offsets does not alter these homogeneous blocks. The zero-state execution differs from the original only by linear functions of x_S. A preload vector -D x_S thus changes final Y by exactly -D x_S and final X by zero, even though intermediate recipes, owners and X may change. The ground preloads cancel all final Y discrepancies, while X still finishes as Mx. The actual Hub inverse restores x without changing any Y or implicitly erasing a side record. Direct public ascents preserve cut co-location and legal direction. Zero births at cut charts are legal; every later geometric event in F is unchanged.

If every F operation writing an X is an invertible elementary operation using only other original X records, the X map is independent of all replaced owners. Since the old execution restores raw X, M=I. This subgrammar needs no final corrective inverse. More generally M must be reconstructed and checked.

The check is essential. At a cut with original x at Hub and owner B=x there, let F create C=x, add C into the original to make zero, add B back, then clear B,C. The original execution restores x; with B zero, F leaves original zero. With no other Hub record carrying x, no deterministic Hub-only operation recovers it. Ground knowledge does not count as Hub side information. This is a singular-M control, not an obstruction to an additional paid repair. The admitted simultaneous-restoration criterion gives the more general rank[M;C_Hub]=n when actual retained pure-X side records remain at Hub. With arbitrary Y feedback, a side record containing y nuisance must instead satisfy the full-column observation criterion; rank of its X projection alone is insufficient (z=0,s=x+y is a counterexample).
