# Reset through an actual homogeneous-Y skeleton

Author theorem extending the reviewed known-source cut reset. A legal
replacement prefix is required; no source values or chart moves are granted.

## Statement and proof

Let P be a legal linear prefix with a fixed named live-record interface,
and F a fixed legal continuation completing all required X and Y endpoints.
Let S consist of X coordinates supplied by actual paid raw ground records.
Suppose a legal replacement prefix Q reaches exactly the same interface
charts and live owner names, and satisfies:

1. Every live record has exactly the same coefficient on every original
   y column as under P, including owners and originals.
2. Every live record has exactly the same coefficient on every x column
   outside S as under P.

Q may differ on x_S. Extra source owners are named separately, never
silently read by F, and really retired. Both prefixes and F may contain
arbitrary Y feedback.

Then FQ ends as X=Mx, Y=y+Dx+Delta x_S, where D is the desired target
map and M-I has columns only in S. If M is invertible, preload
-Delta x_S into initial ground Ys before Q, execute Q and F, then apply
a genuine M^-1 word to originals cocharted at Hub. This restores all
endpoints. A singular M needs a separately proved actual side-data
restoration criterion; none is granted here.

Indeed, let H be the old cut matrix on the independent Y inputs, including
all owners. Write the old/new cut maps as Ax+Hy and A'x+Hy, with A'-A
supported on S. For the continuation endpoint map T, correctness of FP
for arbitrary y implies T_X H=0 and T_Y H=I. Thus T(A'-A)x has support
only on x_S and no y residue reaches final X. Replacing initial y by
y-Delta x_S propagates the preload through the same H to every owned
copy and feedback-dependent row. Its final X effect is zero and final Y
effect is exactly-Delta x_S. The actual Hub inverse finishes the proof.

The preload must precede Q. Adding it only to cut Ys after building
Y-bearing owned fields need not follow H, so the two transfer identities
would not apply. A physical copy of y must carry the same preloaded change
as its source. The executable control tests this timing separately.

Any actual known ground row space G may replace coordinate S if every row
of A'-A lies in G and the corrections are synthesized from real ground
records. The raw-S form simply has a concrete acquisition invoice.

## Predecessors and scope

Root's cut reset has homogeneous H supported only on original Y rows;
its replacement can initialize all owners as zero. C's ground-output
normalization gives the same global correction principle through the final
Y transfer. The new constructive requirement here is a legal skeleton
reproducing y-bearing owned fields at the cut. The claim is not that every
cut signature has a cheap skeleton.

The formal all222-column identity is proved here and checked in the finite
controls. The global native_all_columns foundation remains open, together
with owned_erasure, compiler, precision_tape, and analytic_recovery. Every
copy, move, birth and dirty retirement is paid.
No all111 construction or kappa claim.
