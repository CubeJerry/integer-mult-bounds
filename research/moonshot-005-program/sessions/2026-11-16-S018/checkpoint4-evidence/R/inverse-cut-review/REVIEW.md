The general lemma concerns an invertible factorization M=P W. Each block of
P inverse is a finite sum of tensor lifts of unitary words whose inverse
column supports are at most 2^s. Each block of W has word support at most
2^t. These statements refer to the individual word catalogue, not arbitrary
numerical sums. If an independent Gaussian job G of rank r is ordinary
encoded at the endpoint, selecting its input column gives
P inverse O e_j G=W R inverse e_j. For every individual left word T H G,
v=(T H) inverse (T H v) and inverse sparsity imply support(T H v)>=2^(r-s)
in every column. When r>s+t this catalogue is projectively disjoint from
the right catalogue. Finite tensor separation, with actual scalar phases,
forces both sides zero. The left block column is injective, a contradiction.
The finite catalogue includes all ordinary inverse and endpoint recipes.
Its width threshold is essential; no isolated width-one rank test suffices.
