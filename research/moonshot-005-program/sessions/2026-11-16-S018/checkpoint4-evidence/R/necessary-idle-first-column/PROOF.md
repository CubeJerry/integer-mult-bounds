## A one-sided stripping lemma

For any ordinary r,h, the equality

    pi1(a r a) + a h = 0

forces h=0 (and the projected term zero). Expand r into its fixed literal
monomial tensor words. The projection selects precisely the terms whose
one-column words A R A belong to the rank1 Gaussian class, grouping exact
projective coincidences with their retained phases. Left multiplication by
a^-1 sends each selected term to R^tensor V a, a pure rank1 word. It sends
a h to ordinary h. Faithful separation of ordinary and rank1 classes gives
h=0. The catalogue includes these stripped words. This does not bound the
