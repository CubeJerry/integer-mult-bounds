## 1. Conditional-rotation first interface, arbitrary second interface

B's third-column theorem concerns

    Crot=[[T,0,-N],[0,I,0],[N,0,T]], N=I-T,

where T is an ordinary self-adjoint projector with ordinary first/second
backward transports T1,T2. The exact word is L Crot U Q L with arbitrary
ordinary unit Q. The source and every endpoint encoder, decoder and inverse
belong to a fixed finite collective root-four catalogue, enlarged by all used
products. At sufficiently large faithful widths divisible by four, assume
regular trace(N1)+regular trace(N2)=1 but the augmentation rank of their
two-bank diagonal projector is not one.

Writing the last column of Q as (p,z,w), direct multiplication gives

    (a²T1p-a³T2z-aNw,
     aT1p+a²N2z-Nw,
     aN1p-a²N2z+Tw).

Independent (4,2,1) endpoints exclude ordinary and cubic source classes, hence
w=0 and z=N2z. The last-column quadratic part is then
(a²T1p,a²z,-a²z). A formal source-path census confirms that the full quartic
component is confined to the first output bank: Crot10=0 makes every lower
row contain at most three source factors. An independent invertible quartic
job has rank D, so its image fills that bank. Endpoint independence therefore
forces ker(z) subset ker(T1p).

Consequently (N1p,z) is injective: a vector in its kernel also has T1p=0,
then p=0, and the injective Q column makes that vector zero. At a faithful
width this ordinary column has a left inverse in the finite group algebra.
Indeed its positive Gram matrix is invertible in every represented irreducible
block, and faithfulness includes every block. This is an algebraic necessity,
not an uncharged physical inverse. It splits a free bank inside diag(N1,N2),
contradicting the regular-dimension equality/augmentation theorem.

The argument correctly covers both spectator signs and the collective C4
half-projector. It does not cover C_T: that interface's third source column
is (0,a,0), so setting w=0 there would be false. It does not cover the Klein
or sequential first interfaces. This corrected scope is essential.

## 2. The transferred Klein column and independent endpoints

For the original Klein C, put U=a², P'=(I+a t a^-1)/2, M'=I-P', and

    e=(I+s1)(I-t1)/4.

Suppose Q's last column satisfies pe=e and ze=we=0. The published transferred
column does: its N0 has the minus-s1 factor. The other columns are unrestricted.
The actual quadratic last-column restriction is (Ue,0,0). The quartic decoder
row (b0,b1,b2) of a genuine endpoint inverse must annihilate it, so b0Ue=0.
The independent literal check verifies

    Ue=(M'U+M'Us1)/2,
    Ut1U^-1=a t a^-1 ordinary,
    Us1U^-1 nonordinary.

The sets of one-column words G U and G U s1 are therefore disjoint even
projectively. Faithful tensor separation, with the full fixed catalogue of
b0M' included, forces b0M'=0. Thus b0=b0P'. This is an ordinary annihilator
statement valid at the faithful widths; it is not a finite-dimensional
subspace argument at width one.

The independent source-path census also confirms that every quartic term
uses the first column of Klein C. Its second output component is fixed by P'
and its third is zero. For an actual endpoint factorization
Efull=O diag(F4,F2,F1) Iin, the row F4 Iin[0,:] is surjective. Therefore the
quartic encoder column c=O[:,0] obeys c2=0 and P'c1=c1. The actual inverse

    Cfree=(P'c0,c1), Dfree=(b0,b1)

is a split free-bank embedding in diag(P',P'). Its regular trace is one and

This proves the claimed entire partial-column family, not only Qhighlow.
The direct D/4 image overlap for Qhighlow is a valid weaker control.

The conclusion also survives an arbitrary ordinary right change of basis on

## 4. Independent review of the coupled-column criterion

C's earlier `klein-coupled-column` criterion uses the original Klein C and
the same fixed finite catalogue and faithful tensor-separation scope. The
absence of classes zero and three in the final column implies w=0 and
z=R2 z. Augmentation of an invertible Q then gives epsilon(p) nonzero and
forces the quadratic output line (1,1,0). These are necessary conditions
on an actual endpoint factorization, not just on a proposed component rank.

Write U=A^2, H={h:U h U^-1 is ordinary}, J=<H,s1>, and let P_H be subgroup
averaging in the finite group algebra. Passing the quadratic double coset
through the left augmentation quotient retains the right H-coset. Its first
two output rows must agree. Since t1,s2,t2 belong to H and z=R2 z, their
difference is proportional to D0 p, where D0=P_H(s1-I). Hence D0 p=0.
For the orthogonal right-support projector N of D0 this is equivalent to
Np=0. The actual inverse row of Q splits its last column inside
diag(I-N,R2), so every irreducible block must satisfy rank(N)<=rank(R2).

The identity D0 D0*=2P_H-2P_H s1 P_H shows directly that rank(N) in each
representation is dim(H-fixed)-dim(J-fixed): equality in the compression
of the self-adjoint involution s1 forces s1 to fix the vector. The literal
ordinary transport of s1 t2 s1 gives P_H s1(I-t2)=0. Commutativity of s2,t2
then gives D0 R2=0, and therefore N R2=0. Orthogonality alone does not
compare their multiplicities. The source-specific return identity
A(Rsrc^-1 Xb)A=Rsrc is checked independently with its exact phases.

The subsequent transferred-kernel construction resolves the module
comparison positively. Its h belongs to H and commutes with s1, so
D0 N0=D0 for N0=(I-s1)(I+h)/4. Thus N<=N0. The literal signed ordinary G
conjugates N0 to R2, proving rank(N)<=rank(R2) in every irreducible block.
The checker independently verifies both G conjugacies and the ordinary
transport of h; B separately replays the complete dyadic transfer words.
The positive module fact and the later endpoint exclusion coexist: the
former solves only this one necessary column condition.
