# Independent review of the dyadic Klein transfer

The positive interface is correct. I reconstructed the literal source,
conditional-Y involution h, signed monomial G and both backward spectator
transports using B's separately pinned exact Gaussian-dyadic engine. I did
not import C's checker or its Q(i) arithmetic. The phase-sensitive
conjugacies, quarter-projector transfer, partial isometry, complete ordinary
words and actual inverses all pass on all three arbitrary inputs. The four
receipts agree exactly with C's counts.

The replay's smaller exact width-one prefix magnitudes are labelled only as
finite controls. C's coefficient-triangle bounds remain the uniform bounds;
one-column cancellations are not promoted to all-width precision claims.

The universal coset argument is also sound: h belongs to the monomial
intersection group H, commutes with s1, and therefore P_H(s1-I)N0=P_H(s1-I).
Conjugation by the explicit G sends N0 to R2. This resolves the old
representation comparison constructively, without an unimplemented spectral
inverse or a projector-to-bank identification.

This review adds the independently derived endpoint theorem in
ENDPOINT-GATE.md. Every completion retaining the transferred last column
acts identically on the specified plus/minus sector. The resulting quadratic
component forces the actual ordinary quartic decoder into two same-plus
channels, contradicting the admitted capacity theorem. Thus the positive
transfer is an exact interface but its entire fixed-column completion family
cannot supply independent (4,2,1) endpoints. This conclusion does not cover
changed last columns or the sequential first completion.

The review also retains a concrete width-one trap: N0 and the first kernel
R1 coincide at width one but differ at width two. The exact all-width
quadratic last-column defect is (16^V-2*8^V)/8, so the apparent full rank at
width one is not a uniform property. The source trace calculation, rather
than a finite rank extrapolation, proves this formula.

Negative controls remove h's phases or G's two signs, omit the first and
last ADD from each word, and reject the width-one kernel substitution.
The primitive replay is verification of the supplied ordinary maps. It is
not a complete supplier, precision, ownership, compiler or recursive audit.
All five foundational assumptions remain. No new complete kappa is claimed.
