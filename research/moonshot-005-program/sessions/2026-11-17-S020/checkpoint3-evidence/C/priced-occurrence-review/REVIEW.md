# Independent review: price every future original-X facet visit

The proposed priced occurrence lemma is valid, conditional on the retained
normalized fitting/occurrence theorem and the audited frozen cuts. It is a
stronger physical consequence of that theorem, not a new normalization proof.
It applies to every characteristic-two field and whole-record block width to
which the retained theorem applies. It does not assume a scalar fitting code.

## Hypotheses

At the frozen cut, no live field occupies a native rank-seven facet. Every
public X original lies below Hub at a chart of rank r_j<7, and ends unchanged
at Hub. Every public Y has its specified native facet as final chart. Public
routes only ascend; owned routes may make arbitrary nested turns. All births,
reads, arbitrary original-Y coefficients, final restoration and charges remain
subject to the admitted model. Let n count these X originals. Let Q0 be the
already executed prefix cost plus the direct remaining public ascents, with
no additional owned movement. Let q count future strictly positive owned MOVE
occurrences, rather than owners or lifetime count.

For the two reviewed cuts n=111. The source reviews establish the required
absence of initial facet fields and the exact public ranks. Earlier partial
receiver corrections do not create an extra facet-traffic allowance: those
receivers are still at proper rank-three charts.

## Proof

Write a_j=1 if original X_j visits a native facet in the continuation, and
a_j=0 otherwise. Distinct native facets have the same rank seven and are
incomparable, so each ascending X can visit at most one. Every Y has only its
own facet as a possible rank-seven visit, which supplies its usual anchor.
Owned upward facet arrivals and downward facet departures inject into the q
positive owned MOVEs: a single nested MOVE cannot perform both, nor connect
distinct rank-seven facets. The retained occurrence theorem therefore gives

    chi <= sum_j a_j + q.

Fix 0<t<1. If X_j visits rank seven, subadditivity along the portions before
and after that visit gives total remaining cost at least
(7-r_j)^t+1. Its direct ascent already charged in Q0 costs (8-r_j)^t. Thus

    delta_j = (7-r_j)^t + 1 - (8-r_j)^t

is a compulsory extra cost when a_j=1. Strict concavity gives delta_j>0;
strict increase of x^t gives delta_j<1. Non-visiting originals and all Y
originals cost at least their direct remaining ascents. Every positive owned
MOVE costs at least one. Consequently

    Q >= Q0 + q + sum_j a_j delta_j
      >= Q0 + chi - sum_j a_j(1-delta_j)
      >= Q0 + chi - n + sum_j delta_j.

The last inequality uses a_j<=1 and 1-delta_j>0. It does not assert that all
n facet visits occur; it grants all of them when minimizing this lower bound.
If strict supplier admission requires Q<B_infinity(t), then necessarily

    chi < n + B_infinity(t) - Q0 - sum_j delta_j.

All inequalities count physical whole-record moves. Tensoring field values
or allowing arbitrary block coefficients does not alter their rank gaps or
the occurrence injection. The retained fitting charge supplies the same
integer chi for nonzero foreign blocks.

## Target-interval extension

For the additional premise chi>=189, the compulsory profiles simplify to

    restored: 380[1]+127[2]+44[3]+103[4]+71[6]+4[7],
    retained: 364[1]+111[2]+12[3]+119[4]+16[5]+71[6]+4[7].

These follow by replacing every remaining direct X ascent with its rank-seven
split, then adding 189-111 unit moves. All coefficients are nonnegative and
every gap is at most seven. Hence their value divided by 7^t is nonincreasing
for t>=0. The exact retained ceiling is

    6 B_inf(t)/7^t = 37(127/7)^t + 74(64/7)^t - 74(1/7)^t
                    + 74 sum_{j=1}^8 ((j+1/7)^t-j^t).

This expression is strictly increasing for t>=0: its first three derivatives
have positive sign, and x^t log x is strictly increasing in x>=1 for every
t>=0, so every displayed difference has positive derivative. Thus the strict
failure at t=199/200 extends down to zero. At t=0 one may either use continuity
or allow delta_j=1 in the weak inequality; only delta_j<=1 was needed for the
price lower bound. Any negative finite-caller correction strengthens failure.
This independently validates root's target-interval corollary, still conditional
on its separate exact chi>=189 algebraic premise.

## Scope and controls

Public downward turns, initial facet fields, changed source cuts, or an
unproved normalized-code premise require a different occurrence application.
Owned upward moves are already included; charging only owned descents would
be insufficient. At t=1 every delta is zero, which recovers the unpriced
linear tariff and shows why strict concavity is used. For t=199/200 the
checker rejects both the zero-delta shortcut and a claimed cap186.
