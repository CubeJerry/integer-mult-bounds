## Exhaustive minimization using the disjoint half supports

Enumerate all 512 canonical words c. Every binary row in G has a representation

    a=c+sum_l lambda_l h_l.

Let B_i be the complement of N_i, including i. Outside the half supports, c must
agree with e_i on B_i. Within one half put J=H_l intersect B_i. If J is nonempty,
the desired correction (c+e_i)|J must be either zero or the entire indicator of
J. A mixed pattern rejects this c. In the valid cases lambda_l is forced to
zero or one. If J is empty, the half is entirely in N_i and lambda_l is optional.
Its optimal contribution to foreign weight is the smaller of

    |support(c) intersect H_l| and |H_l|-|support(c) intersect H_l|.

The halves are disjoint, so optional choices are independent. Taking the minimum
over all 512 canonical words therefore gives the exact minimum foreign support
for each receiver without enumerating 2^28 masks. Repeated representations from
C intersect D do not affect a minimum. This is a complete finite minimization,
not a sampled or numerical search.

The certificate supplies an actual minimizing row and its canonical/half

above 52. Consequently the minimum total capped charge is exactly 96. Choosing
