## Scope cautions

The theorem does not price an arbitrary coded export as one coordinate. A field
born at a proper chart may contain many native coordinates and acquire changing
histories during several moves. Such a continuation need not have its normalized
history contained in C + E_S for a small S, and is outside this gate. Corrections
performed before the 64 source pivots depart, changed prefixes, public turns,
nonlinear operations and different callers likewise require their own arguments.

The retained half sums and mixed low originals of the hierarchical word do not
invalidate the facet accounting: they remain local information until actually
exported downward. An export of a general sum can invalidate the small-S premise,
which is exactly the remaining coded-payload opportunity.
