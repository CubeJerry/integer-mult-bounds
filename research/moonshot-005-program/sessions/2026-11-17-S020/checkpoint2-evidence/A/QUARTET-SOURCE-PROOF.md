# Full canonical acquisition with local source compression and proper retained fields

**Actual partial source constructions; independent review pending. No new κ.**

The known57 source has a cheap way to obtain all unknown information at Hub,
but its restricted canonical code has a severe delivery cost. The present
construction changes that source contract: no coordinate is first purchased
at ground. It obtains the full111-variable rank9 canonical bank while
retaining original carriers below Hub. A refinement also leaves28 actual
proper-chart sums. These are alternatives to the known57 source, not free
adapters to it; neither completes a receiver by itself.

## 3. Two-stage refinement with proper half sums

For each quartet support choose its first two coordinate indices a,b.
Split its actual source roles into the two classes r_a=+r_b and r_a=−r_b.
On this pinned source every half has three or four roles, spans a rank3
chart, and its local canonical restriction has rank3. These are the
actual pair-equality hyperplanes inside the quartet, not the old sign-parity
halfclasses, which have different geometric spans.

At each half chart, first capture the full actual half sum in a stationary
owned field. Then apply the local rank3 triangular encoding. Three encoded
originals ascend to the rank4 quartet chart; a fourth, when present, stays
raw at the rank3 half chart. Across all28 halves, this gives84 encoded
arrivals at the14 quartet charts and19 raw originals held at rank3.

At each quartet chart, the six incoming encoded forms are independent.
The desired local canonical projection has rank4 inside their six-dimensional
span. Apply a second normalized triangular change of these actual six
original records. Four ascend to Hub, and two remain at the rank4 chart.
The28 retained rank4 originals generally carry mixed local forms; they
are not called raw. The same56 Hub originals and eight coordinate originals
again supply the full canonical bank, which the emitted variant actually
descends to ground.

This is a two-stage source hierarchy, with actual lower-source fields:

* 111 raw stationary native caches;
* 28 actual half sums at their rank3 charts;
* 19 raw original inputs held at rank3;
* 28 mixed originals held at rank4;
* 64 original records at Hub, supplying the9-dimensional canonical bank;

All111 arbitrary Y originals are still at ground. No source side
information has been silently installed on them.

To close, move every held original to Hub. Reverse the upper encodings
first, thereby restoring the actual lower-stage forms. Only then reverse
the lower encodings using the raw held originals. This restores every
original coordinate. Reversing in the wrong order is not an available
inverse and is not the emitted word.

## 4. Why this is a useful next interface, and what is still unproved

For a receiver whose normal projects onto a quartet as a multiple of
e_a±e_b, the corresponding actual half chart lies inside that receiver's
facet. This makes the captured half sum a real proper source for a
chronological decoder or for a newly priced mutable descending packet.
No native-line copy has been declared available at this higher chart.
The actual source charts and full coefficient rows are frozen in the cut
inventory for an independent route/cut audit.

Separate containment does not produce simultaneous receiver routes.
A receiver cannot visit two incomparable rank3 charts along one ascent.
The19 raw holders and28 mixed originals likewise have one actual upward
route each. A complete continuation must solve those conflicts, restore
every borrowed original, retain all Y columns, charge every further owner
and movement, and then satisfy the stricter finite-multiplicity and
ordinary/compiler/precision/recursive obligations. None is established by
the positive movement room.

The next concrete attempt is a coupled local correction gadget on one
eight-sign quartet: acquire its pair-equality query sums while source and
receiver couriers still have useful lower-chart routes, and then compose
those stages before all source pivots depart. A successful gadget must be
an actual operation word, not a list of separately available rows.
