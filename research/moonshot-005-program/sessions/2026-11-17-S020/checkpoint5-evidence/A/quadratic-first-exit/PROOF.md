## Quadratic noncover lemma: all real charts

quadratic evaluation matrix has rank27, and the unique nonzero quadratic
vanisher (up to scale) has symmetric matrix rank at least three. Then D is
not covered by two proper subspaces of V.

Indeed, enlarge an alleged two-subspace cover to hyperplanes with nonzero
linear equations a and b. Their product ab is a nonzero homogeneous quadratic
vanishing on D. It must be proportional to the unique certified vanisher.
But its symmetric matrix is (ab^T+ba^T)/2, of rank at most two: contradiction.
This allows arbitrary real hyperplanes and arbitrary strict destination
charts, not merely an enumerated native or rational chart library.
