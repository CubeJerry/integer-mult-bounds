## 2. Relation to the join export

An alternative sends the collector H to H+Q and then exports from rank5 to Q
at rank3. Both transfers have downward gap2 by the dimension formula. The meet
word makes the same-gap descent directly from H to I and avoids the collector's
intermediate rank5 stop.

The source contracts differ. The join word can load an existing original
carrier already at Q. The meet word does not perform that load and instead
retains a lower owned pure packet. Its later transport, carrier loading and
retirement must be paid if needed. No general dominance is claimed.
