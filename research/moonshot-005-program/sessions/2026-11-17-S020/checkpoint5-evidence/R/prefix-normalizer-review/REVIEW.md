## 1. Algebra and chronological order

For u!=v let P=lambda E_uv and Q=P_v. Then PQ=P, QP=0 and P^2=0. Multiplying
the four block shears independently gives

    E01(P) E10(Q) E01(-P) E10(-Q)=diag(I+P,I).

The chronological word is the reverse order of these factors. No zero-bank
hypothesis occurs: the lower block is exactly I, and both off-diagonal blocks
are zero. The literal expressions

    P=lambda*(X+(-1)^u ZX)/2, Q=(I+(-1)^v Z)/2

follow by evaluating the distinguished bit. In ZX the Z phase is evaluated
after the flip; the independent sparse table preserves this ordering. Each
coefficient shear temporarily changes its source bank, performs an ADD, then
undoes its scale and monomial. All required scalars and reciprocals are nonzero
Gaussian dyadics. Two uses of each two-term coefficient give8 ADD,16 SCALE,
12 MONO per within-bank elementary update.

Let c=(1+i)/2,d=(1-i)/2. Direct2-by2 multiplication gives

    [[1,-1],[0,1]] [[1,0],[d,1]] [[1,-i],[0,1]] diag(1,i)
       =[[c,d],[d,c]]=C_b.

Thus the source prefix p=A on the first column is implemented in chronological
order by R,D_b,U01(-i),U10(d),U01(-1). Inverting the elementary updates and
reversing the order gives p^-1. Both complete words use24 ADD,48 SCALE,38 MONO.
They map two arbitrary input banks to (p*x,y) or(p^-1*x,y) exactly. The third
bank of an eventual native call can serve as this restored arbitrary borrower;
it is not consumed or treated as zero workspace.
