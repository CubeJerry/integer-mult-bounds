The literal product is

    Cprime=diag(I,J,I) Rot02(T) Rot12(R) Rot01(S).

The active first-column Gram equals As P+Bs U+(1/2)Bs V. Its three terms
have orthogonal ranges. Multiplication by As P+Bs U+2Bs V gives exactly
I-As N in both orders. Thus its kernel is precisely As N, of dimensionD/4,
not merely a kernel found in one finite computation. The corresponding

(-a c00 a^2 z,-a c10 a^2 z,0). Hence its literal vanishing is equivalent

of separating these classes in an endpoint problem retains the fixed finite

The one-tail heads AJA and AJSA are exact monomial transports of A^2; AJA^2
and AJSA^2 are transports of A^3. The independent checker proves these
