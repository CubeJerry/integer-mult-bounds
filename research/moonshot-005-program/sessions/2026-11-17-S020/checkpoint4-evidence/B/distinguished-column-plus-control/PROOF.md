# A distinguished-column ordinary unit that escapes the same-plus obstruction

**Positive interface in an explicitly enlarged ordinary template. No complete
caller, supplier, or new kappa. The fixed collective-catalogue theorems remain
correct within their stated hypotheses.**

## 1. Actual source and uniform operation

Retain the literal source A=C_b D_(acd) P and the ordinary involution
h=A (X_c X_d) A^-1. Its one-column monomial flips bit b (and c); its extra
root-four phase is retained. Therefore j=Z_b satisfies

    j h=-h j,   j^2=h^2=I.

At every positive tensor width V, define

    H_V=h^(tensor V),
    J_V=j tensor I_16^(tensor(V-1)),
    P_V=(I+H_V)/2, M_V=(I-H_V)/2.

J_V is the monomial phase (-1)^(b in the first four-bit column). It has a
fixed width-uniform address rule and needs one charged full-field MONO pass.
It does not act on every column. Thus

    J_V H_V=-H_V J_V,
    P_V J_V=J_V M_V,
    M_V J_V=J_V P_V.                                   (1)

The distinguished position is fixed before V varies. No list of V unrelated
coefficient words is introduced. This changes the representation model from
pure collective tensor powers g^(tensor V) to the fixed two-part templates
h0 tensor g^(tensor(V-1)). The change is explicit and needs separate admission
in the complete ordinary/physical compiler contract; no earlier collective
hypothesis is silently enlarged.

## 2. An actual unit and free-bank encoder

On two arbitrary banks use

    U=[[P_V,M_V],[J_V M_V,J_V P_V]],
    U^-1=[[P_V,M_V J_V],[M_V,P_V J_V]].                 (2)

Equation (2) is an exact inverse identity at every V. It follows by factoring
U=diag(I,J_V) S, where

    S=[[P_V,M_V],[M_V,P_V]],   S^2=I.

The first encoder column and inverse row are

    C=(P_V,J_V M_V)^T,
    D=(P_V,M_V J_V).

They satisfy

    diag(P_V,P_V) C=C,   DC=I.                         (3)

Hence one entire free ordinary bank is embedded in two same-plus ranges,
with a genuine two-bank completion and inverse, in this enlarged template.
The input and both output banks remain arbitrary.

This does not contradict the collective capacity theorem. Its group-algebra
augmentation sends each collective monomial to1; that is not an algebra map
on the distinguished-column extension satisfying the anticommutation (1).
Equivalently, after separating the first column, augmentation on the remaining
collective group leaves a 16-dimensional prefix representation. Here P_V has
prefix rank8, so the two plus sectors have augmented rank16, exactly matching
one free prefix bank. The former saturated-rank mismatch disappears.

## 3. Literal primitive word and complete local invoice

Let Eij(c) add c times bank j into bank i, restoring the arbitrary source
bank after each coefficient term. In chronological order use

    E10(M_V), E01(-M_V), E10(M_V),
    MONO H_V on bank0, MONO J_V on bank1.              (4)

The first three shears form [[P,-M],[M,P]]. The H_V operation changes its
first row to [P,M], and the last operation gives (2). Reverse (4), negate
each shear coefficient, and invert each monomial for U^-1.

Expand each +/-M_V into its two terms +/-I/2 and -/+H_V/2. For each term,
apply its monomial to the source, scale, ADD to the target, undo the scale,
and undo the monomial. Identity monomials use no MONO. Both directions use

    6 ADD +12 SCALE +8 MONO.

All scalar factors are nonzero dyadics. The maximum universal l1 prefix
bound is5: the three coefficient shears successively bound bank magnitudes
by2,3,5, and both monomials preserve magnitude. Temporary source scales have
absolute value1/2 and are restored exactly after the ADD. The inverse has
the same bound. There is no Gaussian query, new owner, dirty erasure, zero
bank, or hidden source restoration in this local word.

The full supplier still needs its body, precision, native, compiler and
recursive invoice. This local price is not a claim that the target margin
survives a future caller.

## 4. Scope and next constructive use

The collective replacement j^(tensor V) is not interchangeable with J_V.
It commutes with H_V at every even V, and fails (3) there. The checker includes
that width-two negative control, along with exact words, inverses and every
ADD omission at widths1,2. The all-width proof is (1)-(4), not the finite
checks.

The old transferred-column endpoint obstruction ends by requiring the split
(3) with P_V=(I+h^(tensor V))/2. This explicit ordinary unit removes that final
capacity contradiction in the distinguished-column model. It does not yet
supply a compatible second interface or cancel every Gaussian class. A useful
next target is an actual endpoint construction using (2), rather than an
isolated rank claim. All other source and endpoint conditions must be proved
again in the enlarged representation.
