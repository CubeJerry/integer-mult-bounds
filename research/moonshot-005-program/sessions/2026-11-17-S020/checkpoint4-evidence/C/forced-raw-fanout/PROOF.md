# Fixed query-bank continuations need seventy-nine more owned moves

**Source-specific closure, with an explicit constructive escape.** The audited
low-retention source cannot complete using only future descending X histories
in C plus its full query-row span. The audited proper-query-export source
cannot complete using only future descending X histories in C plus its sign-
query span. This holds for monotone public routes, arbitrary paid nested owned
routes, every characteristic-two field and whole-record width, and the retained
caller target interval. It does not exclude new descending code directions,
earlier source changes or public downward turns.

## 2. One native-line exit cannot serve all forced facets

Fix a dormant source j. Let D_j be any set of receivers for which the duals
above force j. Suppose only one effective upward departure from L_j occurs.
It enters a strict superchart P of L_j. Every later branch of its nonzero
quotient information lies at a chart containing P: upward movement and cochart
copying preserve containment, while a downward payload in G has zero quotient
information. Thus every forced receiver supplied by that one departure must
have P contained in its final facet.

This conclusion can also be expressed without tracking an individual coded
component. Apply a receiver's dual to every X coefficient row. All initial
observations inside its facet, all other eligible raw units and every descending
payload then have scalar value zero. A nonzero final value must come from the
native j line through an upward path entering that facet. A departure whose
first superchart is not contained in the facet cannot supply such a path.
Subsequent cancellation or mixing with global G does not change this zero-state
argument.

The checker supplies exact real normal ranks and corresponding binary duals.
For 39 of the forty source coordinates, seven independent forced-receiver
normals span the seven-dimensional orthogonal complement of L_j. Hence

    intersection_{i in D_j} F_i = L_j.

No strict superchart P can lie in all those facets. Each of these 39 coordinates
therefore needs at least two upward departure occurrences from its native line.
The remaining coordinate j=3 has demand-normal rank six; its forced demand
requires at least one departure. We make no claim that these lower numbers
are sufficient for a physical multicast. They imply

    total native-line upward departures >= 39*2+1 = 79.       (2)

Every selected forced-receiver role has its own explicit dual, so the normal-
rank argument does not depend merely on an unverified list of demands. For each
rank-seven case the exact nullspace is checked equal to the actual native line.

An owned departure can supply a raw coordinate only from its actual source
line in this argument. If a different native coordinate could replace j inside
a required facet using G, the dual forcing j would annihilate that replacement
and could not certify j. Distinct forced source lines therefore require distinct
departure occurrences even when an owner is later reused.

Only the four still-ground public Ys can perform such departures without an
owned upward move. A monotone public original can visit at most one distinct
native line; no original X remains at a dormant line. At most four of the 79
departures can be public. Thus

    future owned upward MOVE occurrences >= 75.               (3)

## 3. Independent review of the four-facet fresh-information cut

A supplies a separate useful information cut, independently replayed here.
Take U={8,9,14,15}. Every pair of these native roots, including the diagonal,
has nonzero real dot product. Let the chart region R consist of all charts
contained in at least one of their four final facets. This region is downward
closed under chart inclusion.

At the two actual cuts the region contains respectively 183 and 190 fields.
Every one has zero coefficients on the four independent input columns indexed
by U. The only fields carrying those columns are their four raw originals and
four native snapshots. All eight lie outside R because the selected native
lines are contained in none of the selected facets. These statements are checked
against the full independently replayed field inventory, including all auxiliary
Y-baseline, axis-history and query-export fields.

Grant all other X inputs and all initial Y inputs as side information. The
initial region has zero information about the four selected input records,
whereas the required four final outputs inside it have selected-input rank 4m
at width m. An upward MOVE cannot enter a downward-closed region from outside.
Cochart operations and zero births cannot import outside information. Each
downward whole-record transfer into the region contributes rank at most m.
At least four future downward transfers are therefore required. Public records
only ascend, so these transfers are owned:

    future owned downward MOVE occurrences >= 4.              (4)

This cut does not restrict the descending code bank; the fixed-bank premise is
needed for (3). The freely granted G in the preceding necessary decoder test
does not create physical fields in this fresh cut. Its actual initial inventory
still has the verified zero coefficients.

## 4. The complete movement budget contradicts the two obligations

The 75 upward and four downward occurrences are disjoint. Every positive owned
MOVE has cost at least one for nonnegative tariff exponent. Let Q0 denote the
executed source plus its actual direct remaining public closure. Monotone public
subdivision cannot lower those closing costs when 0<=t<=1. Therefore

    Q_complete >= Q0 + 79.                                  (5)

At t=199/200 the favorable necessary limiting rooms are

    low retention:   78.2322504609965249752552408862540195...,
    proper exports:  66.2322504609965249752552408862540195....

Exact rational enclosure of (5) exceeds the retained limiting ceiling by

    low retention:    0.7677495390034750247447591137459804...,
    proper exports:  12.7677495390034750247447591137459804....

No fitting-charge minimum or activation/facet double counting is needed for
these contradictions. The lower profiles are the respective full closed source
profiles with 79 additional unit moves. They have nonnegative coefficients and
all gaps at most seven. Dividing by 7^t makes each lower profile nonincreasing
in t. The retained B_inf(t)/7^t is increasing on 0<=t<=199/200, as shown directly
in the priced-occurrence review, and finite even-caller corrections are negative.
The strict endpoint failure therefore persists throughout that target interval.

The proof is uniform in fields and widths: all forced-coordinate duals are
binary and preserve identity blocks; geometric ranks are real; the information
cut has rank 4m and each record supplies at most m. Unknown-Y equations were
only relaxed favorably, never set to zero in an actual source operation.

## 5. Reproduction, scope and constructive direction

Run check.py with --native, --inputs, --forced, --source-review, --arithmetic
and --out. The prior forced certificate supplies candidate demands, but the
checker independently rebuilds every selected dual from the actual replayed
observations. Source and arithmetic dependencies are pinned independent review

The result closes two specific history banks at two specific cuts. A concrete
new direction is available at these same charts: the actual late pair-sum X
carrier already present at a query plane can be snapshotted directly and
exported one rank to the early pair. Its individual pair sum is outside the old
half-query span. Loading a retained donor and inverting that load at Hub uses
actual fields and changes the global-history premise. Its full word, feasibility
and cost require their own review; this theorem does not preclude it.
