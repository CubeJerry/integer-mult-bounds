# Cross-quartet sources are valid; their fixed half-sum banks incur new native exits

**Independent acceptance of two physical partial words, with separate scoped
continuation exclusions.** Both new sources really acquire fresh original
inputs, use an occupied Y to carry a fresh half sum to a rank-four rendezvous,
and return the sum to a rank-three query through one paid owned descent. The
arbitrary Y baseline is captured and canceled at actual cocharts. All 222 input
columns replay, and the stated inverse order restores every original X at Hub.

The source change also consumes native access. In the one-mode word, 44
originals have left their native lines. In the nineteen-mode word, 109 have
left. At these actual cuts, the natural enlarged half-sum banks require more
future native-cache ascents than the remaining target budget permits. The
larger source remains excluded even if the entire fresh-quartet half-sum space
is granted globally for free. These are fixed-bank results, not exclusions of
arbitrary new code directions or earlier source contracts.

## 3. The actual bank and the complete fresh-half bank

Let C be the canonical rank-nine code, D the full query-row space of the old
parent, and R the new actual fresh half rows. Grant G=C+D+R globally, even
though no such global physical bank is claimed. The one-mode and nineteen-mode
banks have binary ranks 22 and 37.

For a stronger relaxation, consider all ten originally untouched native
quartets. For each coordinate pair in each quartet, include both equality
halves, whether or not that half was selected or acquired. There are 120 such
rows, with total rank 40. Write their span as H_all. The complete relaxed bank
G_all=C+D+H_all has rank 56. Its inclusion of unacquired directions is a
favorable grant for a lower bound, not a source construction.

Fix one of these banks and suppose every future downward X history remains
in that bank. Public routes are monotone upward. Owned routes may ascend,
descend, turn, branch, or reuse owners, with all positive moves paid. Linear
processing and arbitrary block matrices are allowed; the fixed-bank premise
means every block row of a descending X history lies in the corresponding
scalar bank tensored with the block endomorphism space.

Let E be the expired native coordinates: originals no longer at their native
lines. E is the old forty coordinates plus the four or sixty-nine new sources.
For each j in E, the only current field at L_j is its stationary native cache.
Only public Ys 4,5,6,7 remain at ground.

For receiver i, grant all original Y inputs and the X parts L_i of every
current field other than the native snapshots whose chart lies inside F_i.
This includes all fresh baselines and exports when geometrically eligible.
Granting these possibly separated fields together only strengthens a decoder.
After quotienting by G, every future downward history is zero. A necessary
condition is therefore

    e_i in G + L_i + span{e_j : j is reactivated from E intersect N_i}.

The checker recomputes indispensable coordinates for each receiver. For each
selected forced pair (i,j), it supplies a binary dual d with

    d(G)=0, d(L_i)=0, d(e_h)=0 for every h in E intersect N_i except j,
    d(e_i)=d(e_j)=1.

Thus even after every other eligible dormant raw coordinate is granted,
receiver i needs an effective contribution from native cache j. The binary
dual remains valid over every characteristic-two extension field and at every
whole width. Y nuisances have been relaxed in the lower bound; they are fully
present in the actual source replay.

## 4. First-exit geometry and the new acquisition penalty

Suppose native cache j has only one effective future upward departure, from
L_j to a strict superchart P. For any receiver-specific dual forcing j, apply
that dual to every field inside F_i. All other initial observations and all
incoming descending histories are zero. If P is not inside F_i, no effective
upward path from that departure can enter F_i: an upward move cannot enter a
downward-closed facet region from outside. Consequently one departure can
serve all forced receivers only if P lies in the intersection of their facets.

In the actual bank, every expired coordinate except j=3 has forced-receiver
normals of real rank seven. The intersection is exactly L_j, leaving no
possible strict P. Each such coordinate needs at least two departure
occurrences. Coordinate 3 has a nonempty forced demand, requiring at least one.
Distinct native lines require distinct departure occurrences even if an owned
field is reused. At most four departures can be carried by the four still-
ground public Ys; all other departures must be owned ascents.

## 5. Reproduction and constructive consequence

Run check.py with explicit --native, --inputs, --parent, --source-review,
--arithmetic and --out paths. The native data, both words, parent, replay and
arithmetic dependencies are authenticated. RESULT.json records physical source
acceptance; BANK-CERTIFICATE.json records the separate bank-dependent duals,
normal ranks, movement bounds and exact rational intervals. No source builder
or claimed author inventory is imported.

The favorable earlier screen kept all 71 fresh originals native while freely
granting the complete fresh-half bank. That relaxation did reduce the old
source's forced exit count. The actual nineteen-mode acquisition consumes 69
of those originals and creates at least 69 additional indispensable native
departures even under the complete bank. This explains the discrepancy without
invalidating the actual export primitive.

Useful escapes require an earlier access schedule, retained public ground
carriers, genuinely different descending directions, or a changed route
contract. The present theorem does not rule those out. In particular it does
not reject the author's subsequent coordinate-first source proposal. No
complete supplier, new kappa, or foundational discharge is claimed.
