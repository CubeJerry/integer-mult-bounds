# Every dormant raw coordinate must reactivate in the fixed query bank

**Exact source-specific interface theorem.** At both frozen sources below,
every one of the forty dormant native coordinates is individually necessary
for some receiver, even after all other eligible dormant coordinates are
granted. Under the stated continuation premise, at least thirty-six distinct
owned upward departures from native lines are compulsory. This strengthens the
earlier twelve-departure consequence of a single receiver's sixteen-coordinate
minimum. It does not rule out additional paid ascents or a changed history bank.

## 2. The exact local observation condition

For receiver i let L_i contain the X parts of every current field other than
the native snapshots whose actual chart is contained in its final facet F_i.
This includes originals and all eligible baseline, axis-history and export
owners. Grant all original Y inputs as known, favoring the prospective decoder.
If S_i is the set of dormant native coordinates actually reactivated into
upward information reaching F_i, completion requires

    e_i in G + L_i + span{e_j : j in S_i},
    S_i subset E intersect N_i,                              (1)

where N_i is the native orthogonal-neighbour set.

To prove (1), quotient X coefficient rows by G. Every future downward payload
is zero in this quotient. Nonzero quotient information can propagate only
through upward or stationary chart edges. Tracing it backward from F_i reaches
an initial field whose chart is contained in F_i. All non-native-snapshot
fields of that kind are already granted in L_i. The 71 still-active snapshots
add nothing beyond their current raw originals. Remaining sources are dormant
units at native lines L_j contained in F_i, exactly j in E intersect N_i. Zero
births and linear mixing create no additional direction. Granting Y inputs
removes nuisance equations in the favorable direction.

## 4. Thirty-six owned upward departures

A dormant unit can enter a new upward public path only at its own native line.
No original X remains there. No other native line or positive-rank current
public chart can ascend into that distinct line. Only the four ground Ys can
still visit it, and each can visit at most one distinct native line. They can
therefore reactivate at most four distinct members of E.

Every other forced coordinate must leave its line through an owned upward
MOVE, either on the native snapshot itself or on a fresh owner that actually
reads it. Such a first departure can introduce only that line's one new native
coordinate modulo G: descending arrivals lie in G and other native lines cannot
arrive by ascent. Distinct coordinates need distinct departure occurrences,
even if the same physical owner is reused later. Therefore

    owned native-line upward departures >= 40-4 = 36.        (5)

This is an occurrence count, not a lifetime count or a complete-route price.
Later combining raw coordinates does not undo their first departures. Paid
owned ascents are allowed; the theorem excludes their omission under the same
bank and monotone-public premises.

## 5. Activation cost in addition to facet traffic

At t=199/200 let I_o count owned upward native-facet arrivals and downward
native-facet departures. Each owned MOVE contributes zero or one such incidence
and costs at least that contribution. For a compulsory upward departure from
a native line, there are two cases. If it does not end at a native facet, its
incidence is zero and cost at least one. If it does, its gap is six, incidence
one and cost 6^t>2. Thus each of the thirty-six departures pays at least one
additional unit beyond its possible incidence. Total future owned cost is at
least I_o+36. An ascent that itself reaches a facet is not counted twice for
only one unit of cost.

The retained occurrence theorem gives chi<=sum a_j+I_o, with a_j indicating
an original-X facet visit. Combining the previously reviewed compulsory price
delta_j=(7-r_j)^t+1-(8-r_j)^t yields

    Q >= Q0 + chi - 111 + sum_j delta_j + 36.                 (6)

The initial nonglobal information is native-local modulo G, including exported
originals x_d+q whose q part lies in G. The retained normalization/occurrence
premise therefore applies. Exact tariffs give:

| Fixed source and history bank | Strict threshold for chi | Integer cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Low retention, C plus full queries |151.7158235486717...|151|
| Proper exports, C plus sign queries |139.7158235486717...|139|

These are necessary bounds, not exclusions of the full rank-21 banks; no lower
bound exceeding these caps is claimed. At other exponents the additional

to small t without this qualification.
