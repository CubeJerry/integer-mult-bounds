a strict superchart P. Every receiver whose dual forces j must be reachable
through this same first departure. Thus P must be contained in every one
of its forced facets. Equivalently, apply each receiver-specific dual to
the computation: all other eligible sources and all downward arrivals
vanish, so its nonzero endpoint needs an inside path from that departure.

## 5. Scope and constructive implication

These are linear fixed-bank continuation exclusions at actual cuts, with
monotone public routes and arbitrary paid nested owned turns. New complete
descending X directions outside the stated bank, a different earlier
source, or public downward turns can change the conclusion. The nine-
descent theorem is more general than the75-ascent premise, but does not
itself close these budgets.

The first-departure rank argument alone is not a theorem about the cost
of full multicast. A separate exploratory control found two rank-two
