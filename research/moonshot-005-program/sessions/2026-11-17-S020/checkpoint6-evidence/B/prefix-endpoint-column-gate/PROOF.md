# The exact prefix column has no complete (4,2,1) extension

primitive invoices and exact one-column endpoint identity remain correct.
Changing this last column or the first interface is outside this theorem.**

## 1. Exact retained hypotheses

Retain C, H, a, P,N,S1, p,z,w from the frozen positive package
prefix-three-bank-endpoint-column. Thus a acts on the tensor tail, P,N are
the two tau-prefix projectors, and

    C[2,:]=(N,0,P), p=N, z=X P N1, w=X G P P1.

Let Q be ANY ordinary three-bank unit with Q[:,2]=(p,z,w)^T, with an actual
ordinary inverse. The other two columns of Q are unrestricted in the stated
ordinary class. Allow arbitrary ordinary endpoint units O,Iin and their
actual inverses, and exact whole-bank Gaussian heads F4,F2,F1 of tail ranks
4W,2W,W, with their actual finite prefix gauges included.

The ordinary words use a fixed finite prefix-matrix/tail root-four catalogue;
the prefix dimension and the complete tail catalogue are fixed independently
of W. Width-dependent exterior coefficients do not enlarge that catalogue.
Apply the retained tensor-word separation at all sufficiently large faithful
tail widths in a fixed root-four residue class. Consequently distinct
Gaussian ranks and ordinary terms separate even after fixed prefix matrix
coefficients. This is the precise uniformity hypothesis used below. The
claim does not cover an unrestricted width-growing ordinary catalogue.

Put E=L C U Q L and Ebar=H E. An independent (4,2,1) factorization would give

    Ebar=Obar diag(F4,F2,F1) Iin,

with Obar=H O an ordinary unit. Write c1=Obar[:,2] for the actual linear-head
encoder. It is an injective map from a whole D-dimensional bank, D=16^V.

## 2. The source forces a half-bank linear image in a specified output

Let pi(y0,y1,y2)=P y2. From C[2,:]=(N,0,P), and [a,P]=0,

    (P on bank2) L C U=(0,0,P).

The gauge H leaves bank2 unchanged. Therefore, writing qij for Q's entries,

    pi Ebar=(P q20 a+P q21, P q21 a, P q22).          (1)

The right side contains only ordinary and Gaussian-one words. In the
proposed factorization, class separation and the surjectivity of each
Fr Iin[r,:] force

    pi c4=pi c2=0.

Since Obar is onto, pi c1 is onto the entire P output half-bank. Hence

    rank(pi c1)=D/2,
    dim ker(pi|im c1)=D/2.                           (2)

This uses the actual endpoint encoder and inverse; no class component is
declared to be a free bank on the basis of rank alone.

## 3. The fixed column forces too much linear image in the kernel

The exact whole-column identity of the positive package gives its linear
component, after H, as

    Ebar_1[:,2]=(-x,x,a p)^T,
    x=a(-S J z+w).

On the input N sector, zN=wN=0 and pN=N. Thus

    Ebar_1[:,2] N=(0,0,aN)^T.                       (3)

Because a is invertible and commutes with N, this image is exactly the
N half of output bank2, of dimension D/2. It lies in im c1 and ker pi.

On the independent input sector P P1, pP P1=zP P1=0 and wP P1=w. Hence

    Ebar_1[:,2] P P1=(-a w,a w,0)^T.                 (4)

The distinguished sigma factor makes P P1 have exact rank D/4 at EVERY
tail width: its four character projectors have equal prefix multiplicity.
The map w has w*w=P P1, so (4) has image dimension D/4. It is nonzero,
lies in im c1 and ker pi, and is independent of (3), because its third
output bank is zero whereas (3) is supported only there.

Consequently

    dim ker(pi|im c1) >= D/2+D/4=3D/4,               (5)

contradicting (2). Equivalently the actual linear encoder would need rank
at least 3D/4+D/2=5D/4, although it encodes one D-dimensional bank.

## 4. Consequences and scope

Every ordinary completion of the fixed column (p,z,w) fails a full independent
(4,2,1) endpoint identity for this C. Changing the ordinary endpoint complement,
head gauges, or the other two columns of Q cannot repair it within the stated
fixed-prefix/fixed-catalogue class. The proof is a physical dimension argument
after justified Gaussian-class separation; it does not use an augmentation
character on the enlarged prefix algebra.

The positive package remains a useful exact unit/column control. Its attempted
full extension is superseded by this gate. Further construction must change
C or the fixed column so that the linear component forced into ker pi has
dimension at most D/2, or change the source condition that makes pi Ebar
pure ordinary-plus-linear. An unused-complement search with this same column
does not address the obstruction.
