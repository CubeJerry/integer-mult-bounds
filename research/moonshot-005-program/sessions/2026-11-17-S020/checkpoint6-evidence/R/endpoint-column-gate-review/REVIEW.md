# Independent review of the fixed-column endpoint obstruction

## Decision

Accept the dimension obstruction with its stated uniformity hypotheses.
It closes every ordinary unit completion of the particular fixed last
column and first interface in the positive prefix package. It does not
invalidate that package's actual units, inverse words, primitive costs or
exact single-column identity. It does not exclude a changed first interface,
a changed last column, or an ordinary catalogue growing with tensor width.

The positive source was independently reconstructed with C's sparse Q(i)
arithmetic in the adjacent endpoint-column-review package. This gate review
imports that pinned independent reconstruction, not either author checker.
It directly verifies images and ranks instead of relying only on traces.

## Universal argument

Set pi=(0,0,P_tau), viewed as a map from three output banks onto the
tau-plus half of the last bank. Literal multiplication before the arbitrary
ordinary middle unit gives

    pi H=pi,       pi L C U=(0,0,P_tau).

Consequently, for every Q and the rightmost native L,

    pi H E=(P_tau q20 a+P_tau q21, P_tau q21 a, P_tau q22).

Only ordinary and linear Gaussian classes occur. The inherited separation
premise is essential: the prefix dimension and finite catalogue of tail
root-four words must be fixed independently of width, and the relevant
Gaussian rank classes separate at sufficiently large faithful tail widths
in the retained residue class. Finite checks at widths0 and1 do not prove
that separation. The theorem explicitly retains it.

Under this premise, any factorization through independent ranks4W,2W,W
and actual ordinary endpoint units forces pi c4=pi c2=0. Each right endpoint
row has a right inverse, so an isolated vanishing head contribution cannot
be attributed merely to a singular right row. The entire output endpoint
is onto; pi c1 must therefore be onto a D/2-dimensional output half-bank.
Since c1 is an injective encoder of exactly D dimensions, its image has a
kernel of dimension exactly D/2 under pi.

For the fixed column, the complete linear component after H is
(-x,x,aN), with x=a(-SJz+w). Its restriction to input N has image
(0,0,aN), of dimension D/2. Its restriction to the disjoint input P P1
has image(-aw,aw,0), of dimension D/4. Both lie in ker pi and im c1.
They are independent because their output bank supports are disjoint.
The sigma prefix makes P P1 exactly one quarter of the full bank at every
width; this dimension does not rely on a tail character being balanced.
Thus dim(ker pi intersect im c1) is at least3D/4, contradicting D/2.

This is a direct physical encoder dimension argument. No individual
Gaussian component is identified as a free bank without the endpoint
inverse, and no augmentation character is applied to the prefix algebra.

## Exact controls and limitations

At reduced tail widths0 and1, the independent checker verifies the literal
row before Q; reconstructs both forced sector maps; checks their projector
and Gram identities; and computes exact sparse ranks of each image and
their union. The dimensions are respectively(2,1,3) and(32,16,48), against
available kernel dimensions2 and32. Adding the forced projection dimension
requires5D/4 encoded dimensions, strictly more than one whole bank.

Removing either forced sector alone eliminates this particular dimension
contradiction. Replacing the tau-plus output selector with tau-minus fails
the claimed pre-Q source row. These controls delimit the proof mechanism.

The fixed-column extension is therefore closed within the stated class,
even if the unused Q columns, endpoint complements and finite head gauges
are changed. A constructive continuation must reduce the forced linear
kernel or change the source row. The previous positive proof's suggestion
to repair only the unused columns is superseded by this result.
