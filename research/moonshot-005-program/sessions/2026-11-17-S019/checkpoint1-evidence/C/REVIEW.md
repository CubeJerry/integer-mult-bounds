## Independent new block-star proof

Let J={5,35,90,93,96,97,109,110}. These eight native roles are pairwise
nonorthogonal. A block fitting F on the unknown graph has diagonal I_V and
zero off-diagonal blocks for distinct nonorthogonal native roles. Consequently
F[J,J]=I_(8V), so rank F is at least8V.

Suppose rank F is8V. Factor F=C R through an 8V-dimensional space and use the
invertible J factor block to choose coordinates with C_J=R_J=I_(8V). The
factor rows and columns of any outside role e can then be nonzero only in

Their edges are distinct. Every two edge roles incident at a J point are
nonorthogonal; exact integer root products establish every required zero.
For an edge e={a,b}, put D_ea=C_ea R_ae and D_eb=C_eb R_be. The diagonal fitting
identity gives D_ea+D_eb=I_V. Thus

rank(D_ea)+rank(D_eb) >= V.

For a fixed J point a, stack the C_ea vertically and concatenate R_af
horizontally over its incident edges. Their product is block diagonal, since
distinct incident edge roles share only coordinate a and their fitting block
is zero. Its rank is the sum of rank(D_ea), and its inner dimension is V.
Therefore sum over incident e of rank(D_ea) <= V.

Summing these inequalities at the eight J points bounds the sum of all edge
endpoint ranks by8V. Summing the diagonal inequalities for the fifteen edges
bounds the same quantity below by15V, a contradiction. Thus rank F>8V over
**every field and every positive V**, with arbitrary nonsymmetric,
noncommuting blocks. No scalar rank claim has been extrapolated to blocks.
This argument alone does not prove rank F>=9V; it proves a strict8V barrier.
