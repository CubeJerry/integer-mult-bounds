## Source-aware late-code injection lemma

Work over any characteristic-two field and any positive whole-record width V.
Suppose at an actual cut all original X records are raw, with the indices H at
Hub and D at one proper chart J. All records have their actual prescribed
custody. Let C be a desired code with a selected basis c_1,...,c_r. A packet
can be loaded at Hub with the actual H projection of c_k, descend to J, add
the required live original X_d for d∈D, and then descend further. Its value
is exactly c_k; the actual held query is essential.

For each selected receiver i, suppose a stationary field r_i is already
available at J and p_i=c_i+r_i, where c_i∈C, has zero coefficients on D.
Capture p_i at J by adding the actual basis packets and r_i while cocharted.
Choose a distinct held original d_i∈D. Inject p_i into X_{d_i}, giving
x_{d_i}+p_i; move that original upward through an actual chart chain to F_i
and then Hub. At Hub, p_i is a combination of unchanged raw originals H, so
adding those actual records restores x_{d_i}. No inverse or source value is
postulated without an emitted operation.

If the waiting receiver contains

    y_i + x_{d_i} + N_i + r_i,

where c_i=K_i=e_i+N_i, its real read of the arriving original gives

    y_i+x_{d_i}+N_i+r_i + x_{d_i}+K_i+r_i = y_i+x_i.

The receiver may already have left ground before any canonical packet exists.
Its actual early correction and mask acquisitions must be proved separately.
This is what changes the chronological interface. It does not make an
unavailable canonical row an early source.

The sufficient zero-D condition can be relaxed only with a proved Hub inverse:
if the held subsystem is transformed by D'=(I+A_D)D+A_H H, restoration from the
final original records requires I+A_D invertible. The concrete word uses A_D=0,
so every inverse is a literal sum of unchanged raw H originals. It needs no
unverified matrix-factorization or chart-realizability premise.

## Lower-chart helpers and the remaining obstruction

Portable inventories list every actual live field, chart, rank and 222-column
coefficient row at cuts1466 and1730. For each unfinished receiver they list
all fields whose actual chart is contained in its final facet and their exact
linear ranks. Such containment is an optimistic envelope, not simultaneous
custody or a legal chain visiting incomparable charts.

Ninety of the 105 unfinished facets contain at least one raw mask chart. The
mask-rank histogram is 0:15, 1:22, 2:45, 3:19, 4:4. Every mask lies in the span
of that receiver's native orthogonal inputs, so **none increases the optimistic
native-side-information rank**. The five rank-two masks are pairwise
incomparable, and none contains the rank-one mask. Each rank-two chart contains
exactly three native lines. An ascending private suffix can therefore read
at most one such mask chart, together with native lines compatible with that
same chain; coverage of ninety facets is not ninety completed receivers.

The six held original charts at cut1466 are all J. Exactly the already selected
six native facets contain J. They cannot simply ascend into an unfinished
facet. Thus this successful early timing does not by itself supply the global
escape requested by the programme. It identifies the stronger next condition:
canonical delivery must coexist with useful originals or helpers at charts
accessible to unfinished receivers, and those resources must provide a legal
shared correction, not just independent facet containment.

A generalized last-shared-chart obstruction may still exclude native-only
completion after the final cut. Its proof must use the new low-mask source
rather than importing the old all-J invisibility premise. This claim is left
for separate independent audit; no such gate is silently assumed in the
positive word proof.
