# Last shared chart with native-local initial helpers

**Proved scoped obstruction, including nonlinear local processing.** Applied to the frozen early-interleaving cut1730, every target-budget continuation whose future owned movements all go from a native line to ground is excluded. The positive earlier-acquisition construction remains valid. Descents from other proper charts, different source cuts, changed ownership directions and growing side information are outside this statement.

## Quantified source conditions

Consider a finite fixed schedule starting from a specified cut. The unfinished receiver set U has at least two members, raw Y_i=y_i at ground, and final facet F_i. All original X are already at Hub, completed Y are at their distinct final facets, and neither can visit an unfinished facet under the ascending-original rule. Ground has a binary linear code Cx. Each native line L_j retains x_j. Put N_i={j:L_j⊂F_i}, require i∉N_i for every i∈U, and assume K_i=e_i+sum_(j∈N_i)e_j belongs to C.

Initial helpers that can reach F_i without an owned descent must be native-local: a field at H depends only on the x_j with L_j⊂H and already granted ground data. Other initial proper fields must be inaccessible to the unfinished facets. New stationary fields start at zero and may store arbitrary functions of actual cocharted data, including arbitrary Y-bearing histories.

Suppose there are constants g,d,s such that:

* Every nonzero c∈C other than K_i has at least g nonzero coordinates outside N_i, for all i∈U.
* |N_i\N_h|≥d for all distinct i,h∈U.
* After any receiver's last chart visited by another unfinished receiver, the union of additional native variables supplied by initial local helpers and native caches on its remaining ascending chain has size at most s, outside the variables already available at that last shared chart.

Then no exact completion exists with r distinct native-line descent origins if

    r<|U|,  r<g,  and r+s<d.                       (1)

Every future owned MOVE is required here to originate at one of the native lines and end at ground. There may be any number of such MOVEs, arbitrary reuse of origins, arbitrary local nonlinear maps, arbitrary Y feedback, and arbitrary stationary field histories. A bound on distinct origins is weaker than a bound on the total number of movements.

## Proof

Let R be the set of descent origins. Grant W=(Cx,(x_j)_(j∈R),(y_h)_h) everywhere at zero price. The last group consists of the original private Y inputs, not the current completed y_h+x_h outputs.

Chronological induction shows that all descendant payloads are functions of W. At a native line L_j, every initial accessible local field depends only on x_j and W: distinct native lines are incomparable, every receiver brings only earlier information from subcharts, and stationary histories obey the same causal condition. If j∈R, its x_j is already in W. After each descent its payload therefore adds no information beyond W. More generally, information at any chart H depends only on W and native variables with L_j⊂H, by the same induction over ascending originals, local operations and stationary owners.

Choose i∈U\R, possible by r<|U|. Let H be the last chart on Y_i's own future ascending path that any other unfinished receiver Y_h visits at any time, including visits at a different time. It exists because all unfinished receivers start at ground. Every original receiver stays within its final facet, so H⊂F_i∩F_h. Thus all information available by this point is a function of W and the native variables indexed by N_i∩N_h.

After H no other unfinished receiver ever visits a later chart on Y_i's path. At those charts a stationary field can therefore contribute only its initial data, W, or Y_i's own previous information. A field written by another receiver at a different time would make that chart a later shared chart and contradict the definition of H. Repeated local copies and nonlinear functions cannot create another independent input. By the third source condition, the final Y_i is a function of

    W, (x_j)_(j∈N_i∩N_h), and at most s further native inputs.

At least d−r−s>0 members of N_i remain unobserved even after the global grants R. Choose one such j.

The code gap makes each such neighbor indispensable, as a statement about functions rather than just a chosen linear decoder. Indeed C contains no nonzero word supported inside N_i∪R. K_i is excluded because i∉R and i∉N_i; every other nonzero codeword has at least g>r coordinates outside N_i. If e_i were in the span of C and the observed native coordinate rows, adding the known fitting row K_i would give a nonzero codeword supported in N_i∪R whose coefficient at the missing j is1, a contradiction. Linear duality therefore gives two input assignments agreeing on Cx and every observed coordinate but with different x_i. They may have equal arbitrary private y inputs. Their entire allowed observation, and hence every nonlinear function of it, is identical. Exact output y_i+x_i is impossible. This proves (1).

The witness is valid over every characteristic-two field and whole-record width: a binary kernel difference may be multiplied by any nonzero field element in one record coordinate. The finite receipt gives an explicit collision with seventeen global grants and three additional private native grants; granting the missing native data restores decodability as a positive control.
