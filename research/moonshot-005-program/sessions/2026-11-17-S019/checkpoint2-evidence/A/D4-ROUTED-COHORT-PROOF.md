## A reversible routing lemma

Let original inputs be x_j over any characteristic-two field, or over any fixed
V-dimensional vector space over that field. Partition some inputs into disjoint
cohorts D_g and a disjoint common set E. Assume:

1. Every donor line L_j for j in D_g lies in a legal source chart C_g.
2. Every common carrier line for c in E lies in J, and every used C_g contains J.
3. Each carrier has one actual ascending chart chain from its source chart to
   Hub, and every intended receiver read occurs at a chart on that chain.
4. Each original is assigned at most one carrier route; the common carriers are
   distinct and disjoint from all D_g.
5. All additions below occur while source and destination records are physically
   cocharted. Every stationary owned cache is born zero at its chart.

Write g=Σ_{j∈D_g}x_j. Pick p∈D_g. Bring X_p to C_g and, while the other donors
visit C_g, add each X_j to X_p. The original now carries g. The other donor
originals remain raw. The pivot can ascend its chain, deposit g into as many
zero-born stationary fields as the chain permits, and arrive at Hub. Then add
the raw X_j, j≠p, to it, recovering x_p exactly. This transformation is
invertible because the unchanged donor originals provide all masks.

For additional routes, first capture the raw x_c of a common original at J.
Move X_c from J to C_g and add an actual stationary g field acquired from the
raw donors. The original carries x_c+g, may leave copies along its ascending
chain, and is restored at Hub by adding all restored donor originals in D_g.
A receiver reading one such copy also reads the actual raw x_c cache at J;
the two masks cancel. If a receiver reads several masked routes, it reads the
corresponding mask sum, with multiplicity in characteristic two. No source
rank or cached symbolic expression licenses an absent physical read.

These operations restore all X inputs before any subsequent Hub canonical
supplier loading. Hence the ordinary canonical source formulas remain valid
without covariance repair. Arbitrary initial Y columns are never overwritten:
only designated X-linear expressions are added to each Y.
