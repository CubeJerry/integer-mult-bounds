## 2. Credited quadratic proper-cover lemma

If the native support O_i were contained in two proper real subspaces
B_1,B_2 of F_i, choose nonzero linear forms ell_1,ell_2 on F_i that vanish
on B_1,B_2 respectively. Their product is a nonzero homogeneous quadratic

## 3. Source-only causal theorem

Consider any fixed-schedule source computation with the following contract:

1. The only mobile records carrying information about x are the 111 original
   X_j, initially containing independent x_j at their actual lines L_j.
2. Each X moves only upward under real chart inclusion.
3. Other records are zero at birth and remain stationary. No owned MOVE and
   no moving original Y are allowed in this source phase.
4. Local processing may be arbitrary, including nonlinear processing. It
   may use only records physically co-located at that chart.
5. A claimed full correction is an actual field N_i(x) at the actual F_i,
   with correctness for every independent assignment of the inputs.

Additional independent auxiliary inputs may be fixed identically in the
indistinguishability argument; they do not reveal any x coordinate.

Induction on the operations shows that the information at a chart H depends
only on native x_j with L_j subset H. Initially this is true. Local processing
preserves it, stationary births add no input, and an upward move can only
increase the possible dependency set.

Now consider the first acquisition of a full N_i at F_i. Every X that has
visited F_i arrived from a proper preceding chart B_a strictly below F_i.
All its earlier native information lies in B_a. Its entire earlier route is
below B_a. Any stationary owner at F_i is a function of the arriving values;
it has no independently preloaded source. If at most two distinct X originals
visit, every local value at F_i depends only on native inputs whose lines
lie in B_1 union B_2. The proper-cover lemma supplies some j in O_i outside
that union. Vary only x_j. All local observations at F_i remain unchanged,
while N_i changes by that arbitrary nonzero record difference. This is a
contradiction, valid for nonlinear processing and every positive width.

**Every actual full N_i at F_i requires at least three distinct X visitors.**

Each ascending X path can visit at most one of the 111 distinct rank-seven
facets: two distinct facets are incomparable. There are at most 111 total
X-to-facet visits. Consequently

    3 times number_of_full_correction_facets <=111,
    number_of_full_correction_facets <=37.               (1)

In particular, no source in this class can acquire 108 independent N_i rows
at their respective facets. The row independence does not help: each of those
108 particular rows has the same full support-cover requirement.

The theorem does not require invertibility of the X source map, so allowing
singular local changes cannot repair this particular failure. The obstruction
is about absent independent input information, before restoration is tested.

only the X-only source above; at Hub restore X; then acquire every remaining
