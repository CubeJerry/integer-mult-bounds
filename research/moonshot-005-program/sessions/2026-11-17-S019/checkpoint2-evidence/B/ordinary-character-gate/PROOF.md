# A fixed tensor character obstructs ordinary one-job output images

**Proved in the literal root-four ordinary coefficient model; independent review pending. No complete caller, supplier or new κ.**

This result attacks the first singular-prefix route beyond the S018 ordinary-range gate. It does not use injectivity of the two-bank prefix. The key distinction is between a subspace of the correct total dimension and a subspace that an ordinary invertible bank transformation can identify with one full bank.

## 1. Ordinary algebra and the fixed vector

Let H have basis e_0,...,e_(d−1), with d fixed (d=16 for the source below). A literal root-four monomial has

    g e_x = i^(h_x) e_(sigma(x)).

At width V it acts by its collective tensor lift g^tensor V. Ordinary blocks are finite linear combinations of these lifts, with exterior scalar coefficients; products and algebraic inverses stay in the same finite monomial coefficient algebra. Coefficients may depend on the allowed width, but the finite literal catalogue is fixed. We do not extend the conclusion to an arbitrary non-root-four per-column amplitude operator.

For every positive integer k and V=4k, put

    xi_4 = sum_x e_x^tensor4,
    xi_V = xi_4^tensor k.

Every literal g fixes xi_V, because every fourth-root phase has fourth power1 and sigma permutes the sum. This is an exact formula for every k, not an inference from testing width4. Exterior scalar i still acts as i.

Consequently every n-bank ordinary matrix preserves

    S_V = span{e_j tensor xi_V : 0 <= j < n}.

Its restriction is the n-by-n scalar matrix obtained by replacing each literal monomial by1 and retaining every exterior coefficient. This is a well-defined algebra homomorphism on the physical ordinary algebra at V=4k: any relation between monomial lifts annihilates xi_V. Call it epsilon_V. An ordinary unit restricts to a unit on S_V, since its injective restriction is an endomorphism of the same finite-dimensional subspace.

## 2. Necessary one-job image condition

Let Pi be an ordinary idempotent on n banks and suppose its image equals the image of a single bank under an ordinary unit O:

    im Pi = im(O e_j).

Then

    rank epsilon_V(Pi) = 1.                              (1)

Indeed O and its inverse preserve S_V. The intersection of im(O e_j) with S_V therefore has dimension1. Since Pi preserves S_V and fixes its own image, im(Pi) intersect S_V is exactly im(Pi restricted to S_V). This proves(1).

More generally, an ordinary encoding of r whole independent banks requires rank epsilon_V(Pi)=r. The same argument works for any common one-dimensional character subspace of the declared ordinary alphabet. This is a necessary condition, not a sufficient ordinary compiler certificate.

For an ordinary independent Gaussian endpoint, each separate Gaussian-rank output image is of the form im(O e_j). Its ordinary projector must satisfy(1), even when its global physical rank is exactly one-bank dimension D=d^V. Correct total rank alone is insufficient.

## 3. A literal singular first interface

Use the admitted four-bit source A=C_b D_(acd) P_cycle, its exact R2, and the active-square swap

    J=R2^-1 SWAP_ab R2.

Set F=Z_a and use collective tensor notation a=A^tensor V, F=Z_a^tensor V, J=J^tensor V. The exact one-column identities are

    J F=F J,       A Z_a A^-1=Z_d.

Write

    P=(I+F)/2, M=I−P, Fhat=Z_d^tensor V,
    Phat=(I+Fhat)/2,
    Pi=diag(Phat,Phat,0).

Consider every ordinary first interface C whose first two first-column blocks are

    C00=P, C10=J P.                                     (2)

C20 and all other blocks may be arbitrary, subject to C being a unit. This family is nonempty: one actual unit is

    C=E10(J) R02^−(F),
    R02^−(F)=[[P,0,M],[0,I,0],[M,0,P]].

Its first column is `(P,JP,M)^T`. The two-active-bank source prefix is

    K=(a P a,a J P a,0)^T.

It satisfies Pi K=K. Its rank is D/2, not D: the kernel is that of P a, because the first component is a P a and the second is obtained by applying the unit a J to the same P a. Since Z_d is traceless, Phat has rank D/2 and Pi has global physical rank D for every positive V. Thus the old injective-K gate does not apply.

## 4. Universal exclusion for this first column

Use the literal ordered caller

    E=L C U Q L,
    L=[[a,0,0],[I,a,0],[0,0,I]],
    U=[[a,−a²,0],[0,a,0],[0,0,I]],

with arbitrary ordinary units C,Q in the fixed root-four interface and with(2). At sufficiently large faithful widths, a separate rank4 Gaussian component can come only from the four-source term

    −K a [q10 a,q11 a,0].                              (3)

All other words contain at most three source factors and have one-column support at most8, so they cannot belong to the rank4 class of support16. This is the retained finite tensor-word separation argument, not a rank bound on arbitrary sums.

Ordinary multiplication commutes with the Gaussian-class projection. Since Pi fixes K, (3) implies

    Pi E4=E4.

An independent421 endpoint would require rank(E4)=D. The global rank of Pi is D, hence im(E4)=im(Pi). At V=4k, Fhat fixes xi_V, so

    epsilon_V(Pi)=diag(1,1,0),
    rank epsilon_V(Pi)=2.

Equation(1) requires rank1 for one independent rank4 job. This contradiction excludes every such caller at all sufficiently large multiples of4, and therefore excludes a uniform all-positive-width independent421 theorem for this first-column family.

The proof allows arbitrary second-interface clocks, arbitrary lower quartic residues, singular coefficients, arbitrary ordinary C20 and arbitrary completions of C's other columns. It does not require K or Ka to be pure classes or injective. It does not exclude an architecture that changes(2), uses an ordinary alphabet beyond the declared root-four model, or explicitly changes the all-width contract with a separately paid legitimate adapter.

## 5. Sharp component control: the rank4 projection really has full rank

Let S=SWAP_ab in the original coordinates, let B2=A³ S A, and let

    Kdec=A J A² S A^-3.

The retained literal identities give Kdec²=I, Kdec Fhat=Fhat Kdec,

    A J A³=Kdec B2,
    A J A² S A=Kdec A⁴.

B2 is Gaussian rank2; A⁴ is rank4, with the admitted exact gauges. Take the actual unit

    Q=[[0,I,0],[I,S,0],[0,0,I]].

After the ordinary output factor diag(I,Kdec^-1,I), the entire four-source term is

    −[[Phat a⁴, Phat B2,0],
      [Phat B2, Phat a⁴,0],
      [0,0,0]].                                        (4)

Its rank4 projection is the diagonal part, of physical rank D; its rank2 residue is the off-diagonal part, also of rank D. The total four-source operator has rank D/2 because it factors through K and its final row is surjective. Thus class projection can indeed increase numerical rank here. The obstruction is not failure to create a full rank4 component: it is the wrong ordinary character occupancy of its image.

This is an exact component identity for every V. It is not a complete421 endpoint; the ordinary and lower-rank pieces of E remain, and the theorem excludes completing them while preserving(2).

## 6. Why the all-width condition matters

Let G=X_d and let G_V=G^tensor V. Then

    G_V Fhat = (−1)^V Fhat G_V.

For odd V, G_V exchanges the plus/minus eigenspaces. The explicit ordinary decoder

    O_odd=R01(Phat) diag(I,G_V,I),
    R01(Phat)=[[I−Phat,Phat,0],[Phat,I−Phat,0],[0,0,I]],

satisfies

    O_odd Pi O_odd^-1=diag(0,I,0).

So the same image is ordinarily alignable at every odd width. The rank2 character obstruction at widths4k proves that this odd-width alignment cannot be promoted to an all-width ordinary decoder. It does not prove that the whole caller works even at odd widths.

## 7. Actual primitive costs for the controls

`WORDS.json` records full chronological words. A two-bank H2=[[1,1],[1,−1]] is ADD(1→0), SCALE(1,−2), ADD(0→1). Its inverse is this word followed by SCALE(0,1/2), SCALE(1,1/2). Thus R02^−(F)=H2^-1 diag(I,F) H2 costs4 ADD,4 SCALE,1 MONO. E10(J) costs1 ADD and2 MONO by applying J to the source, adding it and applying J again. Its inverse adds two exterior sign scalings.

| Control | ADD | SCALE | MONO |

| Q |4|3|2|

Every original arbitrary bank is retained; no owner is added. The script verifies each whole operator and inverse, all single-ADD omissions, and a conservative bank row-sum prefix bound. The counts cover these component controls only, not missing simultaneous Gaussian normalization, full native sources, field volume, precision/tape, compiler, supplier or recursion. Every MONO is a literal16-table root-four service with its already admitted finite physical lowering. Full field volume is at least16^V; a fixed count of passes is not a complete profitable invoice.

## Open next question

A surviving singular-prefix mechanism must distribute its rank4 output image with the correct ordinary character multiplicities, as well as total dimension. One concrete next attempt is to replace the repeated plus sector `(P,JP)` by a nontrivial rank-D ordinary ambient projector with augmentation rank1 while keeping K noninjective, or to let the ambient projector have rank strictly larger than D so that the full rank4 image need not fill it. The existing complementary `(I−P,JP)` interface is already excluded by S018's injective ordinary-range gate; it is not a new solution.
