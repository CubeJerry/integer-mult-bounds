local inverse-column premises used by the new pair/triple words. Let E be the
canonical evaluation matrix on those q roots.

    (E tensor I_B) diag(Q_1^-1,...,Q_q^-1),

held-coordinate functional is nonzero: canonical evaluation on each native

such a basis spans a coordinate subspace; since every held coordinate occurs

the triple q6/rank6. Their direct-query bases meet the criterion exactly.
