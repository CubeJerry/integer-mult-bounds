## Mathematical proof review

For the full-plane family, the 15-column restrictions are Vandermonde
matrices multiplied by nonzero diagonal matrices. The full 45-row basis
makes shared-port subspaces actual coordinate subspaces; intersections
therefore equal shared rows. Applying the construction independently in
the early and late phases respects causality. The F128 word is a new
realization of the same formula, not an asserted F64 subfield embedding.

For a punctured plane, early source rank is 30 in 45 columns and the late
source block has rank 15 in 30 rows. The full 60-by-60 kernel reduces
exactly to the author's 15-by-15 `L C N` expression. Surjectivity of the
linear map from C to that expression makes its determinant a nonzero
polynomial. An entry of C has rank-one influence and occurs in at most 36
receiver conditions, so the finite-grid existence proof is valid. The new
review additionally checks every original full matrix directly, avoiding
dependence on the reduced determinant evaluator. The early cache is needed
for this displayed interface; setting C to zero loses 15 ranks. Cache
acquisition precedes the early public transform and late use follows real
arrival. The arbitrary late baseline passes through an invertible matrix.

For singleton planes, the source dimension is correctly kept at 540 rather
than 360. Values and first derivatives at 30 distinct nodes determine a
polynomial of degree below 60 in characteristic two. The double-root
divisibility argument is valid; it uses no division by factorials. Caching
only odd coefficients suffices, since the derivative of every even power
is zero. The full early evaluation map is invertible and the late baseline
map is identity. The fixed finite lane words tensor with every positive
body identity; this uniformity is a written construction, not an inference
from the checked sizes.

## Common semisimple proof review

A finite commuting family of semisimple matrices over a finite field has
one simultaneous eigenbasis over a finite splitting extension. The proposed
extension of a hypothetical nonlinear code is valid: run one independent
copy per base-field coordinate of the extension and identify those tuples
with extension symbols. Arbitrary nonlinear operations are lifted
componentwise; they are not evaluated as if their original formulas were
extension-field polynomials. Every port's alphabet scales consistently.

Because the source matrices have base-field entries, this product code's
source maps are the same matrices acting linearly over the extension. One
common conjugation then preserves the original binary native sums and
reduces ordinary quotients to aligned varying scalar stripes. The inherited
cut grants arbitrary cut processing, so this argument does not assume a
free physical compiler for that conjugation. The existing native coloring
certificate gives capacity 538,644r against 587,520r needed. Its deficit is
48,876r. No new full coloring search is claimed in this review; its proof
and prior independent review are explicitly pinned.

The common basis is essential. Distinct donor bases, noncommuting arrays
and common commuting matrices with nilpotent parts remain outside that
particular theorem. The separately recovered commuting-nilpotent triangle
is a valid reason not to silently drop this restriction.
