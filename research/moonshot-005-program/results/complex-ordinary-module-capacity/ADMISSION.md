# Ordinary capacity of idempotent sectors

Fix a finite root-four monomial catalogue on a fixed column space, closed under
products/inverses. At sufficiently large widths divisible4 its collective
algebra is faithfully C[G] for the projective groupG. Coefficients may depend
on width; the catalogue may not grow with it. An ordinary idempotentP and actual
ordinary split embeddingPC=C,DC=I_r satisfy r<=tau(P),r<=rank epsilon(P).
Regular equality r=tau(P) forces rank epsilon(P)=r. The proof uses equality of
images, not the false identityP=CD.

Forn nontrivial same-plus involution sectors,2r<n. Same-minus sectors admit no
positive free-bank split. Balanced plus/minus counts are necessary at regular
equality. Nontrivial subgroup averages of equal orderh obeyhr<n. Physical rank
alone and Gaussian-component projection do not justify applying these results.

Constructive controls preserve genuine escapes: complementary plus/minus
sectors have an explicit ordinary2-by-2 unit; three Klein plus sectors admit
one free bank with an actual left inverse. This is not independent Gaussian
(4,2,1) endpoints or a complete profitable caller.
