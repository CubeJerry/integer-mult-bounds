## 1. Exact current-row criterion

reverse output is b-d. Put M0=I+R. Choose m pivots I and an m-by-n packet
matrix A with nonsingular A_I. Write C_I=A_I^{-1}A and

    J=I+E_I(C_I-E_I^T),    E_I^T J=C_I.

This replaces the pivot Y2 values by their systematic packet rows. It is
invertible, leaves nonpivots unchanged, and is applied at their real common
initial chart. Every subsequent read must use Jd, not stale d. If packet

row(A), the reverse and original-Y2 defects are respectively

    M=I+R J-K,    D=J-I.

The exact identity is

    ker([M;D])=ker(M0-K) intersect ker(J-I).                 (1)

Indeed, Dd=0 says Jd=d; under that equation Md=(M0-K)d. The converse is
immediate. This holds over any field admitting the matrices, and as a
block-matrix identity for all field volumes. It counts both endpoint duties.
