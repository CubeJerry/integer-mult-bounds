# Transient receiver recoding with exact restoration before final departures

## Status and source relationship

This package proves a receiver-side realization of the programme's paired
inverse-transpose interface, then executes it on the pinned complex source.
It changes actual unknown original D values temporarily and restores every
one before its final facet departure. It therefore lies outside the earlier
S011 stationary-original-D control gate. No new owned MOVE is used.

The literal parent variant removes forty unwanted forward coefficients and
adds forty corresponding transpose reverse coefficients. Its forward debt is
4920 -> 4880; its partial reverse residual rank is 82 -> 83. No new whole
forward endpoint or reverse endpoint is claimed. A separate, explicitly
alternative baseline gives an exact composable hosted Gram invariant. This
baseline reopens earlier one-way progress; it does not supersede the selected
construction merely because its invariant is simpler.

The generic inverse-transpose algebra is already proved in
`results/paired-encoder-interface/PAIRED-ENCODER-INTERFACE.md` and
`results/paired-cohort/PROOF.md` of the permanent programme. Those S008
packages and the retained raw-pivot interface retain their credit. The new

instance. Prepared for @CubeJerry with OpenAI assistance.

## 1. Receiver-bank realization

Let F be a field, V a positive integer, and let the entries of four banks
x,b in (F^V)^r and y,d in (F^V)^s be arbitrary and mutually independent.
Let A be an r-by-s matrix. Choose r distinct positions p_i and an invertible
s-by-s matrix M such that row p_i of M is row i of A. Let E be the s-by-r
coordinate insertion matrix at these positions; thus E^T M=A. The existence
of such an M requires the selected rows to be independent. This proposition
does not grant independent physical replicas for dependent desired rows.

Assume the following actual chart and operation conditions.

1. All y,d pairs initially coexist at a chart O, where the chosen elementary
   factorizations of M and M^{-T} are admissible.
2. Pair p_i can travel from O through branch chart L_i to a common later chart
   C. Every other pair can travel from O directly to C. At L_i, actual b_i
   and an authentic current record with value x_i are available.
3. Both banks can be decoded at C, after which each restored pair can travel
   to its required final facet. All preceding and following operations that
   use originals respect this schedule.

Perform D=M d and Y=M^{-T} y at O. At branch i perform

    Y[p_i] += x_i,       b_i -= D[p_i].

At C perform D <- M^{-1}D and Y <- M^T Y. The exact resulting bank map is

    x_out=x,     d_out=d,     y_out=y+A^T x,     b_out=b-A d.

Indeed, D is untouched at branches, so it decodes to d. The Y bank before
decoding is M^{-T}y+Ex, so after decoding it is y+M^T Ex=y+A^T x. The branch
reads use E^T M d=A d. These identities hold for every input and every V,
not merely sampled values. The pair of opposite shears is invertible and
preserves x^T b+y^T d. Existing unknown y values are carried throughout;
no old value is reconstructed by assuming that an unknown baseline is zero.

If a factorization of M uses q ADDs and u SCALEs, the four bank transforms
use 4q ADDs and 4u SCALEs. Branches use another 2r ADDs. A stationary x cache
for each branch requires one INIT, one acquisition ADD, one CLEAR, and its
actual live storage. Such caches do not require transport if acquired and
consumed at the same chart. Their physical acquisition, erasure and ordinary
arithmetic remain part of the inherited source/compiler contract.

For O dimension zero, C dimension c, s final facets of dimension f, and r
branch lines of dimension one, the two-bank transport increment relative
to 2s direct rank-f moves is

    2s [c^t+(f-c)^t-f^t] + 2r [1+(c-1)^t-c^t],

with equal-chart segments omitted. This charges both Y and D carriers. It
also charges the extra branch stop, even when it is taken by an already paid
raw route. It is an invoice under the stated schedule, not a theorem that
all desired coefficients can be covered by such schedules simultaneously.

## Verification classification

The receiver-bank algebra is proved for every admissible field, positive V
and independent inputs under its explicit physical schedule. The emitted
finite source words, exact source identities, ranks, charts, invoices and
mutants are computationally verified by exact rational/signed-Pauli methods.
Because the operations are scalar-linear, the verified source identities
extend coordinatewise to arbitrary V within the inherited source interface.
This does not establish unbounded-size uniform supplier generation, precision,
ordinary physical cost, ownership/erasure compilation, source access, complete
recursive closure, or any of the retained foundational assumptions.

The author replay deliberately reuses the frozen S011 exact history engine;
it is reproduction, not independent mathematical review. Independent review
must inspect the theorem, regenerate the source identities/invoice, and check
that all original inputs and actual chart availability are retained. No new
complete kappa is claimed.
