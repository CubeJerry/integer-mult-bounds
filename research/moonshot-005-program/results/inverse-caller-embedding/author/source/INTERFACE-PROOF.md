## 3. Stage dependence and failure of the inherited body commutation

These A_s are generally different operators on a common ambient array. For a nonzero R_hj, take a head-complement basis vector b_(h,k) with q_j·b_(h,k)=1. Such k exists because q_j is not in span(q_h). At the Fourier frequency z=e_(s,k), the off-diagonal monomial routes send z to P_(s,j)P_(s,h)z. The failure is scoped to these literal retained port adapters. Different coordinated adapters or a different chronological caller are not excluded. In particular a failure of A_s^4=I alone would not settle all possible two-sided phase normalizations.

## 4. Positive reduction to one stage-independent paid bank service

Define the ordinary diagonal operator

\[
\lambda=\lambda_+(Q^2)=((1+i)I+(1-i)Q^2)/2,
\qquad S_Q=\lambda Q^{-1}=Q\lambda^{-1}.
\]

Since Q^4=I, lambda²=Q² and S_Q²=I. Both lambda and its inverse are fixed combinations of identity and the whole-field translation Q². They act on the total width-V field, outside a factorwise tensor product. The same S_Q commutes with every fresh D_s.

Set

\[
\Lambda_s=M_s^{-1}\lambda R M_s,\qquad
T_s=D_s\Lambda_sD_s^{-1}. \tag{5}
\]

Lambda_s and its inverse are actual ordinary bank circuits: per-port monomial maps, a fixed scalar matrix R, and the lambda translations. Their complete array passes and scalar/tape expenses remain charged. This is an explicit physical bank operation on coindexed arrays, not a free read of a departed native chart value.

The useful identities do **not** require [Lambda_s,A_s]=0:

\[
\Lambda_sA_s^{-1}=S_Q,\qquad A_s\Lambda_s^{-1}=S_Q. \tag{6}
\]

Preapply Lambda_s^-1 to Y and V, perform the actual native(4), and postapply T_s separately to the same Y and V output banks. Then

\[
X'=D_sX,\quad Y'=D_s(Y+S_QX),\quad
U'=D_s(U-S_QV),\quad V'=D_sV. \tag{7}
\]

For example, T_s[D_s Lambda_s^-1 Y+D_s A_s^-1 X]=D_s[Y+Lambda_s A_s^-1 X]. The reverse shear is already D_s[U−A_s Lambda_s^-1 V], and T_s restores the independent V baseline. Thus both planes use the original common S_Q, with the full fresh D_s in its correct place. Ordinary signs/subtractions turn(7) into the exact rank-one root gates in the established short caller. The existing four normalized Q queries and the original productive head targets remain. No R-twisted target is credited.

The paid service simplifies further. From O_s B M_s=D_s,

\[
T_s=O_s B\lambda R B^{-1}O_s^{-1}
=O_s\lambda\,\underbrace{Q^{-1}RQ}_{\mathcal N}\,O_s^{-1}. \tag{8}
\]

The second equality uses B=H Q^-1 and the fact that the common H commutes with R,Q and lambda. Consequently the single remaining nonordinary operation is

\[
\boxed{\mathcal N=Q^{-1}R_0Q\quad\text{on112 arbitrary coindexed arrays}.} \tag{9}
\]

It is independent of the stage and satisfies N²=I. Its exact blocks are N_hj=R_hj C_(q_h)^(-tensor V) C_(q_j)^(tensor V). Each caller stage needs two independent applications, one on Y and one on V. A joint implementation may exploit its circuit structure, but may not assume those two arbitrary inputs equal or zero. All O/lambda factors and boundary placements in(8) are explicit ordinary operations. Native chart access is not being assumed free: the new service must actually compute(9) on its entering physical arrays and preserve all required other public/helper outputs.

This is a sufficient interface reduction to an explicit smaller construction. It does not prove that N has a cheap implementation. A different caller or joint acquisition history can avoid this particular modular repair and is outside the reduction's necessity claim.

## 6. The remaining service cannot be declared ordinary

For a nonzero R_hj, the labels q_h,q_j are independent and orthogonal. On one column, C_(q_h)^-1 C_(q_j) has four nonzero translations0,q_h,q_j,q_h+q_j, each of magnitude1/2. Across V columns, the corresponding block of N has exactly4^V nonzero entries in every row. Its coefficient R_hj contributes magnitude1/2 without changing support. The four input-port blocks are disjoint, so every full row of N has exactly4^(V+1) entries, each of magnitude2^(-V-1). This also verifies its unitary row normalization.

In the retained ordinary alphabet, a coordinate monomial has one nonzero per matrix row; a scalar ADD can at most double the current number of monomial paths. A fixed finite circuit with L such additions, arbitrary coordinate permutations/phases, finite zero-owned workspace and permitted retirement therefore has at most2^L entries in any output row. All paths from distinct original input coordinates are counted before any cancellation. To realize N exactly would require L>=2V+2. Thus no V-independent ordinary template realizes N, even with arbitrary fixed monomials and scalar coefficients. Under the retained full-array-pass tariff, an attempted L=Omega(V) implementation is not a negligible ordinary correction at a positive saving.

This excludes only declaring N to be a fixed ordinary normalizer. It is **not** a Gaussian moment lower bound for general paid circuits. A new shared Gaussian service, nonunitary coding/feedback architecture, fused primitive or changed representation can lie outside this observation. Its complete cost must be established.

The same point can be seen locally in T_s. A nonzero block's route product replaces one odd fresh direction u by the orthogonal head direction q_h. Consequently D_s P_(s,h)P_(s,j)D_s^-1 has Gaussian rank two, up to actual ordinary monomials and translations. `ROUTES.json` checks rank two for all448 blocks. If T_s had a fixed ordinary circuit, equation(8) and the ordinary inverse outside factors would give one for N, contradicting the preceding bound.
