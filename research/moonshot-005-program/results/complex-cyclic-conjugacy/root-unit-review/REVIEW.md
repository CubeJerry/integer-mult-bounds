# Independent review of the cyclic quarter-phase unit interface

**Accepted in its declared fixed finite ordinary-primitive interface.** The
four-sector unit criterion, explicit inverse, direct phase control and
width-independent amplification claims follow from the written argument.
This supplies an ordinary interstage family; it does not supply an independent
(4,2,1) endpoint, a profitable caller or a new complete kappa.

For a fixed admitted monomial T, write T e_x=tau(x)e_(pi(x)). Conjugation gives
T^-1 D_b T e_x=i^(b(pi(x)))e_x; the two phases tau cancel exactly. Thus h is
diagonal and its tensor power has phase given by the sum of a fixed Boolean
table over the head columns. No physical T or inverse-T service is needed to
execute a matrix controlled by this phase. This simplification does not apply
to the separate active clock S^-1 D_b, whose permutation and invoice remain.

The four orthogonal spectral projectors of h_V split the three retained banks
into q_s actions. Each sector occurs for every V>=3, since every Boolean count
0,1,2,3 is available. Consequently all-width, eventually-all-width and four
scalar-unit invertibility are equivalent. Fourier interpolation of the four
inverses gives the inverse identity for every positive width, including widths
with absent sectors. This argument is a universal spectral proof, not an
inference from one-column inversion. With residue-dependent coefficients,
each of the four width residues must independently pass its four sector tests.

The direct implementation scans addresses and chooses one of four fixed
three-by-three scalar recipes. A radix-16 odometer changes O(N+V) head digits
over N entries and retains O(V) control storage. This is a combinatorial
control bound in addition to the retained scalar/tape/layout costs; it does
not grant random access or a free masked field operation. At most six weighted
row additions, three scales and three swaps reduce any three-by-three unit.
Each weighted addition needs a source scale, an unweighted ADD and an inverse
source scale. The resulting conservative four-sector invoice is at most
24 conditional unweighted ADD passes, 60 SCALE passes and 12 SWAP passes in
each direction, plus the control and actual scalar primitive costs. The
earlier draft's 12-SCALE count was corrected before admission. No owned full
field is introduced or erased by the in-place invertible recipe.

Orthogonal sector decomposition gives norm at most max_s ||q_s||, and the same
formula for the inverse. Prefix bounds are finite products of norms of the
actual elementary matrices. All coefficients are fixed before width varies;
precision, fixed-scalar implementation and the global compiler/tape foundations
remain explicit. The theorem does not assign them zero cost.

The independent checker imports no author arithmetic. SymPy Gaussian-rational
matrix inversion and convolution verify both inverse products, and explicit
elementary matrices verify the source-restoring expansion of every weighted
ADD. Literal 16-address S,L4,R4 tables verify the four different Boolean
controls and detect the duplicate R4 D_b R4 proposal. All addresses through
width three and exact spectral counts through width seven are finite controls.
A singular fourth sector passes widths one and two but fails width three;
the tensor-of-a-sum substitution disagrees on every width-two column. These
controls support, and are distinguished from, the preceding universal proof.
