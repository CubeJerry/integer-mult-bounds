# Every same-source funded retarget family leaves a global export deficit

**Exact source-specific theorem, pending independent review. No new κ.**

The chart-282 repair cannot be funded solely by redirecting any subset of the 181 eligible chart-3 transfers, even when all of their payloads, every other ordinary coefficient, and the entire source chronology may be redesigned. A four-space cut contains **912 independent original fields**, has no required public output, and has only **911 outgoing whole-field movement occurrences** after the proposed compensated surgery. Every eligible funded redirection preserves that count. Thus at least one additional export from this set is necessary.

This extends the earlier one-donor fixed-clock cuts to **every subset simultaneously**, with arbitrary recoding and retiming of the entire paid source. It is an information-capacity statement about the route interface, not a claim that one extra export suffices to restore all source targets. Funding an extra channel from a move that did not already export this source information, or changing other routes, escapes this particular obstruction and must be evaluated separately.

## 3. Universal communication argument

Fix any characteristic-zero coefficient field and any positive common whole-field width V. Set all original input fields born in T to zero, and leave the 912 fields born in S arbitrary and independent. Initially T contains no information about these 912V coordinates. Local operations, arbitrary coordinate mixing, local copies, zero initializations and erasure do not enlarge the span of input information known in T. An S-to-T movement of one width-V field can enlarge it by at most V. Communication in T, feedback to S and later replies do not invalidate the count; each reply that brings additional information is another S-to-T movement.

Every required output lies in T. Its dependence on the selected original inputs has rank 912V. The redesigns above provide only 911 old or redirected export occurrences, hence at most 911V dimensions. This contradicts exact implementation of W. The contradiction persists under arbitrary retiming and arbitrary recoding of **all** paid source movements, including those outside the changed chart-282 cone.

At least one further width-V export must therefore be supplied. It may be a newly paid crossing or a new source/destination assignment that turns a formerly nonexporting old occurrence into an S-to-T channel. Redirecting another already exporting chart-3 donor cannot perform this role. An independently retained field containing selected input information outside S would itself require prior export and cannot be granted as a free initial condition.

The argument does not claim that one such export solves all restoration duties. The previous one-donor certificates retain their stronger local or fixed-clock information about particular terminals. Those restrictions are unnecessary for the present global rank loss.

## 4. Positive controls, price scope, and remaining question

The unmodified source has 912 export occurrences and implements the invertible target. The archived five-channel control adds a real chart3-to-chart282 occurrence, restoring capacity 912; that source has the separately proved exact public target. Both are checked as positive controls. The actual four-channel word and every simultaneous same-source funded family remain at 911.

No new movement credit is earned by this theorem. A newly paid positive-grade whole-field export costs at least one moment unit, but grades and collateral costs of a changed donor family can differ. In particular, this result does not attach the one-extra-unit price to an arbitrary new high-grade discount without recomputing the full invoice. The precise new obligation is to create an additional export of the 912-dimensional source bank, beyond the crossings already funded in the retained route list.

A useful constructive follow-up must therefore change a nonexporting paid route, alter the original compensated surgery, or supply and price a genuinely new crossing. Any such construction must still restore all 1,289 independent public targets, including the old donors' duties. The current result rules out enlarging the eligible same-source funding roster as the sole repair.
