# Late imported payloads cannot preserve these three codes

## Exact family

Start with a pinned word in RESULT.json. Preserve its source acquisition, charts, routes and later linear updates. Immediately before the last M1 descent of each of P3, P4 and P5, add a payload depending only on the actual imported records X8, X9 and X36. This changes both the subsequent partial retention and terminal common code.

We generously grant every induced historical observation at its actual chart, every geometric raw local, arbitrary local decoding and causal feedback of correctly decoded native raw points. Feedback carries raw values, without additional coded messages or control information from outside receivers. The old targets must remain decodable; threatened old targets are not granted by an unconditional oracle after their supporting code changes.

The theorem excludes source-wide column substitutions, additional common returns, different lower deliveries and newly transmitted coded information.

## Exact projected witnesses

For each imported coordinate q choose these three old demands, in P3/P4/P5 order:

| q | d3 | d4 | d5 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 43 | 29 | 45 |
| 9 | 41 | 42 | 48 |
| 36 | 43 | 29 | 48 |

Vary only inputs in J={d3,d4,d5,q}, holding all other inputs fixed. The checker propagates three formal payload tags through every actual ADD and MOVE. Every historical observation visible at any of the four native receivers in J has projected old row

\[
\sum_{k=3}^5 t_k X_{d_k},
\]

where its formal payload tag is t. Thus no untagged observation varies on J, and every tagged observation is a combination of just three channels

\[
Z_k=X_{d_k}+U_k,\qquad k=3,4,5.
\]

All three channels actually occur at the common chart. The four native roots in each witness are pairwise nonorthogonal. All geometric raw locals are already present in the initial actual record history. M1 is invisible to every witness receiver, so extra intermediate computations confined to M1 add no observation there.

These are exact row-by-row hypotheses, not a whole-bank coverage calculation. The checker compares its zero-payload history with the independent full 222-column replay and then checks every projected observation for every listed word.

## Scalar and block linear conclusion

Work over any characteristic-two field with whole-record width V. Allow arbitrary V-by-V coefficient matrices in the three payloads. Freeze two imported inputs and change X_q by v. If the change in payload k is A_kq v, change the three old demands by -A_kq v as well. Each Z_k is unchanged. Therefore every observation at all four witness receivers is unchanged.

If any A_kq is nonzero, choose v making at least one old demand change. Those old outputs cannot be recovered on both indistinguishable inputs. Targets outside J have fixed values in this comparison; granting all of them globally in advance supplies no distinguishing information. Native raw-point feedback inside J is determined by the indistinguishable observations and cannot repair the collision. Even arbitrary cooperation among the four witness receivers does not help, since their pooled observations remain functions of the same three channels.

Consequently every A_kq is zero. Applying the three witness rows to q=8,9,36 forces every late linear injection coefficient to vanish. This holds for arbitrary characteristic-two extension fields and positive block width. It is not inferred from a finite binary search.

## Source-only nonlinear payload extension

The same argument applies to arbitrary payload functions U_k=f_k(X8,X9,X36), provided their new computation remains confined to the invisible source chart and only the stated payload additions travel on the fixed routes. Fix the other two imported inputs arbitrarily and compare any two values of X_q. If a payload changes, compensate the three old demands by those payload differences. All observations remain unchanged while an old demand changes.

Therefore every f_k is constant in each argument, hence constant on the full input product. Zero-preserving functions must vanish. Arbitrary nonlinear local decoding cannot distinguish the colliding inputs. This argument uses no finite-field counting assumption.

## Boundary and interpretation

A new common raw X_q observation immediately violates the zero-projection premise. An unconditional initial grant of a threatened old demand also invalidates it. Changing all matching upper-cache and Hub uses alters the projected equations and is outside this family. The actual source-wide lower-code substitutions and paid common-return constructions are therefore separate candidates.

The earlier exhaustive 512-choice GF(2) screen found only zero injections preserving all 41 outputs, and the same held for the 42-output retention variant. The present witness theorem explains that result and extends its coefficient and block scope. It gives no movement-price lower bound or complete-supplier impossibility.
