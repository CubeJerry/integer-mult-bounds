# Global rigidity for nonlinear sum-separable native outputs

Arbitrary normalized single-donor functions in a sum over a finite abelian alphabet A become additive coordinate transports when the image has size less than 3|A|⁹/2 and the 221 tree functions are bijections. The alphabet must be elementary abelian of exponent two. The same conclusion holds with at most 54 unspecified nonbijective proper functions. ## Sharp image threshold

A three-donor argument raises the strict cutoff to 2|A|⁹. A native rank-one transvection attains equality in the cutoff while preserving every proper bijection for binary alphabets of dimension at least two. The strengthened bound also holds under the 54-unspecified-defect hypothesis. The proof checks 2,965 common charts and all 12,481 four-cell repairs.
