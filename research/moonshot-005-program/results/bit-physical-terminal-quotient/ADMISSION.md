# A return obstruction under arbitrary linear terminal retuning

With the RS source, H, D and either K variant fixed, the one-way return has rank at least 41,880 even allowing new final linear maps and singular proper foreign blocks. An eight-pivot argument at three receivers and six donors uses rank 1,035 of the early restriction and 2,430 of the stacked restriction. The independent simulation reconstructed all 36 local matrices and the 14,515,200 entries in the two variants.
