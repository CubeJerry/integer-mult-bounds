# The fixed-prefix facet gate survives arbitrary future public turns

**Author extension; independent review requested. No new kappa.** This removes
the six-future-Hub-mode and future public-monotonicity premises from the broad
fixed-prefix gate. The sharper six-mode bounds in `PROOF.md` remain valid under
their stronger hypotheses. The fixed already executed prefix is still a real
premise; this theorem does not constrain a redesigned acquisition prefix.

## Theorem

Fix t=199/200 and one of the exactly emitted coalesced prefixes in this package.
It has all original X at Hub, every public Y at ground or at its own completed
native facet, and all nonanchor native-facet traffic concentrated at F0.
Let Q0 be its closed-partial moment: executed prefix plus the direct rank-seven
ascent of every unfinished ground Y, with no further owned movement.

Permit an arbitrary completing continuation in the admitted native linear
model. Public as well as owned records may turn, revisit charts, exchange
cocharted values and carry arbitrary unknown-Y terms. Owned records may be
born and retired at proper charts. Every original X must end unchanged at Hub
and every Y must end with y_i+x_i at its specified facet. Every operation,
whole-record lifetime, storage, erasure, source, precision and compiler cost
remains charged. Track actual public/owned physical identities as in the
all-route normalization; relabelling cannot transfer a value or exchange a
charged route family for free.

Let M count future positive owned MOVE occurrences and D future downward
public MOVE occurrences. Write

    c=1+8^t-7^t,
    R=B_infinity(t)-Q0.

Strict admission by the pinned complete caller implies

    M+cD < R,                                         (1)
    sum_i ell_i(A) <= 2+M+2D < 2+2R/c,               (2)

for an actual normalized completed native fitting A, with the unchanged
foreign-support charge

    ell_i(A)=1[foreign_support(A_i)>36]
             +1[foreign_support(A_i)>52].

The resulting exact integer bounds are:

| Fixed prefix | R, favorable limiting room | M+2D at most | Full fitting charge at most |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Published canonical pivot | 58.2317787389335... | 58 | 60 |
| Pivot plus28 halfclass banks | 56.0012476685142... | 56 | 58 |
| Halfclass banks plus two proper triples | 49.9034767282225... | 50 | 52 |
| Three proper pairs, plain | 52.1050551035818... | 52 | 54 |
| Three proper pairs, joined | 50.1050551035818... | 50 | 52 |

The exact rational interval endpoints are recorded in
`ALL-ROUTE-RESULT.json`; displayed decimals are informational. No Hub packet
count, ground-ending assumption, fixed history space, tensor decomposition or
future monotonicity is used in this extension.

## Proof of the future movement tariff

Use the admitted reflected potentials

    phi_Y(r)=r^t,
    phi_X(r)=8^t-(8-r)^t.

Each upward public move costs at least its potential increase, and each
downward public move costs at least its potential increase plus c. The proof
by subadditivity and concavity is in the pinned all-route theorem; it is local
to each MOVE and can therefore be restarted at the exact handoff.

All original X start this continuation at Hub and finish at Hub, so their net
potential change is zero. A Y already completed at its own facet also has net
rank-potential change zero. Each remaining ground Y has net change7^t, exactly
the direct closing move already included in Q0. Thus every future public route
costs at least its portion of Q0 plus cD. Every future positive owned move
costs at least1, without any endpoint assumption. The complete moment is at
least `Q0+M+cD`.

For every finite fixed even caller multiplicity, `B_m<B_infinity`. The strict
condition `Q_complete<B_m` therefore gives (1). Since `1<c<2`,

    M+cD >= (c/2)*(M+2D).

This proves the strict numerical inequality in (2) after the occurrence count
below. No future operation was repriced by ignoring its reverse or erasure.

## Why the continuation receives no fresh111-arrival allowance

The earlier universal bound `111+M_owned+2D_public` grants one initial upward
native-facet arrival to each original X. In the fixed prefix those opportunities
have already been used or bypassed: every original X is now at Hub. For an X
to make a new upward arrival at any rank-seven facet, it must first descend
to rank at most6. Match each upward facet arrival to a distinct preceding
downward MOVE interval. Thus future X upward facet arrivals number at most
their future descents.

For an unfinished Y starting at ground, choose its first own-facet upward
arrival as anchor if one exists. The inherited interval injection shows that
all other upward native-facet arrivals number at most its future descents,
including the case where it ends at its own facet by descending from Hub.
If Y already starts at its own completed facet, its anchor was in the prefix.
Every new upward facet arrival then requires a preceding descent from its
initial rank-seven location, or from a subsequent higher location, to rank at
most6. Again there is an injection into its future downward moves.

Future public downward facet departures are at most D. Future owned upward
facet arrivals and downward facet departures together are at most M, because
one positive nested MOVE cannot be both or join distinct facets. Hence future
nonanchor facet traffic is at most `M+2D`.

The exact coalesced prefix census is57 nonanchor incidences, all at F0. The
local fitting inequality has `ell_0<=2`, so its entire historical contribution
is at most2. All other facets have zero historical nonanchor traffic. Combining
the local occurrence inequalities proves

    sum ell_i(A) <= 2+M+2D.

This count includes public turns that complete targets at Hub or relay old
histories through another facet. It imposes no static-history assumption.

## Exactness, controls and interpretation

`check_extension.py` verifies every source receipt's pin, confirms the exact
coalesced profiles and historical facet concentration, then uses the pinned
integer-root arithmetic to enclose R, c and2R/c. The integer results do not
depend on floating-point rounding. The best integer value of M+2D permitted
by the limiting weighted count is also checked; its witnessing counts are
arithmetic controls, not actual source constructions.

The factor2 on D is necessary at the occurrence level. A public Y that has
already reached F_a may go down to the rank-six intersection of F_a and F_b,
up to F_b, down to that intersection again, and up to F_a. Its two downward
moves create four nonanchor facet incidences. The checker verifies an actual
native pair of distinct facets for this control. Charging only D would fail.

The fixed-prefix requirement must not be silently weakened. In particular,
this proof does not permit changing which original X visits which native facet
before Hub, deleting already executed mandatory accesses, or merging moves
back through an immutable source component. The published pivot has a genuine
read/write stop at its completed F0 receiver. The new partial-source cases
are applications to their exact emitted handoffs and require their separate
source reviews.

The result makes distributed initial acquisition a concrete architectural
issue. The published pivot's56 original-X facet reads are concentrated at one
receiver, while any completed fitting for every possible continuation of that
fixed source must have total charge at most60. A fit with charge192 cannot
be repaired merely by spending the unused universal arrival allowance. A
genuinely sparse changed-history fitting, or a deliberately redesigned and
fully repriced acquisition prefix, remains open.
