# A fixed retained-pivot continuation has a much smaller facet budget

**Author theorem, independent review requested. No new kappa.** This is a
source-specific necessary condition for a declared continuation interface. It
does not exclude arbitrary future BIT constructions, and it does not turn a
partial source into a supplier. Its mathematical normalization is inherited
from `bit-all-route-rank-gap/author/PROOF.md`, Sections 2--3.

## 1. The fixed interface and the resulting restriction

Fix `t=199/200`. Start with an emitted coalesced retained-pivot prefix checked
by `verify.py`. In particular, all original X records are unchanged at Hub;
each public Y is at ground or has already finished at its own native facet;
every retained value and its physical chart is fixed. A continuation must
satisfy the following additional premises.

1. The already executed prefix is unchanged. Original X remain at Hub.
2. Public Y trajectories remain monotone upward and end at their own
   distinct native rank-seven facets. All 111 outputs must be correct for
   arbitrary independent X and Y over any characteristic-two field and any
   positive whole-record width.
3. There are six specified, disjoint owned-trajectory intervals from Hub to
   ground. They may have arbitrary legal turns and arbitrary cocharted
   linear operations. Different intervals may use different records; reuse
   is also allowed if the intervening return is charged. All remaining owned
   movements, including movements of already retained records, are extra.
4. The complete source uses the pinned complete paired caller and its strict
   movement admission. All ordinary, lifetime, storage, compiler, precision,
   recursive, source-access and foundational obligations remain in force.

Write `Q0` for the movement moment of the checked closed partial source. This
includes the executed prefix and the direct final ascent of every unfinished
ground Y; it contains no extra owned closing move. If `E` is the number of
future native-facet owned upward arrivals plus owned downward departures,
then

    Q_complete >= Q0 + 6*7^t + E.                       (1)

Consequently strict complete-caller admission implies

    E < B_infinity(t) - Q0 - 6*7^t.                    (2)

The negative correction at every finite even caller multiplicity is omitted
only in the favorable direction. For the joined proper-pair source, the right
side is strictly between 8 and 9. Hence `E<=8`. Every completing source under
this interface has an actual normalized native fitting A satisfying

    sum_i (1[foreign_support(A_i)>36]
           +1[foreign_support(A_i)>52]) <= 10.         (3)

For the already-published retained canonical pivot, without either new bank
extension, the corresponding caps are `E<=16` and fitting charge at most18.
For the plain proper-pair source they are `E<=10` and fitting charge at most12.
These are bounds on the fitting produced by a
complete execution, not on an arbitrarily chosen fitting used in an optimistic
screen. A scalar fitting of charge192 therefore does not come close to this
particular continuation's actual geometric budget.

## 2. A rank potential paying for actual facet incidences

For ranks r in `{0,...,8}`, define

    phi(0)=0;
    phi(r)=r^t                 for 1<=r<=5;
    phi(6)=phi(7)=7^t-1;
    phi(8)=7^t.

For a positive rank move a to b let

    e(a,b)=1 if a=7>b or a<7=b, and 0 otherwise.

This marks every rank-seven upward arrival and downward departure, including
ones at nonnative rank-seven charts. For every pair of distinct ranks,

    |a-b|^t >= phi(a)-phi(b)+e(a,b).                   (4)

Here is a direct proof. The potential is nondecreasing at the specified t.
The only nontrivial adjacent check is `7^t-1 >= 5^t`; the exact rational-root
checker verifies this with a positive margin. For upward moves, (4) follows
from monotonicity if e=0. If e=1 its right side is at most1, and the move costs
at least1. For downward moves with a<=5, subadditivity gives the claim. For
a=6 and b<=5, use

    7^t <= b^t + (6-b)^t + 1.

For a=7 and b<=5, use `7^t <= b^t+(7-b)^t`; the `-1` in phi(7) cancels the
event charge. For a=7,b=6 the inequality is equality. For a=8 and b<=5, use
`7^t <= b^t+(7-b)^t <= b^t+(8-b)^t`. For a=8,b in {6,7}, the required
potential drop is1 and the move costs at least1. These cases exhaust all
ordered pairs. `verify.py` additionally checks all72 inequalities with exact
integer-root enclosures; identically zero cases are simplified symbolically.

Summing (4) on one Hub-to-ground interval gives cost at least `7^t` plus its
rank-seven event count. Actual native-facet events form a subset of these.
Every positive MOVE outside the six intervals costs at least1 and can supply
at most one native-facet event. Distinct rank-seven facets cannot be joined by
a positive nested move. Thus all extra owned movement costs at least
`6*7^t+E`. Public Y final motion costs at least its direct ascent already in
Q0 by subadditivity; extra stops cannot reduce that price. This proves (1).

The bound is not pricing every facet stop at one extra unit relative to an
eight-rank direct transfer. A direct Hub-to-ground transfer costs `8^t`; a
real stop and read at a native facet forces two moves costing `1+7^t`, whose
extra cost is the small but strictly positive `1+7^t-8^t`. The potential's
baseline is `7^t`, so this inexpensive first event is counted correctly.
The rank route `[8,7,6,7,0]` has three events and attains equality in (4).

## 3. Exact native facets in the executed prefix

An unread, unwritten monotone run of one record may be replaced by its one
nested endpoint move. The emitted words implement this replacement, rather
than merely pricing an unimplemented histogram. Pending moves are flushed
before either operand of an ADD is used, before CLEAR, and before a direction
change. Other records' operations commute with postponing this untouched
record's move. Exact rational chart inclusion and all222 independent value
columns are replayed on both the literal and coalesced words.

Every already used nonanchor native-facet event in these prefixes occurs at
F0: 56 original X upward arrivals supporting the pivot's actual local reads,
and one owned downward departure carrying its recoded value. The own-facet
arrival of every completed Y is its uncharged anchor. The other two initial
Hub modes have direct rank-eight moves after coalescing; their unread
intermediate subdivision charts are not additional facet observations. The
proper pair packets, line snapshots and paid rank-two join use only lower
charts. Geometrically equal chart aliases are identified by exact rational
row reduction. The event census is therefore

    t_0(prefix)=57;   t_i(prefix)=0 for i!=0.            (5)

This concentration matters. The admitted all-route normalization gives for
every native facet

    ell_i(A) <= t_i,

where ell_i has maximum2, and t_i counts nonanchor upward arrivals and
downward departures at that facet. Under the declared continuation, original
X supply no new event. A monotone Y can reach only its own rank-seven facet,
whose first arrival is its anchor. Future owned records supply E events.
Hence

    sum_i ell_i(A) <= min(2,57) + E = 2+E.             (6)

This sharpens the universal but deliberately relaxed `111+M_o+2D_p` bound
by using the actual fixed source. It does not alter the inherited definition
of fitting support or its occurrence accounting.

## 4. Complete caller and exact arithmetic

The original caller remains

    B_m(t)=B_infinity(t)+(T2(t)-P2(t))/(36*(m-1)),

for fixed even m, with the exact native source and target profiles pinned in
the inherited checker. `T2(t)-P2(t)<0` at t=199/200. We require the strict
complete-source inequality `Q_complete<B_m`, not merely a positive local
margin. All exponent199/200 evaluations use outward200th-root intervals of
integers at scale10^40. The machine receipts retain rational endpoints as
well as human-readable decimals.

The previously granted six direct rank-eight modes had room L. Equivalently
the event bound is

    E < L + 6*(8^t-7^t).

The exact checked applications are:

## 5. Scope, escape mechanisms, and provenance

The six Hub-to-ground intervals are a stated continuation interface, not a
consequence of the six-dimensional receiver-union cut alone. A source that
delivers information only to proper charts, changes the prefix, moves original
X downward, or permits public Y turns needs a new tariff. No impossibility is
claimed for those mechanisms. Nor does (3) rule out a genuinely new very sparse
normalized fitting created by changing histories below the native facets.

The meaningful next construction task is to produce such sparse actual
histories, or change the interface and reprice it. Further tuning of a
canonical-plus-coordinate static fitting from charge198 to192 does not address
the binding source-specific condition.
