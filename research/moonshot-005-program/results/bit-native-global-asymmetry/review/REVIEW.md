## Mathematical checks

For two rank-p quotient maps with kernels K,K', projections E,F onto the same
actual p-dimensional lift space L have

    ker(E-F)=L direct_sum(K intersect K').

This gives rank(E-F)=rank([f;f'])-p and proves that the discrepancy rank is a
property of the original source pair. It is not a receiver-dependent decoder
quantity. The equal-image assumption is essential. Characteristic two makes
E+F the required square-zero discrepancy in an odd witness.

Summing donor decomposition identities over an odd witness gives I as the sum
of its discrepancies. Removing any one leaves an invertible I+N. Therefore
the total discrepancy rank R is at least16p plus the largest individual rank.
This proves both the17-nonzero-term count and
R>=16p m/(m-1) for m paired planes. These inequalities hold at every positive
block width in the stated quotient model; the proof is not extrapolated from
the finite controls.

The global dual arguments correctly control overlap. Every global plane or
pair flag has total load at most the common denominator, while its indicator
or discrepancy rank is nonnegative. Thus weighted witness inequalities bound
the global sums once, without adding the same source discrepancy several
times. The certificates are feasible duals, not an optimality assertion.

At receiver80, equality of kernels is an equivalence relation. A three-donor
plane therefore has0,2 or3 unequal pairs. The independently reconstructed
pair multiplicities8 and16 give exactly16q+8t for the32-witness discrepancy
count. This proves2q+t>=68, q>=23 and t>=22 when q=23. Granting all16 heavy

## Scope and remaining questions

The quotient and common-lift premises rely on exact saturation without a new
information channel. A rank-r ground repair can change those premises. A
replacement return bank can also change the proper target away from O, so
the odd-witness projection identities need not hold. Accordingly none of71,
152 or51657 is a lower bound on the repair-rank or replacement-rank excess.
The package correctly records these exclusions.

The result rules out a small collection of common-kernel-plane patches in
the original global gluing problem. It does not prove that the necessary
asymmetry can be realized, that the early basis inequalities hold, or that
the same source quotients admit the paid middle/terminal transport. Those
remain construction obligations.
