# A complete finite cut obstruction for four equal-price deletion profiles

## Statement and scope

Use the same genuine paid PR7833 compensation of the pinned PR7830 native
word as in the [S032 seven-deletion closure](../complex-source-hub-s032/ADMISSION.md).
For one choice

\[
e\in\{43,89777,96404,96513\},
\]

delete

\[
D_e=\{86,97232,97241,97320,97323,99064,e\}.
\]

Keep every remaining old route endpoint, its whole-field capacity, and the
original independent input/output roles and target. All surviving local
linear forms, retained histories and legal causal timing may change. Each
message has the same arbitrary positive finite field width V over a
characteristic-zero field. New messages obey the retained nested-chart grade
tariff. This is a four-member source-specific family, not every possible
seven-deletion plan or every complex supplier.

**Theorem.** None of these four profiles has a repair within the retained
complete κ=.005 allowance. Each successful repair costs at least
1+2^(199/200), exceeding that allowance by more than0.868065818837735 native
moment units.

The e=89777 case was proved in the preceding package through the independently
admitted120-pair reduction and one new four-demand cut. The present package
adds the other three grade-four alternatives and gives a direct finite cut
cover for all four, without relying on the earlier120-pair enumeration.

## 1. The price is exactly unchanged

Actual route grading gives

    grade(43)=grade(89777)=grade(96404)=grade(96513)=4.

Every D_e consequently deletes the same profile: one grade-three occurrence,
five grade-four occurrences and one grade-five occurrence. The underlying
paid compensation and complete caller are unchanged. Hence the allowance for
added messages is the same source-bound quantity A as in the prior exact
price audit:

\[
2.125014706817<A<2.125014706818.
\]

The checker authenticates the original rational interval endpoints and checks
the displayed strict upper bound. Ordinary recoding and any additional costs
have not been declared free in a supplier; the graph obstruction is obtained
even when they are granted without charge.

## 2. Two messages and existing-bank endpoints are necessary

For each e, the third explicit initial cut in its COMPACT-CUTS.json contains
six original input axes and excludes their six paired outside terminal slots.
Its target minor has exact rank six, while exactly four old paid exports
survive D_e. This is verified anew from all original route occurrences for
each profile. Therefore any repair needs at least two outgoing whole fields.

The cut for e=96404 must be checked after the changed source closure. Looking
only at the earlier, larger fixed cut gives a weaker boundary; the retained
explicit certificate has rank six and capacity four. No claim about possible
new banks is based on that weaker preliminary observation.

Since three positive-grade messages cost at least three, only two messages
could fit A. A grade-two message together with a grade-one message costs at
least1+2^(199/200)>A. Thus both affordable added messages have grade one, and
both must leave the new six-demand cut.

A genuinely new bank has no old message incidence and no original input or
output role. If it is incident to only one new message, put it on the same
side as the other endpoint; that message is not outgoing. If both new messages
meet the bank, two incoming or two outgoing messages can be made
non-outgoing by placing the bank on the appropriate side. For a two-edge
chain through it, at most one edge is outgoing for either side choice.
Components with two new banks are no better. Consequently a cut extension
exists with at most one added outgoing message, contradicting the required
rank-six demand behind four old exports. Equal-span aliases already belong to
one physical bank and are identified exactly.

All affordable candidate endpoints therefore lie in the1,102 old physical
banks. Exact binary-span inclusion and dimension difference one enumerate all
4,802 directed grade-one possibilities. Repeated endpoint pairs, including
parallel copies of an existing message, are allowed in the enumeration.

## 3. Explicit finite cut covers

Each profile has seven initial cut certificates. Every certificate specifies
a physical-bank bitmask, original input axes, original output slots, a
nonsingular target minor, and its complete surviving old boundary. The bitmask
indexes the lexicographically sorted distinct binary spans of the pinned
source frames, so every physical alias is included automatically.

The third cut forces both added messages to be outgoing. Enumerate unordered
pairs, with repetition, from those outgoing grade-one possibilities. Any pair
violating an initial cut is excluded immediately by a rank/capacity inequality.
The remaining finite pairs are covered by explicit additional cuts:

| Deleted grade-four alternative e | Grade-one routes leaving the six-demand cut | Pairs passing all seven initial cuts | Additional explicit cut certificates | Survivors |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 43 | 432 | 13,944 | 279 | 0 |

For every listed cut, the checker reconstructs its exact target minor and
**enumerates every surviving old route** to obtain the full outgoing capacity.
For each added pair it then adds zero, one or two according to their actual
outgoing incidences, retaining multiplicity. At least one verified cut has
required rank larger than old-plus-new capacity for every remaining pair.
Only that individual inequality is used; ranks of overlapping cuts are not
added.

The whole-field implication is uniform: tensor the nonsingular scalar minor
with I_V. Its required outside rank is rV; a cut with c outgoing messages can
carry at most cV independent coordinates. If c<r, no permitted local linear
recoding, retained history or causal retiming on that graph can implement the
original target.

## 4. What the checker does and does not trust

The proof replay `check.py` reads only literal source data and the compact
explicit certificates. It does not execute the search, reclosure or greedy
compaction algorithms. It recomputes all geometry, actual route grades, target
minors, cut boundaries and endpoint-pair coverage directly. The earlier
120-pair screen is not used by this replay. Greedy cover selection is not
claimed optimal; the supplied finite cover is sufficient.

The exploratory reclosure generator initially retained only boundary edges
that were outgoing before expansion. That could miss blocked edges becoming
outgoing afterwards. The generator was corrected to retain every blocked
edge, and every candidate cut was additionally checked against all actual
old routes. The first exploratory receipts are explicitly invalidated as
method evidence. The corrected counts happened to agree, but that agreement
is not the verification argument. See CORRECTION.md. The direct certificate
checker is structurally independent of the corrected closure calculation.

## 5. The same strict price deficit

Every successful repair needs two messages. The exhaustive cut cover excludes
two grade-one messages. Two messages of larger grades cost at least
1+2^(199/200); three or more cost at least three, a larger number. The exact
integer-power enclosure inherited and rechecked here therefore gives

\[
\mathrm{repair\ cost}-A>0.868065818837735.
\]

This is a lower bound, not an assertion that any excluded profile has a repair
at the lower bound. No completed multiplier, new κ, or foundational discharge
is claimed.
