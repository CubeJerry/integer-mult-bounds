# Exact concave cost of one-chart canonical acquisition

Status: proposed proof, awaiting independent review. No complete supplier or new kappa.

## Proposition and physical scope

Let F be any field. At an actual invertible source cut write the available coordinates as H direct-sum D, where D=direct-sum_a D_a is grouped by held physical charts. All held charts have the same dimension d, with 0<d<m; Hub has dimension m and ground dimension zero. Sources remain available and unchanged during acquisition. Let K be a c-dimensional target code with K intersect D={0}. Put P=projection_D(K), p=dim P, t=sum_a dim projection_D_a(P), Delta=t-p.

Before first ground arrival, each packet is formed at Hub and either sent directly to ground or reads at most one held chart; alternatively it is born at one held chart and sent to ground. Ground may recombine all arrivals. Arbitrarily many dependent packets and repeated reads are permitted. Their first-arrival span must contain K. No earlier source acquisition or other chart access is granted.

Let direct Hub movement cost A, Hub-to-held cost B and held-to-ground cost C satisfy 0<B<A<=B+C. Actual prices A=m^alpha, B=(m-d)^alpha, C=d^alpha satisfy this for 0<alpha<=1. The exact minimum movement is

    (c-p)A + pB + tC.

The previous quotient-complement construction attains it with c-p Hub-only principals, p one-chart principals and Delta proper-born helpers. This is the movement part of this declared acquisition interface; source assembly, reads, ordinary recombination, initialization, retirement, storage, precision and compiler costs remain separately paid.

## Lower bound with arbitrary redundancy

Delete zero-valued packets. Relax any Hub-born packet with zero Hub part to a proper-born packet, and replace a query with zero held part by a direct Hub arrival, using A<=B+C. These changes preserve values and weakly reduce cost. Let n be the remaining arrival count, q the count with nonzero Hub part, and l the direct Hub count.

Since K intersect D=0, projection_H(K) has dimension c, so q>=c. The held projection of the arrival span S is generated by chart-supported vectors. Containing P therefore requires every marginal projection_D_a(P), so n-l>=t. Also S intersect H contains K intersect H of dimension c-p. Rank-nullity gives dim S>=t+c-p=c+Delta, hence n>=c+Delta.

The relaxed movement cost equals C(n-l)+B(q-l)+Al = Cn+Bq+(A-B-C)l. Since A-B-C<=0 and l<=n-t, this is at least

    (A-B)n+Bq+(B+C-A)t
    >= (A-B)(c+t-p)+Bc+(B+C-A)t
     = (c-p)A+pB+tC.

Both coefficients A-B and B used in the second inequality are positive. Independence of packet values, a minimal owner count or a fixed assignment of queries was never assumed.

## Attainment and width

Choose Delta chart-supported vectors complementary to P in D'=direct-sum_a projection_D_a(P). Copy them into proper-born helpers. The other chart-basis vectors supply p Hub-plus-one-chart principal values, corrected at ground by helper combinations; a basis of K intersect H supplies c-p Hub-only principals. This is exactly the previously reviewed query-repair construction, with the displayed movement.

The linear lower bound holds for arbitrary block spaces after counting scalar dimensions. Fixed binary source and query words tensor with I_V for every positive whole-record width V and every characteristic-two field. Arbitrary field coefficients or block maps are algebraic parameters, not free implementation services. Equality for a restricted physical alphabet requires its actual emitted attainment word.

## Programme consequences and material scope controls

At the actual A2 triple cut, m8,d6,c36,p=t24, so the exact acquisition movement is 12[8]+24[2]+24[6], [r]=r^alpha. Redundant one-chart messages cannot lower it at that fixed cut. The hypothetical five-receiver interface c=p36,t40 would have exact acquisition movement 36[2]+40[6], or 9[2]+10[6] per four paid copies. This remains conditional on an actual compatible source.

The transversality premise is material. For H=span(h), D=span(d1) direct-sum span(d2), K=span(h+d1,h+d2), one has c=p=t2 but Hub projection rank1. Two queried packets cost2(B+C); a direct Hub packet and two proper-born helpers cost A+2C. If A<2B the latter is cheaper than the asserted formula. Thus targets with held-only codewords are outside scope.

Earlier point-chart acquisition, later held recoding, multi-chart visits, unequal dimensions, source feedback and a changed destination also change the interface. In particular the concurrently investigated early point-helper construction is not excluded: it pays for access before this frozen cut. This is neither a universal native barrier nor an erasure theorem.
