# A concrete faithful-width threshold for the three-head phase group

**Proved algebraic lemma; independent review requested. No new κ.**
For the actual three-head commuting Gaussian phase group P, the operators
`{g tensor V : g in P}` are linearly independent over C at every integer
width V>=18. Consequently an operator identity at any such width, with
coefficients outside tensor powers, is an identity in the full512-dimensional
group algebra C[P]. This justifies the necessary all-sector passage in the
commuting conservation theorem without treating a width-one quotient as
faithful.

## 1. The actual group, with every phase retained

In the Walsh basis indexed by x in F2^3, C_v has eigenvalue i^(v dot x),
where the dot product in the exponent is lifted from {0,1} to Z/4. Products
of these seven operators have exactly the phase functions

    f(x)=a1*x1+a2*x2+a3*x3
         +2*(b12*x1*x2+b13*x1*x3+b23*x2*x3) mod4,

with each a_i in Z/4 and each b_ij in F2. To see generation, C_ei provides
the linear term x_i, and C_(ei+ej) C_ei^(-1) C_ej^(-1) provides the term
2*x_i*x_j. Conversely the parity formula for v dot x is quadratic modulo4;
its cubic term has coefficient4 and vanishes. The coefficients are unique:
evaluate at the three basis vectors and then at the three pair sums.
Thus |P|=4^3*2^3=512. Every group element fixes the Walsh vector x=0, so
no nonidentity element is a scalar multiple of the identity.

## 2. A uniform trace bound, proved by differencing

Associate to f the symmetric binary matrix B with diagonal a_i mod2 and
off-diagonal b_ij. Direct expansion gives, for all x,z,

    f(x+z)-f(x)-f(z)=2*x^T B z mod4.

Put S_f=sum_x i^f(x), the trace of the actual8-dimensional operator. Then

    |S_f|^2
      = sum_z i^f(z) sum_x (-1)^(x^T B z)
      = 8 sum_(z in ker B) i^f(z).

If B!=0, this has absolute value at most8*|ker B|<=32. If B=0,
the phase is an ordinary character (-1)^(ell dot x); its trace is zero
unless f=0. Hence every g!=I in P satisfies

    |Tr(g)|/8 <= 1/sqrt(2).                              (1)

No numerical approximation enters this inequality. The usual support-rank
description is also visible here: the Walsh transform of i^f is supported
on an affine coset of (ker B)^perp and has2^rank(B) nonzero entries. For
the faithfulness conclusion only (1) is needed.

## 3. Tensor Gram matrix

Normalize the Hilbert--Schmidt Gram matrix of the512 matrices g^tensor V
by the dimension8^V. Its diagonal entries equal1. If g!=h, then

    |G_(g,h)| = |Tr(g^*h)/8|^V <= 2^(-V/2).

At every V>=18, each off-diagonal row sum is at most

    511*2^(-V/2) <= 511/512 < 1.

The Hermitian Gram matrix is therefore positive definite by strict diagonal
dominance (or the elementary quadratic-form bound). Its columns are
independent, proving the asserted injectivity of C[P] into actual operators.
This is a sufficient threshold, not a claim that18 is minimal.

The representation map is multiplicative because actual individual group
operators tensor multiplicatively. It is extended linearly; an expression
sum c_g*g^tensor V is not confused with (sum c_g*g)^tensor V. Thus arbitrary
width-dependent scalar ordinary interstages in the retained architecture
cause no problem: at any fixed width>=18 their evaluated coefficients still
give an injective algebra representation. Each of the eight abstract Pauli
sectors is retained and is faithful as a direct summand at these widths.

## 4. General fixed-head version

The same proof gives a useful explicit dependency for the general-head
one-relation theorem. For d>=1, let P_d be generated by all C_v with
0!=v in F2^d. Its actual Walsh phases are

    f(x)=sum_i a_i*x_i + 2*sum_(i<j) b_ij*x_i*x_j mod4,

so |P_d|=4^d*2^(d*(d-1)/2)=2^(d*(d+3)/2). Generation and uniqueness follow
from the basis and pair directions exactly as above. For every nonidentity
phase, differencing gives normalized trace at most1/sqrt(2), or zero when
the associated B vanishes. Hence the full group algebra is faithful at
every integer tensor width

    V >= d*(d+3).

Indeed its Gram row sum is at most
`(|P_d|-1)*2^(-V/2)<1`. Every subgroup generated by a selected collection
of directions inherits faithfulness at this same sufficient width. This
provides a concrete all-width-to-algebra implication for the arbitrary
fixed-head extension; its threshold is not asserted optimal.

## 5. Verification and scope

The independent checker enumerates all512 explicit phase functions,
reconstructs generation by the seven native directions, proves uniqueness
in that finite representation, and checks the trace bound and Gaussian
support sizes using exact Gaussian integers. It also checks the actual
direction-pair source gauges and finite determinantal-rounding controls
without importing the author's verifier. The universal tensor conclusion
is the written Gram argument, not an extrapolation from tested widths.

This lemma supplies a dependency of a scoped obstruction, not a faster
multiplication algorithm. Larger head groups, the full ordinary Clifford
algebra, a changed source, and physical cost admission remain separate.
