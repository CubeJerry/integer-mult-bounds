# At most five commuting split natives conserve rank at every fixed head count

**Proved conditional on the stated commuting/translation-algebra source
interface; independent review pending.** This extends the three-head
conservation argument without claiming a general ordinary-algebra obstruction.
No new κ.

## 1. The general one-relation theorem

Let U be a finite set of distinct nonzero vectors in F₂^d, for any fixed d.
Let C_v be the actual commuting Gaussian root of translation in direction v,
so its binary Gaussian matrix is vvᵀ. Suppose the linear map

    F₂^U → Sym_d(F₂),     e_v ↦ vvᵀ

has kernel dimension at most one. Consider any finite word on any fixed
number R of retained roles, using actual gauged source blocks
`[[C_u,I],[0,C_w]]` or their physical inverse orientations, with scalar role
matrices over the full ordinary translation algebra between calls and at
both endpoints. If there are m native blocks and the independent Gaussian
endpoints have total Gaussian rank H, then

    H≤2m.                                                (1)

**Proof.** Split by all characters of the translation group generated by the
used directions. Normalize each C_v by a scalar square root of its sector
translation character. If there is no relation among the vvᵀ, the resulting
involutive labels are independent. Flipping one sign at a time in an actual
sector gives the ordinary rank-difference bound: the number of endpoint
monomials using v is at most the number of native labels v. Sum and use
Gaussian rank subadditivity.

If the kernel is spanned by one nonempty subset C⊂U, the actual product of
C_v over C is an ordinary translation. To see this with phases retained,
expand its Walsh exponent
`sum_{v∈C} (v·x mod 2)` modulo four. Vanishing of sum vvᵀ removes all
quadratic coefficients and makes every linear coefficient even, leaving
exactly the exponent of a translation. In a sector this relation is a scalar
±1. Choose one normalization-root sign so that the normalized relation is
`product_{v∈C} x_v=1`; all labels outside C remain independent. The roots have
the required squared sector values, so this is an exact abstract sector
normalization, not a small-width faithfulness assertion.

Fix the outside signs arbitrarily and evaluate the word at a sign assignment
whose product on C is -1. Each single flip inside C enters the physical
relation set. Apply the same determinantal rank-rounding lemma as in
`COMMUTING-PHASE-CONSERVATION.md`. It chooses, separately for each endpoint
row, whether to complement its subset inside C, such that the total incidence
of each label v∈C is at most its paid native count n_v. Complementing C leaves
the Gaussian matrix unchanged because sum_{v∈C} vvᵀ=0.

For a label outside C, flip it between two physical assignments. This directly
bounds its endpoint incidence by n_v, and the preceding complements inside C
do not change that incidence. Sum all incidences and use rank subadditivity
to obtain H≤sum_v n_v=2m. ∎

The same proof applies when the relation space is a direct sum of relations
with disjoint supports: perform the separate complement-roundings one
component at a time. This observation is not needed for the next corollary.

## 2. Distinct binary rank-one directions have no relation of weight below seven

Suppose k distinct nonzero vectors v_i satisfy `sum_i v_i v_iᵀ=0`. Let V be
the matrix with these columns and let r be their linear span dimension. The
row space of V is a self-orthogonal binary code of length k, so r≤floor(k/2).
Distinct nonzero columns require k≤2^r−1. For 1≤k≤5 these inequalities are
incompatible. For k=6 they force r=3. After changing ambient basis, the six
vectors are six of the seven nonzero vectors of F₂³. The sum of vvᵀ over all
seven is zero, so the sum over six equals the missing nonzero rank-one
matrix, a contradiction. Thus every nonzero relation has weight at least
seven. At weight seven the same argument forces the vectors to be all seven
nonzero vectors of one three-dimensional subspace: an actual Fano relation.

If a relation space on k≤10 labels had dimension at least two, choose two
independent relations. Their three nonzero combinations each have weight at
least seven. Every coordinate contributes either zero or two to the sum of
those three weights, so 2k≥21, impossible. Therefore there is at most one
independent relation on any ten distinct directions.

**Corollary.** For every fixed head count d, every commuting split-source word
with m≤5 native calls satisfies H≤2m, with arbitrary retained role count,
arbitrary scalar stages in every translation sector, physical forward and
inverse orientations, and independent Gaussian endpoints in the generated
commuting algebra. The complete fresh-body invoice consequently has the
strict deficit proved in the main conservation theorem.

## Scope and consequence

All phases are retained by the sector normalization, and every actual inverse
source is authenticated by its ordinary gauges. The complete fresh-body
invoice has the same strict deficit `m(1-(8^t-7^t))` for0<t<1 as in the main
conservation theorem. No actual multiplier bound is improved. The result
forces any commuting/translation-sector escape to use at least six natives
and at least eleven distinct Gaussian directions with overlapping relations.
The first overlap constructions and endpoint feasibility searches are kept
separate as explicitly unfinished work.
