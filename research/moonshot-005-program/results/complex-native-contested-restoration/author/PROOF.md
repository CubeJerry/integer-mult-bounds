## Exact bridge for a group of parallel moves

Fix three actual charts a,b,c and let X consist of all k incoming moves to b from a, let Z include every other input available at b, including arbitrary original public entries. Let Y consist of the l outgoing moves from b to c. Let W include every other outgoing value or public terminal at b. On the independent coordinates X,Z the word has

    W = A X + B Z,       Y = C X + D Z.

Consider the single-relay problem that keeps W at b and delivers Y at c. The sender has X, the middle has Z, and the receiver has no external side information. Replacing k first-leg and l second-leg rows by k−r first-leg, l−r second-leg and r direct rows permits arbitrary exact linear recoding. Both original boundary maps have full ranks, meaning rank([A;C])=k and rank([C D])=l. Let pi be quotient by im D. Then the largest possible number of bypasses is exactly

    rank(pi C restricted to ker A).

For necessity, let E X be the k−r retained incoming coordinates. Every u in ker E must lie in ker A, since W remains recoverable. The receiver gets l total scalars and recovers the full-row-rank map Y. Its tuple and Y therefore span the same l-dimensional row space as functionals of the arbitrary original input. In particular every direct row has the form L C X for a row L with L D=0. On ker E, the relayed part carries no variation. Full column rank of [A;C] makes C injective there. The direct part therefore has rank at least dim ker E ≥r on this space. It factors through pi C, giving the bound.

Conversely choose an r-dimensional subspace U of ker A on which pi C is injective. Select r rows L annihilating D with L C U the identity in a chosen basis U. Send coordinates of the class of X modulo U to b. It can compute W and the residual Y−C U L C X from its received class and Z. That residual lies in ker L and costs l−r coordinates onward. The r direct coordinates L C X recover Y at c. All choices can be taken over Q when the word is rational. Delaying a reconstruction to external receivers or reserving supplementary donor stocks changes this interface.

Exact rational bases of both kernels at those 822 triples have zero pairing L C U. Consequently **every one of the 6,959 grouped relays has maximum bypass zero** in the stated stock model.
