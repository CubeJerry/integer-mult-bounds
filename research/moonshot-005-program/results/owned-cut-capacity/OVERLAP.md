# Bounded predecessor and scope check

Canonical source and comparison base:
`e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57`.

The earlier [isolated owned-rank proof](https://github.com/CubeJerry/integer-mult-bounds/blob/4115049c5126cb56133072982d9158acfd74d4b3/research/selected-owned-rank-tradeoff/PROOF.md)
excludes lower Gaussian rank at one return with no correlated side input.
The present package instead measures exact information available jointly
from two main streams, a single helper bank and owned observations at one
intermediate cut. It grants arbitrary normalized linear decoding in the
dimension relaxation and does not assert a paid-rank lower bound.

[Nick Petalas's paired-prefix cut-capacity proof](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/paired-prefix-cut-capacity/PROOF.md)
proves a sharp one-channel change in saturated dyadic extraction capacity
under paired scalar ADDs, with no live owned field at the cut. It credits
Ali Romman's module interface and zxv's earlier fixed-prefix question. That
dyadic-unit criterion is distinct from the Q-linear information rank here.
We do not claim a new Smith theorem or general rank principle.

[The joint cleanup cut argument](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/p104-owned-cleanup-obstruction/PROOF-joint-cut.md)
counts information-bearing owned descents into a frame region for eight
retained delivery observations. [DreamingOfClouds' owned-exit cut bound](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/p104-owned-exit-cut-bound/README.md)
counts owned exits and applies to the older #6833 source. Both explicitly
separate event counts from dimension mass. The present result is a temporal
state-capacity certificate for selected #7000 and does not count frame exits
or transfer their conclusions to the H4 invoice.

The source-specific new evidence is the complete exact1479/338/344 rank
pattern, its sparse lost mode and62 rational nullvectors, the63-versus7
continuation distinction, the arbitrary single-helper-bank62 floor, and
the cochart63-observation interface. The shared-core/H4 input comes from
the retained recovery branch; no claim of priority for its bus algebra.
