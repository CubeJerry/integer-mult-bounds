# Independent review of the second-active-input spectator injection

I verified the all-width obstruction for $K=I+e_{21}(t)$ with arbitrary ordinary $t$, including singular noncommuting coefficients. Every head has rank at most three, some rank is at most one, and $(3,3,1)$ is excluded. The obstruction leaves $(3,3,0)$ open and gives total at most six. My review concerns the theorem, with the companion positive single-column benchmark remaining the author's separate source evidence.

**Physical and gauged rows.** Direct multiplication with $K^{-1}=I-e_{21}(t)$ gives the physical inverse row $((t-N)a^{-1}+ta^{-2},-ta^{-1},P)$ and the physical forward row from $(-I,I,I)$ equals $(a,Sta,S)$. In the stated gauged basis the projected second row is $(Pa,0,0)$. Multiplication by any column $(p,z,w)^T$ of $Q$ gives the author's three $D_j$ expressions. If all heads were at least two, the third-input third row splits into an ordinary and a rank-one term, both of which must vanish. The second row then splits as $a^2N_2z-taz$ and forces $taz=0,p=0$. The ordinary input column now has only $z$ as a nonzero block and a whole-bank left inverse. Square $z$ is therefore a bijection on the physical bank and $t=0$. The already-proved projected-row argument excludes all heads of rank at least two in that case.

**Singular injections.** For an isolated quartic the forward probe gives $-u+v+w=0$. The inverse row before the flat quartic contains at most two inverse Gaussian factors, so its one-letter maximum entry is at most $1/2$. Separating from the at-most-one-call input side gives a zero inverse-row product. Splitting the resulting ordinary and rank-one classes after cancellation gives $Pw=0$ and $Na^{-1}u=t a^{-1}(u-v)=t a^{-1}w$. These steps do not divide by $t$. In the finite-dimensional complexified ordinary group algebra, $w^*w$ belongs to that algebra and the spectral projector $H$ onto its zero eigenspace is a polynomial in it at each width. The full fixed finite tail monomial group spans all resulting products, regardless of the width dependence of exterior coefficients. This analytical projector costs nothing because it is only used in an obstruction. The actual source never executes it. Since $w=Nw$, the kernel has dimension at least $D/2$. Right multiplication by $H$ gives $Na^{-1}uH=0$. The earlier disjoint monomial-coset argument applies to the ordinary map $uH$, giving $uH=vH=wH=0$. The endpoint column therefore fails to be injective. The reasoning applies at all sufficiently large faithful widths and handles singular $t$ without a rank premise.

**Distinct output pairs.** The underlying rotation sends an input $c$ change to the output $b$ position. Each $A$ column uses exactly the two outputs that differ in $b$. Its column supports coincide precisely for a pair of inputs differing only in $c$; all other pairs have disjoint images. For a single unitary with two outputs per column to remain of this class after left multiplication by $A$, each incoming pair must therefore be a $c$-pair, irrespective of relative phases or amplitudes. When a two-call word $AgA$ has exactly two outputs everywhere, its final pairs are $b$-pairs. The swap in $S$ fixes the $b$-pair class and exchanges the $c$ and $d$ classes. For $B$ and $aB$ both pure class one, projective injectivity of left multiplication forces $B$ to consist only of $c$-pair words. Hence $PB$ belongs to the $c/d$ catalogues. If it is also $b$-pair-only, width separation gives $PB=0$, then $B=-SB=0$ because the $c$ and $d$ catalogues are disjoint. No claim about the support of a linear combination is needed.

**The three-column contradiction.** Use the true rightmost columns $D_0a+D_1,D_1a,D_2$. For $(3,3,1)$ only classes one and three are available in every entry. In the third column the remaining terms of the top row already belong to allowed classes. Thus $aX_2$ is pure class one by its four-entry support ceiling. The pair test applied with $PX_2=Pap_2$ forces $X_2=0$. In the second input the projective catalogues $GAGA$ and $A^2GA$ are disjoint after cancelling the last $A$ (support size two against four). Similarly $AGAGA$ is disjoint from both $A^3GA$ and $AGA$ after cancelling outer $A$s (two against four or one). This makes $X_1a$ and $aX_1a$ pure class one in succession. Its projected part $Pap_1a$ has only $b$ pairs, giving $X_1=0$. For column zero the projected probe gives $Pap_0a$ pure class one. In the first full output row the same disjoint-catalogue comparisons isolate $aX_0a$ in the two allowed classes. For $B=\pi_1(X_0a)$, projective injectivity and the four-entry ceiling force $aB$ to be class one. Ordinary multiplication preserves the class projection since it expands into monomial translates. Therefore $PB=Pap_0a$ uses only $b$ pairs; the test forces $B=0$. All three $Pap_j$ vanish. This contradicts invertibility of $Q$ and nonzero $(Pa,0,0)$.
