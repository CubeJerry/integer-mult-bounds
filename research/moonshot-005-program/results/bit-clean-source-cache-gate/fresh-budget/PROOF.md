strictly stronger form of the last-interaction gate. For every receiver i,

## Consequential escape and exact scope

This rules out repairing this clean-source/nine-global-packet construction
by public receiver coding plus a few cheap freshly born returns. It does
not rule out a different higher-rank global code that replaces the nine
packets, changed source transformations, useful owned copies taking their
own routes, or packets acquiring and delivering different payloads at
multiple charts. In particular, splitting a funded packet descent and
updating its actual payload on the way can introduce a different
information interface; it is not silently treated as a fresh single-birth
packet by this theorem. That is an active constructive next step.

The proof does not claim that source history rows are available inside the
original contaminated full native. They belong to the separately executed
and fully priced clean X circuit. The missing physical admission, both
