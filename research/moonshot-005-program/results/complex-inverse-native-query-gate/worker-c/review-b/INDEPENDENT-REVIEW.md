## Inverse-native gate

The input row spaces of its two components are disjoint because their stacked ordinary rows form invertible V. These rank and independence facts are essential.

For split placement, degree three uses both query coefficients and occupies one block. Rank d makes that sole block invertible. Injectivity and surjectivity force the other blocks of the E3 output column and input row to vanish. Ordinary U and V are then block triangular after role permutation, so the complementary blocks are invertible. Their opposite corner is an invertible E2 operator, although the source expression is an untouched ordinary compression of an F1 base. Directness excludes this.

For same-side placement, the first query's degree-two part is one block row R. The last query confines degree three to one output row. The other endpoint row is pure E2, has rank d, and equals cR for a square ordinary c. Full row rank, rather than merely nonzero c, forces physical invertibility of c. A finite unital matrix algebra contains the inverse of an invertible member, by Cayley-Hamilton. Factoring R through the E2 endpoint input row then forces the E3 component through that same row, contradicting independent inputs. Projection through the ordinary factor is justified by bimodularity. There is no entrywise-unit or integral-domain assumption.

Both-before placement uses the transposed algebra as well as the transposed word: `(F_a)^T(F_b)^T` is the transpose of `F_b F_a`. Original transpose stability is unnecessary. The square-cross source inverse is exactly `[[A^-1,-cI],[0,A^-1]]`; the ordinary-cross source is already entrywise F1. Inverting an asserted caller preserves Gaussian ranks and admitted query forms. This algebraic contradiction does not authorize a free physical inverse native. Cancelling a common fresh body does not cancel its cost.

The filtration bounds each endpoint rank by three. Two independent rank-three jobs require degree-three physical rank 2d, while each query placement confines it to a row, column or block of rank at most d. Excluding (3,2) therefore gives total H at most four. With `a=8^t-7^t`, strict concavity bounds the target by `2*7^t+aH`; the source and two unit queries cost `3+2*7^t+a`.
