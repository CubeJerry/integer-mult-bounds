specialization. Fixing all donors except one leaves only packets whose true
source support contains that donor. Their total alphabet bounds that block's
rank. Summing over donors yields the weighted support bound without assuming
packet independence, linear encoders or a linear decoder. The proper endpoint
must be linear, and every informative arrival or bypass must be counted.

The value-based support restriction is essential. An actual K sum can have
more source donors than the D attached to its original owner label. The
theorem expressly allows that escape rather than silently charging such a
sum as a one-D packet.
