# One shared port excludes field-linear geometric and gap15 sources

**Proved and independently reviewed. No new complete kappa.**

For every matrix width, finite GF64 extension and arbitrary separate
GF64-linear whole-bank gauges, the shared-node geometric source and its
single-gap15 matrix-feedback family fail the fixed native H263 identity/S028
cut. Their fibres force fixed amplitude maps, while receivers5 and7 need
different readout ratios26 and49 at their shared positive D108 exit. That
exit must be a function of both aggregates, whose row spaces intersect
trivially; it is therefore constant, contradicting exact capacity.

Nonlinear downstream processing and output relabelling are allowed. Merely
binary-linear gauges, multiple-gap feedback, changed node assignments and
changed targets are outside this version. No return-rank bound is proved.
