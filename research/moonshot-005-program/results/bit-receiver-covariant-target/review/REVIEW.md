## Mathematical review

The actual Clifford nilpotents A=gamma(80)+I and C=gamma(10)+I obey
A^2=C^2=0 and AC+CA=I. Choosing a basis of im A and adjoining its C images
gives the claimed two-by-two matrix-factor representation over F2 itself.
The secondary bank order matters: each reverse transfer acts as the identity
on the entire spinor and moves only right-factor coordinates. It therefore
commutes with every receiver gamma. The exceptional column operator is
(gamma(10)+I) times a right-factor projector and commutes with precisely the
needed foreign receivers. The row-80 operators commute with its common
first-factor nilpotent. This proves the receiver-wise covariance without
silently assuming an intertwining relation between different receivers.

The row and column perturbations each have rank 2w and their native support
sets are disjoint. The two authenticated rank-nine restrictions exclude
intersection with the canonical row and column spaces. The rank-additivity
argument therefore gives exactly 9b+4w, and the retained repair rank is also
4w. No favorable numerical fitting search is used for this conclusion.

J and E generate all secondary units within the first bank; the forward and
reverse transfers propagate them to all banks. Adding both first-factor
off-diagonal matrix units gives the full matrix algebra. Every native edge
retains an identity orientation, so the connected transport argument handles
vertex-dependent invariant subspaces. The common-kernel and joint-image
arguments exclude flat inputs and outputs. The distinction between finite-bank
irreducibility and body-replicated no-flat properties is correct.

The source motivation's double commutator is a partial trace because the
local A2 Clifford algebra is M2 in characteristic two. Its equivariance under
the commuting algebra follows directly from moving the left operator through
both commutators. The proof properly leaves simultaneous bases for the
restricted source family open. The previously admitted arbitrary mixed-row
two-evaluation source does not automatically supply those bases. Transposition
is not included without an additional left/right intertwiner.
