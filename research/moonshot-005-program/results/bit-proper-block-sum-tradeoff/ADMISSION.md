# Proper decoder and payload blocks have a sharp sum bound of eleven

**Proved for one paid copy, exactly nine transcript events, and arbitrary
positive record width over characteristic two.** Let p count nonzero
destination lines and q count payload blocks whose projected native-root
support has rank seven. Then p+q<=11. Ground arrivals and noncommuting
vertex gauges are allowed. A scaled multi-copy assertion is not established.

For an independent proper decoder subset, count its available unit
evaluations on the 25 proper directions and the rank of its proper common
kernel. Their sum bounds the number of proper dual blocks. Separate exact
implementations check all subsets of sizes 3,4,5,6, with respective maxima
7,7,6,5. Actual completed bases attain the bounds; a four/seven pair proves
sharpness of the sum bound. The full-rank event factors turn block inverse
products into these scalar incidence constraints without commuting matrices.

Consequently ground-event count plus full-support-payload count is at least
seven. An event in both categories is counted twice: this is not a claim
about seven distinct events or seven separate physical charges.
