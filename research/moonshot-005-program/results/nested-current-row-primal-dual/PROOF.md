# Nested current-row encoding with physically acquired reverse observations

## Exact result and limits

This package gives a new finite partial code on all 1,880 independent input coordinates of the pinned shared-native interface. Seven acquisition charts of binary dimension six feed three recombinations at dimension seven. Each recombination reads four **actual already encoded rows**, not four original donor values. A parallel inverse-transpose bank carries reverse source information acquired before separation. All 104 first-X differences are restored before the retained eight E8 packets are formed.

Relative to the retained seven-correction E8 parent, the word supplies 196 additional forward coefficients. It completes first-Y roles 112, 113, and 210, and cancels 26 additional coefficients at role 114 and 13 each at roles 211 and 215. The same execution supplies the hosted transpose of these rows, comprising 183 reverse coefficients. The hosted reverse residual has rank 95 over the rationals, retaining the earlier dependent-triple kernel. This is not a completed reverse interface.

There are no new owned MOVEs. All 400 raw public paths remain monotone with total rank mass 3,200. The complete partial H4 invoice retains the native common execution, all outer terms, and all eight rank-nine packets. Its remaining target moment per native is rigorously enclosed around 42.172356521000038. This is positive room for future construction, not an achieved kappa.

**Classification:** exact finite normalized construction and a general algebraic composition lemma, with physical source access, ownership, and transport checked for this word. No new complete kappa. Remaining forward/reverse duties, coupled-source duties, ordinary and precision admission, uniform suppliers, and all five foundational assumptions remain open.

## 3. Local encoders retain every input direction

For an acquisition class G, separate its hosted donor list H_G from coupled controls U_G. Choose one required row for a star, or three independent required rows for a cycle. Their restrictions to H_G have the same stated rank. Select an invertible minor and replace corresponding rows of the identity matrix by these coefficient rows. Call the resulting invertible hosted matrix M_G.

The physical first-X bank is first changed to M_G x_HG using literal cochart arithmetic and temporary snapshots. Then the appropriate W coefficients of the actual coupled controls z_UG are added into the pivot rows. Thus a pivot contains the **full** requested form on all donors in G, not only its hosted restriction. The controls themselves remain unchanged.

At the same chart the independent hosted second-X bank is changed by M_G^(-T). No coupled second-X field is assumed. A later hub inverse subtracts the control additions using the actual restored control values and applies M_G^(-1) to the first bank. The second bank eventually receives the corresponding M_G^T decoder, carrying any injected reverse observations with it.

All nonpivot fields proceed to the hub. The pivot fields remain genuine public fields with original independent input directions encoded inside an invertible global map. Owned snapshots are explicit stationary fields, initialized and cleared at their actual chart. There is no erasure of an independent public direction.

## 4. Recombination reads current rows at a new chart

At a dimension-seven join the four primal carriers contain

\[
(s,c_1,c_2,c_3),
\]

where s is the complete 13-donor star form and the three c rows are complete 11-donor cycle forms. These are actual current values. They have support lengths 13,11,11,11 in the original q coordinates. The two old receivers require s+c_1 and alpha*s+c_2, where alpha is +1 for the first two joins and -1 for the last.

Apply the invertible matrix

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0&0\\
\alpha&0&1&0\\
1&0&0&0\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its outputs are (s+c_1,alpha*s+c_2,s,c_3). Its inverse recovers s from coordinate three, c_1 by subtracting s from coordinate one, and c_2 by subtracting alpha*s from coordinate two. The fourth independent cycle direction is retained.

The parallel second-X carriers are changed by M^(-T). Explicitly their new coordinates are

\[
(b_1,b_2,b_s-b_1-\alpha b_2,b_3).
\]

The incoming b_s already contains a reverse observation acquired at the lower star chart. Consequently the third outgoing dual coordinate retains that observation through a genuinely new encoding stage. It is not reset to an original second-X input. The artifact publishes all four incoming primal and dual rows at every join. A fresh prefix replay checks each row at the exact instruction boundary.

One complete old-receiver form is left in a stationary owned cache at the join. The other form travels on its public pivot to the other old receiver's facet and is cached there. The corresponding second-X pivot follows the same route and reads the original second-Y value at that facet. Both public pivots then continue to the hub. After all deliveries, hub inverses undo the join encoders and the earlier acquisition encoders in reverse order.

## 5. General paired composition lemma with coupled controls

The following invariant explains why stages can compose without ideal-row substitution. Regard all 96 hosted primal carriers together as

\[
a=Rx+Cz,
\]

where R is invertible. The hosted dual bank satisfies

\[
R^T b=b_0-F_H^T d.
\]

Here F_H records the hosted coefficients of every forward observation already stored for later receiver addition. Initially R=I, C=0, and F_H=0.

When a cochart subset is changed by an invertible matrix M, embed it in the full identity matrix. Update a to Ma and b to M^(-T)b. Then R changes to MR, C to MC, and

\[
(MR)^T(M^{-T}b)=R^Tb.
\]

Adding a linear form of the unchanged controls z to a primal carrier changes C but not R, so it requires no fabricated dual field. Suppose a forward observation stores gamma*a_j for receiver i. Its hosted coefficient row is gamma*e_j^T R. If the actual original d_i is present at that chart, execute

\[
b_j\leftarrow b_j-\gamma d_i.
\]

The invariant changes by exactly minus the transpose of that newly stored hosted row. Delaying the forward receiver addition is harmless because the stationary cache retains the acquired row. The dual injection must occur when the matching current R row is present; this construction schedules that injection explicitly.

After the first-bank encodings and all coupled-control additions are undone, R=I and C=0. Hence the first-X bank is exactly x, and the second-X bank is exactly

\[
b_0-F_H^T d.
\]

This proves composition across arbitrarily many legal local encoding stages. It does not prove that an arbitrary desired network can be scheduled: every matrix must act on actual cochart carriers, every d_i must be the original current source value at the injection site, stationary caches must be maintained, and every route must be paid. The present literal word establishes those hypotheses for its two acquisition levels.
