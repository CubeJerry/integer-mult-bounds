**Accepted as a conditional block lemma and an unconditional scoped same-orientation caller exclusion.** The accepted MIDDLE-PIVOT-LEMMA.md has SHA256 `2b160c6fb051e256a7c46d6facf1e40e8f0579b5dba7543bff2c7c8c90869019`.

Root expanded the blocks in their stated noncommutative order. With C_L=[[P,Q],[I,D]], C_R=[[E0,I],[F,H]], W_ii=G and E+DH=0, set R=Q−PD and S=F−HE0. Then W_ai=PG+RH, W_ia=GE0+DS, and Schur(W)=R(I−HG^-1D)S. No factor order can be reversed. Since the outer ordinary elimination coefficient is N_LP+N_LRHG^-1 and belongs to E0, its difference from N_LP lies in F_s. Multiplication by the remaining ordinary factors and N_R puts both terms of Schur(M) in F_(s+t). The inverse G is a proof device, not a free source call.

The pivot's incoming and outgoing ordinary square factors are forced invertible by the physical invertibility of their product AGB. Hence normalizing A=B=I is legitimate without assuming arbitrary ordinary blocks are units. Invertibility of D or H is neither used nor implied. The ordinary-elimination hypothesis is essential: taking N_L=N_R=I, P=E0=0 and R=S=D=H=I gives Schur=I−G^-1, which need not be ordinary; its elimination coefficient G^-1 fails the hypothesis.

For three ordinary-cross natives on four retained roles and endpoint ranks(3,3,1,1), rank-three output/image and input/row-space both fill the outer active pair. The endpoint ordinary encodings must therefore be block triangular. Their low/low block is an invertible pure E1 pivot, the active/idle elimination is ordinary, and the active Schur complement is invertible pure E3. The middle-pivot lemma gives F2 instead, a contradiction. Whole-word inversion handles three square-cross natives while preserving every physical price in the statement. Mixed orientations are not excluded.

This sharpens the constructive search to a mixed-orientation, genuinely noncommuting caller; it does not prove an all-native conservation law and does not apply unchanged to the later three-role candidate.
