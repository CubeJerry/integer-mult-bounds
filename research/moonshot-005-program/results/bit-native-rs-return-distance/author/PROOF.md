# Whole-network return and distance to the target

The original degree-15 Reed–Solomon source, spread H, selected Cauchy D, spread K and transposed polynomial facet word act independently on eight copies of two native sectors, of widths 96 and 384. The source-first whole-word calculation on all 111 roles gives determinants 29 and 33. Its return rank is 426,240. All 6,470 proper blocks are also invertible. See [the full original-source calculation](../stream/PROOF.md).

Keep receivers 0 through 54 and donors 56 through 110. Both a dense output-first construction and an independent original-column forward cascade give determinants 41 and 52 on these disjoint restrictions, at dimensions 5,280 and 21,120, respectively. Together they give rank 211,200. Invertible changes of source and output coordinates only multiply this restriction by block-diagonal invertibles. It has no own diagonal cells.

Changing a local linear map alters the restricted end-to-end matrix by rank at most the smaller of its input and output capacities on this cut. For several changes, telescope one altered map at a time. Inputs at H can originate only at selected donors, and only wires toward D charts whose dual meets the receiver set can contribute. Inputs at D must come from H incident to selected donors; its relevant exits go to K incident to selected receivers. At K only D incident to selected donors can contribute and only exits to selected receivers are counted. These true-route bounds hold even when other maps have been changed. Each selected source or facet has capacity at most 3,840.

The m=870 cap of 34,688 requires a rank reduction of at least 176,512 on this cut. Sorting chart capacities gives the following single-family minima. At least 353 H charts, 742 D charts, 264 K charts, 248 mixed H/D/K charts, or 46 source/facet banks must change when the indicated family alone is retuned. These are necessary conditions. The separate 19-receiver theorem also requires earlier-stage changes when the entire suffix is free.
