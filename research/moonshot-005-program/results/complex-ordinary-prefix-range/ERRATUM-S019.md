# Ordinary image projector versus free-bank module

The frozen PURE-PREFIX-IMAGE-GATE-v3.md describes an ordinary rank-D image projector and an ordinary injective free-bank column/retraction as equivalent certificates. The converse is unjustified in a general coefficient algebra. For O=Q×Q, diag(e+,e+,0) has balanced physical rank equal to one bank, but simple-component multiplicities(2,0) differ from a free bank's(1,1).

Read the free-bank column/retraction condition as an alternative sufficient certificate. The gate proof uses the ordinary projector directly and obtains a free endpoint column from the hypothesized independent-job endpoint; its argument is unchanged. The positive control has an explicit ordinary decoder and is unaffected. Frozen predecessor bytes and receipts remain preserved.
