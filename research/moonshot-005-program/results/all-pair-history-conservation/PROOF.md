## Exact source proof

Introduce independent increments at every permitted role/cut. Write A_x for
their E responses and B_x for their arrival responses. An exact reverse pass
and row-span elimination derive candidate relations B_x=Lambda_x A_x.

A second pass initializes each identity's final adjoint to -lambda_o E and
inserts its unit observation at the actual read cut. It runs the literal word
backward: ADD updates source adjoints; SCALE multiplies; COPY propagates to
its source then removes the overwritten destination; MOVE changes actual
chart; INIT/CLEAR implement the recorded lifetime. Every changed role is
checked at its current chart: the relevant identity coefficient must vanish
if this is a permitted S_x station. Unchanged states retain previously checked
coefficients. Observation insertion is checked on the correct read boundary.

Therefore every permitted increment has zero coefficient in the claimed
difference. All 71,262 cuts are covered, with 75,534 changed-role checks and
17,520,911 nonzero adjoint entries. Agreement with the historical totals is
corroboration; the newly replayed bytes establish the current evidence.

The independent reviewer reconstructs all 208 itineraries and 2,846 arrivals,
then checks exact adjoints grouped by pair without importing this extractor.
A wrong lambda coefficient and an additional forbidden chart each fail with
a concrete nonzero adjoint. Review work counts differ because its grouping
differs. No COPY-guard endpoint is needed: treating COPY as linear assignment
overgrants executions, and real guard-valid words are a subset.

## Histories, feedback and scope

Initial receiver-chart values depend only on fixed common inputs. Direct
controls cannot touch them while there because S_x and R_y are disjoint.
INIT adds zero; subsequent local operations combine already available values;
CLEAR removes one. The initial values and arrival list thus determine every
common observation at R_y. Restored E makes every arrival perturbation zero.

If w(u,q) lists actual feedback-dependent increments, the unchanged linear
word still obeys Delta E=A_x w and Delta history=B_x w after substitution,
even for nonlinear coordinate-mixing choices of w. The exact factorization
forces zero history response when E is restored. Fixing u and all independent
receiver-side inputs while varying q gives identical observations, excluding
any decoder of those data. This applies the retained general feedback theorem
to all 104 pairs; it does not claim a new general control principle.

The access grant is per donor, not the union of every donor's stations.
New charts, correlated receiver records, altered processing outside S_x,
changed endpoint contracts and different words remain open. Multiple donors
exchanging signals across carriers require new matrices. The restored
60-to-213 swap does not contradict this result: the required pair is 60-to-172,
and that primitive also adds a return. E8 packets likewise change access.
