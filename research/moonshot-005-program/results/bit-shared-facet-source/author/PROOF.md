## 2. General two-facet transfer lemma

Let \(S_a,S_b\) be source sets contained in two distinct proper facets,
and set

\[
 I=S_a\cap S_b,\quad E_a=S_a\setminus I,\quad
 E_b=S_b\setminus I,\quad z=\sum_{j\in I}x_j.
\]

Assume two distinct original donor lines \(L_{d_a},L_{d_b}\) lie in
\(D=F_a\cap F_b\), while neither donor belongs to \(S_a\cup S_b\).
The displayed native instances satisfy these assumptions because all sources
are spinors (`h=1`) and both donors are D8 roots (`h=0`).

Before any donor moves, retain actual stationary point copies
\(R_{d_i}=x_{d_i}\) on their own source lines. Each common source ascends
\(L_j\to D\to H\), depositing into a stationary cache \(Z=z\) at D.
The donor originals ascend to D and receive

\[
 X_{d_a}\leftarrow x_{d_a}+z,
 \qquad X_{d_b}\leftarrow x_{d_b}+z.                 \tag{1}
\]

The recoding of the complete X vector is
\(T=I+uz\), where \(u=e_{d_a}+e_{d_b}\) and z denotes its coefficient row.
Since \(zu=0\), \(T^2=I\) in characteristic two. This is an invertible change
of two original records, not the replacement of two independent originals by
two copies of z.

Donor \(d_i\) ascends from D to \(F_i\), deposits into cache \(C_i\), then
continues to Hub. Each exclusive source ascends
\(L_j\to F_i\to H\) and contributes there. Thus the actually acquired caches
are

\[
 C_i=x_{d_i}+z+\sum_{j\in E_i}x_j=x_{d_i}+m_i.       \tag{2}
\]

Every common original is now pure and physically present at Hub. Adding its
sum z to each donor again restores both donor originals. All other originals
were only read. The inverse uses exactly \(2|I|\) actual Hub ADDs; no free
inverse or unpriced original recovery is asserted.

Now create each descending packet at Hub with value \(g_i\), obtained through
actual cocharted original reads. Its route and values are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{chart}&\text{operation}&\text{value after operation}\\
H&\text{load actual seed}&g_i\\
F_i& P_i\mathrel{+}=C_i&g_i+m_i+x_{d_i}\\
L_{d_i}&P_i\mathrel{+}=R_{d_i}&g_i+m_i\\
0&\text{arrive}&g_i+m_i.
\end{array}                                                       \tag{3}
\]

The line stop is legitimate because \(L_{d_i}\subset F_i\), and the read uses
the actual retained point copy. The donor original itself is already at Hub
and is never granted a remote read. The packet lifetime is continuously
\(8\to7\to1\to0\). This proves the algebraic and geometric interface for
arbitrary characteristic-two extensions and widths: every coefficient is 0 or
1, and the entire operation sequence tensors with \(I_V\).

The lemma is source-aware. It assumes the stated source sets can reach the
specified charts before other commitments; it does not preserve their previous
unconsumed upward paths. After this word the listed original sources are at
Hub. Their point copies cannot ascend to a later acquisition facet.
