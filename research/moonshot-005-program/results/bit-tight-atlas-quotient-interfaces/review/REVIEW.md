On each of the other 15 planes the equal-size sumset argument makes all fibres with the other coordinates held fixed translates of one subgroup. A translation in that subgroup preserves every coordinate except its own. In a zero sum of such translations, any one also preserves its own coordinate since it equals the negative sum of the other translations. Injectivity forces it to vanish. Thus the 15 subgroups give a direct sum of order m^15 whose cosets are exactly the remaining coordinate's fibres. This proves the quotient statement for arbitrary role-specific functions.
