## Fixed-code return bank

For a fixed actual proper map `Gz+Bx`, with G invertible and no uncounted
X-dependent ground information, arbitrary correctness at y=0 forces the
ground transcript to determine `(I+B)x`. Its image contains exactly
`|F|^rank(I+B)` values. This proves the nonlinear-message lower bound on
this interface. A row factorization `I+B=LR` gives the matching preload
`G^-1(y+LRx)` and exact output y+x. Restored originals, actual return
owners, finite field-to-binary expansion, dirty CLEARs and the gap-eight
MOVE are explicitly retained. The proof concerns a supplied proper map;
it does not construct that missing map.

The conservative ordinary invoice is sound. Formation and decoding each
cost at most MC binary ADDs, and Gaussian elimination with three-ADD swaps
costs at most M^2+3M for the inverse. Adding these to the predecessor's
complete upper bound gives the stated18M^2+53M+111C0+2MC. Replacing only
the return owners gives5M+C INIT/CLEAR and7M+C peak bounds. The review
does not infer a generated full coefficient table or a completed compiler.

The identity `r-h=9b-dim(H intersect row(I+B))` follows directly from the
dimension formula. The equality-rank native theorem identifies a vertex
gauge orbit; it does not identify the literal old return row space. Small
positive excess remains a distinct target. A singular foreign block is
permitted by the changed-bank interface if all endpoint equations hold.

The native two-column example has the claimed ranks. Independently
enumerating all511 nonzero binary canonical codewords gives minimum weight
48, which in particular excludes one- and two-role support. The proof's
rank-one update therefore works for every nonzero alpha, including the
singular donor-rescaling witness alpha=1. Exact controls at thirteen native
pairs and all three nonzeroGF(4) values give q=10 and retained r=2. The
author correctly notes that this example does not establish a gap after
optimizing all physical endpoint gauges.

For the native seed LR inequality, averaging by the three incidence types
gives `(4a-2b, 5c-2a, 2b+c)<=h`. The exact positive dual
`(54,42,120)/11` has objective `(12,12,30)` and total216/11. Its matching
nonnegative relaxation witness is correct. Thus the sum of the27 kernel
defects is at mostfloor(216h/11), giving a fixed-label common kernel of
dimension at least1794 at b=5760,h=202. Invariance and a global canonical
direct summand are not consequences. The root two-star construction is a
separate concrete warning against that stronger inference.

The checker tests688,128 complete all-X/all-Y small endpoint instances:
every three-bit zero-diagonal proper map and every invertible three-bit
late map. For each proper map, it independently factors the return values
by row-basis messages and checks image cardinality; removing a necessary
message coordinate produces a collision. These controls support, but do
not replace, the general linear-algebra and counting proof.

## Common-row envelope and the actual seventy-row family

The full-column condition `P B_ij=P` forces the upper-right zero block in
the common-row decomposition. No restriction on the lower-left block is
needed. Consequently `rank(I+B)<=9k+111(b-k)`, with equality whenever the
bad-coordinate diagonal block is invertible. IndependentGF(4) controls at
six width/good-row combinations include arbitrary lower-left mixing and
attain the exact envelope. The budget table's entry at excess102 gives
first even multiplicity92, so5759 correct rows suffice for this movement
screen. This is a sufficient target, not an existing construction.

The stronger existence claim concerns the actual source affine product,
not independently assignable edge matrices. After the already-reviewed
downstream specialization, source input columns are separate affine
variables. Their prescribed correct-row right side is zero on all bad
columns. Setting those source columns to zero is therefore a valid
polynomial witness where `det(I+B_bad)=1`. The witness itself has singular
sources and is correctly excluded as a physical code.

Multiplying this nonzero polynomial by the previously established source
and foreign-invertibility factors gives total degree38,538,150, below
2^30. The finite-grid nonzero-polynomial lemma supplies an actual invertible
assignment. Adding all maximal foreign-defect factors gives75,352,450,
also below2^30. Thus an actual member of the retained seventy-row family
can have replacement rank632220, with excess580380. This is a bad-member
existence result, not a lower bound on every member or a universal barrier.
