## 1. Sharp owned Hub-route tariff

Fix0<=t<=1, pricing every positive rank-difference s by s^t. Let an actual
owned record travel from Hub(rank8) to ground(rank0), with arbitrary nested
turns and intermediate linear operations. Let I count its ascending arrivals
at rank7 facets plus its descending departures from rank7 facets. Then

    paid movement moment >= 7^t + I.                (1)

No monotonicity, field restriction, scalar record width or no-feedback premise
is needed. The marked portion must be a continuous actual record route;
distinct lifetimes cannot be concatenated through an uncharged CLEAR/rebirth.

Subtract one from the cost of each counted edge. The resulting rank-edge
weights are nonnegative, since every positive edge costs at least one. Remove
closed rank-subwalks. This can only lower the adjusted cost and leaves a simple
rank path8→0, visiting rank7 at most once. The reduction is a relaxation:
equal ranks need not have represented the same real chart, but the adjusted
rank-edge prices depend only on the ranks, which is all the lower bound uses.

If rank7 is absent, the adjusted path has its ordinary price, at least8^t by
the metric inequality for |u-v|^t. If rank7 is present, its adjusted8→7
prefix costs at least one. Direct entry8→7 costs one. Otherwise the final
entry is u→7 with u<=6; the preceding prefix has no rank7 incidence, so its
cost is at least(8-u)^t. The total adjusted prefix is at least

    (8-u)^t+(7-u)^t-1 >=1.

The7→0 suffix has just its first departure counted, so its adjusted cost is
at least7^t-1. Their sum proves(1). The path8→7→0 attains equality. Arbitrarily
many6↔7 repetitions also attain equality, so the tariff really permits turns.
At t=0 positive rank costs are one, as the right-hand limit.

Every other positive owned MOVE costs at least its own facet incidence.
Every fixed monotone route born at rank r<7 and ending at ground has no facet
incidence and costs at least r^t. These actual route portions must be disjoint;
all later movement of a retained bank is separately charged.
