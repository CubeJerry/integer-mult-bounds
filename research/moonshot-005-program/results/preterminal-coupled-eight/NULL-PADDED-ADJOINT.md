# A paid rectangular adjoint extension after coupled preconditioning

**Proved algebraic interface, conditional on the stated physical schedule.
No complete global source compiler or new κ.** This extends the existing
reciprocal M/M^{-T} identity and retains its attribution. The new point is an

## 1. Genuine input and output dimensions

Partition p primal indices into U and H, with |U|=u and |H|=h. There are p
genuine independent primal fields a, but only h genuine dual fields b_H. The
coordinates U have already had their reverse source duty completed by the
preterminal change of variables, or are otherwise genuinely primal-only. They
are not aliases for b. Let receiver banks c,d have m independent fields, with
every d physically available at its stated receiver chart and unchanged until
read. All field-volume coordinates are arbitrary.

Let M be an invertible p-by-p matrix, choose m distinct pivot coordinates with
selection matrix E, and set A=E M. At a common acquisition chart C, initialize
u actual owned fields to zero and use them for b_U. Thus

    b_tilde = J_H b_H,           (b_tilde)_U = 0.

These are actual initialized storage fields, not fictitious independent inputs.
Apply M to the genuine primal bank and M^{-T} to the padded dual bank. Route
both pivot fields to the actual receiver facet F_i and perform

    c_i += (M a)_(I_i),          (M^{-T} b_tilde)_(I_i) -= d_i.

Bring all primal and dual fields to hub H_* and apply M^{-1} and M^T. Then

    a_out = a,                 c_out = c+A a,
    (b_tilde)_out = J_H b_H-A^T d.

The genuine hosted dual outputs are therefore

    (b_H)_out = b_H-(A_H)^T d.

The auxiliary values -(A_U)^T d are retired under the retained dirty-owned
erasure contract. The genuine outputs a,d,c_out,(b_H)_out have the explicit
inverse c=c_out-Aa and b_H=(b_H)_out+(A_H)^T d. Erasing auxiliary values loses
no genuine input information because d and a remain. The physical erasure
tariff and foundational premise are still owed.

This works over every field admitting M^{-1}; the preterminal coupled input
map itself requires characteristic different from two. It does not admit a
complex dyadic construction as a BIT provider in characteristic two.

## 2. Exact additional transport formula

Suppose all primal fields a_j start at lines L_j contained in C, each genuine
dual b_j for j in H starts at the same L_j, and all receivers satisfy
C contained in F_i contained in H_*. Write ell_j=dim L_j, c0=dim C,
D=dim H_*, f_i=dim F_i, and set0^t=0 for an omitted equal-chart segment.
Compare with one direct L_j-to-H_* route for each existing genuine carrier.
For0<t<=1 define

    Delta_j = (c0-ell_j)^t+(D-c0)^t-(D-ell_j)^t,
    delta_i = (f_i-c0)^t+(D-f_i)^t-(D-c0)^t.

The exact new-minus-old transport moment of the padded implementation is

    sum_(j=1..p) Delta_j + sum_(j in H) Delta_j
       + 2 sum_(i=1..m) delta_i + u (D-c0)^t.

The first two sums split real carrier ascents. Every receiver pivot is visited
by both banks, including any auxiliary pivot, so its detour is counted twice.
The final term is the newly paid C-to-H_* ascent of every initialized auxiliary
dual field; there was no original raw route to credit for it. An auxiliary
pivot replaces its direct ascent by its two facet legs through delta_i.
If C=H_* and a receiver is lower, this nested schedule is invalid.

Every supplied matrix factor must operate on actual current cochart rows,
every introduced chart map needs correct phase/rank lowering, and later
overlapping groups may not reuse spent raw ascents. This formula is a one-block
invoice, not an additive global schedule unless the schedules actually coexist.

If a chosen elementary factorization of M has s additions and t0 nonzero
scalings, the full four transform/decoding passes cost4s ADD and4t0 SCALE,
and the paired receiver actions cost2m ADD. Additionally count u INIT, u CLEAR
and u auxiliary lifetimes. A source-specific sparse lowering may use fewer
ordinary operations but must emit and count them. Peak concurrent storage,

## 3. Sharp capacity gate for literal full-bank storage

After receiver injections and before the dual decoder, the logical bank is

    z = M^{-T} J_H b_H - E^T d.

Assume this complete p-coordinate z must be represented by physical linear
fields without consulting outside current observations of b_H or d. Its
information rank is exactly

    h + rank(A_U).

Multiply its coefficient matrix by invertible M^T. The result is
[J_H, -A^T]. The J_H columns supply all h hosted coordinate directions.
Modulo their span, the remaining columns are precisely -(A_U)^T on U.
Thus the rank is h+rank(A_U). At least that many scalar field dimensions
are needed to determine z for every independent b_H,d. Conversely, a basis
of that many coordinate rows of z determines every other row, proving sharp
algebraic attainment.

Relative to h existing dual carriers, this literal full-bank representation
needs at least rank(A_U) extra independent field dimensions at the cut. This
is not a universal storage or transport lower bound for calculating only
b_H-(A_H)^T d. It does not apply when authenticated receiver values are also
locally available, when unused logical coordinates are never materialized,
or with another chronology. With extra current observations append their
matrix and use the appropriate quotient rank. Algebraic attainment supplies
no physical acquisition or delivery schedule.
