## 1. Omitted-frame geometry and fourteen source classes

Use the eight odd binary vectors

    z=(7,25,42,52,76,82,97,127).

They are absent from the entire104-port source. Their canonical native real
root rows r(z_i), of squared length18, are pairwise orthogonal. Define

    g_i(h)=r(z_i).r(h)/18.

The root frame spans the same real eight-dimensional root space as the
source, so for every source h,j

    G_hj = sum_i g_i(h) g_i(j).

Every source root has exactly four nonzero g_i(h), all +/-1/2. These four
indices are precisely the modes whose binary line is contained in F_h:

    g_i(h)!=0  iff  z_i.v_h=0.

The104 ports partition into14 classes J according to this four-mode support
S_J. Each class has seven or eight ports. Its common chart

    C_J=span_F2{z_i:i in S_J}

has dimension3 and is contained in every actual facet F_h, h in J. Each of
its four mode lines is strictly below C_J. Every hosted restriction J_H has
at least five members in this chosen frame, and the4-by|J_H| matrix
A_J=(g_i(h)) has rank4. The builder records an actual four-column minor and
constructs exact inverses. All geometry and all104^2 rational factorization
entries are checked directly from the pinned native source; no finite search
is promoted to a statement about a different root configuration.

The fourteen-class and inverse-transpose algebra has predecessors in the

## 2. Actual reverse Gram acquisition with original D restoration

For a class J_H choose four pivot positions p_i giving an invertible minor
of A_J. Replace those rows of the identity matrix by the four A_J rows,
obtaining M_D. This is invertible: eliminating the nonpivot identity rows
reduces its determinant to that nonzero four-column minor. The emitted
factorization uses only dyadic scalar ADD/SCALE operations.

At chart0 all class D fields contain their genuine independent originals.
Apply M_D. Its pivot p_i now contains

    sum_(h in J_H) g_i(h) d_h.

Move that public pivot through the actual line L_i=<z_i>. Add its current
row to one owned accumulator R_i, which was zero-initialized at that same
line. Then move the pivot to C_J. Nonpivots move directly0->C_J. Once every
class field is present, apply M_D^{-1} at C_J and restore each D_h=d_h.
Move each to its own final facet. No spent route is restarted.

There are eight stationary accumulators, one per mode line, shared by all14
classes. Each class contributes to its four supported modes. After all
classes, their actual rows are exactly

    R_i = sum_(h in H) g_i(h)d_h.                              (3)

The replay checks (3) before any of them moves to Hub, and checks every
individual D restoration at its own C3 boundary. Each R_i then makes its
own paid rank8 ascent to Hub. At Hub the actual B rows consume these modes
with coefficients -g_i(h), yielding B=b-G_HH d. All eight owners retire.
This is an actual source acquisition, not an inference from Gram rank8.

## 3. Forward reception by inverse-transpose Y coding

First handle each coupled source u using one real rank1 entry call: copy
its original y_u at0 to an owned field, move it to the actual initial X_u
line, subtract it from X_u, and retire the owner there. This creates the
a_u=x_u-y_u required by the native target. It changes no Y field. The eight
entry calls remain fully charged.

After the designated X/B compiler slot (currently200 direct raw ascents),
all104 authentic a_h are at Hub. Form

    F_i=sum_h g_i(h)a_h

in an actual owned field and descend it to L_i by a rank8 call. Keep all
eight F_i there until every required class read is complete.

For class J order its hosted ports first and its coupled ports last. Define
M_Y by replacing the same four hosted pivot rows of the identity by

    (c_h g_i(h))_(h in J),    c_h=1 or1/2 as appropriate.

Its hosted diagonal block is exactly M_D, its coupled diagonal block is the
identity, and its lower-left block is zero. Hence it is invertible even
though there is no independent D bank at the coupled ports. This is not a
physical aliasing of missing D fields.

Apply M_Y^{-T} to the actual Y bank at0. Move its pivot p_i through L_i,
where it adds the authentic F_i. Move all pivots/nonpivots to C_J and apply
M_Y^T there. If E inserts at the four pivot positions, then

    M_Y^T(M_Y^{-T}y_J+E F_S)
       = y_J + M_Y^T E F_S
       = y_J + (c_h sum_i g_i(h)F_i)_(h in J).

This is the Y component of(1). Every unknown original y survives; no donor
is mistaken for zero, and the coupled columns are explicitly weighted by1/2.
After decoding, every Y goes to its required facet. The forward owners retire
only after their real final reads.

The D and Y class encodings deliberately occur at different times. D is
restored before X/B work; Y remains at0 until all a values reach Hub and the
forward modes reach their lines. There is no circular requirement that a
forward packet exist before the X donors can reach Hub.

## 6. Exact remaining compiler interface and sufficient composition

The slot is between markers `XB_slot_begin` and `XB_slot_end`. At its start:
all X carry the authentic a on their initial lines; all B carry independent b
on their initial lines; all D carry original d at their own facets; all Y
still carry independent original y at0; and the eight real reverse owners(3)
are retained on their actual mode lines.

Put F=C(I-G). A sufficient replacement slot must:

1. restore all104 X=a at Hub and deliver all96 B to Hub with
   B=b-(I-G_HH)d;
2. leave every D=d, every Y=y and every retained reverse owner unchanged;
3. create authentic stationary correction records whose real later reads
   add exactly F_j a to each Y_j, with all owners explicitly retired;
4. provide an actual chronological chart path for every read. For the
   unchanged Y decoder, later charts must form a chain between that Y's
   group C3 and its facet. A full final-facet record is one sufficient
   special case; split records need their actual additional Y stops charged.

If the slot does this, the seed's reverse consumer gives B=b-d. Its forward
receiver stage and the correction records give

    Y=y+CGa+C(I-G)a=y+Ca.

For a hosted port this is y+x. For a coupled port it is
 y+(x-y)/2=(x+y)/2, while X=x-y already. Thus the complete native private
target is obtained on all400 fields. This is a proved conditional composition
identity with explicit physical premises. It is not a complete conditional
kappa theorem: the slot has not been constructed, and the uniform supplier,
ordinary physical/compiler, precision, source-access and foundational
contracts have not been discharged.

The slot may need more general current-row Y decoding or actual mixed-bank
relays to be efficient. Such a change is legitimate, but its whole source
and invoice must be rebuilt and proved. The inexpensive seed must not be
silently combined with old X/B or Y paths that do not meet these charts.
