# What a significant intermediate saving must change

**Scoped price theorems and target requirements. No new complete κ.**
The current open binary supplier, even with every positive paired-caller arm
count allowed, cannot produce raw saving κ at or above
`0.003879928046155`. The unchanged rational word independently has raw ceiling
below `0.00388013644475`. A balanced-source route to κ = .0043 still requires
every original directed native donor. These conclusions rule out specific
shortcuts; they are not upper bounds on integer multiplication or on changed
physical sources.

## 1. The current binary source, with all caller lengths granted

For `0 < t < 1`, write `[r] = r^t`. The actual #7786/#7835 native paid and
productive helper profiles are

```
N = {1:1376, 2:1281, 3:54, 4:44, 5:17, 6:102}
H = {1:8, 2:9, 4:43, 5:8, 6:1, 8:389}.
```

For each positive integer arm count `a`, grant the paired-caller extension

\[
G_a(t)=4a(H(t)-N(t))+111W_a(t),
\]

\[
W_a(t)=2(14a+1)^t+4\sum_{j=1}^a(7j+1)^t
          -4-4\sum_{j=1}^{a-1}(7j)^t.
\]

This retains all `4a` native occurrences, the four query banks and all body
idles. At `a=9` its complete signed histogram is exactly the authenticated
36-call receipt. The obstruction generously allows every other arm count
without asserting that a new physical caller or ordinary service has been
supplied at that count.

**Theorem 1.** For every integer `a >= 1` and every
`0.003879928046155 <= κ < 1`, one has `G_a(1-κ) < 0`.
At the crossing of the best arm, `a=9` is the unique integer optimum.

**Proof.** Strict concavity of `x^t` gives

\[
\Delta W_a=2[(14a+15)^t-(14a+1)^t]
             +4[(7a+8)^t-(7a)^t],
\]

which strictly decreases with `a`. Set
`J_a = a W_(a+1) - (a+1) W_a`. Then
`J_(a+1)-J_a=(a+1) Δ²W_a < 0`. The sign of `J_a` is the sign of
`W_(a+1)/(a+1)-W_a/a`, and the native term in `G_a/(4a)` is independent
of `a`.

At `κ_u=.003879928046155`, exact directed intervals give

```
J_8  in ( .121203849694468,  .121203849694469)
J_9  in (-.105404130327547, -.105404130327546)
G_9  in (-.000000000381874566916337,
         -.000000000381874566916336).
```

Thus `a=9` globally maximizes `G_a/(4a)` over all positive integers, and
even that value is negative. This is an all-arm proof, not an extrapolation
from a finite scan.

To extend to every larger κ, normalize by `8^t`. The native contribution is

\[
(H-N)/8^t=389-1368(1/8)^t-1272(2/8)^t-54(3/8)^t
 -(4/8)^t-9(5/8)^t-101(6/8)^t.
\]

It strictly increases with `t`. Rewrite the wrapper as

\[
W_a/8^t=2((14a+1)/8)^t+4((7a+1)/8)^t-4(1/8)^t
 +4\sum_{j=1}^{a-1}
 [((7j+1)/8)^t-(7j/8)^t].
\]

Each summand increases with `t` on `[0,1]`. For a difference term, the
derivative is a difference of `x^t log x`; this function has positive
`x` derivative `x^(t-1)(1+t log x)` when `x >= 7/8`. Indeed
`log(7/8) >= -1/7`. Consequently `G_a(t)/8^t` strictly increases with
`t`, completing the proof. The opposite signs at
`.003879928046154` and `.003879928046155` enclose the original best crossing.

## 2. Why tensor cutoffs, mixtures and repetitions cannot bypass it

For positive histograms `(P,T)`, the moment of a tensor product factors.
If `P(t) >= T(t)` for every admitted factor, then
`product P_i(t) >= product T_i(t)`. Positive weighted sums preserve the
same inequality. Splitting a paid rank `r` into positive ranks summing to
`r` can only increase its `t`-moment for `0<t<1`; discarding productive
slots can only decrease the productive moment. Any finite composition of
these operations therefore preserves failure at or above the theorem's
ceiling. This includes heterogeneous arm products and positive mixtures,
as long as they retain the declared source and moment contract.

This is the precise reason that a different tensor power, cutoff, tail tilt,
ordinary stock depth or vanishing compiler loss cannot produce a significant
jump from this native profile. A new cross-factor sharing construction,
additional productive identities or legitimately lower paid work changes
the premise and must be priced and verified as a new source.

Optimizing the arm count separately at representative larger targets gives:

| κ | Best arms | Native calls | Required net improvement per native |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| .004 | 9 | 36 | >1.284215691721080 |
| .0043 | 8 | 32 | >4.459941333825857 |
| .0045 | 8 | 32 | >6.558552957143805 |
| .005 | 7 | 28 | >11.738999605816155 |

Each optimum has the same global discrete-concavity proof, with exact
adjacent signs. As a **changed-profile control only**, removing five unit
jobs per native would give a positive .0043 gap of 17.28187731757256… at
eight arms. No operation achieving that deletion is asserted. This contrast
identifies the size of a useful source change rather than proposing more
digits of a fixed root.

## 3. The separate complex bottleneck remains

The authenticated #7830 complete 48-call rational invoice has a unique raw
crossing in `(.00388013644474,.00388013644475)` throughout the retained outer
range `0 <= κ <= 1/32`. The interval verifier checks both endpoint signs
from the entire paid and productive histograms.

For the signed profile coefficients `d_r`,
`G'(κ)=-sum d_r r^(1-κ) log r`. Bounding every positive coefficient at
`κ=1/32` and every negative coefficient at `κ=0` gives the uniform upper
bound `-791738.364668143490419… < 0`. The logarithms use exact rational
series bounds. Thus the crossing is unique on that range.

At .0043 the whole rational caller is short by
427.27640348200730… moment units, or more than 8.90159173920848 per native
copy. Improving the binary supplier alone cannot repair this unchanged
complex invoice. Retuning or replacing the complex caller is outside this
fixed-caller claim.

Raw movement price, uniform/covered supply, completed ordinary BIT and the
final multiplication exponent remain different interfaces. The complete
retained outer theorem still requires compatible strict complex and ordinary
binary savings above the requested final κ. This result discharges none of
the five foundational assumptions.

## 4. Exact return budget for a less ambitious physical source

For the balanced non-return movement profile, put

\[
Q_0(t)=\frac{4451}{8}+\frac{7213}{16}2^t+24\,4^t,
\]

and let `b` be the **actual field bank width**, `q` the complete one-way
return rank. The normalized source price is
`Q_0(t)+(q/b)8^t`. Every binary extension lane appears in both the paid
and productive scaling; it is not a free volume divisor.

Let `G_old(t)=T_2(t)-P_2(t)` be the original paired-caller gap with

```
N_old = {1:1386,2:1253,3:62,4:47,5:9,6:104,7:10}
H_old = {2:1,4:25,6:1,8:410}.
```

These are the admitted sharing interface's profiles, deliberately distinct
from the newer current native in section 1. Define

\[
L(t)=\frac{37\,127^t+74\,64^t-74
       +74\sum_{j=1}^8[(7j+1)^t-(7j)^t]}6.
\]

The complete finite even-m caller gap factors exactly as

\[
18(m-1)\left[L(t)-Q_0(t)-(q/b)8^t
                 +\frac{G_{\rm old}(t)}{36(m-1)}\right].
\]

Hence its strict movement condition is

\[
\frac qb<R_m(\kappa):=
\frac{L(1-\kappa)-Q_0(1-\kappa)
 +G_{\rm old}(1-\kappa)/(36(m-1))}{8^{1-\kappa}}. \tag{1}
\]

The analogous asymptotic expression `R_infty` is an unattained ceiling in
the displayed positive-target range because `G_old < 0` there. It is not
an infinite caller. At zero saving the old gap is positive: `G_old(1)=1506`.
The favorable finite credit is retained below.

Each `R_m` and `R_infty` strictly decreases with κ on `[0,1]`. To prove
this, normalize every term of `L-Q0` by `8^t`; it has positive terms at
64 and 127, negative terms below 8 and the same increasing adjacent
differences as section 1. The normalized original caller gap also increases
with `t`, since `H_old-N_old` has only negative coefficients below 8 and
one positive coefficient at 8. Positive finite scaling preserves this
monotonicity.

At the actual bank `b=3840`, the largest admissible integer return ranks are:

| .00387992802601 | 38,046 | 38,141 | 38,173 | 38,189 | 38,189 |
| .004 | 37,045 | 37,558 | 37,729 | 37,813 | 37,814 |

## 5. A significant intermediate gain still needs every directed donor

The inherited all-native converse gives `q >= (29/3)b` after any single
directed proper donor is deleted. For 6,462 of the 6,470 directions it gives
`q >= (68/7)b`, which becomes `q >= 37303` at `b=3840`. These are the
admitted Clebsch and punctured-Clebsch entropy/rank results, not new local
dual certificates in this package.

Combining those lower bounds with (1), strict monotonicity and exact root
enclosures gives the following upper endpoints for this balanced tariff:

| Omitted direction | m=2 κ ceiling | m=870 κ ceiling | limiting κ ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Any one of the 6,470 | <.00399110317610 | <.00422187529437 | <.00422258539286 |
| A regular direction, continuum bank | <.00396916383783 | <.00416339008389 | <.00416398770486 |
| A regular direction, bank 3,840 | <.00396914669777 | <.00416334439344 | <.00416394192654 |

Consequently **κ >= .0043 cannot be reached by deleting even one directed
native donor while retaining this tariff and return contract**, regardless
of finite even multiplicity. This includes arbitrary ranks on all other
proper blocks. The nonlinear entropy analogue requires the corresponding
physical broadcast-cost interface; no new nonlinear physical adapter is
assumed here.

This refines the construction target. The .0043 return allowance is much
larger than the .005 allowance, but it still forces a globally coordinated
source. Endpoint omission is not a shortcut to that significant intermediate
milestone. A different non-return tariff can change the conclusion.

## 6. Source-specific candidates and the normalization control

The current two-star word has actual bank 3,840 and inherited disjoint-minor
return lower bound 182,875. The eight-field-row projection word instead has
bank **480**, five binary extension lanes and return lower bound 52,860.
Its proper MOVE census, divided by all `480*5` paid lanes, is exactly Q0.
The two-star source/facet edits add no MOVE to its balanced source census.

At zero saving, `R_infty(0)=629/48`, while the maximum over **all finite
even m** is the larger `R_2(0)=55/3`. Both sources already have
`q/b > 55/3`: respectively `36575/768` and `881/8`. Since every finite cap
decreases with κ, neither fixed measured word can pass any nonnegative κ
under this source tariff and the exact return contract, even with the most
generous finite-m choice and before ordinary costs.

The bank distinction matters: falsely assigning bank 3,840 to the projection
would give `52860/3840 < 55/3` and reverse that zero-saving test. The verifier
checks its original-column count `2*111*480*5` and complete paid census to
reject that normalization. This is a scoped price corollary of inherited
rank certificates, not a fresh full replay of the very large native matrices.

## Verification and open next questions

Run `python -I -B verify.py --out FRESH_DIRECTORY`. The standard-library
verifier authenticates eleven predecessor inputs, reconstructs the complete
arm-nine and rational invoices, proves every displayed sign with directed
integer-root intervals, locates each threshold with exact bisection and
reproduces the receipt. Its proof-relevant checks use exceptions and remain
active under `python -O`. The independent proof review is a separate gate.

The power engine uses 160 binary exponent digits on a 384-bit dyadic grid;
each square root and multiplication rounds outward, and the unused exponent
tail is bounded by the final root interval. Logarithms use the positive
`atanh` series after reducing the argument to `[1,2]`, with an explicit
geometric tail. No floating result determines a sign.

Next useful construction targets are (i) an actual binary source with net
per-native improvement above 4.459941333825858 at .0043; or (ii) a coordinated
balanced source with q at most 36,875 at m=870, while preserving all donors
and its paid ordinary implementation. Either still needs the separately
improved complex source and the complete supplier, ownership, uniformity,
precision and outer contracts. The exact five-unit profile control is not
an algorithm. No upstream PR or selected certificate is changed.
