## The transferable complex interface

The main useful separation is between (a) an exact finite native operator, (b) occurrence-level composition, and (c) physical realization and asymptotic price. An altered word can use this architecture once its own contracts are proved.

**Conditional common-chart lifting lemma.** At every positive multiplicity f, suppose each actual chart v has a specified invertible whole-field operator T_v(f), and every route u to v realizes exactly T_v(f) T_u(f)^(-1), including its absolute phase. Suppose every local row is a scalar linear combination of fields in one actual chart, with each rational coefficient applied once to the entire field. Conjugate a field resident at v by T_v(f)^(-1). Every communication becomes identity on the abstract field; every local combination retains its scalar coefficients. Induction over the literal causal schedule proves that its endpoint is the scalar incidence matrix tensored with the common field, conjugated by the fixed entrance and outlet chart operators. Consequently, two such schedules with the same entrance/outlet charts and exactly the same complete scalar endpoint matrix implement the same endpoint at every f, including correlated inputs and arbitrary spectator contents.

This is a short mathematical inference from the explicit relative-chart mechanism in COMPILER and WEIGHTED-REVIEW, not a claim that an S031 candidate already satisfies its hypotheses. For a constructive repair it explains why an exact full 1,289-field scalar endpoint certificate could be a sufficient algebraic bridge. Merely preserving selected terminals, total rank, a few cuts, or the endpoint matrix on one particular input is insufficient.

The hypotheses must be checked at actual chart identities. Canonicalizing equal binary spans in our cut screens is an admissible relaxation for impossibility; it does not by itself produce the actual whole-field adapters or authorize exchanging histories between distinct chart labels. Any positive witness must choose concrete chart endpoints and verify the corresponding relative maps. For the complex atlas, tensor-intrinsic phases act once per column while exterior edge coefficients act once per field.
