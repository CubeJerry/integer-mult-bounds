## 1. Enlarged interface

Work over any field F and any whole-record width V>=1 in the actual rank8 BIT
geometry. Independent original X inputs ascend from111 lines L_j to Hub;
independent original Y inputs ascend from ground to111 distinct facets F_i.

There are q_H descending lifetimes born at Hub and q_P further descending

to ground before owned clearing. Z_j is its first-ground value before mixing.
Zero-born stationary caches and separately charged ascending records are allowed.
No other independent nonzero input, uncounted restart, path reversal, cross-chart
read or descending lifetime cleared before ground is covered. Track physical
lifetimes through renaming, including all prefix acquisitions.

Actual cocharted X/Y/packet/cache operations may interleave freely. A proposed
handoff's nonlocal state is never promoted into free initial input. Proper
early termination would require a separate extension of the present theorem.
Every complete candidate must still restore all original X values and close
all owned lifetimes. X restoration is not needed for this necessary normal-form
deduction, and is not waived as a construction requirement.

## 2. Rectangular actual cells

Identify chart aliases and aggregate chronological operations at each proper
nonzero C. Formal inputs U contain all ascending arrivals and actual original

P_out contains those records leaving below and the b_C descenders born at C.

    P_out=N_C U+M_C P_in,

    P_in=R_C(P_out-N_C U)+J_C T_C,                         (2)

## 3. Normal form and mode count

Let G concatenate every actual first-ground Z_j and every T_C. Each history

    H_C=L_C G+E_C y+B_C x,                                (4)

Induct on the finite chart poset from low dimension upward. Ground histories

there are local. Every outgoing descender, including a newborn, has its next
arrival at a smaller chart, or its first-ground boundary. Substitute these
normalized values into(2), preserving T_C and image condition(3). Every local
history is generated by U and P_in. Left multiplication creates no new source

    rank(G_x) <= (q_H+q_P)V+sum_C nu_C
