# Inverse-support cuts exclude every clean first-two interface

## Main theorem

Let a=A^tensor V be the retained literal four-bit source and
L=[[a,0,0],[I,a,0],[0,0,I]], U=[[a,-a²,0],[0,a,0],[0,0,I]].
Let C,Q and endpoint encodings be fixed finite ordinary units, with literal
scalar phases and ordinary inverse recipes retained. If both a C00 a^-1
and a C10 a^-1 are ordinary, no word E=L C U Q L is ordinary-equivalent
to independent Gaussian jobs(4,2,1) at all sufficiently large faithful
widths. C20 and Q may be arbitrary ordinary, noncommuting and outside the
entire source-square algebra. This excludes every coherent first interface
R(D_b^tensor V)diag(I,h,I), for arbitrary ordinary h and Q.

## Argument

Independent421 has three component images of rankD in direct sum.
Clean transport gives rank4 image equal to the graph column
V=(a s a^-1,a t a^-1,0). Last-column rank3/rank2/ordinary projections and
image directness force C=[[s,b,0],[t,d,e],[u,f,0]], Q02=Q12=0 and unit
s,e,q22,Q_A. The top/bottom quotient is the exact ordered identity
J E=[M,0], M=diag(a,I) B U_A Q_A L_A, B=[[s,b],[u,f]] a unit.

For any factorization M=P W whose inverse prefix lies in the span of
unitary one-column words with inverse support at most2^s and whose suffix
has support at most2^t, an independent Gaussian rank r exceeds neither
s+t. Indeed multiplying an asserted endpoint column by P^-1 separates
words with support at least2^(r-s) from words with support at most2^t.
The lower bound follows by applying the sparse inverse to each column.
Faithful finite tensor separation then forces the column zero, contradicting
injectivity. This is a word-catalogue proof, not a support claim for sums.

Here P=diag(a,I) B U_A has P^-1=U_A^-1 B^-1 diag(a^-1,I), of inverse
degree2, and W=Q_A L_A has degree1. The asserted rank4 output column has
injective top/bottom projection by the graph image, contradicting this
F2/F1 cut. Ordinary stable cancellation and unproved inverses of singular
individual entries are unnecessary. The original L U L=(0,-a^4;a²,0)
has inverse prefix degree3 and suffix degree1 and attains rank4, showing
why the theorem does not exclude every three-native architecture.
