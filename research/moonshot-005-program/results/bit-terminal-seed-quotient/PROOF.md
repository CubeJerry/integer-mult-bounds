## 2. General sharp quotient lemma

Let D,W,N,E be finite-dimensional spaces over a field, with N contained in D. Put

    n=dim N, r=dim((D+W)/D), k=dim(N intersect E),
    z=dim(E intersect(D+W))-dim(E intersect D).

A space T of dimension at most q, containing N and satisfying W contained in D+T, exists exactly when q>=n+r, if arbitrary lifts are allowed. Its maximum possible desired-code intersection is

    max dim(T intersect E)=min(dim E, k+z+q-n-r).     (2)

Quotienting T by D proves the size condition. To bound the intersection, map T/(T intersect E) into the ambient quotient by D+E. The kernel contains the image of N, of dimension n-k. The image contains the image of W, of dimension r-z. Hence dim T-dim(T intersect E)>=n-k+r-z, proving the upper bound.

These lifts together with N span n+r dimensions and supply W modulo D. Their intersection with E has dimension k+z. Add independent E vectors until E is contained or the q-dimensional budget is used. Each adds one to the intersection, proving(2). In the application W,E,N and chosen lifts are pure X and can be actual Hub seeds.
