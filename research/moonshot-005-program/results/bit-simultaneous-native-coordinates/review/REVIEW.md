# Independent review of the synchronized 111-role partial decoder

**Verified.** The balanced source supports 69 common field-coordinate corrections at every native role from true late Y visits to donor lines. One early native-plane cache for each donor raises this to 76. All these outputs are genuine y+x on the same coordinates of all 111 donors. The argument admits GF(64). The other Y coordinates retain their independent input plus an explicitly linear function of the selected X coordinates. The bank has 5760 paid field slots per role and this partial result does not give a complete supplier or a new κ.

## Native graph and independent flow

The checker reads the pinned 120-line layout and balanced factor. It recomputes the D4 orthogonal involution from the integer dot products and the 16 A2 planes contained in every D4 by inclusion. Denote the per-native early capacity on plane H by e_H=(12/m_H)+f_H for m_H>0, where m_H is the number of native donor lines and f_H is the pinned factor. The empty plane has e_H=0. Put x_H=m_He_H and y_H=max(8−e_H,0). The late graph for j has source-to-H capacity 45y_H for each H through j, H-to-D capacity 24−x_H for each true H inside D, D-to-K capacity x_K for K in the orthogonal D4, and K-to-i capacity 45e_K for native i in K perpendicular to j. At D the number of incoming Y is 384−sum x_H from its first-side planes plus exactly the paid split-carrier deficit, which restores equality to the outgoing Y count. At every K the total outgoing capacity equals its 45x_K incoming Y. All 111 donors have exactly 5760 early X and all 111 terminals exactly 5760 late Y.

For each donor the checker sets k=floor((sum of its true 45y_H line visits)/d_j), with d_j its native orthogonal degree. An independent C++ Dinic computation attains k times d_j after a k-capacity terminal arc at every neighbor. Every primal arc and divergence is checked, while its input-line cut excludes any larger k. The common minimum is 69 for donor 80, whose 4320 visits serve 62 neighbors. For the cached version choose one already-paid H through j. At least 45e_H early X lanes of that donor visit H. Assign the selected independent raw coordinates to separate such lanes, record their original values in stationary H backups and let the originals continue unmodified. Those values can add arbitrary selected-input forms to any of H's 45(24−x_H) *late Y* lanes; source-to-H is replaced by that number. This replacement includes the existing line visits and does not add their capacities twice. For every donor a plane maximizing this replacement's extra capacity attains the resulting floor, now with uniform minimum 76. Each assignment needs no more selected coordinates than actual early slots at that donor and H. Distinct donors may share a plane; their genuine early records are distinct and local additions superpose.

## A single choice of all mixing matrices

Give the H,D,K,F mixers independent dense scalar variables at each site, acting on all and only the genuine Y records present at its stage. Every Y route proceeds forward and every record passes a given site at most once. For a fixed donor, an integral flow of k times d_j gives that many edge-disjoint unit paths after capacity expansion, ending in k distinct positions at each receiver. The F mixers permit the same predetermined k output positions to be used in this proof for every donor. At each site each incoming used lane is paired with a distinct outgoing lane; complete each local partial pairing to a permutation of all local Y lanes. The matching path minor of the donor's transfer is then a permutation minor. For an H cache model its port-injection bases may be viewed as added scalar inputs on the outgoing H slots, or equivalent independent additions before an invertible H mix. Either convention preserves this routing witness. Other donors' injections are zero during this individual polynomial nonvanishing argument.

Multiply one such nonzero donor minor for each of the 111 donors and the determinant of every local site mixer. The resulting polynomial is nonzero. An individual variable in one site gives a rank-one perturbation to a donor matrix since the transfer factors before and after this single DAG site. Multilinearity in that perturbation implies degree at most one in each donor minor, regardless of how many paths use the chart. The variable can occur only for donors whose native line belongs to that chart. The exact maximum native populations are 3,12,36,62 for H,D,K,F respectively. The variable also appears with degree at most one in its own site's invertibility determinant and never in another site's determinant. Individual degree is therefore at most 63. By the finite-grid polynomial lemma the product has a nonzero specialization over GF(64). This proves existence; the package supplies integral routing witnesses and a field-size bound, without enumerating one global numerical coefficient assignment.

Fix such a choice. For each donor separately its transfer to the predetermined coordinates at every neighbor, stacked together, is surjective. Solve one right-inverse system to make its chosen k raw field coordinates contribute the same k-by-k identity block at every neighbor. The resulting source additions to the real Y lanes are independently selectable linear forms in that donor's own raw inputs, captured on its line or on its one paid plane. One local Y lane may receive additions from several donors; linear superposition is permitted. A nonneighbor can receive none of a donor's additions because every route from its native line to that facet is increasing in chart containment. Thus the selected terminal projection of actual donor injections is precisely the orthogonality matrix times the common selected source coordinates.

## Whole-input preservation and scope

All early genuine X records travel raw to Hub. Before leaving their native line the selected ones are captured by local stationary owners, and at the chosen H the cached construction makes its additional captures. A read-only field bank can add any linear form of its contents to any current Y at that chart. Its backup INIT, bitwise field ADD, retention and dirty CLEAR require ordinary accounting; the backup itself does not move. Chosen raw X continue to their own genuine Hub endpoints. On a sufficiently long field block arbitrary invertible local GF(64) maps have reversible binary ADD implementations; finite operation counts and their caller costs remain open until compiled.

Let M be the transfer of all genuine Y records with every donor injection set to zero, including their passage to their assigned output labels. It is a product of invertible local transformations and known permutations, hence a global bijection of the full late Y bank. The ground stage first forms the prescribed Y plus the selected coordinates of K_i from the actual nine canonical packets, then applies M inverse while all genuine independent Y fields are co-located. It can do this by reversing the recorded local matrix factors on their physical record labels at ground. In particular the inverse acts on all original Y inputs; replaying it after adding the sparse canonical contribution is legitimate without any Y=0 assumption. The subsequent actual late network contributes the identity Y on every slot, precisely the chosen K_i on the selected slots, and the true donor additions. On the selected slots the total correction is K_i+(OX)_i=X_i over characteristic two, where O is native orthogonality. The other slots are their own original Y plus a linear nuisance depending only on selected X. This does not establish their full targets.

For a separate global information comparison, binary row reduction gives rank(O)=108 and rank(I+O)=9, and the total genuine donor-line Y visit capacity is 481500 field slots. In a construction that uses canonical ground information only by transforming Y+KX and then supplying separate source-to-late interfaces, choose Y=−KX. Late Y starts independent of X and the requested final values now range over OX, of dimension 108×5760=622080. The line visits plus any extra crossing or stationary source interfaces must distinguish that many field-symbol dimensions. Extra such interfaces therefore require at least 140580 field slots of range. This count allows arbitrary deterministic processing after the strictly prescribed ground use. If the entire nine canonical words may be reused arbitrarily, fix Y=0 and expose them explicitly. They have joint dimension at most 9×5760. The line visits and all additional source interfaces together with those words must transmit the desired 111×5760 independent inputs, leaving 106020 field slots beyond the line visits. Both figures are information capacities. The added interfaces can be stationary native-plane and higher-chart taps; neither inequality is a count of additional paid MOVEs.
