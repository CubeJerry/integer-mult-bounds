## Literal chronology and proof

Initialize one stationary AFF_z owner at the specified chart for each nonempty J_z. The listed originals already visit that chart. Immediately after the original's first arrival, and **before its reversible aggregate/fork recoding**, ADD its still-raw X_j value into AFF_z. The ADD leaves the original unchanged. Thus AFF_z contains precisely r_z. All captures finish in the base source prefix before principal queries begin. No source is assumed available merely because its native line lies below a chart.

Each P_z already visits its acquisition chart on its paid descending route. Query AFF_z there, adding r_z to its actual current value. All subsequent source queries add fixed, unchanged values, so the packet differs from the old packet by r_z until its first retention. It does not acquire a second independent free payload. The first retained field therefore becomes a_z+r_z. The next ADD of that same retained field back into the principal resets the principal to zero exactly as before. Consequently the newly loaded h row, every subsequent through-cell, every line retention and the terminal principal value remain unchanged. This proves the entire later source suffix is unaffected except for the explicitly changed first-retention readers.

For z=0,1,2, both old twin receivers already ascend through C0 (also named M0), dimension 5, and read the first retained value there. Their values consequently gain r_z. The actual nesting is C0 subset D_iq subset F_i and F_q, with dimensions 5,6,7. Insert D_iq on each old receiver's final C0-to-facet move and read AFF_z. This adds r_z again and cancels the perturbation in characteristic two. The receiver then reads its original held private value and completes exactly as before. AFF_z is stationary at D_iq throughout; this is an actual cocharted read.

For z=3,4,5, the old receiver reads its changed first retention at M1 and later reaches its own final F_i. AFF_z is already at F_i. Read it during that existing visit, cancelling r_z without any extra MOVE. Every other receiver reads unchanged fields, so every endpoint is preserved. This includes independent arbitrary initial Y values, since the only differences are the two cancelling r_z terms.

Retire every AFF_z after the old word. Originals and all pre-existing owner lifetimes are unchanged. The new field can be nonzero at CLEAR, and its dirty retirement is explicitly counted. The argument is an identity of binary linear operations, so holds after tensoring with the identity on any record width and after extension to any field of characteristic two.

The old first-retention value is **replaced**, not retained for free alongside the new one. The independent r_z remains at the higher chart and is available only to receivers that actually reach that chart, or through additional explicitly paid transport. A later envelope must use a_z+r_z at C0/M1 and r_z at its true chart. It may not grant a_z and r_z together at the lower chart.

## Complete incremental accounting

Let a count nonempty h0 subsets among z=0,1,2, b count nonempty h1 subsets among z=3,4,5, and w=sum_z |J_z|. Relative to the 30-output base, the added invoice is:

- INIT: a+b.
- ADD: w+3a+2b: w raw captures, a+b principal reads, 2a+b receiver corrections.
- MOVE: 2a additional calls, all on original Y receivers.
- Dirty CLEAR: a+b.
- No new owned MOVE and no original X MOVE or recoding change.

Each h0 twin pair replaces two rank-2 Y moves by four rank-1 moves. At t=199/200 the movement-moment increment is a*(4-2*2^t). The h1-private changes add no movement. All INIT/ADD/retirement costs remain part of the physical invoice; the small movement increment is not a complete supplier saving.
