## Coefficients and local compilation

Use the two-dimensional binary regular representation of

    F4=F2[lambda]/(lambda^2+lambda+1).

A pair of independent whole records is written as one F4 vector only as
notation. Multiplication by lambda is a two-by-two binary map across those
records. It is never an operation inside one record.

Let p_i=1 for even i and lambda for odd i. Let q_i=lambda for even i and1
for odd i. Thus p_(i+1)=q_i, q_(i+1)=p_i, and both have period2. Use

    Round1 at A_i,B_i:        T1_i=[[1,p_i],[1,q_i]],
    Round2 at A_i,B_(i+1):    T2_i=[[1,0],[1,1]],
    Round3 at A_i,B_(i+2):    T3_i=[[1,1],[p_i^-1,q_i^-1]].

Their determinants are p_i+q_i,1, and p_i^-1+q_i^-1, all nonzero.
Replacing each scalar by its two-by-two multiplication matrix gives an
invertible four-by-four binary map. Gaussian elimination reduces it to
identity with row ADDs and three-ADD row swaps. Reversing that elimination
word implements the map, without ancillas or movement. Its determinant1
makes it invertible over every characteristic-two k.

The literal local compiler lengths, in event order, are

    Round1: 7,6,7,6; Round2: 2,2,2,2; Round3: 9,10,9,10.

The full forward network therefore uses72 ADDs; its inverse uses the same72.
This does not contradict S020's homogeneous F4 failure, which used one
fixed matrix on every edge of each round. Here T1_i and T3_i alternate.
Nor does it contradict the binary two-record gate: every meeting now has
four independently paid records and a GL4(F2) local map.

## Caller normalization and boundaries

joint native supplier Q2, the conservative invoice would be

    P_m^(2)=2[(m/2)P2-18(m-1)N]+18(m-1)Q2,
    T_m^(2)=2[(m/2)T2-18(m-1)H].

instantiated caller. It excuses neither copy's operations and does not
prove compatible unbounded-size, recursive, compiler, precision or erasure

Scalar proper-cover/occurrence bounds are not inherited. Both Y banks share
each facet and mix through actual ADDs. Those bounds require fresh premises
and a valid rank-weighted or physical occurrence argument.

ADDs and within-record permutations preserve constant-bit-vector records.

the two entries are separate whole records: constant records remain
constant, while the local map acts on four constant coefficients. The
literal two banks and their complete invoices are essential.
