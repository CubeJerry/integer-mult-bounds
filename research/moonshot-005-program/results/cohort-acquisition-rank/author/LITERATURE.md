method in index coding. Credit Ziv Bar-Yossef, Yitzhak Birk, T.S. Jayram and
Tomer Kol, *Index Coding with Side Information*, IEEE Transactions on
Information Theory57(3),1479--1494(2011), DOI10.1109/TIT.2010.2103753; the
FOCS2006 predecessor appeared on pages197--206, DOI10.1109/FOCS.2006.42.

Primary author-hosted text:
https://webee.technion.ac.il/people/zivby/papers/indexing/indexing.full.pdf
