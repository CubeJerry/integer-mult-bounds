# Global rigidity of rank-nine native fits

The actual root graph has 6,470 directed off-diagonal proper cells on 111 roles. Let $M$ have its own diagonal blocks $I_b$, zero on nonforeign off-diagonal pairs, and arbitrary foreign blocks over any field. Suppose $\operatorname{rank}M\le9b+h$. It suffices to assume invertibility on the 221 edges of the exhibited input/output spanning tree. At $h=0$ this forces every foreign cell to be invertible and the whole fitting to be a simultaneous native gauge of $K\otimes I_b$ in characteristic two. In every odd characteristic such a tree-invertible fitting must instead satisfy $h\ge b/8$.

## The local rank implication

Each cited square has two disjoint exterior row roles and two distinct exterior donor roles and all four cells are proper. Its eight anchors are disjoint from all four endpoints, and no two distinct anchors are foreign. Their principal submatrix is block diagonal with invertible diagonal. For every corner $(i,j)$ and every anchor $c$, at least one of $(i,c)$ and $(c,j)$ is nonforeign. Row and column elimination of the eight anchor pivots therefore leaves the original two-by-two square unchanged. This also holds after separate role-wise invertible transformations on the row and column sides, since zeros stay zero and the anchor diagonals stay invertible. The physical fitting rank bound gives rank at most $b+h$ for that square.

Normalize the 221 bipartite tree entries to $I_b$ by independent row/column basis choices. If three corners have rank distances at most $e_1,e_2,e_3$ from $I_b$, replace those three by $I_b$. Rank subadditivity bounds the new square rank by $b+h+e_1+e_2+e_3$. In any row/column ordering, the resulting three-identity square has rank $b+\operatorname{rank}(U-I_b)$, where $U$ is the fourth corner. Thus $\operatorname{rank}(U-I_b)\le h+e_1+e_2+e_3$. The other three original blocks and $U$ may all be singular.

`check.py` verifies the 221-edge connected tree and every anchor, support, separation and earlier-corner hypothesis directly from the contracted root inner products. The 4,668 and 1,581 additions cover the exact remaining 6,249 foreign entries, without repeating a corner or taking credit for another simultaneous-layer result. The unweighted transcript already yields maximum error $3h$. The different weighted certificate is independently checked in `weighted.py`. Its counts of coefficients $0,1,2,3$ are 221, 4,668, 405 and 1,176, respectively. A wrong-level addition and a nonexterior anchor fail different checks.

## Own labels and simultaneous gauges

After the separate normalization write the own diagonal as $D_i=S_iT_i$. In characteristic two the canonical $K$ has rank nine. A different disjoint nine-square $K_{R,C}$ is certified for each role $i$, with $i$ outside $R\cup C$. Its integer determinant is odd, and its ten-square border including canonical own $i$ has even determinant. There are no other own diagonal entries in the border. Replacing all proper entries of the actual border by their canonical values costs at most $q_ih$, where the weighted receipts give $q_i\le10$. The bordered canonical matrix with the still-arbitrary $D_i$ has rank exactly $9b+\operatorname{rank}(D_i-I_b)$ by the Schur complement. Hence $\operatorname{rank}(D_i-I_b)\le11h$. This gives genuine simultaneous native gauges $G_i=S_i^{-1}$, with $\operatorname{rank}(M_{ij}-G_iG_j^{-1})\le14h$ at every original foreign cell. Equality with the gauge form follows at $h=0$. The independent checker also constructs separate disjoint odd nine-pivots for every role.

If a tree edge can have corank at most $\delta$, complete it to an invertible matrix by a perturbation of rank at most $\delta$, ignoring the endpoints of other edges only while choosing the gauges. Normalize those invertible approximants. The original tree edges now have rank distance at most $\delta$ from $I_b$. The same induction gives $ch+(2c+1)\delta$ for each weight-$c$ entry because $c=1+\sum c_j$ across each rectangle. For a diagonal frame with $p_i$ foreign blocks and total weight $q_i$, replacement costs at most $q_ih+(2q_i+p_i)\delta$. The independent exact receipts give $\max(2q_i+p_i)=47$. Therefore every diagonal $D_i$ is within $11h+47\delta$ of $I_b$. The same *one family* of native gauges fits every foreign edge within $14h+54\delta$, including all role-wise label reconciliation.

## Odd characteristics

The supplied disjoint ten-square canonical integer submatrix has determinant 2, so its tensor with $I_b$ has rank $10b$ over every odd-characteristic field. Its 22 foreign cells cost seven in total on the weighted transcript. The actual fitting has rank at most $9b+h$; changing those cells to canonical increases rank by at most $7h+36\delta$. Hence $8h+36\delta\ge b$. At $\delta=0$ this excludes the limiting 134-field and $m=870$ 128-field excess budgets when $b=3{,}840$. The independent search in `odd_search.py` additionally found a different disjoint integer determinant $-2$ of weight seven, recorded in `output/ODD.json`.
