# Common operator layers each require canonical innovation

**Proved all-characteristic-two-field/all-width theorem; independently
reviewed. No physical source, complete supplier or new kappa.**

For a target block fitting with arrival charge at most194, let d be its
quotient dimension modulo the nine-dimensional canonical tensor code.
If all coefficient blocks preserve a common invariant flag with layer
widths v_a over any field extension, then

    d >= V + sum_a ceil(v_a/17).

The weighted denominator is17-min(b,9), where b counts double-saving rows.
In particular every simultaneously triangularizable fitting requires d>=2V.
All commuting coefficient families satisfy this conclusion, including
nonsemisimple or nonsplit families: they triangularize over an algebraic
closure and matrix ranks do not change. Noncommutativity by itself does
not remove a common flag.

The [proof](author/PROOF.md) applies the admitted proportional quotient
bound to diagonal layers of the entire fitting-plus-canonical stack. A
rectangular block-minor lemma proves the required additive rank bound.
The [independent review](review-root/REVIEW.md) checks this universal step,
field extension, native sharp code and independently written controls.

An explicit scalar two-patch fitting has charge193 and quotient dimension2;
its identity tensor lift attains2V. It is an algebraic boundary control,
not a physical decoder. Under the point-retained interface its small
supported innovation still cannot complete all endpoints. Quotient bounds
are not unrestricted fitting-rank bounds or packet counts.

The prior [density theorem](../bit-operator-quotient-density/ADMISSION.md),
its native geometry, and the [point handoff theorem](../bit-point-retained-handoff-gate/ADMISSION.md)
are explicit dependencies. Both strict suppliers, full source realization,
and all five foundations remain open. A fractional-width construction must
supply coefficient families without a complete common invariant flag.
