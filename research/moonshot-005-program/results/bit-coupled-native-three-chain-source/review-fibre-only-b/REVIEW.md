# Independent review: donor-dependent quotients with fixed common fibres

## Verdict and admitted scope

**Accepted.** The frozen `fibre-only` package constructs a positive paid
source on the seven donors `0,25,45,47,61,62,100`, with common inverse fibres
at H263, H642 and H135, while genuinely changing four individual quotient
kernels. Its full 111-source invoice is **584,743,880 ADD**, with
**2,517,120 INIT** and the same number of dirty CLEARs. There is no extra
source MOVE.

The separate closing proposition is also accepted in its stated scope:
retaining the specified **101 native whole inverse-fibre subspaces** and
native source-cut ranks excludes each of the five native closing centres
containing at least two existing donors. Replacement source maps may mix
all original bank coordinates. The proposition concerns these retained
fibres; it does not exclude a construction which moves them.

These are a local positive source theorem and a conditional closure
obstruction. They do not supply a complete endpoint, downstream H/D/K/facet
word, complete return, or improved multiplication exponent.

## Closing theorem: tightness and arbitrary bank mixing

For a proposed replacement quotient at flag (j,Q), preservation of the old
whole inverse fibres forces its rows to annihilate their span S. The
review computes S using **native receivers only**, independently of the
larger geometric census used for the positive example. At every flag in
the five certificates its annihilator E has dimension k in each component.

The original full bank is the direct sum of sixteen copies of the marked
and unmarked components. Thus the full annihilator has dimension
`16*(3+12)=240`. Each native source view contains sixteen ordinary quotients
with total capacity 3840 fields. Invertibility of that source view forces
each quotient to have rank 240. A replacement quotient has its row space
contained in E and the same dimension as E, so its row space equals E.
This step remains valid for arbitrary mixing between components and
lanes. It does not assume the replacement respects the displayed direct
sum. Equivalently, expansion over GF2 multiplies each relevant dimension
by six and gives the same argument for arbitrary binary linear maps.
