# A connected three-centre source on seven actual donors

## Statement and scope

At the pinned native bank of 3,840 GF(64) fields, there is one paid original
source on each donor in

\[
J=\{0,25,45,47,61,62,100\}
\]

that simultaneously supplies the canonical foreign identity inverse fibres
at the three connected H interfaces

\[
H_{263}=\{0,61,62\},\qquad
H_{642}=\{45,61,100\},\qquad
H_{135}=\{25,45,47\}.
\]

The two shared donors are **different**: 61 joins the first two centres, and
45 joins the last two. Both have one literal source at all 28 incident
planes and one causal early basis. The construction does not give either
shared donor an independent source for each adjacent centre.

For each component, the exact verifier checks all 441 geometric foreign
cuts of the seven sources, including all 408 native cuts, and all 108
geometric centre/receiver inverse fibres. Of these inverse fibres, 101 have
native receivers, giving 303 native donor/centre/receiver fibres. All three
donors of a centre agree on the **entire** k-dimensional source inverse
fibre, not only on selected vectors or a rank witness.

The three designated interfaces contain no donor 10 and are invisible to
receiver 80. Thus none of these local canonical requirements directly
specifies a block on either perturbed S028 star. This is an eligibility
fact, **not** a proof that the complete source extends to the S028 target.

This package is a source/H construction. It gives no common D/K/facet
continuation, no complete fitting matrix, no whole return rank, and no new
complete multiplication exponent. It is a separate source word from the
44-centre/all111 construction. The seven new words cannot simply replace
seven words of that packing while retaining all its centre claims.

## 1. Native point quotients and one common original domain

Use the field

\[
\mathbb F=\mathbb F_2[z]/(z^6+z+1).
\]

The marked and unmarked components have k=3 and k=12, respectively. A scalar
spinor component has original input dimension 16k, and a source plane has
k rows. The 16 actual spinor lanes give

\[
16(16\cdot3+16\cdot12)=3840
\]

fields per original donor bank. Nothing is divided by the dimension of a
successful subspace.

For each selected centre H, orthogonal projection identifies the 27
off-centre source planes of each of its three donors with the same 27
singular points. Identify equal point quotients across those three donors.
Take the transitive closure over the three centres. The native geometry
gives 34 quotient classes. Every donor still has 28 distinct classes among
its 28 incident planes. There is no identification of two different source
planes on the same donor.

Write q_g in F^k for the quotient vector at class g. Initially the vector
q=(q_g) has 34k coordinates. The quotient class for the central source
plane of donor j at H is its control C_(H,j). These controls belong to the
same 34-class system as every off-centre quotient; they are not appended
independent copies.

## 2. Two general port equations

At a centre H, let V be the actual 3k-by-3k native Vandermonde matrix on
the sorted component ports, using nonzero field nodes 1,...,3k. For a port
d with row v_d, define the two control differences

\[
\Delta_d(q)=
\begin{pmatrix}
v_{d,0}C_{H,j_0}+v_{d,1}C_{H,j_1}\\
v_{d,0}C_{H,j_0}+v_{d,2}C_{H,j_2}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Here each v_(d,s) is the consecutive k-column block for the actual sorted
donor j_s. Let x_0,x_1,x_2 be the three singular points on the port's
radical line, and let g(H,x_t) be their quotient classes. Impose

\[
U_{H,d}\Delta_d(q)+
\sum_{t=0}^{2} A_{H,d,x_t}\,q_{g(H,x_t)}=0,
\tag{1}
\]

where U is invertible 2-by-2 and each A is an unrestricted 2-by-k matrix.
All coefficients are generated by the explicit SHAKE-256 tags in
`construction.py`; the frozen seeds are 1 for k=3 and 3 for k=12.

There are 3k ports per centre, two equations per port, and three centres.
The resulting matrix R has shape 18k by 34k. The exact ranks are

| k | Shape of R | Rank of R | Dimension of ker R |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 54 by 102 | 54 | 48 |
| 12 | 216 by 408 | 216 | 192 |

Let N be the reduced-echelon kernel basis with free columns in increasing
order. Its columns give one common coordinate domain F^(16k). The source
matrix at actual flag (j,h) is exactly the k-row block

\[
F_{j,h}=N_{g(j,h)}.
\]

This definition is simultaneous. In particular, the matrices at donors 61
and 45 are not selected separately for their two centres.

### Why this escapes the independent-mask obstruction

The older ramp family reserves one selected share at each point of a
radical line. A single point then contributes rank at most one to the two
port equations. Three such reserved groups exhaust a k-row source quotient
when three centres are coupled; an unseen radical port can leave a hidden
mask direction.

Equation (1) permits the same existing point quotient to contribute two
independent linear forms. No new field is sent. The quotient still has
exactly k rows. This removes the rank-one restriction on a point's
contribution to the two port equations. The source is obtained from the
joint equations, so it does not retain independent port masks that need to
be transported afterward.

## 3. Proof of whole inverse-fibre agreement

Fix a selected H and a geometric receiver i containing it. Its off-centre
view consists of 15 singular points. By the literal quotient
identifications, the three donors have the same 15k-by-16k annihilator
matrix A_(H,i), up to an irrelevant ordering of quotient rows.

The finite verifier checks rank(A_(H,i))=15k. Hence

\[
K_{H,i}=\ker A_{H,i}
\]

has dimension k. For every port d seen by i, all three radical points of d
belong to this 15-point set. On K_(H,i), the point terms in (1) vanish.
Since U_(H,d) is invertible, both control differences vanish. Stacking the
k visible ports therefore gives

\[
V_{i,0}F_{j_0,H}|_{K_{H,i}}
=V_{i,1}F_{j_1,H}|_{K_{H,i}}
=V_{i,2}F_{j_2,H}|_{K_{H,i}}.
\]

The checker also verifies rank([A_(H,i); V_(i,0)F_(j0,H)])=16k. Thus the
common readout is injective on the whole k-dimensional fibre. This is the
source compatibility needed by the local canonical foreign sum target.

The actual source cut at each foreign receiver consists of 16 plane
quotients. Every one of these 16k-by-16k matrices is checked at full rank,
for both component sizes and all seven donors. The inherited native H
word has invertible restrictions on the delivered ordinary donor
quotients, so this is the actual source/H rank condition. None of these
seven donors has a singleton source plane.

The native fibre counts are 33 at H263, 34 at H642, and 34 at H135.

## 4. Causality and physical owners

H263 and H642 each have 90 actual early original slots in the pinned
bank-480 route. H135 has 45 and uses the established mixed early/late H
word. At a designated mixed centre, the early source takes the first
spinor half and the late source takes the second. Other half quotas use
the deterministic local frame from the existing source compiler.

For each donor and each component, all real early rows form a square
32k-by-32k invertible matrix. The frozen early seed is zero at every
donor. The compiler reads the original route IDs, reduces this matrix to
identity, replays its inverse on every field basis vector, and emits the
required owner permutation. The complementary late parities are formed
from original X fields before departure, kept in distinct stationary
caches, read at the actual Y arrivals, and dirty-cleared after their last
use. The inverse early word and all cache operations are charged.

The source matrix construction changes neither the physical H maps nor
their prescribed nodes. It changes the original source functions supplied
to those maps.

## 5. These are genuine source changes

For every one of the seven donors, `NON-RS-WITNESSES.json` gives 16 actual
ordinary source planes whose new marked scalar stack has rank below 48.
The ranks are 47 except for donor 45, whose witness has rank 46. These
sets are not failed native receiver cuts: all native receiver cuts passed.

The old ordinary RS source has rank 48 on any 16 distinct ordinary planes,
by the Vandermonde determinant. Repeating the marked witness over the 16
spinor lanes gives a full-bank observation rank at most 3,824, or 3,808
for donor 45, even if the unmarked component has its maximum rank.
Consequently that observation is not injective, whereas the old RS
observation is injective. This property is invariant under an arbitrary
whole-bank input bijection and independent bijections on the observed
plane quotients, including nonlinear bijections. The new sources therefore
cannot be obtained by such relabellings of the old RS words.

## 7. Replay and negative controls

From the repository root:

```sh
python -B research/moonshot-005-program/results/bit-coupled-native-three-chain-source/author/check.py --repo . --out /tmp/three-chain-check
python -B research/moonshot-005-program/results/bit-coupled-native-three-chain-source/author/compile.py --repo . --out /tmp/three-chain-compile
```

`--emit-words` on the compiler retains every packed field operation and
every original X/Y holder dictionary. Their uncompressed payload hashes
are already recorded in `COST.json`. The checker's optional
`--rank-library` is only an exact GF64 rank accelerator; the default
NumPy replay has no compiled dependency.

Deleting one actual marked source quotient changes a checked cut rank
from 48 to 45. Flipping one coefficient of one donor's central control is
rejected by the whole-fibre common-readout check. Both negative controls
are included in the receipt.

## Open next questions

1. Extend the simultaneous construction to four connected centres. For a
   generic tree of four centres, literal point identifications give 36k
   quotient variables and 24k port equations, leaving only 12k dimensions.
   A fourth-centre construction must use dependencies among these
   equations or relax the shared point-table premise while retaining the
   required inverse fibres. Merely adding another generic block of (1)
   is insufficient.
2. Construct compatible D/K/facet maps on the same source. The present
   local inverse fibres are a necessary source boundary condition, not a
   complete endpoint realization.
3. Preserve both exact S028 stars and check the complete return at or below
   34,688 fields. Avoiding those stars at the three designated H interfaces
   does not establish this global claim.
