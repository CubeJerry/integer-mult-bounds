## Tight H capacity aligns entire fibre partitions

Two nonorthogonal retained donors j,k lie in one actual A2 plane H. Fix a native receiver i perpendicular to H with i != 80. Its source view at either donor contains sixteen planes. Fix its fifteen quotients off H. In coefficient coordinates the remaining fibre is a coset of

    W_(j,i,H) = span(phi_(j,i,H)) tensor F^s,

where phi is the monic degree-15 polynomial with those fifteen actual evaluation nodes as roots. Its value at H's distinct node is nonzero. Thus every fibre has |F|^s elements. Pulling back by any original source bijection preserves that cardinality; denote the resulting original-coordinate fibres at j,k by U,V.

Fix other original inputs and the other visible source messages. Only the shared H can carry these variations to i. Its 45 outgoing ports have fifteen visible to i, with two genuine physical lanes: together exactly s field symbols. No later deterministic processing distinguishes more than |F|^s variations through this cut. The required output distinguishes every element of a translate of U+V, so

    |U| = |V| = |F|^s,       |U+V| <= |F|^s.       (1)

Finite subsets of an abelian group satisfying (1) are cosets of a common subgroup of that order. All translates U+v have the same cardinality as U+V and hence coincide. Differences in V stabilize U. The stabilizer has at least |V| and at most |U| elements, forcing equality. Holding one fibre fixed while varying the other shows that all fibres at either donor are cosets of the same subgroup L_(i,H). Their partitions of the original additive bank coincide as unlabelled partitions.

This uses no linearity of source-coordinate changes or later processing. It uses the actual cut and its exact additive demand. The composition of the two original source bijections therefore matches the coset partitions of W_(j,i,H) and W_(k,i,H), simultaneously for every such receiver i.

## Joins of partitions give a nonlinear invariant

The equivalence relation generated by the coset partitions of W_1,...,W_t has precisely the cosets of their sum as its classes. Each class has |F|^(dim(sum W_a)) elements. A bijection preserves this join and its class cardinalities. Therefore for every indexed receiver subset I omitting 80, the matched partitions require

    rank{phi_(j,i,H): i in I} = rank{phi_(k,i,H): i in I}.    (2)

The s channels multiply both dimensions by s; scalar extension preserves the polynomial ranks. The arbitrary-bijection argument includes, but is stronger than, ordinary linear rank invariance.
