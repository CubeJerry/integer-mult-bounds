# Initial helper-input substitution: valid interface, source family excluded

For arbitrary cochart helper inputs h, a row ℓ and column α with ℓα=1, the two block operations `Q−=ℓh`, `h+=αQ` give Q=q−ℓh and h′=h+α(q−ℓh). Thus ℓh′=q. If the complete native returns those helper inputs exactly and no other endpoint depends on them, then at their common final chart T one restores h by subtracting αQ and restores q by adding ℓh. This costs 2|suppℓ|+2|suppα| ADDs and no new MOVE, ownership or SCALE. All coefficient admissibility and physical typing requirements remain explicit.

A donor can extend this interface across helpers whose initial charts lie along its own funded itinerary. Choose a nonzero coefficient at the last required chart as pivot p and normalize ℓ_p=1 within the admitted scalar grammar. As Q visits each helper's chart before the native starts, subtract that helper's ℓ contribution. At the pivot chart Q=q−ℓh is complete; add Q into H_p. Only H_p changes. If all helpers used by ℓ end at T, subtract Q from H_p there and add ℓh back into Q. The cost is 2|suppℓ|+2 ADDs, with every original donor route used once.

If a genuine local historical decoder of ℓ is available at a receiver chart, the receiver may wait there and accumulate its terms at their actual source events. Its running raw carrier supplies the accumulator, so one recipient needs no snapshot oracle. The decoder must cancel all arbitrary dirty common input directions and retain exact source-event coefficients.

This algebra does not establish a source witness. The separately reviewed [initial-helper annihilation theorem](../initial-helper-annihilation/PROOF.md) proves a stronger failure for the relevant native endpoint dependencies: eligible initial helper-input directions are absent from every source field historically available on the required receiver itineraries. Thus no linear local decoder, even with all histories granted, can recover a nonzero eligible input form in that scope. Multiple helper initial encodings and waived timing do not repair it. This is a source-specific exclusion, not a universal impossibility theorem.

The difference-carrier programme therefore proceeds to genuine midrun injections. Their future response can differ from the response of every admissible initial helper-input mode because earlier owned snapshots and public values remain fixed when the helper is changed. The full suffix, COPY validity, off-terminal endpoint correction and physical control events must then be proved directly. No new κ.
