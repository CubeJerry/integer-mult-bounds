## Why six binary bits suffice

A late Y output can use early snapshots and late arrivals. Take those snapshots **before** the early chart transformation by literal identity copying. At an initial native line use the raw X coordinates, retaining them for later visiting Ys. At a final facet use the arriving early snapshots and incoming Ys directly. These choices give only one variable chart map between a particular incoming/outgoing pair. Ground Y and canonical descents are held zero when defining proper transfers. Thus a nonzero proper raw donor must belong to the receiver hyperplane.

In the binary polynomial ring of these coefficients form $P=\det G_X\det G_Y\prod_{j\perp i}\det B_{ij}$.
