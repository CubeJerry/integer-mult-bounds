# The D3 obstruction survives arbitrary native input permutations

The two explicit fixed left source/H words at native donors 26 and 29 cannot realize the prescribed identity edges at D3, even if each donor independently applies an arbitrary bijection of all its original records. The result permits different bijections at all outputs and arbitrary downstream operations subject to the same saturated D3 boundary. Its invariant is the cardinality of a block of a join of partitions. This strengthens the previously computed linear-gauge rank obstruction.

## Finite-group statement

Let $A$ be a finite abelian group of raw source values. Consider two independent donors with raw variables $x,y$ in $A$. At every receiver $i$ under consideration, after fixing every other independent input the desired output determines $x+y$. A fixed reversible relabelling of that sum at receiver $i$ also suffices.

At each donor $j$, permit an arbitrary fixed bijection $g_j$ from its raw group to the input labels of its prescribed left word. In the coded labels, variations in any coset of $V_{ij}$ leave all observations at receiver $i$ outside one boundary unchanged. Assume each coset has $M$ elements and the available common boundary has at most $M$ possible joint messages. The same $g_j$ must serve all receivers.

For fixed $i$, take any block $P$ of the partition $g_1^{-1}(A/V_{i1})$ and any block $Q$ of $g_2^{-1}(A/V_{i2})$. Independent variations through $P$ and $Q$ can change only the common boundary. The set of required sums $P+Q$ therefore has cardinality at most $M$. Either input alone supplies $M$ distinct sums. Thus $|P+Q|=|P|=|Q|=M$.

Translate $P,Q$ separately so that both contain zero. The translated sum contains each translated summand. Equality of the three cardinalities makes all three the same set $S$. Then $S+S=S$ and $0$ belongs to $S$. A finite additive subset with these two properties is a subgroup. Undoing the translations proves that $P$ and $Q$ are cosets of one subgroup. The subgroup is determined uniquely by either individual block through its translation stabilizer.

Fix a block of the first donor and vary the second donor's block through its entire partition. Then fix a second-donor block and vary all first-donor blocks. Both partitions are precisely the cosets of the same subgroup $W_i$ of the raw group. This conclusion holds for each receiver separately, even though $W_i$ may vary with $i$.

For a set $I$ of receivers, join the corresponding equivalence relations by taking the transitive closure of their union. Because the two pulled-back partitions coincide receiver by receiver, their joins on raw inputs must be identical. A bijection preserves joins and their block cardinalities. In the coded coordinates of donor $j$, the join of coset partitions of $V_{ij}$ is the partition into cosets of the subgroup generated by all $V_{ij}$ for $i$ in $I$. In the linear case this is their subspace sum. Hence the sizes of these generated subgroups must agree across donors for every receiver set $I$.

## Native application

Take the twelve proper native receivers at D3 with labels 2, 3, 4, 5 and 88 through 95. Their S028 target is the identity at both donors 26 and 29. Fix all other original inputs and baselines. For a fixed receiver and donor, invert the genuine left marked 96-by-96 matrix. The eight D3 columns of its inverse form the only directions leaving all eleven other marked D inputs unchanged. The independent unmarked sector is full rank. Over eight true copies the available D3 variation has 64 field coordinates and the receiver sees 64 field coordinates at that D3 boundary. Thus $M=64^{64}$.

The independent physical review gives dimensions 94 and 96 for the sums of the twelve eight-dimensional component fibres, respectively. Across the eight true copies, the joined block sizes expressed as binary powers are $2^{4512}$ and $2^{4608}$. Their difference contradicts the necessary equality for arbitrary bijections. This argument permits nonlinear donor preprocessing that mixes all native bits, donors with unrelated input permutations, nonlinear reversible output labels, and nonlinear downstream processing under the stated boundary. The two explicit fixed source/H words and their saturated eight-field-per-copy D3 boundary are essential hypotheses. A physically changed left source or H code can have different partitions.

## Finite controls

The checker exhausts all pairs of subsets of matching cardinalities in the eight-element binary vector space and checks the coset conclusion in every extremal case. For a separate three-receiver example it compares the direction triples $(e_1,e_2,e_3)$ and $(e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2)$. Every pair of directions has the same join-block size. The three-direction joins have block sizes 8 and 4, respectively. Exhausting each donor's 40,320 arbitrary bijections gives disjoint sets of relabelled partition systems.
