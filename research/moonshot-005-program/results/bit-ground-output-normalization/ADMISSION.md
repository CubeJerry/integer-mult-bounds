# Normalize output forwarding at an actual common ground boundary

**Admitted exact preparation criterion and physical controls.** Suppose all original Y fields are at actual ground with independent raw values, every other boundary record is y-free, and the named pure-X ground fields have row matrixG. A fixed legal continuation W already has X_out=x and Y_out=Fx+My, with all owners closed and correct final charts. Then a ground preparation using Y ADDs and reads ofG, leaving other records unchanged, produces Y_out=y+Dx iff M is invertible and row(D−F) is contained inrowG.

The [proof and explicit compiler](../../sessions/2026-11-16-S018/checkpoint2-evidence/C/GROUND-OUTPUT-NORMALIZATION.md) first loadY+=HG with HG=D−F, then apply an actual binary ADD decomposition ofM^-1. This adds no MOVE, INIT or CLEAR; ADDs and downstream tariffs are retained. Necessity follows from MA=I and MLG=D−F. All intermediate Y feedback is permitted under the complete finalX condition. The theorem holds for every characteristic-two field and positive width via the fixed binary tensor template; it is ordinary boundary linear algebra made physically explicit, not a new lower bound.
