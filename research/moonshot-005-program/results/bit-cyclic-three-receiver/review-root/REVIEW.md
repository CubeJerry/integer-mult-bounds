## Exact quotient theorem

Root independently recomputes the three completion quotients and their local
ambiguity spaces, then follows the forced-class argument. For base B, allowed
extension H and quotient Q=H/B, choose a fitting representative a_i. Every
other fitting row differs from a_i by H intersection L_i, where L_i is the
receiver's granted local original-input space. Therefore a global added
observation subspace E must satisfy [a_i] in E+J_i, with J_i the image of
H intersection L_i in Q. If J_i is already forced into E, the class [a_i]
must also belong to E. The independently computed forcing chains exhaust Q.
They give exact added-information dimensions4,2,5 for G+W, G+C and T+C.

Scalar extension of the binary kernel/image equations gives the same result
over every characteristic-two field. At block width V, an arbitrary row space
of block observations is a subspace of Q tensor K^V; the forced-class proof
forces all V copies of each quotient direction. No scalar commutativity of
V-by-V coefficients is assumed. Equal-observation kernel counterinputs also
exclude nonlinear deterministic decoders of those same observations.
