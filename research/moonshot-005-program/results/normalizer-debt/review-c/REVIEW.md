## 2. Universal omission proof checked directly

For Y, the erroneous active local map is
`(u0,u1)↦(−u0−2u1,u1)`, hence its conjugate is
`I−2x (e0+e1)C⁻¹`. In the left stage basis, `(e0+e1)C⁻¹=e_A`; in the right
basis it is `e_A+2e_Hi`. These equalities follow by evaluating the covector on
each actual basis column. They give exactly the two rank-one Y formulas in
the author's proof.

For V, the erroneous local map is
`(u0,u1)↦(u0+2u1,−u1)`, so the conjugate is
`I+2(x−w)e1C⁻¹`. The left basis gives `x−w=G_i` and
`e1C⁻¹=e_B−e_Gi`; the right basis gives
`x−w=−2B+J_i+4Σ_(j>i)J_j` and `e1C⁻¹=e_B+e_Ji`.
Thus all four site maps are exact, with the retained ordinary signs and
subtractions included.

The four final witnesses are unaffected by other omissions:

| Omitted site | Final coefficient differing from identity |
|---|---|
| Left Y at i | A input to F_i output is2 modulo4 |
| Right Y at i | H_i own-input coefficient is−3 |
| Left V at i | G_i own-input coefficient is−1 |
| Right V at i | J_i own-input coefficient is3 |

For left Y, earlier sites leave F_i untouched and the root coefficient is
±1. Its own site contributes±2; later left sites alter that coefficient only
by multiples of4, and no right site touches F_i. For each other witness,
earlier sites cannot introduce the future leaf's input into the root or the
current active leaf. The relevant site sets the displayed diagonal
coefficient; later sites never touch that leaf. These are uniform
chronological arguments for arbitrary a and arbitrary omission patterns.

Every nonempty pattern has at least one witness. Therefore the theorem's
quantifiers are established: for every a≥1, width V≥1 and nonempty omitted
subset, some legitimate input fails. The claim does not cover altered
ordinary row operations, new encoded roles, changing stage adapters or
relocated normalizer calls.

## 4. Separate completed-row gate

The exact fourteen native classes each have binary pairing
`diag(J4,J4)` on their two halves and Gram correction diagonal blocks I4,I4.
For one completed scalar row `wuᵀ`, let d count its nonzero diagonal blocks.
The source/receiver pairing rank is d∈{0,1,2}; rank subadditivity on the two
identity-four blocks gives `Σd≥8` per class and direction.

For a nested owned chain, let U be the union span of all visited charts and
I their intersection. Actual gathered source normals lie in U, and every
deposited receiver facet contains I. Thus pairing rank is at most
`dim U−dim I`. Initially this difference is zero. An upward step grows the
union by at most its rank and leaves the intersection unchanged; a downward
step shrinks the intersection by at most its rank and leaves the union
unchanged. Total travel therefore dominates the pairing rank, including
arbitrary nested turns. Subadditivity gives movement moment at least d^t.

Since `d^t≥2^(t−1)d`, each class/direction costs at least `4·2^t`; fourteen
classes and two separately completed directional planes cost at least
`112·2^t=223.2250188734424...`. This exceeds151.5715925151256..., the available
minimal-source repair allowance.

For width-V payloads with arbitrary internal linear maps, each term has
diagonal-block rank at most V, while each required identity block has rank4V.
The spatial support pairing rank is **at least** the number of nonzero
diagonal blocks; equality is not needed if internal products vanish.
The same inequality follows after normalizing the lifted move cost by V^t.
Thus the width extension does not assume scalar internal maps.

As a positive accounting control, the four genuine omitted-frame coordinates
of each class factor its Gram matrix exactly. Each row touches both halves,
and can travel from W4 to the radical K2 if original input/output access is
granted free. This attains the ideal four rank-two completed-row price in
one class/direction. It is not a completed native source. The separately

The row gate requires one completed payload per separate chain, one native
class per row, separately completed forward/reverse planes, no preexisting
uncharged required rows and no new productive helper credit. It does not
apply to a changing-payload history serving several rows, cross-class
sharing, a general N service, or a new productive boundary contract.
