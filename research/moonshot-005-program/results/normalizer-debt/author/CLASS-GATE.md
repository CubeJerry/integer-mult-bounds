## 1. Chain tariff and the scope of a row

In one directional plane, suppose a class correction is a finite sum `Σ_k w_k u_k^T`. Each term is one completed linear payload carried by its own owned chain, with no productive credit. The coefficient support lies inside that one class; cross-class aggregation is excluded. Its gathers use actual original source records on charts containing the source lines, and its deposits use receivers on charts inside their prescribed facets. The reverse plane uses the time-reversed convention. All required source access and original-field moves may be granted free for this lower bound. There is no uncharged preexisting record that already contains the required row.

The source/receiver binary pairing rank r_k gives movement moment at least r_k^t. This is the one-chain row-price principle credited in DreamingOfClouds' PR#7583, applied here to a new exact class geometry. A direct proof also permits nested turns: let U be the union span of all charts visited by the chain and I their intersection. Every gathered source normal lies in U; every receiver facet contains I. Therefore `r_k<=dim U−dim I`. Along a nested upward step of length d the union dimension grows by at most d and the intersection does not shrink; along a downward step the intersection shrinks by at most d and the union does not grow. Thus total rank travel is at least `dim U−dim I`, and its concave movement moment is at least r_k^t. Time reversal proves the reverse case. A changing-payload history that supplies several different rows is outside the single completed-row premise.

Let d_k be the number of the two diagonal blocks on which `w_k u_k^T` is nonzero. The exact displayed binary pairing gives r_k=d_k when d_k>0, and r_k=0 when neither diagonal block is touched. A term supported only on a cross-half rectangle has no diagonal contribution. Each term has rank at most one on each diagonal block. Rank subadditivity for the two I4 blocks therefore yields `Σ_k d_k>=8`, irrespective of cancellations elsewhere or the number of terms.

For0<t<=1 and d∈{0,1,2}, `d^t>=2^(t−1)d`. Consequently one class in one directional plane costs at least `4·2^t`. All14 classes and the two separately completed directional planes therefore cost at least

Rank subadditivity gives the same row count and the same normalized inequality after dividing by V^t. Arbitrary linear maps within each payload do not remove the rank bound.
