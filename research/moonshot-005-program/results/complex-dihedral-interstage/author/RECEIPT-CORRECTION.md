# Finite-control count correction during independent review

The lead's independent reconstruction found 62 distinct partial maps of the
six-cycle fixing 0. The initial author checker reported 93 as a map count,
but that number counted two equivalent orientation encodings at the
antipodal vertex 3. Every encoded map passed the same extension test; this
was a diagnostic-label error, not a gap in the universal cycle lemma.

The final checker records both 93 encodings and 62 distinct maps. It also
moves elapsed wall time out of the deterministic JSON receipt and into a
separate stdout timing line. The source-bound proof is unchanged.
