    A_d^d=product_j C_j,

At each width allow invertible role matrices over the full ordinary monomial
Clifford group algebra on the fixed d heads. Suppose an exact all-width caller
with three actual native halves on arbitrary pairs of R retained roles satisfies

    N3 Q N2 P N1 = E diag(A_d^k1,...,A_d^kR) F.

The two internal matrices P,Q are unrestricted ordinary units. Each native may
use either authenticated L/U orientation, scalar role gauges, or a literal
