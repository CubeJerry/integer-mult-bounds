# Fixed disjoint source cohorts do not bypass the chart-channel bound

**Proved corollary of the clipped rectangular theorem, pending independent
review. No new κ.** Cross-root recoding is not by itself sufficient to change
the one-hop source premise.

Partition the native root roles into disjoint nonempty cohorts J_i. Group all
b original banks at those roots, so target cohort size n_i=b|J_i| and total
n=111b. Suppose cohort i is recoded at a common real chart C_i containing
every L_j for j in J_i. All its outgoing width-V source channels then ascend
to single E6 charts containing C_i and thereafter to containing final facets.
Within-cohort source recoding is arbitrary and may be rectangular, singular,
block-valued and noncommuting. Mixing at E6/final facets is permitted as in
the base theorem. Global histories and forbidden extra source transport retain
the same explicit scope.

Any such E6 chart is contained in at most three native final facets, so a
physical encoded channel reaches at most three target COHORTS. None is its
own: if j belongs to J_i, then L_j lies in C_i and in the E6 chart, while L_j
is not contained in F_j. Thus the E6 chart cannot be contained in F_j for ANY
j in its source cohort. The zero own-cohort condition survives grouping.

The source matrix E is block diagonal by these larger cohorts. Transposing
I=G+DE therefore gives the same clipped filtration, with possibly unequal
n_i and m_i. At most2n physical source channels still imply

    rank G >= V ceil(119n/1250)
           = V ceil(13209b/1250) >9bV.

For exactly n channels the older bound V ceil(n/4) remains. Counting a bank's
support as3|J_i| at the original-root level loses the stronger grouped
filtration and incorrectly suggests that a sufficiently large fixed cohort
alone is an escape. The geometric common-chart restriction keeps every
own-cohort output inaccessible to that entire cohort's local contribution.

A source with overlapping, successively interacting cohorts that has no such
fixed common-chart partition may escape this factorization. So may actual
nonlocal descending histories, extra effective observations or a larger paid
global code. Those are concrete changed interfaces, not consequences of this
corollary. Existing private-cache/packet constructions can involve overlapping
acquisitions and are not claimed excluded without an actual factorization.
