# Early source preservation excludes every repeated two-dimensional field module

## Theorem and exact scope

Retain the original balanced native source and its canonical row code,
the prescribed early X and later Y chronology, the ordinary quotient
capacities, and all actual proper routes. Let F be any finite field of
characteristic two. Suppose a common original-source coordinate decomposition
is `G=(F^32)^r`, and each ordinary quotient is, up to a bijective relabelling,
`A_(j,H) tensor I_r`, for an arbitrary rank-two map `A_(j,H):F^32->F^2`.
Different donors and planes may use different maps, and noncommuting
coefficient blocks are allowed. Copies of each particular base map are
identical. Actual balanced dimensions require r=180s for a positive integer s.
The larger singleton encoders are unrestricted within their real capacities.

**No complete native code exists in this interface.** Even granting arbitrary
nonlinear functions at early source steps and downstream charts, the full
plane boundary could carry at most `(41152/15)r` field information dimensions,
strictly less than the canonical-zero input dimension3264r. For r=180 the
capacity is493,824, the required dimension587,520, and the deficit93,696.

This extends the separately reviewed binary-two source gate. Merely changing
its coefficient field to F4, F64, or another finite characteristic-two field
does not evade the original causal constraints. Larger coupled modules,
independently varying blocks, cross-copy source gauges, extra informative
routes, and changed canonical banks remain outside this theorem. It is not
a MOVE tariff, a full source construction, or a new kappa.

## 1. Retained local target and early-source facts

For an ordinary plane H let m=2 or3 be its native donor count. Let I_H consist
of the receivers whose source cuts are tight for every native donor at H.
The existing quotient theorem and common-function lemma give target spaces

`W_i tensor F^r`, with `dim_F W_i=2` in `(F^2)^m`.

Each W_i projects isomorphically onto every donor coordinate. Each port seen
by i factors through this target, even if its actual encoder is nonlinear.
Any two joint-tight receivers share3 or6 genuine H-to-D4 exits. A shared
subtuple has full product range because all15 ports at a receiver jointly
have exactly its2r-dimensional target capacity. Thus

`r*dim(W_i intersect W_k)>=2r*shared_ports/15>0`.

The W_i are pairwise-intersecting graph planes. These facts and their exact
native incidence were independently checked in the binary-two package.

The balanced factor has x early fields per donor at every H-to-D4 flag.
Here x is4,5,6 for m=3, and6 for m=2. In the current normalization each donor
contributes `a=xr/4` early source values at H. Each actual early H exit has
`e=mxr/180` field values; there are45 exits, so `45e=ma`.

The entire genuine early source tuple must be a bijection on the original
X bank, since all original X are restored at Hub before the later Y wave.
There is no extra informative H-to-Hub bypass in the retained routes.
Consequently H's early outputs must preserve its ma-dimensional actual
early input. Under uniformly distributed original X, the global early tuple
is uniformly distributed over its full product alphabet; H's actual early
inputs are therefore independent of the early inputs at other planes.
A later chart cannot restore information that this local step discarded.
Stationary H snapshots do not provide an additional route to Hub.

These are the original source/compiler and chronology contracts, not newly
granted operations. A source that uses extra informative escape paths or
changes the chronology requires a separate analysis.

## 2. The only possible nonstar geometry is a three-dimensional top

Over every field, a pairwise-intersecting family of two-dimensional spaces
either has a common line or is contained in one three-dimensional space T.
For completeness, choose distinct W_1,W_2 with intersection L. If W_3 misses
L, its two distinct intersection lines place it in T=W_1+W_2. Any W outside
T would have to meet W_1,W_2 in L; meeting W_3 would then force L into W_3.
Thus all W lie in T. This is the same elementary star-or-top argument as
in the binary predecessor, without the binary-specific final step.

A true nonstar family spans T of dimension3. Its projection onto every
donor's F^2 has rank2 because it contains a graph plane. We now exclude this
top alternative using the actual early paths, rather than the field size.

## 3. An information bound on the covered early outputs

Let `Z_1,...,Z_m` be independent uniform ordinary quotient inputs, each in
`F^(2r)`. These are available as genuine local variables by source saturation
and the retained canonical-zero reduction. Let E_j be the actual early
tuple from donor j. It is a function of Z_j: the early coordinates are part
of that donor's full quotient emission. Its entropy is at most a field units.
No linearity assumption on E_j is needed.

Let T(Z) be the `3r` linear coordinates spanning the hypothetical top.
Let Y be the tuple of all covered early H-port values, where a port is
covered if at least one receiver in I_H sees it. Every member of Y factors
through a W_i contained in T, so Y is a function of T(Z). Causality also
makes Y a function of the actual early tuple E=(E_1,...,E_m).

Measure entropy in units log|F|. A common deterministic function satisfies
`H(Y)<=I(T;E)`: since Y is a function of both arguments, data processing gives
`I(T;E)>=I(Y;E)=H(Y)`. For any chosen donor j,

`I(T;E) <= I(T;E_-j)+H(E_j)
         <= I(T;Z_-j)+a
         = r+a`.

The last equality uses `H(T)=3r` and `H(T|Z_-j)=2r`, since T's projection
onto donor j has rank2r. This proves the bound for arbitrary nonlinear
early encoders and covered-port functions.

If u actual early ports are uncovered, grant them their full ue capacity.
All early H outputs together then have entropy at most `r+a+ue`, while
preservation of the actual early X input requires ma. Thus a nonstar top
would require

`ma <= r+a+ue`, equivalently `45(m-1)x-u*m*x <=180`.

The new exact native check verifies the opposite strict inequality at
**every one of the1108 ordinary planes**. The minimum integer margin
`45(m-1)x-u*m*x-180` is30. This excludes the top alternative everywhere.
Every ordinary plane's target family must therefore have a common line.

## 4. Global information after excluding tops

Color each receiver by its actual base target W_i. Distinct colors have
intersection exactly the common line. A receiver with no monochromatic
incident port neighborhood could receive only the common r-dimensional
part, instead of its2r-dimensional target. The coloring therefore satisfies
the admitted finite self-cover condition.
