## 1. Geometry and value fields are distinct

The actual source chart geometry is over the rationals, embedded in the reals.
Let r_j be a nonzero rational vector spanning native source line L_j, and let
F_i=L_i^perp be its native seven-dimensional receiver facet. All111 lines and

Values may lie in an arbitrary characteristic-two field k and may have any

only certifies that exhibited rational determinants are nonzero over Q/R.

Let A be any native block fitting obtained from the admitted all-route
normalization. Its diagonal blocks are I_V and prescribed noncontact blocks
are zero. For each receiver define its foreign source support

    J_i(A)={j!=i:A_ij is a nonzero V-by-V block}.

Every r_j for j in J_i(A) lies in F_i. Define the real ranks

    d1_i=dim span_R{r_j:j in J_i(A)},
    d2_i=dim span_R{r_j r_j^T:j in J_i(A)}.

The first is at most7 and the second at most28, the dimension of Sym^2(F_i).
Define the new geometric facet charge

    gamma_i(A)=1[d1_i=7]+1[d2_i=28].                   (1)

This charge can be used alongside the old cardinality charge ell_i; neither
is asserted to classify all possible local decoders.

## 2. The geometric incidence theorem

Retain the actual-route normalization and its local facet identity, including
arbitrary nested public and owned routes, turns, proper births and retirements,
unknown Y terms, and singular/noncommuting blocks. At F_i choose one upward
arrival of its designated public Y as anchor when one exists, and the zero
subspace otherwise. Let t_i count every other upward arrival and every downward
departure there. The normalized local X support is contained in the union of
the anchor's proper predecessor chart and the proper predecessor/successor
charts supplied by those t_i occurrences. This is the same local fact used
by the admitted36/52 cardinality test.

**Theorem 1.** For every such normalized fitting and every receiver,

    gamma_i(A)<=t_i.                                  (2)

**Proof.** If t_i=0, the foreign roots lie in one proper subspace of F_i, so
d1_i<=6 and d2_i<=21. If t_i=1, they lie in a union P union Q of at most two
proper subspaces of F_i. Choose nonzero real linear forms a,b on F_i vanishing
on P and Q, respectively. Their product `a(z)b(z)` is a nonzero homogeneous
quadratic polynomial on F_i, because the real polynomial ring is an integral
domain. It vanishes on every supported source root. Consequently the supported
quadratic evaluations cannot span all28 dimensions: d2_i<=27. Thus gamma_i
is at most1. For t_i>=2, gamma_i<=2 gives the result immediately. The anchor
is explicit; a full linear span forces one nonanchor incidence, and a full
quadratic span forces two. QED.

This is a necessary geometric condition, not its converse. A quadratic rank
below28 need not supply a factorization into two real linear forms, compatible
chart chronology, available values or an actual decoder.

Summing (2) and using the admitted all-route occurrence injections yields

    sum_i gamma_i(A)<=111+M_owned+2D_public.            (3)

Under the retained complete caller and target kappa>=1/200, its exact
weighted tariff gives `M_owned+2D_public<=83`. Hence every completing source

    sum_i gamma_i(A)<=194.                            (4)

The same geometric charge can replace ell in the fixed-prefix bounds, since
