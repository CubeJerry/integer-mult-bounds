# A single-grade compiler budget for the covariant BIT target

The rank tariff of the S028 two-sided covariant target clears the complete
single-grade criterion at fixed even multiplicity 870. Its limiting raw
saving is strictly between .00500220831285 and .00500220831286. Every
finite multiplicity on this tariff misses the one-stage bootstrap threshold
.00500576643216… from the selected ordinary seed. There is room for a
different completion from the same raw target. The explicit product type
below allows compiler and cover losses before a self-hosted ordinary
service reaches .0050000002, conditional on constructing the fully
normalized physical word and satisfying the stated contracts.

**Status.** The profile, inequalities and product type are proved. The
physical-word implication is conditional. The S028 algebraic endpoint and
its partial inverse lifts do not yet meet its hypotheses. Both suppliers,
the outer recursive multiplication theorem and all five foundations remain
open for the programme. No new complete κ.

## 1. Source and statement

    H = {2:1,4:25,6:1,8:410}.

Expanding the retained paired caller at fixed even m≥2 gives two positive

    P_m = 18N + 222m[1] + 222m Σ(j=1..8)[7j] + 18(m−1)Q
    T_m = 18H + 111m[127] + 222m Σ(j=1..9)[1+7j].

These follow from P_m = (m/2)P_2 − 18(m−1)N + 18(m−1)Q and
T_m = (m/2)T_2 − 18(m−1)H with the **original** paired P_2,T_2 on the
right. Parentheses here denote histograms. For a positive histogram X put
X(s)=Σ_r X_r r^s. Multiplying *both* histograms by the number of actual
paid lanes preserves every moment comparison. The proof charges every
extension copy, integer productive role, repeated paid request and split
piece.

**Theorem 1.** For every even m≥2 and every 0<t<1, the single-grade chord
condition from [PR #7828] is equivalent, for these histograms, to
T_m(t)>P_m(t). There is exactly one crossing at m=870, with strict
saving bracket (.00500000060450,.00500000060451). The supremum of raw
savings in 0<κ<1 for which at least one even finite multiplicity passes is
in (.00500220831285,.00500220831286). The supremum is unattained.

**Theorem 2.** At m=870, multiply both histograms by 23,040, the 3,840
field lanes times six binary copies in the proposed GF(64) specialization.
At raw saving .0050000005 the explicit 2^42-fold product type in
[the receipt](output/RESULT.json) has grade K≥49^v. Keeping this type
and splitting paid products to ranks at most 13^v makes the complete
paid-to-productive t-moment ratio at most 3/8. In the single-grade

**Conditional transfer.** Suppose a physical chronological source realizes

This proves a precise sufficient route from the missing physical source;
it does not assert that the remaining contracts already hold.

## 2. The continuum test and its sharp ceiling

For any nonempty positive histogram X, (log X)'(s) is the
X_r r^s-weighted mean of log r. Its derivative is the corresponding
variance, so it is increasing in the weak sense. Write P',T' for the
derivatives with respect to s. Directed rational enclosures give the
following deliberately weakened integer inequalities. The last row uses
the coefficient of m in the positive histograms. It represents no
infinite caller.

| Histograms | c | T'(0)−T(0) log c | P'(1)−P(1) log c |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| m=2 | 9 | >4,171 | <−27,247 |
| m=4 | 13 | >3,617 | <−116,067 |
| Coefficient of m | 13 | >1,944 | <−1,810 |

For m≥4 the expressions at c=13 are affine in m. The last two rows
extend both signs to every such m. For m=2 use its own c. Thus on the
entire interval [0,1], (log T)'(s)>log c>(log P)'(s). In particular any
secant slope of log P is below log c. If T(t)>P(t), log T therefore
stays strictly above the line joining (t,log P(t)) and (1,log P(1))
on all of [t,1]. Necessity follows by evaluating that line at t. This
step needs the actual slope separation. In the upstream control
T(s)=70+256^s, P(s)=18·16^s, t=1/2, both endpoint gains are positive,
but the chord fails at s=3/4.

The same argument makes T/P strictly increasing in s, both on each
finite histogram above and on the slope histograms. Put
G_m(k)=T_m(1−k)−P_m(1−k), and let g(k) be its coefficient of m.
The strict raw bracket above follows from evaluating G_870 at its two
rational endpoints with opposite signs. Evaluating the slope g at the
ends of the second bracket gives opposite signs and fixes its unique
zero k_infty. At k=1/200, G_2<0, and G_2 stays negative at every
larger saving since T_2/P_2 decreases in k. For k≥k_infty we have
g≤0 and G_m=G_2+(m−2)g<0 for every finite m. For k<k_infty one has
g>0, so all sufficiently large fixed even m pass. At k=1/200, m=868
fails, m=870 passes and g>0, proving the first even crossing there.

The one-stage threshold from b_0=.00385248 for merely exceeding 1/200
is 3112961/621875000. It exceeds k_infty. Thus no m in this architecture
can fund that particular one-stage strategy through a positive raw
invoice followed by a cover that earns no additional exponent. Alternate
supplier grammars and gains from a changed physical word are outside this
exclusion. Acyclic reuse can still work. With the proposed covered C,
the successive stage suprema F_C(b)=C/(1+C−b) from b_0 are
.0049942692746…, .0049999716450…, .0050000001567… and
.0050000002992…. The third ideal stage allows the target to be exceeded,
and the fourth allows the stated ordinary offer. The fixed-program
self-hosted construction below avoids choosing a stage depth.

## 3. A specified integer product type with a polynomial width bound

Use the actual integer profiles obtained by multiplying by 23,040. They
contain 42,455,715,840 target slots and 416,127,105,216 paid requests.
The unscaled moment gap at κ_raw=.0050000005 is in
(.01053057182384,.01053057182385). Put t=1−κ_raw,
W_k=(23,040 T_m)_k and P=23,040 P_m. Let a_k be the directed lower
bound for k^t from the checker, S=Σ W_k a_k and p_k=W_k a_k/S.
At v=2^42, choose counts n_k of one most probable multinomial type for
the rational probabilities p_k. The checker emits all 13 integer counts
and verifies that no exchange of one occurrence improves its probability.

Let K=∏ k^{n_k} and B=v! ∏ W_k^{n_k}/∏ n_k!. The most probable type
has probability ≥(v+1)^(−13) since there are at most (v+1)^13
types. Thus BK^t≥S^v/(v+1)^13. The certified integer counts give
Σ n_k log(k/49)>46,000,000,000, hence K≥49^v. Holding this type
preserves distinct, independently owned copies of every retained role.
Other original roles receive owned zero fields. The chronological product
still runs and pays their requests.

Set J=13^v. Pay every original product request R≤J unchanged. Split R>J
into consecutive pieces of size J and a possible smaller positive last
piece, retaining the whole-job adapter. Their charge is at most
2R J^(t−1). The complete split charge is at most
P(t)^v + 2 J^(t−1)P(1)^v. Set

    delta = min(1−P(t)/S, 1−P(1) 13^(t−1)/S).

The receipt proves v delta>630 while 13(42+1)+3=562. Applying
(1−delta)^v≤2^(−v delta) bounds the full split-to-retained ratio by
3/8. This also certifies an actual finite product type without expanding
4,398,046,511,104 raw factors or computing its factorial as an integer.
The finite enumeration is a mathematical construction, not a runtime
benchmark.

For the homogeneous job recursion, a paid child receives at most
(13/49)^v times its parent's active rank. With D active native levels
before a terminal of size <K, the last native level, if present, has at
least K axes. Therefore N≥K(49/13)^{v(D−1)}≥(49/13)^{vD}.
Divisibility by B^D suffices for the homogeneous batches; the same
productive fields are reused sequentially across root tiles. Since
B≤(42,455,715,840)^v and (49/13)^19>42,455,715,840, the complete
productive width is ≤N^19. For 3,840 binary lanes the corresponding
bound would be N^18. The 23,040-lane result accounts for the six real
copies. The predecessor's bounds N^16 and O(N^51) are specific to its
other source profiles.

For any fixed true ambient A and raw instruction list, plugging this
finite word into the homogeneous schedule gives a finite pre-cover
operation count. For example, a macro has at most (A Q_0)^v split
children for Q_0=416,127,105,216, since every original product request
has at most A^v axes. The recursive number of visited macros per tile
per initial owner is at most (1+(A Q_0)^v)^D. Each fixed macro executes
all its raw operations, discarded-role lifetimes, split bindings and
retirements. These bounds, at most N root tiles, at most N base peel
operations per visit and the N^19 owners are polynomial in N for this
fixed A, v and source. They give no price for a physical field, group,
bank, address map or bit operation.

## 4. Conditional physical implication and costs

The parked source/H/D/K word is still missing. The conditions needed to
use the price chain are explicit.

1. For every allowed positive column count, the same finite ordered source
   program must complete all independent arbitrary role outputs and restore
   its owners with the displayed integer histograms. Bind every paid and
   target position to a rational interchange projector in its actual
   ambient A. Give both sides of every whole-field adapter, lift each one
   along spectator axes, prove their endpoint identities on the whole
   field, and retain the integral/characteristic-two payload semantics.
   Address coordinates at the later odd cover prime and the binary
   extension used in the payload play different roles. Show the physical
   lift for the complete 870-rail caller, including its native base
   fields, arbitrary Y values, returns, precision, local ADDs, snapshots,
   movement, initialization, storage, erasure and restoration.
2. Under this contract use the finite single-grade schedule from #7828.
   For true powered ambient M=A^v its complete rank-one count per
   productive field is at most 2(3M)^(1−t) N^t for N≥2M. With
   t_u=1−.0050000004>t, choose and fix N so large that
   N^(t_u−t)>2(3M)^(1−t). This funds strict uniform BIT on the
   normalized word, using complete spectator fields throughout. Every local
   instruction, rank-one glue, cleanup and adapter contributes to a finite
   ordinary-work and backing constant for this fixed source.
3. Choose an odd q outside all finite denominator, rank-witness and
   determinant exclusions of the *new* word. Invoke the delayed cover only
   on the final rank-N identity projector, and require the permitted
   arbitrary-lift endpoint and weighted good/bad-class analysis. Its
   charge multiplier is at most 1+64N^5/q. For
   u=.0050000004, C=.0050000003 choose q sufficiently large that this
   multiplier is strictly less than N^(u−C), including the source's
   q>2^45 N^5 guard. Effective finite selection follows from the strict
   margin and finitely many exclusions. Good rows, high and low routes,
   fallbacks, weighted leaves and their returns are paid. This gives
   μ(1−C)<1 for the actual charged coarse word if the lifting premises
   hold.
4. Use #7826's homogeneous self-hosted word at rational ρ=1/128. At
   w=ceil(e^ρ), reserve the whole R(w)^D high row, with
   R(w)=W_N |GL_N(F_q)| q^{N²(w−1)} and D bounded by
   2+ceil(log_N max(1,ceil(e/w))). Every smaller ordinary invocation
   acquires and restores its own row. Charge two reversed bodies, three
   signed shifts, a ragged tail, high exchange and complete-row restriction.
   Require its open semantic, bounded-local-backing and parking hypotheses,
   a contracting coarse path, all bad children and both actual physical

   At the strict covered price μ(1−C)<1 and C<ρ<1−C. The characteristic
   max(σ,1−ρ,ρ) is strictly below 1−C, so its fixed logarithmic factor
   is absorbed. In particular one fixed program can offer
   B=.0050000002. The selected ρ=1/250 fails the guard at this target.
   Recompute all cutoff and stock constants at 1/128. Exact bit
   permutation introduces no extra rounding error.

The outer consumer must still support the newly priced physical reserve
and compact movements. It requires its own strict **complex** supplier,
global neighbourhoods, complete caller and numerical recovery. The present
proof supplies no complex word. The five retained assumptions are
native_all_columns, owned_erasure, compiler, precision_tape and
analytic_recovery. A source-specific intermediate compiler theorem alone
does not remove the programme-wide assumption.
