# The native nine-bank information floor

Every zero-error index code on the authenticated 111-role native side-information graph has at least n⁹ transcripts for an alphabet of size n. Every linear square fitting on this support over any field has rank at least 9b, whatever its proper coefficients. The binary canonical fitting attains the abstract minimum. At b=3,840 this leaves only 128 symbols above the 34,560 floor in the m=870 tariff. A physical realization within that window remains open.

## Equality and the price of losing a donor

The converse also forces every proper linear block to have rank at least b−6h when the total return has rank 9b+h, without a tree assumption. At the exact minimum all 6,470 proper blocks are invertible and the simultaneous native classification applies. For arbitrary nonlinear zero-error codes, removing any one authorized donor at its receiver raises the necessary number of transcripts to at least n^(55/6). In the abelian sum-separable model every exact-minimum proper function is bijective and the entire fitting is a simultaneous elementary-binary additive transport. At b=3,840 any deleted directed proper block requires at least 35,200 symbols. The permitted rank 34,688 forces all 6,470 blocks individually to have rank at least 3,072.

The proof checks an asymmetric 54-term entropy certificate on 2,673 authenticated native diagrams. Three implications per directed edge suffice.
