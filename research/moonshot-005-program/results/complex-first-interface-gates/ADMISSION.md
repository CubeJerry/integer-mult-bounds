# Two first-interface gates for arbitrary ordinary second interstages

**Admitted scoped universal exclusions; no general three-native impossibility.**
Use the actual ordered three-bank product E=L C U Q L with the literal source
a=A^tensor V. C and Q are ordinary units whose complete finite monomial catalogue,
including endpoint encodings, is fixed before V grows. All absolute phases are
retained. At sufficiently large faithful widths, independent heads(4,2,1)
necessarily have exactly Gaussian-rank components1,2,4, each of operator rankD.

The [stacked first-column theorem](../../sessions/2026-11-16-S018/checkpoint3-evidence/R/active-first-gate/PROOF.md) treats
C=[[w,v,0],[g,h,0],[0,0,k]]. If the vertical stack of the rank3 components of
a w a² and a g a² is injective, no arbitrary ordinary unit Q can yield the
target. Individual pivots may be singular. The idle output forces q21=q22=0;
the forbidden rank3 stack forces q12=0; component separation then forces w=0.
The last input column fills the middle bank with rank2, leaving a different
nonzero unit with no permitted component. Clean forward transport of w and g
is a sufficient stack condition. Actual complementary projectors supply a
control where both individual pivots are singular but the stack is injective.
B's [independent review](../../sessions/2026-11-16-S018/checkpoint3-evidence/B/ACTIVE-FIRST-REVIEW.md) verifies this argument
and reconstructs the literal source and formal equations separately.

The [middle-pivot theorem](../../sessions/2026-11-16-S018/checkpoint3-evidence/B/MIDDLE-PIVOT-ALL-ORDINARY-GATE.md) treats
C=[[0,b,c],[t,d,e],[0,f,g]] with a t a^-1 ordinary. Every other first-interface
block and all of Q may be arbitrary ordinary. C-unit makes t a unit and [c;g]
injective. Rank3 and ordinary equations force q12=q22=0. The last input column
then fills the middle output bank with pure rank2. Every rank4 contribution is
also confined to that bank, while rank1 has rankD, so E has rank at most2D,
contradicting its invertibility on three banks. Root's [independent review](../../sessions/2026-11-16-S018/checkpoint3-evidence/R/middle-pivot-review/REVIEW.md)
uses separately written free-product algebra and a noncommuting complementary
projector control, without importing B execution code.

The second theorem closes the entire prior residual middle-pivot family even
with arbitrary clocks and ordinary Q outside the source-square normalizer.
The first closes a different broad class including complementary singular
active pivots. Neither theorem covers every first-column shape or a failed
injectivity/transport premise. General first-column switching with actual idle
coupling, different native order, or another source remains open. The controls
with failed premises are not positive callers. Both proofs use faithful tensor
separation of a fixed finite catalogue; small-width matrix ranks alone are not
an all-width exclusion or a sufficient positive endpoint test.
