The previous fresh-bridge preflight showed why paying separately for every

instead changes the actual values carried during one of its existing reverse
and forward mode trajectories, then restores both old mode duties exactly.
A real native word removes 52 paired coefficients through a rank-three

## 1. A source theorem for opposite traversals of one station chain

Let k be any field, let V>=1, and let

    L < C_1 < ... < C_s < H

be a chain of actual nested charts. At station C_j let there be source
banks x_j,b_j in k^(p_j V) and receiver-source bank d_j in k^(m_j V).
All these banks may have arbitrary independent contents. Let

    P_j: k^(p_j V) -> k^V,
    Q_j: k^V -> k^(m_j V).

No invertibility or commutativity is imposed on these rectangular block
maps. Let g_X,g_D in k^V be the actual functions delivered by an already
existing forward and reverse mode service. Their original duties are:

* the reverse owner reaches H with value g_D;
* the forward owner reaches L with value g_X;
* the old receiver response from this forward mode is alpha_j g_X;
* the old source-side response from this reverse mode is -beta_j g_D.

The following physical premises are explicit, rather than consequences of
the displayed factorization.

1. The actual reverse owner can be acquired at L with the changed value
   g_D+sum_j Q_j^T d_j. Every required change of the early source encoder,
   and every original-input restoration, is performed and charged.
2. The original d_j banks remain available at their C_j until the reverse
   visit. Each b_j remains there until that same visit. The x_j sources
   make real stationary caches A_j=P_j x_j at C_j, then genuinely arrive
   at H before the forward owner is initialized.
3. At H the forward owner can be formed as g_X+sum_j P_j x_j. The original
   x_j values used here are actual restored Hub records, not departed
   records implicitly read through an oracle.
4. Later receiver fields can make actual paid reads at C_j. Their old
   mode decoder is changed from alpha_j to alpha_j-Q_j by a genuinely
   available encoding/decoding circuit. The availability and invertibility
   of that circuit must be separately established for the chosen source.
5. All caches have real lifetimes and admissible retirement, and every
   original route and owner transition follows its stated chart chain.

Then the following chronological construction adds the paired matrix

    K_{t,j} = Q_t P_j if j<=t, and 0 otherwise,             (1)

to the old forward map, and adds -K^T to the old reverse map. Both old mode
endpoint values g_X and g_D are restored. All original x and d contents
are unchanged. Statements about uniform physical compilation, scalar
production and erasure are not supplied merely by this interface theorem.

### The ascending reverse trajectory

Immediately before the visit to C_j, maintain

    R = g_D + sum_(t>=j) Q_t^T d_t.                        (2)

At C_j first perform b_j <- b_j-P_j^T R. Then subtract Q_j^T d_j from R,
using the actual d_j bank still present there. The owner ascends to the
next station; b_j may now take its actual remaining route to H. Induction
establishes (2). After the final station, R reaches H with exactly g_D.
At H add P_j^T g_D to each actual b_j before its old reverse-mode consumer.
The net added response is

    -P_j^T sum_(t>=j) Q_t^T d_t
      = -sum_(t>=j) (Q_t P_j)^T d_t.                       (3)

The b_j bank in the second read is its actual changed current row. Nothing
assumes that the first read left the original b_j available as an untouched
copy. The old -beta_j g_D contribution can now run literally with the
restored mode. All original d_j fields are preserved and may depart after
their real local subtraction.

### The descending forward trajectory

Initialize the old forward owner at H with

    F = g_X + sum_j P_j x_j.

It descends through the same station chain. On arrival at C_t its value is

    F = g_X + sum_(j<=t) P_j x_j.                         (4)

Before removing the station's source contribution, copy this actual value
into a zero-born stationary output cache Z_t. Then subtract the real
stationary A_t=P_t x_t from F. This establishes (4) for the next lower
station. The owner eventually reaches L with its original g_X value.

The later receiver response is

    (alpha_t-Q_t) g_X + Q_t Z_t
      = alpha_t g_X + sum_(j<=t) Q_t P_j x_j.              (5)

Together (3) and (5) prove the paired map (1), including all same-station
terms. Reading R after its station subtraction or capturing F after its
source subtraction would omit those diagonal station terms. Both wrong
orders have explicit counterexamples. A dirty output cache adds its unknown
baseline to (5); zero initialization is a necessary premise here.

All transposes occur in the correct order. Thus the proof permits arbitrary
noncommuting V-by-V blocks, not only coordinatewise scalar operations.
Unknown original y and b baselines are retained throughout. The old modes
may themselves be arbitrary unknown functions of the source; the algebra
cancels their added effects exactly rather than assuming their values zero.

## 2. What is shared and what remains charged

The construction uses the two existing mode lifetimes. If their old routes
were each one rank-r MOVE, and the refined routes have positive ranks
h_1,...,h_{s+1} with sum h_i=r, the additional owned movement moment is

    2[sum_i h_i^t-r^t],       0<t<=1.                      (6)

Owned rank mass is unchanged, but the movement moment generally increases.
The actual source X/B stops, D/Y stops and early codec route changes must
also be included. A trajectory is not charged as a new whole rank factor
each time its payload changes, nor is a new transition treated as free.

One stationary source cache is sufficient at each station with nonzero P_j,
and one stationary output cache is sufficient at each station with nonzero
Q_j. In the scalar whole-record case, let sP and sQ count the nonzero P and Q
coefficients, and let a,b count the two kinds of nonempty cache station.
Outside the modified early and late codec bodies, the additional explicit
ADD work is

    4 sP + 2 sQ + a+b.                                    (7)

The four P terms are stationary acquisition, Hub forward augmentation,
local B consumption and Hub B compensation. The two Q terms are reverse
station subtraction and final receiver consumption. There is one actual
source-cache subtraction and one output-cache copy per applicable station.
Every new cache also has an INIT and CLEAR, live storage, and its inherited
physical erasure/precision cost. The native word below records the exact net
ordinary count, including the changed codec bodies.

### Actual history dimension

If g_X is independent modulo the disjoint P_j x_j row spaces, the complete
forward crossing history has rank

    V + sum_j rank(P_j).

Indeed consecutive crossing rows differ by P_j x_j, and the final crossing
contains g_X. The disjoint source input blocks make the added ranks sum.
Similarly, if g_D is independent modulo the disjoint Q_j^T d_j row spaces,
the reverse crossing history has rank V+sum_j rank(Q_j). These statements
concern all actual boundary rows of one specified lifetime. Reusing a
numerical owner label in earlier coupled-entry calls does not add those
unrelated lifetimes to the measurement.

This explains the escape from the isolated fresh-factor tariff. The old
funded trajectories carry a sequence of genuinely different information
rows. Every source change and every crossing is still executed and priced.
The useful final correction rank can be smaller than the entire history
rank, and neither rank alone establishes a complete compiler.

## 7. Optimistic global check and the next structural obligation

This primitive is not by itself a complete architecture. With the old class
Y decoding retained, every additional receiver cache read must lie above
that receiver's C3_J. A single monotone trajectory of cache stations cannot
serve two different classes J and J'. To see this, suppose its earlier
read chart contains C3_J and its later read chart lies in the final facet of
a receiver h' in J'. Then all four omitted mode lines spanning C3_J lie in
F_h'. By the exact omitted-frame incidence, their four indices are contained
in the four-element support of J'. Therefore the two supports, and hence
the two classes, are equal.

Consequently, eight such class-preserving mode-pair streams alone can change
at most eight of the fourteen classes. At least six classes keep the seed's
nonzero offdiagonal debt. This is a source-specific restriction on this
particular unchanged decoder interface, irrespective of how favorable the
route refinement arithmetic is. It is not a rank lower bound for all
changing histories or for a changed receiver geometry.

The substantial next obligation is to combine actual funded prefix histories
with a receiver code whose real cache reads can cross the old class boundary,
or to change the paid source trajectories and decoder jointly. Square or
branching collectors, other original-route recoding and legitimately shared
history remain separate construction questions. Their acquisition, endpoint
restoration and entire tariff must be proved in one actual word. Repeating
the present local optimization cannot resolve this class restriction.

This source is separate from the S011 changed-preload proper-chart relay,
which uses r=(G-I)(d_H,0_U) and leaves original D restoration debt. Here the
retained omitted-line source is rebuilt with every original D restored and
both old Gram modes preserved. No results or prices from those alternative
sources are added together.
