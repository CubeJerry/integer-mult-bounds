## 1. Exact fixed-frontier theorem

Let F be a finite field and x range over F^N. Before the selected H steps,
the complete original-owner frontier is z=E x with E invertible. The
selected H input-owner sets I_s are disjoint. At H_s retain the specified
p_s-by-p_s signal matrix P_s, of rank p_s-d_s, and retain every other
original frontier coordinate. Set D=sum_s d_s. Assume every later old
observation is a function of these retained signals, and the only additional
X-dependent information available for restoration is a transcript of e
field symbols. Exact restoration of all original inputs requires e>=D.

The transcript may be any deterministic joint function of the entire
input, and the restoration may be nonlinear. Arbitrary invertible coupling
in E is allowed. For proof, reorder the frontier only in the mathematical
calculation. Its retained map is diag(P_1,...,P_s,I)E, of rank N-D. Each
fibre has |F|^D inputs with identical old signals. Correct restoration
requires distinct extra transcripts for all these inputs, proving e>=D.
No physical permutation is declared free by this rank calculation.

Choose a rank-restoring d_s-row complement L_s at each H_s. The reviewed
paid-H compiler extends [P_s;L_s] to an invertible owned-record map, sends
D complements to Hub, reverses the maps there, restores the original
owners and clears the auxiliary zeros. Thus D is attainable as an
information count in this direct-export interface, conditional on the
surrounding early word. All ADDs and extra INIT/MOVE/CLEARs remain paid.
Information optimality is not an optimized operation invoice.

For body width V>0, each retained fibre has |F|^(DV) inputs. Over GF(2^w),
at least Dw additional width-V binary records are needed. Coefficient
extension or body packing does not divide away the missing information.

## 4. Boundaries and controls

Changing retained signals, adding other informative routes or restoration
side information, dropping original restoration, or using H steps without
a disjoint input frontier changes the hypotheses. For different routes,
the information bound is not automatically a gap6 movement lower bound.
No general network tariff is proved.

The checker binds the literal owner partition, exact quota census and
strict integer budget transitions. Small exhaustive binary controls show
that two independent losses need four transcripts and one combined parity
does not suffice. An overlapping-input control has only one lost dimension,
showing that disjointness is essential. The universal proof is the rank
and cardinality argument, not those finite examples.
