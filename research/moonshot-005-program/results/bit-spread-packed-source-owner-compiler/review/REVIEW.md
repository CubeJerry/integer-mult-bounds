# Independent binary price on every emitted packed donor

The verifier reads the emitted compressed field primitives and holder dictionaries for each source. Its polynomial reduction independently prices the six-bit scalar additions and reverses each nonzero in-place multiplier on its six basis inputs. It checks that every primitive is invertible, that each replica has the specified field width, and that every original early holder at each patched donor participates exactly once in the input placements. All early shuffles stay on their donor. Every late write lands on a distinct actual Y at the specified donor line and H chart. The verifier independently accumulates the cost of every complementary background donor from the reviewed RS manifest. Coefficient identities come from the separate chart and singleton reviews.
