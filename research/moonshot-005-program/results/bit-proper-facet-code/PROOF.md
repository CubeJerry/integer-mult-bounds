## 2. Proper shortening theorem over every characteristic-two field

Let k be any characteristic-two field and V any positive integer. Extend
C to k and tensor with k^V. For a real chart H, define the shortening

    W(H)={w in C_k tensor k^V : w_i=0 whenever R v_i is not contained in H}.

For every proper linear subspace H of R8, W(H) is zero unless H is one
of the nine rank7 facets above. At the corresponding facet H_a,

    W(H_a)=c_a tensor k^V,       dim_k W(H_a)=V.

Proof. First take a scalar word over k. Its nine coefficients in the fixed
binary basis span a finite-dimensional F2 subspace of k. Expand them in an
F2 basis lambda_1,...,lambda_d. The word becomes sum_h lambda_h c_h for
binary words c_h in C, and its support is exactly the union of the supports
of the nonzero c_h: independence of the lambda_h prevents coordinate
cancellation. If this union lies in a proper real chart, every constituent
is one of the nine proper words. Two different ones have full-dimensional
support union, so all nonzero constituents are the same word. The scalar
word is therefore a k multiple of one c_a, and its real support spans H_a.

For a vector-valued word, apply that argument to all V coordinate
projections. Its support is the union of their supports. Again different
proper words cannot occur together. Every coordinate is a multiple of the
same c_a, giving exactly c_a tensor k^V. This argument does not assume that
a general packet or decoder was a scalar tensor product in advance.
