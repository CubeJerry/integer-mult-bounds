## Why 352 additions do not reduce the actual reverse rank

Let S be the eight selected hosted port indices. The source-derived matrix
R has nonzero rows only outside S and nonzero columns only inside S. Hence

    R^2 = 0,       (I-R)(I+R) = I.

There are 352 nonzero entries, but the desired hosted target is b-d, so the
current reverse residual is `(I+R)d`. Its rank is exactly 96. No hosted reverse
pair is complete. The transpose additions are components that would be useful
with a compatible reverse-E8 baseline; that baseline is not present and is
not granted by this invoice.

This distinction matters for both the research state and further pricing.
Counting the 352 terms as completed reverse obligations would give a false
picture of the global deficit. The new result is a physically realized
partial reciprocal code, together with a precise test that its terminal
state does not yet remove the binding reverse information requirement.

## Sharp append-only consequence for this terminal state

Consider continuations that retain this exact terminal interface and only
add new field-linear arrivals at the hub. No earlier recoding or changed
common processing is granted. All 96 original d fields remain at their
proper eight-dimensional facets. The hub's b bank is b+R d; the other hub
observations have no new d information beyond those mixed fields.

Fix the common inputs and the other private inputs. For arbitrary change
delta in the 96 independent d values, change the original b values by
`-R delta`. Every current hub observation remains unchanged. The required
target b-d changes by `-(I+R)delta`, an invertible 96-dimensional map. Thus
the target variation on a fibre of the current hub information has dimension
96 per field coordinate. Every new full-field arrival can contribute at
most one such dimension. At least 96 arrivals are necessary. This is the
programme's existing terminal-information argument applied to the new
source state; it is not a new global transport lower bound.

The bound is attained within the retained contracts. For each original d_j,
initialize an owned record at its facet, copy d_j, move it one rank to the
hub, subtract its column of I+R from the b outputs, and retire the record.
The 832-instruction literal suffix is in
`APPEND-ONLY-REPAIR-SUFFIX.json`. It has 96 rank-one calls, 96 INITs/CLEARs,
96 acquisition ADDs and 448 decoder ADDs. Every MOVE pair already occurs in
the authenticated source or paired word. The full type walk verifies the
78,627-instruction composition, and its 96 hosted reverse rows are exact.

The target margin becomes

    41.01712837582312... - 96 < 0.

Therefore this append-only completion cannot meet the target even before
remaining forward, coupled, ordinary and foundational costs are charged.
Earlier acquisition, global recoding, additional funded information or a
changed common interface are outside this obstruction and remain possible
research directions.
