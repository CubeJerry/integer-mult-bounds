Let F be a finite field of characteristic two and let `G=(F^32)^r`, where actual balanced replication requires `r=180s` for positive integer s.

Base matrices may differ between donors and planes and need not commute; the copies of each particular base matrix are identical in the common original coordinates. Singleton encoders and downstream deterministic processing may be arbitrary within the real capacities.

Fix one native donor j. Grant the ground decoder any number of additional deterministic messages determined by the entire raw bank X_j and the original canonical values. Even if this information is supplied without movement cost, **no complete native code exists in this source representation**.

For all 111 choices of j, a freshly proved zero-slice boundary bound is strict. Over arbitrary F its maximum is `(14089/5)r`, below the required 3232r. The minimum deficit is `(2071/5)r`, or **74,556 field dimensions at r=180**. Over F2 the stronger binary graph-plane bound gives minimum deficit `(2316/5)r`, or 83,376 at r=180.

This concerns helpers determined by one fixed raw donor bank. It does not cover arbitrary mixed syndromes, replacement banks, larger coupled source modules, independently varying copies, or new proper routes. We do not subtract a helper rank from an old deficit: the saturation reduction and every affected coloring instance are recomputed.

## 1. A zero slice neutralizes the helper and preserves local freedom

Omit receiver j. For each i different from j, restrict K to donors outside `N(i) union {j}`. The checker verifies rank nine for all 12,210 ordered pairs. Thus arbitrary values of the remaining foreign donors can be extended by outside donors to satisfy KX=0 while keeping X_j zero. Outside donors have no proper paths to receiver i. On this slice,

`X_i = sum_(k in N(i)) X_k`,

so recovery at every retained receiver is exactly recovery of its varying foreign sum. The remaining 110 outputs recover every varying input; the omitted X_j is already zero.
