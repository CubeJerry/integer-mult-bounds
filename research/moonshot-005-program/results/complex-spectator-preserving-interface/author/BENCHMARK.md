# An all-input three-class source with a full quartic component

The S021 remote checkpoint mentions a pending all-input S3 benchmark without a published construction. This is an explicit reconstruction from its admitted literal source. Equality with an unpublished earlier candidate is unknown.

Take $K=I$ in the theorem. Write $P_j=a^{-j}Pa^j$, $N_j=I-P_j$, $s_2=\mathrm{SWAP}_{ab}$ and $h=\mathrm{SWAP}_{bd}^{\otimes W}$, acting trivially on the prefix. The source transports $P_1=(I+Z_\sigma\otimes\mathrm{SWAP}_{ad}^{\otimes W})/2$, $P_2=(I+Z_\sigma\otimes s_2^{\otimes W})/2$ are the admitted exact identities. The tail permutations $s_2,h$ generate $S_3$. Define
$$
 V=P_2+N_2h,\qquad J=N_2hN_2,\qquad T=5J-4J^3.
$$
The group algebra gives $TJ=JT=N_2$. This follows by expanding in the twelve-element group generated by $s_2,h,Z_\sigma$. The checker performs exact rational convolution in that group. Equivalently the two nontrivial eigenvalues in absolute value of the compressed transposition on its active subspace are $1$ and $1/2$. Thus
$$
 V^{-1}=P_2+T(N_2-N_2hP_2).
$$
Indeed $P_2 Vx=P_2x$, while $N_2 Vx=N_2hP_2x+J N_2x$. This proves invertibility on the whole arbitrary input, including every positive tail width and both prefix signs.

Use a balanced unit with an actual ordinary inverse
$$
 Q=E_{02}(I)\,\mathrm{Swap}_{02}\,\mathrm{diag}(I,V,V^{-1})
   =\begin{pmatrix} I&0&V^{-1}\\0&V&0\\I&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The two-bank balanced diagonal is the product $E_{12}(V)E_{21}(-V^{-1})E_{12}(V)J_0^{-1}$, with $J_0^{-1}=E_{12}(-I)E_{21}(I)E_{12}(-I)$. Implement each group-algebra shear by its finite rational-dyadic expansion in the twelve monomials. For each signed power-of-two summand, transform the source bank by the monomial and the signed power, add onto the target, then restore the source. This uses only ADD, SCALE by dyadic units and MONO. The checker constructs the complete forward and inverse words, counts every pass, bounds all prefix triangular norms and checks literal equality of the primitive product in the group algebra. No extra Gaussian calls, zero banks or temporary owners occur.

The admitted gauge $M=O_0^{-1}LCU$ has rows $(a^2P_1,-a^3P_2,-aN)$, $(aP_1,a^2N_2,-N)$ and $(a,0,S)$. Using $P_2V=P_2$ and $N_2V=N_2h$, exact block multiplication gives the entire source matrix
$$
 MQL=\begin{pmatrix}
 -aNa&-a^3P_2a&a^2P_1V^{-1}\\
 a^2P_2-Na+a^2N_2h&a^2N_2ha&aP_1V^{-1}\\
 a^2+Sa&0&aV^{-1}
 \end{pmatrix}.
$$
The one-letter words $AsA$, $A^3s_2A$, $A^2hA$ and $A^2s_2hA$ have respective Gaussian support ranks $2,2,1,1$. The checker separately establishes the Gaussian Walsh-block property of these matrices. It also checks the exact matrix on every input at width one in both prefix sectors. Different conjugations of the input by tail permutations are paid ordinary monomials, preserving these classes. Consequently all input columns use only classes $1,2,4$ at every width, and the **entire quartic component** is the single full-bank corner
$$
 \pi_4(MQL)=-\tfrac12 e_0 a^4 e_1^T.
$$
At sufficiently large faithful widths all three classes are nonzero; single-letter support examples do not prove all-width class independence on their own. The scalar $-1/2$ is a genuine field scalar at every width.

The theorem rules out any independent quartic endpoint for this same word. In fact this example gives a direct algebraic explanation. If a summand $H$ were the independently isolated quartic component, then $E^{-1}H$ would be an ordinary idempotent for $E=MQL$, since it would equal the input-gauge conjugate of a coordinate projection. The checker supplies an exact reduced-width failed-idempotence control for the displayed $H$ when applicable; the all-width impossibility is proved separately. Thus all-input class purity and a full invertible high corner by themselves do not supply the remaining independent jobs. The local pass receipts do not admit a physical full caller or a new κ.
