## 1. General protected projection theorem

Let F be a field. Consider a finite linear record word on independent original
vectors x_1,...,x_n and arbitrary y_1,...,y_n in F^V. Assume the word restores
every X_j, implements Y_i=y_i+x_i on a specified set S, and leaves every other
Y_i unchanged. Original records follow ascending chart routes; owned fields
follow the required descending routes and have fully counted lifetimes.

Choose a set D of original roles, not necessarily contained in S. Require:

1. Each X_d, d∈D, is immutable in the old word: no arithmetic operation writes
   into it.
2. For every d∈D∩S there is a distinct protected owner B_d that is zero-born
   at the actual native chart L_d, filled there from X_d, and legally moved
   to ground. It remains exactly x_d until explicit retirement. Its capture
   is independent of the retained old calculation.
3. Each B_d is actually present at ground before Y_d's first ascent. Repairing
   Y_d there has no later computational readers. It suffices that no Y is ever
   an arithmetic source, as in the concrete word.
4. Every old read from each protected B_d is explicitly identified. Its
   acquisition, route, storage and retirement remain paid.

Define the linear coordinate projection Π_D by

    Π_D(x_d)=0 for d∈D,
    Π_D(x_j)=x_j for j∉D,
    Π_D(y_j)=y_j for every j.

Modify the physical word as follows. Preserve each protected native capture
X_d→B_d and its actual route. Remove every other old arithmetic read of X_d,
and remove every old arithmetic read of B_d into the retained calculation.
Remove all old movements of X_d. Keep every other old operation. Before the
first ascent of each Y_d with d∈D∩S, actually add B_d into Y_d once. A released
original that is not yet used in a new construction stays raw at its native
line and eventually takes the legal direct L_d→Hub move.

**Claim.** All old endpoint equations remain correct. Every retained
intermediate row, except the protected X_d/B_d and the explicitly repaired
Y_d, equals Π_D of its old row. Retained locations are unchanged. The released
original routes have no remaining dependency in the old computation.

### Proof

Every retained original X_j with j∉D and every original Y_j initially equals
its projected row. Zero initialization, linear arithmetic and MOVE commute
with Π_D. An old raw X_d read has projected value zero, so deletion is exactly
the projected update. The independent protected capture remains an explicit
exception. Its owner B_d stays pure x_d, hence every old B_d read also has
projected value zero and is correctly deleted. Induction through the complete
old instruction list gives the stated covariance.

Deleted reads impose no new co-location requirements. Every retained
operation uses its old chart, and each protected capture still precedes the
released original's replacement departure. Since X_d is immutable, no old
write or later read requires its old route or value at an old source chart.

At exit, the retained original X_j has Π_D(x_j)=x_j. If i∈S\D, the old Y_i
endpoint stays y_i+x_i. If i∉S, its endpoint stays y_i, even when i∈D. If
d∈S∩D, the projected endpoint is y_d and the actual ground backup supplies
the missing x_d. Hypothesis3 prevents that repair from entering other
computations. Released originals closed directly at Hub keep their original
raw value. Every endpoint is therefore correct. ∎

The physical map has not discarded any original coordinate: those coordinates
remain in X_d and, where required, in the paid B_d. Only the retained old
calculation is projected. An alternative collector word may replace a raw
closing route, but it requires its own actual acquisition, chronological
reader-correction, restoration and complete-cost proof.

The argument is valid over every field for a linear source word satisfying
the stated hypotheses. The concrete source uses binary ADDs, so its literal
identities hold componentwise over every characteristic-two field and every
positive whole-record width. No finite-width numerical extrapolation is used.

## 2. Exact common-source criterion

Let G be the span of the old actual pure-X fields simultaneously at ground.
Let E be the span of protected raw coordinates {x_d:d∈D∩S}. The new actual
ground span is exactly Π_D(G)+E, provided the only exceptional ground records
are these protected copies. This formula requires no assumption that the
original G survives.

If G has zero coordinates on D\S and contains E, then Π_D(G)+E=G. For every
g∈G, its deleted part g−Π_D(g) lies in E⊆G, so Π_D(g)∈G. Conversely the
same identity reconstructs every g from Π_D(g) and E.

This sufficient condition holds for the actual73-carrier witness and is also
verified by independent binary row-space comparison. It is a statement about
simultaneous ground values at one exact cut. Higher charts and earlier source
times are not granted the protected raw information. Old source histories and
proper imports must be recomputed after projection.
