review. No new kappa and no complete supplier.** This result concerns the
literal late single-copy pair-repair cut and the new three-copy binary
implementation of the F8 frame service. It does not exclude either source
from every possible target-budget continuation.

The source has two distinct unpaid information duties. Eight raised receiver
roles occupy four private rank-six charts. The other 95 unfinished roles
still require a common information transcript. The moves paying the first
duty cannot be the moves paying the second duty. In the linear model their
combined floor is **17 whole-record positive MOVEs per paid native copy**.

## 3. At least two private arrivals per copy and pair

Fix one region P_e={H_e,F_i,F_j}. Condition every original input except its
2b independent records x_(c,i),x_(c,j), including every initial Y. At the cut,
all fields currently inside P_e are independent of these missing records.

Treat all three charts in P_e as one observer. This grants arbitrary free
communication inside the region, so it only weakens a physical lower bound.
The observer can learn new information solely when a record moves from a
chart outside P_e to one inside P_e. Local operations, zero births, reads,
erasures, and moves between its three charts introduce no new information.
A record may leave and return, and may be recoded arbitrarily outside; its
return remains a newly counted arrival.

At completion the region contains all 2b output records y_(c,i)+x_(c,i) and
y_(c,j)+x_(c,j). Under the conditioning these determine all 2b independent
unknown records. Each incoming whole record carries at most V field
coordinates. Linear rank therefore forces at least 2b incoming records over
any characteristic-two field. In the finite-alphabet nonlinear model the
same conclusion follows from conditional entropy: output entropy is
2b V log|k|, while one record arrival increases available entropy by at most
V log|k|.

Every such arrival is an owned positive MOVE. Other original Ys cannot visit
P_e: H_e lies in exactly F_i,F_j, and a public route to another F_h must
remain inside F_h. The existing target Ys start inside P_e and can only move
to their own terminal facet, still inside P_e. All original X are already at
Hub and cannot descend. Hence no original public's already-paid closing
MOVE can be counted as a new private arrival.

Sum over the four disjoint destination regions to obtain 8b distinct owned
positive MOVE occurrences. A single record serving different private regions
requires separate arrivals; those occurrences are counted separately.

## 5. The remaining receiver downset needs nine records per copy

Let D_U be the union of all subspaces contained in any F_i with i in U. It is
a downward-closed geometric region. In the time-unrolled linear continuation,
cut every owned downward MOVE whose destination lies in D_U, and replace the
cut output by an independent whole-record variable z_e. The source audit in
Section2 supplies the actual starting data; it is not replaced by a fresh
unconditioned native source.

Fix all initial Y, all axes, and all original X outside U. A path to terminal
F_i that avoids the cut can use only local processing and upward moves after
its last initial source inside F_i. Upward movement cannot enter F_i or D_U
from outside. Every possible downward entry into F_i is among the cut events.
Consequently the uncut dependence M0 of remaining outputs on remaining inputs
has support only on native neighbour blocks and has zero diagonal blocks.
The completed map is identity, so, with each actual cut output's complete

    I - M0 = sum_e A_e C_e.

The left side is a fitting on U with bV-wide blocks. Section4 implies rank at
least 9bV. Every cut record has dimension V, so there must be at least 9b
whole-record downward occurrences. Dependence of a later cut value on earlier
cuts is fully allowed; independence of the cut payloads is not assumed.

## 6. Why the two charges add

Every chart in P_e contains H_e. Its only possible containing native facets
are F_i,F_j, both indexed in T. Therefore P_e is disjoint from D_U. A private
arrival has destination in P_e; a remaining-transcript event has destination
in D_U. No event can belong to both sets, regardless of owned route turns,
reuse, recoding, or source rank. This gives the stated sum of floors.

These are **future** movement charges. Source closures have no owned MOVEs;
they contain only already-paid upward public endpoints and owner retirements.
Any alternative monotone public route costs at least its already-counted
single remaining jump, by subadditivity of r^t for 0<t<=1. Consequently the
new owned events add at least their number of unit charges to the recorded
complete source movement moment. They cannot be absorbed by removing an
old closing MOVE. No ordinary, ownership, storage, or recursive expense is
removed by this argument.
