# Independent affine-family, common-response, and receiver review

The [proof](PROOF.md) establishes two new points: a two-overlap construction
raises the common response on the forced four-space from rank two to four;
the actual eight-output receiver requires a stronger contrast-rank bound.
The exhibited matrix has contrast rank 28, while the actual receiver requires
at most 24. No fifth whole receiver or new complete exponent follows.

The independent checker also verifies the complete exact affine reduction
from the [construction package](../bit-coupled-fifth-s033/PROOF.md):

- For every one of the 44 local systems, the intersection of its H-observation
  core with the fixed four-frame span is exactly generated by the retained
  sections of the donor's inputs in {4,5,7,8}.
- The four parent ingress maps jointly have rank eight for each donor and
  retained receiver.
- These identities prove that, on the nonsingular-section domain, source
  interpolation is equivalent to fixing 88 D coefficients, leaving an open
  subset of an 833-dimensional affine space. The individual source-map
  extension fibres have total dimension 13,368 over GF(64).

For the equivalence, write E(j)=I(j) intersect {4,5,7,8}. The independently
verified intersection basis consists of the vectors L(r) T_old[O(r),E(j)].
Preserving their H-prescribed images requires

    M(j,h,r) S_new(j,r)^(-1) T_old[O(r),E(j)]
      = M(j,h,r) E_coordinates(j).

Stacking the four rank-eight parent maps cancels M. Multiplication by the
invertible new section makes this equivalent to

    T_new[O(r),E(j)] = T_old[O(r),E(j)].

The union of these equations is exactly the 22-by-4 fixed rectangle. Conversely
these equations fix the images of an entire basis of every intersection,
which is precisely the necessary and sufficient condition for extending a
linear source map from the two prescribed subspaces. This is an exact
equivalence, rather than an inference from the earlier tangent dimension.
