## Near-full one-stage restriction

An independent bit-mask computation on all 12,210 ordered donor pairs gives minimum ordered difference 21, minimum unordered intersection 23, minimum degree 55 and total 6,470 foreign incidences. Removing at most 20 arbitrary receivers keeps all 111 signatures nonempty and pairwise incomparable. Their intersection charts inherit incomparability: the donor line realizing $U$ belongs to $C_U$; membership in $C_W$ would imply $W\subseteq U$. Each monotone receiver-bank path visits at most one of these incomparable anchors. With two scalar donors lacking raw ground backups, at least three common receivers each need a terminal arrival from at least one of these donors. Their two single-private-terminal originals can cover at most two receivers. Thus at most one donor may lack a whole-record backup in this model.

With $s\ge91$ active roles, write $d_j V$ for the independent ground raw information from donor $j$ over $b$ copies. Its residual demand at each incident receiver has rank at least $(b-d_j)V$ upon restricting all other donors. Each paid bank copy at a visited singleton anchor, or at its one private terminal, can deliver at most $V$ dimensions from its permitted donor. As each such service has only one donor's innovation in this model, their sum bounds the incidences by $(s+111)bV$. This gives $(s-56)\sum_j(b-d_j)\le(s+111)b$. Arbitrary linear mixing **within the $b$ copies of that donor** is covered. A carrier carrying new degrees of freedom from several donors, a later subcohort, or a coded ground side-information channel is outside the proof. Counting the same mixed carrier separately for each donor without this restriction would be invalid.

At $s=91$ and $s=111$ the backup demand is at least $(3683/35)b$ and $(5883/55)b$, respectively. Fixed canonical preload and the baseline public geometry alone leave strictly between 11 and 12 movement units per copy for these raw backups.
