## 1. The declared routing interface

A public helper MOVE is *suffix neutral* when a formal increment immediately before it has zero response in every final public common field outside hub 127 and in the unique COPY zero-target guard. Exact backward adjoints of all 458 offhub outputs and that guard classify the 4,553 public helper MOVEs: 1,472 neutral and 3,081 nonzero. Each rejected event has a recorded exact nonzero output coefficient. ADD, SCALE, COPY, INIT and CLEAR are propagated literally; COPY introduces a pre-event zero-target coordinate. Normalized MOVE is the identity. No modular-zero inference is used.

For every donor Xj, grant original q_Xj at every chart of its original raw-main itinerary before common execution. Caches remain locally available forever. A reached chart can send a pure q along a neutral helper MOVE at its native event time. This grants replacement of the current dirty payload, extraction of q at arrival and eventual common restoration; it does not prove restoration. Also grant either 33 retained or all 47 original **separate clean second-owned** reservations at native times, ignoring their original duties and cost. These are not interventions on the common-owned fields.

Define S_ij=1 if a chronological path reaches any chart of receiver Yi's original raw itinerary. Receiver visits can wait until caches arrive. Every positive entry has an explicit source-event path, seed chart and arrival time. The graph grants all individually reachable coordinates simultaneously, although their paths may compete for one-field slots. Each receiver additionally obtains all paid broadcasts for free.

Only these q-coordinate observations are admitted as source-dependent side information. Arbitrary dirty common observations, ADD-mediated common responses and travel through unlisted native fields are excluded from this interface. This is essential: the graph is not a description of every admissible native controller.

Further diagnostic graphs freely retime clean-owned arcs; the largest additionally grants arbitrary reuse and retiming of all 1,710 raw-main MOVEs. That last grant reuses reservations already consumed in the initial hoisting. It is deliberately more permissive than a physical schedule, and supplies no construction.

## 2. Exact gap in the eight-packet code

The E8-type root representation and endpoint coding mechanism have prior research provenance; no claim of a new abstract root code is made. The104 actual active ports comprise70 of binary weight3 and34 of weight7. With coordinate9 distinguished, define a(v) in Q^8:

- Weight3 containing9: value2 at the two other coordinates,0 elsewhere.
- Weight3 excluding9: value1 at the triple,−1 at the other five coordinates.
- Weight7 missing i<j≤8:2e_i−2e_j.
- Weight7 missing i and9: value1 at i,−1 elsewhere.

The source Gram matrix has diagonal8, off-diagonal0 at odd binary dot products and off-diagonal±4 at even products. Its rank is8. Each active port has 48 other even-dot active ports. Receiver Yi's native final frame is exactly v_i-perp. Thus the packets P_k=sum_j a(v_j)_k q_Xj satisfy

    q_Xi = [a(v_i)·P − sum_{j≠i}(a(v_i)·a(v_j))q_Xj]/8.

There are 48 nonzero correction terms. These are **even** neighbors, not the odd cliques of a different decoder architecture.

For each i, the other odd-dot root columns span exactly the seven-dimensional orthogonal complement of a(v_i). Consequently if only individual even-neighbor coordinates are supplied, each of the 48 is necessary for these eight packets: a packet decoder vanishing on all unknown odd columns is proportional to a(v_i), and cannot also vanish on a missing even column. A directly supplied weighted aggregate is a different, potentially useful interface.

For the actual timed graph, none of the 104 receivers can decode using the eight packets plus all its granted individual side information. The receipt proves this for any decoder, not merely the displayed formula: it gives z with

    z_i=1,   A z=0,   z_j=0 whenever S_ij=1,

where A has columns a(v_j). Inputs differing by z yield identical packets and identical known coordinates, but different desired q_Xi. The first example is z_0=1,z_4=−1,z_6=−1,z_10=1. In the largest graph only the five directly delivered demands succeed; the other 99 have explicit indistinguishability witnesses. Positive controls supply all 48 even coordinates; negative controls remove one and verify failure. No finite experiment is extrapolated to a different source.

## 3. Acyclic subsets bound all linear broadcast codes

**Lemma.** Let independent q_1,...,q_n each have dimension V over a field K. Receiver i recovers q_i from a common linear broadcast B and coordinates q_j with S_ij=1. If the directed graph i→j when S_ij=1 has an acyclic induced subset U, then dim(B)≥|U|V. Thus at least|U| full-field packets are required, for every positive V and every field K.

**Proof.** Grant all q_j outside U globally. In a topological ordering of U, the last receiver has no side-information edge to another member of U, so recovers its q_i from B and the outside inputs. The preceding receiver can then recover its input, continuing backwards through the order. Therefore the independent tuple q_U is a linear function of B after outside inputs are fixed. The broadcast map restricted to q_U is injective and has rank at least|U|V. Each field packet has dimension at most V. The same argument applies if B collects interactive linear messages and each is granted globally. QED.

## 4. Conditional H4 invoice

The inherited ideal H4 profile at t=199/200 permits corrective paid t-moment strictly below 98.39907695 per native. This is the conditional bound in `results/shared-core-temporal/PROOF.md`, not a realizable native or a completed multiplication theorem.

If completion broadcasts only rank9 packets from hub 127 to empty chart3, even the43-packet lower bound in the largest graph exceeds the allowance. More sharply, retaining the prescribed eight rank9 root packets requires at least 35 further full-field broadcast dimensions in that graph. Grant every further packet a globally delivered rank1 call. The price is still at least

    8·9^(199/200)+35 > 8·8+35 =99 >98.39907695.

The strict inequality9^(199/200)>8 is exactly 9^199>8^200, so no numerical rounding is involved. The timed graph requires 46 additional packet dimensions after the eight root packets. Coupled reverse repairs, all clean-owned duties, distribution, common restoration, storage and ordinary work were granted free. They cannot reduce this price.

This corollary concerns packets added to the declared side-information interface. A paid operation that changes the base interface through additional independent dirty observations is not merely such a packet and requires its own all-input analysis. The result does not prove a bound on all native controllers, nor on every possible use of eight root packets.

## 5. The next construction obligation

The eight-packet identity remains potentially useful if its weighted side-information aggregates can be supplied by a stronger physically restored interface. Pure neutral-helper transport, even with the generous raw-main grant, cannot supply a completion at the current invoice.

Its displaced original q891 can geometrically reach intermediate chart 549 along helper slots 33250,34743,36501,36503. These four pure-payload replacements still require joint common restoration. Their original second-owned duties are also unpaid. This candidate is recorded separately and is not part of the proved construction.
