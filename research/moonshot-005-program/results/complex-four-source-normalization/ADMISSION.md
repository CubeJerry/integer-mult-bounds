# Four nonlinear source types with literal paid native adapters

**Proved exact classification and source reduction, independently reviewed.
No complete caller, supplier or new kappa.**

Every one of the128 odd three-bit Boolean-sign sources is explicitly monomial-
conjugate to a signed representative with

    f_(j,w)=j*c+w*b*c+a*b*c,  (j,w) in F2².

The actual conjugator uses at most three NOTs and one sign-phase pass.
At every positive width there are exactly four projective similarity classes,
distinguished even under arbitrary complex similarity by the zero-pattern of
(trace,e2). There are eight ordinary similarity classes at odd widths and four
at even widths. The absolute sign is (-1)^(epsilon*V), not a width-independent
one-column phase. The proof gives closed formulas from all Boolean coefficients.

Head similarity is transported through the actual three-bank native K(a).
For lambda=±1 the exact identity K(lambda*a)=D_lambda K(a) E_lambda uses
two unit ADDs and two SCALE(-1) passes in the negative case, with no Gaussian
query or helper. Conjugating all banks supplies the complete source adapter.
All monomial services, actual inverses, bank operations and full payloads are
paid. Native count and Gaussian profile are preserved; added ordinary constants
must still fit any eventual supplier invoice.
