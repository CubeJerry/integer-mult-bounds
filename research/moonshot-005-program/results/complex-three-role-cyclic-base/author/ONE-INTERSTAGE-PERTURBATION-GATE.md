# One internal ordinary modification cannot upgrade the exact three-role base

**Proved structural obstruction for the displayed source-bound base; independent review pending. No new kappa.** Starting from K(a)^3=a² I_3 with K(a)=L(a)T, changing only one internal ordinary interstage cannot produce independent head ranks(3,2,2). The ordinary replacement may contain arbitrary noncommuting coefficients and zero divisors. A viable perturbation of this base must coordinate BOTH internal interstages or change another source premise.

Use the same finite phase-exact Gaussian-rank components and ordinary-bimodule algebra as the admitted source gates. The head a has rank1 and a² has rank2. All original inputs remain arbitrary and all outer ordinary encodings are invertible.

An arbitrary replacement of one internal T is equivalently an arbitrary ordinary insertion P in K² P K or K P K². Input/output insertions alone are ordinary boundary encodings and cannot change endpoint component ranks.

Since K³=a² I_3 and K=L T, the first word is ordinarily equivalent, by scalar-bank conjugation with T, to

    M=a² L^-1 P L.                                 (1)

Here a² I_3 commutes with the scalar bank matrix T. The inverse L^-1 is used only to rewrite an already asserted three-native word; it is not a free physical call. Write P=(p_ij), with all p_ij ordinary. The entries of(1) are

    [[a p11 a-p21 a,
      a p11+a p12 a-p21-p22 a,
      a p13-p23],
     [a p21 a,
      a p21+a p22 a,
      a p23],
     [a² p31 a,
      a² p31+a² p32 a,
      a² p33]].                                    (2)

The first two output rows have degree at most2, and their third-column entries have degree at most1. Any rank-three component lies in the third output row and has zero third input block.

Suppose M were an ordinary encoding of one independent rank-three and two independent rank-two Gaussian jobs. Its rank-three component has physical rankD, so the output column of the rank-three job must occupy exactly the third output row, with an invertible ordinary block there. After placing the rank-three job last, the ordinary output encoding is block lower triangular with an invertible bottom-right block; its2-by2 top-left ordinary block is therefore invertible. Consequently the first two output rows are pure E_2 and are an invertible ordinary encoding of the two independent rank-two jobs.

Their third-column entries in(2) belong to F_1, so they vanish. Since a is invertible,

    a p23=0,  a p13-p23=0  imply p23=p13=0.

P is now block lower triangular with invertible active2-by2 block and invertible p33. The first two rows of M are therefore supported exactly on the first two input roles and have full row rank2D: their active block is a product of the invertible two-role matrices [[a,-I],[0,a]], P_aa and L(a). Hence the input row space of the two rank-two endpoint jobs is exactly the active two-role input space.

It follows that the two rank-two endpoint input rows annihilate the third input role, so EVERY rank-two component of M must have zero third input block. This contradicts M33=a² p33, which is a nonzero invertible pure rank-two block. Equivalently, the rank-three component is confined to the same active input space already used by both independent rank-two jobs. The contradiction uses the complete independent-input contract, not just the existence of nonzero rank-two/rank-three components.

An explicit independence control is P=I+e31 I. Formula(2) becomes [[a²,0,0],[0,a²,0],[a³,a²,a²]]. It has a real rank-three component, but its rank-two component is invertible on ALL three roles, of rank3D. The two component labels alone therefore do not give the required independent(3,2,2) endpoint.

The second word K P K² is ordinarily equivalent to L P' L^-1 a² for an arbitrary ordinary P'=T P T^-1. Transposing this expression and swapping the two active roles reduces it to(1) with a replaced by a^T. Gaussian rank and ordinary status are preserved, so the same contradiction applies.

Thus modifying only one internal interstage of the exact base is insufficient. The result does not exclude two coordinated interstage changes, other native orientations or different retained-role/source contracts. It does not change the favorable prospective(3,2,2) invoice or provide a complete supplier.
