# Proper-chart completion requires changed source information or facet arrivals

**Author classification: proved source-specific algebraic lemmas and a conditional movement exclusion. Independent review pending. No new κ.**

This package turns the completed native forward map into a receiver-geometry
test. It does not construct a supplier. It uses the programme's admitted
minimum-rank block-fitting theorem, credits its proof below, and proves an
appended-one-mode obstruction directly from exact native pair witnesses.
All statements concern the actual pinned104 first-X ports, arbitrary positive
field volume V, and characteristic-zero linear operators unless explicitly
extended. An algebraic row space is not assumed physically available.

## 2. Arbitrary eight-mode completion forces full local facets

**Theorem 1.** Let F be a characteristic-zero field and V>=1. Suppose all104
normalized target rows can be decoded from one common row space U of
dimension at most8V and coordinate spaces Local(C_h), where C_h<=F_h.
Then C_h=F_h for every h.

Write e_h tensor I_V=K_h+L_h, with all K_h rows in U and L_h supported
on coordinates whose source lines lie in C_h. Stacking gives I=K+L and
rank(K)<=8V. Since L_hh=0, K_hh=I_V. If G_hj=0, L_j is not in F_h,
so K_hj=0. An authentic orthogonal eight-frame gives an identity8V minor
of K and hence rank(K)=8V.

The admitted full104 minimum-rank block-fitting rigidity theorem gives
invertible V-by-V operators T_h with

    K_hj = G_hj T_h T_j^(-1).

This inherited result assumes neither positivity, symmetry nor commuting
operators. For every off-diagonal Gram neighbor j of h, L_hj=−K_hj is
invertible. Hence every such L_j is in C_h. Their binary span is F_h,
forcing C_h=F_h.

## 3. Canonical Gram plus one extra field: orthogonal receivers conflict

**Theorem 2.** Fix U0=row(G tensor I_V). Let W be any V-by-104V matrix over
a field of characteristic different from2. Call receiver h proper if

    e_h tensor I_V belongs to U0 + row(W) + Local(C_h)

for some C_h properly contained in F_h. Two orthogonal native roots cannot
both be proper. Operator coefficients may be noncommuting, W may have
deficient rank, and W may be obtained by any means. This is an algebraic
capability statement, not a price or acquisition assertion.

For orthogonal b,c choose an authentic orthogonal frame B containing both.
The exact certificate supplies one for every one of the2860 such pairs.
Put U_B=G[B,:] tensor I_V, whose B columns form I_(8V), and normalize

    W' = W − W_B U_B.

The exact factorization G=G[:,B]G[B,:] follows because B is an orthonormal
basis of the real root span. All its entries are half-integral, so this
identity also holds over every field of characteristic different from2.
Thus U_B spans the stated U0 in every admitted characteristic.

This preserves U0+row(W) and makes W'_B=0. No B source line lies in F_b
or F_c. The B columns of a proposed decoder therefore force

    e_b tensor I_V = G_b tensor I_V + A_b W' + L_b,
    e_c tensor I_V = G_c tensor I_V + A_c W' + L_c,              (1)

with square V-by-V A_b,A_c and L_h supported in C_h.

Some Gram neighbor j of b lies outside proper C_b because all48 neighbors
span F_b. Then A_b W'_j=−G_bj I_V is invertible, so A_b is invertible.
The same holds for A_c. When G_cj=0, both its target and local block vanish,
giving W'_j=0. Therefore every exclusive neighbor

    S_(b|c) = {j != b : G_bj != 0 and G_cj=0}

must have its entire L_j in C_b. The exact binary span of this set is

    T_(b|c) = F_b intersect (v_c+511)^perp,                    (2)

of dimension7. Here511 is the all-ones binary vector. Every exclusive j has
v_b dot v_j=0, v_c dot v_j=1, and odd v_j, proving inclusion in(2).
Seven independent certified members prove equality. Since C_b is proper,
C_b=T_(b|c), and similarly C_c=T_(c|b).

Every common neighbor j has v_c dot v_j=0 and odd v_j, so it lies outside
T_(b|c), and likewise outside T_(c|b). Hence(1) gives

    A_b W'_j = −G_bj I_V,    A_c W'_j = −G_cj I_V,
    A_c A_b^(-1) = (G_cj/G_bj) I_V.                           (3)

For every pair, actual common neighbors contain both a same-sign and an
opposite-sign coefficient pair. The two versions of(3) force I_V=−I_V,
impossible in the stated characteristic. No operators were commuted.

If W has fewer than V rows, the first invertible product already excludes
even one proper receiver. If W has more than V rows, A_h is rectangular
and this proof no longer applies. Wider or fragmented appended information
requires separate accounting and a separate theorem.

## 4. At most eight proper receivers; at least96 exceptions

**Lemma 3.** No nine native roots are pairwise nonorthogonal.

Their twice-Gram matrix M would have diagonal2 and off-diagonal±1. Its
rational rank is at most8, since all roots lie in an eight-dimensional
space. Choose a primitive nonzero integer null vector z. Modulo2, M=J+I
in size9; its kernel is zero or the all-ones vector. Primitivity forces
z mod2 nonzero, so all z_i are odd. But modulo4,

    z^T M z = 2 sum_i z_i^2 + 2 sum_(i<j) M_ij z_i z_j
             = 2*9 + 2*(a sum of36 odd integers) = 2 mod4,

contradicting Mz=0. The authentic nonorthogonal eight-set
[0,1,2,4,55,78,82,84] attains the geometric bound. This does not assert
that a single W actually serves all its receivers.

**Corollary 4.** Canonical Gram plus one V-wide appended field can complete
at most eight receivers under the single proper-local-space premise.
An all104 completion must therefore have at least96 receivers whose
information escapes that premise. The result holds for arbitrary operator
decoding at every positive V, not merely scalar coefficients.

This excludes neither a new rank-nine code not containing canonical Gram
nor changed earlier sources. It does not require96 new whole movements:
existing carriers or inexpensive route stops can provide the extra geometry.

Exact checks cover759 frames, all2860 orthogonal pairs,5720 directed
exclusive spans, and both common-neighbor signs for every pair. Exclusive
counts24,26,28,30 occur with directed frequencies56,1344,3840,480. These
finite native witnesses support the proof for all V; random tests or finite
coefficient searches are not substituted for an asymptotic argument.

## 6. A narrow full-route ninth-mode family is over budget

**Conditional movement theorem5.** Consider an all104 completion satisfying
all of the following premises.

1. Its a-projected global information is contained in canonical Gram plus
   one new V-wide field W. Every other proper-chart source history obeys
   the coordinate grammar U0+row(W)+Local(C).
2. Original X/B fields follow monotone line-to-hub paths, and each Y follows
   a monotone ground-to-own-facet path. D fields carry no a-information.
   Besides these raw fields, the only a-bearing carriers are the16 funded
   full modes and the one new mode. The eight coupled-entry rank1 owners
   retain their old short paths and supply no facet input.
3. Each old or new mode carrier visits at most one native facet. A single
   monotone route has this property; a tour with turns need not. Stationary
   caches start with zero a-information and acquire it only from actual
   arriving a-bearing fields. There are no free initialized copies.
4. The new mode's actual route has displacement rank8, so its movement
   costs at least8^t. This is a route premise, not a consequence of the
   mode's algebraic independence. Proper-born or differently acquired new
   modes are outside this price theorem.
5. Seed movement charges are retained, apart from refinements that only
   add nonnegative movement. There are no refunds from removing old Y/D
   pivot stops or changing earlier source histories. Any such refund must
   be explicitly subtracted before drawing the conclusion.

Then this family cannot fit the seed's complete fixed-m MOVE allowance
at t=199/200, even if every other new operation and cleanup service is free.

If a receiver has no other a-bearing arrival at its final facet, all earlier
proper local charts are nested along its monotone path. Their coordinate
contributions fit its last proper chart C_h, and its remaining contributions
lie in the common global space. The terminal state therefore satisfies the
single proper-local-space premise. A stationary facet cache would also need
a previous a-bearing arrival. By Corollary4, at least96 distinct receivers
require another a-bearing facet arrival.

Grant each of the16 old full modes and the new mode one exceptional receiver
for free. This even grants a benefit to old reverse modes whose unchanged
values have zero a-projection. At least96−17=79 receivers still require a
raw X/B arrival. Native facets are incomparable, so one monotone raw route
can visit at most one. Each adds at least delta8. Hence

    additional movement >= 8^t +79 delta8
                        = 9.09989479129914...,

exceeding the limiting room by0.256375828276359... . Directed integer
200th-root arithmetic proves the strict sign. The positive fixed-m penalty
strengthens it for every integer m>=2.

The scope restrictions matter. A rank7 new route with the same79-stop
allowance costs8.114864937374806... optimistically and is not excluded.
Its actual birth, acquisition, paired adjoint, owner return and erasure
still need proof and pricing. A separate fresh reverse field cannot be
declared free; it may consume the remaining room. Reusing an old reverse
history requires a concrete chronology preserving every old duty.

Deleting an old pivot detour1+2 and replacing it by3 refunds
1+2^t−3^t=0.009514532159105... . Exactly27 such refunds cover the displayed
limiting deficit arithmetically before any other new expense;26 do not.
No compatible decoder that obtains these27 refunds is asserted.

With only eight global modes, Theorem1 requires104 exceptions. Even after
granting16 mode facet arrivals, the lower bound88 delta8 is only
1.317372854658465... and fits the room. Thus these results do not exclude
a changed decoder that assembles genuine local information through original
paid routes. They identify the source-access and receiver-geometry theorem
that such a construction would need.

## 7. Verification, negative controls and remaining obligations

`check_geometry.py` reconstructs the finite native facts without producer
imports. It is an author checker, not an independent-agent review. Its
deterministic certificate includes all frame, span and sign witnesses.
Controls delete essential exclusive-basis elements, duplicate a frame
member and replace an opposite-sign witness. Characteristic2 is explicitly
excluded. Prior independent review of minimum-rank rigidity remains a
separate dependency.

Two positive boundary controls prevent a universal impossibility claim.
At full facets, e_h=G_h+(e_h−G_h) is formally available for every h.
Appending one coordinate row a_b gives a rank-nine global space and
formally completes receiver b even at chart0, while all other receivers
use full local facets. These are algebraic availability examples; neither
is claimed to be a legal clean physical word.

`price.py` uses the pinned directed interval engine and full caller. It
retains the narrow negative result and positive scope controls. Floating
values are display only. Scalar primitives, grid coefficients, precision,
owner cleanup, recursive supplier admission, strict compatible BIT/complex
savings and all five foundational assumptions remain visible obligations.

The next construction is a source-realized two-predecessor facet decoder
or a proper-born extra mode with genuine acquisition, paired use and clean
erasure inside the actual budget. A wholly changed rank-nine fitting is
also unexcluded. A new proper chart without new source information does
not remove the proved gate.
