# S011 correction: nonmonotone auxiliary imports break local source support

The complex worker initially proposed extending the fixed104-preload result
to arbitrary auxiliary routes with no further information-flow restriction.
That extension was invalid. It was caught before freezing or programme
admission and explicitly withdrawn in the agent exchange on2026-11-14.

The invalid inference was: since no d_h has a representation supported at
a proper subchart C of F_h in the original r coordinates, every decoder must
have an auxiliary physically visit F_h, even if auxiliaries may move downward
arbitrarily. A downward auxiliary can instead import r-information from
outside C into C. A public D record at C can then have a representation using
initial r_i whose lines are not contained in C. Its own upward route does not
restore the lost source-support invariant. For example, an auxiliary already
holding d_h could import it at a proper C; this example identifies the logical
failure and does not claim cheap physical acquisition of that payload.

The frozen author package preserves two valid, separately scoped statements:

1. If all104 carrier routes are monotone and only local stationary arithmetic
   is permitted, the support invariant follows by induction. All96 final
   facets need auxiliary visits, which eight monotone auxiliaries cannot make.
2. A separate terminal-tour extension permits nonmonotone auxiliary routes
   only while preserving local initial-r support for every proper-chart
   import; nonlocal exports may be consumed at final facets. Under this added
   restriction,96 facet visits imply at least88 incomparable-facet transitions
   and price176. The fixed caller upper allowance is below149.833.

No unrestricted arbitrary-auxiliary176 claim is part of the frozen proof.
Genuine preterminal nonlocal auxiliary imports, including branching copies
into multiple receiver groups, remain a constructive research direction.
Future sessions should not remove the proper-chart hypothesis when citing
the terminal-tour price. The exact affine decoder-space rigidity itself was
unaffected by this correction.
