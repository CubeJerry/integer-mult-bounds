## 1. Complete affine decoder freedom at a final facet

space of scalar constraints on r in (2) is

    A[U,:] r=0.                                           (3)

These eight rows are independent. Every scalar row that decodes d_h from r
is therefore

    f_h=A[h,:]+lambda A[U,:],       lambda in k^8.          (4)

situated at a chart C, its formal
initial-record support can be restricted to those i with L_i contained in C.
Indeed each initial r_i starts at L_i; local linear arithmetic and stationary
copies preserve this property, and nested upward motion preserves it. This
is a statement about a representation in the formal r coordinates; because

In particular a decoder using only the initial records local to F_h must
vanish on every i for which
v_h.v_i=1. The canonical row A[h,:] already vanishes there: these positions

    K_h={lambda : lambda A[U, {i:v_h.v_i=1}]=0}.            (5)

## 2. No nonzero output direction is available at a proper subchart

Suppose d_h had a decoder supported in C properly contained in F_h. That
decoder is also legal at F_h, so (8) applies. Every hosted normal supporting
(8) would have to lie in C, contradicting their rank8 span F_h. Thus d_h has
no local-initial-record decoder at any proper subchart of its final facet.

For volume V, a row of an arbitrary operator-valued decoder differs from the
canonical scalar decoder by an arbitrary linear combination of the eight
constraint blocks A[U,:] tensor I_V. Apply the scalar kernel (5) to every
coefficient matrix entry. Every allowable difference still vanishes on the
hosted input blocks. The hosted blocks of a decoder of d_h are therefore
A[h,j]I_V. More generally a decoder of any nonzero coordinate functional
ell(d_h) has hosted coefficients A[h,j]ell. Every nonzero scalar coefficient
in (8) remains nonzero on that functional. Hence even one nonzero direction
of d_h cannot be represented at a proper subchart of F_h. This proves the
all-volume statement without assuming operators commute or are invertible.

## 3. Precise closed post-observation interface

The lower bound below considers exactly the following deliberately generous
decoder family:

1. Initially there is one actual record r_i at each L_i, as in (2), on104
   full-field carriers. Any assignment to96 public D carriers and eight
   auxiliary carriers is allowed.
2. The96 public D carriers follow nested upward routes to their distinct
   prescribed final facets F_h and must restore the arbitrary original d_h.
   They may mix, scale, copy to stationary records or use general linear
   cochart operations. All scalar/storage costs are granted free for the
   obstruction.
3. At most eight auxiliary carrier trajectories may transport additional
   information. Unlimited stationary zero-born records are allowed. No new
   informative moving trajectory may be initialized elsewhere or substituted
   by a different source. Other X/B/Y banks do not carry D information into
   this post-observation network. This is a closed D-side interface.
4. No actual original d value is granted for free at another chart. All
   decoder information derives from the104 initial r records through the
   stated routes. In the main monotone interface, all eight auxiliary
   routes are also nested upward routes.

The separate terminal-tour extension permits arbitrary auxiliary paths, but
requires that every auxiliary value communicated to a public D field or a
stationary record at a proper preterminal chart C have a representation using
only initial r_i with L_i contained in C. Nonlocal imports may be consumed
at final facets. Equivalently, the proper-chart source-support invariant is
retained before the terminal repair. A sufficient rule is to allow auxiliary
nonlocal exports only at final facets. This restriction is substantive:
arbitrary preterminal imports are not covered by the terminal-tour bound.

This interface grants the entire global preload (2) and permits the proposed
eight zero lanes to be genuine paid moving fields. It excludes covertly

## 4. Every final facet needs an auxiliary visit

Fix h. No other public D carrier can visit F_h: its different rank8 final
facet F_j is incomparable with F_h, so a monotone upward route ending at F_j
cannot pass through F_h. If no auxiliary visits F_h, the only moving record
ever entering that exact chart is the public D carrier that must end there.

Before its first arrival at F_h, that carrier's last chart is some proper
subchart C of F_h. Its value and every stationary record it can leave at F_h
derive from information already carried from C. The earlier source-support
induction gives a representation using only initial r_i with L_i contained
in C. Zero-born local records and arbitrary local linear arithmetic on this
one arriving field cannot produce a nonzero direction of d_h that was not
available in that representation. Section2 therefore makes final restoration
impossible.

Hence every one of the96 distinct F_h must receive an informative auxiliary
visit. A monotone auxiliary can visit at most one rank8 facet. Eight monotone
auxiliaries cannot implement this decoder. The same visit requirement holds
for the separate terminal-tour extension, precisely because its stated
proper-chart source-support invariant preserves the preceding argument.

The argument allows caches left by an auxiliary that visits before the public
D carrier. Such a cache is precisely evidence that the necessary auxiliary
visit occurred; it does not evade the count. A zero auxiliary initialized
locally from the sole public arrival supplies no new information.
