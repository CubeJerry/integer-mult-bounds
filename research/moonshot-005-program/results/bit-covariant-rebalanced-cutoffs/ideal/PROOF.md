# The nine-bank information floor fixes the ideal raw ceiling

The native converse proves q≥9b throughout the stipulated one-way side-information model. In the retained field tariff set b=3,840 and q=9b+h. Keep every other original paid and productive rank of the m-paired caller fixed. The movement coefficient on grade eight becomes 9+h/3840. Setting h=0 maximizes the raw surplus for every fixed even m. This lower return can be used as an upper price bound whether or not the physical network realizes it.

At m=870 the ideal raw crossing lies strictly between 0.00504096857045 and 0.00504096857046. The supremum over finite even m lies strictly between 0.00504325518580 and 0.00504325518581 and is unattained. These bounds apply to this fixed nonreturn tariff and productive inventory.

Write Δ_m(t)=T_m(t)−P_m(t). Both inventories are affine in m. The log derivative of a positive moment is the grade-weighted mean of log grade, nondecreasing in t by its variance. Directed bounds at m=2, m=4 and the coefficient of m separate the worst endpoint derivatives on [0,1] by log 9 or log 13. The m=4 and slope comparisons then give strictly increasing T_m/P_m in t for all even m≥4. At the limiting crossing the slope vanishes and Δ_2<0. Every finite m therefore misses it. Just below that saving the slope is positive and a sufficiently large even m passes.

The least even m passing the exact raw 0.005 comparison depends strongly on h. The verifier checks both neighboring even signs and the affine slope. At h=135 both the initial value and the slope are negative. These are necessary prices. Each source and its ordinary service must still be constructed.
