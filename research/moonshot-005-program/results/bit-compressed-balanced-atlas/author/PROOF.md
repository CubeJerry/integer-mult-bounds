# A literal bank-1920 balanced atlas with smaller finite field programmes

## Results and precise scope

The balanced native route atlas admits an **integral factor-three compression** without changing its normalized movement profile. There are 1,920 field coordinates per actual native role, 120 source coordinates per ordinary donor-plane flag, and eight public lanes per plane-middle flag. Every public owner is an actual original X or Y. No fractional owner, virtual native bank, free copy, or additional route is used.

On this compressed atlas:

1. One causal linear programme over GF(2^27) restores all original X and has all 6,470 foreign proper-transfer blocks invertible.
2. The inherited mixed-69 and late-69 flow witnesses imply one programme over GF(2^28) with all foreign blocks invertible and 23 common identity rows. This uses integral max-flow existence after scaling the old witnesses; it does not claim a new synchronization mechanism.
3. The exact public movement counts are 1,068,240 gap-one moves, 865,560 gap-two moves, 46,080 gap-four moves, and 17,280 canonical gap-eight moves. Dividing by all 1,920 coordinates gives the original balanced profile.
4. The replacement-bank or retained-repair excess limit at this bank is 67 field symbols. A hypothetical physically realized rank-31 repair first passes the finite movement comparison at even m=84. The compression does not itself construct that repaired code.

The normalized movement is unchanged. The advantage is a smaller literal finite source and a smaller ordinary implementation bound. The full native endpoint, a numerical complete coefficient table, admissible recursive costs and the inherited foundations remain open. There is no new kappa.

## 1. The correct integral margins

Use the pinned 111 native roots within the 120-line chart completion, the 1,120 A2 planes, 3,150 middle D4 charts and the retained integer factor u_H. If H has m actual donors, let `x_H=12/m+u_H` and `e_H=max(8,x_H)` as before. The nonempty early flag counts m*x_H are 12, 15 or 18. Thus after division by three, the eight-lane flag has

`h_H=m*x_H/3` early X lanes and `8-h_H` late Y lanes.

These are integral. The empty H has no early lanes. Each actual donor j contributes `15*x_H` early originals and `15*(e_H-x_H)` late visits towards H. Their sum over H is respectively 1,920 and 1,440 or 1,500. An ordinary full source emission has `15*e_H=120` values; a singleton has 180.

Uniformly assigning x_H/3 donor owners to each of the forty-five flags would fail at x_H=4 or 5. That uniformity is unnecessary. At H, list the `15*x_H` original early owners of each actual donor and assign the resulting list to the forty-five columns, each with h_H slots. Both totals equal `15*m*x_H`. This is an explicit integer transportation assignment. The source-to-H movement and H-to-D movement meet at a real chart where arbitrary local recoding is already permitted.

For late input at H, assign `15*(e_H-x_H)` actual native-line visits per donor and `15*(24-m*e_H)` direct ground-to-H originals to the forty-five columns of size `8-h_H`. Their totals agree. All counts are nonnegative integers. The right half is the dual assignment: per native terminal on K, give `15*x_K` late terminal originals, `15*(e_K-x_K)` early terminal visits, and `15*(24-m_K*e_K)` direct early closures from K-perp to Hub.

This changes only which actual owner follows each allowed branch. The total capacity of every edge of the time-expanded chart network is precisely one third of the old capacity. Same-chart early snapshots remain the same allowed operation, with actual ownership paid separately.

## 2. Pairing actual owners through the middle charts

Every D has sixteen incoming H flags of eight lanes. Let `l_D=sum_(H subset D) h_H`. Its left early-X count is l_D and left late-Y count is `128-l_D`. The complementary right flags have late-Y count `l_(D-perp)` and early-X count `128-l_(D-perp)`.

The original reserve inequality divides exactly by three:

`r_D=l_D+l_(D-perp)-128 >= 0`.

Pair right early-X slots with left early-X owners. The remaining r_D genuine X owners close from D to Hub. Pair left late-Y prefixes with right late-Y owners. The remaining r_D genuine Y owners begin at ground and move directly to D. The latter are original Ys assigned by their final native terminal; they are not synthetic public holders.

Lexicographic matching fixes every choice. The generator assigns distinct X labels by their initial native line and distinct Y labels by their final native facet. It materializes 426,240 complete original-owner routes: 213,120 X and the same number of Y. Every one of the 111 source and terminal banks has exactly 1,920 originals. There are exactly 23,040 middle X closures and the same number of Y births.

An X starts at its genuine native line of rank one and ends at Hub of rank eight. A Y starts at ground of rank zero and ends at its genuine native facet of rank seven. Each public original therefore has total rank rise seven. Routes through added labels use them only as rational charts; no added native line or facet bank is introduced.

The uncompressed line-delimited route certificate has SHA256

`82a00840e956cac70b6b2986bedf419684372ffea3f9ad5ad3b08f2241613aec`.

Every route's containment, actual native endpoint, unique original identity, phase capacity and rank-jump sequence is checked. This is a complete finite holder-routing certificate, not merely a count of feasible margins.

## 3. All foreign pair path witnesses remain integral

Fix a native foreign pair j to i. It has sixteen eligible source planes, each with fifteen visible middle exits. Route 120 symbols through each plane. The donor offers `15*e_H>=120` total inputs and `15*x_H>=15*h_H` early inputs. Thus each visible early exit can receive an early input, while extra early input can feed a later exit through a stationary snapshot.

Each of the sixty eligible D charts sees four such source planes and four complementary target planes. Every nonempty h_H is at least four and every complementary early-X capacity `8-h_K` is at most four. Hence the four incoming branches have enough early values for all required early outputs. Match those first, then biject the remaining values to late outputs. These are integral counts in units of one field record.

At each right K, the 120 arriving values contain `15*(8-h_K)` early values. Its actual native terminal offers `15*x_K` late entries and `15*e_K` total entries. Since `h_K<=x_K`, the number of early terminal entries needed for 120 outputs is no greater than the number that arrived early. A same-chart snapshot permits an extra early value to feed a later terminal Y.

This gives 1,920 edge-disjoint paths in the permitted two-wave graph, with no return from late to early. The checker verifies every native incidence and capacity inequality used by this construction for all 6,470 directed foreign pairs.

## 4. One simultaneous finite-field code

Put independent scalar coefficients on every allowed local linear operation. The pure early and pure late maps are square on all `111*1920` public owners. Untouched original routes give an identity specialization for each. Every foreign transfer determinant has its own path specialization from the preceding section, so it too is a nonzero polynomial.

Multiply the 6,470 foreign determinants and the two pure-wave determinants. A path has degree at most ten in the same convention as the retained balanced proof. The total degree is at most

`10*(6470+2*111)*1920 = 128,486,400 < 2^27`.

The finite-grid lemma supplies a common assignment over GF(2^27). The checker provides an explicit irreducible field modulus `0x8000027`, namely `t^27+t^5+t^2+t+1`. Exact Rabin tests check irreducibility. Exhaustive coefficient selection in a fixed order followed by exact elimination specifies a finite code; a numerical assignment of all chart coefficients is not claimed.

Invertibility of the global early and late maps forces their square local phase blocks to be invertible. Apply the early inverse at Hub to restore every original X before any Y leaves ground. Form and move the actual canonical bank. Apply the pure-late inverse at ground to the arbitrary original Y bank after its canonical preload. The endpoint is the same retained rank-only endpoint, with every foreign B_ij invertible. It is not the fully synchronized native sum.
