## 1. Exact simultaneous interpolation criterion

Let A,B be n-by-m matrices over a field, with the same domain coordinates.
There exists G in GL(n) such that GA=B if and only if ker A=ker B.

Necessity follows from injectivity of G. For sufficiency define
f(Ax)=Bx on im A. Equal kernels make this a well-defined linear isomorphism
im A -> im B. Extend a basis of im A and its image basis to full bases of
the ambient n-space. Sending the former full basis to the latter gives G.

If columns of C must remain pointwise fixed while L must map to U, apply
the criterion to A=[C,L] and B=[C,U]. Equal ranks of A and B alone are
insufficient: their full relation spaces must agree. Basis completion gives
an actual invertible G. The condition concerns complete parameterized
linear frames, not arbitrary nonlinear coordinate maps.
