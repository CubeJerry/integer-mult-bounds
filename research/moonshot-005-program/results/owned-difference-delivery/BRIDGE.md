## Exact observation and retirement premises

Let Delta be copy two minus copy one of the selected native, with independent
main inputs and the same arbitrary entering helpers. At each pinned MOVE event,
its certificate proves a CURRENT destination-cochart identity
`Delta_owned = sum_j a_j Delta_observer_j`. There are 32 virtual observer terms:
4 main, 25 helper and 3 owned. Absolute coefficients are at most 33, denominators
at most 16. A single physical common-coded helper does not supply its difference.

For example, before event 33272, `Delta_1520 = -Delta_749`, while the ordinary
source values satisfy `value_1520 + value_749 = entering_helper_749`. Setting
that arbitrary entering helper to a nonzero complete array and all other inputs
to zero disproves replacement of the common owned value by minus its physical
helper. The difference identity holds because the common dirty helper cancels.
Every certificate witness has a checked pure entering-helper residual.

A physical replacement requires a finite expression `Delta_r = sum_l b_l o_l`
in K nonzero ACTUALLY LIVE destination-chart B observations. Their versions,
physical owners, coefficient services and complete acquisition/transport costs
must be supplied. Their owners are distinct from the old difference owner r,
and all observations remain unchanged throughout the macro. The old r is at A.
Under the retained owned-erasure and zero-initialization services:

1. CLEAR r at A, ending its old owned lifetime.
2. INIT the reused physical slot r as zero at B, beginning a new lifetime.
3. ADD r += b_l o_l for each nonzero term, at B.

The result is exactly the normalized row and chart of the removed DIFFERENCE
MOVE. The original COMMON owned MOVE remains. No source observer aliases r;
its old retirement therefore cannot invalidate the reconstruction. Induction
through all ten occurrences gives the same later virtual rows/charts, provided
all current physical observation and lifecycle premises hold. This universal
linear statement for every allowed complete-array size does not construct those
physical observations. If the allocator needs a new slot instead of reused r,
its labels, history, backing and fees must be added.

| Source-level quantity | Change for ten replacements |
|---|---:|
| Difference Gaussian MOVEs | minus 10: rank 1 six times; ranks 2,3,4,6 once each |
| ADD | plus sum K_i, after flattening actual physical observations |
| INIT / CLEAR | plus 10 of each |

| Physical history, backing, control, precision | recompute with their real tariffs |

Only if all 32 virtual differences already exist as literal distinct cochart
fields is sum K_i=32. This is not the runtime ADD count of an unbuilt shared bank.
Flattened decoders can be larger and need fresh coefficient/precision bounds.
CLEAR, INIT, source-control motion and erasure are not free physical services;
any additional recursive calls they cause belong in the added paid profile.
For a literal synchronized two-owned-bank baseline, the original 51-label,
54-lifetime, peak 43 allocation gives 102 labels, 108 lifetimes, peak 86. These ten
replacements give 118 lifetimes and the same peak 86 upper bound before decoder
sidecars. An active-peak bound is not a historical-backing or compiler proof.

If a changed-main scheme represents a virtual owned difference only by decoders,
there may be no literal old difference field. Updating its representation may
need no MOVE at that event, but the actual physical ledger must be counted from
that scheme. Do not subtract jobs from a profile that never included them.
