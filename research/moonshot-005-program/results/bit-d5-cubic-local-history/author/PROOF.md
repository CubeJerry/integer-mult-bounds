## 3. A reversible block carrier identity

Every z in Z_D has z_1=z_18, because those coordinates are the two signs of edge34.

This persists with arbitrary V-by-V matrix coefficients. Let Z be any V-by-111V block row formed from the five scalar basis words with arbitrary matrix coefficients. Then Z_1=Z_18. Define U:k^V to k^{111V} to place the same vector in original slots1 and18 and zero elsewhere. In characteristic two,
\[
 ZU=Z_1+Z_18=0.
\]
Therefore
\[
 T=I+UZ,\qquad T^2=I,\qquad ZT=Z.
\]
Applying T adds the unchanged mode Zx to both original carriers and leaves every other original unchanged. A form containing both carriers with the same coefficient is unchanged. A form containing exactly one receives Zx.

The identities require no commutativity among the matrix coefficients. A stationary acquisition of Zx before the two updates makes the simultaneous algebra compatible with a sequential implementation. Restoration at Hub is possible by the same involution, since ZT=Z.

This statement does not declare acquisition or restoration free. Every original in supp(Z) must actually reach a chart where the mode is assembled; source-path constraints, genuine retained point copies, local additions, initialization and retirement remain charged. Moving an unserved original to D also constrains its future acquisition paths. The literal word and its invoice are separate evidence supplied by Worker A.

## 4. Stability of the canonical rows outside D

For each receiver i, let
\[
 N_i^{out}=\{j\notin D:j\ne i,\ r_j\cdot r_i\ne0\},
 \qquad
 O_i^{out}=\{j\notin D:j\ne i,\ r_j\cdot r_i=0\}.
\]
The exact source check proves
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(C|_{N_i^{out}})=8
 \quad\text{for all111 receivers}.                         \tag{1}
\]
The kernel of restriction is therefore the line spanned by K_i, since K_i vanishes on the noncontacts.

Suppose a block fitting A has row space contained in
\[
 (C\otimes k^V)+(Z_D\otimes k^V).                         \tag{2}
\]
For each receiver, write its V-row block as a canonical part plus H_i from Z_D tensor k^V. Since H_i is zero outside D, the prescribed zeros on N_i^{out}, together with(1), imply
\[
 A_{ij}=K_{ij}B_i+H_{ij}
\]
for some V-by-V matrix B_i.

If i is outside D, H_ii=0, so the own block gives B_i=I. If i is in D, the remaining noncontact zeros require H_i to be supported on {i} union the real contacts of i inside D. Such a codeword in Z_D is zero. Indeed, if i belongs to edge{a,b}, orthogonal D5 root lines consist of the opposite sign on that same edge and both signs on edges disjoint from{a,b}. Its only nonzero Eulerian word is the odd triangle, excluded by the even-edge condition. The same conclusion is independently verified as rank5 of the restricted five-word basis for all eighteen native i. Hence H_i=0 and B_i=I in this case too.

Thus every fitting satisfying(2) retains the exact blocks

\]
at every receiver. This proof applies entrywise to arbitrary block coefficients. It does not assume that C tensor k^V is actually contained in row(A), only the containment(2).
