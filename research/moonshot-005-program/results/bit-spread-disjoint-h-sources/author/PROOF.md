## Why 24 disjoint charts in this class are impossible

Write a for the quadratic vector of receiver 80. An eligible chart
has three native roots, omits donor 10 and has no native view from
receiver 80. On any such binary plane the polar functional against a
is nonzero. Its value equals one on exactly two of the three nonzero
vectors, including the case that a belongs to the plane. Direct
enumeration gives 47 eligible native root IDs with that value after
omitting donor 10. Each eligible donor-disjoint chart uses two of
the 47. Hence their count is at most floor(47/2)=23. The emitted
disjoint family attains the upper bound and the verifier checks the
two-root property on all 540 eligible charts. This ceiling uses the
disjointness and identity-target restrictions. It gives no ceiling
on charts sharing a donor or permitting perturbed target blocks.
