## Review of the universal linear-information argument

Fix a legal field dimension V. On the independent input-difference variables,
every granted initial or routed observation lies in the coordinate space
generated by its reachable input coordinates, tensor the V-coordinate field
space. Arbitrary local linear coding, retention, and waiting preserve this
inclusion. Giving every extra message globally from time zero enlarges the
allowed knowledge and therefore is a valid relaxation, even when actual
messages causally depend on earlier messages.

The four requested rows restricted to the common-unreachable coordinates have
rank 4V. Their base contribution at the four deadlines is zero, and the old
entry preloads also restrict to zero. Thus fewer than four global V-field
messages cannot span all four observations.

For the stronger claim, let E be the span of at most three extra paid fields
and H the span of the sole informative nonquiet helper field, all restricted
to the unreachable coordinates and granted globally. If these suffice, their
sum contains the 4V-dimensional target space while having dimension at most
4V; equality follows. In particular both E and H lie in the target space.
At its sender, each row h of H is b+e for a granted base row b and e in E.
Since h and e are target rows, b belongs to the intersection of the sender
base space with the target space. The independently checked intersection is
zero, also after tensoring by the V-coordinate identity. Hence H is contained
in E, contradicting rank 4V. This establishes the stated all-V conclusion
under the precise linear model, not by extrapolating a finite search.

The argument requires the prescribed independent q input domain and excludes
new unpriced cross-chart channels. General nonlinear coding, an altered
intermediate read contract, or changed clock/geometry is not evaluated here.
