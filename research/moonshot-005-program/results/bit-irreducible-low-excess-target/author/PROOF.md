# An irreducible native fitting inside the paid return-rank screen

**Explicit algebraic target. Physical source and decoder realization remain open. No new kappa.**

For every characteristic-two field F and every integer w>=2, put b=60w.
On the authenticated native 111-role foreign-contact mask, there is an
explicit binary-coefficient proper transfer B such that:

1. Every one of its 6,470 foreign b-by-b blocks is invertible; all other
   blocks, including the diagonal, are zero.
2. `rank_F(I+B)=9b+2w`. Its excess return rank and its retained-canonical
   repair rank are both `2w=b/30`.
3. The native transport representation is irreducible: if subspaces
   W_i<=F^b satisfy `B_ij W_j subset W_i` on every foreign ordered pair,
   then every W_i is zero or every W_i is F^b.
4. It has no positive-dimensional flat input subsystem and no
   positive-dimensional flat output quotient, even with independently
   chosen vertex embeddings or quotient maps.

At b=480,1920,5760 the excesses are respectively16,64,192. All have the
same normalized movement screen, with first successful even retained
paired multiplicity870. This is an endpoint target, not a claimed physical
code or a new multiplication bound.

The predecessor one-star target kills flat input subsystems but has
b-ceil(b/62) common left rows. The older two-receiver188 target explicitly
retains5,572 common left rows at b5760. The new conclusion is irreducibility

## 1. Native support and two separated stars

Let O be the111-by111 binary native orthogonality matrix and K=I+O.
Use a=80 as a receiver and d=10 as a donor. The exact root reconstruction
verifies the following source facts:

- rank K=9; the native contact graph is connected.
- a and d are distinct and nonforeign, with degrees62 and61.
- Restricting K to columns outside `N(a) union {d}` has rank9.
- Restricting K to columns outside `{a} union N(d)` has rank9.

Consequently the canonical row code has no nonzero vector supported on
`N(a) union {d}`, and the canonical column code has no nonzero vector
supported on `{a} union N(d)`. Symmetry of K gives the second assertion.
These statements persist after tensoring with I_b and after any field
extension of F2. They are authenticated finite geometry facts; no graph
is supplied in place of the native root table.

## 2. An explicit collection generating the full matrix algebra

Split F^b into60 labelled w-dimensional coordinate banks, with bank0
called U. Write u_0,...,u_(w-1) for its standard basis. Let J and E be
b-by-b matrices supported on U:

    J = sum_(k=0)^(w-2) e_(u_k) e_(u_(k+1))^T,
    E = e_(u_(w-1)) e_(u_0)^T.

For each bank l=1,...,59 put

    T_l = sum_(k=0)^(w-1) e_(u_k) e_(l*w+k)^T.

There are61 matrices in the ordered list `(J,E,T_1,...,T_59)`.
J is nilpotent of index w. E and every T_l are square-zero because w>=2
and the source and target banks are distinct. Thus I+N and I+N^T are
invertible for every listed N, with explicit finite geometric-sum inverses.
All coefficients are zero or one.

**Full-algebra lemma.** The unital F-algebra generated by all N and N^T
in this list is M_b(F).

For0<=r,s<w,

    J^(w-1-r) E J^s = e_(u_r) e_(u_s)^T.             (1)

Thus every matrix unit inside U belongs to the algebra. Multiplying these
units by T_l on the right gives every `e_(u_r)e_(l*w+s)^T`; multiplying by
T_l^T on the left gives every `e_(l*w+r)e_(u_s)^T`. Their products through
any one U coordinate give every matrix unit between arbitrary banks.
These are all b^2 matrix units, so the algebra is the full matrix algebra.
This is a written all-width identity, not an extrapolation from closure
computations at small widths.

## 3. The native block assignment

Order the62 neighbors of a and assign the61 matrices in the above list
to its first61 neighbors. At its final neighbor assign zero. Order the61
neighbors of d and assign the same list to all of them. Define

    B_(a,j) = I_b + N_j       for j in N(a),
    B_(i,d) = I_b + M_i^T     for i in N(d),

where N_j is the first assignment, and M_i the second. Put B_ij=I_b at
every other foreign ordered pair, and zero at all nonforeign pairs.
Since a and d are nonforeign, the two altered sets of blocks are disjoint.
Every native edge retains at least one identity direction. No receiver
or donor has been removed, and no original input is fixed.

Each modified block is invertible by section2. The unmodified foreign
blocks are identities. Because `N=(I+N)+I`, the unital algebra generated
by all native foreign blocks contains every generator in section2 and
therefore equals M_b(F).

## 4. Exact fitting and retained repair ranks

Write `Delta=B-O tensor I_b`. The row-star part has image U in receiver a
and rank w; one T_l is already surjective onto U. Its row space is supported
on N(a). The column-star part has row space U^T in donor d and rank w;
one T_l^T is injective on U. Its column space is supported on N(d).

Because d is not in N(a), the two row spaces have disjoint coordinate
supports. Because a is not in N(d), the two column spaces likewise have
disjoint supports. Thus `rank Delta=2w`. Its full row support is inside
`N(a) union {d}` and its full column support inside `{a} union N(d)`.
Section1 therefore gives

    row(Delta) intersect row(K tensor I_b) = {0},
    col(Delta) intersect col(K tensor I_b) = {0}.

Use the retained rank-additivity lemma: for M and a rank-r update UV with
both row and column separation, `rank(M+UV)=rank M+r`. A short proof is
that row separation makes V surjective on ker M. Thus im(M+UV) contains
im U, and consequently contains im M as well; column separation makes
their sum direct. Applying it here yields

    rank(I+B)=9b+2w,
    rank([K tensor I_b; I+B])-9b=2w.                 (2)

The same actual excess applies to either of the already proved paid
return-bank interfaces. This does not supply their missing physical B.

## 5. Irreducibility, including vertex-dependent subspaces

Suppose subspaces W_i satisfy the transport inclusions in the theorem.
Every foreign B_ij is invertible and both edge directions exist. Hence
adjacent W_i and W_j have equal dimensions. Native connectivity makes
that dimension the same everywhere.

At least one edge direction is an identity. Its inclusion, together with
the equal dimensions, gives W_i=W_j. Connectivity therefore makes all
spaces equal to one subspace W of F^b. Every foreign block preserves W,
so the full matrix algebra from section2 preserves W. A nonzero vector
in a full-matrix-algebra-invariant space can be sent to every basis vector.
Therefore W is zero or F^b. This proves the claimed irreducibility.

This is irreducibility of the finite internal b-coordinate transport
representation. It is not a claim about an implementation with additional
owned registers. Tensoring the finite word with a positive body identity
repeats this module; that repeated representation is generally reducible.
The no-flat conclusions below do persist under such tensoring.

## 6. Flat input and output maps are excluded directly

A flat input subsystem consists of injective maps Z_i:F^s->F^b with
`B_ij Z_j=Z_i` on all foreign edges. Every reverse block B_(j,a) is an
identity, so Z_j=Z_a for j in N(a). The forward equations give
`N_j Z_a=0` for all row-star generators.

The common kernel of these generators is zero: T_1,...,T_59 annihilate
only vectors in U, and ker J intersect ker E is zero inside U. Thus Z_a=0
and s=0. This argument allows arbitrary local embeddings; it does not
assume one literal common coordinate subspace.

Dually, a flat output quotient consists of surjective P_i:F^b->F^s with
`P_i B_ij=P_j`. Every reverse block B_(d,i) is an identity, so P_i=P_d
for i in N(d). Then `P_d M_i^T=0` for all column-star generators. The
span of the images of all M_i^T is F^b, being the transpose statement of
the zero common kernel. Hence P_d=0 and s=0. Literal common left rows are
a special case, so there are none.

After tensoring every block by a body identity, the common-kernel and
joint-image statements tensor in the same way. No positive-dimensional
flat input or output module is introduced by this replication. This is
separate from the finite-bank irreducibility qualification above.

## 8. Verification, limitations and next question

The author checker reconstructs all111 roots' contact mask, checks the
native restriction ranks and connectivity, builds the complete binary
fitting at several finite widths, and checks every altered foreign block.
It verifies the full-matrix-unit identity at small w and full-width selector
coverage symbolically. Controls distinguish a one-sided star, omitted
coupling, a nonnilpotent diagonal mutation, and an invalid center pair.
Exact source bytes and the inherited budget are pinned.

The new constructive question is to realize this particular irreducible
map through the actual source, H, D, K and F operations. A successful code
must mix the independently separated source classes somewhere in its
actual transfer implementation. Generic invertibility alone does not imply
this endpoint, and the local algebra does not settle any native causal cut.
The source and decoder construction, uniform compiler and precision,
ownership/erasure, both strict BIT/complex suppliers, full recursive caller,
and all five foundational assumptions remain open.
