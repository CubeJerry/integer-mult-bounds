# Independent review of the paid four- and seven-source native words

## Decision

Accept the four-source F4 word and its stated foreign-subsum theorem.
The independent replay agrees on all 32 original binary columns, all
original final charts, every owned helper lifetime, all strict chart
inclusions and the complete invoice: 134 ADD, 48 INIT, 48 dirty CLEAR,
80 MOVE with profile 24[1]+24[5]+32[7], peak 26 owned binary records.

The same independently implemented replay also accepts the seven-source
F8 word on all 210 original binary columns: 3933 ADD, 441 INIT, 441 dirty
CLEAR, 651 MOVE with profile 126[1]+315[5]+210[7], peak 129 owned records.
For the seven-source word this is a second implementation check by its
author; the root agent performs the separate peer mathematical review.

The later shared-D4 version also passes the independent replay on all
210 columns: 4684 ADD, 567 INIT, 567 dirty CLEAR, 966 MOVE with profile
126[1]+189[2]+441[3]+210[7], peak 132 owned records. Its new D4 moves are
checked by actual rational rank-four span inclusion, not label types.

## Independence

The review checker imports neither compiler nor author replay. It uses
sets of original input-column indices, rather than the authors' packed
integer linear rows. It constructs exact rational bases for source lines,
A2 planes, receiver facets, ground and Hub. Every MOVE is checked by strict
rank increase and equality of the target rank with the rank of the union
of both actual bases. It does not accept a move merely because its labels
look like a permitted pair of chart types.

Owned records must be fresh at INIT, begin zero, be live and co-located
for every ADD, and be cleared exactly once. Original X and Y records
cannot be cleared. Final X equals original X at Hub; final Y equals its
own arbitrary baseline plus precisely the requested foreign sources at
the actual native facet. Thus the result is not a zero-Y-only identity.

The checker detects removal of a receiver ADD, removal of a paid source
MOVE, and addition of an independent Y baseline bit to another baseline.
Each negative control fails for a concrete semantic or locality reason.
Output JSON pins the word, author result and native layout hashes.

## Scope accepted

These words explicitly pay private plane-to-facet deliveries and upward
owned helper moves. They are valid fragments in the retained paid
nested-owned model. They do not furnish the full balanced atlas, do not
prove a free common plane broadcast, do not add a new complete kappa, and
do not satisfy a stricter descending-only helper restriction.

The four-source quotient proof is sound: at receiver i, both donors on a
visible pair plane use the same remaining line as a lift, while their
source kernels are different. The two visible query forms at each donor
are independent and their decoded contributions give the identity. The
seven-source proof replaces this two-dimensional argument by an explicit
five-dimensional Vandermonde dual basis, with no change in the restoration
or input-baseline obligations.

The review is against the exact hashes recorded in four/RESULT.json,
seven/RESULT.json and seven-shared/RESULT.json. Any later compiler or word
change needs a fresh replay.
