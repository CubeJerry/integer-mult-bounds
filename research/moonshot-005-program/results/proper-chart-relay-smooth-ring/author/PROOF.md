## 2. Twenty-output continuation remains impossible

Let M20 be the terminal400-input public map of the pinned20-output word, with every old owner retired. Suppose a subsequent exact finite computation uses only coefficients in S[i] and fresh zero or independent auxiliary inputs, and has no separate correlated old-input record. It cannot restore all96 original D inputs for arbitrary original data.

Indeed, the frozen independent audit gives public rank399 modulo7 and398 modulo127. Neither7 nor127 is inverted in S. Map S[i] into F_(p²), extending the canonical residue map and assigning i a root of X²+1. The explicit nonzero kernels of M20 remain kernels over that extension field and contain nonzero D components. Any later S[i]-linear operation continues to annihilate those kernels. Exact original-D identity output would not annihilate them, a contradiction.

At field width V the127-kernel has dimension2V. Therefore any augmented starting state permitting exact recovery needs at least2V additional scalar coordinates containing old-input information. Independent helpers do not alter the bound: extend the kernel direction by zero on their independent inputs. Correlated helper records would be additional retained information and must be accounted for as such.

The lower bound is sharp for global arithmetic row-module recovery. The two genuine ground Gram records q1 and q4 from the frozen audit make the terminal D matrix full rank at3,7 and127. In particular they eliminate every prime not inverted in S that divides its determinant

Consequently the augmented S-row-module is S^96. As in the dyadic companion, this is only an existence statement for a global scalar decoder. It does not place the records at needed cocharts, close their lifetimes, or pay their movement and precision costs.

An additional distinction is useful: the seven old relay-bank rows do repair the terminal20 matrix modulo7 and127, hence suffice arithmetically over S if retained. Their failure modulo3 in the dyadic theorem no longer matters after3 is inverted. ## 3. Forty-four-output boundary control

For the44-output word the independently reconstructed remaining D determinant is

480,000 = 2⁸·3·5⁴,

which is a unit in S. The exact square D matrix therefore has an S-linear inverse, by its adjugate divided by this unit. Thus the terminal public map is abstractly invertible over S (and S[i]) with no additional retained record. A mod3 or mod5 kernel cannot obstruct S-linear continuation because the relevant residue maps from S do not exist.

This does not provide a physical cochart decoder, bounded-scalar implementation or precision theorem.
