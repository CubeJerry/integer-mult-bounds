## What the argument establishes

contribution in every characteristic-two field and every positive internal
bank width. It applies to arbitrary linear use of the retained canonical
row code: injectivity on the columns outside a receiver's proper facet
forces that receiver's canonical row to be exactly its native K row.
The remaining proper-path matrix must therefore be O tensor identity.
This argument does not grant new physical access to packets or apply to
packets which have acquired noncanonical information.

For endpoint gauges the spanning-tree construction is valid without
commutativity. In the factorization B_ij=R_i C_j the order of the inverse
factors is forced. Every selected four-cycle has three earlier edges and
one new edge, first over the core donors and then over the other donors.
Consequently the 6,249 equations are sufficient as well as necessary.
The common left-fixed space of the normalized blocks gives the exact
partial-coordinate optimum: propagation along tree edges determines all
receiver and donor row maps from the root row map. Conversely a basis of
that common space extends to an invertible whole-bank gauge. Field
enlargement does not change this obstruction for a fixed matrix assignment.

The advertised 5,760-dimensional companion-matrix example is sound.
Neither t^2+t+1 nor t^3+t+1 has zero or one as a root in characteristic two;
both the companion and its difference from identity are invertible over
every characteristic-two extension. Its 69-dimensional fixed space is
therefore exact. The example is expressly algebraic; no physical balanced
word realizing it is asserted. A better choice of interior coefficients
remains open.

There is a positive corollary for the existing 69-row code. One may align
every proper block at a chosen receiver by an invertible source gauge,
