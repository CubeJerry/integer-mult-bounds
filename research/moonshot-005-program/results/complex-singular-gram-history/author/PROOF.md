## 1. Source and universal notation

Use the pinned104-port source and its400 private quotient fields. The96
hosted ports form H; the eight coupled ports form U. For any positive whole
field volume V and arbitrary independent vector inputs over a characteristic
zero field, set

    a_h=x_h                   (h in H),
    a_u=x_u-y_u               (u in U),
    c_h=1 (h in H), c_u=1/2 (u in U).

The retained eight genuine rank-one coupled entry transfers establish these
values before any altered source work. They remain charged.

The binary initial X/B line is L_h=<v_h>; the final Y/D facet is
F_h=v_h^perp, dimension8. The raw X/B routes ascend to Hub, dimension9;
Y/D start at chart0 and ascend to their facets. Each v_h is odd, so its own
line is not in its own facet. The normalized real Gram matrix G has diagonal1,
rank8, and G^2=13G. Distinct real-nonorthogonal roots are precisely the native
source lines contained in one another's facets.

Let z_0,...,z_7 be any orthogonal real root basis, with squared norm18,
whose binary lines are genuine available charts. Put

    g_i(h)=r(z_i).r(h)/18,
    G_hj=sum_i g_i(h)g_i(j).

Let I be the set of actual native ports whose binary normal equals one of
these z_i; write s=|I|, u=|I intersect U|. If z_i=v_b is actual, then

    g_i(b)=1, and g_j(b)=0 for j!=i.                         (1)

This fact is decisive: holding all X_b, b in I, at their original lines does
not withhold any contribution from a different basis mode.

For every other port, require its nonzero mode support to have size4,
span a binary C3 lying properly in its final facet, and form one of14 classes.
In each class require the four rows restricted to its actual hosted ports to
have rank4. Choose actual hosted pivot columns. Replacing those identity rows
by the four Gram rows gives an invertible D encoder M_D. The Y encoder M_Y
uses c_h g_i(h), keeping hosted rows first and coupled rows last. It is
invertible with the same hosted diagonal block. The exact emitted inverses
must have admitted scalar coefficients; both instances here use dyadics only.
These are explicit hypotheses, not consequences of an arbitrary orthogonal
basis. Both literal instances satisfy them.

## 2. Original D acquisition and the paired native self contribution

For every class outside I, use the retained reciprocal codec mechanism:
encode the original D bank by M_D at0; send the four pivots through their
mode lines; add each pivot to the stationary zero-initialized R_i there;
collect the whole bank at its C3; apply M_D^{-1}; and finally send every
restored original D to its own facet. This gives the actual records

    R_i=sum_(h in H minus I) g_i(h)d_h                       (2)

at L_i. Each D is restored before it departs the C3. No original D at a basis
port is silently included in (2).

For each hosted basis port b with z_i=v_b, its B_b still waits at L_b and
its original D_b still waits at0. Create an owned q=d_b from that actual D;
move q from0 to L_b, a paid rank-one ascent; perform B_b-=q; and retire q at
L_b. Send D_b directly0->F_b. This preserves D_b and costs one extra genuine
moving lifetime. Its endpoint is L_b, not its birth chart. The inherited
owned-erasure premise permits its nonzero retirement; no home-return or clean
uncomputation is claimed.

Move every B to Hub. Move every nonbasis X to Hub; the basis X remain at
their original lines. Return each actual R_i along L_i->Hub, rank8, and
apply the old consumers B_j-=g_i(j)R_i for every hosted j. Retire the R_i.
The resulting reverse map is

    B_j=b_j-1_(j in H intersect I)d_j
             -sum_(h in H minus I)G_hj d_h.                 (3)

All reverse coefficients therefore have a real chronological source. The
extra self delivery is required even though the algebraic transpose formula
is obvious.

## 3. Actual singular capture and preserved full Gram value

For every mode i, all nonbasis X inputs are now restored at Hub. Initialize
one forward owner P_i there and form the actual partial sum

    p_i=sum_(h not in I)g_i(h)a_h.                           (4)

Move it Hub->L_i, rank8. For an omitted basis root, (1) shows that (4) is
already the full old Gram value

    f_i=sum_h g_i(h)a_h,

so leave P_i at L_i until its final old decoder read.

For an actual basis root b, X_b still contains its original a_b at L_b=L_i.
Execute the following cocharted operations in this order:

    X_b += P_i;                      X_b=a_b+p_i=f_i
    Z_i := X_b;                      actual zero-born stationary cache
    P_i -= X_b;                      P_i=-a_b.              (5)

The two mutable-register operations have matrix

    [[1,1],[-1,0]]

on (X_b,P_i), determinant1 and packet-through block0. The bare identity is
standard. The source-specific result is that the full displaced duty f_i is
copied from the actual updated X_b, at the exact line all its remaining old
consumers can visit. It is neither a free row read from another chart nor the
partial sum p_i copied before it has acquired a_b.

Move X_b along its original remaining L_b->Hub route. At Hub subtract the
same actual nonbasis sum (4), using the already restored nonbasis X. This
restores X_b=a_b exactly. No future basis X or original y value is read during
this restoration. In particular, there is no requirement that all X reach
Hub before any mode is formed. Formula (1) resolves that chronological cycle.

Move P_i from L_b to0, a real extra rank-one descent. At0, Y_b still has its
original y_b; perform

    Y_b -= c_b P_i = y_b+c_b a_b.                            (6)

Retire P_i at0 and move Y_b directly to F_b. The stationary Z_i still holds
f_i at L_i. It remains until every old nonbasis consumer has read it.

This accounts for the final location of every altered object: restored X_b
at Hub, completed Y_b at F_b, new payload P_i retired at0, and old-duty cache
Z_i retired at L_i after its real last read. The forward owner does not return
to Hub. The cache and packet retirements are generally nonzero. Their
conditional admissibility is inherited owned erasure, not a new cleanup
lemma.

## 4. Old nonbasis decoders and the exact paired endpoint

For each nonbasis class, encode its actual Y bank at0 by M_Y^{-T}. Each
pivot travels through its mode line and adds the genuine f_i there. Use Z_i
for a captured native mode, and the original P_i for an omitted mode. Move
all fields to C3 and decode by M_Y^T, then send each Y to its own facet.
The inverse-transpose identity gives

    Y_h=y_h+c_h(Ga)_h       (h not in I),
    Y_b=y_b+c_b a_b         (b in I).                         (7)

The old modes needed for (7) are all physically present together at their
respective lines before these codecs begin. Every unknown original y survives.
Let L agree with G outside rows I and have row e_b on each b in I. Combining
(3) and (7) gives the full map

    X=a,   Y=y+CLa,   B=b-L_HH^T d,   D=d.                   (8)

Thus R+L_HH^T=0 exactly. It is important that replacing row b of L does not
by itself complete the entire B_b column: B_b still contains contributions
from nonbasis D. The correct claim is the residual-rank drop proved below.

The map on all400 public inputs is invertible: recover a from X and d from
D; recover y=Y-CLa; recover hosted x=a and coupled x=a+y; recover
b=B+L_HH^T d. This algebraic inverse proves public information retention.
It does not supply physical owned erasure or an endpoint-restoring inverse
word.

All formulas are vector-linear and valid coordinatewise for every positive

    U0 + span{a_b:b in I}.                                 (9)
