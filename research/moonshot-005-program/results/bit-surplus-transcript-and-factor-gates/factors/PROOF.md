# Redundant physical transcripts with minimum input and output factor ranks

**Theorem.** Keep any of the three S021 source cuts and its original linear whole-record model. Allow an arbitrary finite number N of future downset descent events. Form the chronological fitting K=A C on the 95 remaining roles, where C stacks the full projected input dependencies of the N events and A is their output response in the network with all those events cut. Assume

    rank K = rank A = rank C = 9bV.

Then the same five-layer lower bill of b·42.96457333333333… holds, including arbitrary numbers of redundant, unused, dependent or partially informative records. Therefore no target-feasible physical continuation keeps all three factor ranks at this minimum, regardless of N.

Indeed row C = row K and col A = col K by inclusion and dimension. The induced-rigidity theorem classifies both as gauged canonical spaces. If A_e has a nonzero column, the record's original destination B is contained in each terminal F_i where that column responds: after opening all downset events, downward re-entry into any terminal facet is a fresh cut. The response column is canonical and its role normals span at least seven real dimensions. Hence B has rank ≤1. Events ending higher have zero direct output response in this open network. They can still affect later payloads in the actual network.

Unlike the exactly-9b proof, individual low events need not innovate. For any downset event starting at rank ≤5 inside D_U, its expansion after cutting all downset descents has a local initial term supported in S(B) of its origin and terms from strictly earlier downset cuts. The local term is the difference of canonical rows, since the entire row space of C is canonical. Its support rank ≤5 forces it to vanish. Moreover every earlier downset event making an actual contribution through this open network ended in a subspace of B: all remaining paths reaching B are local and upward. Thus only earlier *low-landing* downset events can occur in that expansion.

Fix r=1,…,5. To reconstruct the row space of all downset payloads ending at rank ≤r, retain the actual downward crossings of boundary r and the local residuals L_j of the upward D_U-to-exterior inlets ending at rank ≤r. Each L_j is supported on its origin native role set. Chronologically, an event crossing r is already retained. A downset event with low inside origin has no fresh local term by the preceding paragraph. A downset event with low exterior origin can obtain projected data only from earlier low-landing downset events, actual crossings of r, or cut inlets, since no initial U-sensitive low exterior field exists. An inlet's downset-side expansion uses its local L_j and earlier low-landing downset values. This proves the reconstruction by induction. An inactive high-landing event can assist later only through one of these physical channels.

All downset events with nonzero direct decoder response end at rank ≤1, hence lie in the reconstructed low-landing family. Their row span contains row K through the decomposition K=sum_e A_e C_e. In particular a canonical space of dimension 9bV is contained in the sum of the C_r crossing blocks and the inlet residuals. Quotienting the crossing row span leaves a canonical subspace of dimension at least 9bV−C_r V within the inlet-generated span. The 95/9 projection-density bound therefore gives exactly the five-layer inlet inequality. The same rational dual and source-specific split premiums establish the claimed cost.

**How to interpret the rank condition.** Even when K has minimum rank, extra physical records can increase one or both factor ranks. Sylvester's inequality gives

    (rank A − rank K) + (rank C − rank K) ≤ NV − rank K.

At N=9b+s and minimum rank K, the sum of the actionable excess dimensions is at most sV. A local packet carrying a genuinely new input direction, or a response using a genuinely new output direction, falls outside the equivalence-of-spans theorem. An increased fitting rank can do so as well. This identifies the algebraic change required of a redundant transcript under the source budget. It never declares all redundant records unhelpful.

The inference concerns the canonical row and column spaces of the actual factorization on the conditioned U inputs and outputs. ## One scalar dimension above the minimum on either factor

**Proved for the literal scalar cut.** More generally retain arbitrary record width V, minimum fitting rank 9bV, and assume that the sum of the two *absolute field dimensions* in excess of this rank among A and C is at most one. The scalar one-copy source is then excluded for every physical transcript length. In particular this excludes every ten-single-bit event continuation of the scalar cut with rank-nine fitting, even if its input or output factor has rank ten. Fittings of rank ten remain open.

A nonzero canonical row or output column cannot be supported in the union of two real native flats of dimension at most five. Indeed each such flat is contained, on the 95 native roles, in a ternary flat of rank at most five: its vanishing integer minors remain zero mod 3. The ternary span can be augmented to rank five on the native ternary spanning set. The exact flat enumeration gives at most 20 roles on any one flat. Among the 511 nonzero binary codewords on these roles, only masks 7,19,42,129,410,466 have weight at most 40. Their (weight, maximum intersection with any ternary rank-five flat) are respectively (37,16), (40,16), (37,16), (40,12), (40,17), (35,16). In each case two such flats cover fewer than the entire support. Every nonzero vector in a locally gauged block-canonical space contains a nonzero scalar binary canonical support: undo the local invertible coordinate gauges, and apply a binary linear functional nonzero on one resulting component. This proves the same union bound for all block dimensions and finite characteristic-two fields.

Consequently in any one-dimensional enlargement of a canonical row or column space, all vectors individually supported in a rank-five native flat span at most one scalar dimension. If a nonzero such vector lies in the canonical space, its support already violates the bound. Otherwise any two such vectors have proportional images modulo the canonical space; after scaling, their difference is canonical and supported on the union of their flats. The difference must vanish.

If A has minimum rank while C has one additional dimension, the active decoder responses still end at rank at most one. Inside a rank-at-most-five origin, every local residual of a downset descent lies in row(C), since it is an actual payload minus linear combinations of earlier payloads. The sparse residuals just shown span at most one common scalar dimension. Grant it free to every layer of the earlier reconstruction. The surviving reduced subspace of canonical rows has dimension at least 9bV−C_r V−1 before the inlet-density bound. The extra allowance therefore subtracts at most (sum of the five layer weights)/V from the total movement lower bill. At b=1 its minimum occurs at V=1, giving a lower bill greater than 38.22 and above the scalar room.

If C has minimum rank and A has one extra dimension, every direct response column of an event landing above rank two is supported on the orthogonal normals to its destination. That normal space has rank at most five, so all these high-landing responses span a common subspace B of at most one scalar dimension in col(A), with B disjoint from col(K). Quotient the output space by B. The quotient preserves the rank of K, but all surviving direct decoder response uses events landing at rank at most two. The reconstruction therefore applies for r=2,3,4,5, with canonical input rows and no new term. Retaining the same source-specific public allowance and dropping the first layer subtracts only 9b from the five-layer bill. The scalar one-copy lower bill exceeds 33.96, again over the room. Neither argument identifies public credits with uncharged arbitrary transfers.
