## General cache-exchange principle

The same construction applies whenever three caches form a triangle of native
receiver facets. Cache `Q_ab` must be born at a chart contained in both facets,
each original contributing to it must still ascend through that chart to a
common upper chart, and the cohorts must admit the proposed inserted-facet
itineraries without violating other source obligations. Orient the triangle
cyclically. Keep each old cache at its birth chart for one receiver, and copy
its actual contributing source values at the other receiver's facet. Each
receiver then reads one cache at an intermediate chart and one at its endpoint.
This removes the common-lower-chart cache descents at the cost of explicitly
counted public ascent subdivisions and stationary cache ownership.

The disjoint cohorts and rank pattern in this instance make the count above
especially simple. Overlapping cohorts require an additional compatibility
proof; they are not covered automatically by this instance.
