# Source changes that no original-input gauge can undo

At an ordinary Reed–Solomon source the28 source-plane quotients are distinct-node evaluations of degree-less-than16 polynomials, with the required spinor and component repetitions. Every choice of sixteen distinct ordinary planes therefore gives an injective observation of the entire original bank. This follows from the Vandermonde determinant and remains true after any invertible original-input change or independently invertible quotient-frame changes.

For each of the111 new donor stars, `output/NON-RS-WITNESSES.json` gives sixteen actual ordinary source-plane IDs. In the marked k=3 scalar component, direct GF64 elimination gives rank r<48. The unmarked component can contribute at most its full3,072 fields, while the sixteen repeated marked spinor lanes contribute at most16r. The full selected observation thus has rank at most

    3,072 + 16r = 3,840 − 16(48−r).

The concrete deficiencies are at least16,32,48 or64 fields, depending on the witness. All selected observations exclude the corresponding genuine singleton plane, so its literal three-block replacement does not alter this argument. Real early/late frames and owner permutations are invertible on these ordinary quotients and preserve the deficiency.

A bijective change of the original input preserves whether an observation is injective. Independent bijective changes of its output coordinates also preserve this property. The source inequivalence therefore holds even for nonlinear bijective gauges on these interfaces, including arbitrary full-bank binary-linear input changes mixing both components and all field bits. It is not merely inequivalence under scalar or copy-wise frames.

Each witness is checked from the same original-coordinate source matrices used in the positive source/H construction. Its sixteen planes are not a claimed deficient native receiver view: every actual native receiver's sixteen-plane source cut still has full rank. The certificate establishes a structural change of the source configuration, not a new return-rank bound or a complete endpoint.
