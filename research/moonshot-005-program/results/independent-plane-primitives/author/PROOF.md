## 2. Four source words

Each word uses local fields0..111 for its first bank and112..223 for its second
bank. `local_to_original_fields` records the precise embedding into the
448-input source. All112-by112 signal coefficients, including zeros, are
checked.

| Word | Initial banks | Final banks | Exact raw map |
|---|---|---|---|
| forward-XY | L_h, 0 | H, F_h | (x,y) → (x,y+Rx) |
| inverse-XY | H, F_h | L_h, 0 | (x,y) → (x,y−Rx) |
| forward-UV | L_h, 0 | H, F_h | (u,v) → (u−Rᵀv,v) |
| inverse-UV | H, F_h | L_h, 0 | (u,v) → (u+Rᵀv,v) |

The names U,V denote the old source's B,D fields. Their raw initial charts
are respectively line and ground; this is preserved even though the source
of the U update is the second bank. Each field retains its own arbitrary
baseline exactly.

**Decomposition theorem.** Restrict either full word to operations whose
destination belongs to one plane, then rename its224 registers as above. Every
retained ADD source lies in that same plane. Every omitted operation reads and
writes only the other plane. All MOVE instructions have no owned field. Thus
this restriction is a self-contained literal word, with exactly the raw map
shown in the table.

**Proof.** The full source has only MOVE and ADD. Its ADDs are Y_h ← Y_h+cX_j
or B_j ← B_j−cD_h. There are no cross-plane operations. MOVE updates only its
own register and chart. Deleting all other-plane operations therefore leaves
the full chronology of every retained register unchanged. Every actual ADD
still sees both registers at the same chart. The full target restricted to
that invariant field set gives the displayed map. For the inverse word,
reverse the instruction order and reverse the signs of all ADDs; MOVE
endpoints are exchanged. Both literal compositions are the identity. ∎

**Interleaving corollary.** Any order-preserving interleaving of the XY and UV
words, including placing either plane entirely earlier, has their product map
and the sum of their invoices. Their supports are disjoint, so every event of
one commutes with every event of the other. Reusing the same public bank in a
later caller is allowed only after establishing that caller's actual chart,
body-history and original-input obligations; the corollary itself supplies no
such reuse for free.

## 5. Universal extraction identity and conditional budget

For any invertible linear map P, four arbitrary banks (a,b,c,d) can be
initialized by ordinary additions to
(w1,w2,w3,w4)=(c,a+d,b+c,d). Three abstract shears give

1. w1 ← w1+P w2;
2. w2 ← w2+P⁻¹(w3−w1);
3. w3 ← w3+P(w4−w2).

After w1 ← w1−w3 the output is
(P a,P⁻¹ b,c+P d,d). This follows directly after step2 from w2=P⁻¹b,
and after step3 from w3=c+Pd. A fourth P shear can remove Pd. Forming and
restoring the difference sources by reversible additions is part of a caller;
their body states must agree when such additions are performed.

The accompanying exact budget makes the following assumptions explicit: the
three half services occur at three distinct fresh rank8 stages; their
coefficients and body histories can be aligned to P,P⁻¹,P with a physically
admitted single rank1 head bank for the remaining Pd cancellation; at each
stage the other two banks receive a paid rank8 idle; and the final four
independent productive targets are two rank25 jobs and two rank24 jobs. Under
these assumptions only, the entire displayed Gaussian invoice is

\[
T=224[25]+224[24],\qquad
P=112[1]+336([2]+[3]+[5]+[6])+672[8].
\]

Its directed rational moment gap is32.73982388151937... at t=199/200.
The positivity justifies investigating this chronology. It is **not** an
actual caller certificate: coefficient alignment, temporary-source
restoration, all original baselines, ordinary target adapters, complete
precision/recursive costs and any further head queries remain unproved.
Every additional rank1 bank costs112, so one extra whole head-query bank
already exceeds this displayed gap.
