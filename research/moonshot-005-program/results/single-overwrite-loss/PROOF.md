# Every individually neutral pure-payload overwrite loses a common mode

**Proved source-bound obstruction for one overwrite and a fixed suffix.
No new κ.** The result gives an exact missing-record obligation for turning
the routing graph's hypothetical pure-signal arcs into a physical channel.
It does not exclude swaps, retained records, changed suffixes, joint repairs
or general common-dependent encoders.

## Proposition and source

Use the programme's pinned1,480-input native, SHA256

For each such occurrence e, let h_e u be its actual baseline payload,
T u the complete1,480 common endpoints, and z_e the complete endpoint
response of a unit additive pulse at e. The exact source identities are

    T is invertible, z_e is nonzero, h_e T^-1 z_e=1.             (1)

The statement holds for all1,472 occurrences, not just sampled parameters.
There are824 with h_e in the span of hub-only endpoints and648 without;
neither case avoids (1). The source's scalar coefficients are rational,
not assumed dyadic. The literal full map T, its inverse, every h_e and every

Fix any such e, any V>=1 and arbitrary independent common inputs u and
private signal q, each a complete V-coordinate field over characteristic

Assume no extra record depending on the pre-overwrite common input is kept
outside the final native public outputs. Grant the decoder all final common
outputs at one place and grant q itself for free. Even under these grants,
the original common endpoints cannot be universally reconstructed. Exactly
one further independent V-dimensional common record is necessary in the
unrestricted algebraic sense and one record h_e u suffices. This is a record
dimension statement, not a proved price or route for acquiring that record.

## Proof

The replacement injects delta=q−h_e u. The output is

    Y=(T−z_e h_e)u+z_e q.

Put v=T^-1 z_e. By (1), v is nonzero, h_e v=1 and

    (T−z_e h_e)v=z_e−z_e=0.

Thus u and u+v, with exactly the same q, have identical Y while their wanted
common outputs differ by z_e. This excludes arbitrary decoders of the
declared data, not merely a particular linear decoder. The construction
is coordinatewise; tensoring the witness by any vector of F^V loses V
independent dimensions.

The exact rank is1,479V. Indeed T−z_e h_e=T(I−v h_e), and I−v h_e is an
idempotent with kernel span(v) and image ker(h_e), since h_e v=1. A new
record with at most V independent coordinates can restore at most that
many dimensions. One full field h_e u suffices algebraically, because
Tu=Y−z_e(q−h_e u). Its actual retention, movement and subsequent erasure
are physical obligations. In particular a temporary copy of h_e u that
is immediately cleared does not supply the missing record.

When h_e=λ_e W for the hub endpoint matrix W, the older scalar terminal
formula would divide by1−λ_e z_e. Every one of these824 cases has
λ_e z_e=1, so that formula is singular on the actual source. The other648
cases cannot use that formula because h_e is outside row(W). This explains
why an individually neutral route is not automatically a usable pure-q
channel with free common restoration.

## What remains possible

The theorem assumes exactly one overwrite, the literal suffix and no retained
pre-overwrite common record. The first overwrite's lost information may be
kept in a physically routed carrier or in another actual history. Several
jointly designed changes can use different response and record matrices;
their correct test is the joint restoration row-containment criterion.
Those hypotheses must be checked anew, and this single-overwrite theorem
must not be added up to claim a general k-record or paid-call floor.

For example, when h_e=λ_e W and the original q carrier can legitimately be
swapped with the helper payload, it can retain h_e instead. If that carrier
subsequently reaches the hub, then λ_e Y=q, so a local snapshot
delta=λ_e Y−h_e allows restoration and recovery of the raw q carrier.
This is a valid algebraic escape from the no-record premise. It still needs
a source-compatible swap, a receiver observation, the carrier's paid route,
and complete ordinary/lifecycle accounting. No such complete native is
asserted by the single-overwrite certificate.
