## 2. A whole-record compiler for the coefficient maps

Let L be the three-by-three binary matrix for multiplication by lambda in
F2[lambda]/(lambda^3+lambda+1), in the basis1,lambda,lambda^2. It is useful

still reads or updates one entire k^V record, and its movement tariff is the
ordinary one-record tariff.

For every two-by-two matrix T over F8, replace each entry a by its binary
three-by-three multiplication matrix M_a. This gives a six-by-six binary
matrix E(T). The regular representation respects sums and products, so
E(TS)=E(T)E(S). If T is invertible over F8 then E(T) is invertible over F2.
Its determinant is therefore1, and it remains invertible over every k of
characteristic two.

Every invertible binary n-by-n matrix is a product of whole-record ADDs.
Gaussian elimination uses an ADD to clear an off-diagonal pivot entry.
When a pivot swap is needed, swap records u,v using the three ADDs

    u += v; v += u; u += v.

There is no scalar-rescaling step because every nonzero binary pivot is1.
Reducing the target matrix to identity records a sequence of involutory
ADDs; executing those ADDs in reverse order realizes the target matrix.
This proves the compiler for arbitrary records and widths, rather than just
for a finite input sample. No borrowed record, initialization, erasure, or
additional movement is needed at a cochart meeting.

## 7. Why the previous compiler obstruction remains valid

coordinate permutations. These preserve the subspace where each individual
record is a constant bit vector. A within-one-record lambda map on repeated
three-bit blocks sends(1,1,1) to(1,0,1), so it cannot be compiled by those
primitives, even with restored arbitrary borrowed records.

Here the three entries of(1,1,1) are three independent whole records. The
same binary linear operation sends three constant records to three constant
