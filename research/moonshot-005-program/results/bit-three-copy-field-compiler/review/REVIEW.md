## Why this compiler is legal

whole-record primitives preserve constant bit vectors, so that interpretation
was not available. The new source has three genuinely independent, separately
charged records at each geometric role. The polynomial coordinates of K are
these three records, each of arbitrary positive record width. Multiplication
by a field element is a 3-by-3 binary matrix across those records. A two-role
invertible K-matrix therefore becomes an invertible 6-by-6 binary matrix.

Gaussian elimination over F2 compiles any invertible binary matrix into
whole-record ADDs. A row exchange takes three ADDs, so no exchange, SCALE,
within-record mask, bit extraction, or uncharged ancillary record is assumed.
Every intermediate operation is on six whole records already at the same
actual chart. Reversing the ADD word gives its exact inverse. The three local
words have lengths 10, 16 and 14. The review reconstructs their matrices using
separate polynomial arithmetic; all 40 single-ADD omissions change the matrix.

This argument works at every positive common record width: every scalar
binary matrix is tensored with the identity on the record. It also holds over

It does not require that the tensor product with F8 itself be a field. The
earlier single-record constant-vector obstruction remains valid in its stated
model. Here the constant subspace has six independent record coefficients,
