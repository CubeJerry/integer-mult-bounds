# Independent review of the rectangular tensor row gate

**Accepted for characteristic-zero row decoders on the stated odd-label
geometry, direct tensor rows, and the retained eighteen-stage caller.
No new κ.** Root review of Worker A proof SHA256
`b5a732709aab1eec0bec173a7011f1e0509c4251ccc1159b1c06bbb5cf45fef0`.

The Hadamard identity `Q o M=I` is the correct decoder condition. For one
rank-one contribution, multiplication by its two diagonal coefficient
matrices leaves rank at most the rational rank of its support rectangle.
Subadditivity therefore bounds n by the sum of those rational ranks. A
uniform rational/binary rectangle-rank ratio proves the stated row-mass
floor. Repeated labels duplicate rectangle rows or columns without changing
either rank, so their allowance is justified. Rational ranks agree over
characteristic-zero extensions; this is not a characteristic-two theorem.

The author's modular rank computation is exact because every possible
nonzero integer minor has absolute value below65537. Independently,
`check.py` uses exact integer elimination with row gcd normalization,
without any modular-rank inference. It enumerates all ordered rectangles:
16,256 and65,536 for g=2,3,4, including empty sets. The sharp ratios are
1,4/3,3/2, agreeing with the author's transpose-reduced enumeration.

The moment conversion `r^t >= g^(t-1) r` has the correct direction for
0<t<1. Tensor pairing ranks multiply, giving the density floor used in
the complete caller. The root's separate integer-root routine independently
reproduces the negative upper surplus at g=2,3,4. For g>=5 the already
reviewed free-decoder dimension gate applies, so the all-integer conclusion
does not extrapolate from finite small-g tests. Normalized exponent
monotonicity extends the scoped rejection to stronger positive-exponent
targets as stated.

The rejected four-label proposal originated as a root exploratory idea.
Its correct diagonal did not establish a decoder: the three cross pairs
with111 are forbidden odd cells and have nonzero Gram coefficients. The
exact rejection is preserved rather than promoted as a low-cost factor.
The legal alternative has row prices3+1. A separate legal two-label factor
has a favorable relaxed eight-stage caller sign; the root independently
confirms it and retains its missing source/caller costs. It is a surviving
lead, not a supplier certificate.

Shared ascents, different callers, different bilinear geometries and actual
source changes are outside this gate. Both strict supplier interfaces and
all five foundations remain. Credit Worker A, DreamingOfClouds' small-factor
insight, the retained caller lineage, @CubeJerry and OpenAI assistance.
