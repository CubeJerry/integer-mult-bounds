## A cut charging arbitrary transcripts

For each W and each rank threshold k ∈ {4,5}, associate an allowed input set K(B) to chart B. Below k use L(B)={j in W : R_j ⊆ B}. At or above k use M(B)={j in W : for every i in W with B ⊆ F_i, R_j ⊆ F_i}. An empty list of such i gives M(B)=W. Each of L and M grows by inclusion under ascents, and L(B) ⊆ M(B). Thus no ascent loses any allowed input. The initial W-sensitive part at B uses only L(B). At receiver F_i the allowed set is contained in its known native neighbours.

Broadcast once the record at each downward movement S to A for which K(S) is not contained in K(A). After conditioning outside W, induction shows that every field at B is computable from the broadcast and its K(B) data. Local operations preserve this assertion. An unbroadcast movement transmits a function whose inputs remain available, and a broadcast movement is given. All required receivers consequently decode, so there must be at least 9b broadcast records for every W,k. This cuts all movements that lose allowed support even when the actual transmitted function uses less.

Choose W with the stated weights and k independently uniformly from {4,5}. For a unit descent s to s−1 that stays below k, a loss requires W to meet S(S). When it stays above the threshold, a loss requires W to contain a newly relevant facet normal in A-perpendicular, a space of rank 9−s. For the threshold crossing bound the probability by one. At s=1 through 8 this gives α_1, α_2, α_3, c, c, α_3, α_2, α_1, where c=(1+α_4)/2=913585/999993. At any gap g≥2, one is at most c·g^t. A private arrival has destination of rank six or seven outside D, hence K(A)=W, and contributes zero. Ascents also contribute zero. Summing gives 9b≤c times nonprivate downward cost. It also gives 9b≤c times the integer nonprivate downward gap sum G. For the three source multiplicities ceil(9b/c)=10b. Since each gap g≤8 costs g^t≥g·8^(-1/200), adding the private arrivals proves the result.
