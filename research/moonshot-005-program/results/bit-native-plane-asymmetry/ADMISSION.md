## Exact statement

Fix a receiver and an odd set of tight donors meeting every visible plane
in zero or two donors. Let s be the ordinary bundle dimension and b=16s.
At a paired plane the two donor coordinate projections E and F have the
same image, though their kernels may differ. Their sum N has N²=0 and
rank at most s. Summing the donor decompositions gives

\[
I=\sum_HN_H,\qquad
\sum_H\operatorname{rank}N_H\geq16s+\max_H\operatorname{rank}N_H.
\]

In particular at least seventeen plane differences are nonzero. This is
valid at every positive block width over characteristic two. It excludes
common plane kernels without a common-MDS assumption. Independent donor
gauges and genuinely different donor kernels are not excluded.

If exactly seventeen terms are nonzero, each has rank s and the family is,
up to a common change of basis, the displayed seventeen-term simplex tensored
with I_s. Explicit square-zero matrices realize this minimum and each splits
as a sum of two projections onto the same image. This proves sharpness of
the abstract operator bound, not a compatible source on the native graph.
