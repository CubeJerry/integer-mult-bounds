**Proved exact linear interface; independently reviewed. No new κ.**

code K have dimension c and held projection P of dimension p. Define
t=Σ_a dim π_a(P) and Δ=t−p. Each packet reads Hub rows and at most one held
chart before its first ground arrival; ground may mix received packets.

The minimum dimension of the joint span of first-ground-arrival packet
values containing K is exactly c+Δ. A chart-supported quotient complement
attains it with c principal packets and Δ helpers with zero Hub part. Each
helper is actually initialized at its held chart and descends to ground;
ground recombination produces a basis of K and the helpers are retired.
All reads, INIT, MOVE, recombination and dirty retirement are paid. Source
records remain available and unchanged for later actual decoders.

This is an acquisition-span and arrival lower bound, not a peak-owner,
complete-history or universal-erasure lower bound. The all-field linear
argument is distinct from implementation: fixed binary constructions tensor
with I_V for every positive width and characteristic-two payload field;
other scalar implementations retain their own costs.
