# Optimal repair of one-chart canonical acquisition by proper-born helpers

**Proved linear-interface construction, independent review pending. No new κ.**

## 1. Exact model and lower bound

At a fixed invertible actual source cut, let the current input-coordinate row
space be H⊕D, with Hub rows spanning H and held rows partitioned by their
actual charts: D=⊕_a D_a. All held records remain available until their queries
finish. Let K⊆H⊕D be a desired terminal code of dimension c. Put

    P=π_D(K), p=dim(P), P_a=π_(D_a)(P),
    t=Σ_a dim(P_a), Δ=t−p≥0.

An arriving packet is formed from actual Hub rows plus reads at at most one
held chart, then descends to ground. A zero-Hub-part packet may instead be
initialized at that held chart, read its held rows and descend. Before first
arrival no packet reads a different packet. At ground allow ordinary linear
mixing of arrived packets to obtain a basis of K and retire extra helpers.
All source access, directions, lifetimes and linear operations are charged.

The minimum possible dimension of the JOINT SPAN OF FIRST-GROUND-ARRIVAL
packet values is exactly c+Δ. In particular scalar independent packets need
at least c+Δ arrivals. This is not a bound on peak storage or total transient
history, and is not a minimum erasure theorem under other restoration rules.

For the lower bound let S be the span of those first-arrival packet values.
Its held projection is generated by chart-supported vectors and therefore
splits as ⊕Q_a. K⊆S implies P_a⊆Q_a, so dim π_D(S)≥t. Also S∩H contains
K∩H of dimension c−p. Rank-nullity gives dim S≥t+c−p=c+Δ. This argument
holds over every field, independently of matrix commutativity.

## 2. Attainment with exactly Δ proper-born helpers

Let D'=⊕P_a. Choose a basis B of D' made of bases from each separate P_a.
The images of B span D'/P. Choose Δ members E⊂B whose images are a quotient
basis. Then D'=P⊕span(E). Every member of E is supported at one actual chart.

Choose any linear lift L:P→H such that K is generated by the graph
{L(v)+v:v∈P} together with K∩H. Extend L to D' by setting it zero on span(E).
For each b∈B\E, write b=p_b+e_b under that direct sum. The p_b form a basis
of P, because B\E is a basis modulo span(E). Form a principal packet

    L(p_b)+b

from its actual Hub part and a single read combination at b's held chart.
For each e∈E, initialize a helper at its actual held chart, copy e and descend
directly to ground. Also supply a Hub-only basis of K∩H. There are
p+(c−p)=c principal fields and Δ proper-born helper fields.

At ground replace each principal L(p_b)+b by subtracting the known linear
combination e_b of helpers. The result is L(p_b)+p_b, and together with the
Hub-only kernel basis these are a basis of K. Retire the Δ extra helpers.
The first-arrival span has dimension c+Δ, attaining the lower bound. The
source originals are read without alteration and remain for later decoders.

Every coefficient is explicit finite linear algebra on the source cut. For
fixed binary bank spaces the operations are ordinary emitted ADDs on whole
paid records, and tensor with I_V for all positive widths and characteristic-
two payload fields. For arbitrary coefficient fields the algebraic theorem
does not grant a free implementation of arbitrary scalar maps: implementation
and all precision/compiler costs remain separate premises.

## 3. Physical prices and what is NOT being counted

In ambient dimension m, a principal querying chart of dimension d_a pays
[m−d_a]+[d_a]; a Hub-only principal pays[m]; a proper-born helper pays only
[d_a], plus its INIT, held reads, lifetime and dirty retirement. It incurs
no Hub-to-chart MOVE because its zero value is genuinely initialized there.
The basis algorithm selects exactly which actual charts support helpers.
All ground recombination is charged, for example at most pΔ scalar ADDs when
binary coefficients are used, in addition to actual Hub/held seeding reads.
Source-native assembly, X restoration and other output endpoints are not
supplied by this interface lemma.

The source/transport history can have dimension strictly larger than c+Δ:
Hub seeds and pre/post-query states are distinct actual observations. Only
the first-arrival span attains c+Δ. Retiring helpers does not erase their cost
or turn their earlier information into free receiver access. Sequential reuse
cannot evade the span lower bound; each new source-bearing arrival still
belongs to the counted interface. No claim equates this dimension with peak
owner count or a universal minimum of physical dirty CLEAR operations.
