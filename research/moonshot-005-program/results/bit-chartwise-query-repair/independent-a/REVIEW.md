## Proposition and lower bound

Fix the actual invertible source cut H⊕D, with D the direct sum of the independently held chart-coordinate spaces D_a. Put K⊆H⊕D, c=dim K, P=π_D K, p=dim P, P_a=π_a P, t=Σdim P_a and Δ=t−p. Each source-bearing packet arriving at ground is formed only from actual Hub rows and rows at at most one held chart. In particular it cannot read another packet before first arrival. No additional uncounted ground information is supplied.

Under precisely this interface, the minimum dimension of the joint span of first-arrival packet values is c+Δ. I independently verified the lower bound. If S is that span, π_D S is generated by chart-supported vectors. It therefore splits as a direct sum of its chart subspaces. Since P⊆π_D S, it contains every marginal P_a and has dimension at least t. Also S∩H contains K∩H, of dimension c−p. Rank-nullity proves dim S≥c+Δ over every field.

The bound concerns the first-arrival source span and consequently the number of scalar packet acquisitions. It does not lower-bound peak simultaneous ownership. For example the two target rows x0+x2 and x1+x3 from four separate held charts have c=2, Δ=2 and acquired span dimension4. Acquire x0 and x2, combine them at ground, clear the latter owner, and then acquire x1 and x3 and combine them: the schedule uses only three peak live owners. It still performs four source acquisitions and pays both retirements. The reviewed proof explicitly preserves this distinction.

## Constructive attainment and zero-Hub helpers

The subtle step is choosing a chart-supported complement, not an arbitrary complement of P. Choose a basis B of D'=⊕P_a as the union of chart bases. The images of B span D'/P, so a greedy quotient basis selects exactly Δ actual members E⊆B. Their span intersects P trivially, and D'=P⊕span E. Every selected helper is supported at one real held chart.

Choose a linear section of K→P. Its graph is v↦L(v)+v, where L(v)∈H; supplement it by K∩H. Extend L to D' by making it zero on span E. The remaining p basis vectors b∈B\E project to a basis p_b of P along span E. Their source packets L(p_b)+b each have exactly one chart-supported held part and an actual Hub part. The Δ selected vectors e∈E have exactly zero Hub part. They can therefore be initialized at their actual held chart rather than transported from Hub.

After arrival, subtract the known helper combination e_b from each principal packet. This leaves L(p_b)+p_b, and adding the c−p Hub-only kernel rows gives a basis of K. There are exactly c principal fields and Δ helpers; their first-arrival span has dimension c+Δ. The complement selection, lift extension and correction coefficients are all finite explicit linear algebra. Original rows are read without alteration.

For a fixed binary bank instance, every source/correction coefficient is0 or1 and the construction emits ordinary whole-record ADDs. It tensors with I_V for every positive block width, and remains valid for characteristic-two payload fields. This introduces no masked-array or unpriced cross-bank primitive. General coefficient fields give the algebraic theorem; arbitrary scalar multiplication and precision costs require their own actual implementation. The principal proof correctly makes that qualification.

The resulting binary ground repair takes at most pΔ ADDs, in addition to all seeding/query work.
