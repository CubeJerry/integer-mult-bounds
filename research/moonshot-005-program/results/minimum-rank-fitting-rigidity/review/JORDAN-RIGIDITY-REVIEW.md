## Exact statement accepted

For either the 96 hosted roots or all 104 first-X roots, let G be their fixed
dyadic normalized Gram matrix. If a V×V block matrix K over any characteristic-
zero field has identity diagonal, zero blocks wherever G has zeros, and scalar
rank 8V, then K_ij=G_ij T_i T_j⁻¹ for invertible T_i. Conversely every such
gauge satisfies the hypotheses. Neither symmetry, self-adjointness, positivity
nor invertibility of individual contacts is assumed. The identity minor on the
fixed orthogonal eight-frame proves that 8V is the minimum possible rank.

## Independent algebra review

The proof is a general finite-source criterion, instantiated here by two
separate exact certificates. Three properties of rank-one projectors S_i are
used: they span Sym8; their entire relation space is generated by differences
of orthogonal-frame sums; and their square/orthogonal test tensors span the
666-dimensional symmetric square of Sym8.

Factor K=C R through its B identity minor. Each P_i=R_i C_i is a rank-V
idempotent because C_i R_i=I. Orthogonal source pairs annihilate each other in
both orders. For an actual eight-frame f, C_f R_f=I on an 8V-dimensional space,
so R_f C_f=I as well and the frame sum of its P_i is identity. Consequently all
certified projector relations hold on P_i. This rigorously defines a unital
linear map Φ from Sym8 to Mat_(8V), with Φ(S_i)=P_i.

The defect Φ(X)∘Φ(Y)−Φ(X∘Y) is a symmetric **bilinear** map. It vanishes on
each tested square by idempotence and each tested orthogonal pair by two-sided
annihilation. The tensor spanning statement therefore makes it vanish on the
whole domain. No assertion about an arbitrary nonlinear variety is inferred
from finite testing: finite spanning of a linear domain is exactly what the
bilinear argument requires.

The chosen B factorization makes Φ(E_pp) the standard coordinate block
projections. The Jordan identities with those projections imply that
Φ(E_pq+E_qp) has only its (p,q) and (q,p) blocks. In particular all its diagonal
blocks are zero, so the p diagonal block of Φ(S_i) is g_ip² I. As it is also
R_pi C_ip, every allowed contact is automatically invertible. This step does
not rely on a genericity assumption or a limit from nonzero contacts.

For completeness, write U_pq for the (p,q) block of Φ(E_pq+E_qp). The square
identity gives U_pq U_qp=I, and the triangle Jordan identity gives
U_pq U_qr=U_pr in that order. With D_p=U_0p and D_0=I this yields
U_pq=D_p⁻¹ D_q and hence Φ(X)=D⁻¹(X⊗I)D. From an allowed p contact choose
T_i=g_ip⁻¹ C_ip D_p⁻¹. The (p,q) blocks of P_i imply
C_iq=g_iq T_i D_q and R_qi=g_iq D_q⁻¹ T_i⁻¹. Multiplication proves the claimed
gauge formula for all blocks, including the forbidden zeros. These identities
are valid without commuting the matrix blocks.

## Mathematical consequences and limits

Every minimum-rank fitting code is block-diagonally equivalent to the original
Gram code. Thus whole-coordinate supports, shortened-code dimensions and the
separately proved hosted minimum block distance 42 are invariant. The theorem
classifies nonsymmetric, potentially noncommuting operator fittings, rather than
only scalar tensor lifts or perturbations of a prescribed row space.

This gives a clear reason to examine higher-rank codes, changed source access,
or representations outside this static fitting interface. It is not a lower
bound on every physical multiplication construction. It neither implements
the T_i nor prices them, and it does not supply missing current rows, routes,
original endpoint restoration, compiler/precision services or BIT admission.
Any transport consequence must still establish its own timing and access
hypotheses. All five programme foundations remain visible and open.
