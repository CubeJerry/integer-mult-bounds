## 2. Local realization of a prescribed carrier subspace

**Lemma (systematic cohort realization).** Let S be a cohort of k original
inputs, all available at its actual common starting chart, and let R be any
r-dimensional binary subspace of their coefficient space with r<=k. Given
r existing upward run paths from that chart to Hub, the source can carry a
basis of R on r distinct original records, send the other originals directly
to Hub, and restore every original at Hub. It needs no new moving record or
stationary owner.

**Proof.** Choose a reduced row-echelon basis v_1,...,v_r of R with distinct
pivot source labels p_1,...,p_r. At the common chart transform the original
record X_(p_t) by adding exactly the unchanged nonpivot originals appearing
in v_t. Its value becomes `sum_j v_tj x_j`. Other pivot records do not occur
in that sum, because the pivot submatrix is identity. Every nonpivot original
stays pure. Assign pivot p_t to run t and send the remaining originals to
Hub. Once all are at Hub, repeat the same ADDs; the unchanged nonpivot values
cancel, restoring each x_(p_t). All other originals were already pure.

The exact formation-plus-restoration cost is

    2 sum_t(weight(v_t)-1) ADDs.

No field multiplication, hidden inverse, zero-width record, unrecorded copy
or source access outside the common chart is needed. The argument is a
literal binary identity and holds at every whole-record width over every
characteristic-two field. A fixed ordered basis, rather than just its span,
could require an additional basis conversion; no such conversion is claimed
free here. The present construction chooses and uses the systematic basis
itself.

Changing which original label follows a given run preserves the movement
histogram: every member first ascends from its rank-one source line to the
same cohort chart, each run is still used exactly once, and every remaining
original takes the same direct rank jump to Hub. All endpoint labels are
restored. The construction therefore preserves physical carrier counts and
transport ranks while changing the source's algebraic information.
