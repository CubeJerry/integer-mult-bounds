## 2. Rectangular cells indexed by crossing occurrences

Identify geometric chart aliases. At each proper nonzero chart C aggregate
its actual local operations in chronological order. Give every boundary
arrival occurrence its own formal input coordinates, even when the same
physical record has visited C before. Let U_C contain all upward arrivals
and independent original inputs born there. Let P_in,C contain every owned
downward arrival from a larger chart and P_out,C every owned downward
departure to a smaller chart. If their record counts are a_C and b_C,

    P_out,C = N_C U_C + M_C P_in,C,
    M_C : F^(a_C V) -> F^(b_C V).                        (1)

Every local history is linear in these formal inputs. Zero births contribute
no independent coordinates. An upward-to-downward turn contributes an input
in U_C and an output in P_out,C; no new lifetime or erasure is asserted. A
downward-to-upward turn or proper termination can have an input in P_in,C
without a corresponding downward output. Arbitrary interleavings and local
feedback do not alter (1). Actual repeated arrivals are substituted only
after constructing the formal cell. Thus (1) grants neither simultaneous
availability nor a read from the future.

Put nu_C=dim ker M_C. Choose a kernel complement, invert M_C on its image
with values in that complement, and extend by zero on an image complement.
There are maps R_C,J_C,h_C satisfying

    R_C M_C=I-J_C h_C,  M_C J_C=0,
    h_C J_C=I,         h_C R_C=0.

For the ACTUAL historical value T_C=h_C P_in,C,

    P_in,C=R_C(P_out,C-N_C U_C)+J_C T_C,                 (2)
    (I-M_C R_C)(P_out,C-N_C U_C)=0.                     (3)

Retain the acquisition-image condition (3). A birth-only cell has no kernel
coordinate and still obeys P_out,C=N_C U_C. A termination-only cell can have
M_C=0, in which case T_C retains all incoming information. No through diagonal
may replace the full rectangular M_C: another output may retain its input.

## 3. Universal spatial normalization, including route loops

For EACH owned downward arrival at ground take its actual value Z immediately
at the boundary, before subsequent ground operations. Repeated arrivals of
one lifetime are distinct occurrences. Let G concatenate all these Z values
and all T_C. At every proper chart C, every actual record history has a form

    H_C = Lambda_C G + E_C y + B_C x,                   (4)

where B_C is supported on original X lines contained in C. G contains actual
historical values, possibly mixed with arbitrary y. It does not supply clean
inputs or grant physical storage or observation of those histories.

Proof: use induction on chart dimension, not chronological event order.
Ground histories are generated by arbitrary original y and all owned downward
ground arrivals; zero births add nothing. At a nonzero proper C, every upward
arrival is a history at a smaller predecessor and is already normalized.
Original X inputs born at C are local. Every downward departure is the same
value as its arrival at a smaller successor, so it too is normalized, or is
one of the actual ground Z coordinates. Substitute these expressions in (2),
retain T_C and condition (3), then express every local history using U_C and
P_in,C. All resulting local X columns lie inside C. This proves (4).

Spatial loops do not make this an illicit chronological recurrence: the
argument proves algebraic identities among already actual values and uses
only strictly smaller charts at each induction step. It asserts no execution
order different from the original finite word. A physical record may cross
the same boundary any finite number of times.

Every owned downward MOVE has exactly one destination: either ground, or a
proper nonzero chart. Consequently the occurrence identity is

    D = D0 + sum_C a_C.

Since nu_C<=a_C V, the X-history rank satisfies the strengthened bound

    rank(G_x) <= D0 V + sum_C nu_C <= D V <= M V.       (5)

In particular it is also at most V times the sum of actual downward rank
differences, because each positive MOVE has rank difference at least1.
Unsplit long MOVEs can have strictly smaller mode capacity than that coarser
distance bound. Stationary helpers, turns, INITs and CLEARs add no modes merely
by being counted as events.
