# A paid complement permits a rank-deficient early H map

**Conditional local compiler and movement screen. Independent review pending.
No complete source, global word, supplier or new exponent is asserted.**

The balanced construction restores all original X at Hub before canonical
formation and before any Y leaves ground. Its square early phase therefore
uses invertible local maps. This result permits one local early map to lose
rank by adding explicit owned records and communication. Canonical records
formed later cannot repair early rank loss for free.

## 1. Exact local interface and minimum complement

Fix a fully early rank-two H chart with p incoming original field owners and
p prescribed outgoing original owners. Its incoming values x in F^p vary
independently after fixing the other frontier values. Replace its square
invertible map by a specified matrix P of rank r, retaining the other early
invertible maps, old outgoing routes and source/caller contracts. Put d=p-r.

Choose a d-by-p matrix L such that A=[P;L] has column rank p. Allocate d new
owned zero records at H. Complete the injective (p+d)-by-p matrix A by d
columns to an invertible square matrix T. Applying T to the original owners
followed by the new zero owners gives

    original outgoing owners: P x,
    new complement owners:    L x.

The original p owners follow their old routes. Send each new complement owner
directly from H to Hub, paying a gap-six MOVE. Once the early wave reaches
Hub, all original and added owners are co-located. Invert the other early
maps in reverse order; at the changed H step apply T^(-1) to its p restored
output coordinates and d complements. This recovers the old p input values
and zeros the added owners. Continue the old inverse sequence and retire the
d zero records with charged CLEAR operations. All X are restored before
canonical formation and before any Y leaves ground.

This is a changed early word with actual added communication. No later
canonical information, synthetic original holder or inaccessible stationary
record is substituted for an export. The inverse calculations at Hub are
ordinary co-located operations on the real owners, as in the retained source
restoration procedure.

The number d is necessary with P and the other informative routes fixed.
Fixing P x leaves |F|^d local inputs to distinguish. The other fixed frontier
values and their downstream processing add no information. Even a nonlinear
additional transcript therefore needs at least d field symbols. The direct
exports attain this information count. This is not a universal price bound
for every possible changed route architecture.

## 2. Ordinary operations and real owners are charged

For F=GF(2^w), expand T into a binary matrix of size s=w(p+d). Gaussian row
elimination gives a finite whole-record ADD word, charging three ADDs per
row swap. A conservative bound for one direction is s²+3s ADDs; the inverse
uses the reversed word with the same count.

The replacement H contribution and its Hub inverse cost at most 2(s²+3s)
ADDs, plus dw INIT, dw gap-six MOVE and dw charged CLEAR, with dw extra
simultaneous binary owners. These replace the old local H and inverse words;
they are not an incremental difference obtained by subtracting unrelated
upper bounds. Every other source, cache, middle and inverse expense remains
charged or must be recomputed for the changed word.

The checker gives a concrete local example. Let M be a 9-by-3 Vandermonde
evaluation matrix at nine distinct GF64 points and set

blocks. The stack [P;L] has rank nine and has a literal invertible 15-by-15
extension T. This operator does not establish compatible source quotients:
those would need common injective central readouts on every required whole
inverse fibre.

In the retained full-bank layout the width-three marked component occurs in

## 3. Exact retained movement screen

Keep the balanced non-return tariff Q0, bank b, paired multiplicity m and
genuine one-way ground return q. If e field complements are sent directly
from rank-two H charts to Hub, the new source moment is

    Q_new(t)=Q0(t)+(q/b)8^t+(e/b)6^t.

The exact necessary movement condition is consequently

    q + e(6/8)^t < b R_m(kappa),       t=1-kappa,

where R_m is the reviewed strict return threshold. Every field/binary copy
remains paid. The checker reconstructs R_m from the retained complete-caller
histogram and proves both signs for each integer cap by rational intervals.

For the six-dimension marked-component example e=96. At kappa=.005,
b=3840,m=870, the largest possible integer return is q=34615, only 55 above
the abstract nine-bank floor 34560. The next integer fails. Two such exports,
e=192, exceed even the limiting movement allowance at q=34560; increasing m
cannot repair that deficit under this tariff. Ordinary and recursive charges
are additional. None of these necessary screens proves that a word exists.

A smaller rank loss is priced at its actual size. A one-dimensional loss in
the marked component costs sixteen field exports, not ninety-six. This
package does not construct a rank-eight provider or another small-loss H map.

## 4. Constructive use and exact limits

An invertible H provider can impose more algebraic conditions than recovery
from its visible ports alone. The paid complement permits investigation of
that restriction while preserving the required early restoration. A sum
provider can use a common central map at its three donors if the source
fibres and readouts are compatible. Only this conditional mechanism and its
local compiler are proved here.

A successful continuation must provide actual source/early bases, shared
H/D/K/facet maps, a measured full return and the complete invoice. Changing
H fibres, central coefficients or proper outputs can change the late map B;
the endpoint must be checked again. No unproved endpoint is preserved by
declaration. No foundational assumption or complete exponent is discharged.
