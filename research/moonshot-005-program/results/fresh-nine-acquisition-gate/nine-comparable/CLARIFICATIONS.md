# Zero outer-projection branches in the mixed-capacity lemma

This clarifies PROOF.md SHA256 c310353906bfd22c13b5b3bc491f71baa7ca030b589220420927357c11d786c9 without changing its bytes or claimed theorem. Root identified these branches during independent review.

In a saturated rank6 group E_j, a remaining capacity2 group may have outer projection dimension2 while the capacity1 group's outer projection is0. The latter group is then contained in E_j. Since the zero-only group is also contained in E_j, all source roots lie in E_j union E_pair, two proper scalar subspaces. The already stated quadratic projector-spanning argument excludes this case. If the singleton projection is nonzero, it has full capacity1 and its intersection with E_j lies in U; only in this branch is it included as an outside group when F is formed.

Likewise, for a saturated rank7 group with two remaining singletons, any singleton of zero outer projection is folded into E_j. If only one outside group remains, all roots lie in E_j union that proper singleton root space, impossible. If neither is outside, the whole source has rank at most7. Otherwise both have full outer capacity1 and their intersections are included in F. No tensor inclusion is asserted for a zero-projection group as a separate full-capacity outside group.

These branches are alternatives already excluded by the earlier two-proper-subspace observation. They do not require a new source census, tensor factorization or price assumption. All scopes and pending independent-review status are preserved.
