## Assessment

The current committed pieces do not yet assemble a complete conditional
theorem above 0.004. Lowering the target nevertheless removes some genuine
numerical obstructions specific to 0.005. An optimistic fresh-eight profile
has positive complete-caller moment at kappa=1/240, strictly above 0.004, with
three fixed copies. That is a useful intermediate budget, not a complete
native endpoint construction or a uniform supplier.

The actual strongest standalone Q remains incomplete. At kappa=0.004 its
limiting allowance for extra work is less than 67.636181788408614926 per private
rail, while append-only reverse repair needs at least 96. All finite
multiplicities have still less room. This source-specific exclusion grants
the remaining forward work free; it concerns the actual terminal interface
after the single common/native itinerary has already been spent.

The new results here are the exact re-pricing at 1/250 and 1/240, including
their finite-m implications. The source identities, geometric classification,
and terminal departure lemma are inherited, explicitly pinned dependencies.

## Complete-caller identity

Let X_t=sum_r X_r r^t, t=1-kappa. Let T/P denote the whole selected caller
target/paid profiles, N the native paid profile, H its helper targets, R the
old raw main bill, and Q the entire proposed private rail paid bill. For
each fixed m>=2 the source composition gives

    T_m = m T -18(m-1)H,
    P_m = m(P-18N)+18N+18(m-1)Q.

With

    d=(P-T)_t/18,
    A_infinity=(N-R-H)_t-d,
    c=(Q-R)_t,

the normalized moment is

    (T_m-P_m)_t/[18(m-1)] = A_infinity-c-d/(m-1).

Both tested kappa values have d>0. Finite m therefore requires c<A_infinity
and m-1>d/(A_infinity-c). This is necessary and sufficient for this
particular moment test, conditional on a complete source and all admission
contracts. It is not a sufficient algorithm-existence criterion.

## Structural issues unchanged by lowering kappa

**Endpoint correctness.** The actual Q implements L, not E. Its lifted
private endpoint is E u_main+L(v_j-u_main), whose error is
(L-E)(v_j-u_main) for arbitrary independent inputs. Altering kappa or m
does not cancel this error. The existing Q retains 4569 changed-basis/4920
original-coordinate forward debts and hosted reverse rank 82. The31-return
consumer reduces reverse rank to 58 with 65 nonzero columns, but is still
incomplete.

**Terminal departure count.** The actual Q has independent d_i at distinct
original facets and the current hosted B rows at Hub. A variation can fix
all Hub rows while changing a required outside target, forcing a first
whole-field departure from each original facet with nonzero residual
column. For m-1 private banks the outside rank is (m-1)V and each MOVE carries
at most V, so at least m-1 departures per facet are necessary even with joint
cross-bank coding. Every positive integer-rank MOVE costs at least 1 for
t>0. This is a chart-node source argument; it does not falsely count
overlapping downset exits as distinct. Earlier recoding, unspent funded
common transport, retained informative records, or changed final
representations are outside this exact terminal theorem.

**Minimum-rank fitting rigidity.** The hosted 96/full 104 characteristic-zero
operator fitting theorem shows every rank 8V fit with the declared zero
pattern and identity diagonal is a block gauge of the Gram matrix. Lower
numerical targets do not change that algebraic result. It excludes changing
support geometry by static minimum-rank reweighting; higher-rank, mixed,
fragmented, earlier-coded and different-interface methods remain open.
It does not supply characteristic-two BIT.

## Consequential next theorem

Construct an earlier joint forward/reverse Q_complete with L=E on every
independent main input, the exact allowed charts and output interface, and
all required field restoration and owned retirement, whose full paid
histogram fits the finite-m inequality. A concrete intermediate numerical
benchmark is kappa=1/240 and m=3: the optimistic fresh-eight histogram leaves
over 1.070605367894541905 per private rail for all additional charged movement.
The current terminal Q cannot be completed within this budget; the change
must precede its separated terminal interface or use an explicitly funded
replacement interface.

After such a source theorem, supplier admission and compatible strict
complex and completed ordinary BIT savings above the desired multiplication
kappa remain necessary. The five foundations remain native_all_columns,
owned_erasure, compiler, precision_tape and analytic_recovery. An unconditional
result additionally must prove or legitimately bypass each relevant premise.
