## 3. Genuine numerical opening for an earlier construction

For each fixed integer m>=2, complete caller target and paid profiles are

\[
T_m=mT-18(m-1)H,\qquad
P_m=m(P-18N)+18N+18(m-1)Q.
\]

Here T/P are the full selected caller profiles, N the entire native paid
profile, H its productive helper targets and Q the entire private-rail paid
profile. Thus no duplicated helper target credit is retained. Write

\[
d=(P-T)_t/18,\quad A=(N-H)_t+(T-P)_t/18,\quad t=1-\kappa.
\]

The exact normalized margin is `A-Q_t-d/(m-1)`. A positive value is a price
test conditional on the complete source and admission contracts.
