## Exact fixed-m formula

For every fixed integer m>=2, let T,P be the complete selected caller's target
and paid profiles, N the whole native paid profile, H its helper target profile,
and Q a candidate private rail paid profile. The admitted composition gives,
conditionally on the required source/physical interfaces,

    T_m = mT - 18(m-1)H,
    P_m = m(P-18N) + 18N + 18(m-1)Q.

For any t in (0,1), put X_t=sum_r X_r*r^t and define

    d(t) = (P-T)_t/18,
    A(t) = (N-H)_t + (T-P)_t/18.

Then

    (T_m-P_m)_t = 18(m-1)(A(t)-Q_t) - 18d(t).

When d(t)>0, the per-rail allowance is A(t)-d(t)/(m-1), strictly below A(t)
for every fixed finite m. If A(t)-Q_t>0, the least fixed m passing this
moment test is floor(d(t)/(A(t)-Q_t))+2. This does not infer source completeness,
grant growing m(n), credit helpers m times, or remove physical/ordinary costs.
