## Chronology and rank

At exact9b cuts, the full payload row collection is independent and lies
in the rigid canonical row space. Subtracting arbitrary earlier cut
contributions is a block triangular change with identity diagonal, so
the residual blocks remain jointly independent. Chart-local native forms
intersect that code trivially below rank seven. The source-specific
equality theorem also forces each cut destination to rank at most one.

With no low exterior entry, any event origin below rank five sees only
initial local information and previous canonical cut values. Its nonzero
canonical residual would be local at that origin, a contradiction. This
gives9b[4], plus the disjoint8b private arrivals. Private arrivals may ascend
or descend; a no-exterior-descent submodel is not automatically vacuous.

With one low exterior entry Z, every low-origin residual has form
D_e=L_e+T_e Z. A left kernel of T_e would give a zero nontrivial canonical
innovation in a local chart, so T_e is invertible. Normalizing leaves
independent F_e=Z+L'_e. Two such blocks have a rank-V difference supported
in the sum of their charts. Since both contain the exterior destination,
the minimum support rank seven forces that destination to be a line,
both origins to have rank four, and their sum to have rank seven.

Every pair difference is therefore in one proper canonical shortening.
Three distinct proper directions are independent over characteristic two.
The three-difference identity consequently forces a common shortening for
all pairs. Its dimension is bV, not V; independent m blocks give
mV<=V+bV and m<=b+1. This handles arbitrary joint-bank maps without
multiplying a scalar support-cover bound.

If the exterior source had rank<=3, its initial local contribution joined
to a rank-four low origin would span at most six dimensions. Subtracting
earlier canonical contributions again contradicts a nonzero innovation.
Thus that exterior descent and all m low-origin descents have gap>=3.
For m=1, summing the two rank gaps and using subadditivity gives at least
the original[4] charge. The m=0 case cannot improve the bill.
