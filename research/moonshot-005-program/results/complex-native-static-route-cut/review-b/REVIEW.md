# Independent review of the static complex route cut

**Accepted: the pinned 803-route interface needs at least 164 additional
whole-field transmissions to implement its actual target.** This remains true
when every local operation and every message payload is redesigned, all old
routes are retimed, arbitrary local histories are retained, and arbitrary
coordinate matrices act on fields of any positive width over a
characteristic-zero field.

The acceptance is specific to the 400-input main operator and the published
initial/final chart interface. It is not a barrier to a changed route list,
another supplier, or integer multiplication. No new kappa is certified.
The exact movement histograms are independently exported here; root's separate
price review checks the numerical caller consequences.

## The exhibited cut

The review enumerates the literal binary span of every published frame and
identifies all equal-span chart aliases. The 128 identifiers in `CUT.json`
are distinct first representatives of their spaces. The full chart 127 is
inside; the zero-dimensional chart 3 is outside. Counting the actual public
owners gives 172 inputs inside and 228 outside; 228 outputs are inside and
172 outside.

Every old MOVE is classified from its actual endpoint spaces. The two
directions have the following exact data:

| Direction | Target block rank | Old route capacity | Old endpoint rank | Additional capacity needed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Inside inputs to outside outputs | 90 | 8 | 8 | 82 |
| Outside inputs to inside outputs | 146 | 64 | 64 | 82 |

Both target ranks and both old endpoint ranks are obtained by independent
rational elimination. All 72 individual crossing records match the
author's published instruction index, register, source, destination and
grade. The eight forward crossings are exactly chart127-to-chart3
movements of grade nine. The reverse crossing histogram is 32 of grade
one, six of grade three, 12 of grade five and 14 of grade eight. All eight
old grade-nine occurrences cross this cut.

As a basis-alias control, the checker reverses every frame basis and, when
possible, shears one vector by another. Its binary span and cut class
remain unchanged. Thus the cut cannot be crossed by merely renaming or
changing the ordered basis of the same physical subspace.

The old endpoint exactly saturates both old directional capacities. This
is a useful positive control: the checker does not falsely exclude the
word that those routes already implement. The need for 164 additional
movements comes from the authentic changed target, not from an impossible
claim about the original word.

## Why the two deficits can be added

Set the original outside inputs to zero. The outside side initially has
no information about the 172 inside inputs. At every stage, all dependence
retained outside on these inputs is in the span of the messages that have
crossed from inside. Local linear computation, copies, zero initialization,
erasure and communication entirely outside cannot enlarge that span. This
statement remains true with unlimited local histories and with feedback:
each later reply crossing from inside is another counted message.

At width V, the required outside output block is E_TS tensor I_V, which
has rank 90V. Each complete arriving field contributes at most V
dimensions. The old list contains only eight arrivals, so at least
82 new inside-to-outside movements are required.

Applying the same reasoning with inside inputs set to zero requires at
least 82 new movements in the reverse direction. These two lower bounds
hold for the same proposed fixed linear protocol, because it must work on
every input. A directed movement belongs to at most one crossing
direction, so their sum is 164. Deriving the two inequalities on different
input restrictions does not double-count a transmission. It counts two
disjoint sets of transmissions in the same complete protocol.

The argument permits arbitrary, noncommuting coordinate maps on a field
bank: only the dimension of each field and the locality of each operation
are used. It also permits fresh canonical charts. Extend the inside set
to exactly the listed 128 binary spaces; any other new space is outside.
An actual crossing has distinct subspaces, so it has positive Fourier
grade under the retained movement model. Any free change of ordered basis
preserves the subspace and cannot cross.

The old list may be used in any order and each old occurrence may carry
any causally available linear payload. These grants enlarge the real
model, so failure of the enlarged communication interface proves failure
of any physical implementation confined to it. The argument does not
assume the old scalar operations, private caches, owner chronology or
original payloads remain fixed.

## Movement changes and price scope

Suppose old occurrences of total movement moment D are removed, including
c crossing occurrences. A retargeted occurrence is counted as removing
its old arc and adding its new arc. The remaining old capacities then
require at least 164+c new crossing occurrences. At any exponent t in
(0,1), each such movement costs at least one, giving the exact symbolic
necessary bound

\[
Q\ge M-D+164+c=M+164-(D-c).
\]

This formula is accepted. It debits the information capacity lost when an
old crossing is removed. Counting only its tariff saving would be
incorrect. Every grade-nine old movement crosses, while all other old
grades are at most eight. Consequently each removed occurrence contributes
at most 8^t to D-c: the grade-nine case uses the strict inequality
9^t-1<8^t for 0<t<1. These symbolic facts support the separately reviewed
price and changed-occurrence thresholds.

If the total number of movements is kept at most 803, the required
236 total crossings exceed the old 72 by 164. At least 164 previously
noncrossing occurrences must therefore be converted into crossings.
This count is specific to preserving the total movement count; it is
not imposed on a source using additional low-grade movements.

This review does not independently re-run the author's decimal price
engine. It exports the original movement histogram, full caller
histograms and caller multiplicity for root's separate exact price
certificate. The cut theorem itself does not depend on a numerical
optimizer, floating-point search, caller price estimate or claimed
optimality over all possible cuts.

## Remaining scope

A candidate can escape by changing the old route multiset, admitting
separately paid input-dependent information through a different interface,
or using a different complex construction. Such a candidate owes its
actual route cost, correct payload acquisition, endpoint identities,
ownership and full supplier/compiler assumptions. The theorem does not
grant free earlier records or the other paid helper routes of the full
1,289-input source.
