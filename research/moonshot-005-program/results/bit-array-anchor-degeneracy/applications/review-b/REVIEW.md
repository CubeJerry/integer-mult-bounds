**Accepted.** The seventeen listed anchor information systems, across

## Full-bank contradiction and unchanged positive results

Each full-bank inverse fibre is the direct sum of sixteen independent
copies of the marked and unmarked component fibres. Forming the sum over
the same receiver family commutes with this direct sum. The full ranks
are therefore

\[
16(45+180)=3600=15\cdot240,
\qquad 16(48+192)=3840>3600.
\]

These are ranks on one original 3840-field bank, not values averaged
over different privately copied banks. The general array-anchor theorem
therefore applies to each frozen source separately. It permits arbitrary
redesign of its neighbours and arbitrary bank/output bijections at the
anchor; the contradiction is a property of its retained information
partitions under the original route and source-capacity contract.

The result goes beyond the existing RS-background obstruction. Every
ordinary donor in the general connected chain is now individually
excluded by its changed array. The all111 construction, which retains
no original RS source bank, is excluded at six listed changed anchors.
The hybrid is excluded at four listed anchors. An unlisted anchor may
fail a different triangle or a different condition; passing this finite
screen is not a completion certificate.

The source arrays, full local cut ranks, designated common fibres,
information-change witnesses and paid source words proved earlier remain
valid. In particular, the hybrid still provides its 101 native common
fibres and four genuinely different quotient kernels. The new conclusion
concerns extension to the exact global target with one of the listed
source information systems held fixed.

## Exact-rank-nine corollary

The admitted global rank classification has precise hypotheses: M is
a square linear fitting on the 111 native roles, its diagonal blocks
are I_b, its nonforeign off-diagonal blocks are zero, and the specified
221 foreign blocks forming the bipartite native spanning tree are
invertible. In characteristic two, rank M at most 9b then forces

\[
M_{ij}=K_{ij}G_iG_j^{-1}
\]

for one invertible native G_i per role, where K is the canonical contact
fitting. The application retains that entire tree-invertibility premise;
it does not assume or infer the classification for arbitrary singular
tree blocks. The inherited classification and its independent review
are pinned and were read for this corollary.

Suppose the proper word had foreign output B with M=I+B. Substitute
`x_j=G_j y_j` at each source and normalize output i by G_i^-1. The
normalized fitting is K and the normalized foreign output is K-I.
Thus every foreign target is the identity. In particular all identity
demands used by the array-anchor contradiction hold. The canonical
target is allowed to have more identity cells than S028; the proof does
not require a nonidentity block at either excluded target star.

This normalization preserves source separation, source alphabet sizes,
proper routes, chronological cut capacities and the distinction between
original banks. It is a mathematical relabeling for a contradiction,
not an uncharged physical transform or a newly compiled word. The
communication obstruction already permits arbitrary local functions.

For a linear native coordinate change, the anchor observations become
F_R G_j, their kernels become G_j^-1 ker F_R, and all their sums and
stacked ranks are transported by the same invertible map. The invariant
rank criterion therefore persists even when G_j mixes every lane and
both components of the original bank. More generally the already proved
whole-bank-bijection version preserves the same partition argument.
The resulting canonical computation is impossible, contradicting rank
M at most 9b.

Consequently every fitting satisfying these hypotheses and realized with
one of the listed frozen words has rank **strictly greater than 9b**.
For a GF64-linear fitting at bank b=3840 this is rank greater than 34,560.
If considering the six-bit expansion with arbitrary binary linear
fitting blocks, the bank has 23,040 binary coordinates and the same
argument gives binary rank greater than 207,360. A binary native gauge
need not be GF64-linear; in that case the whole-bank-bijection formulation
justifies the argument without treating it as a GF64 matrix. These are
consistent normalizations of the rank-nine statement.

## Limits, replay and attribution

The review admits no fixed fractional excess over 9b. In particular it
does not exclude every fitting of rank at most 34,688 at the GF64 bank,
or prove that every affordable near-minimum target is impossible.
The exact S028 target is excluded by its own identity-demand certificate;
other near-minimum fittings require additional analysis. Altering a listed
anchor's information system, changing the route or source-separation
contract, or allowing singular tree blocks falls outside one or more
stated premises. Both strict suppliers and all five foundational
obligations remain open.
