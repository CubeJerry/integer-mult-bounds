# Whole fibre partitions rule out a nonlinear fifth-receiver gauge

## Contract: one common exact quotient for every input value

Fix a finite field E extending GF(64), a positive integer k, and a source bank
X_j=E^(480k) for each of the eleven native donors. The frozen source/H prefix,
with D1509 removed from the proper observations for receiver r, gives a
surjective linear observation map M_jr. Its kernel U_jr has dimension8k and
is exactly the whole isolated D1509 fibre proved in the predecessor.

Allow an arbitrary bijection g_j from a common bank Omega to X_j, independently
at each donor. It need not be linear, additive, continuous, efficiently
computable, or zero-preserving. Also allow arbitrary, donor-dependent relabelling
of observation values. The retained common-quotient interface requires that for
each receiver r there is one equivalence relation Q_r on Omega such that

    M_jr(g_j(x)) = M_jr(g_j(y))  iff  x Q_r y

for every donor j and every x,y in Omega.

This says that *all* whole fibres agree as partitions of the common input bank,
after arbitrary output relabelling. It is equivalent to factoring each
observation through one common quotient by a bijection on its image. The common
partitions Q_r may be nonlinear and need not themselves be coset partitions.

For the linear source gauges in S032, equal kernels imply this contract at
every baseline automatically. A nonlinear replacement cannot infer it merely
from agreement of the fibres over zero. The theorem is a necessary condition
for a proposed replacement that retains this exact quotient interface.
It does not assert that every possible nonlinear supplier must use that
interface.

## General finite partition theorem

Let U_1,...,U_s be subspaces of a finite-dimensional vector space X over E.
Let P_r be the partition into cosets of U_r. The join of these partitions
has exactly the cosets of U_1+...+U_s as its cells.

Indeed, one step inside a P_r-cell changes a vector by an element of U_r,
so any finite chain changes it by an element of the sum. Conversely, writing
a sum as u_1+...+u_s gives a chain of s steps, one inside each relevant coset.
Thus the equivalence relation generated by all P_r is exactly equality
modulo the sum. Every join cell has cardinality

    |E|^(dim(U_1+...+U_s)).

A bijection of sets commutes with forming the join of equivalence relations:
it maps each finite equivalence chain to a chain, and its inverse does the
same. It also preserves cell cardinalities. Therefore, if donor-specific
bijections map each donor's family of coset partitions to one common family,
then the sum dimensions must agree at every donor for every receiver subset.

No linearity of the bijections or common partitions is used. Finiteness of E
is essential to the cardinality argument: distinct finite dimensions over an
infinite field need not have different set cardinalities.

There are eleven native D1509 receivers and462 five-element subsets.

Consequently **no set of five native receivers can satisfy the stated common

bijections**. Any larger set contains a five-set and is also excluded.

The frame matrices are over GF(64). Their nonzero minors and kernel identities
persist in every finite field extension E. Repeating k copies multiplies
both discrepant dimensions by k. Thus the theorem allows arbitrary bijections

Arbitrary output labels do not affect an observation's fibres. Replacing the

affect the removed-D observation kernels. A different source/H observation

## Why zero fibres alone are insufficient

In F2^3 take three lines spanned by1,2,3, and compare them with lines spanned
by1,2,4. Their zero-fibre sets all have size2 and their pairwise intersections
are {0}. The permutation swapping3 and4 and fixing all other points maps
the three zero fibres respectively to one another. It is a nonlinear bijection.

Yet the first three lines span dimension2 and the second family spans
dimension3. Their joined full partitions have cells of size4 and8. No
bijection can identify all three complete partitions. For the displayed
permutation, the first-line equivalent pair2,3 becomes2,4, which is no longer
equivalent modulo the first target line.

The finite control checks all24 bijections with these prescribed zero-fibre
images; every one fails the whole-partition test. This demonstrates the exact
hypothesis separating the theorem from an unjustified nonlinear no-go claim.

A complementary positive control uses F2^4, the lines spanned by1 and2, and

    g(x) = x XOR 1 when bits4 and8 are both set, otherwise x.

This is a nonlinear involution preserving both whole coset partitions.
The checker accepts it and verifies that their join cells retain size4.
The theorem therefore does not exclude nonlinear changes merely for being
nonlinear.

## Remaining escape

A nonlinear construction outside the gate must do more than rename the same
whole bank values while retaining every receiver's observation partition.
It must change the actual information available at some proper cut or the
exact consumer interface, with the corresponding physical and price proof.
This result does not supply a universal impossibility theorem for nonlinear
native suppliers, nor does it address infinite coefficient fields.
