## Mathematical audit

For coset equivalences generated by subspaces U_r, an equivalence chain changes
a vector by a sum of elements of those subspaces. Conversely any such sum gives
a finite chain. Their join is therefore exactly the coset partition of the
sum. A bijection transports these chains in both directions, so it transports
the join relation and preserves each join-cell cardinality.

Under the displayed iff contract for every x,y and a single donor bijection
g_j shared across receivers, all donors' joined partitions must pull back to
the same relation on Omega. Their join cells have sizes |E|^d. A strict rank
discrepancy thus contradicts that contract over finite E. No additivity,
zero-preservation or linearity of g_j, nor linearity of the common quotient,
is used. Surjectivity can be read as surjectivity onto the observation's
image; only the fibre equivalence relation enters the argument.

The quantifiers are sufficient: every five-set has one disagreeing donor pair,
so even gauges chosen afresh for that particular five-set fail. A larger set
contains such a five-set. Receiver labels remain the physical receiver labels;
relabeling observation values does not permit a donor-dependent reassignment
of which physical receiver is being compared.

Coefficient extension preserves both ranks, since nonzero minors remain
nonzero and the certified kernels retain their full dimensions. Tensoring
with an identity factor multiplies both unequal dimensions by k. Arbitrary
