# Two virtual demands exclude one extra D-slack field

**Proved for the fixed augmented source and stated two-feedback information
family; independent review pending. No new κ.** The result applies to every
characteristic-two field, every positive whole-record width V, arbitrary
block coding and arbitrary D-offset seed lifts. It is not a general q9
impossibility theorem. Changed cohort source spaces, broader feedback forms,
new information outside D+W, or larger acquired W intersection remain outside
its hypotheses.

## 1. The paid interface being examined

The independently reviewed augmented source has actual cache space D of
rank42, W of rank15, D∩W of rank9 and A=D+W of rank48. It therefore requires
six independent W quotient directions. The source words and native geometry
are authenticated by the checker; the source live-word SHA256 is
`8939d815b7e1b1dfa89e3e0a6812e5b427f98ddf3020a3300c6232aaa803db52`.

For q∈{1,...,7} and depth l∈{1,...,7}, consider retaining the old P0 delayed
feedback and adding the class-zero delayed feedback on query q. Let

    k0=e0+R0,class0,  N0=the actual P0 terminal,
    kq=eq+Rq,class0,
    Nq,l=sum of actual class-one q-cache rows at depths at least l.

The two mandatory seeds and all four displayed normalized rows lie in D.
Write B=span(k0,N0,kq,Nq,l). We grant B globally for free. The actual added
feedback has not been asserted to be a complete decoder; this is an
optimistic necessary information interface.

A ninth full principal lifetime is a legitimate paid candidate, with extra
movement [8]=8^(199/200). With two mandatory feedback seed slots, q9 leaves
seven free width-V seed slots for six W quotient directions. The case tested
here allows those seven fields to be arbitrary lifts inside A, giving up to
one width-V extra direction in D. Explicitly, for any characteristic-two
field k and V≥1, let S satisfy

    S⊂A⊗k^V, dim S≤7V,
    W⊗k^V⊂D⊗k^V+S.

Set T=S∩(D⊗k^V). Projection modulo D has dimension6V, so dim T≤V. No choice
of a W-contained complement, scalar-only matrix, or fixed seed basis is
imposed. The claim is that S+B⊗k^V cannot supply the complete native fitting,
even with all local input coordinates freely available to each receiver.

The restriction S⊂A matters. A seventh quotient direction outside A is a
different information interface, and is not excluded by this theorem.

## 2. Virtual demands from equal quotient classes

For native receiver i, define the coordinate side-information space

    L_i=span{e_j: r_i perpendicular r_j}.

Suppose i,j satisfy

    A∩L_i⊂D, A∩L_j⊂D, W_i+W_j∈D.

Any full fitting would provide, for each u∈k^V,

    f_i=e_i⊗u+ell_i, f_j=e_j⊗u+ell_j in S+B⊗k^V,

with ell_i and ell_j local. Since W_i,W_j are native fitting rows, the first
two inclusions force f_i+W_i⊗u and f_j+W_j⊗u into D⊗k^V. The third inclusion
then puts f_i+f_j in D⊗k^V. Because B⊂D, this sum must lie in T+B⊗k^V.
Thus the single slack space T must serve the derived, or virtual, demand

    delta=e_i+e_j, side information L=L_i+L_j:
    delta⊗k^V⊂T+(B+L)⊗k^V.

This derivation eliminates the six arbitrary quotient-lift directions
without assuming anything about their D offsets.

## 3. One-width intersection lemma

Take two virtual demands (delta_a,L_a) and (delta_b,L_b), each nontrivial:

    delta_t not in B+L_t.

Define the fixed binary spaces

    J_t=D∩(B+L_t+span(delta_t)), for t=a,b.

If dim T≤V and T supplies either nontrivial demand, its image modulo
(B+L_t)⊗k^V must contain the V-dimensional space generated by delta_t.
It follows that dim T=V and that its entire image is exactly that demand
space. Therefore

    T⊂J_a⊗k^V and T⊂J_b⊗k^V.

If the exact source spaces satisfy

    J_a∩J_b⊂B+L_a

(or the same inclusion with b), then T is invisible to demand a, a
contradiction. This proves the all-field, all-block exclusion. It is a rank
and subspace-intersection argument, not a conclusion from enumerating binary
slack rows. Fixed binary intersections commute with extension of the
characteristic-two field and with tensoring by k^V.

Each single requirement may be satisfiable with one slack field, while the
two together are not. This is why separate axis fits or independent local
receiver tests miss the obstruction.
