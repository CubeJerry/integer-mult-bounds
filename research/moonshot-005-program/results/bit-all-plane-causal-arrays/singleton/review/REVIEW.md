# Independent review of all eleven singleton Hermite interfaces

## Decision

Accept the positive local singleton theorem. The same F128 used for the
ordinary-plane interfaces gives an explicit causal map on all genuine
singleton holders. Every actual receiver obtains rank 360 in the real
540-dimensional source input; the aggregate over all 45 ports retains
all 540 source dimensions. The theorem correctly keeps the extra 180
dimensions of these slack incidences and does not claim a nonexistent
360-dimensional source-cut rigidity.

The ordinary, punctured and singleton constructions together provide
explicit local interfaces at all 1119 nonempty planes. They do not
establish global source quotient compatibility, later D-to-K-to-F
transport, all original-label restoration, or a complete supplier price.

## Hermite argument and independent determinant checks

For thirty distinct field nodes, simultaneous evaluation of f and its
formal derivative has rank sixty on the coefficients of degree below
sixty. The double-root argument is valid in characteristic two: both
f(alpha)=0 and f'(alpha)=0 imply divisibility by (T−alpha)^2. The thirty
relatively prime squared factors have degree sixty, so they cannot
divide a nonzero polynomial of degree below sixty.

Only first derivatives are used. There is no invalid division by a
factorial or use of higher ordinary derivatives in characteristic two.
The confluent Vandermonde determinant is the fourth power of the
ordinary pairwise-difference product, with no nonunit factorial factor.

The independent checker reconstructs all eleven singleton donor/plane
identities, all forty-five D4 exits, and every actual fifteen-port native
receiver selection from the pinned root table. It verifies x=e=12 from
the actual factor. It then directly computes all 360 sixty-by-sixty
Hermite determinants by Gaussian elimination over independently
implemented F128 arithmetic. Every determinant is nonzero and equals
both the author's retained value and the closed fourth-power product.
The full early V90 determinant is also nonzero.

Every actual D4 port lies in at least one of these full-rank receiver
sets. Thus each port's twenty-four source forms have their asserted
dimension, and the fifteen ports visible to any receiver form a direct
sum of rank 360. All ports together have source rank 540 because the
early map alone is invertible. These are actual shared port spaces,
not separate private messages chosen independently for each receiver.

## Physical holders, cache and arbitrary baselines

At each singleton flag, twelve real early source holders and twelve
real late direct-ground Y holders exist. The checker partitions them
using holder indices 2*stripe+b at every local port. Each genuine holder
occurs once. The coefficient order is defined on this physical order,
so no input permutation or additional movement is needed.

The derivative depends only on odd-indexed coefficients. Exactly 45
early fields per stripe, or 315 binary records, are copied into owned
stationary cache records before the early V90 map. The derivative ADDs
read only those cached records after the actual late holders arrive.
Every late baseline remains arbitrary and independent; the map on
late Y itself is the identity.

The borrowed V90 ADD word is pinned exactly and independently replayed
with the derivative stage on all 1260 original binary columns. Every
derivative operand is checked to be among the actual cached odd
coefficients. All cache owners remain dirty. Omitting one cache copy
changes the final map; omitting the derivative rows reduces the shown
receiver rank from sixty to thirty.

The complete public map is triangular with invertible early block and
identity late block. It preserves all early and late information, but
the eventual global construction must still implement its required
original-label inverse and ground preload. The local proof correctly
does not declare those operations free.
