# Paid port repartition and the genuine 416-input all-hosted source

## Status and purpose

This package proves a finite, source-specific change of native interface and
constructs two partial sources under the changed interface. It does not prove
a complex supplier or a new multiplication exponent. All five foundations
(`native_all_columns`, `owned_erasure`, `compiler`, `precision_tape`, and
`analytic_recovery`) remain visible. In particular, exact scalar-coordinate
replay and finite signed-map certificates do not discharge uniform physical
realization or precision obligations.

The new mechanism is **paid promotion of the eight coupled ports to genuine
hosted ports**, followed by rebuilding the entire Gram/parity source for all
416 arbitrary main inputs. The old coupled fields are not silently replaced
by zero-valued dual inputs. Every new D is independent and contributes to the
paired reverse map.

The real Gram matrix, omitted-root frame, reciprocal class codec, parity-half
identity, and rank-56 parity residual are credited predecessors. The new
results are the actual complete native conversions, exact changed-caller
cancellation, and the fresh all-hosted chronology and invoice. The bare
two-by-two linear identities below are elementary, not claimed as novel.

## 2. Actual conversion lemma

Suppose r old hosted ports are made coupled and s old coupled ports are made
hosted. In the two emitted cases s>=r, allowing reuse of the 2r vacated
public slots and appending 2(s-r) genuine new public inputs. Keep the entire
old complete word after the exact recorded register renaming.

### 2.1 Hosted to coupled

At O, an owned record copies y and moves to L_h (rank one), where it updates
X to x-y and retires. Independently scale Y by two at O. The old complete
hosted map now gives

\[
X=x-y,\qquad Y=2y+(x-y)=x+y.
\]

Scale Y by one half at its actual final facet. The desired coupled map
results. The removed old B/D pair is replaced by two genuine owned zeros at
its old initial charts. Its entire old chronology remains and all its MOVEs
are still paid, now marked owned. The old complete map sends its final
values to (0-0,0)=(0,0), so both zeros may be retired at their literal old
endpoints. `check_adapters.py` separately pulls back each discarded output
through the actual prefix and proves its row is zero. Kept-output replay
alone would not check that fact.

This conversion adds one rank-one MOVE and three owned lifetimes: the entry
copy and the two zero extensions. It does not refund any old operation.

### 2.2 Coupled to hosted

At O, a genuine owner copies y, moves to L_h, and adds it to X. Feed x+y,y
to the retained coupled map. It returns

\[
X=x,\qquad Y=x/2+y.
\]

After the old body, a new owner copies x at H, descends to F_h (rank one),
and adds x/2 to Y, giving y+x. This is an actual post-body route, not a free
query. For the new independent B/D inputs, a third owner copies d at O,
moves to L_h, and applies B<-B-d. B and D then make their own original
rank-eight ascents to H and F_h.

This adds three rank-one MOVEs, two rank-eight MOVEs, and three owned
lifetimes. No old duty is displaced and every new dual input is arbitrary.

## 4. Fresh all-hosted Gram source

Use the credited omitted eight-root frame and let g_h be the resulting
8-component rational coordinate row of port h. The exact real Gram matrix
is G_hj=g_h g_j^T, with diag(G)=1 and

\[
A^T A=13I_8,\qquad G=AA^T,\qquad G^2=13G.
\]

All 104 ports are now hosted. Each has independent (x_h,y_h,b_h,d_h); no
coupled-current variable x-y or factor 1/2 is present. Recompute, rather than
inherit, each class's D and Y codecs on all its original inputs. Fourteen
classes have four mode coordinates each, meeting at their actual C3 chart.
Each class coefficient matrix has rank four, witnessed by its four recorded
pivots and exact invertible encoder.

For each mode i, initialize a reverse owner at its omitted line. Encode
the class's original D bank at O. Its four pivot fields visit their mode
lines and add the actual encoded value to the relevant owner, then rise to
C3; nonpivots go directly O->C3. Apply the exact inverse encoder at C3.
Thus every original d_h is restored before its remaining rise to F_h, and
the eight owners contain

\[
q_i=\sum_{h=1}^{104}g_{hi}d_h.
\]

Move all X/B from their original lines to H. Bring each q_i to H and
subtract g_hi q_i from each B_h, then retire it. Form the forward modes
p_i=sum_h g_hi x_h at H, descend to their omitted lines, and deliver them
through the genuine dual Y codecs. The final map is

\[
X=x,\quad Y=y+Gx,\quad B=b-Gd,\quad D=d.

be reused on this domain.

\[
C_{4,T}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}

\]

\[
z_h=-\sum_{j\in S}G_{hj}x_j\quad(h\in T),\qquad
B_j\mathrel{+}=\sum_{h\in T}G_{hj}d_h\quad(j\in S).
\]

to its original facets. Acquire, move and consume the unchanged full104

facet rise. Thus this word has

\[
X=x,\quad Y=y+Lx,\quad B=b-L^Td,\quad D=d,
\qquad L=I+G-\operatorname{blockdiag}_{C_3}(G).
\]

There are 4608 offdiagonal coefficients left in L, all cross-class, and the

\[
L-I=Q^T(E^TE-I_{56})Q,

## 6. Verification, boundaries and next obligations

The package carries four actual words, literal native/caller pins, exact
replays, ordinary invoices, directed movement intervals and phase-sensitive
new map recipes. The complete variants introduce 15 and 24 ordered chart
pairs. The fresh Gram source introduces no pair beyond its pinned seed;
the C4 source introduces 264. Signed-phase mutation is rejected in every
case, including a retained map in the zero-new-map case.

Adapter controls flip every new entry/dual/output shear. An explicitly
dirty fake zero is caught by the discarded-output check. Partial-source
controls omit D/Y decoders, reverse/forward deliveries and C4 corrections.
Suppressing a promoted independent D drops the input rank to 415 and fails
the full target, demonstrating why old zero-coupled mode banks cannot be
silently reused. Exact root brackets, not floating displays, decide the
first feasible m. Reproduction is author verification, not independent
review; independent reviews belong outside the frozen author package.

These words are separate alternatives to the earlier singular-Gram,
proper-born clique, and 400-field coupled-import sources. Their gains must
not be added. The changed full interface and all-hosted source form one
coherent composition; further composition needs a literal new word and a
new whole-system invoice.

The next obligation is a paired, physically acquired completion of the
rank-56 all-hosted residual within the available allowance, possibly through
changed funded histories or a new receiver representation. A one-row
import without its actual transpose service cannot complete a hosted
column. Promoting old coupled ports also removes the zero-input premise of
the recovered seven-coupled-mode relay; adapting that alternative requires
rederiving its actual source histories for all 104 independent D inputs.
The orthogonal repartition demonstrates lawful interface freedom, but its
new coupled frame does not itself reduce the 96-hosted residual or excuse
the adapter costs. Both strict suppliers, every uniformity/precision cost,
and the five foundations remain open. **No new kappa.**
