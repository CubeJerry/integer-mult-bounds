# Independent binary replay of the entire covariant difference

The physical early row collection and late column broadcast implement the complete S028 difference on all 123 modified edges, including its five internal 64-by-64 matrices. They cost 20,269,944 binary ADDs, 768 INITs and 768 dirty CLEARs on the existing public routes. This gives the proper rank-128 difference as a standalone word. Implementing and combining the canonical foreign identity contribution within the budget remains open.

The verifier authenticates the S028 assignment and 106,560 base route records, then checks every shear, scalar, exchange, individual cross-copy shear and coded tap against its true chart and phase. It reconstructs field multiplication from the 63 nonzero polynomial-basis powers. Each six-bit scalar circuit is tested in both directions on every field element. Six input bit planes pack 64 independent master coordinates. All six output bits at each of the 426,240 fields are retained. For each side this compares 982,056,960 binary transfer entries, including every zero and off-bit coefficient.

The column transfer is the actual forward map. The formal row transfer uses field adjoints. Under the nondegenerate field-valued bilinear pairing, transposing a field shear exchanges the two indices and keeps its field coefficient. The actual primal early word traverses source, H, D and F80 in forward time. The final field entries 0 and 1 expand to zero and identity on the six physical bits. Ground and Hub pure inverses are literal reversals of paid binary gates on the corresponding complete wave.
