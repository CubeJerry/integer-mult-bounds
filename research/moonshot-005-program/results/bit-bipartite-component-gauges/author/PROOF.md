# Independent input and output gauges for the two-sided target

Over any characteristic-two field F, let B be the S028 two-sided target of bank b=3840. For each input j and output i choose subspaces W_j and U_i of dimension d in F^b tensor F^s for integer s>0. Suppose (B_ij tensor I_s)W_j=U_i on every foreign edge. Then there is one Z≤F^s with every W_j and U_i equal to F^b tensor Z in the given coordinates. Conversely all such systems work. This permits unrelated gauges on the two sides of each native role.

The unperturbed identity edges form a connected bipartite graph on 220 vertices after removing output 80 and input 10. Its 6347 edges are reconstructed from root dot products. Hence all remaining spaces equal a common W. The two deleted spaces may still differ at this point.

In the predecessor's 60 banks of width 64, F_l maps tail l to bank 0; R_l is the reverse. Both vanish elsewhere. Each internal generator N acts only in bank 0. Fix S=I+F_1 and C=I+R_1. Relative row maps S^{-1}B_(80,j) and column maps B_(i,10)C^{-1} preserve W after tensoring. Both anchors are involutions. The unital algebra of the relative matrices on one base body contains all F_1+F_l and R_1+R_l, since two forward or two reverse transfers multiply to zero. The product (F_1+F_2)(R_1+R_3) is the central projection P. For a row-internal N the relative map minus I is F_1+N since F_1 N=0. For a column-internal N it is R_1+N since N R_1=0. Compression by P recovers N. Subtraction recovers F_1 and R_1 and then every transfer.

The predecessor's exact internal-algebra certificate shows that all these generators span the full algebra M_b(F) under products and sums. The relative algebra is therefore full, even with the two gauges freed. At multiplicity s it acts as M_b(F) tensor I_s. Diagonal matrix units separate the coordinate fibres of W, and off-diagonal units make every such fibre the same Z. This proves W=F^b tensor Z, invariant also under S and C. The two omitted spaces are thus forced to agree. This is a statement about the fixed target and field-linear endpoint subspaces.
