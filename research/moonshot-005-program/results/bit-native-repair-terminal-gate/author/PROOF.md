# A minimum native-mask rank repair, and the terminal-chart obstruction it does not remove

**Exact positive algebra; negative result for the specified public-network
architecture. No complete supplier.** The native correction N has rank108.
Deleting three native entries makes it invertible, and those entries admit
cheap actual facet captures. Nevertheless a public-Y-only network cannot
realize the repaired correction under the stated terminal-source interface,
even with arbitrary fixed multi-bank mixing. Its rows require full facets,
whereas the incoming public rows carry only proper-chart native support.

This extends the programme's retained last-shared-chart mechanism to a
simple arbitrary-bank terminal-rowspace statement. The underlying scalar
principle is not new: see `bit-last-shared-chart/ADMISSION.md` and
`bit-clean-source-cache-gate/ADMISSION.md`. Their source-specific nonlinear
and chain-capacity conclusions are not imported into this changed source.

## 1. Native matrix and exact minimum rank repair

Use the authenticated111 real roots, source SHA256
`8cd5303821f7cb529ab756e1c6d6697672023a9d63193a676fd0b7c62308ab4e`.
Let N_ij=1 exactly when r_i is orthogonal to r_j. Thus N has zero diagonal,
and K=I+N is the rank-nine canonical fitting.

Exact binary elimination gives rank N=108 and rank K=9. Define R to have
one in positions(0,1),(1,0),(2,3) and zero elsewhere. All three entries are
native contacts. The binary matrix M=N+R is invertible. The certificate
records M and its full inverse and checks both products equal I111. This
identity remains true over every characteristic-two field and after tensoring
with an identity at any positive whole-record width.

Since rank(N+E)<=rank N+rank E, every additive repair that makes N invertible
has rank at least3. R has rank3, so this is a minimum-rank repair. Each of
its three single-edge omissions leaves rank110. It is not merely a diagonal
repair that would require a forbidden own-source native read.

## 4. Arbitrary-bank terminal-span theorem

Fix any b>=1 independent original Y banks per role and any positive record
width V. Let F_i be their distinct rank-seven final facets. Assume:

1. Public paths are monotone. An original Y whose final facet is F_i cannot
   leave F_i after first entering it.
2. After ground preconditioning, the native correction component starts
   from zero and is acquired only from actual local native inputs and
   cochart linear operations. No informative auxiliary enters a final facet
   except its specified terminal source fields.
3. The b public Ys entering F_i are its only incoming Y-dependent fields.
   Arbitrary linear processing at F_i, including zero-born local scratch,
   is allowed. Its other source inputs are the specified raw terminal cache
   coordinates J_i. No new communication enters or leaves this local region.
4. Exact endpoint restoration leaves the net ground-code contribution Kx.
   Hence after projection away from J_i the required native component is
   N_i tensor I_(bV), restricted to its uncached coordinates S_i.
5. Those uncached native source lines span all of F_i.

Then this architecture cannot complete receiver i.

**Proof.** Decompose each field linearly into its ground-baseline component
and its later native-input component. Every native component in a moving
public field is supported on source lines contained in its current chart:
this holds at actual native acquisition and is preserved by every cochart
linear operation and upward move. Immediately before each Y first enters
F_i, its native component is therefore supported in one proper chart of
F_i, of rank at most6. The entry MOVE changes no value.

Postpone processing confined to F_i until all b Ys have entered. This does
not alter incoming values, because no earlier entrant can communicate back
out of F_i under the premise. Final original-Y identity forces the local
linear map L on these bV incoming Y coordinates to be invertible: the final
bV independent private-Y coordinates cannot have rank bV through a smaller-
rank map. Terminal raw-cache additions do not carry private Y information.

Project all X coefficients away from J_i. The incoming native rows must then
be L^(-1) times the target N_i tensor I_(bV). Every nonzero scalar row of
L^(-1) gives the same nonzero coefficient vector on every geometric source
role in S_i. Thus every nonzero incoming row has geometric support spanning
F_i, of rank7. That contradicts its proper-chart support on arrival. The
argument is unaffected by arbitrary block coefficients or mixing among
independent banks. QED.

For the repaired source, J_0={1}, J_1={0}, J_2={3}, and all other J_i are
empty. The checker supplies seven independent uncached source roots at
EVERY receiver. Thus all111 roles meet the full-span premise. The three
additional stationary raw facet values do not rescue the proposal.

The ground-code premise is essential: a different actual ground map, new
terminal packets, additional Y-bearing field arrivals, changed source
histories or extra owned communication can change the conclusion. This is
not a universal impossibility theorem for κ=.005, coding across copies,
all public-Y networks, or every nine-packet interface.
