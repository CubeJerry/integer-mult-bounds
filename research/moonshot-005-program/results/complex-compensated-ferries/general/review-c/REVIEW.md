## Restored-cut first-departure proof

At the cut, write the Hub state as `b + (M' - I)d`, with each original `d_j` at its own distinct facet. The only other fields carrying D information are those source fields; first-X/first-Y rows are D-independent and all owner records are retired. For an unreturned coordinate `j`, change `d_j` by `δ` and change `b` by `-(M' - I)e_j δ`. The Hub state stays fixed, while the required target `b-d` changes by `-M'[:,j]δ`.

Whenever `M'[:,j]` is nonzero, some information about this independent `δ` must reach the Hub from the original `d_j` facet. There is no retained mixed record that can supply it. A local ADD/COPY can move the information only into a cocharted field; at least one physical field must make a strict chart move out of that distinct facet. Its first move may go either upward or downward; either direction has positive rank, and a route that first descends must still reach the required Hub later. Distinct starting facets require at least 65 such departures, even if later fields merge, code across outputs, or use singular intermediate maps.

For whole-record width `V`, a full-rank unreturned block column requires `V` independent information dimensions to leave each facet. They may travel in one or more whole-`V` records. Thus the lower bound is 65 whole-record departures per rail, not 65V MOVE events. Each such record MOVE costs at least `V` rank-one units, so after normalization by `V` the per-rail movement-moment lower bound remains 65. In the scalar source, a nonzero column is sufficient and the bound is 65 unit-rank movement atoms.

For the orthogonal line-deposit class, diagonal 1 proves the required 65 scalar columns nonzero; with whole-record blocks, diagonal `I_V` proves full column rank `V`. For a broader scalar restored-cut system, this argument must be stated conditionally on those 65 columns remaining nonzero. For a broader block system, the corresponding condition is full-rank unreturned columns. The proof does not establish either condition for an arbitrary redesigned source. If an informative D-bearing record is retained outside the Hub/facet cut, the departure argument does not apply.

## Scope notes

- The exact rank-8 free optimum is a relaxation for off-diagonal orthogonal line deposits, not a representation of every possible packet itinerary. A route that carries a D value up to Hub and then descends to a nonorthogonal receiver is outside that restricted matrix support; it still pays its moves.
- The 65-departure result is a distinct restored-cut theorem and is broader than the rank-8 relaxation, but requires nonzero target columns and no surviving mixed D record.
- The claim excludes an append-only suffix after this restored cut under those premises. It does not exclude preterminal retention, additional mixed observations, or a changed global source/current-row code.
- The fixed-m pricing identity applies to admissible multiplicities `m >= 2`; the `d/(m-1)` formula does not cover `m=1`.
