# Admission: exact marginal return-rank optimization

**Proved arbitrary-field theorem with independent GF4 review. No new kappa.** For fixed M0,A,C,D and rank(F)=m<=b, the minimum of rank[M0,A+CF+DL] is rank(M0)+z+max(0,r-a), where W=im[M0 D], a=rank(A mod W), r=rank(C mod W), and z=rank(A mod(W+im C)). The minimum is attained over the original field.

This concerns the completed fitting, unlike the minimum proper-information-rank lemma. Actual C,D must include all owners and side outputs. Other sources and the shared word stay fixed. Dropping target equations gives an optimistic lower bound; separately optimized donor gains cannot be added.
