## Ordered terminal identity

Write successive packet updates after a read as z_(t+1)=U_t z_t+L_t x, where every U_t is invertible, and L_t is a cache row at the packet's chart D_t. Additive edits have U_t=I; pure units have L_t=0. If P_s=U_(N-1)...U_s and P_(t+1)=U_(N-1)...U_(t+1), then

    z_s=P_s^(-1) Z - sum_(t>=s) P_s^(-1) P_(t+1) L_t x.

The order of factors is essential and the formula holds for arbitrary block size without commutativity. Every D_t lies inside the earlier read chart C. Each L_t has nonzero input columns only at root lines contained in D_t, hence in C. Left block multiplication preserves this column-support condition. Thus the difference between the earlier read and a linear function of the final payload is local at C. Terminal payloads may be taken at different chronological clearing times: all are fixed linear maps of the same original inputs, and no physical simultaneous availability is being granted.

Apply this identity to every packet read. Track each public Y row as its arbitrary-Y part, its linear function of the nine terminal payloads, and a residual X row B. Initially B=0. Its diagonal column is zero because its own root line is not contained in its perpendicular facet; all prescribed noncontact columns are zero for the same reason.

## Stronger arbitrary monotone-source gate

Let r_i count the original X paths visiting F_i, and let C_k be a path's chart immediately before first entry there. Each C_k is proper. Its arriving row is supported in C_k by source locality. After arrival all source operations and retained histories at F_i are generated by these rows, so their total support is contained in the UNION of the r_i proper C_k. Their linear span can equal F_i; the union bound intentionally does not replace that union by its span.

Let s_i count descending packet paths visiting F_i. Each eventually leaves for a first proper chart D_p because its terminal destination is zero. All later source edits after that departure are supported in D_p. Later edits while the packet is still at F_i belong to source histories already covered by the r_i arrival charts. The backward terminal formula therefore adds at most one further proper support subspace D_p for each such packet, even with repeated reads and ordered block units.

The Y path's earlier residual support lies inside its own last proper C_Y. It follows that

    S_i subset C_Y union (union_k C_k) union (union_p D_p).

If r_i+s_i<=1, this is a union of at most two proper subspaces of F_i. The proposed geometric bound says such a union contains at most 52 full-E7 root lines, whereas actual |S_i| is 55 through 62. Thus r_i+s_i>=2. Summing yields at least 222 visits. A single ascending X path or descending packet path visits at most one seven-dimensional facet, so the 111+9 declared paths give at most 120. This is a contradiction. It is unaffected by arbitrary block/noncommuting coefficients once the terminal support premise holds.

## Controls and boundaries

Invertibility of every packet unit matters. A zeroing SCALE can discard the only global payload; one final field then no longer represents its earlier global information. Descent matters: a packet can read a nonlocal x at zero, ascend to its root line, and add x to cancel its terminal payload. The read-minus-terminal difference is then not local at the zero chart. Generated-cache ancestry matters: preloading a full local correction at each final facet defeats the arrival count without any arrival. No such preloading is authorized by the declared source.

A Y-bearing moving copy can branch through incomparable proper charts and merge at a final facet; its two histories need not fit in one last-proper-chart subspace. The no-Y-owned-copy hypothesis is therefore substantive.
