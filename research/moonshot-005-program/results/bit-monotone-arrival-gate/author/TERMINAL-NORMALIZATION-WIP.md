## Precise additive-packet interface

Use a fixed pure-X source whose stationary history at chart C uses only input
root lines contained in C. There are q persistent whole-V owned packet
lifetimes. Each packet follows a monotone descending chart chain and may add
arbitrary local source-cache linear functions at charts it visits. Nonzero
scales/block units are allowed. It has a final payload Z_p; no earlier global
payload is discarded by CLEAR/re-INIT or overwritten from outside the declared
source. Public Y fields start with independent y and follow ascending paths
to distinct7-dimensional facets Fi. They may read packets/caches and mix
cocharted public Y fields linearly, while restoring the arbitrary y map.
There are no Y-bearing moving owned copies or additional informative movers.

At a packet read at C, solve the subsequent additive/unit updates backward:
its current payload is M*Z_p plus a linear combination of later cache edits.
Each later edit is at D contained in C, and its original input support has
root lines in D. Thus the difference from a linear function of terminal
payload is supported geometrically inside C. This is an algebraic identity,
not a grant of physically available future history to the receiver.

After all reads and public-Y mixing, every final output has the form

    Y_final = y + A*x + B*x,     A = L * Z.

A has rank at most qV. Every B contribution starts at a chart containing its
source root line and propagates only upward on public-Y trajectories. It
therefore vanishes at the own input and every noncontact input. If output is
exactly y+x, A is a fitting of the actual graph with diagonalI and prescribed
noncontact zero blocks. For q=9, the admitted minimum-rank theorem forces
rank9V. For scalars over every characteristic-two field, global rigidity
then makes A a vertex gauge of K. The same conclusion holds on the admitted
invertible-Schur block stratum. For a fixed canonical effective decoder,
the punctured-code lemma uniquely forces the canonical terminal encoder,
without a scalar restriction. Arbitrary singular-Schur block fittings remain
outside the support conclusion below.

For the fixed canonical decoder used by the current search, this eliminates
its arbitrary initial packet rows: P_start equals canonical P_terminal minus
the sum of later edits (with the ordered unit transformations included).
The required contact correction K+I is supplied by future edits AFTER each
read plus genuine direct cache reads. The algebra explains how incomparable
future cache charts can help a receiver that never visits them itself.

## Necessary final-facet activity

Suppose A is canonical or vertex-gauge canonical. Its foreign nonzero ports
S_i span the whole real facet Fi, dimension7, in the pinned111-root source.
Consequently B_i must have nonzero coefficient at every port in S_i.

Assume no packet read and no source-cache read is possible at the exact final
Fi. A different public-Y physical trajectory cannot mix with i there: it would
have to end at a distinct same-dimensional facet Fh containing Fi. Thus every
B contribution to trajectory i, including those transferred by other public
records, enters at a proper chart below Fi. There are finitely many such
charts on its ascending path; all are contained in its last proper chart C.
Self operations or outgoing reads after that chart introduce no new support.
B_i is therefore supported on roots in C, a proper subspace of Fi. This
contradicts span(S_i)=Fi. Every receiver whose final facet has no source-cache
history must consequently be visited by an owned packet for an informative
read there.

The clean-X source is reported to visit57 of the111 distinct receiver facets
(geometric equality, not merely chart identifier equality, must be checked).
If the remaining54 count is confirmed, q monotone descending packets can
cover at most q distinct7-facets. Nine cannot cover54. More generally, in
this canonical-terminal interface at least54 packet paths are necessary.
This count is not asserted for arbitrary noncanonical higher-rank codes.

## Explicit limits and controls

A nonmonotone packet can read at0, ascend to a source line, acquire x_j and
return0: its final-minus-earlier payload need not be local at the earlier0
read. Thus the monotonicity hypothesis is essential. Clearing a nonlocal
payload and creating a new one also breaks the terminal relation unless the
discarded information is accounted separately. The lemma does not identify
all virtual future edits with one physically accessible receiver cache chain.

Nonmonotone funded tours, a changed X source supplying histories at missing
facets, new moving owned information, and singular-Schur block terminal codes
are genuine open escape mechanisms. Neither a solver failure nor this scoped
lemma proves a universal BIT supplier impossibility. All complete physical,
ordinary, uniformity, erasure, precision and analytic obligations remain.
