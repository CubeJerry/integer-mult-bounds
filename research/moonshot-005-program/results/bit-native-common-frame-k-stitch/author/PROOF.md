## 1. Exact all-input statement

Let P select coordinates0,...,3 and B* be the S027 irreducible target.
For **every** original X,Y in GF(32)^(111*480), the printed word gives

    P Y'_80 = P Y_80 + sum_(j in S) P B*_(80,j) X_j,
    P Y'_1  = P Y_1  + sum_(j in S) P X_j.                     (1)

All original X values are restored at Hub, and the coefficient of every
original Y is identity in the entire final bank. All additional owners
are initialized, retained at their declared charts and dirty-cleared.
Public owner routes and MOVEs are unchanged.

The four-row computation uses twelve designated aggregate terminal
carriers at each receiver, compared with twenty designated carriers if
all five tiles' four packets were transported separately. All remaining

The packet produced at K370 in the late wave is one field value with

## 2. Joint source charts across the full tile family

For each donor j, order its k_j incident tiles as d_0,...,d_(k_j-1).
Put u_j=P B*_(80,j) X_j and w_j=P X_j. Choose k_j-1 independent four-field
original-input masks

    v_(j,z)=X_j[476-8z : 480-8z],  0<=z<k_j-1.

These use the last four coordinates in distinct eight-coordinate banks.
The first four target rows u_j involve only the first four coordinates
of such banks, apart from the low-coordinate J shift, so u_j and all the
chosen masks are jointly independent. Define

    p_(d_0,j)=u_j+sum_z v_(j,z),
    p_(d_(z+1),j)=v_(j,z),
    q_(d_0,j)=w_j,  q_(d,j)=0 for d!=d_0.                     (2)

Then sum_d p_(d,j)=u_j and sum_d q_(d,j)=w_j. The k_j early four-vectors
are independent: the change from [u_j;v_(j,0);...] is an invertible
triangular block matrix. This is a rooted-tree source assignment (a star
at each donor); no independent tile is allowed to duplicate target-only
source rows.

For each tile use the Cauchy16 matrix A from the preceding construction.
If C_(d,j) indexes its four H flags through j and R_(d,i) its four K flags
through receiver i, set

    U_(d,j)=A[R_(d,80),C_(d,j)]^(-1) p_(d,j),
    V_(d,j)=A[R_(d,1),C_(d,j)]^(-1) q_(d,j).                   (3)

The stacked early U rows have rank4k_j, at most16. Exact pivot columns
complete them to one invertible original-X chart per donor. Each required
row is placed on a distinct actual native source owner. At a shared H,
each tile uses a disjoint cohort of its three donor holders and its own
actual H-to-D carrier. There are at most three such cohorts; each native
donor branch supplies fifteen holders. Their invertible H summations
therefore coexist without overwriting one another's source values.

Only the four nonzero V rows assigned to the first tile are captured for
each donor, at the genuine native line before the joint source chart
changes. They are injected into genuine late Y visits and dirty-cleared.
Zero late rows neither create a free initialized source nor require
independence: their arbitrary original Y holders are preserved by the
global pure-Y inverse. Exactly124 native field caches are needed.

## 3. The common-frame identity at K

For i=80 or1, let K_i be the twelve distinct selected right planes that
contain i. Choose one 4-by12 Cauchy matrix L_i with rows labelled0,...,3
and columns labelled4,...,15 in GF(32). Every four-column submatrix is
invertible. For tile d let R_(d,i) now denote its four columns inside this
common K_i order and put

    C_(d,i) = (L_i[:,R_(d,i)])^(-1).                           (4)

After the ordinary D Cauchy map, postmix its four selected output rows by
C_(d,i). This is an invertible four-row change inside the sixteen original
D owners, so its whole early or late phase remains invertible. Its four
selected packets now have values C_(d,i) z_d, where z_d is the four-vector
sum of the tile's p contributions in the early wave or q contributions in
the late wave.

At each actual K, sum the coded scalar from every incident selected D.
Call this aggregate m_K. The final receiver computes

    sum_K L_i[:,K] m_K
      = sum_d L_i[:,R_(d,i)] C_(d,i) z_d
      = sum_d z_d.                                           (5)

This exact matrix identity establishes compatibility across all shared K
planes. A separate unrelated coefficient choice for each D would not
justify the sum. The verifier checks every inverse in (4), including the
relative ordering of the four K labels.

## 4. Aggregation is a reversible operation on actual owners

At K choose one actual owner visiting the desired receiver. If it already
holds one selected D packet, use that packet as anchor. Otherwise swap
one selected packet onto it. Add every other selected packet into that
anchor holder. All operands are simultaneously present at this K. The
swap and shears are invertible on the original owners, and the destination
now holds exactly m_K without an unrelated original-input baseline.
The remaining original owners retain their side values. No original owner
is overwritten by a supposed free sum, and no new MOVE or K scratch owner
is introduced.

In the early wave, four stationary F80 field owners capture the four
weighted sums in (5) from the twelve actual X terminal carriers. Those
are the only uses of early transformed-X information in final Y outputs.
The complete early source/H/D/K word is then reversed at Hub on every
original X owner.

In the late wave, the twelve aggregate carriers are original Y owners.
The four independent rows of L_1 are completed to an invertible chart on
those twelve owners, then permuted into output positions0,...,3 at F1.
The remaining eight chart rows and all other Y owners are retained. The
full pure-Y word includes every shared H shear, coded D map, K aggregation
and F1 decoder. Its reverse is applied at Ground before the late wave,
so arbitrary original Y is preserved with coefficient identity globally.

The late source rows use only the w_j coordinates. Their side outputs are
tracked explicitly. An exact symbolic support calculation selects four
Y80 owners with no late X dependence; the four early F80 caches are added
there and cleared. No original Y is assumed zero in the final replay.

Every original private mask appears in the early p contributions twice
and cancels in (5). No late source row uses a mask, and no other early

independent of all116 original private mask fields. The author all-column
