# Stitching overlapping D4 tiles with source masks that vanish from every Y output

**Proved literal two-tile construction on the authenticated native atlas.
No complete supplier, full receiver endpoint or new κ.**

The new step is a joint source chart for two D tiles sharing real H planes
and donor banks. Merely duplicating the same four early source target rows
would give eight dependent outgoing original-X coordinates and could not
be completed to an invertible source chart. Four independent original-input
mask coordinates per shared donor resolve that issue. They cancel at a
joint F80 capture and are absent from every final Y output. The masks are
not uncharged initialized inputs: they are arbitrary original X coordinates
which are restored at Hub.

## 1. Precise result and authenticated geometry

Work over GF(32), modulus t^5+t^2+1, on the exact b=480 atlas pinned in
`output/RESULT.json`. Let S=D12 union D99. These are native D4 charts with
eighteen distinct donors and the six shared donors

    5, 7, 42, 43, 44, 45.

Their shared left H planes are 106,113,118,119. Their duals are D116 and
D111. Both contain native receivers 80 and 1. Their shared right K planes
are 340,341,370,371. All left and right planes used by either tile are full
unselected planes: each D flag has one actual early and one actual late
owner, and each native line has fifteen actual early departures and
fifteen genuine late Y visits on each incident H branch.

Let P select input/output coordinates 0,...,3. Let B* be the S027
irreducible endpoint target, whose receiver80 row blocks are the ordered
I+J, I+E, I+T_1,...,I+T_59,I construction. For every original X,Y in
GF(32)^(111*480), the printed word satisfies

    P Y'_80 = P Y_80 + sum_(j in S) P B*_(80,j) X_j,
    P Y'_1  = P Y_1  + sum_(j in S) P X_j.                     (1)

Every original X is restored at Hub. Every original Y coefficient is
identity in the complete final bank. The word has no added MOVE, and all
extra owners are initialized and charged for dirty clearing. The other
Y rows have an explicit, unprescribed X response. This is an eighteen-donor
local aggregate, not all sixty-two/sixty foreign donors of the receivers.

For each shared donor j, the **entire** final Y bank is independent of
the four original coordinates X_j[476:480]. This is a global statement
about this word's actual output response, not merely selected-row
cancellation. Original X owners retain those coordinates after restoration.

## 2. Exact source masks and why the joint chart is invertible

For each donor define the four-vectors

    u_j=P B*_(80,j) X_j,   w_j=P X_j,   v_j=X_j[476:480].

The four rows defining u_j are independent. They do not involve
coordinates 476,...,479; hence [u_j;v_j] has rank eight. At a shared donor
assign the early target contributions

    p_(12,j)=u_j+v_j,   p_(99,j)=v_j.                           (2)

Their sum is u_j. The linear transform from [u_j;v_j] to [u_j+v_j;v_j]
is invertible, so all eight rows in (2) are independent. At a donor belonging
to just one tile, assign p_(d,j)=u_j once.

As in the preceding paid pair tile, use the Cauchy16 matrix
A_rc=1/(r XOR (16+c)). For each tile d let C_(d,j) index its four left H
planes containing j, and R_(d,80) its four right K planes containing 80.
The actual early source rows are

    U_(d,j)=A[R_(d,80),C_(d,j)]^(-1) p_(d,j).                  (3)

Each minor is nonsingular. Consequently the stacked U rows have rank
eight at each shared donor and rank four at the other twelve donors.
`build.py` finds exact pivot columns, replaces those rows of an identity
chart by U, and verifies the resulting whole active chart is invertible.
It then permutes those parities onto distinct actual source owners.
Every step is compiled into literal binary ADD gates.

The rank issue is real. Assigning alpha*u and (1+alpha)*u with alpha=2
would preserve the aggregate u, but their eight source rows have rank
only four. Such a target-only lift cannot be an invertible eight-coordinate
original-X chart. In contrast, (2) uses the minimum four independent
kernel directions needed to make a rank-eight source map whose selected
sum has rank four.

## 3. Shared H owners and shared K routing

On a shared H, each of the three donors supplies one distinct original-X
owner per selected tile. If the actual H-to-d carrier originated at that
donor, it is chosen as that donor's source holder. All other holders are
chosen while excluding the carriers reserved for either tile and every
already chosen holder. Each native branch has fifteen choices, while at
most two are required. Thus the two three-input cohorts are disjoint.

For each cohort an invertible H shear sums its three source values onto
its own actual H-to-d carrier. The disjoint cohorts prove that the two H
shears coexist without overwriting either other's source. At D12 and D99,
the two Cauchy maps act on disjoint original owner sets. For each shared K,
a **single** partial bijection places both relevant D outputs on distinct
actual X owners visiting F80. The partial bijection is completed to a
permutation of the full set of involved owner labels.

All eight chosen packets are physically present at F80. Initialize only
four stationary F80 field owners, and add the corresponding packet from
each tile into each one. Equation (2) cancels every shared mask during
this capture. Exclusive donors contribute once, yielding the first sum
in (1). These are actual paid additions from real original X owners.
No individual masked F packet is later used separately in any Y output.

The entire early original-X word is then inverted at Hub. The joint
source chart, shared H shears, D maps and K permutations are all included
in this literal inverse. Thus arbitrary original mask values are restored
along with every other original X value.

## 4. Late source assignment uses no masks

Late source rows need not be independent of the early rows or of each
other. For a shared donor set

    q_(12,j)=w_j,   q_(99,j)=0,                               (4)

and for an exclusive donor set q_(d,j)=w_j. Let R_(d,1) index the four
right planes through receiver1, and define

    V_(d,j)=A[R_(d,1),C_(d,j)]^(-1) q_(d,j).                  (5)

Capture each nonzero row of V at the true native source line **before**
the joint original-X chart changes. Exactly 72 field captures occur:
four rows for each of the eighteen distinct donors. A zero row creates
no owner and performs no injection; its original Y holder remains an
arbitrary original Y value, which is handled by the common ground inverse.

At the late native line visit, add the captured row into a genuine Y
holder and dirty-clear the cache. Late H holder cohorts are selected with
the same reservation rule as the early cohorts and are pairwise disjoint.
Their shears, the two late D Cauchy maps, and one simultaneous partial
permutation at every shared K deliver eight packets to F1. Put the D12
packets in positions0,...,3, the D99 packets in positions4,...,7, and add
the latter into the former. Equation (4) gives the second sum in (1).

The native late rows use only X_j[0:4]. In particular they use none of the
private mask coordinates. All early X-to-Y information is restricted to
the four already aggregated F80 caches, where masks have canceled. These
are the only two kinds of X-dependent captures in the word. This proves
that no private mask coordinate affects **any** final Y owner.

Unselected native Y holders retain side outputs; they are not free zero
inputs and are not discarded. All potentially affected late fields stay
within the 96 selected native holder labels until permutations, except
for at most four added final sums. An exact symbolic support computation
treats the native cache bits as independent and selects four certainly
clean Y80 fields for the final early additions. This is checked by the
full original-input replay. The actual word has 84 X-dependent final
field rows, including the unprescribed side outputs.

Delete the additions from X-dependent stationary caches from the late
word. Every remaining operation is an invertible pure-Y ADD or permutation.
Apply its complete reverse word at Ground. The final Y coefficient is
then identity globally, including all side outputs, while the X-dependent
terms are exactly those above. The final four F80 caches are added into
the clean target rows and dirty-cleared. All locality checks apply to
every gate operand and stage; the two source waves and shared charts are
not replaced by abstract matrix wires.

## 5. A rejected late-mask variant and full-output comparison

The initial candidate assigned late contributions w+v and v on the shared
donors. It had the same selected targets after F1 cancellation. However,
the native line injections occur on distinct original Y owners, and the
subsequent pure-Y map is invertible. The four independent mask directions
at each of six shared donors therefore survive as rank24 in the **whole**
Y output, even though the selected rows cancel them.

The preferred construction uses (4) and removes that response entirely.
`--late-masks` reproduces the rejected variant, with the same four-owner
joint F80 capture so the comparison isolates the unnecessary late masks.
The two variants have identical pure-Y maps and identical early Y
responses. Their full B_actual difference is supported only on the 24
original mask coordinates and has exact field rank24 (binary rank120).
The preferred word has zero response on those columns. The companion
mask-response check and the independent bridge review record this full
output fact. This is not a claimed reduction of the final supplier's
return rank: neither partial word is a full supplier.
