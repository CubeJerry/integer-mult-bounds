## Independently derived accounting

The reviewer separately derived the odometer cost: a carry through j trailing
coordinates occurs at most `D/L^j` times. Synchronizing/resetting/restoring those
catalogue axes costs `O(j L h poly(ell))`. The resulting geometric sum is
`O(D h poly(ell))`, with a separately included final carry. The fixed header
scans then cost `O(D g h poly(ell))`. No D-sized Count or O(L) charge on each
noncarry step is required.

The reviewer also independently checked `S<=gU<=UH` and `Ut<=UB`, so sparse keep
and radix logs fit `O(DB)` transport backing without an additional sparsity
premise. The original dense scan is still a separate DB time term.

For the conditional complete-context amortization, the review checked that
`log(setup+gC)=O(ell_original+log p+log g)` whereas
`log D=Theta(p^beta log p)`. Under `beta>1-epsilon` and the stated uniform bounds,
the ratio tends to zero. The final statement explicitly limits the source sum
to at most g axis keys in one delivered context. It does not apply that result
to missing/truncated contexts or unpriced sparse-tail delivery.
