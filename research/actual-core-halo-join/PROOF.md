## 4. Literal linear signed-format bridges

For n/2^q, `lift_word` checks sign and magnitude, rejects negative zero,
writes P-q low zero bits, traverses magnitude backwards, and writes the
remaining high zeros/sign extension. Negative values use complement plus
one with a finite carry flag. Every emitted low prefix of r bits equals the
desired integer modulo 2^r; (1) supplies a genuine high sign bit. Hence

\[
 X=n\,2^{P-q}                                                 \tag{3}
\]

exactly. Forward restoration leaves the source at its next component; the
whole field is not rewound per word. End markers control the field traversal,
without decrementing a log(T)-bit counter for each payload bit.

For a returned X, `return_word` reads its sign by a fixed-width scan and
returns to the low bit. Complement-plus-one gives its **unsigned** absolute
magnitude, including the minimum signed word. It drops the first min(d,w)
bits, retains low h-1 bits in one scratch role, and rejects nonzero high
overflow. Reversing scratch gives big-endian magnitude. Zero always has
positive sign. Therefore

\[
 Y=\operatorname{sign}(X)\lfloor |X|/2^d\rfloor,
 \qquad d=P+U-q.                                             \tag{4}
\]

Thus -3 at d=1 returns -1, and d>=w returns positive zero. There is no signed
arithmetic right shift or signed absolute-value overflow. The full A is
applied once after the entire Core. The halo separately applies its own
per-axis normalizers, so the branches have the same normalized target.

Every payload decision observes one current cell or a finite sign/carry flag.
Widths and loops use actual short Count operations on one fixed 64-role
pool. No payload magnitude is decoded into a host integer. Constructors are
sealed. One fixed native input role and one reused scalar scratch role
suffice; scratch and short Count bodies are erased.

On rejection the lift restores its B-source origin and clears incomplete
native output. The return converter restores its native source origin and
clears the failed B-target; it does not roll back overwritten target data.
The composite `mutate` has a valid-domain success contract: if the Core
supplier raises before returning, lifted input remains for caller cleanup.
No automatic retry/rollback theorem is claimed. Successful cleanup compares
entire historical private bodies with their initial native configurations,
not just live prefixes or a false all-cells-blank condition.

## 6. Numerical implication at the actual endpoints

Every principal E_i section has the same row-gap bound. Its normalized exact
inverse has maximum component-l1 norm at most `1/(a_i delta_i)<=1`, and their
tensor is a contraction. Whole-field Euclidean defect epsilon implies

two final component truncations to obtain

\[
 E_{seam}\le 2\epsilon/A+2\,2^{-q}.                          \tag{5}
\]

The actual normalized output radius before final truncation is at most
R_in+2epsilon/A. Toward-zero truncation cannot increase it. It suffices to
take integer R_halo>=2R_in, for example ceil(2R_in), and
`2epsilon/A<=R_in`. This shared carrier differs from
the whole-field R0 in (2). There is no additional sqrt(T) in (5).

Put `tau_i=(1-delta_i)^J_i/delta_i` and `s_i=2W_i+1`. The existing #6880
kept-row comparison applies to these exact principal sections: kept rows
are farther than b_i from the complement. The actual shrinking recurrence
supplies `eta_i=(s_i R_halo+2)2^-q/delta_i`. Here every source coefficient
is **real rational**, as in the retained narrow Reference. One inward-rounded
real coefficient has error less than 2^-q; multiplying a complex input costs
that error times its combined component-l1 radius, without a second factor
two. The added two pays the final real/imaginary truncations. Inward rounding
also preserves the absolute row-mass bound and hence the actual intermediate
R_halo invariant. This eta formula is not asserted for independently rounded
general complex coefficients. The inherited disjoint splice is

\[
 E_{keep}\le 2\epsilon/A+2\,2^{-q}
                      +2R_{in}\sum_i\tau_i/a_i,
\]
\[
 E_{halo}\le\sum_i\eta_i+R_{halo}\sum_i\tau_i/a_i,
 \qquad \|\text{return}-\bigotimes_i(H_i^{-1}/a_i)x\|_{\infty,*}
       \le\max(E_{keep},E_{halo}).                            \tag{6}
\]

Equations (5)-(6) close the retained Core premise with the actual returned

\[
 \ell_b=1+\lceil\log_2(2+h+w+P+q+d)\rceil.

\[

\[
 C_{Core}+A_{bind}+T_{ref}+O\!\left(
