## 1. What the hypothesis actually supplies

IP4 controls independent input rows, total active pencil-row volume,
ordinary maps, work rows, finite algebra coordinates and actual constructor
work. It also supplies an explicit diagonal multiplier and a small known
exceptional set at every spectral tuple. These are the requirements needed
by the shared-pencil and repair callers. Ordinary maps act at the same
physical address; they cannot hide a spectral conversion or arbitrary
uncompiled permutation. This restriction is material.

All `3^m` spectral tuples can be checked in singly exponential work in a
polynomial of m. At each tuple the explicit finite row program can be
evaluated on its full input matrix using polynomially many operations in
the listed coordinate algebra. Multiplication tables, coefficient lists,
row maps and exception lists are bounded by the hypothesis. Dense basis
arithmetic and algebra-axiom checks cost a polynomial in those list sizes,
not in the cardinality of the entire finite algebra. No enumeration of all
scalar values as an alphabet truth table is needed or licensed.

The dovetail totality guard is sound. A generic valid constructor with a
fixed `c_* sqrt(m)` stage-volume bound supplies the finitely many initial
cases outside IP4's promise. For large m, the compact branch and its exact
checks halt within `2^(m^C')` work. By that time a fair two-branch dovetail
has used only the same order of work and space. A generic branch finishing
earlier cannot have emitted more bits than this charged running time.
Therefore the guard preserves the claimed eventual profile, even though
the unknown numerical cutoff need not be available to the resulting
machine. All emitted programs are checked before acceptance.

The F4-valued final multipliers make every nonzero discrepancy a unit and
give the already checked two-predictor Hermite identity. A three-power
check-node algebra of degree O(R) has explicit unit differences in
characteristic two. It increases a `2^(m^C)` description by only a fixed
power. Temporary coordinates are genuinely charged; a one-output ABP
whose other rows are unused is not promoted to a full-rate evaluator.

## 2. Rational Walsh versus characteristic-two interchange

The rational Newton/division predictor is used for the numerical Walsh
template only. Its count divisions and inverse gaps are never reduced
into the characteristic-two data algebra. IP4 supplies a distinct
characteristic-two interchange construction, with the reviewed order-three
closure implementing the actual permutation target on every basis
coordinate. This separation avoids assuming that the rational predictor
has a modular specialization.

The Walsh template's endpoint and representative changes are the fixed
binary column maps and signs enumerated in B's map audit. Their physical
movement is charged through the completed interchange caller. The rational
row maps and repair tables have polynomial-m heights and dimensions, and
the explicit frequency and column tables have `2^O(m) poly(m)` size.
Consequently they lie inside `2^(m^C)` together with the IP4 interchange
data. Word-indexed arithmetic alone is not being asserted to have the same
physical tape cost; the separate accepted movement compiler is used.

For a stored rational coefficient a/b, ordinary integer division produces
its dyadic approximation at the requested numerical precision. Its
denominator and numerator lengths are in the finite envelope. Division
has polynomial setup cost in those lengths and the precision, already of
the type paid by the accepted setup threshold. Record multiplication
retains the fixed short multiplier. The extra operator error is bounded
by coefficient approximation error times the finite row/array norms and
the charged number of occurrences. Those norms and counts are finite
template data, so the accepted precision ledger absorbs them with a fixed
polynomial enlargement of P and its threshold. No additional tower is
introduced by rational coefficient preparation.

The new data characteristic does not change the geometric address field.
The characteristic-two label/routing note uses its separate rational Gram
coordinates and compatible address radix, already charged in the finite
envelope. Coefficient denominators of the Walsh row maps are not extra
geometric address maps.

## 3. Profile, eligibility and comparison

Under IP4, the full finite data, actual constructor and retained workspace
fit `P_m<=2^(m^C)`. The accepted distance choice `d=Theta(J)` and complete
label invoices pass at

    log2 m_J = O(J^3 log2(J+2)).

Using the independently reviewed physical contract and common-cap
schedule then gives

    log2 D_J, log2 L(N_J) <= 2^(C J^3 log2(J+2)).

This pays generation, the space coefficient through chunking, physical
assembly, restart factors and the fixed absolute prime-shape cutoff. The
exact shape tests and baseline keep the machine total without assuming
a numerical value of that cutoff.

To check actual eligibility, put `s=log2 L(n)`, `z=log2 s` and choose an
available dyadic J within a factor of two of

    c (z/log2(z+2))^(1/3)

with c a sufficiently small absolute constant. Then
`C J^3 log2(J+2)<=z/2` for large s. Both the log-prefactor and
`log2 L(N_J)` are at most `sqrt(s)`, so the selected candidate is eligible.
Its exponent loss contributes `O(s/J)`, which dominates that prefactor.
Thus the conditional upper envelope is

    log2 H(n) = O(s (log2 log2(s+2)/log2(s+2))^(1/3)).

The separately proved formula construction has a different actual profile:
`P_m<=2^(2^(C(log m)^2))`. For every fixed C0, that displayed envelope is
eventually larger than `2^(m^C0)`; it does not establish IP4. Its accepted
composition instead gives the unconditional upper-bound scale

    s (log2 log2 s)^(1/3)/(log2 s)^(1/6).

The IP4 conditional scale is smaller by a factor of order
`(log2 s)^(1/6)`. Both remain above `s^beta` for every fixed beta<1 as
upper-bound scales, and neither establishes a lower bound on the actual
running time or reaches the polynomial-s overhead benchmark.

## 4. Precise scope refinement

A characteristic-two hypothesis is not literally implied by a dyadic
`Z[1/2]` representation valid in every odd characteristic: that base has
no characteristic-two specialization. IP4 requires a representation in
one specific characteristic, while a field-uniform integral hypothesis
has a different requirement. The relevant canonical order-three integral
predictor has 3 inverted and does admit characteristic-two specialization.
B corrected the earlier phrase that IP4 was logically weaker after this
review identified the issue. It does not affect the conditional implication.

The profile of the generic order-three constructor remains a valid record
of that route. It should not be presented as the best unconditional result
after the separate integral formula construction has been accepted.

No new finite replay is necessary for this composition review: the
order-three matrix identities, labels, both repair directions, exact
finite unrollings and physical common-cap invariants have already been
independently replayed. This review checked the new totality and profile
implications directly, rather than treating numerical samples as a proof
