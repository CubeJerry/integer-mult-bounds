## Current complete implication reviewed by C

For `L(n)=1+log2 n` and `s(n)=log2 L(n)`, the accepted fixed-field
construction gives the programme's current upper envelope

    T(n)=O(n sqrt(L(n)) H(n)),
    H(n)=2^O(s(n) (log2 log2 s(n)/log2 s(n))^(1/3)),
    visited cells=O(n).

The new count-bit formula explicitly supplies the former IP4 premise.
The complete independent gate is
`REVIEW-A-FIXED-FIELD-EXTRACTION.md`. It uses the reviewed order-three
closure, rational numerical Walsh template and proved physical/common-cap
contracts. The fixed source prime-shape cutoff is retained without a
numerical value. No Weak-ETH assumption enters the upper bound.

This is a mathematical algorithm/profile construction, not an executed
complete tape machine or a proof-assistant build. The displayed scale
does not reach `H=O(s^alpha)`, `log H=O(s^beta)` for fixed beta<1, or the
constant-overhead endpoint. No benchmark kappa is assigned. Comparisons
are between explicit programme envelopes, not with an unspecified
published o(1).

## Remaining gaps

The all-odd-characteristic dyadic compact-predictor hypothesis remains
open. A cheap physical correction for the separate nilpotent spatial-rank
route also remains open. Neither is an assumption of the accepted
fixed-field theorem. The restricted barriers do not imply a lower bound
on all overhead algorithms. Better routing, module compression or a
sharper variable-parameter physical compiler remain possible future
directions, with no additional theorem asserted here.
