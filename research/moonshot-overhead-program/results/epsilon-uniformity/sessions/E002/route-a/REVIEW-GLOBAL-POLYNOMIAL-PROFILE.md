## 1. Accepted conclusion

For L(n)=1+log2 n and x=log2 L(n), the composed construction gives one
fixed finite-alphabet multitape full-product machine with

    T(n) <= n sqrt(L(n)) 2^{O(x^(3/4)(log2(x+2))^(1/4))},
    cells ever visited = O(n),

with absolute constants and an exact fallback at all exceptional lengths.
It gives every fixed exponent beta>3/4 in the weaker envelope
`2^{O(x^beta)}`. It does not establish the log-free beta=3/4 envelope,
polynomial-x overhead, a constant overhead or a lower bound.

The substantive new input is a polynomial-m *proportional* processor while
finite construction remains as large as `2^{m^C}`. The global argument does
not silently reuse the old expanded coefficient as a polynomial quantity.

## 2. Finite family and compact execution: P2

The strengthened threshold `log2 m>=2^42 J^3 log2 J` dominates the predecessor
Q threshold and the larger power-of-two predictor's complete call invoice.
The accepted characteristic-two pencil, finite four-child contraction,
three-transform swap word, leading padding and occupied-reserve calls are
all inside Lambda_I. The independent I5 calculation supplies the half
margin at rho=1/2+1/(2J). The rational Walsh quarter margin remains the
predecessor Q5--Q7 one. Trying power-of-two exponents u in m=2^u+1 overshoots
the first sufficient log-size by only a fixed factor, so
`log2 m=O(J^3 log2(J+2))` is actual constructor growth.

The rational address dictionary is polynomial in m in count, dimension and
bit height. The complete unit list includes pivots, diagonal numerators,
denominators and supplied inverses; its product has polynomial bit length.
C's compatible-radix lemma consequently gives numeric b polynomial in m.
The multidigit block supplies the possibly exponentially many module rows
using only polynomially many address digits. No b>W assumption is retained.

All exponentially wide cyclic row factors are executed from polynomial-size
path/grade descriptions. Coefficients and module masks are computed on
bounded-width counters, without scanning a 2^{m^C}-entry table per datum.
Optional exhaustive validation fits the stated singly exponential setup
bound because module dimensions, formula-degree expansions and frequency
case counts are each singly exponential in a polynomial of m. Validation
archives are discarded and are not an instruction-dispatch structure.

This last distinction is necessary: replacing all ordinary finite opcodes
by an expanded transition-list search would invalidate P2--P7. The written
fixed dispatcher and compact-plan scans avoid that failure explicitly.

## 3. The physical interchanger: P3

The C1--C5 factor executor processes each coefficient slice after its one
spatial Q_j operation. It never repeats the charged spatial operation for
each monomial path. Its masked grade rotations and finite small row maps
use constant tape count and polynomial-m actual work per payload volume.

The characteristic-two repair is exact on arbitrary data over the one fixed
extension E. Only the known discrepancies are elements of F4*. The cube
identity is therefore valid even though the data themselves lie in E.
The check and lift layouts are `H0 tensor I_(32w)` with the recorded zero
padding; the converter regrouping acts on finite row coordinates and does
not require zero initial module data.

HS/HA prices the actual rational address operations by bit height. It
retains every field-permutation call in the source Bruhat factorization.
Small radix and batch extraction add polynomial-m work, while their padding
is already charged to the complete spatial coefficient. Temporary keys
and spectral tags are stripped before a recursive call.

The corrected individual-child fraction matters. Grouping identical module
calls preserves their total weighted invoice but can give child fraction
up to `2/N_label`, rather than `2/W_I`. Since `N_label>=3`, the bound 2/3
still gives a fixed geometric parked-volume series. Finite repair children
have fraction 1/16. Full-volume reserve descents clear the main arrays first
and park only their descriptors. One E-coordinate encoding is maintained at
all depths. Thus P3's time and visited-space bounds have polynomial-m
coefficients, with no alphabet-degree or metadata product across recursion.

## 4. Outer physical routing: P4--P5

The source guarded-XOR primitive requires actual offset/control arithmetic,
not merely a computable address map. P4 supplies a uniform finite grammar
with bill `m^c k^e`. Its guard is larger than the additive-log guard in A's
outer audit but is still valid. Indeed

    2^G >= m^c (2k)^(e+4)

for the displayed P4 choice, after harmless absolute enlargements. The
fraction `80k 2^-G` therefore pays fixed-degree destination calculations and
exceptional stable sorting. Complete target fibres pay the ordinary prefix
work; a long prefix is advanced by an amortized counter rather than reread
per payload symbol. If an active slot is shorter than G, its direct bit cost
is at most `GV`, already inside a polynomial-m/log-k coefficient.

The guarded-XOR, eight-shift and position-permutation suppliers use the new
P3 interchange, keeping the source borrowed-coordinate restoration. The
controlled-block and Beneš construction has only the source's fixed number
of additional logarithmic factors. The numerical record width is an
absolute multiple of k, so O(k)-bit exceptional tags cost only a constant
factor while present. They are not recursively retained as payload.

The source outer control table degrees are absolute: at most seven axes of
width at most `20 ceil(log2 k)` give at most `2^140 k^140` entries. This
supplies an actual fixed exponent for table construction and guards.
It does not substitute an arbitrary user program into a claim of
`poly(k)` time. Prime-shape work, phase series, square-root bisection and
short products remain explicit fixed algorithms.

## 5. Numerical Walsh: P6 and the padding boundary

The amended S3 and P6 correctly retain the active-width toll
`1+a^rho`. The exact interchanger is proved first, then called by numerical
Walsh column changes. There is no circular recurrence and no declaration
that those address changes are ordinary linear scans.

The exact source `walsh.tex` pads the leading spectator count once to a
multiple of W_F^j for the maximum piece depth. Each internal split then
has *exact* child volume V/W_F. Consequently P6 as displayed, with
coefficient `sigma_F/W_F`, is valid. The factor of at most two for initial
leading padding is in the initial physical volume, not multiplied at every
level. If an alternate implementation instead padded locally at every
node, its recurrence would need `2 sigma_F/W_F`; P2's quarter margin would
still suffice, but that is not needed for the declared source layout.
This distinction resolves a potential apparent missing factor of two.

On a fixed number of tapes, the source's W_F aligned stream roles may be
represented by moving the finite residue coordinate `[W_F]` past the
leading spectator coordinate, then reversing that move after the call.
Pad only the W_F coordinate to a power of two and move its O(log W_F)
binary digits; temporary padding is removed before children. Alternatively
stable short-key partitions do the same grouping. This costs polynomial m
per volume because W_F is polynomial m, and does not sort by or attach the
unbounded leading spectator index. It preserves the divisibility
`r/W_F` by `W_F^(j-1)` required in the source recursion.

The rational normalization was checked in source `binary.tex`: the child
axis transforms supply the full slot-width power of two, and converter
normalizations cancel around the small row table. Thus W2 coefficient
heights are polynomial m independently of the slot width. On-demand
selection emits the chosen exact rational matrix; W1 applies it to k-bit
records in `k poly(m)` steps. There is no full spectral-table scan and no
use of finite-field arithmetic for numerical rational coefficients.

The source's general k-by-k products retain its fixed O(k log k) tape
multiplier. The slower elementary long/short rational algorithm is used
only when one operand has polynomial-m height, and the threshold k>=m^K
makes its complete bill polynomial m times k. No quadratic k^2 product
is substituted into a per-record Fourier phase operation.

Since A_F>=m and W_F<=m^C, root padding W_F^j is at most `(1+s)^C` for
an absolute exponent. The available `2^(k-s)>=2^(k/2)` spectator rows pay
this uniformly. This is stronger than merely fixing m first and then
asserting a parameter-dependent polynomial. The geometric recurrence,
base-A pieces, position permutations and source short butterflies give the
stated fixed logarithmic factors with polynomial-m coefficient.

W3 bounds the local logarithmic norm gain and the *number* of whole-array
rounding events. Its first-common-ancestor argument includes intermediate
partial sums; completed intervening transforms are unitary. Above a
polynomial-m k threshold the accumulated amplification fits an absolute
multiple of k precision. The proof controls integer width, fractional width
and signed exact intermediates, so this is not only a final-error claim.
The source fixed norm and recovery budgets may therefore be used unchanged.

## 6. Full digit profile and additive costs: P7--P8

The outer integer construction still has `Nk=Theta(u)` and
`k=Theta(L(u))`. Its one unknown prime-shape cutoff is absolute and separate
from the new parameter-dependent precision/preparation threshold. Actual
shape tests and exact fallback make its unspecified numerical value
irrelevant to correctness.

The source synchronized short transforms restore every borrowed occupied
coordinate, and the unchanged final norm/error budget gives distance less
than 1/2 from the integer convolution coefficients. Rounding followed by
carry propagation yields the exact complete 2u-bit product. This retains
the pinned source short-transform theorem as a mathematical input; this
review does not claim to rederive all of its Fourier identities.

The proportional profile in P7 is polynomial m, while construction and
remaining non-volume work have the separate envelope
`2^{m^K} L(u)^{m^K}`. Coarse branch counting is sufficient for this additive
bound. Repeated ordinary scans and arithmetic on physical node descriptors
are already charged to their volume; a fixed template is not regenerated
exponentially once per independent payload record. Treating such an action
as additive would be wrong, and the compact processor explicitly excludes it.

The fixed fourth logarithmic power is absorbed with the reserved
`1/(2J)` slack. Its exact supremum costs O(J^4), and J<=m, so no expanded
coefficient returns through that absorption. S1 then absorbs the additive
profile above `log2 u>=m^{K'}` for a larger absolute K'. Its generation
space remains charged to visited cells even when the archive is erased.

## 7. One machine, all lengths, and common space: P9--P11

The dyadic rule `J^4 h<=q`, J=2^h, is an actual finite integer rule computed
from the input length. Maximality supplies the two-sided comparison

    J=Theta((x/log2(x+2))^(1/4)).

Its profile scale Z=J^3 log2(J+2) is o(x), irrespective of how large the
fixed constructive constants are. Thus log m and log r are O(Z), while
`log2 n=2^x-1`. The logarithm of all allowed generation/setup work is at
most `2^{O(Z)}(1+x)=2^{o(x)}`. Construction, retained data, repeated allowed
setup and all applicability thresholds consequently fit the prescribed
caps at every sufficiently large n. Multiplication by the r^2 chunk-call
count changes this logarithm by only O(Z) and is harmless.

The wrapper has `r>=8m^K`, `u=ceil(n/r)` and eventual `r<=n`. Hence its
core bound is `m^K u<=n/8+m^K<=n/4`. Input, output, assembly and the
separately bounded preparation occupy a fixed number of marked regions.
Those regions are reused across chunk products and fallback. Caps stop an
attempt before crossing the mark; discarded work still lies in the same
visited union. This proves uniform O(n) visited space rather than an
O_J(n) space statement with an unnormalized constant.

The r^2 exact chunk products and actual positional assembly obey
`r^2u<=2rn` and O(nr) additional work. P11 therefore has log-overhead
`O(log m)+O(x/J)`, which is exactly the claimed 3/4-power logarithmic scale.
The candidate is eventually below the n^2 step cap. Before then, capped
attempts and exact schoolbook fallback halt correctly within uniform space;
the finite exceptional length range is absorbed by an absolute eventual
big-O constant. No free envelope infimum, machine advice or unavailable
cutoff oracle is used.

## 8. Corrections and residual scope

No mathematical correction is required to the reviewed Theorem P bytes.
Two points should be preserved in any later edit: P6's missing-looking
padding factor is justified by *global* spectator padding, and numerical
S3 must retain its active-width routing toll. Neither simplification may be
silently removed. The grouped spatial volume bound must remain 2/N_label.

The current proof is enough for the stated written-theorem admission.
A variable-arity scheme, an improved incidence invoice, a sharper space
coefficient or an implemented complete tape multiplier would be new work
and is not used here. Component exact replays support the corresponding
finite identities only; they do not experimentally execute this astronomical
complete algorithm or establish practical thresholds.
