## What is assumed, and what is proved

RV-I requires a correct shared-pencil tape processor with recursive child
bit-volume mass at most a fixed power of the direction dimension times
`(1+R/N)(1+mr*/N)`, and the corresponding **visited-cell** workspace bound.
It includes both free-rectangle and low-rank delivery, compact uniform
construction, actual ordinary work, occupied auxiliary restoration and the
explicit main/reserve/finite-repair volume bounds. Merely counting outputs
or changing address-field widths cannot establish this contract.

RV-W requires a correct numerical shared-pencil processor with normalized
Fourier-axis call count bounded by a fixed power of its direction dimension
times `1+R_f/N+mR_c/N`. Its remaining scalar work, address calls, coefficient
heights, visited array regions and effective applicability tests are bounded
by fixed polynomials in the local parameter. Address calls are charged
through the completed exact interchanger. The source occupied batch and
finite-repair interfaces must remain valid.

The theorem proves exact rectangle counts, their optimized parameters, the
two distinct recursive schedules, actual preparation absorption, absolute
precision, outer exact recovery and common linear-space/all-length transfer
from these local contracts. Neither hypothesis is a restatement of the
multiplier's desired runtime. Neither has been implemented or proved here.
The counted rectangle outputs do not automatically align input records in
the common coset or spectral layouts.
