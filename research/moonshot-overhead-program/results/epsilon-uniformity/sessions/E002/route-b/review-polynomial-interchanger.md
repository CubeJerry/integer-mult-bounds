## Decisive checks

1. The polynomial batch/label controls are not conflated with the enormous
   independent module coordinate. A succinct coefficient factor operates
   on the module coordinate by its proved path/index rules. The source
   spatial endpoint maps are independent of that module index.
2. Applying all scalar monomial terms occurs inside the local row map;
   it does not execute each spatial query anew for each term. Source
   first/last endpoint sharing therefore retains its recursive call count.
3. When identical spatial calls on module rows are grouped, their volumes
   add. The weighted recursive invoice is unchanged, but the space ratio
   is at most `2/N_label`, not `2/W`. C uses the correct weaker ratio,
   bounded by `2/3`; geometric parking still holds.
4. Sort keys, comparison masks and role/path metadata are stripped before
   every paid child. Their polynomial local time/space can therefore be
   charged without inflating the child's recurrent volume.
5. Check padding is explicit: with `R=32 B w`, genuine check rows are
   `32 floor(B/32) w`, and zero padding fills exactly `R/32=Bw` rows.
   A converter phase is `R/16`; the `(copy16,half2)` regrouping becomes
   the child's 32-valued duplicate coordinate. The module coordinate is
   preserved and every original input remains independent.
6. The changed count window is paid. `D_pred<=24 sqrt m` and
   `R_pencil<=64 D_I(1+lambda)` give the first term coefficient 12,288
   in `8 D_pred R_pencil`; the leaf term is at most `3 sqrt m`.
   The claimed `2^14` and later `2^17` constants safely cover both.
7. The multidigit leading block has sufficient width for W and at most
   a factor-two row-padding loss. The base width and direct digit work
   are polynomial m under the fixed small radix. The literal binary-to-
   radix loss `<b^2` is polynomial m and is paid in I7, not discarded.
8. One fixed macro processor uses the compact grammar and endpoint data.
   Scanning an unrelated expanded transition list is excluded explicitly.
   The fixed E coordinate encoding is retained through spatial children;
   no repeated scalar-field extension is charged as free.

The recurrence I4 follows with local coefficient `m^K` and its retained

## Remaining limits

The numerical rational Walsh processor and its coefficient norms are a
different algebra and still need their own proof. The full integer assembly,
source outer permutation costs, parameter selection and common O(n) cap
must compose before a new multiplication theorem is asserted.

For a later variable-local-parameter route, I7 cannot be substituted at
`m=m(e)` and iterated unchanged: its polynomial-m padding and workspace
factors can compound into a fixed positive power loss. Such a route needs
a new volume and field-encoding ledger. It is outside this review.
