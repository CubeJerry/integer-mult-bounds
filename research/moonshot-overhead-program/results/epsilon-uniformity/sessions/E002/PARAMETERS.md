## Dependency order

1. Finite count formulas, geometry and full-rate factorization supply
   exact identities and call counts from the predecessor programme.
2. Height-priced maps, small radix, succinct factors and on-demand repair
   give the complete exact interchange supplier.
3. Interchange supplies guarded XOR and binary column changes. Their
   active-width cost remains in the numerical Walsh recurrence.
4. Rational scaling/on-demand coefficients and absolute precision supply
   the separate numerical Walsh arithmetic and error bounds.
5. Short-Fourier/CRT/carry assembly yields the separated full profile;
   direct selection and chunking give one common visited-space bound.

No unproved compact-executor or setup-profile hypothesis remains at the
written-proof admission gate. Mathematical source inputs and verification
limits are recorded in `lead/ADMISSION-P.md`. Earlier complete bounds and
the original weighted-search certificate retain their separate scopes.
