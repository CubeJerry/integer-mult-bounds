## 2. The two coefficient domains are composed correctly

The characteristic-two geometry changes the scalar transfer itself. Its
low-rank bound follows from the Boolean distance selector and tensor
rank factors over `F2`; it does not reduce the old odd-characteristic
transfer modulo two. Rational address Gram coordinates remain separate.
Their values at the two required distances give the same endpoint
relations, and the odd address radix avoids their denominators and units.

For order-three data queries, `C_j=Q_j+L_j` and `C_j^2=Q_j+U_j`
combine both query powers into one shared swap pencil and pointwise
comparison masks. The masks are applied in the original layout. Equal
fields have all order-three gates defined as the identity, so they do
not become an unhandled orbit type. The diagonal and converter words
are fixed finite-field maps; no numerical Fourier interpretation is
needed for them.

The completed word is the ordinary swap product with `F2` coefficients.
Consequently expansion of the **completed** program in one common `F2`
basis preserves independent coordinate inputs and multiplies calls and
rows by the same factor. Intermediate order-three transforms need not
act separately on each binary coordinate. A fixed common algebra is
used throughout the finite and spatial recursion; no new coefficient
extension is adjoined at each recursive call.

The numerical Walsh side uses the distinct rational Newton construction.
Its parked accumulator is workspace, while each routed pencil acts on
the original number of active rows. The two-evaluation repair uses the
actual nonzero discrepancy set; it does not divide at accidentally
correct outside-window rows. Rational coefficients are never mapped
into the characteristic-two data ring. The completed exact interchanger
is invoked to move their binary encodings.

## 3. Finite costs and physical numerical preparation

Given the count theorem's polynomial-size, degree-controlled formulas,
the stated full-rate compiler has `2^(m^C)` row and generation bounds.
All `3^m` shifts, frequency/exception cases, finite coordinate tables and
fixed-depth contraction nodes fit the same bound after increasing `C`.
This uses explicit basis arithmetic, not a truth table over all elements
of the resulting algebra. The common node extension and completed batch
width are included before choosing the compatible address radix.

The rational Walsh replacement has polynomial row dimensions and
coefficient heights, with `2^O(m) poly(m)` exact spectral/column tables.
The auxiliary audit enumerates source coordinate changes, representative
maps, phases, rectangle row maps and both boundary conversions. Its
ordinary-operation overcount does not multiply the recursive axis bill:
the shared first and last endpoint ordering remains the source ordering.

Each rational coefficient is approximated once in setup by integer
division at the selected precision. Per-record multiplication then uses
the fixed short multiplier. There is no per-record division whose
quadratic bit cost has been hidden in a short multiplication bill.
Finite update counts and coefficient heights bound logarithmic local
amplification by `poly(P_m)`. For sufficiently large fixed `K`, the
threshold `k>=P_m^K` absorbs these gains times `log(2+k)` into an
absolute multiple of `k`. The coefficient approximation error enters
the same operator-error estimate. This is compatible with the existing
source precision and aggregate setup-clock mechanism.

Polynomial-height rational geometry, numeric compatibility values and
bounded radix search also fit `P_m<=2^(m^C)`. Thus both branches,
description retention, actual generation, movement and precision use
one finite budget. Neither branch retains the older larger predictor
as an unpriced subroutine.

## 4. Call mass, parked space and the common cap

The order-three repair has four sequential child phases of volume
`V/16`; total normalized recursive mass is `1/4`. The inverse and
positive targets are both supplied, and finite leaves use direct
swaps. This is a terminating finite composition, not a same-size
circular invocation. The final three-transform word gives
`8 D_pred R_pencil+3m 4^(-a)` calls per input row. The rational Walsh
closure has two child phases of volume `V/8`, again total mass `1/4`.

Depth-first processing therefore parks geometric volumes. Copies,
check rows and Newton accumulators may contribute a parameter-dependent
constant number of parent arrays, already included in `c_j`; they do
not introduce an extra recursion-depth factor. Spatial reserve calls
retain descriptors after the main arrays are released. The complete
ordinary swap target permits the existing source spatial recursion.

For the final wrapper, `r_j>=4c_j` and
`n>=4(c_j+d_j)` give explicitly

`c_j ceil(n/r_j)+d_j <= n/4+c_j+d_j <= n/2`.

The separate condition `n>=r_j U_j` makes each chunk eligible. The
additive retained-generation workspace is present in this calculation;
it is not dropped merely because each template is finite. Input,
accumulator, output, interpretation and cleanup have the separate fixed
linear allowance. Physical caps and sequential restart attempts bound
the union of visited cells, including failed attempts.

## 5. Joint profile and scope of the stronger rate

The explicit trial bound has `log m_j=O(J^3 log J)`. Its interchange
loss `3x/d+10d^2 log(4x)` is absorbed at the stated
`x0=2^40 J^3 log J`, and the numerical Walsh loss is smaller. The
leading-row and reserve factors have strict room in the acceptance
margin. Power-of-two exponent trials overshoot by only a fixed factor;
they do not require an uncharged optimizer.

Substituting the finite budget into the physical and chunk profiles
gives both `log D_j` and `log L(N_j)` at most
`2^(C J^3 log J)`. The sparse choice
`J=2^floor(log_2 Y)`, `Y=(z/(2C log z))^(1/3)`, makes both at most
`sqrt(s)` for `s=log L(n)`, `z=log s`. Thus the candidate is actually
eligible, and `s/J` gives the claimed residual exponent. Generation,
cleanup and failed attempts remain charged by the restart bill.
