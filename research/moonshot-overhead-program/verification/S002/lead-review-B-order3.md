## Objects read and checked

The lead read the complete characteristic-two label geometry, order-three
interchange proof (including the later four-element discrepancy identity),

finite-algebra descent, Hermite check/lift and spatial recurrence interfaces

The power-of-two distance selector uses Lucas parity, an explicit rank
factorization, and first-difference rectangles. The rational address Gram
construction remains separate from the characteristic-two data algebra.
Its dimensions, numeric radix and all physical row maps remain charged.
The displayed routing growth is a bound on this specified construction.

For the new closure, direct multiplication verifies TS=I, TCS=Delta,
and Q=C Delta C^-1 Delta^-1 C Delta on each nonfixed two-point orbit.
Every local operator is set to identity at a fixed point. Tensoring these
identities preserves the actual order of all pointwise diagonal factors.
The forward and inverse converters each contain two target transforms.
The final swap word contains three target transforms. This avoids treating
a converter as a free known permutation.

The integral order-three predictor gives an F4-valued diagonal multiplier
on each shifted spectral row. The central-window defect is below 1/32;
32-fold duplication makes the budget integral. The generic matrix-root
width, common cyclotomic algebra, all shifts and all exception tables are
retained. The scalar algebra is fixed throughout the finite and spatial
recursions. The eventual target Q is over F2, so full-rate coordinate
expansion over the entire algebra is legitimate at the completed caller;
no such descent is assumed for an intermediate F alone.

If e=(p-z)Xhat and Zhat=(p-z)^2 e on the exception set, then the residual
of PX+PZ+FZ is (p-z)(Xhat+Zhat). Every nonzero p-z in F4 has cube one.
This proves the two-predictor version, including rows accidentally corrected
by specialization. Known supersets of exceptions suffice. The containing
algebra may have zero divisors: nonzero elements of its embedded F4 remain
units. No arbitrary discrepancy inversion is used.

Each repair has four sequential child phases, each on 1/16 of the parent
rows. Regrouping keeps labels and batch roles, with the spectral table
between the complete forward and inverse words. Hence total recursive
mass is 1/4; truncating at h=ceil(log2(m)/4) bounds the batch growth by
16m and the direct-leaf contribution by O(sqrt m). The three-factor word
therefore costs at most 8 b_pred R_route+3m 4^-h shared-swap calls per row.
The finite unrolling is a full-rate identity on arbitrary inputs, so
correlations among checked rows do not invalidate recursion.

The physical proof retains occupied reserves, bounded stack parking,
comparison scans, original-coordinate masks, actual address maps and fixed
alphabet encoding. It invokes the already audited fixed-parameter source
compiler and does not infer a uniform new H from finite closure alone.

## Quantitative scope

The actual generic representation still has a double-exponential finite
profile. The smaller characteristic and two-predictor identity do not
remove that representation cost. The proposed IP4 compact full-rate
hypothesis is a separate conditional bridge and needs its own review.
No cheap extraction/correction follows merely from low rank of an
address-space error operator. The present proof supplies a different,
semisimple row-repair construction and thus does not use that missing step.
