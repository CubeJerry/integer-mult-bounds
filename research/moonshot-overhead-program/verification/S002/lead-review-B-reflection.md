# Lead review of the finite physical reflection family

Status: **accepted as a fixed-parameter full-rate source-compatible component**. This does not certify a new complete integer-multiplication overhead bound. Reviewed 2026-11-25 against the pinned November tape interchange proof and Fourier repair construction.

## Algebra and address semantics

The half-angle recurrence is exact in the specified cyclotomic quotient algebra over every odd characteristic. On a two-point swap orbit, Q and the signed comparison Z anticommute and square to the identity. On a fixed point, E supplies the correction making every R_t equal to 1. Thus R_t is an involution on every address, and R_(t+1) R_t R_(t+1)=Z+E. Disjoint field pairs give commuting operators, so the next family product simultaneously diagonalizes every query of the current family. The original product F_0 is the required permutation.

The actual routed pencil is split into a shared-swap pencil plus a separate pointwise comparison/equality map in the original physical order. No address-dependent diagonal matrix is commuted through a routing endpoint. Keeping a copy of the input makes this split implementable by the source's linear scans, with a finite coefficient depending on the complete template.

## Repair, full rate and the two recursions

The integral divided-power predictor, all shifts, matrix root and four-stage Hermite repair remain intact. Their constant defect sets have at most one-sixteenth of the rows. The two conversion phases each process one-eighth of the rows, retain routing labels, and are sequentially separated by the spectral correction. Enlarging the root batch by 8^h supplies complete label-specific batches at every finite-family depth. The geometric invoice is therefore a_m sum_(d<h) 4^(-d)+m 4^(-h), and h=ceil(log2(m)/4) gives O(sqrt(m)(R(m)+1)) calls per input row.

This finite-family recursion is distinct from the source's unbounded spatial recursion. After the finite F_0 program is assembled, the source digit split bounds each main-call volume by 2V/W_h; the 2(D_route-2) reserve descents retain their original full-volume role. They run after merging and clearing the main workspace. Consequently RF7 has the stated normalized coefficient, and the source's depth-first stack proof applies. A single finite scalar algebra is encoded throughout all spatial levels; it is not re-extended at each child.

The complete F_0 program is base-field linear on arbitrary algebra-valued inputs. Basis descent therefore supplies independent base-field input/output rows at full rate. Intermediate F_t need not act componentwise, and the proof correctly does not claim that it does.

## Replayed evidence and limits

The lead copied `verify_reflection_family.py` to a fresh temporary directory and replayed it with Python 3.12.14. All 75 quotient-ring identities, 4096 local conjugacy basis cases, fixed-point cases, label-preserving gather/scatter checks, and full-rate repair examples through 768 rows passed. Controls for wrong half-angle, missing fixed-point term, unchanged converter target, wrong check order, wrong return label, missing repair and premature inverse phase all failed as intended. The exact replay record and script hash are in `lead-replay-B-reflection.json`.

The finite checker evaluates spectral matrices directly. It does not implement the generic matrix root or the source physical router, and it is not a timing test. Their use here is justified by the reviewed all-size source interfaces. Numerical/address heights, enormous finite generation, and the common-cap quantitative compiler remain separately charged. The main call invoice removes a sqrt(log m) factor at fixed label geometry; this alone does not yield a stronger H(n), and no such claim is admitted.
