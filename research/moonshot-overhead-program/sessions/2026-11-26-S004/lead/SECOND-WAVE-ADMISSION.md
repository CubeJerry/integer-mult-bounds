## Why the conditional composition closes

JR improves both distinct geometries. Interchange uses anchored disjoint
inner sides and outer products, retaining its Gram conditions. Numerical
Fourier needs only cross orthogonality, so arbitrary correlated sides
require no anchor. Odd-distance free load becomes (1+c)^L(t+1)^c; the
charged rational matrix is unchanged. The resulting local logarithmic
losses are O_c((log m)^(2/3)) and O_c(sqrt(log m)), respectively.

Power arity for interchange and log m of order (log active width)^(2/3)
for Walsh pay the actual local routing toll and telescope to the result.
Improving only interchange would leave the numerical logarithm binding.

Returned JR lists and instructions have polynomial-m size. Bounded search
has exponential time but polynomial workspace. New rational bases, heights
and required units are recomputed before radix selection. Membership is
charged finite control, not a new per-scalar mask. The root field, grouped
repair, O((1+lambda)V) endpoints, 16/N main-child fraction, 1/16 repair
contraction and eightfold reserve allowance remain charged. Numerical
batch divisors remain powers of two. Combined preparation is honestly
2^(k^eta') for fixed eta'<1, not 2^(k^o(1)). Compact local chains,
computed precision guards, exact outer output and the capped common-space
wrapper are retained. Constants may depend on the fixed JR witness c.
