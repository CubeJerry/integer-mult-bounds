## Correlated-side Fourier transfer

For even L, odd distance makes every source direction orthogonal to every
destination direction. The source explicitly takes H as the destination
span, allows the pairing restricted to H to be degenerate, and uses the
nondegenerate full ambient pairing for dual bases. The inclusions
`H subset d_v^perp` and `H^perp subset d_w^perp` suffice. Neither side
must be a coordinate product. Paid per-query representative changes and
phases remain in the physical bill. Fourier therefore needs no anchor;
interchange retains its own anchored within-side Gram conditions.

The unequal-position set and common outside letters are unique for each
edge, so disjoint JR pieces preserve exact multiplicity one. For a fixed
vertex its common outside word is determined, giving no extra q factor.
Free load is at most `(1+c)^L(t+1)^c`; charged load is at most 2^L.
The weighted identity stays over dyadic rationals, separate from the
binary Gram field.

With even L=Theta(sqrt(log m)) and t=ceil(2+2log(m)/L), the charged
mass is at most one, q^L is polynomial in m, and the full endpoint bill
has logarithm O_c(sqrt(log m)). The anchored interchange expression at
d=Theta((log m)^(1/3)) is O_c((log m)^(2/3)). Both profiles improve;
changing only interchange would retain the Fourier logarithm.

## Recurrence, precision and final cap

Interchange's logarithmic widths decrease geometrically, summing its
2/3-power local losses to the root loss. Numerical arity has log m of
order y^(2/3): its local routing logarithm is O_c(y^(2/3)), while its
child logarithmic loss is O_c(y^(1/3)). The decrease of y^(2/3) over a
width step dominates that child loss with fixed slack for the additive
toll. Exact integer rounding is covered by the tested fixed base.

Batch divisors remain powers of two and their product is polynomial in
active width. E002's exact divisibility guard therefore still prevents
per-level padding. Local routing uses active a-bit fields; global selected
position gathering is paid once. Polynomial-m branching and gain sums
give the retained computed O(k)-precision guard. Outer CRT, occupied
restoration, phase budgets, short products, full output and carries are
the same source operations with the newly supplied interface bounds.

The wrapper uses r=2^(O_c(s^(2/3))) chunks. Its extra r factor stays
inside the envelope, chunk logarithmic sizes are asymptotically unchanged,
and actual preflight, step and visited-space caps with exact fallback
cover every finite length. Marked regions are reused for one uniform
O(n) visited-cell cap.

For fixed c in JR, bounded enumeration halts even on failure. JR implies
eventual success at all guarded large shapes. This is an explicit finite
graph premise, not an oracle for a multiplier. JR is the only additional
mathematical hypothesis of JRT-U and remains unresolved.
