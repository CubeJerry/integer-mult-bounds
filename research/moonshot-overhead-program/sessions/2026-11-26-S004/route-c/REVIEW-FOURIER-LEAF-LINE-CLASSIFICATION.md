# Independent audit of canonical Fourier leaf lines

**Verdict: pass for LC1–LC3, the q>=4 coset classification and the
binary exception.** This refines the ordinary leaf maps without claiming
that all possible batching schemes must pay the earlier lambda invoice.

The sum e_i of each coordinate's q-1 orthonormal neighbor vectors has
the same pairing with every generator. Those generators span the
nondegenerate auxiliary space, so all e_i agree. Its norm is one and
its pairing represents the norm functional. The even-coefficient
subspaces E_i are nondegenerate, mutually orthogonal and orthogonal
to e, and have dimension q-2. Within each E_i, the internal sibling
spaces account for q-1-t dimensions. The remaining r_i(s) have distinct
positive-block parities and the compensating singleton-block parity,
so they supply exactly the missing t-1 dimensions. This proves LC1.

The level-zero identity is important: its singleton vector is e+u_i,
where u_i lies in the sum of positive-level internal blocks. It cannot
be omitted when q>=4. For a rectangle, the u_i terms arise only in
zero-state coordinates, disjoint from the selected positive internal
blocks. The canonical representative therefore has no component in
its own selected U_R, and the DA5 sign simplifies to the identity.
This does not require a complement avoiding all internal blocks at
once; components in other blocks remain. It is compatible with the
nonzero-phase examples for an arbitrary previously fixed basis.

Distinct positive states have distinct r-coordinate patterns in their
odd affine cosets. With the state fixed, a different level-zero unequal
set changes the representative by a nonzero sum of u_i in zero-state
coordinates, outside the common U_R. The u_i are independent and
nonzero for q>=4. Thus distinct incident canonical rectangles have
disjoint odd-vector cosets. This excludes sharing a single geometric
odd line between them, but not different shears into a common transform
coordinate, other basis circuits or more general linear batching.

At q=2 the internal and r spaces vanish and every odd source direction
is e. The remaining destination transforms may genuinely be accumulated
before their common e-axis call. Together with source preconditioning
this gives four free axis calls per row, with the usual ordinary maps
still charged. SI's literal lambda invoice is therefore not a bound
on this changed executor. The independent correction-kernel obstruction
is valid: each same-parity principal block of any charged C in C+F=I
is an identity of size N/2 because F has only opposite-parity support.
Hence rank(C)>=N/2 and the retained charged coefficient is at least m.

The isolated `replay-fourier-leaf-lines/` replay passes all eight Gram
families, 140 affine cosets, 588 coset points and 332 source-label checks.
All 70 omitted-level-zero-offset adverse controls are retained. The q=2
cases collapse as claimed, while no coset points coincide for q>=4.
