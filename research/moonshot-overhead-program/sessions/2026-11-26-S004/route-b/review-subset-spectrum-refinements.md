## Physical matching constant

Each four-coordinate target-root mass is separately affine in every
p_i. Successively moving each coordinate to an endpoint without
increasing that mass proves that its minimum is attained at one of
the 16 vertices. The five symmetry classes listed in the note have
respective minimum masses 1/16, 3/16, 3/16, 11/64 and 9/64. Thus
every root eigenspace has at least D_group/16 actual dimensions.
Taking the minimum of their five integer dimensions and matching that
many original joint eigenvectors across all five roots loses no hidden
rounding factor. Their selected spans remain invariant under every
original query because the original joint eigenvectors were retained,
rather than arbitrarily mixing inside a target eigenspace. Therefore
theta>=5/16 is valid uniformly in unequal q_i. The two-coordinate
order-three endpoint minima 1/4, 1/4 and 5/16 likewise give theta>=3/4.

This argument is a comparison-basis existence proof. It introduces no
free physical matching or basis-change operation into the network.

## Ratio subgroup and sharp limiting error

The normalization T=S/a contains 1. Hence T^j is an increasing sequence
of finite nonempty subsets of G. If T^(j+1)=T^j, each t in T maps
T^j injectively into itself; finiteness makes it onto and gives inverse
invariance. The set is invariant under the subgroup generated by T,
contains 1, and thus equals G. Before completion its size strictly
increases. Starting from one element, completion by j=d-1 follows.

Choose one padded word of length d-1 for each product value. The words
are distinct because their products are distinct, so their joint
eigenspaces are disjoint. Each has actual dimension fraction at least
p_*^(d-1). Matching the minimum multiplicity across the d spaces gives
theta>=d p_*^(d-1), with no uniform-multiplicity assumption. This
justifies SB11 rather than relying only on distributional convergence.

The target coset is the fixed a^m G. On every non-exceptional stratum
one regular factor makes its target uniform over that coset. The
exceptional mass decays exponentially, so scalar constant prediction
attains the limiting rank error 1-1/d. The sector lower bound yields
the same constant under the stated support-degree or active-volume
conditions, including inverse queries, ports and fractions. Fixed
d and fixed positive p_* are essential to these asymptotics.

For {zeta,zeta^-1}, the ratio zeta^2 has order r/gcd(r,2). The even-r
correction is therefore required: its target occupies a coset of a
proper subgroup and a claimed ambient 1-1/r sharp constant would fail.
For r=2 the two displayed roots coincide and d=1 correctly gives zero.

The physical matching proof in the first section legitimately avoids
the general corollary's fixed p_* assumption for the equal-field root,
whose fraction 1/q can vanish. It proves its own uniform retained
fraction instead of applying SB11 with a nonexistent lower bound.
