## 1. B's direct coefficient-rank obstruction

I read `route-b/direct-rank-routing-obstruction.md`, its exact checker, and the cited count-predictor degree statements in `routes/A/power-two-degree-predictor.md`, `routes/A/characteristic-two-count-formula.md` and `routes/B/structured-shift-check-refinement.md`. This is a different proposed executor from the literal geometric rectangle invoice.

For a D-variable restriction of an affine pencil, all variable-dependent column images lie in the fixed span of its D coefficient images. A constant row basis makes every row outside that span constant, so determinant degree is at most the sum of the coefficient ranks. Taking determinants of a formal square full-row identity gives degree DW, even in positive characteristic: a nonzero polynomial and its power retain their multiplied total degree in the integral domain. Factor determinant degrees add because none is zero. Averaging over all D-subsets counts each coefficient exactly binom(m-1,D-1) times and gives total rank at least mW. Arbitrary stage count, constant mixing and enlarged full-rate width do not evade this argument.

The application uses the source's two query-power symbols as independent formal affine inputs before substitution. On the Boolean restriction, shifted ternary digits become the chosen bits. The top squarefree coefficient is the sum of T consecutive powers of omega; Lucas parity makes every binomial coefficient in row T-1 odd. T is a power of two, hence not divisible by three, so the sum is nonzero. The formal degree is at most D=T-1. No higher-degree monomial can multilinearize to the degree-D squarefree term, establishing every required D-variable restriction. The condition D<=m holds on the asymptotic admitted parameter range; smaller cases remain base cases.

Rank subadditivity transfers the theorem back from the substituted coefficient to the two original query powers. Thus the proposed separately routed coefficient-rank invoice is at least m, independently of factorization width or length. The regular-split recurrence and its fixed-minimum-arity consequence are valid positive-invoice deductions. The sharper e/poly(log e) expression additionally uses the adaptive logarithmic-depth bound explicitly stated in the note.

The claim does not lower-bound every physical execution of these pencils. The accepted geometric method already shares queries through address-dependent layouts and is outside the separate-rank price. A finite-spectral identity lies outside the formal-polynomial premise: a polynomial nonconstant off the allowed cube can agree there with a constant identity of zero query rank. This correctly limits the formal theorem. The later SQ extension below closes the proposed finite-spectrum escape for essential target functions, so that distinction alone no longer motivates another separate-query route.

## 2. C's finite-alphabet occupied-payload bridge

I read `route-c/FINITE-ALPHABET-PAYLOAD-PACKING.md` and its complete checker. Packing b=floor(log₂Q) independent bits into one canonical algebra index is injective. Storing s=ceil(log₂Q) bits gives1<=s/b<=2. Only the completed whole-row spatial permutation is used in decoding; intermediate algebraic additions need not act as binary XOR and may leave the encoded input subset. Thus odd characteristic and nilpotents present no encoding obstruction, provided the original algebra program is valid and its arithmetic is explicitly paid.

The fixed-tape layout implementation is constructive for arbitrary b. Each of b complete passes extracts one coordinate stream and overwrites its positions on an alternating s-bit record stream. Long known runs pay initialization, and zero runs are omitted. There is no per-bit full-address scan or b-head assumption. Packing/unpacking costs O(sV) with an absolute number of regions at most2V. The codec is standard; the useful new interface is its occupied-row and recursive volume accounting.

The alpha=s/b and leading beta<2 factors really are charged: the binary call invoice is alpha beta Q_m<4Q_m. A scalar child fraction at most1/16 gives an incoming binary child at most V/4. Finite repair retains its one algebra and does not recode at each phase. Reserve ownership requires a completed permutation boundary, decoding and released main scratch; the bridge states that condition rather than applying it to unfinished circuits. A child using another codec returns exact parent bits, so nested field/algebra changes are valid.

This does not supply an odd-characteristic predictor, denominators/inverses, rank theorem, or cheaper geometry. Those remain separate obligations. In AQ the binary-field codec remains stronger, with volume equality; no weaker codec is silently substituted into its proof.

## 4. Spectral-only and approximate-query extension

The lead proposed the product-difference argument and B independently checked, extended and wrote it in `route-b/spectral-query-rank-obstruction.md`. I read the complete proof and checker. Telescoping products at tuples differing in one coordinate bounds output-difference rank by the sum of that coordinate's coefficient ranks. No invertibility, formal identity, degree bound or squarefree restriction is needed. An essential scalar output has full difference rank, so its query price is at least W per essential coordinate.

Subtracting the two error matrices gives the stated `(1-2epsilon)W` bound. For a product target on at least two distinct nonzero symbols, uniformly resampling one coordinate to a different value has uniform marginals and always changes the target; averaging proves the average-rank version. The actual structured-shift matrix predictor has at most W/32 exceptional rows, so its separate query bill is at least15mW/16. The scalar count predictor is essential at each coordinate because one of the first two consecutive-count steps avoids the only possible wrap. The m=1 actual family uses T=2.

Padding a fixed linear straight-line program to one temporary-register state, then applying fixed input injection/output projection, preserves the rank argument. A query on r registers changes its state map by rank at most r. Thus extra scratch, free fixed mixing and rectangular input/output alone do not evade it. Address transformations mixing joint spectral fibres are outside the model, as is value-dependent control. The regular split telescopes product arities to e up to a polynomial in log e on an AI-depth chain, proving the displayed optimistic certificate floor; it is not a physical runtime lower bound.
