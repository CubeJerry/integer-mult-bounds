## Accepted: the nested literal-invoice obstruction

Route A's Theorem N covers every nesting depth ell>=2, unequal power-of-two
distances d_i>=2 and q=2^t. Its scalar selector is exactly the elementary
symmetric tensor e_(d_i) of the previous square-zero selector. This identity
has the intended diagonal-plus-far support; the proof includes the parity
at both endpoints k=0,d_i, vanishing interior k and unsupported larger k.

Restricting d tensor positions to a complement of the kernel and the rest
to the kernel leaves one injective summand. Iteration yields the actual
rank-fraction lower bound `2^(1-P(t+1))`, P=product d_i. Thus the result
does not infer a rank lower bound from a sufficient rank upper bound.

The rational Gram calculation is also exact. Integer far adjacency is
invertible because it reduces to I plus a square-zero matrix modulo two.
Its odd valency preserves the constant/nonconstant rational decomposition.
The constant mode cannot cancel: n*d and 2*h*delta have different 2-adic
valuations. This gives D=2+h*(n-1), with no use of binary data rank as
rational address rank.

The exact product-rectangle incidence and actual occupancy factor imply
that `D(1+lambda)(1+m rank/N)<=m^(1/J)`, J>=64, requires

    log2 m >= 2^(ell-2) J^3 log2 J /128.

The lead checked the uniform-depth inequalities directly. No constant
depends on ell, and no additional occupancy<=1 premise is needed. The
source upper invoice is being bounded below as an **invoice**, not used
to infer a lower bound on arbitrary algorithms. The statement and limits
are accepted. C's independently derived symmetric theorem is a simpler
corroborating result, with explicit constant 1/12 in its optimized incidence
bound; it is not counted as an unrelated second discovery.

## Accepted: succinct scalar factors, with their exact interface

B's characteristic-two power-of-two cyclic sum has two terms at a grade i:
advance that grade and apply the left factor at phase i, or retain it and
apply the right factor at phase t-i. A single-factor traversal ends at one
affine leaf, so it creates O(S) masked rotations rather than a product of
queries. Product nodes choose a child phase and retain the unused child's
rows. Scaling/padding are paid and zero coefficients do not shrink the
independent-row architecture. All masks use the input grade before rotation.

The grade-coordinate increments are contiguous block rotations in packed
lexicographic rows. Each token uses a fixed number of reusable streams;
the number of passes and row-counter bits are polynomial in the compact
formula. The field modulus is supplied and copied, or in the intended
O(log m)-degree field is constructed by the previously charged finite
enumeration. No arbitrary-degree irreducible constructor is called
polynomial without proof. Large trailing-range counters are amortized over
complete fibres. With these qualifications, the scalar-slice executor is
accepted. It does not execute the spatial query operators or establish a
complete shared-pencil implementation.
