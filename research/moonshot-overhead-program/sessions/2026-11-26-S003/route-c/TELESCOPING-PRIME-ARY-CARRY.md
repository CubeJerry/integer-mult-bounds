# The prime-ary carry gate has a polynomial formula

Status: **proved finite formula; exact coefficient checks passed; independently accepted by Route A** in `../route-a/review-telescoping-prime-ary-carry.md`. This resolves the specific PA-formula gate in A's `prime-ary-carry-formula-gate.md`, whose p-ary recurrence now gives a proved absolute polynomial-m grammar. It is a finite compiler improvement, not a new complete multiplier bound. The factor-p top-length inflation remains.

For a prime p, set B_a(X)=binom(X,a), 0<=a<p, over F_p. On n=p+1 inputs Y_1,...,Y_n (the last may be the incoming carry digit), put

    G_p(U,V)=sum_(a=1)^(p-1) B_a(U) B_(p-a)(V),
    K_p(Y)=sum_(j=2)^n G_p(Y_1+...+Y_(j-1),Y_j).     (TPC1)

Every prefix in TPC1 is an ordinary affine formula. No prefix is an uncharged shared circuit node: its entire formula is copied wherever needed.

## Exact polynomial identity

Over Q, Vandermonde gives

    G_p(U,V)=binom(U+V,p)-binom(U,p)-binom(V,p).

Summing over prefixes telescopes, yielding

    K_p(Y)=binom(sum_i Y_i,p)-sum_i binom(Y_i,p).

By the many-variable Vandermonde identity the right side is precisely

    sum_[0<=a_i<p, sum_i a_i=p] product_i B_(a_i)(Y_i).

Both the displayed product formula and TPC1 have coefficients in Z localized away from p: their only factorial denominators are a! with a<p. Therefore the identity reduces to an exact formal polynomial identity over F_p, even though the intermediate expression binom(X,p) separately has a p in its denominator. We do not reduce those separate rational summands modulo p.

For ordinary input digits y_i in {0,...,p-1}, the total S is at most p²-1, and K_p(y)=binom(S,p) modp=floor(S/p). This is the ordinary outgoing digit carry. Equivalently, each G_p measures one carry in sequential addition of the prefix residue to the next digit, and their sum counts all these carry increments. That scalar interpretation does not replace the formal proof.

## Actual factor lengths and full formula size

Give every input length one. Every prefix sum and every affine factor prefix-r has addition budget one. The product defining B_a(prefix) has actual factor length a. Each term B_a(prefix)B_(p-a)(Y_j) has length exactly p. All remaining additions may be placed at the top with budget p. Thus the only addition budgets are 1 and p; no sum of intermediate degree is rounded to p and then multiplied by a nonconstant factor.

There are p(p-1) product terms. A term has p affine factors, and a prefix has at most p variable occurrences. Copying all prefixes and scalar constants gives at most 8p^4 arithmetic nodes under the explicit size convention of the checker. Coefficients are generated by factorial arithmetic below p and modular inversion, in polynomial p bit operations. This proves PA-formula with an absolute exponent.

For a p-ary tree, A's recurrence now applies with A_p,B_p bounded by fixed powers of p:

    S_(r,k)<=C A_p^r B_p^k binom(r+k,k),
    r=ceil(log_p m), k<=r.

For 3<=p<=m this is m^K for one absolute K. Fixed offsets, Lucas products and the D+1 Newton terms retain an absolute polynomial size. The top length remains the least p-power L>=D, hence L<=pD. The order-four p=3 high initial digit is still retained. The independent cyclic module may have width L^(m^K), but its log width and succinct grade grammar are polynomial m.

This improves the growing-characteristic count syntax from m^{O(log p)} to m^K. It does not prove the required physical address maps or eliminate the factor p in the call invoice. The complete odd-characteristic compiler and its global cost remain separate work.

## Exact verification

`verify_telescoping_carry.py` constructs the actual formula with copied-size accounting and assigned factor lengths. For p=2,3,5,7 it expands the formula and independently computes the coefficient of T^p in the truncated generating product defining PA1. All four formal identities passed, covering 6,469 nonzero coefficients. This comparison tests formal polynomial equality, not only equality of values on a finite field.

The checker passed 223 ordinary-digit tuples, 517 actual addition-budget checks and eight wrong-residue or wrong-prefix controls. The unfolded formula sizes were 17,101,639,2169, respectively, below 8p^4 in every case. Its shared Python object references do not discount repeated formula occurrences: node sizes count the complete unfolding. The result is `telescoping-carry-verification.json`; the script hash is `bf0513d59c75929c4ebafd24beca5d28f7f591379c95a4fb853493d467535057`.
