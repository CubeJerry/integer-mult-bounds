## Shared endpoint order and coefficient compatibility

The affine factor's scalar coefficient slices depend on row grades, shift
roles and its fixed phase. They are independent of the physical address
and geometric label. Consequently they are legitimate common row matrices
in the source shared-pencil identity and commute with an address change
applied identically to every row. Their packed implementation changes row
grades only. It does not perform one spatial query per masked token.

The original-layout comparison terms are evaluated separately. Their masks
are not commuted through source endpoints. Rectangle source copies and
prepared destination accumulators are retained across all queries, so the
reserve-only preparation is paid once per incidence. The low-rank route
changes layouts only on the r_* intermediate streams as in the source.
Retaining polynomially many prepared copies costs a polynomial parameter
multiple of volume, which is explicitly permitted and charged.

The hyperbolic completion and star-basis realization preserve the exact

rectangle transition on reserve coordinates is unimodular with the stated
dyadic star and anchor operations. Upper-target elementary shears may cost

have O(Dg) actual operations, not just polynomial bit-height matrices.
The deliberately large Cg=2^20 is compatible with A's independently counted
1024Dg endpoint bound and retains the complete source incidence/rank factors.

## Grouped volume and control layouts

Identical coordinate calls can be grouped only within one geometric
endpoint or intermediate stream. Such a group has one label's row volume,
at most 1/N of the current repair node. The bound is not 1/W. Module and
shift rows are arbitrary prefix spectators; their data need not agree.
Direct finite leaves are processed separately and have root fraction
16^(-h). Thus the stated maximum main-child fraction is strictly below
one quarter, while the **sum** of all child volumes is exactly what the
normalized Qm invoice charges.

The scalar field is encoded once and retained at every spatial depth.
The duplication32 belongs to coarse roles; the invariant module is w.
Checks have B coarse roles including zero padding. Two-family phases have
2B roles, and sixteen parents regroup to32B child roles. This moves only
polynomial-sized coarse fields, leaving w and the physical suffix intact.
The comparison keys are produced after the complete forward converter and
stripped before any recursive inverse conversion. Hence four phases each
have raw scalar-volume fraction1/16, with total mass1/4. Sidecars remain
parent workspace and do not inflate the call invoice.

The leading block uses ell=ceil(log_3 W) digits. Padding to a multiple of W
costs beta<2; the packed main identity preserves each leading row when
completed, which justifies deletion of known padded indices even though
intermediate row matrices mix them. The source's one-digit b>W condition
is genuinely replaced by an explicit multi-digit procedure.

All leftover leading/remainder digits have polynomial count and fixed
radix3. With f=floor((e-ell)/(m+2a)), the spatial recurrence is

    T(e)<=m^C+(beta Qm+2a)T(f),  f<=e/m.

CI4 provides the fixed contraction. Base width ell+m+2a is polynomial m
because log W is polynomial m. Descriptor arithmetic has absolute degree,
so its absorption constant is independent of the growing template.

After padding, every grouped main child is below half the parent volume.
Depth-first parked payloads sum geometrically. Main arrays are cleared
before full-volume reserve descents, which retain only descriptors.
Regions are reused across siblings and phases, so the argument controls
cells ever visited. Binary-to-ternary range padding has factor below9.
No hidden number of tapes or per-depth alphabet expansion remains.
