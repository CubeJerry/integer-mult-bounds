# Cumulative space at the native and range ports

The [whole-program space ledger](routing-outer-space.md) correctly charges the union of tape locations over successive independent requests. Fix the maximum incoming binary bit volume $D\ge1$ at one routing call site and put $W_*=9D$. Each [native traversal](node-integrated.md#arena-stack-and-visited-cells) begins at the same marked origin and has actual root volume $W<W_*$. A one-head interval of length $\ell$ containing that origin lies inside $[-\ell,\ell]$. Consequently different requests attaining their largest displacement on different tapes remain covered by summing the uniform interval of *each* designated head.

The 1,024 native private heads contribute at most $1024(16W_*+9)\le25{,}600W_*$. Each of the two end stacks is bounded separately by the combined single-traversal high-water $(3\cdot 2^{12}+2)W_*$. Allow either sign relative to the common origin, giving at most $49{,}162W_*$ for both. The 17 direct heads may use the permissive total $51W_*$ individually, yielding $1{,}751W_*$. The additional 128 native data/descriptor ports give $896W_*$. Thus the native part costs at most $77{,}409W_*$ in the cumulative accounting.

The [range program](address-range.md) realizes all its 15 tape intervals inside $[-1,W]$. Its raw data, genuine extents and padded extents are at most $W$, and all exported descriptors include their right boundary at or before $W$. The $e=0$ counter has no nonzero digit and its head stops at zero. The four separately marked descriptor-copy inputs, data ports, and normalization return use the same length envelope. The two 15-head directions plus 32 extra marker/format heads therefore satisfy the ledger's $62(18D+5)\le1426D$ bound. The frozen origin is available inside a guarded sort because each dense interchange receives a staged raw bank, while its tagged exceptional records are handled by separate scans.

It follows from the other suppliers' stated allocations that the full arithmetic can retain more of the numerical slack. Charge the 160 guarded bulk heads at their displayed $160(8D+5)\le2080D$. Each routing family then uses at most $(9\cdot77{,}409+1426+2080)D=700{,}187D$, plus its separately absorbed setup. The ordinary outer records and the real Walsh roles give $D\le1024Nk$ and $D\le100Nk$ at their two call sites, respectively. Including the ledger's remaining $2^{22}Nk$ gives $791{,}204{,}492 Nk$. For $n\ge129$ we have $b=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil\ge8$, $b\le n/8$, and $4h\le4(n/b+1)\le n$. Hence $k\le b$ and $Nk<8(n+b)\le9n$. Together the recorded suppliers give the fast-branch bound $7{,}120{,}840{,}428\,n<2^{33}n$. This last calculation uses the Walsh and other outer allocations in the main ledger. The independent per-head audit here specifically addresses the native and range ports.

The end-stack rules in the [physical handoff](recount-handoff.md) give a further reduction with no directional assumption on the 1,024 ordinary heads. From the fixed bottom a stack pushes one position right per deposited symbol and pops back to that bottom. Its cumulative union is therefore at most $(3\\cdot2^{12}+2)9D+2$ even when the two stacks peak in different requests. Insert $W_*=9D$ before absorbing additive markers. The two stacks and all remaining two-sided native heads then use at most
$$
[1024(144+9)+2((3\\cdot 2^{12}+2)9+2)+17(918+1)+128(18+5)]D=396{,}463D.
$$
The range and guarded banks give $399{,}969D$ per routing family. Consequently $9[399{,}969\\cdot1124+2^{22}]=4{,}083{,}835{,}140<2^{32}$ is also a valid fast visited-cell coefficient per input bit. This argument preserves separate maxima for each stack.
