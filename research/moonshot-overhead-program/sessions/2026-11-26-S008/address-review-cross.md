# Independent verdict on the cross-anchor bounds

Both the [incompatible-view lower bound and the narrowest-coordinate construction](routing-cross-anchor.md) hold in their stated models. The first prices complete independently populated rows in the specified spatial-call compiler. The second minimizes every vertex's rectangle incidence among covers confined to individual full first-difference boxes and retaining the zero-far-pair condition within each side. At an actual nonbase arity, the exact incidence reduction against the old partition exceeds $2^{5947}$. The optimized logarithmic order is still $\Theta((u^2\log_2 u)^{1/3})$. The compared counts concern endpoint preparations.

**Complete-row views.** Fix a query and its unchanged spectators. In the [source common-address map](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/interchange.tex), the main pair contributes $P_i x$ and the two reserves contribute an element of $p_i^\perp$ plus the spare coordinates. A reserve-only bijection may be arbitrary. Fix one physical early prefix after that bijection. As the late reserve varies, its contribution is a single nonempty set $T_i$ independent of $x$. The fine fibres in this coarse block are exactly $T_i+P_i x$. Reduction modulo 3 gives the partition invariants
$$
W=\operatorname{span}(T_i-T_i)\subseteq p_i^\perp,\qquad
F=W+\operatorname{span}p_i.
$$
Differences and spans here use the reduced ambient coordinates including the spare. A physical chart can reorder the common addresses differently in every rectangle. Pulling its cuts back to the shared source address $z$ gives the same $W,F$ whenever the source uses one view, as both directions of each remaining address permutation respect those cuts. For any participating oriented column $w$ of endpoint $i$, write $w=P_v c+w_0$ in $F$, $w_0\in W$. Pairing with $p_v$ identifies $c$ as the corresponding unit vector because the same-rectangle Gram is $I_2$. Thus $w$ agrees with the matching column of $p_v$ modulo $W$. Pairing with any other endpoint proves all mutual oriented Gram blocks are $I_2$.

For different nonanchor depths $b>c$, the opposite half at $b$ is contained in the source half at $c$. One source coordinate can therefore agree with the destination coordinate, giving far count $d-1$ across opposite orientations. For equal depth vectors and differing destination anchors, make the remaining $d-1$ selected letters differ in exactly that outer position, using the halves of size at least two. All other outer positions differ in at most one letter. The resulting same-orientation far count is one. In each case a Gram entry changes by the unit $1/d$ modulo 3. Hence the $M=(q-1)^d(t-1)^{d(d-1)}$ source requirements are pairwise incompatible for every $f\ge1$. Each complete new incompatible view needs a separate cut-crossing edge in the recorded tree of row copies. One full field exchange introduces at most one new partition per participating row, yielding $(M-1)V$ after summing complete row volumes. Fragmented deliveries and algebraic mixtures before materialization fall outside this tree.

The [E002 linear compatibility theorem](../../results/epsilon-uniformity/sessions/E002/review-c/COMPATIBLE-ENDPOINT-REUSE.md) already supplies rational Gram necessity and sufficiency for fixed linear preparations with triangular tails, and a lower bound of $(q-1)^d$ reuse components. The new proof includes fully nonlinear reserve preparations, arbitrary permutations respecting both prefix cuts, the simultaneous depth factor, reduction modulo 3 at all positive widths, and the whole-row exchange charge.

**Arbitrary correlated subrectangles.** In an inner directed box with opposite coordinate sizes $s_j$, choose $c=\min_j s_j$ labels whose letters are all distinct in every coordinate. They are pairwise far. Every fixed opposite vertex requires at least $c$ incidences, even with overlapping rectangles and arbitrary nonproduct sides. Pinning source and destination letters separately in any narrowest coordinate attains $c$ at all vertices. In a full outer box, every projection of a legal correlated side onto one changing inner position must itself be far-pair-free. Any two labels witnessing a far pair in a projection would already violate the outer condition. The product of the inner cliques has $\prod_i c_i$ labels, and an arbitrary legal side contains at most one. The product pinning construction attains the resulting bound simultaneously at all sources and destinations. Nonempty rectangles confined to distinct complete boxes have disjoint edge sets, so these bounds add at every vertex. This proof requires within-side far count zero. The rational Gram enforces it exactly, and the radix enforces it when $3^f>d$. Covers allowed to straddle boxes obey a different incidence problem.

**Independent arithmetic.** The regular inner factor is $A_d(t)=\sum_{b=1}^t2^{t-b}(b^d-(b-1)^d)$. I recomputed it by an exact alternative with only $d$ terms. Define $F_0=1$ and $F_d=\sum_{j=1}^d\binom dj F_{d-j}$, the ordered Bell numbers. Then
$$
A_d(t)=2^tF_d-\sum_{j=1}^d\binom dj t^{d-j}F_j.
$$
For $d>0$, $\sum_{b\ge1}2^{-b}(b^d-(b-1)^d)=F_d$ by the exponential generating function $1/(2-e^z)$. Shifting the omitted tail by $t$ and expanding the difference proves the formula. There are $\binom hd^{d+1}$ support choices and the full incidence uses $A_d(t)^d$. The inequalities $A_d(t)\ge\max(2^{t-1},t^d)$ and $A_d(t)\le 2^{t+d}d!$ give the claimed $2^{\Omega(d^2\log_2\log_2 d)}$ ratio on $t=8d\log_2 d$, $m=2^{d^2(t-2)}+1$. In fact $u_0/(d^3\lceil\log_2(u_0+2)\rceil)\to8/3$ for $u_0=\lceil\log_2m\rceil$, and consecutive dyadic $d^3$ differ by a factor of eight. The integer rank-guard threshold lies strictly above $(t-1)d^2$ and at most $td^2$. Taking $e=m^{4096}$ realizes this exact odd arity. Under $d^2 t\ge u+2d^2-2$, the two estimates $d(t-1)$ and $\tfrac12d^2\log_2(1+(u-2)/d^2)$ still give $\tfrac16u^{2/3}(\log_2u)^{1/3}$ when split at $d=(u/\log_2u)^{1/3}$. This verifies the rate limitation on the stated positive invoices.
