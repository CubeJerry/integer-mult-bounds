The [all-length statement](all-lengths.md) and [piecewise threshold](routing-piecewise-walsh.md) are written proofs about one fixed physical program. The entries below distinguish source-level kernel theorems, deterministic executions and independent reconstruction of the full invoices.

| Recursive mass | [Volume count](child-volume.md), [ordinary costs](node-root-parameter.md), [formal child-weight inequality](native-contraction.lean). The written count includes every repeated child and full reserve. Lean verifies the analytic decrease from that mass supplier. |
