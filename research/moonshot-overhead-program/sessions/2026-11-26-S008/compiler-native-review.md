## Controller and local work

The base enters the direct machine after a linear scan of the canonical binary width and converts its marked data/parameter ports with their stated linear costs. Even at `V=1`, `e=0`, the 256 bound covers the fixed operations. More generally `|e|<=e+1` and `|G|+|S|<=2V`. On a nonbase, compute the fixed-degree root from `e` independently of the not-yet-defined short-word machine. The multiplication loop caps at `e+1`, so it requires only the fixed long registers. The greatest odd root differs from the real root by less than two.

For a legal adaptive node the 16-way repair depth is `ceil(log2(m)/4)`. All three temporal repair trees together have fewer than `16 sqrt(m)` nodes. The geometric list contains both forward preparation and the two destination passes, plus rank singleton conversion. Coarse label/rank support is enumerated before issuing each recursive service. There are at most `m^38` unweighted local events. Each can expand into up to `m^18` ordinary coarse-role pairs with exactly one already prescribed grouped service. The `m^20` allowance covers both directions and fixed surrounding operations. In particular a rejected label or incidence does not acquire a native child.

Writing `u=1+ceil(log2(2V+1))`, one has `b_long+2<=2^11 u` and `V>=2^{u-4}`. The inequality `55+5log2(u)<=u-4` holds for `u>=1024` by value and monotonicity. One long shape therefore costs at most `m^52 V`, *additive* per delivered pass. A shape can choose any slot via a scan of the long memory and a separately charged short-word index setup. The field and mask calculators use strictly short words at individual physical records. Alternatively even recomputing `m^48` transitions per occupied bit for every expanded transfer fits the displayed `m^62 V` per transfer. ## Visited space and width dependencies

A finite frame at volume `U` has up to 128 payload banks of at most `4U` each. With data and one delimiter per bank, this is at most `640U` for `U>=1`. The contraction by 16 along a finite chain sums to at most `640(16/15)2V`; even adding `512V` for the outside native word stays below `2^12 V`. Contracted spatial volumes halve. Full same-volume reserve edges park **zero** payload, and consecutive siblings reuse the same stack interval. This gives at most `3*2^12 V_root` for the payload end stack and its live descendant.

Separately, the complete binary control frame, including the finite tables, fits `2^12 m^34(b_long+2)^2` cells. Since `m^4096<=e<=u`, the frame uses at most `2^34 u^3` cells. At most `(u_root+1)^2` spatial/finite frames can coexist, including sequential full reserve ancestors. The sum lies below `2^36(u_root+1)^5<=V_root` on the adaptive domain. Control banks and both stacks run from fixed origins or moving stack ends, so reusing an emptied bank for later siblings never increases its high-water beyond the length bound. Adding all 1,024 physical ordinary/private/control head intervals of up to `8V_root+4`, end stack origins and adapters stays below `2^18 V_root` also in the constant-volume base-only cases. A full tally of visited territory here includes discarded temporary words.

I separately reran the published child schedule (1,063 rounding cases, including 874 in fixed-exponent range, and 36 exact literal schedules), direct/role recount (538/504 independent cases), shape/packing/compare simulations, 312 dyadic actions (1,800 stages), and the complete 1,488 contracted/526 full handoff tests.
