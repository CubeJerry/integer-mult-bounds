# Raw product for the bounded initial range

The input consists of two equal positive-length, most-significant-first strings separated by one symbol on a single tape. The output is otherwise blank and its positions starting at zero hold the full \(2n\) bits. These are the [original conventions](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/lean/OAI/Computability/NearSquareRoot/Specification.lean). [schoolbook.py](schoolbook.py) generates the 19-state five-tape fallback using the fixed transition interpreter in [node-tapes.py](node-tapes.py).

Copy the operands to two delimited banks. For each input digit write one zero on the accumulator, allocating exactly \(2n\) digits. Its marked digit initially denotes offset zero. At multiplier bit \(j\), if the bit is one, add the multiplicand starting at the mark and propagate its carry. The new accumulator contains the multiplicand times the processed multiplier digits with their correct binary weights. A partial product is at most \((2^n-1)^2<2^{2n}\); carry cannot leave the bank. Restore the multiplicand head to the low bit and the accumulator head to the mark. Advance the mark right one place. Finally read the accumulator backwards and write only raw bits at output positions 0 through \(2n-1\).

The intervals give at most \(8n+9\) visited cells, including all five initial head positions. One iteration reads the multiplier, takes at most \(2n+1\) bit-add transitions, propagates through at most \(2n\) carry positions, seeks the mark in at most \(2n+1\), restores the multiplicand in \(n+2\) and shifts the mark in two. Copying, initialization and readout use at most \(10(n+1)\) additional steps. Thus \(64(n+1)^2\) bounds the raw machine. Exhaustive inputs through five bits and 50 extra pairs up to 128 bits gave 1,414 exact products.

To dispatch on \(n\ge2^{2K}\) for \(K=2^{2^{48}}\), measure \(n\) in binary from the first input word, restore the raw head, and set \(q=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\) by counting the bits of that measured length and subtracting one. For \(q>0\) test whether its bit length is at least \(2^{48}+2\), a 49-bit constant. This is equivalent to \(q\ge2K\). The \(q=0\) branch is direct. Each length counter starts at its low bit, increments over the trailing ones and returns. Over \(d\) increments there are at most \(2d+1\) toggles, plus their return tours. Delimiters, one constant comparison and restoring the input head fit \(256(n+1)+512\) transitions. Fewer than 32 small tapes each use at most \(n+50\) cells. The direct branch including dispatch therefore costs at most \(2^{12}n^2\) steps for every positive \(n\) in that branch. Dormant large-input heads are part of the common space constant. For large inputs the dispatcher alone is run, with its linear cost included in the fast budget.
