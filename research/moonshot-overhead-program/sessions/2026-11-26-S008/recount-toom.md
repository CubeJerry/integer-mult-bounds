# Independent six-tape short-product audit

For the actual program in [word-toom.py](word-toom.py), the sharper numerical bound below keeps the classical Toom exponent. It applies to arbitrary unsigned words of the same positive padded length. Let $p(w)=\max(1,w-6)$ and $\alpha=\log_3 5$. Then
$$ T(w)\le 2^{24}p(w)^\alpha-2^{23}p(w),\qquad S(w)\le256(w+1). $$
The original [local count](word-toom.md) is sufficient. This improvement to the word bound does not change the asymptotic rate established for the complete multiplier.

## Exact local content and clock

The argument and product ports are little-endian. A register in a frame is a fixed track of one of $8w+64$ columns. The machine’s unbounded arrays and its pending return stack are on tapes. The 22-bit column alphabet, five stage tags, sign, individual instruction choices and bounded carry states are finite and independent of $w$. The base probe always reads at most nine bits. Thus its Python shifts on these values range over a fixed set. Within the small branch the eight shifts/additions use a fixed sixteen-bit state. Each pure-control computation between two physical actions can be folded into the finite transition table. Charging its stated 2,048 steps also permits a bit-serial implementation. At $w\le8$ the emitted tape actions themselves total $26w+31$ before the three-step top-level return: $4w+1$ reading the pair, $4w+8$ restoring both heads, $4w$ writing the $2w$ bits, $4w+4$ returning the product head, and $10w+18$ clearing and restoring both inputs. When $w>8$ the failed nine-bit probe costs exactly 80 actions.

For nonnegative limbs, $v_1+v_-=2(c_0+c_2+c_4)$, $v_1-v_-=2(c_1+c_3)$, $v_2-c_0-4c_2-16c_4=2c_1+8c_3$, and $u-t=3c_3$. Each actual division is by a positive divisor on a nonnegative exact multiple, even when the minus-one evaluation changes sign. Low-to-high track updates implement two's-complement reduction modulo $2^W$. The intermediates in these four identities fit the stated signed width, so their high-to-low divisions read their true nonnegative representatives. The minus-one child receives absolute magnitudes and returns an unsigned product. Its stored XOR of the two signs restores the signed value. No child depends on an unrepresented unbounded host sign or carry. The $m$ initially unshifted columns in each Horner update consume no temporary bit; the later columns read the preceding result values in order. Every resulting nonnegative partial fits in the final product.

For $w\ge9$, $m=\lceil w/3\rceil$. An internal frame plus its two boundary cells has length $8w+66\le16w$. The actual child width $m+3\le3w/4$. Successive smaller frames append immediately beyond the preceding right marker. A completed frame is erased in reverse, and the parent exposes its own tagged left end by a backward walk from its right boundary. This return never scans a larger ancestor’s parked frames. Siblings therefore reuse the same space. All live frame intervals on a path have total length at most $64w$ plus fixed outer boundary cells. For the other five tapes the longest uses are $w,w,2w,\lceil w/3\rceil,8w+64$, respectively, with constant boundary cells per tape. These cover the visited union even when the arguments or interpolated result have many high zero bits. They imply the stated numerical space coefficient, including the small root case.

## Solving the same physical recurrence

The local charge at $w\ge9$ is at most $2^{20}(w+1)\le2^{22}p(w)$. There are five child calls and each has $p(m+3)\le p(w)/3$. Because $p(w)\ge3$ at a nonbase, the comparison is within $[1,\infty)$. The function $F(z)=2^{24}z^\alpha-2^{23}z$ increases there. For $1\le u\le v$, $v^\alpha-u^\alpha\ge(v-u)u^{\alpha-1}\ge v-u$. Since $3^\alpha=5$,
$$ F(p)-5F(p/3)=\tfrac23,2^{23}p\ \ge\ 2^{22}p.$$
The base bound $2,048$ fits $F(1)$. Induction on $w$ therefore proves the displayed time inequality. No real exponent is computed by the machine.

As one explicit outer simplification, $5^{15}=30,517,578,125<31,381,059,609=3^{22}$, so $3/2-\alpha>1/30$. For $k\ge2^{2048}$ the four-factor logarithmic bill $(1+\log_2 k)^4\le k^{1/30}$. To check all lengths, put $j=\lfloor\log_2\log_2 k\rfloor\ge11$. Then $4(j+2)\le2^j/30$ by induction from $j=11$, while $1+\log_2 k\le2^{j+2}$. Thus on this explicit range a bounded number of $O(k)$-bit word products times even four such logarithms fit a constant times $k^{3/2}$. Numerical coefficients and the actual number of outer calls still have to be accounted for in that caller.

A port can retain two adjacent or separate bounded input records and normalize their two's-complement signs for at most $128(w+1)$ extra transitions when the source heads arrive at the respective records and the destination is positioned for append. Construct a unary $w$ ruler from its canonical binary width in at most $26w$ transitions by [the exact converter](recount-node.md). Copy the two input records simultaneously to private stacks while scanning that ruler, using at most $4w+5$ tests, transfers and ruler resets. Record the signs in two finite flags at the high-bit ends. For MSB-first inputs, reading the stacks backwards supplies little-endian argument bits, and one carry flag per negative word implements bitwise complement plus one. Each step consumes and clears its stack cell. Returning the two fresh argument heads to home costs a second traversal; allow $8w+16$ for this reverse copy and return. For LSB-first inputs, use one additional forward stack traversal and return within $8w+16$. The signed output fits $2w$ bits. Read its unsigned product low-to-high into one staging stack, apply the required output sign with carry, and emit either high-first by a backward traversal or low-first by a rewind and forward traversal. Erase the consumed output/staging words and restore their heads within $24w+32$. Clearing the ruler and the fixed port markings takes at most $4w+12$. These ceilings sum to less than $128(w+1)$ for every $w\ge1$. Positioning the outer record streams before entry remains the enclosing caller’s operation.

## Independent replay

[The independent driver](recount-toom.py) runs the same finite program with a different tape implementation. It detects movement below a home delimiter, checks every written symbol’s finite range, requires all original scratch cells to be blank on return, and compares the exact unsigned result with ordinary multiplication. It singles out negative, positive and zero evaluations at minus one, top-bit padding, both boundary limbs and random independent words. Its 87 calls through width 110 produced 194 negative return tags, a largest count of 7,180,308 actions and a largest union of 3,105 cells. Each small case satisfied the exact base clock, and every case met the declared space bound.

```sh
python3 -B research/moonshot-overhead-program/sessions/2026-11-26-S008/recount-toom.py
```

The clock and space estimates are written proofs of this physical subroutine. The interpolation is the classical Toom-3 identity, already used in the source contribution. This independent audit did not formalize a new multiplication algorithm.
