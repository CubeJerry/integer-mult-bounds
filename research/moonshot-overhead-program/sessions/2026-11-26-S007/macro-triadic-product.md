# A single polynomial product at arbitrary polynomial-logarithmic binary precision

Write $A_H=\mathbb Z/2^H\mathbb Z$, $T=\lceil\log_2(s+2)\rceil$. For each fixed $K>0$ and rational $0<\eta<1/2$, two arbitrary length-$s$ polynomials over $A_H$, given in increasing natural coefficient order with $1\le H\le (1+T)^K$, admit ordinary multiplication in $O_{K,\eta}(sH T/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ steps, using $O_{K,\eta}(sH)$ distinct cells through halt on a fixed deterministic finite-alphabet multitape machine. Only short integer arithmetic in the proof uses the known $O(h\log h)$ integer multiplier. The two balanced transforms below remove the top-level full-volume triadic sweep cost. ## Uniform polynomial batch over the literal coefficient ring

For each fixed $\gamma>0$, $K_0$ and $K_1$, the [occupied binary batch construction](lane-packed-convolution.md) extends to $D^\gamma\le J\le D^{K_0}$ independent pairs over $A_H$, $H\le(1+\log D)^{K_1}$. A batch costs $O(JDH\log(D+2)/(1+\log\log(D+3))^\eta)$ steps with $O(JDH)$ cells and returns all ordinary products in packet order. The [modulo-four batch proof](mod-four-batch.md) supplies the nonfield parts of this argument. Lift the same degree-$e$ binary defining polynomial to $A_H$, forming a free local unramified algebra. An odd order root of $X^j-1$ lifts from the residue field by Newton steps with unit derivative. All needed distinct-node differences and denominators are units by reduction. Residue-field matrix pivots lift to units; a nonunit scalar $u$ in a shear is the sum of the two units $1$ and $u-1$. Every unit-shear, signed inverse and borrowed-scratch commutator is an identity over the ring. The mask-specific compiler avoids any characteristic-two-only sum of scalars. The full tensor-shear supplier is now the [56-letter all-precision construction](precision-striped-pascal.md). Each address transports one complete $H$-bit value for each true independent component. Initial setup costs $\operatorname{poly}(e,H,T_D)=D^{o(1)}$ with $T_D=\lceil\log(D+2)\rceil$. The local routing tables have the previous $T_D^{O(1)}$ size, separate from the actual scalar factors.

The short arithmetic invoice can use the conventional triadic recursion over $A_H$ independently of this new result. At order $3^Q$, decompose into $2n$ child products in $S_m=A_H[Y]/\Phi_m(Y)$ as in the first-carry note. A complete radix-three stage performs a bounded number of contiguous $H$-bit additions/subtractions per coefficient, monomial rotations/folds, and linear scans. Interchanging individual bit positions of binary-padded axes costs linear bit-volume per exchange, hence $O(Q)$ per bit for the unaccelerated transform and layout. Inverse length-$3^v$ scales may be applied once per transform by multiplication with $3^{-v}\bmod 2^H$, at $O(H(1+\log(H+2))^C)$ per coefficient by short integer arithmetic. This also bounds multiplication by the scalar $1/3$ in untwisting. Initialization at bounded $Q$ uses a constant number of such base products per coefficient. Thus at polynomial length $z$ the conventional independent supplier costs $O(zH(1+\log(z+2))(1+\log\log(z+3))(1+\log(H+2))^C)$; its normalized recurrence is bounded by $\tau(Q)\le2\tau(\lfloor Q/2\rfloor+1)+O((1+Q)(1+\log(H+2))^C)$. A sequential suspension/return schedule reuses child scratch. Even using per-call time as an upper bound on its space is sufficient inside the batch, since its short argument satisfies $\log z=O(T_D^{4/5}\log T_D)$. The jet products and constant applications have total short operand length $O(er)$ per jet, so after all $O(\log T_D)$ synchronized slots this arithmetic costs $O(T_D^{4/5}\operatorname{polylog}(T_D+2))$ per actual bit. The fixed number of external sorts and all positive-precision Pascal, occupied-lane, group and space bills in the cited proofs therefore establish the uniform batch. No fast single long product is called in this argument. To respect the striped hypothesis inside a mask sector, take a slab of $2^{2j}$ complete $H$-bit scalar lanes, with $H$ padded to a power of two only for physical movements. For $j>T_D^\rho$ the true width $Per$ counts whole slots and exceeds that slab, and $H\le T_D^{K_1}\le j^{K_1/\rho}$ meets the local precision bound. For $j\le T_D^\rho$ use $j$ direct scans of full $H$-bit sums. Removing and restoring each scalar word's binary padding adds linear work and a constant volume factor.

## Two balanced transform factors

Take $M=3^Q$ minimally with $\varphi(M)>2s-2$ and absorb bounded $s$ separately, so $M=\Theta(s)$. The input product fits without reduction in $S_M=A_H[U]/\Phi_M(U)$. Use one top Cantor–Kaltofen layer: $m=3^{\lfloor Q/2\rfloor+1}$, $n=M/m=3^v$, so $m=3n$ or $9n$, $n\varphi(m)=\varphi(M)$. Group the input in the established order as an $n$-term polynomial with coefficients in $S_m$, where $Y=U^n$. Two cyclic convolutions, one after twisting by monomials in $Y$, suffice. Each cyclic convolution consists of two forward transforms at $\rho=Y^{m/n}$, $n$ pointwise products in $S_m$, and one inverse. Untwisting, solving the two-by-two reconstruction, and folding back are the same exact integral identities as in the original layer; only $3$ and $n$ are inverted. The $2n$ pointwise ordinary pre-reduction products of length $\varphi(m)$ form a valid batch, $2n=\varphi(m)$ or $\varphi(m)/3$. Fold their outputs modulo $Y^m-1$ and subtract the high third from the lower two. Its total cost fits the theorem.

To transform the array $(a_j)_{j<n}$ choose $n=AB$, where $A=3^{\lfloor v/2\rfloor}$ and $B=3^{\lceil v/2\rceil}$. Both are positive asymptotically and differ by at most three. Write $j=j_0+A j_1$, $0\le j_0<A$, $0\le j_1<B$, and $k=k_1+B k_0$, $0\le k_1<B$, $0\le k_0<A$. The identity
$$\rho^{jk}=(\rho^A)^{j_1k_1}\,\rho^{j_0k_1}\,(\rho^B)^{j_0k_0}$$
gives first $A$ length-$B$ transforms using root $Y^{m/B}$, then one pointwise twiddle $Y^{(m/n)j_0k_1}$ per ring word, then $B$ length-$A$ transforms using $Y^{m/A}$. Every twiddle is monomial multiplication in $S_m$. Temporarily append the missing third of its $m$ coefficients as zeros, rotate by the given exponent modulo $m$ and subtract the high third from both lower thirds. The coefficient lists are traversed sequentially in $O(mH)$ steps per full word. Exponent arithmetic costs $\operatorname{poly}(T)$ per word, absorbed in its $\Theta(mH)$ bits. For inverse use the negative exponents and multiply the final words once by the actual scalar $n^{-1}\bmod2^H$ for normalized inversion. This final scaling costs $O(sH(1+\log(H+2))^C)$.

## Each large cyclotomic word contains independent smaller transform lanes

For either factor put $L\in\{A,B\}$, $h=m/L$, $W=Y^h$. Since $\Phi_m(Y)=Y^{2m/3}+Y^{m/3}+1=\Phi_L(Y^h)$, collecting powers of $Y$ by their remainder modulo $h$ gives the exact free-module decomposition
$$ S_m=\bigoplus_{u=0}^{h-1} Y^u S_L(W),\qquad S_L=A_H[W]/(W^{2L/3}+W^{L/3}+1). $$
The canonical coefficient of $Y^{ht+u}$ is coefficient $t$ of lane $u$, $t<\varphi(L)$. Every transform factor is a power of $W$ and preserves these arbitrary coefficient lanes. The entire step consists of $J=(n/L)(m/L)=M/L^2$ length-$L$ transforms over $S_L$. The interstage twiddle is applied after returning all lanes to $S_m$ order and may mix these lanes there.

With $v_2=2^{-1}\bmod L$ and sign $\varepsilon\in\{+1,-1\}$, the unnormalized output on one packet is
$$ W^{\varepsilon v_2 i^2}\sum_{j<L}\big(W^{\varepsilon v_2 j^2}x_j\big) W^{-\varepsilon v_2(i-j)^2}. $$
This is a cyclic convolution using a prescribed $S_L$ kernel. The identity only requires $W^L=1$. Monomial multiplication rotates a length-$L$ padded word and subtracts the high third, costing $O(LH)$ steps per actual word. Chirp exponents cost $\operatorname{poly}(T)$ per word, absorbed in its $\Theta(LH)$ bits. Write the kernel once and replay its scans. At $p=\varphi(L)$ pack each outer coefficient polynomial in the power basis at stride $2p-1$. One ordinary $A_H$ product of length $D=L(2p-1)$ contains every ordinary-convolution row with $W$-degree $<2p-1$. Reduce each row modulo $W^L-1$ and then $W^{2L/3}=-1-W^{L/3}$. Fold outer rows with equal index modulo $L$. These are boundedly many complete scans and $H$-bit additions per coefficient. The opposite transform uses the opposite chirp signs.

Both $L^2=\Theta(n)$ and $D=\Theta(n)$. The full packet count $J=M/L^2=\Theta(n)$ lies in $[D^{1/2},D^2]$ for large $n$, $JD=O(s)$ and $\log D=\Theta(T)$. Actual $H\le(1+T)^K$ meets the polynomial-logarithmic batch requirement after adjusting its fixed exponent and cutoff. The all-precision batch above therefore supplies each macro step in $O_\eta(sH T/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ time and $O(sH)$ cells. Correlated signals and repeated chirps are eligible since the supplier is correct on every assignment of its disjoint coefficient registers. The two dependent transform stages run in order.

## Actual order, space and integer guard

Each coordinate change involves a fixed number of axes. When the active $L$-index is innermost among the outer digits, the form is $[\text{outside fibre}][j<L][t<p][u<h][\text{$H$-bit coefficient}]$. Reorder to $[\text{outside fibre}][u][j][t][\text{coefficient}]$, process all packed jobs, and reverse to reconstitute the $S_m$ words. The first and last Cooley–Tukey transposes use the explicit digit orders above. Pad the fixed number of participating axes to their next powers of two, including the low $H$-bit word width if convenient. This inflates size by a fixed constant. The [raw-bit position-permutation proof](structured-orbit-directory.md) performs any fixed rearrangement of the resulting $O(T+\log H)=O(T)$ address positions in $O_\rho(sH T^\rho(1+\log T))+\operatorname{poly}(T,H)$ time and $O(sH)$ cells, including at $H=1$. Input padding and later compaction take stream scans; binary counters at fixed component boundaries can be advanced with amortized constant head motion, including the periodic low-word padding. There are boundedly many macro transforms per top layer. The held operand, full transform banks, batch scratch and local word buffer occupy fixed disjoint $O(sH)$ intervals with common reset origins. The time and distinct-cell claims for one polynomial pair follow.

For two $N$-bit unsigned integers choose $w=\lceil\log_2(N+2)\rceil$, $s=\lceil N/w\rceil$, and $H=2w+\lceil\log_2(s+1)\rceil+1$. Interpret their $w$-bit blocks as $A_H$ coefficients. Every true integer polynomial product coefficient is at most $s(2^w-1)^2<2^H$. The ordinary modular product returns these nonnegative coefficients in order; propagate base-$2^w$ carries by a single pass and $O(H)$ local bits. Now $sH=\Theta(N)$, $T=\Theta(\log(N+2))$ and $H=O(T)$. The elementary-route integer time is $O_\eta(N\log(N+2)/(1+\log\log(N+3))^\eta)$ and cumulative visited space is $O_\eta(N)$. This improves the $O(N\log N)$ construction used for small arithmetic. The campaign's accepted $N\sqrt{\log N}\,2^{O(\Phi)}$ envelope is asymptotically faster; this corollary leaves that integer frontier in place. The long polynomial theorem and the first lifted-CRT carry benefit independently. [Classical integer arithmetic on short words](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2021/193-2/p04)
