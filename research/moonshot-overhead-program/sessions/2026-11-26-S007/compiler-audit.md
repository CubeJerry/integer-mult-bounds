# Independent review of the wide-batch binary convolution compiler

Let $T=\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil$ and $V=nB$. With the [wide Pascal theorem](striped-pascal.md), the positive-characteristic construction gives $O(VT/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ fixed-tape steps and $O(V)$ visited cells for fixed $0<\eta<1/2$ and $n^{1+\delta}\le B\le n^K$ with fixed $\delta>0,K>1+\delta$.

## The source words

The abstract word definition allows $U^{\otimes j}$ blocks. The actual positive-characteristic construction gives the stronger form needed here. Every radix-three stage consists of disjoint triples, scalings and permutations. Gaussian elimination on a triple uses a bounded number of elementary directed pair additions. Swap coordinates to reverse the orientation. Diagonal conjugation normalizes a nonzero coefficient to one. A zero coefficient is an identity. Thus a local elementary shear slot consists entirely of disjoint directed pairs and singletons. [Radix words, lines 81–104](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex#L81-L104)

The Toeplitz word implements padding and truncation as disjoint pair updates into separate registers. Its circulant step transforms the source and destination, performs one layer of scalar pair additions, and inverts both transforms. Composing two updates restores the arbitrary initial scratch value by cancellation. The lower triangular replacement restores its whole second register by a commutator. Reversal covers the upper triangular case, and successive Newton factors reuse the scratch. This checks the intermediate operations as well as the final matrix. [Occupied workspace, lines 155–217](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex#L155-L217)

For the actual scratch ratio $C_0$, group $k$ to $2k-1$ original prime axes, $k=1+\lceil\log_2\lceil C_0\rceil\rceil$. Every borrowed entry belongs to that same group. Disjoint fibre sets act in parallel, and the bounded number of fibres per set and axes per group act sequentially. The resulting group word acts on exactly $a_g=\prod_{i\in g}p_i$ positions. Pad with identity letters to align monomial and shear slots, then tensor the letters at like slots. The product of slot tensors equals the tensor of complete words. Its depth is $O(\log T)$ and there are $G=\Theta(T/\log T)$ groups. [Fibre proof, lines 227–252](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex#L227-L252)

## Two unconditional Pascals enforce every mask

Permute each local directed pair into an even/odd slot with the target first. Pair remaining singletons arbitrarily. Each setting of the high bits selects $U_\lambda=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&\lambda\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, with $\lambda=1$ on a normalized active pair and zero on a passive pair. The formula also handles arbitrary coefficients. The radix-three root supplies $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$. For nonzero $\lambda$ take $(s,t)=(\omega\lambda,\omega^2\lambda)$. For $\lambda=0$ use $(1,1)$. Put $D(s)=\operatorname{diag}(s,1)$. Then
$$
D(s)U D(s)^{-1}D(t)U D(t)^{-1}=U_sU_t=U_{s+t}=U_\lambda.
$$
Tensoring these five slots over all groups gives two unconditional $U^{\otimes G}$ operations on the same low positions and three pointwise $E$-diagonals. Each tensor diagonal at an address is the product of its local entries. This avoids a spectator-dependent bit reorder.

One proposed invariant fails: with one group in its tail and a second on a real pair, the tensor acts as $I\otimes U$ and can change dummy entries. The correct invariant is that each pattern of dummy groups is a separate invariant subspace of the complete letter. Arbitrary dirty dummy values cannot reach native outputs. Zero-filled dummy sectors remain zero. The intermediate phase passes can mix singletons from unrelated lanes, which the composite restores.

## Dimensions and tape geometry

With $u_g=\lceil\log_2(Ga_g)\rceil$, $M_g=\lfloor2^{u_g}/a_g\rfloor$ and $M=\prod_gM_g$, each field has $O(\log T)$ bits and $G\le M_g\le2G$. Total padding satisfies $\prod_g2^{u_g}/(M_ga_g)\le(1+1/G)^G\le\exp(1)$. For $d$ prime axes, $d\le\min_i p_i$ eventually, since $d=\Theta(T/\log T)$ and the primes are $\Theta(T)$. Thus $M\le(2d)^d\le2^dN\le n^{1+o(1)}$. The residual $P=\lceil B/M\rceil$ satisfies $P\ge n^{\delta/2}$ and $PM\le2B$ eventually. With $erN\le9n$, the entire padded extent is $O(V)$. Rounding $e,r,P$ separately to powers of two costs less than a further eightfold. The actual $e,r$ are used by the field and jet arithmetic. Each ordinary output index has at most four source triples: the index fixes the field-coordinate residue modulo $\lceil e/2\rceil$ (at most two choices), then the jet-coordinate residue modulo $\lceil r/2\rceil$ (at most two), then the cyclic coordinate uniquely. Use empty external controls in the simultaneous small-block lemma and the disjoint $u_g$-bit fields as targets. Retain a low residual-lane packet of $\Theta(T)$ bits as the record. The complete address has $\Theta(T)$ positions. Each slot's permutation and support tables together occupy $T^{O(1)}$ bits, even with all lane copies and holes. Precompute zero tests at setup, so the tables required by the source controller are generated from polynomial-$T$ descriptions in polynomial-$T$ time. The field constants used during their construction may be larger. The bit-position corollary extends to a raw-bit permutation, including one that moves the innermost positions. For width $h$, compose the desired permutation with at most $\lceil\log_2h\rceil$ position transpositions to fix that many low positions one at a time. Apply the high-position permutation on $\Theta(h)$-bit records. Undo the transpositions on the raw stream using the interchange theorem with $u=1$, at $O(V)$ each. This yields time $O(Vh^\rho(1+\log h))$ for fixed $1/2<\rho<1$. For both unconditional Pascals, place the $G$ low row bits immediately above $2G$ residual lane bits, with all other indices outside. Since $\log P\gg G$, each slab has width exactly $(2^G)^2$, within the striped theorem's two-sided aspect requirement. ## Uniformity, short products and visited space

Use the source's containing field of degree $e=\mathrm{lcm}_i(p_i-1)\varphi(3^j)$ and a deterministic binary degree-$e$ irreducible for its power basis. For each required small order $m$, the roots of $X^m-1$ are distinct and in this field. For every basis value $\beta$, compute the absolute trace of $\beta X$ by repeated modular squaring and split the current polynomial factors by gcd. Nondegeneracy of the field trace pairing separates every pair of roots. Exact order tests find a primitive root. This costs $\operatorname{poly}(e,T)=n^{o(1)}$ setup steps and cells. Scanning all local field and address tables once per global address is paid by its $P$ lanes. [Extension orders, lines 94–115](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/packing.tex#L94-L115) · [Shoup's deterministic irreducible construction](https://www.shoup.net/papers/irred.pdf)

A slot charges $O(VG/(1+\log G)^\eta)$ for the two Pascals, $O(VT^\rho(1+\log T)^3)$ for routing, and $O(VT^{4/5}\operatorname{polylog}(T)4^{\log^*n})$ for scalar/jet products, whose length per jet totals $O(er)$ and whose maximum length satisfies $\log(er)=O(T^{4/5}\log T)$. Multiplying by $O(\log T)$ slots fits the clock. The constant number of endpoint sorts has $n^{O(1)}$ records of width at least $n^{\delta/2}$ and $O(T)$-bit keys. A short multiplication may occupy as many cells as its full stated time, still $n^{o(1)}$ per invocation. Successive jets and Pascal slabs use common local banks with monotone outer input/output heads. The two inputs, output, active and suspended banks have constant multiplicity, and each slot reuses the same $O(V)$ intervals. This bounds the union of cells ever visited. ## Independent replay

All 126 masks of ranks 1 through 6 passed on 5,460 complete field-basis columns (299,592 entries), including opposite orientations and every nonzero coefficient in $\mathbf F_4$. Native sizes $(3,5),(5,7),(3,5,7)$ exercised bumped fields with genuine tail holes. Both smaller shapes passed on a complete basis. The native fixtures compared 150,528 field entries, including 24 mixed payloads. Dirty-hole inputs caused 3,476 changes confined to dummy rows and zero native leaks. Removing the second full Pascal was detected at all six ranks. The finite replay checks the new pair compiler. Its asymptotic tape and Fourier costs use the cited theorems.
