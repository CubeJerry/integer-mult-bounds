# Literal CRT at each fixed 2-adic precision

For every fixed integer $H\ge1$, set $A_H=\mathbf Z/2^H$. For $b=3c$, $q=2^b$, one deterministic fixed finite-alphabet multitape machine maps an arbitrary coefficient polynomial in $A_H[X]$ of degree $<q$ to all its monomial remainders at the literal binary FS1 factors, and conversely, in $O_H(qb)$ steps and $O_H(q)$ visited cells. With the [accelerated single-product theorem](macro-triadic-product.md) and the [structured FS1 binary directory](structured-orbit-directory.md), its clock sharpens to $O_{H,\eta}(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$ for every fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$. The machine and the implied constants may depend on $H$.

## Polynomial products over the fixed coefficient ring

In the [modulo-four compiler](mod-four-batch.md), replace its small unramified scalar ring by $S_H=A_H[t]/(t^4+t+1)$. The monomial/Fourier four-coordinate word can be lifted by induction. Given a word $V$ correct modulo $2^j$, its target error modulo $2^{j+1}$ is $I+2^jM$. In that single layer the product of corrections adds the corresponding residue matrices since $2j\ge j+1$. The sixteen conjugated residue diagonal directions spanning $M_4(\mathbf F_{16})$ in the modulo-four proof are independent of $j$. Lift the conjugating monomial/ternary-Fourier words, use diagonals $I+2^j uE_{ii}$, and multiply the required corrections. Each stage gains one bit. The ternary root and odd-order evaluation roots lift by their unit derivatives. The fixed ternary recursion is defined over $\mathbf Z[1/3,\zeta_3]$, and each specialization retains the free repair rank. Elements of fixed $S_H$ occupy a constant number of bits. In the varying-degree extension of $A_H$, every nonunit shear coefficient $\lambda$ is the sum of the two units $1,\lambda-1$; normalization and the active/passive pair construction continue to work. The short independent Cantor–Kaltofen supplier and its one-level outer split use odd transform lengths and a fixed number of $A_H$-arithmetic operations per base coefficient. This gives single length-$D$ polynomial multiplication over $A_H$ in $O_H(D\log(D+2))$ steps and $O_H(D)$ visited cells. The batch construction and the short ordinary multiplier remain separate algorithms.

## An integral ghost calculation for all global binary coordinates

For $P=\sum_i a_iX^i$, take the canonical integer representative of each $a_i\in A_H$. Put $R=\mathbf Z[X]/(X^q-X)$, free abelian on $1,\ldots,X^{q-1}$. In length-$H$ 2-typical Witt vectors of $R$, sum $a_i$ copies of the Teichmüller monomial $[X^i]$ and call its coordinates $C_j$. The ghost homomorphisms give
$$
 w_j=\sum_i a_i X^{2^j i}=\sum_{h=0}^j 2^h C_h^{\,2^{j-h}}
 \quad(0\le j<H).
$$
The $C_h$ belong to $R$ by the universal integral Witt addition polynomials. At any $z$ with $z^q=z$ in characteristic two, functoriality shows that their binary reductions evaluated at $z$ are exactly the Witt coordinates of $P([z])$, where the coefficients of $P$ act as ordinary integers. [Witt vectors and the ghost homomorphisms, §3.2](https://kskedlaya.org/prismatic/sec_Witt.html)

Only the binary reductions of earlier coordinates need to be stored. If $A\equiv B\bmod2$, successive squaring gives $A^{2^s}\equiv B^{2^s}\bmod 2^{s+1}$. Thus $w_j-\sum_{h<j}2^h\widehat C_h^{\,2^{j-h}}$ modulo $2^{j+1}$ gives $2^j C_j$ modulo $2^{j+1}$ for any binary coefficient lifts $\widehat C_h$. Read the $j$th coefficient bits. The monic relation ensures that coefficientwise division in the chosen basis is legitimate. Every multiplication is an ordinary length-$q$ product over the fixed precision followed by one fold of exponents $i\ge q$ to $i-q+1$. In $w_j$, multiplication of a positive exponent by $2^j$ modulo $q-1$ is cyclic rotation of its $b$ address bits, with both endpoint words fixed. Its raw binary address-position permutation costs $O_{H,\rho}(qb^\rho(1+\log b))$ for any fixed $1/2<\rho<1$. A finite number depending on $H$ of these products and binary FS1 calls obtains all coordinates of $P([z])$ in every actual $K_g=\mathbf F_2[X]/g$. At their length $q$, the accelerated product and the complete structured binary CRT each cost $O_{H,\eta}(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$.

The early fold matters. With $P=1+X^{q-1}$ over the unquotiented integer polynomial ring, $C_j\bmod2$ has degree $(2^j-1)(q-1)$ for $j\ge1$. In $Y=X^{q-1}$, the leading term of $C_0^{2^j}=(1+Y)^{2^j}$ below $Y^{2^j}$ has coefficient $2^j$, while the previously computed higher coordinates contribute only degrees $\le2^j-2$. Thus the coefficient at $Y^{2^j-1}$ in $C_j$ is $-1$. The quotient calculation avoids allocating this extent.

## The literal local roots, coefficients and inverse

Use $W_H(K_g)$ at each degree-$d$ irreducible, with $z=X\bmod g$. Write the literal root as $\alpha=[z]+y$, $y\in2W_H(K_g)$. For any coefficient polynomial $P$, the ordinary Hasse derivative is $P^{[m]}(X)=\sum_{i\ge m}\binom{i}{m}a_iX^{i-m}$. Since $y^H=0$,
$$
 P(\alpha)=\sum_{m=0}^{H-1}P^{[m]}([z])\,y^m.
$$
Generate the global Hasse coefficient streams by scanning; Pascal's recurrence maintains the fixed number of binomial coefficients modulo $2^H$. Apply the ghost calculation to every needed derivative, and use the binary CRT for its coordinates. Locally apply the same universal calculation to the degree-$d$ polynomial $g$ and its derivatives. For these short inputs one may use raw degree $O_H(d)$ polynomials and reduce their binary components modulo $g$. Newton iteration on the displayed polynomial in $y$, starting at zero, lifts the simple factor root in $\lceil\log_2 H\rceil$ steps up to a fixed base convention. The constant term is even and the derivative has residue $g'(z)\ne0$. If the derivative itself is evaluated to full precision, include $g^{[H]}$ as well. A unit inverse uses one extended binary GCD followed by Newton lifting. Local Witt addition and multiplication use their fixed integral coordinate polynomials and compact arithmetic in $K_g$. Thus all this setup and work costs $O_H(d\,\operatorname{polylog}(d+2))$ per factor. The literal quotient is identified with $W_H(K_g)$ by evaluation at $\alpha$: the power basis gives a square map between free rank-$d$ modules over $A_H$ whose reduction modulo two is an isomorphism.

Given $v=P(\alpha)$, extract its first Witt coordinate as the unique binary power-basis polynomial $r_0$ of degree $<d$. Evaluate this specified $r_0(\alpha)$ by the same short ghost/Taylor procedure. Subtract it and divide by two into $W_{H-1}(K_g)$. On a vector with zero first component the quotient discards that component and takes the unique square root of every remaining component, since multiplication by two in characteristic-two Witt coordinates is Verschiebung after Frobenius. Repeat on the truncated literal root to recover all actual coefficient digits $r_0,r_1,\ldots$. The short square root uses the parity-column identity in the [first-carry proof](first-witt-carry.md). This works on every degree, including proper subfields.

For the inverse, reduce the supplied arbitrary $A_H$-coefficients of the literal residues modulo two and apply the binary inverse CRT. Call the recovered binary polynomial $F_0$. Use the forward procedure to subtract all literal remainders of $F_0$ at the current precision. Every coefficient is even. Divide the coefficients by two and repeat at precision $H-1$. The process recovers the original monomial coefficient digits for any legal independent residue tuple. All binary transforms and polynomial products are sequential, the factor directories have total degree $q$, and the number of active array banks is fixed for each $H$. To generate the literal factors once in the sharper clock, feed $x^d$ to the structured adapter at every selected point of actual degree $d$. Its minimal polynomial $g$ is monic, so the unique monomial remainder of $x^d$ consists exactly of its $d$ low coefficients. The known degree adds the leading one, including at $x=0,1$. Computing $d$ by the normal-word period and $x^d$ by short powers costs $O(q\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ across $O(q/b)$ point records, and the same priced structured adapter returns every factor in order. This gives both stated clocks and the visited-cell bound.

For variable precision, this implementation asks for Witt coordinates of many different Hasse derivatives. Even discarding coordinates annihilated by $y^m$, it allocates $H(H+1)/2$ global binary transform calls in its general schedule. The lifted-word length, local Witt coordinate polynomials, ring operations and tape constants also depend on $H$. The result gives a family of fixed-precision machines; the required uniform growing-precision integer-multiplication overhead remains open.

For the integer caller, put $H=\Theta(b)$ and $qH=\Theta(n)$. If the displayed schedule materializes its $H(H+1)/2$ separate binary FS1 outputs, each occupies $q$ bits. Writing these outputs alone takes $\Omega(qH^2)=\Omega(nb)$ fixed-tape steps even with free arithmetic and reusable space. This rules out the $O(n\sqrt b(1+\log b)^C)$ target for every fixed $C$ for this separate-call implementation. A fused method could share outputs or avoid materializing them; this count places no lower bound on that different computation.
