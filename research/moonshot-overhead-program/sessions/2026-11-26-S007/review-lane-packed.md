# Independent review of the batched binary tape convolution

I checked the hypotheses of the three external ingredients and followed the native tape addresses through the input, one synchronized layer, and the ordinary output. The reviewed [construction](lane-packed-convolution.md) yields $O_\eta(nB\log n/(1+\log\log(n+3))^\eta)$ steps and $O(nB)$ distinct cells for $n^{1+\delta}\le B\le n^K$ and fixed $\delta>0,K,0<\eta<1/2$. Its proof uses an ordinary binary multiplier on shorter jets with no recursive use of this batch theorem.

## The power basis and the number of native coordinates

Write $T=\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil$. The shifted-prime lemma supplies distinct primes $p\in[CT/2,CT]$ with product exceeding $n^2$ such that the largest prime factor of every $p-1$ is at most $(CT)^{4/5}$. A trial sieve finds the list. The source proves the lemma by Bombieri–Vinogradov and does not need an individual prime progression hypothesis. Take the explicit, possibly larger extension degree $e=\operatorname{lcm}_p(p-1)\varphi(v)$, where $v$ is the largest power of three at most $8CT$. Its bit logarithm is $O(T^{4/5}\log T)$. The comparison $e=n^{o(1)}$ allows a polynomial in $e$; it does not allow enumerating the $2^e$ field elements.

Shoup's deterministic fixed-characteristic algorithm constructs an irreducible binary degree-$e$ defining polynomial. With its power generator $\theta$, the first $e$ powers are a basis. There is no need for $\theta$ itself to generate the multiplicative group. Every required cyclotomic polynomial splits and is squarefree in this field. For a binary basis element $u$ of $E$, modular squarings compute the trace polynomial $\sum_{i<e}(uX)^{2^i}$ modulo a factor. Gcd splits between the roots with trace zero or one. The trace pairing is nondegenerate, hence the $e$ basis elements distinguish every distinct root pair. Repeating the splitting yields linear factors; all roots of the initial cyclotomic polynomial have the prescribed exact order. No search of size $2^e$ occurs. The trial sieve, field algorithm, trace partitions, local linear algebra, and constants for all the short Fourier words cost $\operatorname{poly}(e,T)=n^{o(1)}$ even if a fixed multitape simulation has polynomial overhead. The chosen positive extension contains $\mathbf F_4$ as well. The longest active prime prefix satisfies $n/(CT)^2<eN\le n/(CT)$, so $\log_2 N=T-o(T)$ and the number $d$ of prime axes is $\Theta(T/\log T)$. For $r$ in the stated range, $erN\in[8n,9n]$. Let $E_0=\lceil e/2\rceil$, $R=\lceil r/2\rceil$, $J_0=\lceil N/2\rceil$. Then $E_0RJ_0\ge n$. The sum of two occupied $\theta$-coordinates is below $e$, the sum of their jet exponents is below $r$, and the sum of their cyclic exponents is below $N$. The mixed-radix integer $a+E_0t+E_0Rj$ is additive on monomials. A returned index has at most two possible $a$ and then at most two possible $t$, and $j$ is fixed by these choices. The exit's runwise xor handles every collision.

## Physical input, masks, and output

An axis's local Fourier map is an invertible word on occupied registers. The radix-three constant transforms are products of permutations, scalings and individual pair shears. Toeplitz additions, the two commutators, and Newton factors only compose or take disjoint copies of such words, so before tensor synchronization the shear slots are disjoint directed pairs. The cited source explicitly restores arbitrary scratch values. Grouping a bounded number of axes costs $O(\log T)$ slots, not a product over axis lengths. Permutation locations, masks and their inverse tables are of size $T^{O(1)}$ in each local block. Their preparation may inspect larger field coefficients once. The resulting explicit address-only tables can be read by the polynomial-$T$ generators required by the physical small-block lemma. Arithmetic scalars remain on a separate setup tape. [OpenAI, local words and occupied-fibre proof](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex)

For $G=\Theta(T/\log T)$ grouped axes, $M_g=\lfloor2^{k_g}/a_g\rfloor\ge G$ implies a bounded product of dyadic padding ratios. Also $M=\prod_gM_g\le(2d)^d\le 2^dN$, since every chosen prime exceeds $d$ for large $T$. Thus $P=\lceil B/M\rceil\ge n^{\delta/2}$ and $PM\le 2B$. Each original lane is exactly one $(\ell,s_1,\dots,s_G)$, including an explicitly zero-padded final bank. Intake transposes real packet-major data, forms true mixed-radix addresses in $T^{O(1)}$ operations per $P$-bit strip, then uses one wide sort per operand. The real height of $nM$ records has logarithm $O(T)$; $P$ beats a fixed power of that height. Their $O(T)$-bit keys do not create a new short-record sort. The exit costs one further whole-volume sort and the physical transpose back. There are no sorts between synchronized letters.

In each normalized local block, put genuine directed pairs of each independent lane digit at adjacent even/odd positions. Couple the remaining singletons arbitrarily and assign them an inactive flag. At fixed high prefix each low bit pair is either exactly a true local $U$ in its own lane digit or identity. Different native lane digits can be adjacent only in such identity pairs. The active positions depend on that prefix, not on the $2^G$ low suffix. One physical low-bit gather, independent per-prefix permutations, streamed stripes and inverse moves implement the tensor; their workspace heads return only over the current prefix or slab, not the entire parent. Alternatively $U_s U_t$ has off-diagonal $s+t$, so $(s,t)=(\omega,\omega^2)$ and $(1,1)$ implement the same exact active and inactive matrices via two complete $U$ layers. Characteristic two is used in both identities.

A direct application of the striped theorem to all $W=Per$ spectator bits at once would exceed its upper lane-width hypothesis for small $j=|A|$. The manuscript's slab compiler corrects this. For $j\le T^\rho$ use $j$ linear scans per bit. Otherwise $2^{2j}$ is much larger than $T$, and $W\ge n^{\delta/2}>2^{2G}$ after a fixed startup cutoff. Round $W$ to a power of two once and split every active-parent run at $S=2^{2j}$. Exchanging the slab index with the active coordinates while preserving the final low $S$ bits creates $2^j$ rows with $S$ independent bit lanes, exactly in the proved width range. Each address routing call uses the unchanged low $\Theta(T)$-bit record, so the source movement theorem applies through this transformation. Sum volumes over all prefix, passive and slab choices; this yields $O(V)$, with $V=O(nB)$, and the per-slot Pascal term $O(VG/(1+\log G)^\eta)$. The two-dimensional bit transposes at entry and exit quote a separate paid low-tile method, which handles the source theorem's $\Theta(T)$-bits-per-record requirement. ## Short products and cumulative space

Within one native row, the suffix $[\text{lane}][\text{jet}]$ makes each genuinely variable pointwise product contiguous. One stride-$2e-1$ polynomial product handles the two-dimensional unreduced word convolution; keep $r$ row blocks and use a fixed reversed reciprocal to perform $O(r)$ field reductions. Total operand length is $O(er)$ per independent jet and the maximum short operand is $O(er)$; there is no second multiplicative factor $r$. A scalar letter is bounded by the same calculation, even when its input is an arbitrary dense jet. All coefficient-dependent table scans and scalar products for a full native row cost $n^{o(1)}$ beyond the paid arithmetic. The residual $P$ lanes absorb that price. A pointwise product addresses both evolving operand banks in parallel. The external sort, monomial and shear paths never require moving a collection of one-bit records by an unpriced sort.

The unconditional Harvey–van der Hoeven multiplier at size $s=O(er)$ gives per-bit cost $O((1+\log s)4^{\log^*s})=T^{4/5+o(1)}$ across all short products in a slot. Multiplying by $O(\log T)$ remains $o(T/(\log T)^\eta)$. The $(\log T)$ striped costs sum to $O_\eta(T/(\log T)^\eta)$ because $G=\Theta(T/\log T)$. A short call's entire time, and hence the span of any visited interval, is $n^{o(1)}$. A fixed set of cleared, marked origins suffices for these calls, independent of how many distinct input jets are read. The outer operand heads advance on separate tapes; local data-dependent banks are rewound over their own occupied extent. Global sort and permutation banks individually use $O(V)$ distinct cells, and a fixed number of routine types shares or reuses each region. This bounds the union of visited cells over the full computation, including initialization, monitored extents, reset, retained left operand and output.
