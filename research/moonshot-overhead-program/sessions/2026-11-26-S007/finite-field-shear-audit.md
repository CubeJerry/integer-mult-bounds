# The ternary Fourier supplier over four-bit scalars

Fix a real $\theta>1/2$. The November 24 Fourier manuscript gives $U^{\otimes k}$ for the *upper* shear $U=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ over every field in $O_\theta(2^k(1+k)^\theta)$ binary linear gates. In characteristic two the radix in that proof is three. The source proves the gate count and an addressing procedure whose unit is an operation on an $O(k)$-bit word. The following specialization fixes the alphabet, counts independent base rows through the repair, and gives the live-storage and parent-transfer costs of a sequential execution. The cost of its address permutations is a separate hypothesis.

## One positive four-coordinate word

Use $F=\mathbf F_{16}=\mathbf F_2[x]/(x^4+x+1)$ and $\omega=x^5$. In the increasing-power bit encoding $\omega=6$ and $\omega^2=7$. For $D=(\omega^{ij})_{0\le i,j<3}$ and trit negation $J=(0)(1\;2)$, $D^2=J$. The factor $3$ in the general identity is one here. The three-coordinate matrix $C'$ having diagonal one and off-diagonal $\omega^2$ satisfies

$$ C'=D\operatorname{diag}(1,\omega,\omega)D J. $$

The twelve $C'$ slots of Jain's finite word act on specified triples of four coordinates. Expanding every slot by this identity, retaining its triple and conjugating the complete product by binary coordinate reversal gives $U^{\otimes2}$. This gives 24 *forward* $D$ slots, each with one spectator coordinate. Every other slot is monomial over $F$. Consecutive monomials may be combined, leaving at most 25 monomial slots. Reversal changes the lower shear to the upper one. The short Lean replay checks the two identities directly, plus the actual product, every slot type and the count. Both new checks report only `propext`. It imports `lean/MobiusLean/Word.lean` at the pinned Jain commit, whose finite four-coordinate data are used unchanged. Neither the October exponent nor its large repair construction enters the November supplier. Tensoring this fixed word $\lfloor k/2\rfloor$ times gives direct sums of $D^{\otimes j}$ on known fixed-symbol sectors. The total dimensions of the sectors for one slot are exactly $4^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}$. An odd leftover bit costs one pairwise shear layer. Each normalized arithmetic cost is therefore $O_\theta((1+k)^\theta)$. A four-bit scalar multiplication by a fixed constant is a fixed binary linear map. The output of the complete word on binary input is binary. In characteristic two $U^{-1}=U$, so the same circuit supplies the inverse upper shear in the same coordinate order. Reordering the sectors on tapes has its own cost.

## Full-rate specialization and batched calls

The fixed-radix scalar ring is $R_3=\mathbb Z[1/3,\zeta_3]$. It maps to $\mathbf F_4\subset F$ by $1/3\mapsto1$ and $\zeta_3\mapsto\omega$. The repair can use higher $3$-power roots. In particular, $F^\times$ has order 15, so even a primitive ninth root is unavailable in $F$. Expand the *finite free repair extension* over $R_3$ first. Multiplication becomes a fixed matrix over $R_3$, and every ternary Fourier call acts separately on each basis component. Then specialize the entries of these fixed matrices into $F$. In the proof of unit Vandermonde minors the only inverted cyclotomic differences divide a product equal to 3, a unit under this map. The inverse matrices already exist before specialization. [Full-rate repair, lines 188–263](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/repair.tex#L188-L263)

For a free basis $e_1,\ldots,e_t$ the value $\sum_j e_jx_{ij}$ accepts *arbitrary* component inputs $x_{ij}$. The target has base coefficients. Each output has the $t$ expected independently transformed components. Thus a template on $W_0$ extension rows having $\sigma_0$ axis calls becomes a template on $W=tW_0$ independent base rows having $\sigma=t\sigma_0$ calls. No extra factor $t$ enters $\sigma/W$. All four-bit data can take arbitrary $F$ values at recursive boundaries, including after monomial scalings.

For the chosen fixed $\theta$, the source takes $m\equiv1\pmod3$ large enough and fixes $A=2(m+D_0-1)$ so that $\sigma/W<A^\theta$. It then chooses a fixed reserve $r$ with

$$ b_*=(\sigma/W)(1+W/3^r)<A^\theta. $$

For $k$ trits put $f=\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor>0$. The inactive words give $Q=3^{k-Af}$ independent rows. $Q\ge 3^r$ and $Q<3^{r+A}$. Group them in batches of $W$ and fill the last short block with fresh zeros; discard the matching zero-row outputs. There are exactly $\lceil Q/W\rceil\sigma$ directional full-row calls. Each operates on $3^{(A-1)f}$ independent fibers of width $3^f$. Its input length is $3^{Af}$. Dividing this exact total fiber length by $3^k$ gives $\sigma\lceil Q/W\rceil/Q\le b_*$. The final bounded digit transforms have constant normalized arithmetic cost. Each inverse Fourier call is a positive call followed by trit negation, because $3^{-f}=1$ here. [Fixed template, reserve and recurrence](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/kernel.tex#L270-L339)

The construction is effective for fixed rational $\theta>1/2$. All finite designs follow explicit integer, polynomial and cyclotomic formulas, and their actual integer row/call counts test $\sigma/W<A^\theta$ by raising positive rationals to a fixed integer power. One may hardwire any passing finite design or enumerate the source choices until strict success. The source proves a passing choice exists. In either case all constant tables fit in finite code or fixed work regions. There is no claim of usable numerical constants. This argument, on its own, gives no bound when $\theta$ is allowed to vary with $k$ or when $\theta=1/2$.

## A sequential call boundary without an ancestor scan per fiber

Suppose the local layout routines have already materialized the ordered fibers of a directional call in a contiguous bank, with the entries of each fiber consecutive. A constant number of ordinary monotone passes suffices to run the child programs, restore the corresponding parent order and reuse the same fixed active banks. Two additional tape heads maintain an input stack and a result stack. Neither stack needs random access.

At a calling node, move its other boundedly many live row banks and loop state onto a storage stack. Stream the complete list of pending fibers in reverse child and entry order onto the input stack, above a delimiter. Use bounded cell tags to mark fiber boundaries. At each child, pop exactly its next input to a near-origin working bank. Once the recursive call has returned, append its forward-order result to the result stack above this caller's result delimiter. Any child that needs its own input/result stacks uses the same two tapes, placing fresh delimiters above the parent's current tops. Every descendant removes its own frames before returning. Parent data below those delimiters never move during a child computation. When all siblings finish, pop their concatenated results backwards into an appropriately oriented ordinary bank, then restore the caller's saved rows by one pass. Separate storage, input and result stacks suffice for all depths. Bank scans, reversals, delimiter traffic and parent state transfers sum to $O(3^k)$ at a node, apart from the explicitly costed local permutations and the execution of children. There are only fixedly many batch calls and directional rows at that node. In particular, an individual fiber of width $3^f$ never requires rescanning any parked row of width $3^{Af}$.

The result bank can be filled by first walking to the end of its reserved span and writing backward; this is one linear pass. Parent-local live arrays have stack length $O(3^k)$. A running child contributes $O(3^f)$ with $f\le k/A$. The sum along one active recursion path is $O(3^k)$. Stacks grow from fixed origins and are restored on return, and active banks reuse fixed near-origin regions. This also bounds cells *ever visited* by $O(3^k)$, assuming local layout routines use fixed reusable $O(3^k)$ regions. An amortized counter for $3^{(A-1)f}$ children can be replaced by the fixed physical delimiters. The delimiters add one constant number of cells per nonempty child. The fixed scalar table is read by finite control. Enumerating query phases in natural digit order may use a traversal stack, with a constant number of extensions per prefix and constant local exponent state. Its $O(k)$-bit address is a description of a permutation, and does not supply a constant-time remote write on a tape.

If all fixed local address and axis layouts cost $O(3^k L(k))$ time, this boundary gives

$$ T(k)\le C L(k)+ b_* T(\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor) $$

for normalized ternary tape work. For $L(k)=O((1+k)^\gamma)$, induction yields $T(k)=O((1+k)^{\max(\theta,\gamma)})$ because the coefficient has strict slack at exponent $\theta$. The outer four-coordinate word additionally needs its fixed-symbol sectors in contiguous order in both directions, plus each monomial product order. Sector packing, phase calculation and movement of arbitrary fixed linear trit-column changes must be charged at their physical cost. The published word-address algorithm alone cannot discharge them.

## A finite replay that uses independent input rows

Two fixtures use $E=\mathbf F_4[t]/(t^e-\omega)$, for $e=3,9$. These are fields: the possible $t$ have multiplicative orders 9 and 27, and the multiplicative order of 4 modulo those integers is respectively 3 and 9. Their Vandermonde nodes use actual primitive ninth or twenty-seventh roots. In characteristic two the source Hermite polynomial becomes $g(y)=y^4$. For $z^3=1$, $g(y)-z=(y-z)^4$, so $D_z(y)=(y-z)^2$. The checker reconstructs the error from the left Vandermonde minor, solves the transposed system for the outgoing checks and realizes the full pipeline with distinct ordinary input arrays, explicit tensor Fourier matrices and unsigned field arithmetic. It compares *every packed base component* against a separately transformed input row.

Across 85 support sets it checked 763 extension basis columns, 222 independently addressed binary basis columns, and 2,619 ordinary-output component entries over 111 distinct base rows. Omitting a bad row is detected in both fixtures, and intentionally dropping the transpose in the right inverse gives 102 bad columns. Exact replay includes tests in which higher roots do not live in the four-bit main scalar field. These two small templates test the general specialization and independent payload argument. They do not enumerate the November proof's large characteristic-independent design or measure a tape permutation.
