# Root-directory routing by two independent prefixes

Set $b=3c$, $a=2^c$, $q=2^b$, $K=\mathbf F_q$, $L=\mathbf F_a$, and $T=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/L}$. The following fixed-tape adapter turns the $O(q/b)$ selected plane words at $x=t+vy+wz$ into the actual degree/Lyndon FS1 residues in $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log(b+2))^\eta)$ time and $O_\eta(q)$ total visited cells, for fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$. It inverts arbitrary independent FS1 residues. It improves the short-key routing in the [projected directory](point-factor-directory.md); the full [CRT](full-plane-crt.md) has the same clock when using [the batched convolution](lane-packed-convolution.md).

## Actual source and target coordinates

Embed the existing FS1 degree-$b$ field, with its specified normal element, in $K$. Its irreducible defining polynomial splits there. Gcd partitions by $\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbf F_2}(uX)$, for a binary basis of $u$, distinguish all roots by nondegeneracy of the trace pairing. This gives an isomorphism and the image of the prescribed normal basis in $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ steps. Write $n(x)$ for that normal word in the FS1 bit order. Frobenius rotates it and its least rotation $l(x)$ is the actual Lyndon key for full-degree $x$.

Write $s=(t_0,y_0,z_0)$ for three $c$-bit words. Use **the existing S006 normal basis** on the trace $t$, and fixed additive coordinates for $y,z\in L$. The map $s\mapsto n(t+vy+wz)$ extends to a binary linear isomorphism on all $b$ bits because $T(t)=t$ and $v,w$ span its kernel over $L$. Tabulate the three contributions to $n(x)$ and to the power-basis word of $x$ in $a\operatorname{poly}(b)$ steps and cells. Row-table scans and XOR generate both words per point in $O(b)$ amortized steps.

Restore lexicographic $s$ order by a wide sort of entire planes on $t_0$, then in each plane route its $a$ rows of $ab$ bits from their true multiplicative/zero order to additive order. Exchange the two $c$-bit grids, route the other axis, and exchange back. The bit-position permutations apply to $b$-bit cells at $2c$-bit addresses. All three wide sorts, including the separate inner calls on each plane, cost $O_\eta(qc/(1+\log c)^\eta)$ and use $O(q)$ reusable intervals. Their inverse keys are generated by the same tables. [Tal, Corollary 6.2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/) · [Physical permutation lemmas](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/permutations.tex)

At each point determine the minimum cyclic rotation by rank doubling. Ranks of all $b$ cyclic length-$2^j$ substrings give the next ranks by sorting pairs of ranks at $i,i+2^j\bmod b$. Make the latter by a single cyclic shift of the local index-ordered stream. Two binary radix sorts of $b$ triples of $O(1+\log b)$ bits take $O(b(1+\log b)^2)$ steps in a round, restoring index order. At length at least $b$ the minimum rank and its multiplicity determine $l(x)$, its unique shift $r$ for full degree, and the proper-period case. All local scratch is polynomial in $b$ and reused; overall time is $O(q(1+\log b)^3)$.

## Two independent short prefixes

For large $c$ set $H=\lfloor\log_2(c/(C(1+\log_2 c)^2))\rfloor$ for a sufficiently large fixed $C$. Thus $1\le H\le c$ and $2^H=\Theta(c/(1+\log c)^2)$. Take a direct algorithm on bounded exceptions. Keep a point for the main routing when its period is $b$, the first $H$ bits of $t_0$ equal one constant bit $e$, and $l(x)$ begins with $0^H$. The S006 $\alpha$ is least modulo rotation and complementation in its own basis, and $\alpha+1$ is its complement. If a pair misses the constant prefix, its orbit words have no cyclic $0^H$ run. Among $k$-bit words at most $2^k(1-2^{-H})^{\lfloor k/H\rfloor}$ miss this marker, by testing disjoint windows. At $k=c,b$ this is at most $2^kc^{-8}$. Thus exceptional trace planes hold $O(qc^{-8})$ points, full words without a marker give $O(qc^{-8})$ further points, and proper-period points number $O(2^{b/2})$. Route all exception labels by ordinary $O(b)$-pass binary radix sorts of $O(b)$-bit records, for $O(q)$ time. Full-degree exceptions already have their exact Lyndon key. For proper subfields match ordinary minimal polynomials to the actual proper-degree FS1 inventory, at total $O(2^{b/2}\operatorname{poly}(b))$.

Fix $r$ and $e$. Write $m=\operatorname{rotate}_r(n(t+vy+wz))$. The affine subspace of full addresses with $(t_0)_{[H]}=e^H$ and $m_{[H]}=0^H$ has dimension exactly $d=b-2H$. The selected trace coordinates are independent. In the ambient FS1 normal basis every binary combination of them is invariant under $\operatorname{Frob}^c$, since $T(x^{2^c})=T(x)$. Its support is a union of triples $\{i,i+c,i+2c\}$. The $H\le c$ consecutive positions of the rotated full word meet each triple at most once. Their spans intersect trivially. The combined map has rank $2H$, so the two affine right-hand sides are always consistent.

## Scatter, change the affine address, and compact

Stable radix sort on $(e,r)$ takes $O(\log b)$ whole-stream passes and preserves lexicographic $s$ order in each group. In that group, use Gaussian elimination separately on the affine equations in $s$ and in $m$, always choosing the rightmost remaining nonzero pivot column. In reduced form each pivot bit depends only on preceding free bits. If two valid full words differ, their first difference must be in a free position. Extracting free positions in original left-to-right order therefore preserves lexicographic order on both sides. The induced map on the two $d$-bit free-address spaces is explicitly invertible affine. All eliminations, offset vectors and its two matrices cost $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ steps per group.

Merge the sorted true $s$ stream with the $2^d$ complete affine source words in free-bit order, inserting a blank record at each missing word. To update the full solution after a binary increment of the free counter, XOR its columns for the trailing ones and first new bit. Store the columns in increasing significance. An increment that changes $j$ bits costs $O(jb)$ including column-head return; the average is at most two flips. Thus filling and comparing costs only linear time in the padded volume. After the address change, scan the target in free-bit order, dropping blank records. The surviving run is now sorted by $m=l(x)$. The numerical reply and a bounded number of labels occupy $\Theta(b)=\Theta(d)$ bits per record.

Here is a fixed-tape algorithm for the required arbitrary invertible $d$-bit affine address map on $V$ bits of records. Split the binary matrix into two roughly equal row and column halves and permute rows to obtain an invertible top-left block $A$; the first half of its columns is independent. Use the factorization
$$
\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\CA^{-1}&I\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\0&D+CA^{-1}B\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}I&A^{-1}B\\0&I\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Recurse on the two invertible diagonal blocks simultaneously. At each depth each cross-half factor flips individual target bits by parity functions of the unchanged source bits. Across all blocks those source bits form a common disjoint control set of at most $2d/3$ bits, including odd three-bit blocks. The controlled-small-block lemma on one-bit targets applies. Its generators compute explicit parities in $d^{O(1)}$ steps. The row pivot permutations at each depth combine into one specified bit-position permutation. Affine translations use fixed flips without controls. Applying the $O(1+\log d)$ layers costs $O_\rho(Vd^\rho(1+\log d)^4)+\operatorname{poly}(b)$ time, any fixed $1/2<\rho<1$, and $O(V)+\operatorname{poly}(b)$ cells. Reverse all factors for the inverse. This acts on full binary address intervals with $\Theta(d)$-bit records as required by the theorem. [Controlled small-block lemma and bit-position corollary](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/permutations.tex)

Summed over both values of $e$ and all $b$ shifts, the padded volume is $O(b^2 2^{b-2H})=O(q(1+\log b)^4)$. A single group uses $O(q(1+\log b)^4/b)=O(q)$ cells. Process groups serially from an advancing compact input head. Reset all local banks to the same origins before the next group. Output only genuine compact records to a separate advancing head and store group lengths. They total $O(q)$ bits. The dense work sums to $O_\rho(qb^\rho(1+\log b)^8)+\operatorname{poly}(b)$, which fits the theorem at $\rho=3/4$. Merge these $O(b)$ sorted genuine runs pairwise and include the exception stream, at $O(q(1+\log b))$ time with a fixed number of resettable streams. The result lists every occurrence in degree/Lyndon order with duplicate occurrences adjacent.

## The actual replies in both directions

Forward, apply the local baby-step/giant-step moment extraction and Padé procedure to obtain the binary power-basis residue at each actual point. This costs $O(qb^{7/10}+q\sqrt b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$. Route the replies, combine identical keys and emit exactly their degree-many bits. Inverse, first perform the same deterministic forward key route with payload-independent shape records. Read the resulting sorted incidence stream beside the FS1 directory input and attach each supplied residue to every occurrence of its key. Stable radix sort on the short $(\mathrm{exception},e,r)$ tag gives the target-$m$-ordered runs. Reverse the full affine scatter/compaction within each run, then merge their $s$-ordered outputs with the exceptional $s$ run. Evaluate by blocked Horner at the actual $x$ before reversing the three source-axis rearrangements. These fixed operations stream only the genuine $O(q/b)$ occurrences outside a single local padded array. Every factor occurs because each Frobenius orbit meets a stored trace fibre. This proves both directions for arbitrary independent FS1 input, including proper subfields, duplicate occurrences, and the original root choices. The [finite replay](verify-marked-directory.py) checks genuinely distinct top-field presentations, cyclic rank doubling, exact coordinate subspaces, right-pivot parametrizations and the recursive address compiler. It uses small artificial marker lengths to exercise main and exceptional routing below the asymptotic cutoff. [Deterministic counts](marked-directory-cases.json)

Across nine $(b,H)$ cases at $b=3,6,9,12$, the replay checks 156 independent prefix systems, 68,760 affine address images in both directions, and 7,048 actual geometric occurrences per direction. These include 5,780 duplicate occurrences, 2,094 and 2,052 genuine points in the two constant-prefix branches, 2,902 exceptions and 238 proper-degree occurrences. All 1,268 repeated-case FS1 factor positions were correct. The full multiplicative/zero grid order was restored at every tested position.
