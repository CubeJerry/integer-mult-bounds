# Full-rate binary polynomial multiplication for a wide batch on fixed tapes

Let $T=\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil$. For fixed $\delta>0$, $K>1+\delta$ and rational $0<\eta<1/2$, there is a fixed deterministic finite-alphabet multitape machine which multiplies $B$ independent pairs of length-$n$ binary polynomials for $n^{1+\delta}\le B\le n^K$. It returns all $B(2n-1)$ binary coefficients in the original packet order. Its time is $O_{\delta,K,\eta}(nBT/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ and its tapes visit $O_{\delta,K,\eta}(nB)$ cells through halt. Repeating one operand in all the pairs specializes the result to a fixed kernel. The packed chirp uses $n=(2c-1)(2^c-1)+O(c)$, $B\asymp 2^{2c}/c$, within this range with, for example, $\delta=1/2$, $K=3$ at sufficiently large $c$.

The algebraic packing and the Fourier words come from the November 24 positive-characteristic convolution construction. The tape result places original independent inputs inside every short coordinate block, implements the resulting local permutations and mask-dependent shears, and prices the entry and exit layouts. The theorem uses the [striped binary Pascal supplier](striped-pascal.md) and the separate published tape interchanger. The machine only calls an existing ordinary tape polynomial multiplier at lengths whose logarithms are $O(T^{4/5}\log T)$. ## The physical dimensions and field setup

Set $L=CT$ with the absolute constant in the source's shifted-prime lemma. Trial division through $L$ extracts its primes $p_i\in[L/2,L]$ for which $p_i-1$ has no prime factor over $L^{4/5}$. The list product exceeds $n^2$. Let $v$ be the largest power of three at most $8L$ and use the field $E=\mathbf F_{2^e}$ with $e=\mathrm{lcm}_i(p_i-1)\varphi(v)$. Thus $\log e=O(T^{4/5}\log T)$ and every $p_i$ and $v$ divide $2^e-1$. A deterministic fixed-characteristic irreducible construction produces a binary monic defining polynomial of degree $e$ in $\operatorname{poly}(e)$ bit time. Any resulting power basis works in the source's packing. Primitive roots can be found without enumerating $E$. The cyclotomic polynomials $\Phi_{p_i}$ and $\Phi_v$ split into distinct linear factors. For each of the $e$ polynomial-basis elements $\beta$, split the current factors by gcd with $\sum_{j<e}(\beta X)^{2^j}$ reduced modulo the factor. For each pair of distinct roots $u,w$, nondegeneracy of the absolute trace gives a basis element with $\operatorname{Tr}(\beta(u-w))=1$. The combined partitions therefore isolate all roots. All degrees in $X$ are $O(L)$ and modular squaring keeps coefficient words at $e$ bits. The polynomial number of operations in $e,L$ costs $n^{o(1)}$ time and cells. Choose a single root of each required order and use powers for smaller ternary orders. [Shoup, New Algorithms for Finding Irreducible Polynomials over Finite Fields](https://www.shoup.net/papers/detirred.pdf) · [Prime and extension choice](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/packing.tex)

Use the longest prefix with $eN\le n/L$, $N=\prod_{i=1}^d p_i$. Then $n/L^2<eN\le n/L$, $d=\Theta(T/\log T)$ and some $r\in[L,9L^2]$ gives $8n\le erN\le9n$. The source injects an input coefficient numbered $a+E_0t+E_0Rj$ as $\theta^az^tX^j$ in $J[X]/(X^N-1)$, where $J=E[z]/z^r$, $E_0=\lceil e/2\rceil$, $R=\lceil r/2\rceil$ and $j<\lceil N/2\rceil$. The three lower-half exponent ranges prevent field reduction, jet truncation and cyclic wrap of products of original monomials. Use $j\mapsto(j\bmod p_i)_i$ to put the cyclic coordinate into a prime grid. Apply two forward transforms, multiply the two jets at corresponding addresses, and apply one inverse. In reverse CRT layout the output bit at $(a,t,j)$ contributes to $a+E_0t+E_0Rj$. Multiple contributions to one index are added in $\mathbf F_2$. Keep indices $0$ through $2n-2$. Divisibility, reductions and the inverse integer CRT use actual binary computations of polynomial cost in $T$ per short address.

## The elementary local words

An axis of prime length $p$ uses a primitive root of ternary order between $2p$ and $6p$. The source's geometric Fourier factorization gives a word of $O(1+\log L)$ letters for $F_p\oplus I$ on at most $C_0p$ field registers. A letter can be taken to be either a permutation with nonzero scalar at each coordinate, or a disjoint set of directed pairs receiving unit upper shears while all other coordinates are fixed. This more specific form follows from the radix-three construction: each fixed $3\times3$ elimination is a bounded number of elementary pair shears with monomial interleaves. The shifts $J,R$ and pointwise additions in the Toeplitz commutators are disjoint pair updates. The group-word products, inverses, and disjoint calls pad elementary slots by identities, preserving the form. A scalar shear splits into diagonal conjugation and a unit shear; a zero coefficient contributes an identity there. All these words and their address tables are effective. Construct the short matrices, Newton coefficients, reciprocal series, ternary powers, constant-size eliminations, and suspended-fibre schedules directly from the recurrences in the source. Polynomial work in $e,L$ suffices since their non-tensor dimensions are $L^{O(1)}$ and field operations cost $\operatorname{poly}(e)$. After this setup, the address-only tables on a block occupy $T^{O(1)}$ bits per letter, separately from their $e$-bit scalar entries. [Local pairs and restoring words](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex)

Use $k=1+\lceil\log_2\lceil C_0\rceil\rceil$. Group $k$ to $2k-1$ consecutive prime axes at a time as in the source's occupied-fibre lemma. At large $n$ there are at least $k$ axes; bounded exceptions may use ordinary convolution. A group has native size $a_g=\prod_{i\in g}p_i=T^{O(1)}$. Axis fibres are partitioned in batches of $\lceil C_0\rceil$ to $2\lceil C_0\rceil$ fibres. An individual fibre borrows its neighbors' actual field values and returns them intact. This holds when some borrowed entries are already final outputs. Traverse a bounded number of rounds per group and synchronize all groups' words by separate monomial and shear slots per local step. This gives $O(1+\log T)$ whole-array slots, each a tensor of elementary native letters on $[a_1]\times\cdots\times[a_G]$, where $G=\Theta(T/\log T)$ and $\prod a_g=N$. All local work takes place on native group coordinates, before the addition of independent lanes described below.

## Independent lanes fill the binary address block

Set $k_g=\lceil\log_2(a_gG)\rceil$, $M_g=\lfloor2^{k_g}/a_g\rfloor$, and $M=\prod_gM_g$. Then $G\le M_g\le 2G$, $k_g=O(\log T)$ and $k_0=\sum_g k_g=O(T)$. Since each prime is $\ge L/2$ and $d=O(T/\log T)$, $M\le(2G)^G\le (2d)^d\le 2^dN=n^{1+o(1)}$. Write $P=\lceil B/M\rceil$, so $P\ge n^{\delta/2}$ for large $n$ and $PM\le B+M\le2B$. Each input lane has unique number $\ell+P\operatorname{rank}(s_1,\ldots,s_G)$, with $0\le s_g<M_g$, $0\le\ell<P$, for an ordinary fixed mixed-radix rank. A final partial bank is filled by arbitrary independent extra lanes, for example zeros. On group $g$ use the full $k_g$-bit coordinate $y_g=x_g+a_g s_g$. Native $x_g<a_g$ fill the initial $M_ga_g$ values and all tail values become dummy. The complete-address volume inflation is bounded by
$$\prod_g 2^{k_g}/(a_gM_g)<\prod_g(1+1/M_g)\le(1+1/G)^G\le\exp(1).$$
Each full address row contains $P$ jets, usually as $[\ell][\text{jet coordinate}]$, with $W=Per\ge n^{\delta/2}$ bits per row. The full bit volume $V=2^{k_0}W$ lies between $erNB$ and $2\exp(1)erNB=O(nB)$. Each native block action keeps its own lane digit $s_g$. Different original polynomials remain independent through the entire Fourier computation.

## Actual tape layouts

Fix $1/2<\rho<1$, say $\rho=3/4$. The published small-block lemma simultaneously permutes disjoint $O(\log k)$-bit fields of a full $k$-bit record address for $O(V(1+k)^\rho(1+\log k)^3)+k^{O(1)}$ time and $O(V)+k^{O(1)}$ cells. It requires records of $\Theta(k)$ bits. Pad our common wide payload to a power of two and divide it into $2^{\lceil\log_2 T\rceil}$-bit records. Include its high within-row bits in the address as occupied spectator positions. The resulting $k=\Theta(T)$ since $\sum k_g=\Theta(T)$, $\log B=O(T)$ and $\log(er)=o(T)$. All targets are disjoint $k_g$-bit blocks, with no external controls. The prepared address-only tables and inverses can be regenerated in $T^{O(1)}$ tape steps. The source bit-position corollary costs $O(VT^\rho(1+\log T))$ with the same space. [Tape permutations, including setup, records and space](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/permutations.tex)

The two-dimensional bit transpose at the endpoints also admits this record-width convention. Pad both dimensions to powers of two, at most fourfold. Write each axis as a high part and $u=\lceil\log_2 T\rceil$ low bits. Keep the $2^u$-bit column-low payload intact while permuting $[\text{row high}][\text{row low}][\text{column high}]$ to $[\text{column high}][\text{row high}][\text{row low}]$. Transpose the consecutive $2^u\times2^u$ tiles locally. Each adjacent bit-position swap permutes each four consecutive equal-length runs as $0,2,1,3$ at the appropriate scale. Read each tile in four-stream bank sweeps and return, charging even $O(u)$ for a counter per bit on each of $O(u^2)$ passes. Now keep the $2^u$-bit row-low payload intact and rearrange the higher fields as $[\text{column high}][\text{column low}][\text{row high}]$. Remove padding by scanning. This gives $O(VT^\rho(1+\log T)+V(1+\log T)^3)$ time and $O(V)$ cells. The rectangular outer dimensions $n,B$ and the later jet transpose's dimensions $er,P$ are at least $T$, and their total log-width is $\Theta(T)$ due to $P\ge n^{\delta/2}$. The same method applies in reverse.

Take packet-major inputs $[\lambda<B][i<n]$ into $[i][\lambda]$ by this transpose. Pad lanes to $PM$. A record is the $P$ consecutive bits for given $i,s$. Compute its key $(y_1,\ldots,y_G,t,a)$, including the integer CRT grid conversion. Stable sort the $nM$ wide records by this key, and fill all missing coordinates by a monotone rectangular scan. Transpose the order $[\text{jet coordinate}][\ell]$ to $[\ell][\text{jet coordinate}]$ inside each row and reuse the workspace. Process both operand arrays with independently advancing heads. On return undo the row transpose, drop dummy addresses, and tag each genuine coordinate strip of width $P$ by $(i,s)$ with $i=a+E_0t+E_0Rj$. Sort, XOR equal-key records in stream order, discard indices outside $[0,2n-2]$, emit zeros for any missing keys, remove final-lane padding, and transpose back to the packet-major endpoint. Every external sort has $n^{O(1)}$ records of width $P\ge n^{\delta/2}$ and $O(T)$-bit keys, with polynomial-$T$ key generation. Tal's fixed-tape Corollary 6.2 gives $O_\eta(VT/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ time and $O(V)$ visited space. There are a bounded number of external sorts over the complete multiplication. [Tal, §§2.3 and 6, Corollary 6.2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/)

## One monomial or shear slot

A native permutation $\pi_g$ takes a genuine $y_g$ to $\pi_g(x_g)+a_gs_g$ and fixes its remaining values. Simultaneously permute all blocks by the small-block lemma. The prescribed scalar for the tensor at one address is the product of its native factors, taking the identity for a dummy native slot. Table reads, scalar computations, and returns of setup heads cost $n^{o(1)}$ even if the entire setup is traversed once per full row, absorbed by $P\ge n^{\delta/2}$. All $P$ jets at the address receive the same $E$-scalar. Scalar application to arbitrary dense jets is invoiced below.

For a shear slot orient each native pair $(u,v)$ as $u\gets u+v$. In group $g$ collect all copies $(u+a_gs,v+a_gs)$ over $s<M_g$ into successive slots $(2h,2h+1)$ with $h<t_g$, where $t_g=M_g$ times its native pair count. Fill the remaining slots with genuine singletons and all tail dummies. This defines a permutation of the full binary block. Apply these normalizations simultaneously. Write $h_g=\lfloor y_g/2\rfloor$, $b_g=y_g\bmod2$. Gather all $G$ low bits into a suffix by a bit-position permutation. At each fixed $(h_g)_g$, exactly $A=\{g:h_g<t_g\}$ are active. Within its contiguous $2^G$-row run, permute the low-bit positions to put passive positions before active ones. The explicit permutation descriptor costs $\operatorname{poly}(T)$ steps per run, absorbed in the residual width. Padding and cutting inside that width lets the bit-position corollary take $k=\Theta(T)$ here too. Stream runs through a dedicated input/output pair and the same local banks: there is no traversal of earlier runs for the next descriptor.

For each passive low setting apply $U^{\otimes j}$ with $j=|A|\le G$ on the consecutive active rows and on every residual payload bit. If $j\le T^\rho$, use $j$ ordinary paired tape passes at $O(j)$ steps per bit, with the identity for $j=0$. Otherwise $2^{2j}\gg T$. Partition the power-of-two padded payload into slabs of $S=2^{2j}$ bits. For large $T$, $W\ge n^{\delta/2}>2^{2G}$, so the partition is exact. Interchange the active and slab indices by the position corollary; the unchanged low $S$-bit field contains a $\Theta(T)$-bit record cut. Each consecutive slab now has exactly $2^j$ rows of $S=(2^j)^2$ bits in the [striped Pascal theorem's](striped-pascal.md) stated width range and order. Use that supplier, transpose back, and undo the per-spectator permutation, global low-bit gather, and native normalization. Each true pair connects only a common lane digit and never crosses a dummy. Passive adjacent slots can hold unrelated lanes, already transformed fibres and arbitrary dummy values. The result tensor acts on the native coordinates in their starting order.

A second implementation eliminates the per-spectator routing. Choose a primitive cube root $\omega$ in $E$. On a normalized active pair set $(s,t)=(\omega,\omega^2)$ and on a passive adjacent pair set $(s,t)=(1,1)$. With $D(s)=\mathrm{diag}(s,1)$ the product $D(s)UD(s)^{-1}D(t)UD(t)^{-1}$ adds $s+t$ times the second coordinate into the first. Thus it acts as $U$ exactly on active pairs. Tensor this identity across groups. It uses two unrestricted $U^{\otimes G}$ layers on the gathered low bits and three short tensor diagonals, including the combined middle diagonal. Each diagonal can be generated per actual address as above, since its factors depend only on native local pair data. Slab each unconditional Pascal call just as in the mask-specific version. Both implementations have the same asymptotic bill.

## Short products, sums and distinct cells visited

An $E$ scalar times one jet, or two general jets multiplied modulo $z^r$, takes binary products with total operand length $O(er)$, largest length $O(er)$. Substitute $Y=w$, $z=w^{2e-1}$ in the bivariate power representatives, multiply once, keep the low $r$ rows, and reduce each polynomial row by the fixed field polynomial using a precomputed reversed reciprocal and two short products. Read and write rows sequentially. Packing the stride zeros and returning the modulus head take linear work in the total short length. Harvey and van der Hoeven's deterministic fixed multitape multiplier costs $O(s(1+\log s)4^{\log^*s})$ steps on length $s$. Hence all short arithmetic in one slot or pointwise multiplication costs $O(V(1+T^{4/5}\log T)4^{\log^* n})$. Even if each subcall visited as many cells as its time bound allows, its local extent is $n^{o(1)}$, reused at common origins after each jet. Put $g(j)=(1+j)/(1+\log(j+2))^\eta$. The slab volumes in one pass sum to $O(V)$. Thus the striped cost including all their multiplicities is $O_\eta(Vg(G))$ in addition to $O(VT^\rho)$ for small $j$. Each of the $O(1+\log T)$ synchronized slots also incurs routing and short arithmetic of $O(T^\rho(1+\log T)^3+(1+T^{4/5}\log T)4^{\log^* n})$ per bit. After multiplying by the number of slots the latter cost is $o(T/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ because $\rho<1$ and $4^{\log^*n}=T^{o(1)}$. The striped charges sum to $O_\eta((1+\log T)g(G))=O_\eta(T/(1+\log T)^\eta)$ using $G=\Theta(T/\log T)$. The three transforms, pointwise products, initialization, external sorts and both endpoint orders satisfy the asserted time.

The polynomial setup occupies $n^{o(1)}$ cells. A fixed number of outer streams, including the waiting operand, each has volume $O(V)$. Global operators use common marked origins with $O(V)$ space from their permutation, sort or striped supplier. Process local runs, slabs, products and transposes with monotone outer heads and reset the separate local banks on every invocation. Track the extent reached in the current invocation, and clear or rewind it in at most its charged scans or time. Nested striped calls use their documented geometric continuation-stack bound. The fixed number of routine types may use disjoint banks, each within an $O(V)$ contiguous interval over the entire execution. The union of all visited cells through halt is $O(V)=O(nB)$.

The conservative batch range suffices for the chirp. In fact, for every fixed $\gamma>0$ the same conclusion holds on $n^\gamma\le B\le n^K$. Enlarge the fixed group size to $k\ge\max(k_{\rm borrow},\lceil4/\gamma\rceil)$. Then $G\le d/k$ and $M\le(2d)^{d/k}\le N^{(1+o(1))/k}\le n^{\gamma/2}$, using $p_i\ge L/2$ and $d=O(T/\log T)$. Thus $P\ge n^{\gamma/2}$ while the largest native size is still $T^{O_\gamma(1)}$ and all local address tables satisfy the $O_\gamma(\log T)$-bit block requirement. The time and space constants may depend on $\gamma$.
