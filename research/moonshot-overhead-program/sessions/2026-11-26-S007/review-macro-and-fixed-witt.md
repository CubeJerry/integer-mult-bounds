# Independent audit of the macroradix product and fixed-precision literal CRT

The [macroradix algorithm](macro-triadic-product.md) prices an ordinary product over $A_H=\mathbf Z/2^H$ at $O(sH(1+\log s)/(1+\log\log(s+3))^\eta)$ steps and $O(sH)$ visited cells for polynomial-logarithmic $H$ in its stated range. The [Witt construction](fixed-witt-precision.md) consequently gives the literal FS1 forward and inverse at every *fixed* $H$ in $O_{H,\eta}(qb/(1+\log(b+2))^\eta)$ steps and $O_H(q)$ total visited cells. The second proof allows its number of local operations and global calls to depend on $H$.

## Cooley–Tukey, the free module, and scalar lanes

Use $M=mn$ a power of three with $m/n=3$ or $9$ and $\varphi(M)>2s-2$. The balanced $n=AB$ has $B/A=1$ or $3$. With $j=j_0+A j_1$, $k=k_1+B k_0$ and $\rho=Y^{m/n}$, actual multiplication of exponents gives
$$\rho^{jk}=(\rho^A)^{j_1 k_1}\rho^{j_0 k_1}(\rho^B)^{j_0 k_0}.$$
The middle twist acts on the whole $S_m=A_H[Y]/\Phi_m$ word after putting its first-stage lanes back into increasing $Y$ degree. It can mix those lanes. For either $L=A,B$, put $u=m/L$ and $W=Y^u$. Then $\Phi_L(Y^u)=\Phi_m(Y)$ exactly as monic polynomials over the integers. Grouping $Y^{ut+i}$ in increasing $t<\varphi(L)$ for each $i<u$ proves $S_m=\bigoplus_{i<u}Y^iS_L(W)$ over the actual coefficient ring, including all its zero divisors. Powers of $W$ act independently on these free slots. Principal-root orthogonality also specializes to this ring: for each nonzero frequency modulo $n$, the relevant geometric sum is an integer polynomial multiple of $\Phi_m$, and $n$ is a unit.

For each free lane apply the $L$-point odd Bluestein transform. Its half exponent uses $2^{-1}\bmod L$ on integer indices. Pre/post factors are powers of $W$, so cyclic rotation and subtraction of the highest third suffice. Binary coefficient positions within each $A_H$ word stay intact. Stride $2\varphi(L)-1$ separates ordinary products with inner degree at most $2\varphi(L)-2$. First reduce inside each resulting word (cyclically modulo $W^L-1$, then the monic $\Phi_L$), then fold the outer convolution index modulo $L$. The fixed kernel can be written by the same monomial rotations and traversed once per actual packet. The inverse uses opposite signs and either two axis normalizations or the one final unit $n^{-1}$.

The resulting ordinary operand length is $D=L(2\varphi(L)-1)=\Theta(L^2)$. Its true independent packet count is $J=(n/L)(m/L)$. Since $m/n\in\{3,9\}$ and $L^2/n\in\{1/3,1,3\}$, $J/D$ lies between fixed positive constants for large parameters. In particular $J\ge D^{1/2}$. The total volume per stage is $O(sH)$. No outer digit reversal occurs. Reshaping a cyclotomic coefficient index into $t,u$ is a contiguous read; each remaining shape change permutes a bounded number of complete rectangular axes and incoming/outgoing heads advance through those arrays. Constant-factor padding to binary ranges and the paid raw-bit-position theorem therefore fit the bound.

For varying $H$ the binary compiler's slabs must consist of *complete* scalar slots. Use physical slot stride $H'=2^{\lceil\log_2H\rceil}$ around address motions, take $2^{2j}$ complete slots per slab, and strip/reinsert the scalar padding around the precision Pascal supplier. There are $Per$ genuine slots in each full address row, not $Per/H$. A local $j>T^\rho$ implies $H\le T^K\le j^{K/\rho}$, meeting the precision bound at its own active dimension. For smaller $j$ the direct complete-word addition passes cost $O(j)$ per actual bit. The local Hensel/field/batch setup and all scalar-table passes are polynomial in $H,T,e$ and still $s^{o(1)}$. The smaller jet supplier has normalized $T^{4/5}\operatorname{polylog}T$ cost even allowing short arithmetic factors in $\log H$. All short polynomial calls are sequential in renewed fixed banks.

The [independent executable](verify-macro-ck-independent.py) implements both balanced transforms, all free lane sets, native operand packing, full middle twist, outer two cyclic products and reconstruction. Across five unequal/equal and differing-precision cases it compares both transform signs on 39,204 scalar entries, checks 19,602 ordinary coefficient positions including zero padding and issues 14,256 actual macroradix packet products. Omitting the middle twist changes 15,314 checked entries. [Macroradix receipt](macro-ck-independent-cases.json)

## Ghost divisions, true roots, and factor setup

For $R=\mathbf Z[X]/(X^q-X)$, its free integral basis makes division of an exactly $2^j$-divisible coefficient array unambiguous modulo two. In any commutative ring, $a\equiv b\pmod2$ gives $a^{2^i}\equiv b^{2^i}\pmod{2^{i+1}}$ by factoring each difference of squares. Thus a previous ghost coordinate's *single* bit reduction suffices to compute its contribution $2^h C_h^{2^{j-h}}$ modulo $2^{j+1}$. Reducing the integer polynomial modulo $X^q-X$ before doing these divisions is valid: the fixed monic ring quotient preserves the exact divisible identity, and its additive group has no 2-torsion. The positive exponent map in the remaining global source $P(X^{2^j})$ is a cyclic rotation of $b$ address bits, including the fixed all-zero and all-ones addresses. At fixed $H$, at most $j<H$ one-bit-position moves implement it in $O_H(q)$ steps. A fixed length-$H$ Pascal counter prepares the binomial coefficients in the Hasse derivatives, mod $2^H$, by a single update per input position for each fixed entry.

For $z=X\bmod h$ in the actual FS1 factor field, $[z]$ is its Teichmüller lift; the root of the *literal coefficient lift* of $h$ is $[z]+y$. The translated little polynomial in $y$ has even constant and unit linear coefficient. Newton starting at zero stays in the even ideal and doubles its valid precision, using Hasse order $H$ as needed to evaluate the derivative fully. Since $y^H=0$ the finite Hasse formula evaluates the required ordinary polynomial at that root. All universal Witt-coordinate operation counts here depend on fixed $H$. At degree $d$ their field products, fast monic remainders and extended Euclidean inverses together cost $O_H(d\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ physical steps, with polynomial local scratch reset per factor. The actual FS1 polynomial labels can be emitted by the moment/minpoly generator in the trusted root directory order. The total degree is $q$ even with small and proper-degree roots.

If the first Witt coordinate vanishes, division by 2 into length $H-1$ removes it and takes a square root of every surviving coordinate. In the actual power basis of $F_h$, separate even/odd terms, $h=A(X)^2+XB(X)^2$. Separability gives $B(z)^2=h'(z)\ne0$ and $\sqrt z=A(z)/B(z)$. For a field word $C(z)^2+zD(z)^2$ the root is $C(z)+\sqrt zD(z)$. It includes $h=X,X+1$ and uses no per-factor scan of its $2^d$ field elements. The repeated subtractions against the *literal short monomial polynomial* and truncated root consequently recover precisely the required $A_H$ coefficients. On inverse a binary CRT on the actual input parity determines an entire coefficient bit plane. Its forward correction leaves even residue *coefficients* and division continues at one less precision. This gives all independent inverse records. Constant-many $O_H(q)$ global streams and the single reusable local workspace suffice through halt.

It compares Hasse evaluation and Newton's root to direct signed monic polynomial division on the literal quotient at $H=4,5,6$, including proper subfields and nontrivial root corrections. Numerical exponentiation and sparse trial sizes in this oracle test the semantics; the all-size proof above uses the quasi-linear local routines.
