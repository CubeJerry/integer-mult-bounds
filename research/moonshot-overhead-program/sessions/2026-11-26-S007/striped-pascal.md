# The binary Pascal transform on wide tape records

Let $d\ge1$, $n=2^d$, and put $U=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ over $\mathbf F_2$. Write $n$ rows of $B$ arbitrary bits each, with the lane index varying fastest. For the application $B=2^{2d+1}$. More generally take $n^\gamma\le B\le n^K$ for fixed positive $\gamma,K$. There is a fixed deterministic finite-alphabet multitape machine for each fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$ that applies $U^{\otimes d}$ to every lane in this physical order. It takes
$$
O_{\eta,\gamma,K}\bigl(nB(1+d)/(1+\log_2(d+2))^\eta\bigr)
$$
steps and visits $O_{\eta,\gamma,K}(nB)$ cells. The same machine inverts the transform since $U^2=I$. There is a circuit proof with a much smaller exponent in $d$. The distinction in the tape proof is the cost of actually moving the rows.

**Sources.** The November 24 [tensor shear lemma](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex) represents $U^{\otimes2}$ by a finite invertible word on four coordinates. In characteristic two its letters are monomials and a ternary Fourier block fixing the fourth coordinate. The [prime-radix kernel theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/kernel.tex) gives an effective recursive program for powers of the ternary block, including the actual batches, permutations, phases, work rows and child multiplicities. Its indexing bound assumes unit-cost operations on address words. Here each such whole-array rearrangement will be paid. The [tape interchange theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/interchange.tex) supplies fixed-tape interchange with any fixed exponent $\tau>1/2$ and linear visited space. [Tal, §§2.3 and 6, Corollary 6.2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/) explicitly allows any such rational $\tau<1$, giving $\eta=1-\tau$. These inputs are independent of the proposed multiplication overhead.

## Moving one layer

For $N$ records of common width $\beta\ge N^{\gamma'}$ and binary destinations computable in $\operatorname{poly}(\log N, d)$ per record, append equal-width destination keys and stably sort. The sort takes $O(N\beta(1+\log N)/(1+\log(2+\log N))^\eta)$ steps and $O(N\beta)$ cells. Allow a constant enlargement for headers, scalar coordinates or bounded arrays. Scan out the payload, remove keys, clear the sort interval and return heads in linear extra work. Mark the extreme cells reached in this invocation, extending the marks on first crossing. Returning over its own interval costs the current sort's volume even when a previous call used more scratch. A fixed extra field for the source row permits tie-breaking or an inverse sort. Copies and fanout of bounded multiplicity can be expanded into complete marked streams before sorting. The algorithm here only needs a constant number of such operations per fixed-template invocation. With $N=O(3^k)$ for $k\le d/2$, $\beta=\Theta(B)$ satisfies the width hypothesis and dominates the polynomial work for making the address, all fixed-degree arithmetic and preprocessing per row. Even at a small descendant, the width remains $B$ bits. A scan of one row pays for reading its dimensions, returning the control head and generating the destination. The case of a single record is a direct scan.

At a pointwise instruction, store one component per lane of a fixed finite field, with a constant number of bit planes. Align the fixed set of input rows and scan the planes together. Finite-field addition and multiplication by the prescribed constant have a fixed truth table. The operand order and the number of spectator coordinates are part of the shape. Rewinding the corresponding work banks costs a constant number of their lengths.

## The actual ternary recursion

Use the prime-radix construction at $\ell=3$, fixing a template with exponent $\theta=3/4$. In characteristic two all inverted threes are units and the primitive cube root lies in $\mathbf F_4$. The source first expands the finite free repair extension over its cyclotomic base ring. An extension-valued independent row carries one independent base row per basis component, and each directional call expands by the same factor. Only then specialize the coefficient matrices to the four-bit field containing the cube root. Tensors of $f$ columns reuse the same finite constants. At this fixed arity $A$, finite width $W$ and number $\sigma$ of selected-axis calls, choose the source integer $r$ so
$$
\lambda=(\sigma/W)(1+W/3^r)<A^{3/4}<A .
$$
These are constants of one program. At active ternary width $k$ put $f=\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor$ when positive. The bounded leftover axes are handled by the ordinary ternary transform. There are $3^{k-Af}$ independent rows, batched in groups of $W$, padding the last. Each selected-axis call on one work row has $3^{(A-1)f}$ fibres of length $3^f$. The total child bit volume, with multiplicity and genuine padding at this node, is at most $\lambda$ times the incoming volume; this follows from $W\lceil3^{k-Af}/W\rceil\le(1+W/3^r)3^{k-Af}$. No factor for the data lanes is lost: those are present identically in every row and child. Coefficient extensions are compiled once at the root, and recursively called transforms have the same field and plane width. The fixed number of simultaneous temporary rows is a live-space factor, not a per-level multiplier on the mathematical child volume.

For each paid call gather the selected trits in lexicographic order as [unchanged trits][selected trits][lane] by a destination sort, transform each consecutive fibre, and sort the returned rows into the caller's precise coordinates. The preceding/following fixed common-coordinate changes act by the source's explicit columnwise linear index maps. Compose them with the gather/scatter keys or route them separately. Signs, phase exponents and finite spectral-repair table indices are computed by scanning columns as specified in the uniformity paragraph of the source. Tables are finite after the template choice. A polynomial-time bit calculation of a key suffices because every local record carries the entire original wide strip. Bounds on the number of distinct linear passes are those of the fixed template, independent of $f$. The mathematical query/reply contents may depend on preceding replies; process these passes in the source order.

## Returning batches and bounding the union of visited cells

A directional call may have exponentially many fibres. Parking or rewinding all parent banks for each fibre would invalidate the estimate. At the start of a selected-axis pass, park surviving parent arrays once on a suspension stack, and push the sorted fibre stream in reverse onto a pending-input stack. For each fibre, pop only that packet into the active bank, recurse, and append its reply to a separate output stack. A child uses these same stack ends above saved markers and restores them on return. After the final fibre, copy the reply extent in its original order (a linear reversal on a temporary bank suffices), restore parent arrays once and issue the inverse layout sort. The continuation records extents and the next batch. Each transfer moves its own length and a descriptor of polynomial size in the root digit width, absorbed by its at-least-$B$-bit record. Ordinary banks use common marked origins. Loading the next child never traverses older pending arrays. There are a fixed number of parent preparations because the template and maximum inactive-digit count per node are fixed.

Let $V$ be unpadded local volume. The fixed number of live row banks, sort scratch, suspended arrays and pending/returned packets occupy $O(V)$ marked cells. Each child carries $3^f B$ field coordinates, compared with $3^k B$ at its parent, a ratio at most $1/3$. The sum along a descent is bounded by a constant times the root volume, including fixed-width batch padding. Ordinary heads return to their origins and stack heads to their own ends. Each tape reuses the same intervals on later fibres. The maxima of these intervals over the fixed tapes bound all distinct cells visited through halt. Controls per level are absorbed in the wide record and the geometric packet bound.

Take $g(k)=(1+k)/(1+\log_2(k+2))^\eta$. Local scans, key generation and finite arithmetic cost $O(V)$ per fixed instruction, and a sort costs $O(Vg(k))$. The maximum time per bit with the root lane width maintained therefore obeys
$$
t(k)\le Cg(k)+\lambda t(\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor).
$$
The ratio $g(\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor)/g(k)$ tends to $1/A$. Take a fixed cutoff beyond which $\lambda g(f)/g(k)\le(1+\lambda/A)/2<1$. Below it a direct ternary transform takes a fixed number of volume scans. Induction gives $t(k)=O(g(k))$, including returns over the true multiplicity of child fibres.

## Four-coordinate word and the external order

For $d=2j$, tensor the source's four-coordinate word $j$ times. An explicit four-bit word expands each of the twelve three-coordinate slots of Jain's checked matrix product into two forward ternary Fourier transforms and monomial factors. The complete word has 24 Fourier slots and at most 25 intervening monomials. [Word and specialization](finite-field-shear-audit.md) An independent existence argument follows from the published shear lemma. Dovetail the enumeration of finite binary field extensions containing a cube root and of finite words in their monomial and three-coordinate Fourier generators. Verify each $4\times4$ product. The shear lemma ensures termination. This absolute search, or a hardwired checked word, contributes a constant in time and visited cells. A monomial tensor letter has one permutation and one scalar computed from the $j$ digits. Route rows by their computed destinations and apply their scalars lane by lane.

For a Fourier letter acting on three of four symbols, read the four-ary address, compute the mask of positions in its ternary block, and gather the active trits in positional order. Key each row by (mask, ternary index) with fixed-width binary fields. Each row has a distinct key. Sort once and process each consecutive mask sector. A dedicated pair of top-level input and output heads streams the sectors through reusable recursive scratch, advancing only across the current sector. A mask of weight $k$ gives exactly $3^k$ rows, acted on by $D_3^{\otimes k}$. The singleton symbols are determined by the mask. Recreate the original address while reading the returned sector and sort back before the next letter. Sorting costs $O(nBg(d))$; the sectors have total length $n$ and $g(k)\le Cg(d)$. Sector scratch is reused. An odd remaining digit needs one direct paired pass. The result is binary since the external matrix is $U^{\otimes d}$. Strip the constant extension planes on output.

The proof imports the published ternary batch identity and Tal's fixed-tape routing. The ordinary series of complete binary butterfly passes takes $\Theta(nBd)$ steps. The priced wide-record algorithm is asymptotically faster. A square-root-times-polylogarithmic tape clock remains open.
