# A fixed ternary word for the Pascal tensor at every binary precision

Let $R_H=\mathbb Z/2^H\mathbb Z$, $U=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ and $S_H=R_H[e]/(e^4+e+1)$. There is a 56-Fourier-letter word for the exact matrix $U^{\otimes2}$ over $S_H$ for every $H\ge1$. Its supported triples, diagonal positions and permutations are fixed. Its diagonal units depend on $H$ and are computable in polynomial bit time. The striped transform and its inverse on $2^d$ rows of $B$ independent $R_H$-values take $O_{\eta,\gamma,K}(2^d BH(1+d)/(1+\log(d+2))^\eta)$ fixed multitape steps and $O_{\eta,\gamma,K}(2^dBH)$ cells ever visited when $2^{\gamma d}\le B\le2^{Kd}$ and $H\le(1+d)^K$, for fixed $\gamma,K>0$ and rational $0<\eta<1/2$. The entries are the integer bit-subset incidence coefficients. At $H>1$ these differ from ordinary binomial translation coefficients already at $d=2$.

## Sixteen adjustable units

The reduction of $S_H$ is $k=\mathbf F_{16}$ in the specified power basis. A scalar is a unit iff its reduction is nonzero; a residual inverse lifts via $v\gets v(2-uv)$. The source primitive cube root $w_1$ lifts uniquely from $w^2+w+1=0$ since $2w+1$ is a unit. Start with the checked 24-forward-ternary-letter word for $U^{\otimes2}$ over $k$. Lift its constants in the literal power basis as in the [modulo-four replay](verify-mod-four-word.py), using the actual lifted Fourier matrices. The resulting $V_H$ is invertible and has the right reduction. Set $P=U^{\otimes2}$ over $R_H$. [Base shear proof](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex) · [Explicit word and provenance](finite-field-shear-audit.md)

Take the sixteen pairs $(B_i,k_i)$ of the modulo-four rank test. The reductions of $C_i=B_iE_{k_i,k_i}B_i^{-1}$ form a $k$-basis of all $4\times4$ matrices. Each $B_i$ is one ternary Fourier matrix on a triple followed by a diagonal of cube roots on that triple, acting identically on the fourth coordinate. Lift it by the same actual root as $V_H$. Define
$$W(u)=\left[\prod_{i=1}^{16}B_i\big(I+(u_i-1)E_{k_i,k_i}\big)B_i^{-1}\right]V_H.$$
Starting with $u_i=1$, maintain $W(u)=P\pmod{2^t}$ and $u_i=1\pmod2$, initially $t=1$. For $t<H$, compute $L=((P-W(u))/2^t\bmod2)\,\overline P^{-1}$ coefficientwise in the free rank-four basis. Solve the fixed invertible $k$-linear system $\sum_i d_i\overline C_i=L$. Replace $u_i$ by $u_i+2^t\widetilde d_i$ with any power-basis lifts. All products of two perturbations vanish modulo $2^{t+1}$, and every unperturbed conjugated diagonal is identity modulo two. Therefore
$$W(u+2^t\widetilde d)-W(u)=2^t\bigl(\sum_i d_i\overline C_i\bigr)\overline P\pmod{2^{t+1}}.$$
This proves the next bit. At most $H-1$ constant-dimensional passes compute all sixteen units. Each $B_i$ costs one ternary transform, and its inverse uses one forward transform with the reversal permutation and the actual scalar $3^{-1}$. The resulting $24+32=56$ Fourier slots are independent of $H$; only the sixteen diagonal units and the existing lifted scalars change. The fixed tangent basis can be hardwired or regenerated once over its four-bit field.

## Uniform bit cost of the tensor powers

A root row contains $B$ arbitrary $H$-bit coefficients. An intermediate $S_H$ value has four $H$-bit coefficients. Additions and multiplication by arbitrary prescribed constants take $O(H(1+\log(H+2))^C)$ ordinary multitape steps for an absolute $C$: use a bounded number of short integer products, additions, coefficient truncations and degree-four reductions. Reading, storing and returning to the constant is included. Per-row source addresses, products of $O(d)$ monomial factors, and Fourier scales take $\operatorname{poly}(d,H)$ time, absorbed in the $B\ge2^{\gamma d}$ genuine inputs maintained through recursion. Tal sorts the wide rows without inspecting payload bits, charging linearly in the transported $H$ factor. Extension-ring scalar work is a separate term.

Put $g(k)=(1+k)/(1+\log(k+2))^\eta$, $L_H=(1+\log(H+2))^C$. For the fixed template parameters $A,\lambda$ with $\lambda<A^\theta$, $1/2<\theta<1$, the ternary step count per bit obeys $t(k)\le C_0(g(k)+L_H)+\lambda t(\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor)$ at recursive sizes. A bounded-$k$ call costs $O(L_H)$. The $g$ contribution is $O(g(k))$ by the strict $\lambda<A$ argument in the striped proof. The constant-$L_H$ forcing contributes $O(L_H(1+k)^\theta)$ by induction using $\lambda<A^\theta$. All mask sectors in one of the 56 letters have total volume at most a constant times the starting volume. Summing their true multiplicities gives normalized work $O(g(d)+L_H(1+d)^\theta)$. For $H\le(1+d)^K$ this is $O_{K,\eta}(g(d))$. A remaining odd axis uses one direct paired sweep. For the inverse, $U^{-1}=DUD^{-1}$ with $D=\mathrm{diag}(-1,1)$; negate the prescribed rows before and after the same transform. At positive precision applying the forward map twice can produce wrong data.

All parked streams, fibre returns and recursion stacks use the identical row/call multiplicities of the binary proof. Each live payload widens by a factor $O(H)$. Polynomial-$H$ Hensel setup and reused short-arithmetic scratch fit the root $O(2^dBH)$ volume. Incoming and outgoing rows and recursive stacks reuse the same marked intervals through halt. This proves the claimed physical time and total distinct-cell bounds.
