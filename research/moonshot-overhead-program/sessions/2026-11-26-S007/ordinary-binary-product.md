# An ordinary binary polynomial product through the selected planes

The [stronger ordered directory](binary-transform-and-product.md) now matches the direct-grid clock on this proof’s individual-factor path as well. The short-key invoice below remains a fallback.

For each fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$, the S007 supplies multiply two ascending lists of $n$ binary coefficients in
$$
O_\eta\!\left(n(1+\log n)/(1+\log\log(n+3))^\eta\right)
$$
steps and $O_\eta(n)$ distinct tape cells over the complete computation. The output consists of all $2n-1$ ascending binary coefficients, including zeros. This is a fixed-alphabet, fixed-multitape statement. The proof uses the all-size [batched tape convolution](lane-packed-convolution.md), the [ordinary-to-trace conversion](relative-trace-radix.md), the [smaller grouped transform](packed-chirp.md), its [two-fibre join](fibre-join.md) and the [plane charts](full-plane-crt.md).

## A product without a degree-$b$ factor sort

Handle $n=1$ directly. Otherwise let $b$ be the least positive multiple of three with $q=2^b\ge 2n-1$, so $2n-1\le q<8(2n-1)$ and $b=\Theta(1+\log n)$. Set $c=b/3$, $a=2^c$, and $K=\mathbf F_q$. Append zeros to both input streams to length $q$ and apply the selected-plane evaluation to each. Each forward transform ends at $O(q/b)$ ordered words of $K$. All words have $b$ bits, and both streams have identical serial order. At every position return $f(x)g(x)$ in $K$ using one short field product. Start the inverse plane transforms with precisely this stream. The word at $x$ is $(fg)(x)$. For each $u\in K$ there is a selected $x$ and some $j$ with $x^{2^j}=u$. Frobenius transports $(fg)(x)$ to $(fg)(u)$ since both inputs have binary coefficients. Consequently all repeated selected representatives, proper subfields and the two members of a twisted pair satisfy the inverse's compatibility conditions. A polynomial of degree below $q$ is determined by its values at all $q$ field elements. The inverse returns the unique degree-$<q$ representative of $fg\bmod(X^q+X)$. Since $\deg(fg)\le 2n-2<q$, this is the ordinary product. Copy the first $2n-1$ bits and return the output head.

There are three successive chirp batches per transform, one in $L=\mathbf F_a$ with $\lceil a^2/c\rceil$ independent packets and two plane axes over $K$ with $2Ra$ packets each. Here $2R$ is the number of selected planes, including both planes in every stored trace pair. The packet lengths are $(2c-1)(a-1)$ and $(2b-1)(a-1)$ respectively. Each batch has total bit volume $O(q)$, input length $2^{c+O(\log c)}$ per packet and $2^{2c-O(\log c)}$ independent packets. Independent lanes means separate storage and disjoint operations for these jobs. Their inputs may have repeated fixed operands or be correlated by earlier transforms. The uniform batched multiplier works for every assignment. The two plane axes are successive phases. Give each its own full multiplication call.

Every whole-volume key sort in this schedule has wide records. The smaller transform's root order uses $a$ rows of width $\Theta(a^2)$. Its actual child-factor to trace-pair order uses $O(a/c)$ rows of width $\Theta(ca^2)$. The batch multiplier uses its independently paid wide entry and exit sorts with at least a fixed power of the packet length as payload. Their clocks and the striped Pascal clocks are $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$. Small metadata lists and their short-key sorts cost at most $a^2\operatorname{poly}(b)=o(q)$. The other physical operations are address-bit permutations with exponent $\rho<1$, $O(q\log b)$ local index moves and $O(q\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ short field or point arithmetic, with the existing $O(qb^{2/5}\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ matrix terms. Every one fits the displayed transform bill. No degree-$b$ FS1 inventory is an argument of the selected-grid product.

Two nonidentical inputs require two forwards; the inverse is a separate third transform. The finite number of source streams, waiting operand and return streams occupy $O(q)$ bits. Reuse the same marked workspace intervals at each transform. A field operation on $b$ bits, including modulus setup and its head return, takes $O(b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ time and the common local workspace is $\operatorname{poly}(b)$. All input counting, zero padding, copying, trimming, clearing and returns fit the bound. This completes the claimed cumulative-space accounting.

## Independent FS1 product and its inventory

There is a second complete implementation. Retain every actual degree-$b$ FS1 factor and its degree-$d$ coefficient stream in the caller's prescribed degree/Lyndon order. The inventory contains exactly the monic irreducibles of degrees $d\mid b$, each once. Indeed the roots of $X^{2^b}+X$ are all of $K$, its derivative equals 1, and a root of exact degree $d$ belongs to $K$ precisely when $d\mid b$. For each divisor $d$ the number $I_d$ is fixed recursively by $dI_d=2^d-\sum_{j\mid d,\ j<d}jI_j$. In particular $\sum_{d\mid b}dI_d=q$ and the product of the inventory polynomials is exactly $X^q+X$.

Run two full FS1 forwards, multiply the two residues modulo their common actual monic factor in each position, then run the full inverse. Ordinary multiplication at length $d$ and fast monic remainder by the reversed reciprocal cost $O(d\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ per factor, even if the reciprocal is prepared on demand. Streaming one modulus beside the two residues and recycling the local banks adds only $O(q\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ time and $O(q)$ visited cells. The top [factor directory](point-factor-directory.md) uses $\Theta(b)$-bit records and gives this version the time $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log\log(b+3))^\eta)$. Equality in its quotient implies ordinary equality at the same degree threshold as above.
