# Polynomial convolution over Z/4 for polynomially many independent inputs

Fix constants $0<\gamma<K$ and a rational $0<\eta<1/2$. For $D^\gamma\le J\le D^K$, one fixed finite-alphabet multitape machine multiplies $J$ arbitrary pairs of length-$D$ polynomials over $A=\mathbf Z/4$ in $O(JD\log(D+2)/(1+\log\log(D+3))^\eta)$ steps with $O(JD)$ cells visited. It returns every coefficient in its original packet order. Each input coefficient has two bits. The argument extends the [binary batch construction](lane-packed-convolution.md) at fixed precision.

## A genuine lifted shear word

Let $S=A[t]/(t^4+t+1)$ with residue field $k=\mathbf F_{16}$. Lift the specified order-three root to $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$ by its unit derivative. Use $D=(\omega^{ij})_{i,j<3}$ with one spectator. Lift the nonzero scalars of the [verified four-coordinate word](finite-field-shear-audit.md) to units and use the actual $D$ at the Fourier slots. The resulting product $V$ reduces to $U\otimes U$, for $U=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.

The kernel modulo two consists of $I+2M$; multiplication adds $M$ over $k$. Conjugating a diagonal $I+2uE_{ii}$ by an allowed $B$ gives $I+2\bar u\bar B E_{ii}\bar B^{-1}$. On three chosen coordinates, $D E_{ii}D^{-1}$ has all nine residue entries nonzero. Conjugation by a residue unit diagonal weights each ordered off-diagonal entry by a distinct nonconstant character on $(k^\times)^3$. Character orthogonality isolates each one since $15$ is invertible in $k$. Vary the triple and include the ordinary diagonal directions. These projections span all $4\times4$ matrices over $k$. Linear combinations become products of actual conjugated diagonal units in $S$. The inverse of $D$ is $3^{-1}$ times a positive transform and a coordinate negation on the triple, with the spectator fixed. Correct $(U\otimes U)V^{-1}$ by this procedure. The complete word has bounded length.

The [executable word check](verify-mod-four-word.py) instantiates the published 24 Fourier slots and the projector correction. The naive lift misses 14 of 16 matrix entries. The corrected word agrees in every entry over $S$. [Certificate](mod-four-word-receipt.json)

## Fixed-radix batches and the long convolution

The November ternary kernel is defined over $\mathbf Z[1/3,\zeta_3]$ and explicitly specializes under ring homomorphisms. Its finite free repair module has a fixed multiplication table over that base. Tensor it with $S$ and use the same basis. A rank-$t$ free template still accepts $t$ independent base rows and multiplies both recursive calls and rows by $t$. The strict normalized child inequality in the [striped supplier](striped-pascal.md) survives. Use the actual inverse factors $3^{-j}$, each of constant size modulo four. Elements of the fixed repair algebra take a constant number of two-bit coordinates. The tape permutations, row-count recurrence and bounds for visited cells apply to these same records. The lifted word therefore supplies $U^{\otimes j}$ on the stated wide rows over $A$. [Ring-universal kernel](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/kernel.tex)

For a batch as small as $J≥D^\gamma$, enlarge the fixed group size to $k\ge\max(k_{\rm borrow},\lceil4/\gamma\rceil)$. Take $k$ to $2k-1$ prime axes per group. With $T=\lceil\log_2(D+2)\rceil$, the number of axes is at most $T/\log_2(CT/2)+O(1)$ and the group count $G$ is at most that number divided by $k$. The product $M$ of the occupied lane multiplicities has $\log_2 M\le G(1+\log_2G)\le (1/k+o(1))T$. Thus $M\le D^{\gamma/2}$ and the residual strip has width $P=\lceil J/M\rceil\ge D^{\gamma/2}$ with $PM\le2J$. Since $2G=o(T)$, the required striped slabs fit. The fixed group address tables still have polynomial size in $T$. This includes the linear-size child batch in the outer triadic split.

For the large field of the binary batch proof, choose the same residue extension of degree $e$ and lift its defining polynomial coefficientwise to obtain a free unramified ring $E/A$. For each odd required order $m$, find a primitive residue root by the already charged binary procedure. One Newton correction to $X^m-1$ lifts it uniquely because the derivative is a unit. Its order is exactly $m$ by reduction. Distinct powers at the required nodes have unit difference, all Fourier lengths are units, and the geometric Newton, Toeplitz, inverse-series and occupied-neighbour identities in the [source](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/layers.tex) use only such units or unipotent additions. In the elimination of a fixed invertible short matrix, an invertible residual column has a pivot with nonzero reduction. Choose that unit for elimination. A nonunit scalar shear with coefficient $u$ is a product of two shears of coefficients $1$ and $u-1$, which are units. Zero coefficients can be omitted. This preserves bounded slot expansion and restores the borrowed data exactly. The normalized directed pairs can use the mask-specific native schedule of the binary proof; on each active row perform additions in $A$. Equivalently use two global Pascal calls with $(s,t)=(\omega,1-\omega)$ for an active pair and $(1,-1)$ for a passive pair, choosing a primitive cube root in $E$.

The lower-half mixed-radix encoding is an injection into $E[z]/z^r$ on an odd cyclic grid. Occupied input exponents still add without wraparound, and output collisions are summed in $A$. Multiplication of two jets reduces to one ordinary length-$O(er)$ product over $A$ and $O(r)$ short monic reciprocal reductions of size $O(e)$, including scalar-times-jet as a special case. For these calls use the separate Cantor–Kaltofen triadic algorithm in the [first-carry proof](first-witt-carry.md) with time $O(s\log(s+2)\log\log(s+3))$ on length $s$. Its proof invokes no batch result. Since $\log(er)=O(T^{4/5}\log T)$ for $T=\lceil\log_2(D+2)\rceil$, this costs $O(T^{4/5}\operatorname{polylog}T)$ per jet coefficient even after the $O(\log T)$ synchronized slots. At every outer-layout operation the independent two-bit coefficients are complete payloads. All labels, external sorts, polynomial-batch padding, setup and shared scratch have the same size bounds as in the [binary proof](lane-packed-convolution.md). The short-call time and hence its distinct visited cells are $D^{o(1)}$ on reusable local tapes. These estimates prove the statement.

Applying one outer Cantor–Kaltofen split produces a polynomially wide batch of ordinary products of square-root length up to constants. The paid outer triadic transforms and scans give $O(s\log s)$ time, $O(s)$ visited cells for a single mod-four product. Its special square supplies the one global divided-square polynomial needed for the first carry. [Outer split and actual literal-factor correction](first-witt-carry.md)

The [balanced two-stage transform](macro-triadic-product.md) also prices the outer triadic transforms below the level-by-level clock. It gives $O_\eta(s\log(s+2)/(1+\log\log(s+3))^\eta)$ time and $O(s)$ cells for one ordinary modulo-four product.
