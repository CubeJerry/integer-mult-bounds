# Routing the selected trace planes into the literal FS1 orbit order

Let $b=3c$, $a=2^c$, $q=2^b$, $K=\mathbf F_q$, and $L=\mathbf F_a$. In either direction the two evaluated $L^2$-planes per S006 quotient representative can be converted to the ordinary coefficient bits of every binary factor residue in the original FS1 degree/Lyndon order in $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$ fixed multitape steps and $O_\eta(q)$ cells visited through halt, for every fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$. The input order is the actual plane order, with each of its two axes enumerating powers of the prescribed generator followed by zero. The only steps charged at the displayed bound are sorts on genuinely wide records. The normal-coordinate routing uses $O_\rho(qb^\rho\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ steps for any fixed $1/2<\rho<1$, apart from the previously proved per-point moment and composition cost $O(qb^{7/10}+q\sqrt b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$. ## Affine permutations on an array of individual bits

Let $d$ bits address a complete array of $2^d$ single-bit entries. For a given invertible $d\times d$ binary matrix $M$ and shift $v$, send address $u$ to $Mu+v$ in $O_\rho(2^dd^\rho(1+\log d)^4)+\operatorname{poly}(d)$ time, using $O_\rho(2^d)+\operatorname{poly}(d)$ cells. The same bound times a linear factor in the payload applies to bounded or polynomial-width payloads by exposing complete within-row spectator bits and padding once.

First perform arbitrary specified permutations of the $d$ address positions on raw bits. Let $h=\lceil\log_2(d+2)\rceil$. By composing on the left with at most $h$ swaps of positions any position permutation can be made to fix the $h$ least-significant positions pointwise. Its remaining permutation acts on records of $2^h=\Theta(d)$ bits, the precise hypothesis of the published position corollary. Undo the extra swaps, each at linear cost by the tape interchange theorem with $u=1$, arbitrary leading, intervening and trailing ranges. This also allows any chosen $h$ positions to serve temporarily as the payload. [Tape interchange theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/interchange.tex) · [Bit-position and controlled-block permutations](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/permutations.tex)

Gaussian elimination gives a position permutation and triangular unit factors $PM=LU$. A triangular matrix with diagonal blocks $B,C$ is the product of $\operatorname{diag}(B,C)$ and one half-block update $Y\gets Y+DX$ (in the upper case exchange halves). Recurse on diagonal blocks, splitting each into two parts differing by at most one. Runs may be padded to the next power-of-two *length of the schedule* or indexed using their literal uneven sizes. At a given depth all updates are disjoint and have a single combined source set $X$ and disjoint target set $Y$. The recursive uneven halves can always be chosen with control size at most $\lceil 2d/3\rceil$ at nontrivial nodes, since a half of any block of length at least two has at most two thirds of its positions, apart from the harmless integer rounding. Each target bit's flip is a fixed linear function of these sources. If the complement of the targets is small at some depth, choose the shorter side for $Y$ by splitting each block accordingly; in fact both sides obey the same two-thirds bound. At least $d/3$ bits are untouched in this round. All matrix descriptions have polynomial size and ordinary matrix elimination takes polynomial setup.

Choose two disjoint $h$-bit chunks $H_1,H_2$ among unchanged positions, possible for sufficiently large $d$. Split every flip into its component using controls in $H_1$ and its component using the rest. The former ignores $H_2$, the latter ignores $H_1$. In each subpass permute an ignored chunk to the final field and keep it intact. The small-block lemma takes the other $d-h$ bits as address, with $\Theta(d)$-bit payload and all targets one-bit blocks. Its common external control set has at most $2d/3\le(1-\delta)(d-h)$ positions for an absolute $\delta>0$. Its truth tables for a given control setting are generated by vector dot products in $\operatorname{poly}(d)$ bit steps. Undo the positional conjugation after each subpass. The affine offset is one more depth of constant flips, implemented by partitioning the targets into halves to leave payload positions. There are $O(1+\log d)$ triangular levels. Degenerate small $d$ uses a bounded table. All routines reuse near-origin banks. This proves the raw-bit claim without placing an address or an auxiliary tag beside every bit.

## Full-trace plane slices

Use the *actual* FS1 degree-$b$ field $K_0$ and ordered binary normal basis $(\beta,\beta^2,\ldots,\beta^{2^{b-1}})$. Deterministically fix an isomorphism $K\to K_0$ by splitting the ambient defining polynomial over $K_0$ with the same basis-trace gcd method as in the [batch field setup](lane-packed-convolution.md), taking any one root. Compute the change-of-basis matrix in $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ time. Frobenius is the cyclic right rotation $F$ of an FS1 normal word, written from its first basis coefficient to its last. Split each such word into three consecutive $c$-bit blocks $X,Y,Z$. The relative trace's normal word consists of three copies of $X\mathbin\oplus Y\mathbin\oplus Z$. The repeated block is a binary basis representation of $L$. This follows by adding the three rotations by $0,c,2c$ positions. No compatibility between the FS1 and plane construction's field polynomials or choice of normal elements is required.

Call a trace $t$ regular if its Frobenius period in $L$ is $c$ and the orbit of $t+1$ is disjoint from its own. Every orbit of regular traces has exactly one member among the selected $2R$ geometric planes: the quotient orbits identify precisely an orbit and its complemented orbit, and both chosen planes are evaluated. For a source point $x$ above selected $t$, let $w$ be its word in $K_0$. For a full-period $w$ let $\lambda$ be its lexicographically least rotation and $k$ the unique integer $0\le k<b$ with $w=F^k\lambda$. The irreducible polynomial of $\lambda$ is the exact degree-$b$ factor occupying that FS1 word. For its monomial residue $r=\sum_{i<b}r_i Y^i$ the planned ambient bit array is
$$ A[F^i\lambda]=r_i\qquad(0\le i<b). $$
With $t$ regular, output from $x$ the $c$ bits $r_{(k+j)\bmod b}$ for $0\le j<c$, placing bit $j$ at $F^jw$. Every field address above a regular trace has exactly one such source $(x,j)$: its trace selects uniquely the marked $t$ and $j<c$. Positions above the same degree-$b$ factor occur at three points in this selected plane, namely $x,F^cx,F^{2c}x$. Their starting offsets differ by $c$ modulo $b$, so their three slices partition all $b$ coefficients. A degree-$c$ point or a point above an irregular trace is assigned to the exception stream below. The same is done when the leading substring of $\lambda$ is outside the fast prefix range defined next.

At each selected point compute the required $r$ by the established deterministic moment/Padé method. The normal word $w$ is obtained in $O(b)$ steps per point, after tabulating the normal-basis words for $t$, $v y$ and $w_0 z$ for all elements $y,z\in L$ and the two fixed plane directions $v,w_0$. These tables and all their visits in enumerating the planes cost $O(a\operatorname{poly}(b))$ setup and constant $O(b)$ scans per point, using a rewound table for the inner axis. The lexicographically least cyclic rotation with its index can be computed on one-head tapes in $O(b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ steps per point. For example, rank the cyclic substrings of length $2^h$ at all $b$ start positions. To double, cyclically shift the ordered rank stream, attach the second rank and position, radix sort the $O(\log b)$-bit pair keys, assign equal ranks to equal pairs and sort back by position. Stop when the compared length is at least $b$. This also detects a short period. The number of selected points is $O(q/b)$. No $b$-bit header is charged once per raw ambient bit.

At a marked trace plane the two grid axes initially follow the powers of a fixed generator and zero. Relabel the first axis by its $c$-bit additive coordinate in $L$, using a sort of $a$ rows with $ab$ bits each. Transpose the two axes and do the same for the other one, then transpose back. The relocation tables for the two orders have $a\operatorname{poly}(b)$ bits and can be generated in original or target order. Use a sequential precomputed list of destination keys; a single plane pass reads it once, resets it after $a$ rows, and never scans a full table *per row*. Each actual row width is polynomially large in its record count. All such sorts across disjoint planes cost $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$ time and $O(q)$ reused cells by Tal. These axis reorders can be performed on the $c$-bit residue slices or the original $b$-bit values. [Tal, §§2.3 and 6, Corollary 6.2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/)

Transpose $[y,z][j<c]$ to $[j][y,z]$ inside each selected regular plane. Padding the short $j$ axis to its next power of two is less than twofold; moving its $O(\log b)$ digit positions using raw interchanges gives $O(Vb^\rho\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ or less, for total $V=O(q)$. Each resulting row has $a^2$ actual bits. Its target trace header $s$ is the repeated length-$c$ block of $F^j(t)$. Sort these at most $a$ wide rows by $s$ and insert the unoccupied trace rows as zeros. For each occupied pair $t,j$, compute the affine map from the two additive $c$-bit plane axes to the first two normal-word blocks $(X,Y)$ of $F^j(t+v y+w_0 z)$. Since the third block must be $s\oplus X\oplus Y$, these two blocks uniquely determine a point on its trace plane; the affine map is bijective. Generate its columns by applying the field isomorphism and cyclic rotation to the $2c$ basis directions, costing $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ per entire $a^2$-bit row. Apply the raw affine permutation above. The array is now $[s][X][Y]$. One global affine address permutation sends it to $[X][Y][s\oplus X\oplus Y]$, which is precisely the actual FS1 normal word. All full-trace regular, accepted-prefix orbits thus have their $r_i$ at the stipulated raw addresses. Other addresses contain zero.

In the other direction undo that global permutation, both types of row permutation and the within-plane digit transposes. Each selected source $x$ receives its $c$-bit slice. To recover all $b$ coefficients at every regular selected point, retain three copies of that plane and route one or two Frobenius-$c$ shifts into alignment. On each trace-$t$ plane, $x\mapsto x^{2^c}$ and its square are invertible affine maps on the $2c$ additive axis bits: the two $L$ coordinates are fixed under this field automorphism. Receive slices from $x,F^cx,F^{2c}x$, and use $k,k+c,k+2c\pmod b$ to assemble them in increasing coefficient order. This consumes three copies of the real input volume. Recover the point value by the previously charged blocked modular composition of $r$ at $x$. Finally return to the original multiplicative-grid order. For legal independent FS1 inputs this recovers all point words required by plane interpolation.

## Cyclic windows on an untagged binary array

Put $h=\lceil\log_2 b\rceil$, $J=2^h$ and $R=\min(J,\lceil C h\log_2(b+2)\rceil)$ with a freely chosen absolute constant $C$. For one integer $p<R$ let its $h$-bit spelling be fixed. For every $b$-bit candidate $p\sigma$ with tail $\sigma$ of length $b-h$, produce the $b$ entries
$$ A[F^j(p\sigma)]\qquad (0\le j<b) $$
in row order $[\sigma][j]$. Processing a separate $p$ costs $O_\rho(qb^\rho\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ time and $O(q)$ cells, with a single one-bit raw input array. In particular there is no $q$-by-$b$ table of copies or addresses.

Partition the offsets $0,\ldots,b-1$ into two consecutive halves. For a half $H$ use the cyclic union $D$ of its length-$h$ forward windows, so $|D|\le\lceil b/2\rceil+h-1$. An instance of the prefix $p$ at offset $j\in H$ is exactly the condition that the original full word is a right rotation by $j$ of a candidate starting with $p$. Apply a fixed position permutation so all $D$ positions come first, followed by the other positions. Each $D$-pattern $d$ now owns a contiguous spectator row of $2^{b-|D|}$ bits. Compute all offsets $j\in H$ whose window in $d$ equals $p$, and copy the complete row once for every such $j$, recording just $j$ per copied *row*. Across this half the copied volume is exactly $|H|q/2^h$. Every fixed $j$ prescribes $h$ independent address bits. The original scan and all copies together cost $O(q)+2^{\lceil b/2\rceil+h}\operatorname{poly}(b)$ time. A row may contain many occurrences; reread the buffered row once per actual copy. Unused rows need only their initial scan.

Stably radix-partition the emitted rows by $j$ alone in $O(q(1+\log b))$ steps. For fixed $j$, the original $D$-patterns with its $h$ prescribed bits stay in lexicographic order. Deleting the fixed positions preserves precisely the lexicographic order of all $|D|-h$ unconstrained positions. The spectator coordinates stayed intact. Consequently this $j$ group is a full array on $b-h$ bits. A fixed permutation of those positions puts it in the tail order of $F^{-j}$ of the original word, omitting its prescribed leading prefix. Stream all $j$ groups, then transpose $[j][\sigma]$ to $[\sigma][j]$ with the $j$ axis padded to $J$, at cost $O_\rho(qb^\rho\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ and $O(q)$ space. For each $\sigma$ keep the $b$ bits exactly when $p\sigma$ is its full-period Lyndon representative and its trace is regular. Candidate verification uses $O(b\operatorname{polylog} b)$ local steps per $b$-bit row by cyclic doubling or a merged Duval stream, never per raw input cell. The surviving rows are already in FS1 order within this prefix. Process $p=0,\ldots,R-1$ successively, giving full-degree ordinary factors in their correct global order.

The inverse starts a zero $[\sigma][j]$ array for each prefix and inserts its actual FS1 residues at the same selected candidates. Transpose, undo each tail permutation, and invert the short $j$-key stable partition. The latter inverse can precompute all its decision-flag streams from the deterministic $D$-pattern/occurrence enumeration, with one bit per copied *row* per radix pass. There are at most $b2^{\lceil b/2\rceil+h}$ such rows. Generating the sorted flag streams with ordinary key-only radix passes costs $2^{b/2}\operatorname{poly}(b)$ time and cells. With two input heads for the sorted branches, each inverse pass consumes the next row specified by its stored flag. XOR multiple restored sibling rows back into their $D$-pattern, output zeros where there are none, and undo the first position permutation. XOR the two half arrays and the arrays for successive $p$ into one persistent $q$-bit bank. For each admitted full-period word $u$ exactly one candidate $F^{-j}u$ is its Lyndon word. All other candidate rows contain zero. Thus the inverse produces precisely the bits $A[u]$, including the rotations where the same $p$ occurs multiple times. The checksum here uses uniqueness of a full-period necklace representative; period-short factors take their own real degree below.

## Exceptional factors and the total invoice

There are at most $\sum_{d\mid b,\,d<b}2^d\le b2^{b/2}$ elements of short ambient degree. The number of short-period traces is at most $c2^{c/2}$. If a full-period trace also satisfies $F^j t=t+1$, then $0<j<c$ and $c\mid2j$, forcing $c$ even and $j=c/2$. There are at most $2^{c/2}$ roots of $Y^{2^{c/2}}+Y+1$. Thus all irregular trace planes together contain $O(ca^{5/2})$ ambient elements, including any exceptional full-degree factor they represent. Further, if the leading value of the least rotation is at least $R$, every cyclic length-$h$ window has value at least $R$. On a uniformly chosen binary word the $\lfloor b/h\rfloor$ disjoint length-$h$ windows are independent. If $R=J$ there are no such words; otherwise their number is at most
$$q(1-R/J)^{\lfloor b/h\rfloor}\le q\exp(-R/(6h)).$$
Choosing $C$ sufficiently large gives $O(q/b^A)$ such elements for any fixed desired $A$. These properties are invariant under Frobenius. Each selected geometric point occurs only once in the marked-plane inventory. Both the number of exceptional selected points and exceptional directory factors are bounded by $O(b2^{b/2}+ca^{5/2}+q/b^A)$. Small degrees below a fixed cutoff may use direct arithmetic.

Send these exceptional points and actual FS1 factors to the [ordinary polynomial-key join](point-factor-directory.md). Each can afford a fixed polynomial in $b$ for minpoly, value extraction, headers, composition, ordinary stable radix sorts and serial restoration: choose $A$ larger than that fixed exponent. Determine the classification in the FS1 stream from the degree, the true normal word, its initial prefix, and the cyclic period and complement test of its trace block. At a geometric point determine the same predicates after normal conversion. Proper degrees use the actual independent FS1 field for that divisor and a comparison via the monic polynomial key. The exception join emits its sorted FS1 replies or its restored sparse geometric serial replies. The full regular factor stream is already in increasing prefix and tail order. Run the actual FS1 degree/Duval enumerator once, reading the next record from the appropriate stream and outputting its $d$ bits. In inverse direction make that very same ordered split. The full trace classification costs $O(b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ per degree-$b$ word by cyclic doubling; the full enumeration costs $O(q\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$. When returning geometrical point values, merge exceptional serials with the dense regular stream by one monotone scan. No further sort of the full factor inventory is needed. [FS1 factor order and field convention](../2026-11-26-S005/route-a/binary-factor-stream-compiler.md)

The $R=O((1+\log b)^2)$ normal-array passes, their two halves, $O(b)$ tail slices of true aggregate volume $O(q)$ per prefix, and all candidate tests together contribute $O_\rho(qb^\rho\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ steps. The two additive-axis relabels and the target-trace reorder use a fixed number of real wide-record sorts over aggregate $O(q)$ volume, each at most $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log b)^\eta)$. Their inverse uses the same calls. All raw-bit banks, copy/reply streams and sort regions have common marked origins, each of size $O(q)$. An individual half has at most $O(q)$ copied payload bits; flags used to reverse its stable $j$-key sort occupy $2^{b/2}\operatorname{poly}(b)=o(q)$ bits, separately reusable for every prefix. The affine permutation setup, moment scratch and enumeration of all per-trace tables use $a\operatorname{poly}(b)=o(q)$ cells. Per-plane operands and per-$j$ slices are streamed into reusable scratch at common origins, with their outer heads advancing across only their own extents. Only the $q$-bit accumulation bank persists across the prefix loop. A constant number of disjoint intervals on fixed tapes therefore bounds the union of all cells visited through halt. This proves the claim for the real external order and independent inverse input.

At $b=3,6,9,12$ every factor is checked in its genuine FS1 order using ordinary polynomial division, with independent random FS1 replies in the other direction. The ten case settings deliberately lower the prefix threshold and include entirely exceptional, mixed and fully admitted prefix ranges.
