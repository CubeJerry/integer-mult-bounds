# Independent review of the root relative-trace planes

Let $b=3c$, $a=2^c$, $h=a^2$, $q=a^3$, $K=\mathbf F_{2^b}$, $L=\mathbf F_{2^c}$ and $T=X^h+X^a+X$. For each S006 least quotient representative $\alpha\in L$ of an orbit in $L/\mathbf F_2$, process both planes $t=\alpha,\alpha+1$. For each such $t$ the input is the actual remainder $A_t\equiv f\pmod{T+t}$ in increasing global $X$-monomials, encoded in the specified $K$ polynomial basis. The inverse starts from independent degree/rank-ordered FS1 residue polynomials. I checked the endpoints, the bivariate substitution and its inverse, the small-factor normalization, the costs and the all-point coverage.

## Plane bijection and its inverse

The restriction of $T$ to $L$ is the identity. The kernel $V$ has dimension two over $L$. Choose $v\ne0$ in $V$ and $w$ outside $vL$ in $V$ by elimination using the fixed ambient field. The grid point is $x=t+vy+wz$ for $y,z\in L$. Its Frobenius power is $x^a=t^a+v^a y+w^a z$. The matrix $M=((v,w),(v^a,w^a))$ has nonzero determinant: vanishing would imply $(v/w)^a=v/w$ and hence $v/w\in L$. The maps $(y,z)\mapsto x$ and $x\mapsto (x,x^a)$ give exactly the required $h$ evaluations at the $t$-fibre.

Split $X^{i+aj}$ into $U^iW^j$, $i,j<a$. Evaluation after the affine substitution $(U,W)=(t,t^a)+M(Y,Z)$ therefore equals the original univariate evaluation. Each elementary substitution preserves the total degree bound $2a-2$. In characteristic two a slope $U\mapsto U+\lambda W$ inserts the term at $(s,j+i-s)$ precisely for submasks $s\subseteq i$, with multiplier $\lambda^{i-s}$. For $\lambda\ne0$ prescale row $i$ by $\lambda^i$, perform the fixed-column Pascal transform with row rotations, and postscale row $s$ by $\lambda^{-s}$. Translations have the same prescale and postscale without the index shift. The zero-slope case is immediate. A square grid of side $2a$ prevents wraps. Its $K$-word rows have binary width $2ab$, satisfying the wide Pascal hypothesis. Interchanging the variables is a square address-field interchange with the field-bit coordinate as spectator. There are a fixed number of such operations for LU plus two translations. Roots $t=0,1$ require no special inverse. All field multiplication bills on $b$-bit local words have $b\operatorname{polylog}(b+2)$ time, covered by the final clock.

Forwards, exponents $a\le i\le2a-2$ fold to $i-a+1$ by $Y^a=Y$, and similarly for $Z$. One fold per axis suffices. Numerically interpolate the resulting canonical grid separately on both axes. For the inverse, start with any $h$ independent $K$ words, invert the two transforms, apply the inverse affine substitution, and set $W=X^a,U=X$. Total degree bounds the resulting degree by $2h-2a$. For $C+X^hD$ its high part satisfies $\deg D\le h-2a$, so the actual remainder is
$$
 C+(X^a+X+t)D,
$$
and has degree below $h$. The grid and this polynomial agree at all $h$ distinct fibre points; uniqueness gives the original $A_t$ and proves the inverse even on arbitrary grids. The collisions in $i+aj$ are paid: split each $U$-row into low/high halves at $a$ and add the high part into the next $W$-row by one shifted scan before the final high fold.

Both grid axes use the order $0,1,\xi,\ldots,\xi^{a-2}$ for a fixed generator of $L^\times$. The odd length-$a-1$ chirp handles the nonzero part and the saved zero-point word distinguishes the two endpoint coefficients. One ordinary binary polynomial product at coefficient stride $2b-1$ gives the ambient raw convolution without field-coordinate overlap. Reduce each local binary coefficient modulo the fixed degree-$b$ polynomial and fold cyclically. The inverse uses opposite phase and normalization one. For each axis, keep contiguous rows, transpose one field-word square between them, and treat the other axis as rows. Per grid, every full scan, transposition, affine scratch, both field-word transform banks and output uses $O(hb)$ ever-visited cells except the one reusable local ordinary-binary-multiplier scratch. All planes can reuse it: its time $O(ab\log(ab)4^{\log^*(ab)})=o(q)$ already bounds its visited space. The actual number of processed planes is $P=2R_{\rm quot}$, where $R_{\rm quot}$ counts cyclic Frobenius orbits in $L/\mathbf F_2$. With $g_j=\gcd(c,j)$, Burnside on normal words under rotation and complement gives
$$
 P=\frac1c\sum_{j<c}2^{g_j}\bigl(1+\mathbf1_{2\mid c/g_j}\bigr)=a/c+O(2^{c/2}).
$$
A complemented rotation fixes words exactly when every coordinate cycle has even length. Thus $P=4$ at $c=2$, although ordinary Frobenius on $L$ has three orbits. The raw total of all actual grids is $Phb=(3+o(1))q$. Thus there is room even to retain all representative grid values.

## From grid values to the exact full FS1 stream

If $x$ has orbit size $e$ and $s=T(x)^2+T(x)$ has orbit size $d$, then $d\mid e$. Exactly $e/d$ of the conjugates of $x$ occur in the retained pair of planes of this quotient orbit. In the untwisted case $T(x)$ has period $d$ and only one of the two planes in the pair is reached by these conjugates. In the twisted case its period is $2d$, and each plane is reached $e/(2d)$ times. The two evaluated planes are distinct even when conjugate. Every irreducible of degree dividing $b$ therefore occurs and no spurious factor occurs. To avoid an uncharged lookup, append to each retained grid value the binary minimal polynomial of $x$ and its physical plane position; both fields use $O(b)$ bits. The total number of entries is $Ph=O(q/b)$, including all duplicates. With ordinary mergesort, one skinny pass by polynomial and one by FS1 rank each cost $O(qb)$ steps and $O(q)$ tape cells. FS1 supplies the needed rank directory; it may use an unrelated field/normal basis because matching is by the *binary polynomial*. A single fixed number of sorted-stream joins both chooses one occurrence per polynomial and broadcasts its residue to all applicable locations on the inverse. Restoring the original grid order uses the stored positions and costs the same. Generate points in the physical $y,z$ order by short field recurrences with row resets; even a constant number of short field multiplications per point belongs to this bill.

A fully priced fallback for the selected $x$ recovers the monomial residue from the field word $v=f(x)$. Form $p=\prod_{j<e}(X-x^{2^j})$, compute $p'(x)$ by Horner, and invert it once. This denominator is nonzero by distinctness of the conjugates. Take $w=v/p'(x)$ and generate $w_j=w^{2^j}$. A balanced tree with leaves $(X-x_j,w_j)$ and node merge $(P_LP_R,G_LP_R+G_RP_L)$ yields the literal degree-$<e$ residue $G$ at the root by Lagrange interpolation. Each level's ambient coefficient volume is $O(be)$; binary Kronecker with stride $2b-1$, short modulus reductions and head resets cost $O(be\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ per level. One field inverse by ordinary Euclid can also be paid $O(b^2\operatorname{polylog}b)$. The full per-point bill is $O(b^2\operatorname{polylog}b)$, including local control, and works on proper subfields. In the other direction Horner of the actual FS1 remainder directly gives $v$ at each required root. Grouping/ungrouping finite polynomial coefficients and derivative sums cannot substitute dense Gaussian elimination with a free $O(b^2)$ label: a naïve binary solve would be cubic. The separate moment construction replaces this entire fallback at a lower clock.

These checks yield $O(qb\operatorname{polylog}(b+2))$ and $O(q)$ visited for each full binary root direction when combined with the priced input radix and child transform. This asserts the explicit family $3\mid b$. It does not by itself establish the square-root binary clock or any higher-precision carried transform.
