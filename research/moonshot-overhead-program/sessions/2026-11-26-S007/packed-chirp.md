# All smaller binary CRTs through one fixed binary product per packet

Put $a=2^c$, $h=a^2$, $q=ha$, $m=a-1$ and $K=\mathbf F_{2^c}$. Start in relative-trace order $[j<a][k<h]$ with $p_k(Y)=\sum_j D_j[k]Y^j$. The output is $[u][i<\deg u][k<h]$, containing bit $i$ of $p_k\bmod u$ in the FS1 degree/Lyndon order of the irreducible factors of $Y^a-Y$. Both endpoints have $q$ independent bits. The inverse accepts them all. This is the smaller caller of the [two-fibre join](fibre-join.md).

**Theorem.** In either direction, $B=\lceil h/c\rceil$ ordinary binary polynomial products of length $D=(2c-1)m$ suffice. Their second operand depends only on $c$ and the direction. Other operations on the fixed multitape machine cost $O_\eta(qc/(1+\log_2(c+2))^\eta)$ time and $O(q)$ cells ever visited for each fixed rational $0<\eta<1/2$. The packet lengths sum to $O(q)$. In particular the full smaller binary CRT costs **$O(qc\,4^{\log^* c})$ time and $O(q)$ cells ever visited** unconditionally in either direction, by [Harvey and van der Hoeven, Theorem 1.1 and §2.3](https://www.texmacs.org/joris/cyclomult/cyclomult.pdf).

### Full-rate packing and chirp

Choose a degree-$c$ irreducible $F$, its power basis $e_0,\ldots,e_{c-1}$ in $K$, and a generator $g$ of $K^\times$. Pack consecutive groups of $c$ independent binary inputs as $P=\sum_{v<c}e_v p_{k+v}$, zeroing unused lanes only in the last packet. Its $j$-th coefficient is already $c$ consecutive bits in strip $j$. Every complete packet represents an arbitrary $K$-polynomial of degree below $a$. Writing a field word at every root costs $acB=O(q)$ bits.

Keep $P(0)=P_0$ separately. Set $x=(P_0+P_m,P_1,\ldots,P_{m-1})$. Evaluation at the nonzero roots is the positive length-$m$ Fourier transform of $x$. The negative transform recovers $x$ since the odd integer $m$ represents $1$ in $K$. Recover $P_m=x_0+P(0)$. At $c=1$ the transform is the identity. Otherwise put $s=2^{-1}\bmod m$. By $2ij=i^2+j^2-(i-j)^2$,
$$P(g^i)=g^{s i^2}\sum_{j<m}(g^{s j^2}x_j)g^{-s(i-j)^2}.$$
This is one cyclic convolution with fixed kernel $H_j=g^{-s j^2}$. Negate exponents on return. For uniform setup use the FS1 field construction, trial division of $m$, and the prime-divisor tests on successive generator candidates. Tabulate chirps, inverse chirps and factor labels by short field arithmetic. Even $O(a^2\operatorname{poly}(c))$ setup time and $O(a\operatorname{poly}(c))$ setup space fit the root budget.

Represent each scaled word $z_j$ and kernel word $H_j$ by its binary polynomial of degree below $c$. Form
$$Z(W)=\sum_{j<m}W^{(2c-1)j}z_j(W),\qquad C(W)=\sum_{j<m}W^{(2c-1)j}H_j(W).$$
A product of two word representatives has degree at most $2c-2$. The blocks of $ZC$ contain the exact binary sums for $i+j=r$, with no overlap for distinct $r$. Reduce each block modulo $F$ and add the two output blocks with equal index modulo $m$, using zero for the missing high block. Both ordinary operands have length at most $D$. A rewound low-half bank and simultaneous scan of the high half implements the fold. There are $O(q/c)$ field words over all packets. Short field multiplication and reduction cost $O(c(1+\log c)^2)$ bit steps apiece by logarithmic guard packing, ordinary integer multiplication and monic reciprocal reduction from [S006](../2026-11-26-S006/stable-four-point-bundles.md). The long product is in $\mathbf F_2[W]$. Integer multiplication on the same bit strings would produce other coefficients.

### Return the literal binary factors

For each of the $a$ roots, compute its minimal polynomial by multiplying its conjugate linear factors. Match the resulting binary polynomial to the existing FS1 enumeration by a tape sort of short metadata. Within a factor order roots by multiplicative index, with zero in its own factor. Prepare the $d\times d$ Vandermonde on the $d$ distinct roots of each degree-$d$ factor and its inverse by field elimination. All tables fit $a\operatorname{poly}(c)$ time and cells. The binary polynomial identifies the factor even if FS1 uses a different normal element and field polynomial.

If $P\bmod u=\sum_{i<d}v_iY^i$, then its values on the roots of $u$ are the Vandermonde product with $(v_i)$. Inverting this matrix recovers
$$v_i=\sum_{v<c}e_v\,[Y^i](p_{k+v}\bmod u),$$
since the monic divisor $u$ is binary. Its power-basis bits are the $c$ separate replies. Conversely pack arbitrary FS1 remainders, apply the forward Vandermonde in each factor and the negative chirp to recover $P$. This proves both compositions including $Y$, $Y+1$, proper subfields, and padded lanes. It only uses uniqueness of monic division and of the complete binary CRT.

### Charge the complete physical order

1. Transpose the outside coefficient and packet indices so each polynomial is contiguous. Pad the number of packets to its next power of two for movement only. Each record has $c$ bits and the address has $O(c)$ and $\Omega(c)$ bits. The [tape bit-position corollary](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-integer-multiplication-on-tapes-November-24-2026/build/permutations.tex) at $\rho=3/4$ gives $O(qc^{3/4}(1+\log c))$ time and $O(q)$ cells. Discard padded packets before performing any products. After evaluation put zero last and transpose back, padding again for the transpose.
2. Each root now carries a wide row of $cB=\Theta(h)$ bits. Route the $a$ root rows once to (FS1 factor, local root) using the precomputed metadata. The common width is at least $a^2$. [Tal, Corollary 6.2](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/) gives $O_\eta(qc/(1+\log_2(c+2))^\eta)$ time and $O(q)$ visited cells. Build inverse keys in the reverse source order.
3. In each factor multiply the fixed $d\times d$ matrix over $K$ by its $d\times B$ matrix of packet columns. Tile the latter into $d\times d$ squares, padding the last and the fixed-rank recursion sides by bounded factors. Moving the small row index across the column tile number costs $O(q\log(c+2))$ altogether. Pad the short row field to a power of two for this move and move one of its $O(\log c)$ address bits at a time. Each split or interleaving of two substreams takes a constant number of interval sweeps, with returns. Reverse these moves after the products.
4. The [fixed exact matrix-multiplication scheme](grouped-ambient-words.md) of exponent $12/5$ works over $K$ by scalar extension of its binary bilinear identity. Prepare its two operand combinations, compute one child at a time, and add its contribution to the parked output in a bounded number of tile sweeps per node. The union of visited extents on one recursion branch is linear in the tile size. The short field operations above give $O(d^{12/5}c(1+\log c)^2)$ steps per tile. Since $\sum_u d=a$ and $d\le c$, the total is $O(qc^{2/5}(1+\log c)^2)$. The resulting order is [factor][coefficient][packet][lane]. Discard the unused last lanes. The reverse accepts precisely this compact order.
5. Give input, output and fixed kernel separate heads. Rewind the kernel once per packet, advance the outer streams monotonically and reset ordinary product banks to their common origins. Even without a separate space theorem, a supplier taking $M_2(D)$ steps visits at most $O(M_2(D)+D)$ cells per call. For the cited unconditional multiplier this is $O(ac^2 4^{\log^* c})=o(q)$, and all calls reuse the same region. Rewind/clear the extents of the present call. The whole construction, including tables, parent streams and the wide sort, visits $O(q)$ cells through halt. Multiplying the per-packet time by $B$ gives the stated bound.

Composing the S006 inverse prefix, the [relative-trace radix](relative-trace-radix.md), this construction, and the [two-fibre join](fibre-join.md) reaches each actual grouped monomial remainder. Reverse the steps on arbitrary legal grouped records. The complete coefficient-to-grouped-monomial time is $O(q b\,4^{\log^* b})$. All costs outside the repeated fixed binary product are $O_\eta(q b/(\log b)^\eta)$ on unbounded $b$. A fixed-kernel supplier with a strict saving at that scale would carry the saving through the assembled conversion. The square-root-scale goal also requires improvements to the radix and global directory motion. The additive product basis, the full root-degree-$b$ factor directory, and the higher-precision integer caller remain separate.
