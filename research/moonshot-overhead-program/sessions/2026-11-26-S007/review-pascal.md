# Independent review of the S007 Pascal compiler and radix change

I checked `striped-pascal.md` against the prime-radix recursion and the tensor shear lemma at OpenAI math revision `3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6`, and against §2.1, §2.3 and Corollary 6.2 of [Tal, ECCC TR26–331](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/331/download/). I also checked both directions of `relative-trace-radix.md`. The claimed fixed-tape time and linear visited-space bounds follow from the cited results. The following details delimit that conclusion.

1. **Actual words and sectors.** At a single four-coordinate Fourier letter of the tensor word, fix exactly the subset of positions belonging to the three active symbols. A mask of weight $k$ produces exactly $3^k$ records. Summed over masks this is $4^{d/2}$ for even $d$. The missing symbol in each nonactive position is uniquely determined by the letter, so gathering and scattering use inverse keys with no duplication. The one remaining binary digit at odd $d$ may be taken last, allowing a direct adjacent-pair scan with a one-record buffer and return. For sector traversal, keep the top gathered stream and the top reply stream on their own resident input and output tapes. Copy one consecutive sector into the ordinary active working banks and append its returned rows in sequence. This avoids any traversal of unprocessed sectors. Even one polynomial-size binary control allocation per mask is covered by the shortest sector's $B$ bits.

2. **Finite constants and recursion.** The ternary source works over a localization where 3 is invertible, which specializes to characteristic two. The cube root and the finitely many remaining phase coefficients fit in one fixed finite field. If using the source’s descent, its number $W$ of work rows already counts the chosen coordinates. One can instead represent coefficients in a fixed binary extension containing them all. The same representation is used at every level. Multiplying all $B$ lanes by one such scalar is a bounded number of tape scans. Each word and repair family is fixed before the input arrives; no layer enlarges the coefficient field. The columnwise trit maps and finite-table indices require only polynomially many bit steps per address, even when the source describes word-unit indexing.

3. **The child volume is the padded multiplicity.** For a node indexed by $k$, put $f=\lfloor(k-r)/A\rfloor>0$ and $u=k-Af$, hence $r\le u<r+A$. There are $p=\lceil3^u/W\rceil$ batches, a constant independent of $k$ but essential to the calculation. A selected-axis call contributes $3^{(A-1)f}$ distinct packet transforms of length $3^f$, not a single length-$3^f$ transform. The true child bits are $p\sigma 3^{(A-1)f}3^fB\le(\sigma/W)(1+W/3^r)3^k B$. No replicated estimate for transient work rows should be charged again to this mathematical packet count. A fixed number of distinct-coordinate sorts suffices for each call and each batch. Top masks require only one global gather and one scatter per fixed word letter.

4. **Sort price and resumption.** Corollary 6.2 gives $O(N\beta\log N/(\log\log N)^\eta)$ for records of common width $\beta\ge N^{\gamma'}$, including any common designated key width at most $\beta$. With $N=O(3^k)$ and $k\le d/2$, fixed $n^\gamma\le B\le n^K$ ensures the first inequality and pays a polynomial in $d$ for every key, even at small $k$. Bounded $N$ uses direct scans. Section 2.3 explicitly admits any rational interchange exponent in $(0,1)$ giving the input bound. Thus a separate fixed machine follows for each rational $0<\eta<1/2$ from interchange at $1-\eta$. An arbitrary fixed real $\eta<1/2$ is also bounded by choosing a slightly larger rational exponent in the open interval. Per call, mark the extrema visited on each ordinary tape of the sorter (keep track on the boundary cell and move a marker when crossed). Clearing and returning to the local origin then cost the current invocation's $O(N\beta)$ and never the maximum extent of an earlier call. Parent, pending-input and reply stacks have distinct tape heads. Recursion uses and restores the ends beyond the saved marker for each. A reverse-order extraction of a completed reply extent followed by a linear reversal restores record, packet and bit order. The total transfers at that parent are linear in its volume per fixed instruction; fetching a child costs only the child extent. Suspended-parent and stack extents over one active path are bounded by a geometric series because $3^f B/3^k B\le1/3$. Reuse on other branches bounds the union of cells visited, including both input and output.

5. **Exponent recurrence.** Let $g(k)=(1+k)/(1+\log_2(k+2))^\eta$. Since $f/k\to1/A$ and $(\log k)/(\log f)\to1$, $g(f)/g(k)\to1/A$. The strict source inequality $\lambda<A^{3/4}<A$ permits contraction after a fixed cutoff. Below the cutoff direct transforms cost a constant number of volume scans. Thus $O(g(k))$ time per incoming bit is closed without conflating the parent scratch ratio and the true child volume.

6. **Canonical radix and converse.** Under the shear $Z\mapsto Z+X^e$ each path consumes exactly the corresponding reduction of its $Z$-exponent, so over both slopes all column shifts sum to at most $a(a-1)$. This proves absence of wrap at $2a^2$. Forward blocks after the two shears obey $D_j=A_j+(X^a+X)B_j+B_{j-1}$ because $X^h=T+U$, with the top $B$ zero and each digit of degree below $h$. Synthesis reads $A_j+B_{j-1}$ because it evaluates at $X^h$. Monicity proves uniqueness for independent arbitrary digits. The bounded-carry converse uses the same path argument across both conjugated shears. At odd $c$, $a\equiv2\pmod 3$, so the $X^a$ terms cancel and the $X$ terms combine to 1. Four binary syntheses implement both extension-valued calls. The largest visited column is $a+2^{2u}-1+a(2^u-1)<a^2$, so no materialized intermediate carry can contaminate the reconstruction. Every nontrivial transform in this proof is binary-linear; “superlinear work” describes the cost of the Pascal routines more precisely than “nonlinear part”.

These results purchase $O(qb/(\log b)^\eta)$ for the radix. They do not yet purchase the square-root-scale seam required by the full grouped CRT recurrence. The above uses the *wide-key* Tal regime, and the exponent should stay attached to that specific routing argument.
