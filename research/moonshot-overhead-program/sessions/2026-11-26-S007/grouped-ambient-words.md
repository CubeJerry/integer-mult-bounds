# From grouped binary residues to the ordered ambient monomial words

At the grouped boundary of S006, the coefficient field can be changed in both directions within the unrelaxed binary budget. This is a partial conversion. Its output is the monomial remainder at the top coset, with the required ambient encoding and representative order. The additive routine still needs its product-basis coefficients there.

**Theorem.** For every positive $b=3c$, put $q=2^b$, $v=2^{2c+1}$, and use the S006 choice of polynomial basis in $K=\mathbf F_{2^b}$ and ordered quotient representatives $\alpha$. Let $S=T^2+T$, $T=X+X^{2^c}+X^{2^{2c}}$. For each representative use $s=S(\alpha)$, let $d_s$ be its Frobenius period and $m_s$ its minimal polynomial. In the order of these representatives, feed $v$ consecutive blocks of $d_s$ bits in increasing monomial index $k$. Bits in a block are the coefficients in increasing degree of an arbitrary $u_{s,k}(Y)\in\mathbf F_2[Y]_{<d_s}$. A fixed deterministic finite-alphabet, one-head-per-tape machine emits, in the same block order, the $b$-bit polynomial-basis words for $u_{s,k}(s)$. Its inverse recovers the original bits from every legal word stream. The cost in either direction is $O(qb^{2/5})$ steps and $O(q)$ cells ever visited. A dense per-vector application of the binary coordinate matrices takes $O(qb)$ steps.

For $u_{s,k}=g_k\bmod m_s$, these words are the coefficients, in powers of **the global variable** $X$, of the actual remainder of $f$ on $\alpha+V$. Indeed $S(X)+s$ vanishes there, so reducing $f=\sum_k X^kg_k(S(X))$ gives $\sum_k X^k u_{s,k}(s)$ of degree below $v$. Evaluating at a different conjugate of $s$ generally gives different words. Choosing the root $S(\alpha)$ reconciles this with the stored anchor. If the stabilizer sends $\alpha$ to $\alpha+1$, $S$ still has the same value. There is no substitution $X\mapsto X+\alpha$ in the external monomial records. The physical group order here is indexed by the S006 least quotient representative. An arbitrary permutation of a preexisting file of groups has its own cost.

### Computing and applying both maps

Take the columns of the $b\times d_s$ matrix $A_s$ to be the prescribed ambient words for $1,s,\ldots,s^{d_s-1}$. They are independent by minimality of $m_s$. Row reduction of $[A_s\mid I_b]$ gives $[I_{d_s};0]$ on the left and an invertible row operation matrix on the right. Its first $d_s$ rows form a left inverse $L_s$. Thus $L_s A_s=I_{d_s}$ on **every** independent grouped word and $A_s L_s w=w$ on exactly the word subspace $\mathbf F_2(s)\subset K$. This includes $s=0$ and all proper orbit degrees. The inputs across all representatives and all $k$ are independent, by the graded expansion and CRT in $Y$ in S006. The ambient codomain is precisely this product of subspaces.

Enumerate quotient words using the fixed S006 normal element. Test least orbit representatives by rotations of their first-bit-zero lift, complementing after a rotation when necessary. The orbit length is $d_s$: the map $\alpha+\mathbf F_2\mapsto\alpha^2+\alpha$ is an injective Frobenius-equivariant map from $\mathbf F_{2^c}/\mathbf F_2$ onto the relative trace-value index set. Compute $s$ using this formula since $T(\alpha)=\alpha$. Generate the columns by successive field products, and compute $m_s=\prod_{j<d_s}(Y-s^{2^j})$ if a directory entry is needed. Its coefficients are binary. Each operation here takes a fixed polynomial in $b$ tape steps and cells per queried word, including simple scanned Gaussian elimination and returns. Across at most $2^{c-1}$ words this is $q^{1/3}\operatorname{poly}(b)$ steps. A streaming running offset adds $vd_s$ grouped bits and $vb$ full bits per accepted orbit. No offset lookup is needed at payload speed. The verified count of the S006 orbits gives $bvR=O(q)$ for their number $R$; the input length is exactly $v\sum_s d_s=q$.

For each orbit read successive tiles of $b$ input words as *rows* in increasing $k$. Pad the words to $b$ bits and any final tile to $b$ rows with zeroes. In the forward direction multiply this tile on the right by the zero-extended $A_s^\mathsf{T}$, then write its genuine rows in the same order. In the inverse direction the right multiplier is the zero-extended $L_s^\mathsf{T}$; emit the first $d_s$ bits of each genuine result row. For example the bit at offset $\sum_{s'<s}vd_{s'}+kd_s+j$ contributes the column for $s^j$ to the word at offset $\sum_{s'<s}vb+kb$. Applying $L_s$ recovers that bit as the dot product with its row $j$. Padded rows never become payload.

### A fixed-tape bound for the local binary matrix product

There exist fixed integers $a\ge2$ and $r<a^{12/5}$ and an exact rank-$r$ bilinear identity for multiplying $a\times a$ matrices **over $\mathbf F_2$**. In the pinned September matrix manuscript, the field is arbitrary throughout. Equation (rank-exponent) defines $w=\inf_{a\ge2}\log_a R_a$ using exact rank over that field; the final proof bounds $w<2.372$, not only the arithmetic-operation exponent below it. This is ample slack. Fix one such identity in the program. If an effective construction rather than hardwiring is desired, for successive $a$ enumerate the finite binary triples of linear forms for ranks $r^5<a^{12}$. Check the identity on all pairs of matrix units by bilinearity. For each $a$ the search is finite, and the source proves that this fixed search eventually halts. This is a single absolute cost independent of $b$. Small $b$ may alternatively use dense multiplication. [Rank definition, exact block semantics and the finite-field construction](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Staggered-extraction-for-exact-matrix-multiplication-over-every-field-September-24-2026/build/sections/foundations.tex) · [Final bound on exact rank exponent](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Staggered-extraction-for-exact-matrix-multiplication-over-every-field-September-24-2026/build/sections/parameters.tex)

Pad the tile width to the next power $a^h\le ab$. Apply the fixed identity recursively to square blocks. Its scalar identities remain valid over noncommuting submatrices because every term has its left linear input before its right linear input. At side length $n=a^h$, separate the $a^2$ row-major subblocks by a fixed number of forward scans. Form each required left and right linear combination by scans and XORs. Rewind or park the parent sums, solve one child, and scan that answer into the specified accumulator blocks. Concatenate their rows with fixed repeated scans for row-major output. A constant $a^2,r$ number of banks is permitted since $a,r$ are fixed before receiving the length. If desired, source-to-block routing can instead use a binary counter at cost $O(n^2\log(n+2))$ per node. Rewinding a parent extent on every child costs this same order. Store the unneeded parent arrays as append/pop frames on a fixed number of stack tapes and reuse child working-bank origins. A frame written and later read at the stack end costs at most its length up to its fixed scan factor. The marks therefore bound visited cells by a constant times $b^2+(b/a)^2+\cdots$ and fixed polynomial-sized controls. This is also an upper bound on the union of visited tape intervals across successive tiles because their banks and the emptied stacks reuse the same anchored intervals.

The clock satisfies $T(n)\le rT(n/a)+C n^2\log(n+2)$. With $r<a^{12/5}$, induction absorbing the local term above a fixed cutoff gives $T(n)=O(n^{12/5})$. Per-tile input, output, padding, matrix read and its rewind take $O(b^2\log(b+2))$, included above. There are at most $R(v/b+1)$ tiles. The time over all groups, including uniform setup, is
$$
 O\big(q^{1/3}\operatorname{poly}(b)+Rv b^{7/5}+Rb^{12/5}\big)
     =O(qb^{2/5}),
$$
since $bvR=O(q)$ and $v\ge b$. The two monotone streams occupy $q+O(q)$ cells. The matrices, layouts, counter tapes, recursion stack, and setup workspace use fixed regions of polynomial size in $b$, bounded by an absolute multiple of $q$ including bounded degrees. The parameter-independent search, if used, also has a finite absolute workspace. Reusing all these banks gives the stated bound on cells ever visited through halt. In particular this seam satisfies the charter with $C=0$.

### Scope and finite evidence

The generic batched linear algebra and its asymptotic rank bound are prior work. The application here identifies the two actual word representations, fixes the correct conjugate at the stored anchor and pays for both directions, uniform directory setup and the full ordering. It leaves open the binary radix and index movement preceding the grouped residues, computation of the actual grouped CRT, monomial-to-$B_i$ conversion within each representative, its individual irreducible directory, and precision carries. The integer upper envelope does not change. Root rounding has exactly the existing at-most-fourfold cost at all sufficiently large input lengths.

The [finite check](verify-grouped-words.py) independently compares the output against descending ordinary division by $S(X)+S(\alpha)$ in the ambient field at $b=3,6,9,12,15$. It reconstructs arbitrary independent grouped payloads through all CRT idempotents in $Y$ and back to increasing binary coefficient order. Its physical-order tests include ragged final tiles, proper subfields, wrong conjugates, and words outside the legal image.
