# A full binary CRT on the cubic-extension degrees

Let $b=3c\ge3$, $a=2^c$, $h=a^2$, $q=a^3$, $L=\mathbf F_a$ and $K=\mathbf F_q$. From the contiguous $q$ binary coefficients of $f$ of degree $<q$, the machine emits the ordinary coefficients of $f\bmod u$ for **every** irreducible factor $u$ of $X^q-X$, in the caller's actual FS1 degree/Lyndon order. Its inverse accepts arbitrary such independent residues and returns the ordinary coefficients. Starting or ending at the previously paid S006 prefix adds $O(q\sqrt b)$ steps. The tape count and alphabet are absolute constants.

The fallback using separate published polynomial multipliers is **$O(qb\,4^{\log^* b})$ time with $O(q)$ cells ever visited**. The final section derives this fallback from the binary directory. The [batched construction](binary-transform-and-product.md) gives the sharper complete $o(qb)$ clock. A simpler independent directory calculation gives $O(qb(1+\log b)^4)$ time and $O(q)$ cells ever visited. It reaches the literal individual factors. The requested $O(q\sqrt b\,\mathrm{polylog}\,b)$ time is a stricter target. The argument applies at all positive multiples of three. The integer caller can round its chosen root degree up to such a multiple at a factor of at most four in length as proved in S006. It does not identify the degree-$b$ directory with the degree-$(b+1)$ directory.

### A plane instead of the remaining binary additive layers

Set $T(X)=X^h+X^a+X$. This relative trace is $L$-linear, has $T(t)=t$ on $L$, and its kernel has $L$-dimension two. Choose an $L$-basis $v,w$ of the kernel. The image of $z\mapsto z^a+z$ is precisely this kernel: it is contained in it, and its kernel is $L$. The images of an ambient binary basis span it over $\mathbf F_2$, hence contain two $L$-independent members. The test $v w^a+w v^a\ne0$ finds such a pair. All choices and short field operations are generated once in $\operatorname{poly}(b)$ time except the previously charged root field/representative setup.

The [relative-trace radix](relative-trace-radix.md) supplies $f=\sum_{j<a}D_jT^j$, $\deg D_j<h$. The [packed child CRT](packed-chirp.md) and [actual two-fibre restriction](fibre-join.md) give $A_t(X)=\sum_{k<h}X^kp_k(t)$ at each selected $t=\alpha$ or $\alpha+1$. It is the literal remainder $f\bmod (T+t)$ over $K$. Its values are required on
$$ \mathcal P_t=t+Lv+Lw. $$
The two planes at a representative partition the stored bundle. The S006 quotient orbits are the orbits of $L/\mathbf F_2$ under Frobenius. They give $R$ representative pairs, with $2R a^2 b=O(q)$, including proper subfields and the twisted pairs. Every element of $K$ is Frobenius-conjugate to a selected point: take a power of Frobenius carrying its trace class to the selected quotient class; the trace is then either $\alpha$ or $\alpha+1$. The $2R$ planes give only $O(q/b)$ actual $K$-words. This count is fundamental to the bit budget.

For $A=A_t$, group its coefficients as $P(U,W)=\sum_{i,j<a} A_{i+aj}U^iW^j$. Thus $A(X)=P(X,X^a)$. At $x=t+v y+w z$ for $y,z\in L$ the two arguments are
$$\binom{x}{x^a}=\binom{t}{t^a}+
 M\binom{y}{z},\qquad M=\begin{pmatrix}v&w\\v^a&w^a\end{pmatrix}.$$
The determinant above is nonzero because $v/w\notin L$. Apply this affine substitution to the bivariate polynomial. Reduce the result modulo $Y^a-Y$ and $Z^a-Z$ in the two separate exponents. The rectangular remainder $G$ has degree below $a$ in each variable and the correct value at every point of $L^2$. Throughout substitution the total degree is at most $2a-2$. For an exponent $e\ge a$ a single fold to $e-(a-1)$ suffices. Interpolation on a Cartesian grid of distinct field points is a bijection on these rectangles.

Conversely interpolate the received grid to $G$ of bidegree $<a$ in each variable, substitute the **inverse affine** change, then evaluate the resulting $Q(U,W)$ at $(X,X^a)$. This ordinary polynomial has degree at most $a(2a-2)=2h-2a$. In its split $C+X^h D$ the high half has degree at most $h-2a$. The ordinary remainder modulo $T+t$ is
$$ C+(X^a+X+t)D, $$
already of degree below $h$. This is the unique degree-$<h$ polynomial with the given values on the $h$ distinct points of $\mathcal P_t$. The assertion holds even for arbitrary $K$-valued plane data in isolation. On legal binary factor inputs the selected planes automatically satisfy the Frobenius relations required by the [inverse fibre join](fibre-join.md).

### Pay for the affine chart and both grid axes

The affine substitution factors into two single-variable translations, two off-diagonal shears and one diagonal scaling by Gaussian elimination of $M$; $v\ne0$ is an available pivot. Reverse their order, invert the diagonal and invert the elementary substitutions for the inverse chart. A translation or shear in the first coordinate has the form $U\mapsto U+\lambda W^e$ with $e=0$ or $1$. Pad both exponent ranges to $N=2a$. An input at $(j,k)$ contributes $\lambda^{j-i}$ times itself to $(i,k+e(j-i))$ for each bitwise subset $i$ of $j$. For $\lambda\ne0$, scale row $j$ by $\lambda^j$, rotate its columns by $ej$, apply the [wide binary upper Pascal transform](striped-pascal.md) across rows, rotate output row $i$ back by $ei$, and scale by $\lambda^{-i}$. A zero shear is the identity. The rotation is modulo $N$ and no genuine output wraps since the total degree stays at most $2a-2$. Interchange the coordinates for the second variable. The degree bound also covers all intermediate elementary substitutions. All field-word planes per row are independent binary lanes of Pascal, in width $Nb$, polynomial in $N$. Its cost on one plane of $V=a^2b$ bits is $O_\eta(Vc/(1+\log(c+2))^\eta)$.

The diagonal scaling contributes one field product per row/column scale per entry. Each cell operation on $b$-bit words takes $O(b(1+\log b)^2)$ tape steps. Powers for a column factor live on a bank rewound once per row; a row factor is available on a reusable word tape. Row rotation spools its two intervals. Swapping the two $O(c)$-bit index fields uses the fixed-tape bit-position permutation at $\rho=3/4$, including the $O(Vc^{3/4}(1+\log c))$ cost and linear visited space. Bounded padding, the axis folds and the high-half fold take a bounded number of stream passes plus short field arithmetic. To evaluate $Q(X,X^a)$ group terms $U^iW^j$ by blocks $X^{aj+i}$: split $i$ at $a$, shift the upper strip up by one $j$ and xor, then emit in $(j,i\bmod a)$ order. The inverse initial packing is its simple rectangular read on the original monomial coefficients.

Evaluate or interpolate $G$ on each axis using the length-$a$ chirp from [the child construction](packed-chirp.md). The primitive generator belongs to $L$; the coefficients now occupy $b$ bits in $K$. Use stride $2b-1$ for the ordinary binary polynomial product. The separate zero point, the last nonzero cyclic coefficient and the inverse factor are handled exactly as in the child formula. Process $a$ contiguous row packets, transpose the two grid indices on $b$-bit words, process $a$ further packets and restore the declared order of the $y,z$ axes. Work banks and kernel heads return to a common origin per packet. There are at most a constant times $2Ra$ such packets in either direction, of length $O(ab)$, for $O(q)$ aggregate length. The two signs use two prescribed kernels. Other than these products the grid computation takes $O_\eta(qb/(1+\log(b+2))^\eta)$ steps.

### Match exactly the factor directory

Enumerate the $L$-points in the same order as the grid: the $a-1$ powers of a primitive element followed by zero. Precompute $vy,wz$ tables in that order, rewind the latter once per outer row, and append a serial index with each $K$ point. All other numerical labels below have $O(b)$ bits. The $O(q/b)$ selected roots have total record width $O(q)$, including these labels.

At a selected point $x$ of period $d\mid b$, generate $x,x^2,\ldots,x^{2^{d-1}}$ and the conjugates of its received value $y=f(x)$. Their monic factor $u=\prod_j(Y-x^{2^j})$ has binary coefficients. Recover the unique binary polynomial $r$ of degree $<d$ by interpolation on these conjugates. For a concrete subquadratic-in-volume implementation, compute $u$ by a product tree. Evaluate $u'(x)$ by short Horner and invert it by exponentiation, then set $w_0=y/u'(x)$. The conjugates of $w_0$ are the Lagrange weights. Combine leaves $(Y-x^{2^j},w_0^{2^j})$ according to
$$ (P_L,G_L),(P_R,G_R)\longmapsto (P_LP_R,\ G_LP_R+P_LG_R). $$
The second root polynomial is $r$. It takes the prescribed values and Frobenius fixes its coefficients. This derivation includes $d=1$ and zero. The inverse uses the supplied polynomial $r$ and Horner at each match. It never needs a change between the field polynomials picked by FS1 and the plane construction.

A product of length-$l$ polynomials over $K$, $l\le b$, can be made on fixed tapes by the bivariate Kronecker encoding of FS1, short integer multiplication and monic reductions in $K$. It costs $O(lb(1+\log b)^2)$ bit steps. A product tree pays this aggregated width on each of $O(1+\log b)$ levels. Computing conjugates, the single field inversion and Horner take $O(b^2(1+\log b)^2)$ per point. Two adjacent tree layers and a polynomial-size local workspace can be reused. All point labels and residues thus cost $O(qb(1+\log b)^3)$ in total. In particular the cruder fourth-power bill stated above also covers local counters and all returns.

Generate actual FS1 factors in the specified order with their serial numbers, following its [factor compiler](../2026-11-26-S005/route-a/binary-factor-stream-compiler.md). Its ordinary Lyndon word and product-tree bounds fit $O(qb(1+\log b)^4)$. Sort the candidate residues and the FS1 inventory by padded binary polynomial keys in $O(qb)$ time by $O(b)$ stable bit partitions on $O(q)$ records in bits. Merge identical factors and choose one candidate for each FS1 record. Reorder by the saved degree/Lyndon serial number, then emit just the true $d$ residual bits. The covering argument ensures every factor has a candidate and binary input ensures duplicates agree. In reverse, join the given FS1 remainders to **all** selected root records of the same factor, evaluate by Horner, and sort back by the grid serial. This requires sequential rereading of at most one short residue per emitted match. Tagged stream lengths stay $O(q)$. Reverse the physical and algebraic operations to recover $f$; uniqueness of ordinary binary CRT proves the result on independent inputs.

### Total resource proof and review status

For a plane of size $a^2$, coefficient arrays have at most $(2a)^2$ field words. Process the selected planes sequentially, returning each head to a local origin before the next. Parent streams are finite increasing serial records, with separate resident heads for input and output. The global inventories, duplicate tags, FS1 job and answer streams all have $O(q)$ bits. The smaller-CRT and grid calls have aggregate lengths $O(q)$ and maximum length $O(ab)$; even a product routine using scratch up to its time uses $o(q)$ distinct cells per packet and reuses that interval. Local field/interpolation scratch is polynomial in $b$ and reused. Hence the union of visited tape/address pairs through halt is $O(q)$, including return, permutation, setup and output. All choices follow the pinned suppliers and finite recipes above, with $\eta=1/4$ a single fixed machine.

The unrestricted full-root price through the elementary directory is $O(qb(1+\log b)^4)$, since $4^{\log^* b}=O(1+\log b)$. The conventional binary subproduct/remainder tree with a quasi-linear binary polynomial multiplication routine has the standard upper invoice $O(M_2(q)\log q)$ under superlinear scaling. That comparison concerns the specified standard construction; it is no lower bound on competing CRT algorithms. Stronger directory and recurrence claims require their own proofs.

### Sharper directory and strongest unconditional clock

A second [literal directory algorithm](point-factor-directory.md) replaces the per-root product tree and Lagrange interpolation above. For $K=\mathbf F_2[Z]/F_b$ take the constant coefficient $\Lambda$ in the power basis. Since $\Lambda(1)=1$, its restriction does not vanish on any proper subfield. Compute $2b$ moments $\Lambda(x^j)$ and $b$ moments $\Lambda(yx^j)$ by baby-step/giant-step power projection. The rectangular binary product has dimensions $O(\sqrt b)\times b$ by $b\times O(\sqrt b)$, giving $O(b^{17/10}+b^{3/2}\mathrm{polylog}\,b)$ steps per selected root. A cutoff Euclidean cofactor gives its actual monic minimal polynomial, and a second moment numerator recovers the prescribed power-basis remainder. The factor $Y$, vanishing Euclidean remainder, short orbits, arbitrary legal inverse records and exact FS1 enumeration are included in the cited proof. The inverse uses blocked modular composition. The reduced inventory is routed with the *short-record* form of Tal's corollary on $\Theta(b)$-bit records. Thus all nonproduct work in the complete forward or inverse CRT costs
$$O_\eta\!\left(qb/(1+\log_2\log_2(b+3))^\eta\right),\qquad 0<\eta<1/2\quad\text{fixed rational}.$$

The only larger cost lies in the two families of fixed binary chirp products, in the smaller CRT and the plane axes respectively. Their total ordinary operand length per direction is $O(q)$, each of length $O(2^{b/3}b)$. Substituting the published unconditional binary polynomial multiplier gives the complete literal FS1 forward and inverse clock
$$\boxed{O(qb\,4^{\log^* b})\text{ time},\qquad O(q)\text{ cells ever visited}.}$$
The iterated logarithm of $O(2^{b/3}b)$ differs from $\log^* b$ by a bounded additive constant. For a replacement supplier put $M_0(D)$ for its full time, including kernel preparation unless shared setup is separately charged. The exact residual is $\lceil h/c\rceil M_0((2c-1)(a-1))+4Ra M_0((2b-1)(a-1))$ per direction, up to short scans, using the corresponding prescribed operand in each term. Each supplier must fit the common $O(q)$ visited bound. A normalized $o(b)$ clock on these calls would yield an $o(qb)$ complete binary transform here. Reaching the campaign's square-root clock would also require a stronger bound for the other operations, including directory routing.
