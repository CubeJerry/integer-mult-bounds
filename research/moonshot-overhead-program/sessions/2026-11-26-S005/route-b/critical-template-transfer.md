# A complete polynomial-log-log transfer from two local template contracts

Status: **written conditional theorem; independent review requested**.
Neither local family is constructed here. This proves a source-compatible
implication with an explicit finite polynomial exponent and separates
finite child mass, physical delivery, precision and outer-caller obligations.
It is not a new unconditional bound or an endpoint theorem.

Put L=1+log2(n), x=log2(L). The conclusion will be

    T(n)=O(n sqrt(L) (x+2)^C),    visited cells=O(n).

The machine is one fixed exact deterministic finite-alphabet multitape
full-product machine. Weak ETH is not a premise.

## 2. CT-I: bounded normalized exact child mass

Fix absolute constants K>=1, B_I>=1, c_s>=4 and lambda<1. A single fixed
constructor/dispatcher supplies an exact one-level interchange reduction
for all sufficiently large permitted m. It accepts every independent input
row and occupied spectator value in the source layout. Binary representation,
or a fixed native radix with one constant-volume outer conversion, is allowed.

For current width e, actual incoming encoded volume V, and the guard
e>=m^(K+1), its explicit child width f must satisfy

    e/(c_s m)<=f<=e/m,       f<e.                        (CT1)

All spatial calls have width f. Their **actual incoming volumes**, including
padding, finite repair and reserve calls, satisfy

    (sum_h V_h/V) sqrt(f/e)<=B_I.                       (CT2)

CT2 is a finite count of child volume, independent of completed recursive
runtime. If a source recipe uses f=floor((e-ell)/(m+2a)), its actual guards
must prove CT1-CT2 for that f. Unused axes are not transformed input width.

All remaining physical work costs C V m^K: scalar arithmetic, field
packing, data extraction, scans, address/control computations, description
access, head returns, output and cleanup. Separately allowed template
construction costs 2^(m^K) poly(log(V+2)) work with polynomial(m,log V)
space; the returned plan and actual grouped-call count are polynomial(m).
Thus bounded finite search is allowed but not called polynomial-time.

Local payload occupies C V cells with C absolute. A child either has
V_h<=lambda V and parks at most C V payload, or has V_h=V and begins
after clearing all parent payload, transferring ownership of marked
regions. Full-volume descents retain only polynomial(m,log V) descriptors.
All siblings reuse the same regions. A fixed exact routine handles the
bounded base range in linear volume. These are visited-cell contracts.

The currently proved AQ mass bound grows with m and does not supply CT2.
This does not lower-bound every implementation of interchange.

## 3. CT-W: bounded Fourier mass and paid joint delivery

For the same permitted arities, a fixed constructor supplies an exact
normalized rational Walsh template on W_m independent rows and A_m f
active bits, for EVERY positive even f>=m^(K+1), with root numerical
width k>=A_m f and all complete spectator ranges needed by the explicit
batch divisors, satisfying

    2m<=A_m<=c_s m,
    2<=W_m<=m^K,       W_m a power of two,
    sigma_m/W_m<=B_W sqrt(A_m).                         (CT3)

Every one of its sigma_m children acts on exactly V/W_m incoming bits,
transforming an f-bit slot with complete spectators. Completed children
are unitary. All local coefficient heights, logarithmic intermediate norm
gains, instruction counts and on-demand arithmetic degrees are at most
m^K. Construction obeys the bound above. The identity holds on arbitrary
independent rows, not just a symmetric or invariant sector.

In a concrete physical schedule, separate O(1) whole-active-width exact
swaps. All remaining COMPLETED exact-interchanger calls have width at
most f. For their actual incoming volume divided by V require

    C_(m,k)<=C sqrt(m) (1+log2(m+2))^q
                         (1+log2(k+2))^b_X.            (CT4)

Here q>=0 and b_X>=0 are absolute. This counts all actual common-layout,
query, affine, XOR and reverse-delivery calls AFTER compilation. The
retained guarded XOR implementation, when used, contributes to b_X;
its passes are not omitted or counted a second time. Let b_S be the
root-logarithmic exponent for remaining scalar/arithmetic/scan work
V m^K (1+log(k+2))^b_S. Set b=max(b_X,b_S), the TOTAL exponent used
below. For the retained XOR and short-arithmetic compiler one may
take b>=1. Separately allowed preparation is still charged.

An actual joint executor may alternatively prove the separated local bill

    C V [m^K+(1+log(m+2))^q(1+I(a))]
          (1+log(k+2))^b + permitted preparation,       (CT5)

where a is the active numerical width and I denotes the monotone
upper envelope CT8 for the **completed CT-I** exact interchanger.
CT4 implies CT5 since f<=a/(2m), sqrt(m) sqrt(f+1)=O(sqrt(a+1)),
and log(f+2)<=log(a+2). This comparison is between upper envelopes,
not a lower bound on actual execution time. CT5 allows a
shared representation or cancellations not represented by separate swaps;
all identity, order, space and numerical bounds are still required.

Local payload uses an absolute number of full arrays. Numerical children
shrink by at least one half, finite repair children by a fixed factor,
and scalar/descriptor scratch is polynomial(m,k). Parked arrays, head
returns, erasure and borrowed-coordinate restoration are charged.

CT3-CT4 are open. The present Fourier local mass is subpower in m, not
bounded. Its known sharper physical upper invoice is
C_m<=C m^(3/2)R_m^2. Neither fact is silently strengthened here.

## 4. Exact fixed arities and explicit exponents

Choose one integer R after all fixed degrees so delta=1/R satisfies
delta K<=1/16 and delta(K+2)<1. These inequalities ensure the stated
polynomial shape guards, rather than assuming unspecified guards pass. At width e take a permitted m within
constant factors of e^delta. For an odd family, take m=2^j-1 with 2^j
the largest power of two not exceeding the integer R-th root of e.
The family must contain this bounded-ratio sequence at every sufficiently
large width, or supply a similarly explicit selector. In particular the
selected numerical f, the largest even integer <=a/A_m, satisfies
f>=a/(2c_s m)>=m^(K+1) eventually by delta(K+2)<1. The exact guard
e>=m^(K+1) likewise holds eventually. Thus both contracts actually apply
on every sufficiently large selected shape; they cannot be vacuous. No real root oracle or length advice is used.

CT1 and fixed threshold enlargement give, for the actual integer width,

    log2(f+2)<=rho log2(e+2),    rho=1-delta/4<1.        (CT6)

Choose

    p_I=ceil(log(4 B_I)/(-log rho)).                    (CT7)

Divide the actual exact recurrence by sqrt(e). CT2 bounds the child
multiplier by B_I; the ordinary normalized toll is O(e^(delta K-1/2)).
Complete width-e fields have V>=2^(c e) for fixed c>0, so the separated
constructor has o(V) cost uniformly over larger V and is absorbed in the
same local invoice. For fixed polynomial degree, poly(log V)/V is bounded
by its exponentially small tail once V>=2^(c e).

Induction with (log2(e+2))^p_I gives

    I(e)<=C sqrt(e+1) (log2(e+2))^p_I.                 (CT8)

The child spends at most B_I rho^p_I<=1/4 of that potential; the local
toll and the fixed base fit the remaining margin after choosing C. All
constants are uniform. The geometric parked-volume sum and polynomial
descriptors give one absolute O(V) visited-cell bound. Full-volume reserve
descents add no parked payload and siblings reuse marked regions.

For the numerical template choose m at the same fixed-power scale and
f as the largest even integer <=a/A_m. The fewer than 2A_m remaining
bits use direct butterflies, included in m^K. CT6 again holds above
a fixed base. If T_k(a) is time per incoming numerical bit, put
U_k(a)=T_k(a)/(sqrt(a)(1+log(k+2))^b). CT3-CT5 imply

    U_k(a)<=C a^(delta K-1/2)
              +C (log2(a+2))^(p_I+q)+B_W U_k(f).      (CT9)

Here the child factor sqrt(f/a)<=1/sqrt(A_m) is already included.
Whole-active-width boundary swaps are retained in the second term.
Take

    p_W=max(p_I+q+1, ceil(log(4 B_W)/(-log rho))).       (CT10)

Induction pays the child with at most one quarter of the potential and
both positive tolls with the remaining margin, obtaining

    T_k(a)<=C sqrt(a+1)(log2(a+2))^p_W
                                  (1+log(k+2))^b.     (CT11)

This retains the inhomogeneous routing work. It does not infer endpoint
time from a bounded homogeneous product.

## 5. Batching, preparation, precision and visited cells

Along each actual numerical path f_i<=a_i/A_(m_i) and A_m>=m, hence

    sum_i log m_i<=log a_0,
    product_i W_(m_i)<=a_0^K.                           (CT12)

All W_m are powers of two. The source's a_0<=c k/log(k+2) leaves at least
2^(k/2) spectator rows eventually. This exact power of two is divisible
by the entire product in CT12 after one fixed guard. There is no repeated
spectator padding. Polynomial-m branching implies polynomial(k) actual
nodes and numerical array operations.

Reducing delta once more against all preparation degrees makes even
reconstruction at every node cost at most 2^(k^(1/8)) poly(k). This is
below the smallest numerical volume 2^(k/2)/poly(k). Only compact plans
and polynomial(k) scratch are retained. Exact-interchanger preparation
inside numerical nodes is already paid in CT8.

A path's logarithmic numerical norm/height gain is at most
O(k^(1/16)log k)=o(k). Completed numerical children are unitary and exact
address calls are isometries. The source common-ancestor error propagation
argument, the polynomial number of fault sites and the sqrt(N) array
factor imply that one fixed precision p=C0 k suffices for any fixed
input-norm and output-error allowances of the integer caller. On-demand
coefficient preparation, exact temporaries and rounding are included.
The integer exponent of the majorant is computed in polynomial(k) work;
its eventual bound is tested, with correct fallback on failure.

An absolute number of full arrays at each node and strict child-volume
contraction give a geometric parked-payload sum. Scalar/descriptor data
are polynomial(k), absorbed by Nk. Together with CT-I, fixed marked work
regions and sibling/phase reuse give one O(Nk) bound on **all cells ever
visited**. There is no unpriced parameter-dependent chunk compression.

## 6. Complete integer implication

The actual pinned source uses tested shapes Nk=Theta(n), k=Theta(log n).
Its prime-gap theorem supplies an absolute eventual cutoff; the algorithm
only executes finite shape/coprimality tests. The new parameters do not
alter the source prime intervals.

Inserting CT8 into the source controlled-block compiler yields
C Nk sqrt(k)(1+log(k+2))^(p_I+3) per layer, including guarded XOR and
position changes. CRT has O(log k) depths. CT11 supplies selected-bit
Walsh layers with their initial/final position permutations. Synchronized
short transforms have O(log k) layers. Their phase tables cost poly(k)
setup and full diagonal streams cost O(Nk log k).

The complete source ledger therefore is

    C Nk sqrt(k)(1+log(k+2))^C_*+2^(k^(1/8))poly(k),
    C_*=max(p_I+4, p_W+b+1).                            (CT13)

Using a<=k is conservative; the sharper a=O(k/log k) could save a half
power in the second term, but is unnecessary. Coordinate products, inverse
CRT, final exact rounding and carry propagation fit CT13. The fixed
numerical error margin makes every convolution coefficient round to its
exact integer value. Thus the algorithm writes the exact full product.

**Theorem CT.** CT-I and CT-W imply one fixed exact deterministic
finite-alphabet multitape full-product machine with

    T(n)=O(n sqrt(1+log2 n)(log2(1+log2 n)+2)^C_*),
    all cells ever visited=O(n).                        (CT14)

The exponent C_* is the finite explicit expression CT7, CT10, CT13.
For the finitely many small/rejected inputs use a fixed linear-space
schoolbook routine. All tests eventually succeed under the contracts,
so these inputs affect one absolute constant only. This completes the
conditional implication, with no candidate scheduler or length advice.

Consequently G(x)<=C_*log2(x+2)+O(1) and
delta(n)<=[C_*log2(x+2)+O(1)]/x. There is no instantiated value of C_*
for the present construction because CT2-CT4 remain open. This is not a
new measured saving against the published unspecified o(1).

## 7. Decisive open questions

1. Supply an unbounded exact family with **total** normalized child mass
   bounded independently of m, including reserves and padding. A merely
   polylogarithmic-in-m mass is not CT2.
2. Supply both bounded numerical child mass and an actual joint executor
   satisfying CT4 or CT5. Better delivery alone does not bound the current
   Fourier mass; a fast symmetric-sector action is not full-rate execution.
3. Preserve compact generation, all-input correctness, constant payload
   arrays and numerical norm/height bounds in the same family.
4. An endpoint theorem must additionally replace the source outer
   logarithmic invoice and control the numerical inhomogeneous toll.
   Ideal endpoint suppliers inserted in the unchanged CT13 ledger do not
   certify H=O(1). This is not a lower bound on the actual caller.
