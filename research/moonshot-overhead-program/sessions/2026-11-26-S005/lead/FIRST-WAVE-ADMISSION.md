### Exact CT meaning

CT-I requires a finite exact one-level reduction with bounded normalized
**actual child-volume mass**, polynomial ordinary work, paid uniform
construction, and fixed visited-space allocation. CT-W requires a finite
full-rate Walsh identity with bounded normalized recursive mass, a charged
joint physical delivery schedule, coefficient/propagation bounds, and an
absolute payload coefficient. Neither premise states the completed target
runtime. The actual largest-even child widths are explicitly covered by
the family quantifiers; actual post-XOR call volumes and root logarithms
are counted once.

The implication includes exact interchange closure, numerical recurrence,
whole-path occupied batching, O(k) precision, the actual source integer
caller, setup, finite exceptional inputs, and one O(n) visited-cell cap.
The result is conditional on **all** these local contracts. It is not an
unconditional algorithm, a numerical kappa improvement, or the endpoint.

## Continuation and pivot gate

The square-product proposal is stopped in its stated model. Direct orbit
execution is retained for one constructive wave because RO changes the
representation and gives a genuine local fixed-tape compiler. Its two
binding gaps are the entry/exit converter and the full Fourier child.
RG prevents quietly replacing the latter by a vanishing-relative-rank
repair; it does not price collective incidence execution.

Discovery owns actual flag reordering and converter costs; transfer owns
the all-input subspace/quotient Fourier identity and its normalization.
After a second wave without a changed binding bill, freeze this route
unless a specific new supplier demonstrably changes the prediction.
The lead also tests a distinct label-family mechanism and explicitly
compares it with existing Fourier/Hamming and incidence constructions
before claiming progress. Audit continues on the smallest useful formal
bridge, preserving already checked sources.
