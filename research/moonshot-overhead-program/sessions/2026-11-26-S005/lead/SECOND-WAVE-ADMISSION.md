## What changed and what did not

Ordinary row-space coordinates make controlled linear address maps
local, but the retained one-row Fourier child crosses a constant-rank
fraction of those blocks. SF gives the exact cross-block factorization;
its quotient still has almost the entire long width when f is much larger
than the arity. FC's controlled chart conversion recreates the paid
long-slot interchange. Two substantive waves therefore did not improve
the binding complete invoice. The ordinary orbit route is frozen at
its positive local compiler and exact open converter contract.

BE is a genuine representation change. It moves the actual finite
quadratic repair algebra into a common full-rate Bell/orbit layout,
including nonlinear rational tables of attained parity keys. It avoids
the particular ordinary-orbit obstruction. Its endpoints, however, each
require a full-volume half-width Walsh call. SU rules out saving that
factor merely by choosing a quarter-width encoder in the same class.
BO then checks the actual integer caller: standard encoded products and
outer phases do not erase the remaining invoice. Bell is retained as
a useful local theorem, with its presently priced implementation stopped.

The next discovery wave changes the *outer exact algebra*: polynomial
CRT over Z/(2^H), compressed into all binary-irreducible factors whose
degrees divide a common b. That route is under construction and is NOT
admitted in this checkpoint. Its first gates are all-input recovery,
absolute residue volume, small-product work, uniform factor generation,
and forward/inverse transform costs without circular large products.
