# Independent routes and decision gates

## A. Quantitative paper extraction
Mechanism: optimize j against effective candidate/scheduler constants, not merely choose epsilon(n). Deciding question: can the paper's finite-template construction/search and valid input thresholds be bounded by a computable and useful function of j? Exit when the precise D_j,N_j dominate all possible envelope improvement.

## B. Protected first-principles path
Mechanism: permit constant-rate predictor defects, then repair recursively as another full-rate computation. This attacks rare-exception overhead rather than schedule constants. First falsifier: if a constant fraction of defects requires global permutation/sort/masking that costs at least the initial spectral operation, the proposed saving is transferred. Need an explicit recurrence and a lawful layout. Status open, not proven.

## C. Adversarial structural test
Mechanism: understand the approximation degree required by the Fourier repair scheme, and seek a specific scoped lower bound. Key distinction: lower bounding one predictor representation does not lower bound multiplication. Status open.

## Pivot rule
After two unproductive substantive waves, reassess the architecture, not just adjust coefficients. Never convert B into A merely because A is more straightforward.
