## Dependency direction and scope

Native algebra and owned semantics support the compiled supplier. Physical native and scalar implementations, compiler chronology, backing, and precision must compose in one machine model with the retained complete costs. Analytic/outer recovery consumes those suppliers and adds its own uniform parameter, prime, source-access, setup and exact-recovery obligations. Thus scoped progress can be made separately, but a complete unconditional theorem requires their compatible composition, not five independent finite tests.

Open PRs and local packages are research claims pending independent review unless their exact scope has separately been validated. A merge is archival evidence, not automatic mathematical admission. A counterexample to an overstrong local interface need not refute the selected conditional theorem.
