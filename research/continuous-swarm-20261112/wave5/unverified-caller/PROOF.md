## 2. Complete-array semantics and representation

All fields are Gaussian arrays on the same complete address factor. The raw
unit has A=153 coordinate blocks; each has f positive binary lanes. Additional
external spectator factors and a row multiplicity are preserved by every
operation. This is a mathematical, exact-array ABI, not a claim that its
physical encoding or headers have been supplied. A descriptor selecting some
axes acts on those axes and fixes the entire complement. It never projects
spectators into separate owners or enumerates coefficient payloads.

Let P_G send the basis address x to Gx, and X_b send x to x xor b.
For a retained monomial recipe (G,b,Q,alpha,p), its one-lane action is

`|x> -> i^(p + sum_i alpha_i*x_i + 2*sum_(i<j) Q_ij*x_i*x_j) |Gx xor b>`.

`MONOMIAL` applies its f-fold tensor product on its listed nine central axes.
Thus its constant phase is i^(f*p), with its full quadratic and linear phase
on every lane. `inverse=true` means the inverse of this complete specified
map: y goes to x=G^-1(y xor b), with the negative phase evaluated at x.
This is an explicit finite matrix/phase descriptor, not an opaque SERVICE.

`AFFINE` specifies its rows and offset on its ordered support; its inverse
has the same precise meaning. `AXIS_PERMUTATION` specifies a complete
153-entry column order: input axis j goes to `order[j]`. `TRANSLATION`
complements the listed central bits in every lane. All three preserve the
full external complement and have zero additional scalar phase.

`C` denotes a **completed** positive job on its first k canonical axes,
including return on the same owner before the next descriptor. If
`C_v=(1+i)I/2+(1-i)X_v/2` on one lane, this is the product of the k positive
coordinate C factors on every lane, with the full complement retained.
Providing this mathematical operator is not providing a constant-time
callback, a physical implementation, or a new all-columns theorem.

`ADD` means destination := destination + (a/b)*source with distinct owners;
`SCALE` multiplies its current field by a/b. These scalars act **outside**
the address tensor power. `GAUSSIAN_INTEGER` applies its displayed 2 by 2
integer matrix to each real/imaginary coefficient. `SWAP` exchanges the two
complete current values and retains both bank identities. `INIT` creates a
zero current private field under the retained owned-initialization contract;
`CLEAR` retires the indicated current private lifetime under the retained
owned-erasure contract. None of these semantic declarations prices an
unimplemented physical operation at zero.

The `chart_in`/`chart_out` fields specify contacts, not extra executed Fourier
operations. `['native',a]` denotes the source's exact canonical frame T_a.
`['child-input',ci,ni]` and its output mate are the canonical child contact
between the displayed right and left maps for this one cut. Caller contacts
use the common address basis. Root/helper target contacts identify the
canonical positive input/output job. The intermediate main-entry/main-exit
contacts are defined by the displayed neighboring maps. Continuity of these
labels is checked in addition to the operator identities below.

## 3. Exact native body and distinct helper boundaries

For each actual MOVE a->b on slot r, the accepted recipe is

`T_b T_a^-1 = L_(a,b) C_k R_(a,b)`,

where k=|dim(b)-dim(a)|>0. Chronological execution therefore emits R, completed
C_k on that same field, then L. Each of the three records retains the exact
caller index ci, native index ni, raw MOVE, physical-bank-qualified lifetime,
start frame a and end frame b. No cut is pooled with another occurrence of
the same rank or frame pair. Ordinary ADD/SCALE operands and coefficients are
copied from their actual source event. The single COPY becomes ADD with
coefficient one into its actually initialized, still untouched zero target.
Every INIT and CLEAR remains at its source position.

For a helper slot h, the accepted *whole native endpoint* has a separate
factorization `H_h=T_final(h) T_initial(h)^-1=L_h C_k R_h`.
The stream applies **R_h^-1 before the entire native invocation** and
**L_h^-1 after that completed invocation**. Therefore its net helper map is
C_k. These two factors generally differ; neither is replaced by the inverse
of the other and neither is moved inside an individual native MOVE.
Helpers are independent productive input/output fields, not initialized
scratch or aliases of main fields.

The selected exact scalar route multiplies all 1,480 actual public inputs by
1125 before each native body, and divides all completed public outputs by
1125 afterwards. The boundary monomials commute with these real scalars.
The stream emits both conversions on each actual mapped owner: exactly
2*18*1480=53,280. They are ordinary work and create no new paid position.
The common-grid and odd-divisibility proof is inherited, not inferred from
this census.

## 4. Concrete J boundaries, including the full absolute correction

Write Q=C_q on the nine central axes, and F=C_all on those axes. Let their
f-fold tensor versions have the same letters. For each actual q, the accepted
main-boundary catalogue provides the ordinary maps

`A_q = T_initial(X) Q^-1`,
`B_q = F Q^-1 T_final(Y)^-1`.

The X final frame is exactly F and the Y input frame is identity. Apply A_q
to the incoming X of each plane and B_q to its outgoing Y. Conjugating the
source's scalar-coordinate endpoint yields, with D_native=F Q^-1,

* first A plane: D_native(x, y+Q^-1 x);
* second A plane: D_native(u-Qv, v);
* B plane: D_native(x-Qy, (Q^-1 x+y)/2).

For example the incoming X scalar coordinate is Q^-1 x while Y's is y;
the native coordinate addition y:=y+x becomes the displayed Q^-1 contact.
For the negative X addition, F(Q^-1 x-y)=D_native(x-Qy). These identities
use the actual source coefficients 1, -1 and 1/2; scalars remain outside
the tensor power. The hosted second plane has the same boundary frames as
the first plane, but its two physical owners are different.

For stage s, take the source's orthonormal central basis (q,b_1,...,b_8).
Let R swap b_i with that stage's eight fresh axes, fix q, and fix every
external spectator. Its ordered support is the nine central axes followed
by the eight fresh axes. `stage_route` emits its complete 17 by 17 binary
matrix, and checks R^2=I and Rq=q.

There is a uniquely determined translation delta on this support such that

`X_delta R D_native R = D_s`,

where D_s is the positive job on the eight fresh stage axes. Here is an
exact all-columns proof, not a numerical sample. On Fourier character z,
the exponent of `R D_native R D_s^-1` is

`sum_(i=0..8) parity((R e_i).z) - parity(q.z)
 - sum_(j=9..16) z_j (mod 4)`.

For any vector v,
`parity(v.z)=sum_i v_i z_i - 2 sum_(i<j) v_i v_j z_i z_j (mod 4)`.
All higher terms vanish modulo four. Substituting the orthogonal basis
cancels every quadratic coefficient and leaves even linear coefficients;
the finite checker constructs the *whole* polynomial for every actual q
and verifies this cancellation. Define delta_i to be half that linear
coefficient modulo two. The residual is X_delta, its own inverse. No scalar
phase is missing: the zero character has exponent zero, and each C and
linear permutation fixes the uniform vector. Tensoring this exact one-lane
identity proves the claim for every positive f.

For the first actual q=11, the computed 17-bit delta is 3595. The complete
104-q family is bound by `GATE-RESULT.json`; all 104 corrections are nonzero.
The same 17-axis identity relocates to each of the 18 fresh stage blocks,
fixing all 136 outside axes. This supplies all main-field stage boundaries.

The stream executes R on each incoming main field, then the indicated A_q,
the native body, the indicated B_q, then X_delta R on every outgoing main
field. The displayed endpoint is therefore exactly the caller's D_s
three-plane contract. This is a deterministic placement of the required
ordinary contact translations. It is algebraically equivalent to the
canonical review's auxiliary-frame contact proof; it does not claim that a
previous physical emitter emitted these same internal boundary bytes.
In particular, no auxiliary Gamma Fourier frame is executed for free.

## 5. Actual QUERY, idle and LAMBDA expansions

For QUERY on port pi, let U be the explicit central linear permutation with
columns (q,b_1,...,b_8). Thus U C_1 U^-1=Q, including its absolute phase.
The stream initializes the single serial auxiliary bank under a fresh
QUERY lifetime, adds the complete source into that zero field, applies
U^-1, completes positive C_1, and applies U. For a negative query it then
applies X_q, since Q^-1=X_q Q. It adds the resulting whole scratch to the
actual destination, then clears the lifetime. Source and destination are
distinct; no overwrite or extra rank-one call is introduced. The paid
projection preserves the reviewer's logical `query_scratch,ci,pi` identity,
while the full record also preserves the actual reused bank and lifetime.

For an IDLE_ONE list of distinct stages, `axis_order` is a concrete full
permutation whose first k columns are precisely those 8*|stages| selected
axes. The stream applies its inverse, completes positive C_k, and applies
the permutation. All other axes remain in the complete factor throughout.

For LAMBDA with sign s in {-1,1}, the actual caller meaning is

`Lambda_s z = ((1+s*i)z + (1-s*i)Q^2 z)/2`.

Here Q^2 is the complete translation X_q on all f lanes; Lambda is **not**
the tensor product of its one-lane linear mixture. The concrete expansion is:
initialize the auxiliary bank; ADD-copy z; translate the scratch by X_q;
multiply the destination by the integer matrix [[1,-s],[s,1]]; multiply the
scratch by [[1,s],[-s,1]]; add the two complete integer numerators; divide
that sum by two once; clear scratch. Its net map is precisely Lambda_s,
with no positive child and no extra target. Both coefficient matrices act
outside the address tensor power.

The one final half matters on the selected common grid. On one translation
orbit put z=(g,-g), with g=2^-F, and take s=1. The complete output is
(i*g,-i*g), on the same grid. Each separately halved summand has components
g/2, off that grid. Thus separately dividing the two summands is an invalid
exact-grid implementation even on this benign input. Both signs and more
integer pairs are checked. This is a scoped counterexample to that lowering,
not a counterexample to the source LAMBDA or its accepted common-grid proof.
The retained W+26 temporary format accommodates the complete integer
numerators; exact divisibility of the final sum is inherited from the selected
common-grid invariant. Initialization supplies zero residual under the
retained contract; a different implementation must propagate its residual.

## 6. Root jobs, chronology, ownership and source inversion

The pinned caller proof gives each root owner the diagonal
`Q^epsilon product_(s in S) D_s`, with epsilon in {-1,0,1} and a nonempty
set S. A negative Q exponent is corrected by X_q. The output is then a
positive job on the independent directions (q if present) and the selected
stage axes. The stream explicitly chooses an axis permutation followed,
when needed, by the central q-basis permutation U. Call their composition G.
It applies G at the root entrance, the actual caller, the negative-Q
correction if needed, then G^-1 at the root exit. Consequently the endpoint
on that same root owner is canonical positive C_k. Different root owners
may use different entrance maps because the source identity holds for
arbitrary independent input arrays in the common caller address space.

Each caller J maps its 400 main slots injectively to the actual current root
owners. Its 1,080 helper slots have fresh permanent labels
`['helper',ci,slot]`. The 3,952 root owners and 19,440 helpers are pairwise
distinct productive fields. Their target ranks are the accepted inventory.

The owned bank `['owned',ci,k]` is distinguished from its active lifetime
`['owned',ci,k,init_native_index]`. The 51 bank labels have 54 chronological
lifetimes per invocation; they must not be pooled into one birth per bank.
Every owned operand points to its currently active birth, including descending
MOVE cuts, its CLEAR, and any later reinitialization at a new birth. All 18
invocations reserve their native banks separately. The serial auxiliary bank
`['aux',0]` is shared between QUERY and LAMBDA only after full retirement;
its lifetime additionally retains opcode, caller index and port. It is never
identified with a productive input or with a simultaneously live native bank.

Sequentially emit caller events in their source order. Within J emit its
entry boundaries, all native events in their literal order, and its exit
boundaries. Within a replicated outer event use the actual port-list order.
Within every expansion finish the complete operator before advancing.
Each event has exact parent source indices, verbatim raw operands, a named
expansion part, current owner/lifetime and entry/exit contact. Thus retaining
the first raw event at each native `(ci,ni)` reconstructs every native word;
retaining each outer caller event and each J parent reconstructs the actual
caller. The native body contains no invented scalar event or dropped COPY.
Derived root records point to the actual type/role diagonal metadata.

Raw reconstruction alone would be insufficient: the source/action audit
also compares the emitted scalar coefficient and ordered owners, every
MOVE recipe and phase, and the right/left helper inverse direction. It checks
chart continuity, simultaneous-owner separation, exact lifecycle births,
all native event multiplicities, and the final productive output contacts.
Mutations changing an ordinary scalar, phase, helper side, lifetime, tensor
scope, or deleting an ordinary event are rejected. These mutations can leave
the paid projection unchanged. The paid digest is therefore a cross-check,
not a replacement for ordinary semantics.

Given the ordered full sequence, B_j is uniquely the consecutive ordinary
interval between the j-th and next paid record, with empty intervals allowed.
No new order/nonaliasing theorem for the expanded Pascal/lockstep compiler is
claimed. The prior ordered `iter_units` and leaf locator are later consumers;
their product/completion/split role and source joins remain open here.

## 7. Complete metadata budget and explicit physical exclusions

All source constants are finite and fixed before payload precision f, row
multiplicity, width, or recursive input size vary. The metadata emitter does
not enumerate the 2^(153*f) address factor, any payload bits, or an expanded
recursive occurrence tree. It uses a read-only parsed source/caller/catalogue,
the current native mapping and active-frame/lifetime ledger, and one outgoing
record. Apart from these fixed references its live native state is O(1531)
field records. A verifier may retain additional census state; that is not
the producer's workspace claim.

Setup includes source acquisition/decompression/validation, extraction of the
authenticated q-bases, and either construction or authenticated reading of
the complete 3,057-recipe catalogue and 104 main boundaries. Construction uses
the actual exact nine-dimensional compiler, including its 512-entry Gaussian
state computations and integer bit lengths. No uniform polynomial claim in
a variable native dimension is made. The canonical review's accepted map
digests are compared after construction; lookup of a recipe is not an oracle
for unproved matrix entries. This finite setup is paid once per compiled
template, or again if a consumer elects to rebuild it.

Let H be the emitted record count; let L be the bit length of all retained
source/catalogue data; let b bound every integer/coefficient/index encoding
encountered, including the native birth index. One record contains at most a
complete A-entry axis permutation, a fixed 17-axis matrix or nine-axis
monomial recipe, two owner identifiers, source indices, a rational scalar,
and a bounded raw source event. Its encoded bit length is
O(A*log(A+1)+17^2+9^2+b), with literal JSON key overhead also counted.
Exact decimal conversion and comparisons cost at most O(b^2) elementary bit
operations using school algorithms. Reading/writing the emitted bytes costs
their complete length, not one unit per large integer. The normal receipt
reports that complete canonical JSONL byte count and maximum record size;
the stream is hashed without retaining its full expansion.

A deliberately weak sequential-tape bound suffices without unpriced random
lookup: scan the finite source/catalogue and current ledger to resolve every
record, perform finite matrix/index arithmetic with school bit operations,
and serialize it. This costs
`O(T_setup + H*(L + 1531*b + A^3*b^2) + output_bits*b^2)`
and the stated finite reference/ledger workspace. For the exact fixed
template, all quantities except H/output repetitions are fixed constants;
precompiling the finite tables gives ordinary output-linear serialization
with a fixed setup constant. The Python indexed implementation is a concrete
metadata implementation, not a claim to already emit that sequential machine.
No arithmetic on a variable replica number, path or f is silently O(1): this
raw-unit producer receives none of those as arithmetic inputs. Their future
codecs, descriptors and invocation wrappers must be separately charged.

For a physical realization the complete bill must add, for **every** emitted
operation, payload/source access and gathering, frame/layout changes, its
actual scalar/monomial/child body, copying or SWAP, return to the same physical
owner, work retirement, and retained-history extent. It must also add field
shape/ABI preparation, private-bank allocation, the 53,280 scale conversions,
root and helper boundaries, all recursive children and recursion bookkeeping.
The selected common format has F=p+D, W=p+h+2D+3 and temporary width W+26;
reusing those source bounds does not manufacture a codec between this semantic
ABI and a literal scalar or native-kernel ABI. No numerical physical tariff
is certified by the present serialization counts.

The optimistic full-system effect is therefore a finite additional metadata
and ordinary-service constant, with exactly the same P and T invoice. There
is no component exponent gain to assemble, no change in the selected
certificate, and no improvement of the complete accepted kappa. The complete
bound remains conditional. Finite-precompilation may absorb a proved fixed
metadata polynomial in its existing regime; the physical joins and their
cost proofs still have to be supplied.
