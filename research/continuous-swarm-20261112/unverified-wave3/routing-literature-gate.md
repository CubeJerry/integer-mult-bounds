# Routing literature gate

Bounded Wave3 root reconnaissance, not a new theorem or contribution.

- #6291 already isolates earlier-target frame506 calls: 64 f^2 of 224 f^2 shifts use the forward shear conjugated by an unimplemented high-axis swap. Their completed_swap_oracle is charged to T_old; no fixed-tape transposition follows.
- #2776 already supplies the opposite, later-target ordered cyclic shear in linear time on 14 fixed tapes. Do not conflate the two orientations.
- #3825 already states the restricted split/merge XOR-layout obstruction and transposition relationship. Repeating a three-shear or XOR/transposition reduction would not be new research.
- Harvey and van der Hoeven, Integer multiplication is at least as hard as matrix transposition, arXiv:2503.22848v1 (2025), sections1.1--1.4, explicitly distinguish conjectured full multitape lower bounds from conservative/one-tape restrictions. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22848v1 . It gives no unconditional superlinear transposition lower bound.

No general routing impossibility is inferred. Literal record reversal is consistent with these results and is materially narrower than coordinate transposition. The scalar exact-source installer remains a more concrete new integration target.
