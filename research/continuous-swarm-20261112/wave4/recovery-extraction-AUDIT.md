## Exact prior result and budget

Let original dyadic targets t_i=2^ell_i satisfy s_i<t_i<2s_i and axis zero
be fastest. The selected image is Q_i(j)=floor(t_i*j/s_i+1/2), at address
A(j)=sum_i Q_i(j_i)*2^(sum_(k<i) ell_k). It is not prefix padding. Already
at (s,t)=(3,4), its selected coordinates are 0,1,3, not 0,1,2. Two axes
(s0,t0)=(3,4),(s1,t1)=(5,8) select
0,1,3,8,9,11,12,13,15,20,21,23,24,25,27 in the 32-record field.

The prior Extract owner reads a prepared native Count packet
D,(ell_i,s_i) and a grid Count q, then scans the entire original-order T
field. Each record has two q+3-bit sign-magnitude components and '#';
'^'/'!' mark the B field. It prepares actual T and S clocks, keeps a
packed axis state on fixed stock, copies selected records unchanged,
validates/skips arbitrary holes, and consumes the tail. It preserves the
borrowed field and descriptor origins, protects an S output until consume,
and pays preparation, cleanup and retained borrowed backing. Runtime sizes
are encoded Counts; it is not a newly exported local-instruction graph.

With n=sum ell_i, the complete existing bill is
O(n^3+log(q+1)+T(q+n)) time and
O(H_borrowed+S*q+n+log(q+1)) backing. In the admitted fine family,
n=Theta(p0) and q=Theta(p0), so this is O(T*p0)=O(V), where the framed
logical volume V is proportional to T(q+3). The source setup and complete
T scan are included. O(V) backing additionally needs a genuine bounded-
history admission; a short current borrowed field does not establish it.

Bit preservation introduces no normalization or rounding. Gamma=K_f+K_c,
the two S factors and the three deferred Gamma occurrences in #6993 remain
unchanged. After extraction the separately paid zero-drop codec and q+3
to q+2 native carrier still require their actual radius premise. The output

or recomputation charged. This already addresses the assigned consumer
