# Per-new-cache refinement closes both six-zero components

Local arithmetic is exact over Q at kappa .0038. Global row partition,
linear-domain assumptions and composition remain under C40 review. This
is an optimistic funding bound, not a physical supplier or kappa claim.

The generic pilot and its positive pair upper value are retained unchanged.
For each of128 zero subsets S, the refinement enumerates every proposed
NEW cache set N among the sites not already enabled by P_E+Z. Nempty uses
the exact beta(Z). For each E in nonempty N, compute the individual generic
one-row debit gamma_E from prefix ranks of Z and Z+L_E, not their union L.
Every actual kernel enabling E contains Z plus a new witness from L_E.
The lost target price is consequently at least EACH gamma_E and hence
their maximum. Subtracting that maximum is equivalent to taking the
minimum of the per-E directed upper gains. No common generic vector is
asserted to enable all new caches.

There are384 evaluated cases:128 for cache42487 and256 for pair35448/35452.
Every beta prefix rank is independently recomputed; each individual union
rank is recomputed directly, without the pilot's quotient-reuse formula.
Both components have maximum native upper bound exactly0. There are no
positive native or full-invoice cases. Normal/-O receipts agree except time.

The source supplies a short analytic explanation. Each of the two zero
matrices has six independent rows, and each paid zero-row rank is at most
every target rank in its support. Nonzero determinant matching therefore
funds every subset: its zero credit z is at most beta(Z), for any monotone
price. No cache is already enabled for any of the128 zero subsets.

All zero-row supports have target ranks>=4. Cache42487's L-space also has
support ranks>=4. For its new witness, any column basis of Z+span(u) contains
a column basis of Z plus one further column, costing at least f(4). Hence
its total target debit is>=beta+f(4), covering its rank4 cache credit.

For pair35448/35452, L35448 has minimum support targetrank3; L35452 has
minimum4. Thus low-only credit is bounded by f(2)-f(3), high-only by
f(1)-f(4), and both by f(1)+f(2)-f(4), after discarding the nonpositive
zero-only balance. At .0038 the last expression is strictly negative:
f(4)-f(1)-f(2) has directed lower bound about .98424471722944524. This
closes the generic union-L false positive f(1)+f(2)-f(3).

The extra-column argument is local: all nonzero columns of the augmented
matrix have the stated support-minimum price. A spanning column basis for
the augmented rows restricts to a spanning set for Z, so it contains an old
basis; the remaining column has the minimum stated price. Preparations and
cache lifetimes remain free, so their actual bills cannot improve the bound.

Controls distinguish the low cache's rank3 prefix from the high cache's
rank4 prefix at emptyS, and require the formerly positive both-cache case
to become negative. Exact source literals, helper targets, histories,
native payload/invoice correspondence and17caller copies remain pinned.
Scalar-linear helper promises, arbitrary independent mains and original
retained productive coordinates are essential. No nonlinear promise,
changed producer history or decoded productive type is covered here.

REFINED.json contains all384 cases, individual prefix/debit profiles and
controlling cache. The frozen generic logical payload excludes only elapsed
time, so rerunning the generic computation does not invalidate this check
solely because of timing. Source/global proof acceptance is separate from
the checked local arithmetic; all five foundations remain unchanged.
