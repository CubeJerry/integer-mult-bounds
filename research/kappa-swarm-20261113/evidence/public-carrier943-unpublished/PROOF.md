# One-source injection through original carrier 943

Prepared for CubeJerry with substantial OpenAI assistance. Apache-2.0.

This is a bounded changed-history gate on exact #7481, not a physical rewrite or
a κ claim. It complements the fixed-original-carrier barrier in
`../public-cache-interface/PROOF.md`. `check.py` is an exact rational source
replay. It checks source ownership/frame timing, computes literal old history
rows, and propagates the effect of a latest injection through the whole native
word. The full source pin and executed coverage are in `RECEIPT.json`.

## Every single injection timing and coefficient

For any insertion after30780, the injected source is z. Because no later
pre-cut operation targets885, the candidate target remains z. Linearity makes
each changed history row `H_j + α*c_j*z`, for some timing-dependent scalar c_j.
If a combination of these histories equalled z, then

\[
\sum_j\lambda_jH_j=(1-\alpha\sum_j\lambda_jc_j)z.
\]

Independence of p,h,z forces both λ's to vanish, contradicting the coefficient
one on z. Thus no α or post30780 timing enables the cache. For the latest
injection, the changed rows are exactly `p` and `h+(α/2)z`.
An explicit separating input is

\[
b=2,\quad c=0,\quad a=-\alpha,\quad x1459=2+\alpha,
\quad\text{all other inputs}=0.
\]

It satisfies the promise, makes both changed189 histories zero, and leaves the
target equal to2. The zero input has the same histories and target zero.

For an insertion before the30780 update of885, the injected source is b.
Linearity makes the changed histories `H_j+α*c_j*b` and the changed candidate
target `z+α*d*b`. The final exact rank above says z is outside
`span(p,h,b)`, so that target is outside the changed-history span for every α
and every timing-dependent d,c_j. The case immediately before30780 is included
here. Hence the whole finite cut interval0..35426 is covered by a proof using
two source ranks; it is not an enumeration of35427 full edited words.

Six late-injection rational coefficients are replayed as controls. A second
control has duplicated old history rows receiving different encodings; their
difference does reveal the source. This illustrates why the exact independence
and temporal history argument is necessary and why arbitrary histories must
not be silently granted.

## Sharp information obligation for one extra observation

Let Pα be the changed189 history span and zα the changed target. The result
above proves `zα∉Pα`. A single additional scalar-linear observation v at189
can enable this particular cache exactly when

\[
[v]\in\mathbf F^{\times}[z_\alpha]
\quad\text{in the quotient by }P_\alpha.
\]

Merely carrying a nonzero885 coefficient is insufficient: the old independent
mixtures have to cancel. This criterion is necessary and sufficient at the
information level; actual historical backing, frame operators, whole-column
physical transport, endpoint restoration and precision remain extra work.

For a prospective one-import bank G at a source coframe A in the *late*
injection family, this condition has a useful exact preliminary test. Form R
from the old189 rows and the old A-history rows, and let d list their scalar
injection coefficients. The existence of some nonzero generic α with z in the
new pooled span is equivalent to either

1. z already belongs to `span(R)`, or
2. `rank([R|d]) > rank(R)`.

Indeed, in the second case an old row relation with nonzero encoded coefficient
isolates `α*z`. If neither holds, d is a well-defined linear functional on
`span(R)` and the changed span is the graph of its tilt along the independent
direction z; it cannot contain z. In the first case, if the coefficients are
consistent, the rank-one map on `span(R)` is invertible except possibly at one
α, so a generic α preserves access. Inconsistent coefficients give the second
case. This is an exact enabling gate for a future **additional** import search,
not a statement that a new imported provider or its full bill has been built.

## Optimistic budget and scope limit

At `.0038`, the candidate's native credit before all preparation and repair is

## Bounded one-import gate: completed follow-on

`import_gate.py` executes the additional necessary filter just described for
the latest legal injection cut35426. It reconstructs every original coframe's
strict-prefix initial and MOVE-arrival rows over Q and propagates the unit
injection coefficient beside them. Demoted1459 is excluded as free backing.
Available histories are pooled by GF2 span as an optimistic overgrant of free
monomial normalization; no operator/phase identity is assumed for aliases.

For each available canonical coframe A whose actual A→189 Clifford B-block
rank is1,2 or3, form the union R of its history with the two available189 rows.
Every original row has zero885 coefficient, so z does not belong to span(R).
The only enabling option is therefore an old row relation with nonzero
injection coefficient. Exact rational elimination finds no such relation in
any of the67 available coframes. Both normal and `-O` executions cover67/67
domains, in about5 seconds. The receipt gives every coframe, history size,
rank and nesting flag. The rank histogram is retained there.

Each tariff is independently checked against the actual pinned Clifford
constructor: the rank of the interface's literal symplectic B-block equals
the geometric value
`dim(A)+8−2dim(A∩189)`. The source-defined geometry proof is separately frozen
in `../cache-frame-contract/PROOF.md`; the gate also computes all67 actual
tableaux, so a dimension-only nesting assumption does not set the bill. It
even grants nonnested rank2/3 one-child interfaces that the literal MOVE
grammar does not admit, making this a more favorable necessary filter.

Three controls pass: a source-frame3 rank8 import can supply z from its actual
initial885 and943 rows; duplicated old rows with distinct encoding
coefficients expose the injected value; consistent duplicated encodings do
not. These guard against a checker that simply reports no imports.

`IMPORT-RECEIPT.json` also contains directed rational17-call budgets. Under
this **one-cache-credit** scope, an import of rank4 or more cannot reach
`.0038` even when every other new service is free. Combining that price gate
with the67-domain negative result excludes the latest-one-injection plus
one-import family at that target. It does not exclude two or more imports,
other injection times combined with imports, coordinated scalar edits,
additional newly zero MOVE credits, or a smaller complete bound improvement
using a rank4..6 import. Those are distinct questions. The unresolved public
zero-retag admission and all twelve endpoint repairs also remain compulsory
for any positive successor.

No supplier is emitted. Further research requires a fresh bounded allocation;
no broad producer rewrite was searched.
