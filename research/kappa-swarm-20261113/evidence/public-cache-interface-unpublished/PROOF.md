## What stays fixed

The bound permits new whole-field scalar COPY/ADD/SCALE operations and separately
paid nested MOVE paths, but keeps every original carrier's normalized history
through event 40710 fixed. Thus it excludes injecting a new dependency into
already-paid original carrier 943 (or into another original producer). Such an
injection is a genuinely changed-history problem and must be checked separately.
No claim is made about arbitrary new block encodings, intermediate changed
frames, decoded targets, fractional child jobs, or a general multiplication
lower bound.

The original independent input of demoted helper 1459 is absent as a resource.
It cannot be counted as an already prepared frame-189 cache. Every retained

## Whole-field path tariff

ADD/COPY/SCALE preserve their common frame. Every permitted nested MOVE on a
dependency path is charged rank \(r_i=|d_{i+1}-d_i|\). Therefore
\(\sum_i r_i\ge8\). For \(0\le\kappa\le1\), the increasing subadditive tariff
\(f(r)=r^{1-\kappa}\), with \(f(0)=0\), gives

\[
\text{new paid path cost}\ \ge\sum_i f(r_i)\ge
 f\!\left(\sum_i r_i\right)\ge f(8).
\]

Sharing this preparation with other new calculations cannot reduce the total
new paid bill below a single mandatory dependency path. This dimension argument
is limited to the stated whole-field MOVE model and fixed original carriers;
it is not a claim about all physical linear algorithms. No column-multiplicity
or ambient-rank scaling assertion is needed.

The native net gain is thus at most

\[
f(8)-f(1)-f(8)=-f(1)=-1.
\]

The complete 17-call complex moment is at most its exact #7481 baseline minus
17. Directed rational intervals in `RECEIPT.json` verify that this fails at the
selected `.00363005817`, at the unselected comparator `.003647081039689`, and
at `.0038`. Ordinary costs were optimistically discarded, so adding the
unresolved ordinary/compiler/precision bill cannot reverse this obstruction.

For comparison, a simple new-field construction copies 885 at frame 3, moves
3→105 with rank 1, subtracts retained input 705 at its original entrance, and
moves 105→189 with rank 7, before 705's first original use. The tariff is
\(f(1)+f(7)\), above the lower bound. `verify.py` checks all three actual

## Native ownership and zero-reframe contract

The retained native and precision interfaces have only INIT, CLEAR, MOVE,
ADD, COPY and SCALE. INIT/CLEAR are owned-only. COPY requires a freshly exact
zero owned destination in the precision checker. Every public MOVE is a
strictly upward nested, positive-rank child and remains charged when its data
happen to be zero. The precision envelope explicitly says that a rounded child
need not preserve exact zero and adds one MOVE residual. Thus ordinary public
zero-retagging is currently a **missing source interface**, not an admitted
rewrite and not a theorem of physical impossibility.

Copying a stored public A field into fresh owned A backing and subtracting that
identical stored value with integer coefficients does produce exact stored
zero on the retained common integer grid, including prior shared errors. The
existing triangle envelope does not retain that correlation. A new typed
zero-reframe service could write an all-zero destination buffer or update an
appropriate zero descriptor, since every invertible frame sends zero to zero.
To use it one must explicitly provide its interpreter, zero certificate,
storage/backing/head-return contract, ordinary cost and correlated precision
rule. Neither nonzero owned CLEAR nor equality of GF2 frame spans supplies
this new public service. Same-span observations are safe overgrants for a
negative certificate; a positive physical cache must check actual frame
operators and any phase adapters.

The elementary proposed public rewrite is: INIT fresh owned A backup; COPY
public into backup; ADD public minus backup; perform the unadmitted typed
zero-reframe A→B; ADD a backed B-cache into the public field; CLEAR backup at A.
Owned nonzero retirement remains under the retained owned-erasure assumption;
it is not required to have a zero value before CLEAR. A historical B-cache must
actually have been accumulated at strict earlier observations, with admitted
coefficient arithmetic, and kept alive with paid storage and ordinary access.
The abstract ability to decode a history does not itself furnish that backing.

Keeping a positive-rank rounded MOVE for the zero-reframe step is an existing-
grammar fallback, but retains the rank-8 bill at the candidate and therefore
does not yield the advertised recursive saving. Its residual also has to be
propagated; setting an ideal input to zero does not suppress it.

## Ordinary and supplier bill still required for any successful successor

For a parameterized macro with a separately allocated B-cache built using m
scalar additions, the changed operation counts relative to the removed MOVE

one old MOVE disappears. The retained scalar source fee is
\(s(q)=4(\operatorname{bits}|a|+\operatorname{bits}b+1+
\operatorname{popcount}|a|)\) for an ADD/COPY coefficient \(q=a/b\), so the
scalar increment is \(\sum_j s(\lambda_j)+48\). Two new owned lifetimes add

packing, head returns, exact-grid odd divisions, mass/error envelopes, compiler
constants, caller restoration and target relabeling require a whole fresh bill.
These item counts are an accounting template, not source admission. If an
already-prepared demoted 1459 is the cache backing, omit the separate cache
lifetime and its preparation additions; its preparation and target debit are
still compulsory. The present negative tariff bound already grants all this
ordinary work free.
