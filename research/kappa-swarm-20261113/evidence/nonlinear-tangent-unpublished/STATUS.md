# Wave43 initial mathematics checkpoint

The local constant-rank argument works: a target that is a deterministic
function of its observation map is constant on every observation fiber.
At a constant-rank point, every differential-kernel direction is realized
by a fiber curve, so the ambient linear target annihilates that kernel.
One common tangent therefore preserves all finitely many dependencies.
Zero rows restrict to zero on the tangent. A retained-coordinate
submersion preserves arbitrary retained directions and gives a charged
coordinate pivot basis for the tangent annihilator.

Critical field caveat: a complex conclusion requires a complex tangent
(e.g. a complex submanifold). A real-smooth graph (z,conjugate(z)) has a
perfect deterministic nonlinear cache but need not admit a complex-linear
replacement with the same retained coordinates. Realification needs its
own funding theorem and is not silently assumed.

Other negative fixtures under development: a critical real cubic graph,
a complex cusp observation of a smooth curve, finite-grid digit decoding,
and nonregular digit-encoding graphs. Generic common regular points are
available for finite C1 rank maps near a retained submersion point; the
irreducible complex-algebraic version should follow in characteristic0.
