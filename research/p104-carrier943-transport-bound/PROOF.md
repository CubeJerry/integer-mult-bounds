## Why every affordable new transport is covered

For a Clifford U, let r(U) be its Fourier rank, equivalently the logarithm
base2 of the common nonzero support size of a row. Matrix multiplication gives

    r(UV) <= r(U)+r(V),

because a product row is a combination of at most2^r(U) rows, each with at
most2^r(V) entries. Cancellations can only reduce that support. Thus any new
path carrying information from an original A observation to the required
B=189 output has total integer rank at least r(U_B U_A^dagger). This remains
true with intermediate frames and rank-zero monomial steps.

If the total positive integer rank of the entire new sidecar network is at
most3, every source that can influence its B output is one of the already
pooled radius-three sources (or an original B observation). All of those
observations coincide on the two certified inputs. Branching, sharing,
intermediate combinations and additional zero scratch cannot distinguish
them. Consequently such a network cannot supply the required target.

At kappa=.0038, f(r)=r^(1-kappa), f(0)=0, is increasing and subadditive. Hence
for any network with total integer rank at least4,

    sum f(r_j) >= f(sum r_j) >= f(4).

The complete pinned17-call invoice leaves only
approximately3.4522144390136276 native-price units per call for every added
service after credit f(8) and debit f(1). The directed rational log/Taylor price
check proves f(4) exceeds that allowance: even the optimistic complete moment
M+17*(f(8)-f(1)-f(4)) is strictly negative (upper bound approximately-8.95508).
Networks of total rank at most3 fail by the common separator; all higher
totals fail this optimistic .0038 budget. This exhausts the stated sidecar
transport model, not arbitrary new producers or physical algorithms.

The complete caller comparison assumes the unchanged17-call template and
this per-invocation replacement/cost model. Different cross-invocation
encodings, old paid-payload changes, different initialization resources,
ordinary frame algorithms and reordered native contacts remain separate
