## Exhaustive textual call graph and denominator admission

AST inventory `CALL-SITES.json` confirms exactly two raw-call expressions:
`Arithmetic.remainder` at line20 and `Arithmetic.inverse` at line58.
No Ring is constructed or called. The source uses only raw add/subtract,
compare and divmod, and its own product/modular routines.

1. Every remainder denominator is the current source modulus M. Each such
   modulus first passes `admit`: M>1 and a zero top bit, so
   0<M<2^(P-1). This includes left base/local/digit, right base/local and
   conditional canonical-value reduction, and each inverse qmod.
2. In inverse, r0 initially copies M and r1 copies the reduced base a.
   The base is a remainder, so0<=a<M. A raw division executes only while
   r1.zero() is false, hence its D=r1 is positive. After a division the
   next r1 is its exact remainder, strictly below the previous positive
   r1. Induction therefore gives0<D<M<2^(P-1) at every raw Euclidean call.
   D=1 occurs naturally at the final unit step and is admitted by B10's
   core; this is an essential new integration coverage relative to B10's
   selected sixth call, whose denominator exceeds1.

## Why every boundary composes

The original source's live schedule has at most24 newly owned numerical
Words, or32 including all eight borrowed ones. B10's replacement reserves
only two outputs where old division holds at least scan/Q/R simultaneously,
and preserves the same number of live outputs afterward. Hence free-owner
availability is source-derived, not a new closure assumption.

Induct on actual raw-call occurrences in the terminating original execution.
Before the first, the live/free relation is identity. At each call, source
typing gives the proved B10 ABI; its arithmetic output and stack-pairing
lemma restore the relation. Retained arithmetic between calls is equivariant
under this relation, including alias checks and protected/keep tests. At
producer cleanup use the proved commuting disjoint clears and rebuild the
free-stack bijection. All source values/branch outcomes and serialized
records agree. The original Euclidean descent proves finite termination;
there is no assumption that a partially substituted program terminates by
itself. This is a source-specific repeated-substitution proof, not a new
generic finite-composition theorem.

Each call uses actual bank Word backing and the same four selector windows,
which DIVIDE returns blank at their origins. Q/R are consumed and freed by
the existing numerical continuation. Earlier physical histories remain on
their own owners. All64 numerical roles have their active P+2 frames already
prepared by Entry; selector history is max(old,8,later header/key extent).
There is no new tape per raw call and no old profile moved by a proof
permutation. Descriptor grants and all head returns remain fully paid.

## Optimistic complete budget

Let m,t be left/right record counts and v<=t the number of valid right
coordinates. Let e_i be the Euclidean loop length for the inverse on record
i. The exact un-faulted raw-call count is

J=3m+2t+v+2*sum_i e_i.

Each inverse has at most2P+1 iterations by the existing source descent

J <= (m+t)(4P+5) <= (P-2)(4P+5).
