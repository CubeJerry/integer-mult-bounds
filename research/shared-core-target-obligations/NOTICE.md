The new work was developed with substantial OpenAI assistance in a coordinated
research run for @CubeJerry. Separate construction and review agents pursued
the proposed boundary/selector mechanisms and independently checked their
linear maps, source census, integer-power bounds, inverse columns and small
minors. The independent checker implementations import no contributor scientific
code. They do consume the authenticated predecessor word and receipts and rely
on explicitly retained endpoint and all-column contracts.

The separate [variable-selectors appendix](variable-selectors/README.md)
retains the original agent finite screen alongside a separately implemented
independent verifier. Its finite family, source preparation, proof and receipt
carry their own attribution and scope; it is not part of the eight-check core
validation gate.

The elementary dirty-bus/skew-matrix identity, projection algebra and ordinary
rank arguments are not claimed as new. The contribution is their precise
application to the selected source, its complete hypothetical profile, the
owned-bank threshold, and the scoped repair obstructions with reproducible
witnesses.

This is AI-assisted research and agent-based independent review, not claimed
human review, formal-kernel acceptance or an unconditional multiplication proof.
**No new kappa.** There is no completed H4 native construction or physical
supplier admission. The applied one-way repair theorem and the separate
abstract communication-cut lemma have the distinct scopes stated in
[SELECTOR.md](SELECTOR.md); neither is a general model ceiling.
