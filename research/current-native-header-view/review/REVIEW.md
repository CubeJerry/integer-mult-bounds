## All-size local execution and cost argument

The inherited fixed-schema decoder scans each encoded length and each binary
value, writes only its own field/length roles, and restores the parent. For
field bit lengths ell_i>=1, total header length is E=2*sum_i ell_i+23.
Canonical checks, copies and current-prefix clears make a fixed number of
linear traversals of those bodies.

The only new arithmetic is multiplication of f by fixed 9. Its finite carry
lies in 0,...,8: reading bit x emits `(9x+c) mod2` and updates the carry to
`floor((9x+c)/2)`. The usual low-prefix integer invariant proves the exact
product, including its bounded final carry suffix. Its output length is at
most ell_f+4. Copying z and writing canonical zero are also linear.
Consequently the complete view length is O(E+1), and its encoding, later
retirement and all restored head traversals cost O(E+1).

There are 39 fixed private roles plus the borrowed parent role. All are
distinct. The tables contain only local reads, local writes, unit moves,
fixed jumps and terminal states. No runtime shape is decoded into a host
integer and no field is traversed by a host index. Template construction,
source authentication, and the fixed 3217 instruction rows are setup work;
they are not a free native supplier. The complete relative invoice remains

`T_source_routes + T_native + O_template(E+1)`.

Here T_native includes the already published setup/control/format/cleanup
bill and actual finite native allowances. At this specific first helper the
inherited base-nine placement needs no new route; a different occurrence may
not borrow that fact. General occurrence normalization is excluded.

The private backing bound is O_template(1+max E) over the complete history,
while a later shorter call scans its current prefixes. Erasing dictionary
entries in reviewer hardware is not a claim that physical backing disappears.
The retained maximum extent is reported separately. The new header is retired
even when the called native raises, but the native provider's own exception
and rollback guarantees are not strengthened by that fact. General malformed
physical alphabets or interrupted execution remain outside this review.

## Independent executable method

`table_machine.py` is a separate sparse tape model and a separate finite-table
interpreter. It imports neither the author's runner nor the production tape
or encoding routines. It prohibits writes to the borrowed parent, movement
past a local marker, and modification of sentinel cells before each translated
origin. Observer encoders/decoders independently specify the complete 23-field
bit string; host integers occur only in those observers and fixtures.

`check_header_tables.py` executes the actual emitted PREPARE/RELEASE graphs.
Eight canonical cases cover f=1,2,3,7, remainders 0,8,9,13,144, multiple selected
chunk widths, nontrivial outer/row/stock values, and translated origins. Every
case compares all 23 decoded values and all bytes, restores every head, preserves
the parent, and erases every private body. A separate f=17 call followed by a
small call checks current traversal against a fresh small bank.

Three actual compiled-program mutants change the multiplier 9 to 8, copy the
wrong entry origin, and write zero instead of blank on view retirement. All
must be rejected by the independent oracle or cleanup checks. Three malformed
binary controls exercise an empty header, a truncated final field, and f=0;
the release graph is then checked on its resulting private state. This is not
an arbitrary corrupt-alphabet recovery theorem.

The source check independently reads the authenticated controller and raw word,
reconstructs helper3356 from the first-local owner map, constructs the exact
nested token, and checks the actual typed binder. Fourteen adverse events or
routes cover wrong owners, flat/extra nesting, other cuts, bool/int ambiguity,
wrong ambient/multiplicity/frame, reversed vectors, and after-stage placement.
It does not use a numerical callback as evidence of a Gaussian endpoint.
