## Change and mathematical scope

Prove a paid-rank versus scalar-work obstruction for isolated replacements of
all 23 owned rises in selected #7000. **No new kappa.** This excludes a specific
tempting route to .005, not that target or joint rewrites generally.

Every actual rise has a nonzero coefficient of an independent public array.
Complex-array surjectivity, or spanning scaled inputs on exact grids, forces
a single-input replacement to implement the complete prescribed map.

## Exact result and target relevance

At column count f, a linear replacement with g whole-field ADDs and Gaussian
children of total rank s has row support at most 2^(g+s*f). The rank-r return
has 2^(r*f) equal-modulus entries. Squared operator error is at least
max(0,1-2^(g-(r-s)*f)); exactness or error below 1/sqrt(2) requires
g>=(r-s)*f. Arbitrary monomial maps/scalars and independent scratch are allowed;
external data-dependent side information is excluded.

At t=199/200, paid moment below r^t implies s<r, hence at least f ADDs.
Fixed native templates cannot do this for all f; literal full-field scans cost
Omega(V*f), exceeding O(V*f^t*polylog(f)). Fused/partial-field algorithms and
changed boundaries remain outside scope. The r*f-ADD butterfly is checked
as the admitted scalar escape.

The 23-rise histogram has rank mass56 per native. Removing all 18 occurrences
for free would move the actual moment from about -567.716011 to +434.821363.
The theorem blocks funding that relaxation through these isolated lower-rank
replacements. Joint correlations, changed trajectories, productive targets and
new interfaces remain possible. The selected Gaussian factorization is inherited,
not inferred from binary frame dimension alone.
