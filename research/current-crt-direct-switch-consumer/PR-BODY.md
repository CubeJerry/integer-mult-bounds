## What changed and why

The literal canonical switch predicate still entered through supplied descriptors and returned an unconsumed Boolean. This package connects that boundary to the actual current encoded CRT leaf of #7388: paid source/header scans feed a fixed literal adapter, and its physical output toggles the current wire before the existing physical key/radix path consumes the destination.

The new package is `research/current-crt-direct-switch-consumer`. No selected certificate or supplier is changed. **No new kappa; the multiplication bound and all five foundational assumptions remain conditional.**

## Exact result

For h>=1, p>=h+1 and the explicit matching height/width, source framing, owner and target contracts, one fixed 2500-instruction program on twenty tapes reads p-bit h,s,a,j and the current h-bit wire. It prepares the exact literal predicate ABI, consumes the returned bit at the actual target, and restores all borrowed descriptors/heads and fourteen private owners. The current wire is the only semantic output. Its exact new overhead is

`30p+74h+6*bit_length(h)+10k+261`,

where k is the one-based physical target position. With the unchanged predicate, complete time is at most `1024(h+3)^2+128(p+h+3)` and historical increment is at most `p+16(h+4)`. The layer interpretation additionally requires `k-1=min(j,2h-2-j)`; the paid row-counter and palindromic-target invariant establishes this in the caller.

The exact four-tag #7388 caller and its inverse restore the same source, payload, key bytes, physical backing and heads. A modulus-three variant detects stale current wires that the original modulus-two involution cannot expose. Zero-offset, invalid-left and padded-right source cases also pass. Unit modulus remains outside the retained numerical source domain and is recorded as a rejection, despite being allowed by the standalone predicate.
